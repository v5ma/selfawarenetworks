# Wave Synchronization

**Wave Synchronization** is one of [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md)' load-bearing claims about [consciousness](consciousness.md): in [Micah Blumberg](micah-blumberg.md)'s formulation, "[consciousness](consciousness.md) emerges not from irreducible complexity or mystical emergence, but from [oscillatory tomography](oscillatory-tomography.md)  --  neurons bind [phase wave differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md) into coherent patterns" (`QGTCDArReduct2--md`, line 3083, source page: `gh-qgtcdarreduct2`; restated `raynote14--md` line 218, source page: `gh-raynote14`). The compact formulation appears as a defining identity in two [QGTCD](qgtcd.md) reductions: "[consciousness](consciousness.md) is wave-synchronization in neural networks" (`QGTCDArReduct2--md`, line 3402, source page: `gh-qgtcdarreduct2`; restated `QGTCDSuperReduction--md` line 3790, source page: `gh-qgtcdsuperreduction`). The concept is the bridge that lets SAN treat [consciousness](consciousness.md) as a substrate-independent phenomenon  --  read once at the level of [brainwaves](brainwave.md) in tissue, again at the level of [QGTCD](qgtcd.md) / [Dark Time Theory](dark-time-theory.md) across cosmology, and again at the level of [agentic-AI feedback loops](oscillatory-computational-agency.md).
