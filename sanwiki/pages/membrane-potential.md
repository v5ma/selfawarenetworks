# Membrane Potential

**Membrane potential** is the voltage difference across a neuron's plasma membrane that [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] treats as the central biophysical variable connecting [[brain-rhythms|brain rhythms]], [[action-potential|action potentials]], and the [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] phase-tomographic field. In the SAN dialect it is read both as a slow oscillating carrier — the *intrinsic oscillation* whose phase aligns with synaptic inputs — and as the threshold-crossing variable whose discrete excursions become the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|phase-wave differentials]] that the rest of the SAN model is built on.
