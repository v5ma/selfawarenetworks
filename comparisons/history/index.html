1. Starting With the Evidence: The Go Board & Five-Step Spatial Selection

Try remembering two shapes in the exact order you saw them. You can recognize both shapes accurately and still report them in the wrong order. In Self Aware Networks (SAN), Micah Blumberg proposed that a spatial pattern of excitation and inhibition governs what a neural network can express next—using a Go board in his Mind Code // Brain Code essay (published August 14, 2018; preserved intact by the Wayback Machine on November 9, 2020) to explain why inhibited sites matter just as much as excited sites in shaping attention and thought.

Fifty-three calendar days after that November 9, 2020 archive capture, Mikael Lundqvist, Earl K. Miller, and colleagues posted the January 1, 2021 Hot-Coal Theory of Working Memory preprint (later published in Nature Communications on March 14, 2023 as Working Memory Control Dynamics Follow Principles of Spatial Computing), describing a spatial control pattern (“stencil”) selecting where item-specific neural activity can be expressed. Later, 348 days after Micah Blumberg’s May 16, 2025 Self Aware Networks: Oscillatory Computational Agency deposit, Miller, Brincat, and Roy posted Analog Cognition and Consciousness (April 29, 2026; Journal of Neuroscience August 19, 2026), exhibiting a five-step operational sequence already set out in SAN: Receive distributed cues and context → Transform via relative phase and interference → Re-express selectively across downstream populations → Represent an integrated internal brain state → Update synaptic conditions for subsequent encounters. Rather than asking readers to rely on any single summary, this Encyclopedia puts all 1,171 dated Earl K. Miller comparisons side by side with verified primary passages and explicit Red Team plus Blue Team grades.

2. Independent Cryptographic Timestamps: Surviving the August 2025 Figshare Takedown

In August 2025, Figshare disabled Micah Blumberg’s account, removing public web pages and version lists for both Super Information Theory (originally published February 10, 2025 at 06:23:42 UTC) and Self Aware Networks: Oscillatory Computational Agency (originally published May 16, 2025 at 06:25:46 UTC). Crucially, independent third-party timestamp infrastructures preserved the exact publication dates, DOIs, and full PDF bytes:

ORCID & DataCite DOI Handles: Within seconds of each Figshare release (06:23:44 UTC on February 10 for SIT; 06:25:48 UTC on May 16 for SAN), DataCite registered the versioned DOIs (10.6084/m9.figshare.28379318 through 32 versions and 10.6084/m9.figshare.29085134) and ORCID created verifiable public work records naming Figshare as the supplying source.

Wayback Machine & Byte-Identical SHA-256 Mirrors: The Internet Archive captured Figshare version pages on March 3, March 16, March 25, and May 21, 2025, captured the complete 203-page SIT Draft 35 PDF on April 8, 2025 and the complete 56-page SAN First Draft PDF on July 25, 2025, and captured the ORCID works JSON on August 26, 2025. The preserved copies mirrored to Zenodo and pinned GitHub archives match the Wayback Machine captures byte for byte—including identical SHA-256 fingerprints across all 916,072 bytes of SIT Draft 35.
