# LTD (Long-Term Depression)

**LTD** -- Long-Term Depression -- is the synaptic-weakening counterpart to Long-Term Potentiation ([LTP](./ltp-structural-memory-encoding.md)) within the [Self](./gh-a0206z.md)-Aware Networks treatment of [chemical synapses](./chemical-synapse.md): when a postsynaptic spike fires *before* a presynaptic input, the synapse is weakened, the relevant [receptors](./3d-protein-receptor.md) are removed via endocytosis or exocytosis, and the resulting decrement is bound up with [inhibitory pattern sharpening](./ltd-inhibitory-pattern-sharpening.md) across the [oscillation hierarchy](./neural-oscillation-hierarchy.md). Inside SAN it is read not as a stand-alone Hebbian rule but as one half of an oscillation-coupled plasticity loop that, alongside [tempo-spatial rhythmic LTP](./ltp-tempo-spatial-rhythmic-memory.md) and [bistable synaptic plasticity](./bistable-synaptic-plasticity.md), underwrites the [memory-prediction](./brain-as-memory-prediction-machine.md) account of cognition and the [NAPOT](./napot-theory.md) phase-tomography frame.
