# Holonomic Brain Theory

The canonical companion pages already in the manifest are [[holographic-brain-model]], [[holographic-brain-distributed-memory]], [[holographic-brain-organization]], and the [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT phase-array tomography]] account that supplies SAN's preferred mechanism.

## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks proper, the position the page name asserts is: holonomic brain theory is *partially right* (the wave/interference substrate is real) and *partially superseded* (the literal Fourier-optical implementation is replaced by a [[napot-tomography-not-holography|phase-array tomographic]] one). SAN preserves the holonomic intuition that perception and memory live in interference patterns over distributed [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|oscillatory]] activity but reframes the implementation: rather than postulating quantum-coherent dendritic microprocesses (the controversial Pribram/Bohm extension), the SAN reading routes the wave dynamics through classical [[dendrites-as-computational-units|dendritic computation]], [[apical-dendrites|apical-dendritic]] integration, and [[graded-neural-firing|graded firing]] regimes that produce holonomic-*looking* phenomenology without requiring a quantum substrate. ## Post-SAN

Compared with Pribram's original holonomic brain theory, the SAN reading makes one structural substitution and one theoretical concession. The substitution is to replace literal Fourier-optical / quantum-coherent dendritic microprocesses with classical [[dendrites-as-computational-units|dendritic computation]] feeding [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|traveling-wave]] phase-array dynamics — i.e. preserve the wave/interference phenomenology while rejecting the quantum-coherence implementation. The concession is that SAN treats holonomic theory as a *correct intuition with the wrong mechanism* rather than as wholly mistaken: this aligns it more closely with [[holographic-brain-distributed-memory|holographic distributed memory]] than with [[orch-or-and-quantum-cognition|orchestrated-objective-reduction]], and it generates the testable prediction that what looks like holonomic recall should track measurable phase-array statistics in EEG/MEG (cf.
