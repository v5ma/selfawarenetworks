Entitativity is the property by which individual components — cells, proteins, or neural units — form a **cohesive functional group perceived and behaving as a single entity** rather than as a mere aggregation of parts. Originally a social psychology concept (describing groups perceived as unified agents), it is applied in SAN theory to explain how distributed cellular and neural processes give rise to unified conscious experience.

## Cellular entitativity

At the cellular level, Jon Lieff (via a0597z) describes entitativity as the outcome of synchronized chemical signaling: cells that coordinate through shared feedback loops, chemical gradients, and coincidence detection begin to function *as one*. White blood cells moving against blood flow via sticky protein molecules exemplify this — individual cells exhibiting coordinated behavior that appears to originate from a single purposeful agent.

## Relation to SAN and NAPOT

Entitativity is the mechanism that bridges [[cellular-oscillating-tomography]] (individual cell oscillations) and [[napot]] (population-level pattern projection). When cells synchronize oscillations into a coherent wave pattern, the array achieves entitativity: the array's output is a unified signal, not a cacophony of independent cell states. This is the micro-level explanation for why neural arrays produce *one* perceived qualia rather than thousands of simultaneous micro-percepts.

## Pan-cellular Hebbian learning as entitativity precursor

Lieff extends Hebbian learning to all cells with receptors (not just neurons): any two cells repeatedly co-active tend to associate. Repeated co-activation drives structural changes (receptor sensitivity, gene expression) that increase a cell group's tendency to fire together — increasing its entitativity over time. This makes entitativity a **learned property**, shaped by experience at the cellular level.

## Implications for consciousness

If entitativity is gradable (cells can be more or less tightly coupled into a functional unit), then consciousness need not be binary. The degree of entitativity across a neural array may correlate with the clarity and coherence of a conscious percept. See [[fractal-consciousness]] for the cross-scale version of this argument.
