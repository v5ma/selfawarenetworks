# Attention, Awareness, and Consciousness

> **Image status:** The earlier figure remains preserved in the failed-medical-review archive and is not approved for publication. It incorrectly drew awareness, attention, and consciousness as a fixed anatomical sequence: alpha/theta thalamocortical background, then gamma/inhibitory cortical-column gating, then phase-locked working-memory access. The source-faithful replacement should show overlapping functional dimensions and a distributed sensory-to-action loop, with established physiology and the SAN hypothesis labeled separately.

Attention, perceptual awareness, conscious access, and phenomenal consciousness are related but not interchangeable. They also do not form one universal three-step pipeline.

## Operational distinctions

- **Attention** is selective prioritization. It can alter gain, competition, sampling, memory, and action without guaranteeing that the selected content is consciously experienced.
- **Cellular sensitivity** is a cell's ability to detect conditions and change its state or behavior. Receptor signaling, homeostasis, and adaptive response are established biology; calling these operations conscious is a separate theoretical claim.
- **Perceptual awareness** is evidence that a subject experienced a stimulus or feature under a declared task, measured through report, confidence, discrimination, or a carefully justified no-report proxy.
- **Conscious access** is the availability of content for flexible report, working memory, reasoning, and control. Access can be studied without claiming that it exhausts phenomenal experience.
- **Self-awareness** involves representing some aspect of the organism, agent, or point of view as self-related. It is not implied by stimulus detection alone.
- **Phenomenal consciousness** is the fact and character of experience itself. It remains the strongest explanatory target and cannot be defined into existence by a behavioral or neural marker.

Experiments have dissociated attention from awareness and perceptual integration from conscious access. These findings rule out a simple hierarchy in which attention always causes awareness and awareness automatically becomes consciousness. They do not imply that the processes never interact.

## Historical SAN capability ladder

A [public SAN source index fixed on November 28, 2022](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/4e0cf1ee0975c01dc57182368497d3f0cd864030/0abstractbump.md) proposed a progression that the earlier Wiki correction had removed:

1. living cells detect and respond to local chemical or physical distinctions;
2. neural systems selectively prioritize some signals and possible actions;
3. sensory distinctions become perceptually available;
4. temporally persistent learned patterns supply context and continuity;
5. contextual learning supports goals, a self-model, and identity;
6. distributed perception, report, and action express the resulting observer-agent.

This is best read as a **capability and agency ladder**, not as one mandatory serial pathway through six anatomical stations. A system can display cellular sensitivity without attention, attention without perceptual awareness, or perceptual awareness without a reflective self-model.

The source used the phrase "conscious without self-awareness" for bodily cells. Established biology supports receptor-mediated sensing, intracellular integration, homeostasis, and contributions from peripheral cells to bodily regulation. It does not establish that a non-neural cell has phenomenal experience. The medically bounded SAN hypothesis is that such local sensitivity may be a minimal, selfless precursor or component of consciousness, while a reportable human bodily experience depends on distributed neural and bodily systems.

## Distributed SAN interpretation

SAN can preserve the capability ladder without turning it into a frequency anatomy. Ongoing activity supplies receiver-relative context and readiness; event-related changes alter particular receivers; recurrent pathways determine which distinctions persist, recruit learned assemblies, and become available to memory, report, or action. The [June 12, 2022 background-awareness note](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/3b5b0dd31c738b9ade6c010400aa5e064b368d0d/a0444z.md) proposed that ongoing oscillatory activity can maintain recently used patterns and likely strategies in a state ready for reactivation. That is a measurable hypothesis about latent context and state-dependent readiness, not evidence for a universal theta consciousness code.

The [November 28, 2022 source on attention and observation](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/4e0cf1ee0975c01dc57182368497d3f0cd864030/b0146yhand.md) separately considered stimulus tracking, selective attention, conscious report, recurrent transmission, inhibition, and movement. Its useful operation is:

**distributed detection and tracking -> selective recruitment or suppression -> recurrent updating -> persistent content -> memory, report, or action -> sensory and bodily consequences that update the next cycle**

This is compatible with the [[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic/phasic]] vocabulary when tonic and phasic describe receiver-relative roles rather than fixed alpha, theta, beta, or gamma departments. Phase alignment, phase separation, inhibition, and recurrent connectivity can affect communication and selection, but no one phase-locking event is sufficient for consciousness.

The stronger SAN claim is that the participating network performs the observing operation through these causal relations. Cells and populations are the internal receivers and transmitters; recurrent updates and their consequences collectively produce observer-action. No extra viewer receives a neural picture. See [[screen-metaphor-no-inner-viewer|No Inner Viewer]] and [[distributed-observer-vs-consciousness-module|Distributed Observer vs. Consciousness Module]].

## Established, proposed, and open

- **Established:** cells sense and respond; attention alters neural processing; attention, awareness, report, and memory can dissociate; recurrent and distributed activity contributes to perception and behavior.
- **Supported interpretation:** ongoing state, selective gain, inhibition, recurrent processing, and downstream availability are interacting dimensions rather than exclusive departments.
- **SAN hypothesis:** receiver-relative tonic context and phasic differences organize a distributed transition from sensitivity and selection to persistent context, self-model, and observer-action.
- **Open question:** which causal organization, if any, is sufficient for phenomenal experience rather than detection, access, report, or adaptive control.
- **Stronger experimental claim:** cellular or subsystem sensitivity constitutes a genuine minimal consciousness rather than a precursor, component, or analogy.

## Evidence burden

A SAN experiment should manipulate attention, perceptual visibility, report requirements, and self-relevance independently. It should specify the represented content, separate report preparation from perception, and compare receiver-relative tonic/phasic predictions with firing-rate, recurrent-state, and connectivity models. A claimed access marker must predict flexible downstream use and survive controls for stimulus strength, confidence, decision, memory, and movement.

Testing the capability ladder requires more than finding one neural correlate. A stronger design would ask whether each added operation produces a distinct causal gain: selection beyond detection, persistent context beyond selection, self-related modeling beyond perceptual access, and flexible observer-action beyond report preparation. Cellular sensing should be measured as cellular physiology; phenomenal consciousness should not be inferred from adaptive response alone.

## Primary anchors

- Watanabe et al. reported an early dissociation between endogenous spatial attention and perceptual awareness in visual masking ([Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, 2012](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2012.00016)).
- Fahrenfort et al. dissociated perceptual integration from conscious access under attentional-blink and masking manipulations ([PNAS, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1617268114)).
- Hatamimajoumerd et al. showed why report and no-report conditions can produce different apparent frontal signatures ([Current Biology, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.07.068)).
- The Cogitate Consortium measured conscious-content representations with fMRI, MEG, and intracranial EEG and found results that challenged strong predictions of both global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories. The study supports testing explicit operations rather than treating one region, rhythm, or theory as settled ([Nature, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1)).

## Replacement figure brief

A medically accurate successor should use parallel, overlapping axes for cellular sensitivity, selective attention, perceptual awareness, conscious access, self-model, and action. It should place these axes inside a recurrent sensory, thalamic, cortical, subcortical, bodily, and motor system rather than arranging them as three frequency-coded boxes. The figure should mark tonic context and phasic change as SAN interpretations, show that report and movement are outputs rather than definitions of experience, and explicitly state that no inner viewer or single consciousness module is proposed.
