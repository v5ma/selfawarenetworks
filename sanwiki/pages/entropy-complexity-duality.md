The **entropy-complexity duality** holds that thermodynamic entropy and information-theoretic complexity are not opposed but co-arising: "entropy is the oscillatory dissipative of complexity, so entropy is increasing over time because oscillation configuration complexity is simultaneously increasing."

## Core claim

Standard thermodynamics treats entropy as the enemy of structure — dissipation destroys order. This framework inverts that picture: entropy increases *because* oscillating patterns compete, combine, and evolve toward ever more complex configurations. Dissipation is not the termination of structure but its generative engine. Each entropy-generating event is also a complexity-generating event when viewed at the appropriate scale.

## Universal natural selection

The duality grounds a sweeping unification: the natural selection of spacetime configurations and the natural selection of biological organisms operate through the same mechanism. Oscillating patterns that survive competitive environments do so by increasing their configurational complexity. This makes evolution a physics principle, not a biology-specific one — atoms, ecosystems, and galaxies are all subject to it.

## Self-organizing oscillators

Within this view, competing oscillating systems (atomic, planetary, biological) combine and elaborate to stay viable in high-entropy environments. The apparent "order from chaos" observed in biological evolution is the same process producing galactic structure — both are oscillatory complexity emerging from dissipative competition.
