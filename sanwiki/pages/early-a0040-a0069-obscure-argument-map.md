## Three recovered mechanism gaps

### 1. Carrier-substitutable relational content

The source band asks whether learned content can remain stable while its neural carriers change.
The measurable question is whether relational timing or geometry predicts stable content across
carrier turnover better than cell identity or mean rate.

### 2. Latent-to-conscious promotion

The source band distinguishes processing that later supports memory from content that was
consciously available at encoding. A valid experiment must measure encoding, later recall,
behavioral access, and report separately.

### 3. Simultaneous observer-encoder dynamics

The source band proposes that a population-state change can be both the consequence detected by
the network and the encoding transmitted into its next recurrent step. This can be tested as a
phase-reset and effective-connectivity model without assuming that phase reset equals
consciousness.
