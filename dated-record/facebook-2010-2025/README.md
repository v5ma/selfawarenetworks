# Micah Blumberg's Facebook posts and comments, 2010–2025: 2026 record

This folder records, as of November 18, 2026, 224 of Micah Blumberg's own Facebook posts and comments in which he stated Self Aware Networks ideas. For each one it gives the date Facebook shows, the exact words and the post's Facebook address.

The purpose is a second, independent timestamp. Facebook dates each post itself. This Git commit, published on GitHub in November 2026, fixes the text and those dates as they stood in 2026, so a later reader can check that none of these posts was written or changed after 2026.

- `posts.json`: every post with its date, text, Facebook address and the SHA-256 of its text.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the SHA-256 of `posts.json` and of this file.
- The same records, with their sources, are on the dated record at https://selfawarenetworks.com/comparison-wiki/priority-index/.
