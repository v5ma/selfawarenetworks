# Multiplex

**Multiplex** is the [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) framing of how the [cortex](cortex.md) can keep multiple items, features, or contextual inputs *simultaneously addressable* by encoding each one at a different phase or in a different frequency band of the [neural-oscillation hierarchy](neural-oscillation-hierarchy.md) -- most canonically as gamma "slots" nested inside a slower theta cycle, with each slot carrying a different item. It threads into [oscillatory binding](oscillatory-binding.md), into the [reference-frame](cortical-columns-reference-frames.md) account of column representation, into the [NAPOT](napot-theory.md) phase-tomography reading of how the slots are read out, and into [MVR](multi-vesicle-release.md) / [multi-vesicle-release spatial-encoding](multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding.md) at the cellular level, where a single neuron can multiplex different inputs across its dendritic tree.

## Position

Within [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) proper, Multiplex asserts that the [cortex](cortex.md)'s oscillation hierarchy *is* a multiplexing scheme -- gamma slots nested in theta, theta slots nested in alpha, etc. -- and that the same multiplexing logic recurs at the cellular level via dendritic compartmentalisation and MVR-driven channel coding (`07san--md`, line 658, source URL: https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md; `b0233y--md`, line 129, source URL: https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0233y.md). Read this way, "binding" is not a mystery but a *time-multiplexing* operation: items co-activated within the same theta cycle but at distinct gamma slots are bound; items at the same gamma slot in different theta cycles are not. The reading bundles with [oscillatory binding](oscillatory-binding.md), the binding mechanism the multiplexing implements; with [the neural-oscillation hierarchy](neural-oscillation-hierarchy.md), the slot scaffold; with [brain rhythms](brain-rhythms.md) / [brain-rhythms-in-cognition](brain-rhythms-in-cognition.md), the operating temporal grid; with [gamma-wave consideration-sandwich](gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich.md) / [theta-oscillation background-scripts](theta-oscillations-background-scripts.md), the gamma-in-theta nesting; with [NAPOT](napot-theory.md), the phase-tomography read-out modality; with [MVR](multi-vesicle-release.md) and [MVR spatial-encoding](multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding.md), the cellular multiplex; with [dendritic compartmentalisation](dendritic-compartmentalization.md), the cellular substrate that lets a single neuron multiplex; with [reference-frames](cortical-columns-reference-frames.md), the spatial counterpart; and with [criterial causation](criterial-causation.md), the threshold mechanism that decides which slot's contents commit. ## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream multiplexing-by-oscillation literature, which usually treats multiplexing as a population-level cortical-rhythm phenomenon, the SAN reading both (a) extends multiplexing *down* to the single-cell dendritic level via [MVR](multi-vesicle-release.md) and [dendritic compartmentalisation](dendritic-compartmentalization.md) (`b0233y--md`, line 129, source URL: https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0233y.md) and (b) embeds multiplexing inside the [NAPOT](napot-theory.md) phase-tomography frame as the read-out grain. The structural prediction this generates is that single-cell-level perturbations, such as clamping [action-potential](action-potential.md) width or reducing dendritic compartmentalisation, should produce multiplexing deficits at the *population* level that mainstream population-only models do not predict; see [multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding](multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding.md), [dendritic-mvr-cognition](dendritic-mvr-cognition.md), [oscillatory-binding](oscillatory-binding.md), [gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich](gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich.md), [theta-oscillations-background-scripts](theta-oscillations-background-scripts.md), and [napot-theory](napot-theory.md). The technological-translation thread at `b0313ySci--md` line 621 (source URL: https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0313ySci.md) anticipates a multiplex-array reading of [BrainGate](braingate.md)-style BCIs.
