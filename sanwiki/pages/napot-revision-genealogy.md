# NAPOT Revision Genealogy

> **Boundary note (2026-07-18):** This genealogy preserves historical revision deltas; it is not a ladder of equally validated mechanisms. Older keyboard, printing-press, anti-spike, inceptive-field, and quantum-biological language must be read through corrected dendrite/axon physiology, scale labels, and current evidence posture. See [[receptive-inceptive-dendritic-projection]], [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries]], and [[phase-language-firewall]].

> [!important] Revision order is genealogy, not PWD rank
> The numbered NAPOT records track source development, not processing stages or architectural
> containment. In the current SAN synthesis, every declared receive-transform-project-update
> relation is PWD-bearing. A [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-Wave Differential]] (PWD) is the typed
> departure a declared receiver registers relative to its declared tonic or reference pattern; it
> may involve excitation, inhibition, a missing expected event, delay, or interactions among these
> effects. NAPOT uses PWD throughout but does not contain, define, or outrank it. First explicit
> naming, later page position, and revision order do not make PWD a NAPOT 3 add-on, a final output,
> or a secondary concept.

Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography (NAPOT) was not written once and merely renamed. Successive versions changed the level of resolution and the problem being addressed: arrays, synapses, inhibition, dynamic subnetworks, multiscale routing, motor consequences, quantum-biological branches, nested agency, sensory reconstruction, and neural manifolds.

## Why the revision history matters

Flattening all NAPOT material into one summary hides the intellectual work. The revision record shows which question each version added, which concepts continued into the modern neuroscience line, and which branches became separate physics or evolution programs.

The revisions are not ten equally validated theories. They are dated changes in a developing model.

## Before and alongside the numbered revisions

The September 2012 [[neuron-shape-information-storage|neuron-as-program proposal]] is an earlier
architectural ancestor. It describes learned morphology as a state-dependent transformation,
structured neuronal output, recurrent expectation-versus-input correction, and cooperative
networks of neural programs. It does not use the terms NAPOT, PWD, NDCA, tomography, or phase-wave
differential, and this genealogy does not backdate those later names into it.

The April 11, 2017 [[brain-networking-protocol|brain-networking protocol]] proposal in Neural Lace
Podcast 1 is a second architectural ancestor. It asks whether the difference between a present
neural pattern and a desired pattern could be calculated and transmitted through a discovered brain
protocol. That operation anticipates later receiver-relative update and write-back language, but the
2017 source does not use the names NAPOT, PWD, or NDCA and is not assigned a revision number.

`b0153y.md`, Git-fixed on June 19, 2022, distinguishes transformed neural slices from a complete
image copied everywhere and develops the neural-oscillatory-tomography wording.

The `b0131y` family supplies an even earlier operation with layered dates. Its October 14, 2021
recording asks how repeated partial descriptions, transformed cortical passage, multisensory
coincidence, recurrent scale influence, and coupled assemblies can jointly constrain a model. A
public-path transcript first appears on June 21, 2022 and was substantially expanded through a
same-day edit sequence. Those edits add tonic context, rare deviations, a Shannon bridge,
location-tolerant distributed reconstruction, and stronger biological and cross-domain hypotheses.
The current route stabilized on August 28. A lower-quality precursor transcript described in donor
notes is not used as immutable custody evidence here because this review did not bind it to an exact
public object.

Together these sources establish a pre-revision reconstruction problem:

```text
partial view
-> receiver-specific transformation
-> recurrent comparison with other views
-> stable-enough current model
```

They are pre-revision genealogy anchors, not retroactive NAPOT stages. The stronger claim that
neural relations implement an identifiable inverse solution remains a hypothesis with its own
measurement and perturbation burden. See
[[tonic-context-rare-deviation-tomographic-language]].

`a0036z.md` is a separate early named-NAPOT bridge. Its June 8 source already contains the
external-to-inner-voice and absent-other-model proposal. The July 29 expansion explicitly names
NAPOT and describes one neural array rendering transformed sensory or conceptual information for
a later array. This belongs before the numbered revisions without being assigned a retroactive
revision number.

The June Cascade sources add another pre-revision operator. [[gh-a0141z|a0141z]] and the source
renamed from `a0332z ctpr.txt` to [[gh-a0159z|a0159z]] describe successive arrays detecting,
transforming, and re-expressing patterns under changing head position and orientation. This helps
explain what the later receive-transform-project loop does internally. It is preserved as
[[cascaded-array-perspective-transformation|Cascaded Array Perspective Transformation]], not given
a retroactive revision number.

Two additional pre-revision ancestors are now linked. [[gh-a0348z|a0348z]], internally dated May
13, 2011, joins partial temporal-spatial sensory contributions, pattern completion, prediction, and
one continuously updated multisensory picture. The `a0337z ctpr.txt` precursor was uploaded June 8,
2022; [[gh-a0337z|a0337z]] was first fixed at its current path on June 12. It describes a local
learned pattern unfolding into a larger distributed phase pattern as different arrays contribute
transformed relations. Neither source receives a retroactive NAPOT revision number.

### The pre-revision ensemble receiver

[[gh-a0136z|a0136z]], first current-path Git-fixed June 11, 2022, uses the near-name **Neural
Array-Projection Tomography** and describes an output population receiving many signals,
transforming them through learned synaptic and dendritic state, and emitting a new population
update. The full phrase **Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography** was added to that file
later; it must not be attributed to its June 2022 version. [[gh-a0137z|a0137z]], internally dated
July 12, 2021 but first Git-fixed in 2022, adds a mesoscopic phase-reset proposal: enough
coordinated excitation or inhibition changes an ensemble's collective timing and therefore its
effect on another ensemble.

This is a substantive bridge from a dendritic receiver to a population receiver. It is not given a
retroactive revision number. Established phase reset and entrainment make the bridge testable, but
do not establish whole-brain awareness, a universal cortical-column oscillator, or the claim that
slower activity is always more powerful.

One non-numbered companion source sits alongside the earliest numbered revisions. [[gh-a0306z|a0306z]],
first Git-fixed on August 23, 2022 in commit `fc922f143b68f3c058e8fdc54ceed7b9d6a54749`, explicitly
names the **Phase Wave Differential** while describing tonic, phasic, high-phasic, and inhibited
states and receiver-side detection followed by re-expression. This is the first targeted Git-history
fixation of the phrase found by the current review. It is not the beginning of all precursor
operations, does not backdate the name into earlier sources, and does not make PWD subordinate to a
numbered NAPOT revision.

## How the revisions assemble into the current mechanism

The revision numbers preserve development history. They are not processing stages, a hierarchy from
secondary to primary, or a ranking from obsolete to correct, and NAPOT 10 does not replace the
operations recorded in NAPOT 1. The current Encyclopedia uses the revisions as a branching
genealogy map:

| Revision | Operation carried forward | Encyclopedia route | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| NAPOT 1 | Records an early named formulation of receiving, transforming, projecting, and recurrently updating a distributed working state | [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT technical mechanism]] and [[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] | Established convergent, divergent, and recurrent physiology supports component operations; the complete SAN reconstruction synthesis remains a hypothesis |
| NAPOT 2 | Resolve transformation at dendritic branches, receptors, axons, boutons, and target synapses rather than treating a neuron as one scalar node | [[receptive-inceptive-dendritic-projection|Receptive and Inceptive Dendritic Projection]], [[action-potential-waveform|Action-Potential Waveform]], and [[multi-vesicular-release|Multi-Vesicular Release]] | Compartment and synapse specificity are established; a fixed symbolic output alphabet is not |
| NAPOT 3 | Makes inhibition, delay, and a missing expected event explicit as PWD types alongside excitation and interactions; it does not originate or contain PWD | [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-Wave Differentials]] and [[neural-path-selection-interneurons-choice|Neural Path Selection, Interneurons, and Choice]] | Inhibition and expectation-sensitive responses are established; SAN's joined, receiver-relative PWD interpretation remains testable without being architecturally optional |
| NAPOT 4 | Select a temporary functional subnetwork from longer-lived anatomy through current input, state, timing, and inhibition | [[functional-connectivity|Functional Connectivity]] and [[neural-tuning|Neural Tuning]] | Dynamic functional coupling is measurable; exact frequency matching is not a universal routing rule |
| NAPOT 5 | Join recurrent absorption and re-expression with memory, prediction, reference frames, and multiscale functional reconfiguration | [[tonic-absorption-generative-reexpression|Tonic Absorption and Generative Re-expression]] and [[reference-frames-cortical-knowledge|Reference Frames and Cortical Knowledge]] | The component processes have separate evidence; their complete NAPOT synthesis is a SAN hypothesis |
| NAPOT 6 | Follow the action-side consequences of a receiver through declared outputs, targets, recurrent dominance, and motor effects | [[inceptive-field|Inceptive Field]], [[embodiment-vision-gamma-consideration-sandwich|Embodiment and Vision]], and [[actions|Actions]] | Axonal and motor routes must be anatomically declared; the inceptive field is SAN functional terminology |
| NAPOT 7 | Explore a molecular and quantum-biological branch involving spin, flavins, cryptochromes, weak fields, and energy metabolism | [[qgtcd|QGTCD]] | Historical branch preserved separately from the core neural mechanism; it has a higher evidentiary burden |
| NAPOT 8 | Describe locally distinct oscillators as one reciprocally coordinated sensor-and-actor system | [[entification|Entification]] and [[field-cell-self-bioelectric-information-entity|The Field-Cell Self]] | Nested functional coordination is measurable; identity and consciousness claims are SAN hypotheses |
| NAPOT 9 | State the three-dimensional sensory-rendering and oscillatory-reconstruction problem explicitly | [[volumetric-predictive-rendering|Volumetric Predictive Rendering]] and [[neural-rendering|Neural Rendering]] | A volumetric percept is ordinary experience; the proposed neural reconstruction operator remains unproved |
| NAPOT 10 | Extend the model into population-state manifolds, NDCA, preserved distinctions, choice, and action | [[neural-phase-trajectories-taylor-series-proposal|Neural Phase Trajectories]], [[nonlinear-differential-continuous-approximation|NDCA]], and [[choice|Choice]] | Population manifolds are established descriptive tools; the joined NDCA and consciousness interpretation is SAN-specific |

This map keeps cellular physiology, circuit dynamics, distributed reconstruction, the stronger
observer claim, and the quantum-biological branch visible without presenting them as one already
validated mechanism.

In the current architecture, every row that receives, transforms, projects, compares, integrates,
remembers, renders, or acts is PWD-bearing. The map records when particular wording and emphases
became explicit; it does not assign PWD to only one row. Architectural rank and empirical support
are separate questions: PWD is core to SAN while each proposed biological realization still carries
its own measurement and perturbation burden.

## Current-perspective and external-tomography bridge

`a0054z.md` supplies a pre-revision receive-transform-project account in which successive
populations construct the perspective currently usable by the organism. `a0069z.md` distinguishes
external array tomography, which reconstructs prepared tissue, from the proposed intrinsic NAPOT
operation performed by living neural arrays. The analogy motivates experiments; it does not make
the mechanisms identical or create an eleventh numbered revision.

## The branches, not just the sequence

- NAPOT 2 and 4 deepen cellular and synaptic resolution.
- NAPOT 3 makes inhibition and below-baseline events explicit in the revision record without originating or containing PWD.
- NAPOT 5 and 6 connect multiscale routing to output, action, and functional reconfiguration; Revision 6's dominance language remains source-specific.
- NAPOT 7 is an outward quantum-physics branch toward QGTCD, not the main modern neuroscience line.
- NAPOT 8-10 broaden the account toward nested agency, reconstruction, manifolds, and action.

This branching structure is more accurate than treating every revision as a linear replacement of the previous revision.

## Date boundaries

Revisions 2-6 are routed by repository changelog and source notes from August through November 2022. NAPOT 7 was developed in the December 2022 branch but entered later Git collections. NAPOT 8-10 were explicitly reassigned around January 2, 2024 in the recovered collection.

Event date, claimed development date, first Git date, publication date, and scientific validation remain different facts.

## Recovered NOAT-to-NAPOT bridge

The June 10, 2022 version of [[gh-a0043z|a0043z]] uses **Neural Oscillating Array Tomography
(NOAT)**. The sentence saying that NOAT became NAPOT was added on December 12, 2023. The source also
compares overlapping anatomical sensor/transmitter arrays with functional edge communities while
noting that functional connectivity is not anatomical connectivity. This preserves a name and
problem-history transition without misdating the later edit, validating neural tomography, or
treating functional and anatomical arrays as identical. The later spacetime and
programmable-matter discussion in the same note belongs to the QGTCD/SIT history branch.

## Why NAPOT 11 is not registered as stable

One collection asks for NAPOT 11, 12, and 13 in eight words and associates them with emotion, choice and social cognition, and BCI. Elsewhere the source explicitly reassigns versions 8-10. The 11-13 labels may belong to a book-cycle numbering scheme rather than a settled scientific revision record.

A stable NAPOT 11 requires at least one of:

1. an authored source explicitly titled as revision 11;
2. a dated transcript defining its delta from revision 10; or
3. a changelog entry joining the number to a specific mechanism.

Until then, candidate material remains preserved in the graph without manufacturing a false sequence.
