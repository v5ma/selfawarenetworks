[Neo Mind Cycle](neo-mind-cycle.md) 2 (NMC2) is an evolved [formulation](gh-formulation.md) of the [Neomindcycle](neomindcycle-legacy-wiki.md) framework,
first articulated around 2012, integrating brain-computer interfaces, immersive
extended-reality environments, and a field-theoretic model of consciousness into a
unified cycle of perception, representation, and action.

## The electromagnetic field metaphor

NMC2 introduces the "lake of electromagnetism" as a model for total cognitive capacity:
the brain's combined electromagnetic field constitutes a shared medium through which
[neural oscillations](neural-oscillations.md) propagate and interfere. Consciousness is pictured as patterns of
constructive interference in this lake rather than as localised neural firings.
This is consistent with [distributed phase representations](distributed-phase-representations.md) and feeds into [NAPOT](napot-overview.md)'s
oscillation-tomography account of perception.

## Multi-scale resolution layers

Neural processing is described as "layers of images scaled to different resolutions" -
a hierarchical but simultaneous rendering of sensory input at subcellular, columnar, and
network scales. The mind cycle iterates across these layers continuously, updating each
resolution level on every oscillatory pass. This multi-resolution cycling is the
mechanism SAN proposes for binding [fractal pattern](fractal-pattern.md) structure across scales.

## BCI and XR integration

NMC2 was conceived alongside [brain-computer interface](brain-computer-interface.md) implementations and extended-reality
hardware. The hypothesis: if the brain already cycles through resolution layers internally,
a sufficiently close BCI + XR rig could insert new resolution layers from outside, blending
synthetic percepts seamlessly into the cycle. This is the theoretical grounding behind
MemPalace as a hardware-agnostic spatial memory extension.

## History

The first-generation neomind cycle focused on the oscillatory rhythm itself. NMC2
added the electromagnetic field metaphor and the multi-resolution layer model, making
the cycle compatible with field theories of consciousness and with XR hardware design.
