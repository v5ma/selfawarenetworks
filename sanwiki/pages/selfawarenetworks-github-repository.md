The [`v5ma/selfawarenetworks` GitHub repository](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks) preserves public source material for the Self Aware Networks research program, including papers, equations, source notes, and historical drafts concerning neurophysics, artificial neurology, and biosynthetic interfacing.

It is a public provenance source for [[gh-resnote04|Micah Blumberg's]] SAN corpus and a dated record of the research program's development. Repository history establishes public custody of particular files and revisions; it does not by itself provide independent scientific validation.

**Key documents in the repository:**

- `QGTCD.md` — quick-reference equations for [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation]] (Quantum Gradient Time Crystal Dilation)
- `SAOv9.md/pdf` - a major Self-Aware Networks synthesis integrating [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] and related frameworks
- `Micah's New Law of Thermodynamics` - thermodynamics preprint ([[micahs-new-law-of-thermodynamics]])
- `a0001z-a0020z+` and related source-note series - developing explorations of core SAN concepts
- `Anchoring Memory` - work bridging molecular mechanisms with neural oscillatory dynamics ([[multi-scale-causality]])
- `Building Sentient Beings` series - a proposed route toward artificial sentient neural networks

The repository addresses [[consciousness-as-high-bandwidth-choice-mechanism]] through oscillation-based frameworks, connecting [[gravity-as-information]], long-term potentiation, and artificial neural network sentience in a unified theoretical structure.

MemPalace and other internal research tools have been used to navigate this corpus, but they are not public evidence sources and are not reader routes in this Encyclopedia.
