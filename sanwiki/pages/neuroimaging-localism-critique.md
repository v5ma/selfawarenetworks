# Neuroimaging Localism Critique

Neuroimaging can test where and when a measured signal changes, but a regional association is not by itself proof that one location contains a psychological function. The defensible alternative is not that localization is meaningless. Primary sensory, motor, and association systems have reproducible anatomical specialization. The problem is the stronger inference that a complex diagnosis, emotion, percept, or decision is implemented by one isolated region.

## What the instruments establish

Functional MRI usually measures blood-oxygen-level-dependent change produced through neural, vascular, and metabolic coupling. EEG and MEG measure electrical or magnetic consequences of population currents after volume conduction and source mixing. Each modality therefore requires a forward model, an inverse inference, and explicit spatial and temporal bounds. A statistical map is an estimate under those transformations, not a direct photograph of a thought.

Large-scale studies also show that many cognitive operations depend on interactions among distributed, partially specialized networks. This does not erase local specialization. It changes the question from "Where is the function?" to "Which local populations, pathways, recurrent interactions, body variables, and measurement transforms are required for this result?"

## SAN interpretation

SAN proposes that perception and observer-action arise from distributed recurrent activity rather than from a localized inner viewer. In this interpretation, tonic population activity supplies an ongoing differentiating context or canvas, while phasic departures supply changing information or "ink." That is a proposed operational interpretation, not something established merely by observing a distributed activation map.

NAPOT therefore predicts relationships among measured signals across space, time, pathway, and behavior. A convincing test must specify the scale being claimed: cellular, local-circuit, cortical-column, whole-network, or organismal. Macro-scale imaging cannot by itself establish the proposed cellular mechanism.

## Evidence boundary

- Established: neuroimaging signals are transformed measurements with modality-specific spatial, temporal, and physiological limits.
- Established: complex cognition commonly recruits distributed networks alongside regional specialization.
- Proposed by SAN: recurrent tonic and phasic dynamics collectively implement a rendered working model and observer-action.
- Not established: that a BOLD cluster, scalp source estimate, or other single macro-scale result identifies a stored concept or proves NAPOT.
