## Position

EDS makes a structural claim about *where the cleverness lives* in a neuron: not in some downstream interpreter that reads spikes off the soma, but in the synaptic + dendritic apparatus itself. The doctrine is repeated with deliberately near-identical wording in three files — "Synapses & dendrites constitute what I term an Expert Data Structure (EDS)" ([`GPT2023X--md`](gh-gpt2023x.md), line 30925, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2023X.md); [`GPT2024Jan1toJan22--md`](gh-gpt2024jan1tojan22.md), line 5397, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2024Jan1toJan22.md); [`Hex_7--md`](gh-hex-7.md), line 62, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/Hex%207.md)) — and with the postsynaptic [dendrite](dendrite.md) explicitly named as the *receiver* whose receptor configuration encodes the structure ([`GPT2023X--md`](gh-gpt2023x.md), line 30925, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2023X.md)).

That commitment makes EDS the SAN-side counterpart of [dendritic computation](dendritic-computation.md) / [dendritic pattern detection](dendritic-pattern-detection.md) / [dendritic-pattern computation](dendritic-computation-pattern-detection.md): the [dendrite](dendrite.md) is not a passive summer but a *pattern-specific specialist*, and its synaptic partners — including the [apical dendrite](apical-dendrite.md) of [cortical-column pyramidals](apical-dendrites.md) — collectively realize the EDS. The same logic carries [criterial causation](criterial-causation.md) and [single-neuron pattern detection](criteria-causation-single-neuron-pattern-detection.md): a neuron does not merely fire above threshold, it *selects which input patterns count*, and the EDS is the substrate through which that selectivity is implemented and re-implemented as the cell learns. The substrate-side mechanisms are the [protein-synthesis-via-firing](neuron-rank-protein-synthesis.md) loop, [CaMKII–microtubule LTP](camkii-microtubule-ltp.md), the [KIBRA-PKMζ anchor](kibra-pkm-zeta-memory-anchor.md), the [multi-vesicle release](multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding.md) / [multivesicular spatial information](multivesicular-release-spatial-information.md) readings, the [ion-channel tone](synaptic-ion-channel-tone.md) of each synapse, and the [reliability vs. specificity tradeoff](synaptic-unreliability.md) that single-vesicle release would otherwise impose.

The generalization in [`a0142z--md`](gh-a0142z.md) makes the concept *not just dendritic*. An oscillator — a small population of synchronized cells, or for that matter a [single oscillating cell](cellular-oscillating-tomography.md) — is *also* an EDS in the same sense, because it has constructed (over time) the very reference rhythm against which it measures inputs. In SAN, this connects EDS to [oscillator-level awareness](oscillator-level-awareness.md) and to the [oscillator consciousness threshold](oscillator-consciousness-threshold.md): an oscillator that has learned its *own paper* is the smallest unit able to recognize patterns *as patterns*. The same architecture is what makes [cells](cells-as-pattern-recognition-devices.md) and [pattern neurons](pattern-neurons.md) effective at the single-cell scale and what makes the [fractal-pattern](fractal-pattern.md) reading of memory persuasive: the EDS motif repeats from synapse-and-[dendrite](dendrite.md) up to oscillator and beyond.

EDS also has a self-content reading in `a0206z--md`. In [`a0142z--md`](gh-a0142z.md) the oscillator is described as creating *its own paper* against which it measures inputs — the EDS is a learned reference template, not a generic data structure. That makes the unit *expert* in a literal sense: it carries a domain-tuned reference, not a domain-neutral container. The downstream consequence is that [memory-prediction](brain-as-memory-prediction-machine.md) and [intelligence-as-memory-prediction](memory-prediction-as-intelligence.md) are realized at the level of the EDS itself, rather than only at the system level: each EDS already does memory-and-prediction inside its own slice of the substrate.

## Post-SAN

EDS is positioned against published [computational neuroscience](computational-neuroscience.md) as an *upgrade of the perceptron picture* rather than its denial. Where the standard perceptron treats a synapse as a scalar weight on an input and the neuron as a threshold function, EDS upgrades both: synapses + dendrites carry a learned *template*, the postsynaptic receptor configuration encodes that template, and the neuron's job is to *match* against the template rather than to sum-and-threshold. This is consistent with Tse-style [criterial causation](criterial-causation.md) research, with the dendritic-computation literature on the input side, and with the [multivesicular](multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding.md) reading on the output side. It also explains why purely scalar-weight artificial neural networks are *underpowered* relative to biological cognition: they lack the EDS's expert-template structure and its [neuronal-shape information storage](neuron-shape-information-storage.md). With no Lane G external papers in this packet, the article does not cite particular papers; future Lane B passes pulling Polsky/London/Häusser-style dendritic-computation work, Branco/Stuart, and Tse's *The Neural Basis of Free Will* into Lane G should populate this section directly.
