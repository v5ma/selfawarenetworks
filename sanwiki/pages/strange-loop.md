## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream Hofstadter scholarship and the broader strange-loop tradition, SAN takes three positions. First, it accepts Hofstadter's diagnosis: that the self is a [self](gh-a0206z.md)-referential loop, not a separate substance. Second, it specifically supplies the *neurological* implementation Hofstadter omitted (`a0029z--md`, line 3, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md)) — render-and-detect alternation across [cortical-columns](cortical-columns.md), realized through [phase-wave-differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md) and [feedback-loops](consciousness-as-oscillatory-feedback-loop.md) in the [thalamocortical](thalamus.md) circuit.
