## Core description

The vortex is not a single frequency but a symphonic superposition of brainwave
rhythms. Its role is synchronization: as the brain encounters new stimuli and
forms new synaptic associations, the vortex carries those associations through
the existing oscillatory graph, coupling them to prior learned patterns. This is
the mechanism by which Hebbian-coincidence learning becomes network-wide
knowledge rather than isolated synaptic weight changes.

The image is intentionally dynamical: a **vortex** sweeps through the network,
an **[[attractor]]** stabilizes activity toward coherent states. Together they
describe the same phenomenon — brainwave dynamics that draw disparate learned
patterns into synchronized relationship.

## Relationship to SAN core claims

This [[attractor]] is the substrate for [[neural-oscillatory-synchronization]] and
is what [[napot]] identifies as the pattern-completion engine: incoming sensory
data is "sucked into" the [[attractor]] basin where it merges with prior memory
configurations. The resulting percept is always a combination of new input
and old learned pattern.

Related: [[cross-scale-oscillation-communication]] — the vortex operates across
multiple brainwave frequency bands simultaneously (gamma, beta, alpha, theta),
with each scale amplifying or filtering the patterns flowing through it.

## Hard problem of hidden emergent properties

The same note introduces a framing relevant to SAN: consciousness may be just
one example of a broader class of **hidden emergent properties** — phenomena
arising from particular configurations of matter that are not directly observable
by third parties. The brainwave vortex [[attractor]] is the physical correlate of
consciousness; whether other configurations of matter have analogous hidden
emergent properties remains an open question in SAN philosophy. See [[emergent-properties-consciousness]].
