This note sketches oscillatory Expert Data Structures as a working idea. In this framing, the morphology of synaptic patterns on the [dendrite](dendrite.md) and precise exosome or vesicle releases may contribute to signal patterns, including possible RNA or DNA message carrying in some cases.

Nested oscillating Expert Data Structures are described here as systems that could learn patterns invariant to scale, time, space, and phase, with punctuated phase changes. The related cortical-column idea is that neurons in an oscillator fire in turn rather than all at once.

# Zero-Shot Text-Guided Object Generation with Dream Fields
When I close my eyes and say the word tomato, I see a tomato, see red, and remember properties such as taste. Zero-Shot Text-Guided Object Generation with Dream Fields is used here as an analogy for how Neural Oscillatory Tomography or Neural-Array Projection Tomography might render both visual and sensory properties in the brain.

# "Identifiability analysis and noninvasive online estimation of the first-order neural activation dynamics in the brain with transcranial magnetic stimulation"
This source supports the importance of dendritic morphology for memory and neural firing. Its abstract notes that neural activation dynamics are influenced by neuron type, morphology, ion-channel expression, and other factors.

# "Switches to rhythmic brain activity lead to a plasticity-induced reset in synaptic weights"
"Synaptic connections are functionally strengthened or weakened to form new memories through synaptic plasticity rules that strongly rely on neuronal rhythmic activities. Brain information processing, on the other hand, is shaped by fluctuations in these neuronal rhythmic activities, each defining distinctive brain states, which poses the question of how such fluctuations in brain states affect the outcome of memory formation."
"By combining computational models of neuronal activity switches and plasticity rules, we show that switches to rhythmic brain activity reminiscent of sleep lead to a reset in synaptic weights towards a basal value."
The cited preprint supports a narrower claim: sleep-like rhythmic activity can reset modeled synaptic weights toward a basal value.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.07.15.500198v1
