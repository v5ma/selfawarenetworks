# SAN Manuscript Series Genealogy: 01san Through 13san

The files `01san.md` through `13san.md` are a 2024-2025 research notebook series, not thirteen
finished papers and not one continuous manuscript. Together they preserve theory framing,
Micah-to-AI dialogues, literature comparisons, evidence inventories, worked neural-rendering
examples, correspondence, and philosophy-of-computation arguments.

This page identifies what each file contributes, who is speaking, where it belongs in the SAN
graph, and what must be checked before reuse. A source can be historically and conceptually
important without every scientific statement in it being established.

## The main branches

### Integrative and outward theory

`01san` and `13san` ask how SAN relates to larger theories of organization, computation, and
consciousness. They are useful scope and comparison donors. Their strongest claims require separate
logical or scientific review before a paper presents them as conclusions.

### Conversational mechanism development

`02san` and `03san` preserve Micah refining concepts with a model in dialogue. The Micah turns are
primary evidence of Micah's formulation. The model's replies are interpretation, suggested
formalization, or critique. They must never be merged into one anonymous authorial voice.

### Literature comparison and evidence intake

`04san` through `07san` connect SAN to predictive processing, traveling waves, sharp-wave ripples,
cellular cognition, and a broad bibliography. They are discovery and comparison tools. A cited
paper remains the authority for its own result, and every citation or paraphrase must be verified
before publication.

### The perceptual-screen sequence

`08san` through `12san` form a focused mechanism cluster. The sequence moves from a broad report to
a revised report, then to a concrete geometric example, a public technical exchange, and a private
revision note. Across the cluster, the recurring question is:

> How can distributed neural tissue construct and update a coherent percept without a separate
> viewer inside the brain?

The controlling relationship is `08san -> 09san`: `09san` is not an independent confirmation. It
is a shorter successor that incorporates additional Micah-provided synthesis and more explicit SAN
vocabulary.

## Authorship and evidence layers

| Layer | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Micah-authored statement or dialogue turn | Micah's formulation and its recoverable date | Biological truth or external priority |
| AI response or synthesized report | A structured interpretation, candidate formalization, or critique | Independent evidence, authorship by Micah, or validation |
| External quotation or paper summary | A route to a relevant external result | Accuracy until checked against the primary source |
| Private correspondence | Development history and a potential manuscript donor | Public provenance or permission to disclose recipient details |
| Public social exchange | Public discussion and an argument context | Correctness of every claim or influence on another author |
