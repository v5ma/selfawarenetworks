# Criterial Thresholds

**Criterial Thresholds** are the concrete, dynamically resettable firing and integration thresholds at which a neuron's [[criteria]] for spiking are met — the cellular variables that the [[criterial-causation|criterial causation]] framework of Peter Tse (and [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]]) treats as the unit of [[choice]]. Criterial thresholds are the place where [[criterial-causation|criterial causation]] actually lives. SAN's reading of Peter Tse's *The Neural Basis of Free Will: [[criterial-causation|Criterial Causation]]* (MIT Press, 2013) holds that a neuron does not change its own decision once a threshold is crossed; instead, "its activity can alter the [[synaptic-weight|synaptic weights]]" — and therefore the *thresholds* — of its targets. In SAN's [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] / [[cot-cellular-oscillating-tomography|Cellular Oscillating Tomography]] picture, criterial thresholds are realised in [[NMDA-receptor|NMDA]] / [[chemical-synapse|AMPA]] receptor sensitivities, [[membrane-potential|membrane potential]] offsets, [[action-potential|action-potential]] firing voltages, [[dendritic-spike|dendritic-spike]] initiation thresholds, and the [[GABA]] / [[acetylcholine]] / [[dopamine]] modulation that biases each cell's [[coincidence-detection|coincidence-detection]] window — collectively, the parameters that decide whether a [[phasic-firing|phasic]] burst rides a [[tonic oscillation|tonic oscillation]].

Mechanically, criterial thresholds are the cell-level handle SAN points to when discussing [[neuroplasticity]], [[Long-Term-Potentiation|long-term potentiation]], [[long-term-depression|long-term depression]], [[Hebbian-Learning|Hebbian learning]], [[Engrams|engram]] consolidation, and [[memory-consolidation|memory consolidation]]: each of these is a slow re-tuning of the threshold on which subsequent criterial decisions ride. They are also the variables that [[brain-computer-interfaces|brain–computer interfaces]] like [[BrainGate]] and [[Neuralink]], plus [[Neurofeedback]], [[bioelectronics]], [[optogenetics]], [[nootropics]], and [[brain-optimization|brain-optimization]] regimes, ultimately push on. At the network scale, criterial thresholds in [[inhibitory-interneurons]] and [[Cortical-columns|cortical columns]] propagate up into the [[cortical-traveling-waves|traveling-wave]] / [[brain-rhythms|brain-rhythm]] structure that [[oscillatory-tomography|oscillatory tomography]] reads as [[3d-semantic-segmentation|3D semantic segmentation]] of perception.
