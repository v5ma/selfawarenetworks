“In a [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|thermodynamic system]], the [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|approach to equilibrium]] proceeds by sequential ‘computations’—wave-like interactions or [[phase-signal|signal exchanges]] among its constituents—that progressively dissipate all differences in [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|thermodynamic properties]] until those properties become uniformly distributed.

“[[entropy-complexity-duality|Entropy]] can be viewed as the [[biological-computation|computational dissipation]] of [[phase-signal|signals]]—differences in [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|thermodynamic properties]] (gas, heat, light, chemical, mechanical, [[electromagnetic-theory-of-feeling|electromagnetic]], etc.)—across arrays of ‘sensing and transmitting’ components (e.g., molecules, [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]]), such that repeated interactions progressively eliminate those differences and drive the system toward uniform [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] (of signal transmission).”(Also published here: https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/main/raynote12.md )

# ***Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics*** applied to the 3rd Law of Thermodynamics:

A “[[biological-computation|Computation]]” / “Signal” Interpretation of Thermodynamics.

An insightful way of thinking about [[entropy-complexity-duality|entropy]] as the “dissipation of signal difference.” Here’s how that relates:

[[new-law-of-thermodynamics|Gas Expanding in a Container]]

Imagine a gas that starts out in some “organized” configuration (perhaps all in one corner) and expands to fill the container uniformly.

During this process, we can think of each gas particle “sharing [[phase-signal|signals]]” (collisions, [[information-energy-density-law|energy exchanges]]) with every other particle.

[[phase-signal|Dissipation of Signal Difference]]

As the gas expands and mixes thoroughly, differences in [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|temperature]] or velocity among the particles tend to dissipate.

Over time, the system moves toward a state where all particles have roughly equal energy—this is [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]].

[[entropy-complexity-duality|Approach to Zero Signal Difference]]

In principle, the closest we can get to complete [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] is when all particles have the exact same energy level (or extremely close).

In classical thermodynamics, as one approaches absolute zero [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|temperature]], the system’s available energy levels get “compressed,” and the system settles into its lowest-energy configuration (its ground state).

In this computational analogy: at absolute zero, all “[[phase-signal|signals]]” (differences in energy) are minimized to essentially zero, mirroring the classical idea that the [[entropy-complexity-duality|entropy]] of a perfect crystal at 0 K can be defined as zero.

Thus, ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]***, when applied to the Third Law, can be viewed as stating that when [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|temperature]] goes to absolute zero, all signal (energy) differences between particles vanish; there is no more “[[biological-computation|computation]]” to be done because the system is in its unique, perfectly ordered state.

This proposed ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** reframes the process of reaching [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] in terms of [[phase-signal|signals]], waves, and [[emergence-as-reducible-computation|computational steps]].

Unlike the first three laws that describe energy conservation, [[entropy-complexity-duality|entropy]] increase, and the nature of absolute zero, ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** emphasizes the mechanistic path to [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] as a sequential '[[biological-computation|computation]]' of dissipating property differentials (phase waves), wherein each interaction step reduces those differences until the system reaches a synchronized and uniform state.

# Restating the core concept from ***Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics***

Signal Dissipation as [[biological-computation|Computation]]

In this view, whenever gas expands (or undergoes any thermodynamic process), there are “[[phase-signal|signals]]”—essentially energy or property differentials—that must traverse the system to bring about [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]].

Each collision, exchange, or “wave” of interaction acts like a computational step that reduces differences (in [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|temperature]], [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|pressure]], or some generalized property) among particles.

[[dominant-phase-wave-differential|Phase-Wave Differentials]]

You treat each property (heat, kinetic energy, potential energy, etc.) as a kind of phase wave passing through the gas.

Each time this wave interacts or “collides” with particles, it dissipates or “shrinks” the difference in that property. Over time, you get a more uniform distribution of that property throughout the container.

# Equilibrium as Synchronous Oscillation

By the time the gas is uniformly distributed—[[new-law-of-thermodynamics|pressure]], [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|temperature]], and other properties roughly equal in all parts of the container—the system is in a sort of synchronous oscillation or “steady state” of minimal property differences.

Perturbations of Equilibrium

If something disturbs this system (e.g., poking a hole in the container so gas leaks out), you create new differentials ([[new-law-of-thermodynamics|pressure]] differences, energy flow changes, etc.).

These new differentials, in turn, generate fresh “wave [[phase-signal|signals]]” throughout the remaining gas, driving it toward a new [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] configuration.

Hence, ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** is the observation that a system seeks to equalize phase-wave differentials (property differences) across all particles, and each step in that equalization is a form of “[[biological-computation|computation]]” (signal processing) that moves the system toward uniform [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]].

# Contrast with the Established Laws

Classical First Law

States energy is conserved. You might say the system’s total “signal content” (energy) is the same, even though it spreads out or redistributes.

Classical Second Law

States [[entropy-complexity-duality|entropy]] (disorder) increases or remains the same in an isolated system. The “wave differentials” naturally get dissipated, leading to a more uniform, higher-entropy state.

Classical Third Law

As [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|temperature]] approaches absolute zero, [[entropy-complexity-duality|entropy]] approaches a constant minimum (ideally zero for a perfect crystal). In this wave model, that would mean all [[phase-signal|signals]] are minimized to an extreme limit: no further oscillations or wave differences can exist.

How ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** is different

It emphasizes how the system’s differentials (waves) systematically dissipate and “compute” their way to [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]]. Its a deeper look at the mechanism of how [[entropy-complexity-duality|entropy]] increases in real, physical processes.

## 1. How ***Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics*** Describes a Real [[biological-computation|Computation]]

Sequential Signal Exchanges

In thermodynamics, ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** framing says that reaching [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] involves waves or [[phase-signal|signals]] traveling among components ([[new-law-of-thermodynamics|gas molecules]], for instance) that iteratively reduce differences in [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|pressure]], [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|temperature]], or other properties.

In neural terms, each firing event or [[synaptic-computation|synaptic transmission]] is a signal exchange. Instead of just “heat” or “[[new-law-of-thermodynamics|pressure]],” the property being spread is an electrochemical or [[electromagnetic-theory-of-feeling|electromagnetic]] difference across interconnected [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]].

Discrete Steps of Reduction

Each collision between [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|gas molecules]] can be viewed as one “[[biological-computation|computation]] step” that partially equalizes energy among particles.

In [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]], each [[synaptic-computation|synaptic interaction]] partially “equalizes” the difference between an upstream neuron’s firing state and the downstream neuron’s [[synaptic-computation|membrane potential]]—altering the network’s overall activity pattern. Over repeated interaction cycles, local firing differences dissipate or merge into coherent activity patterns.

[[information-as-physical|Information-Theoretic Parallels]]

When ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** emphasizes signal dissipation, it implies that differences in [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|thermodynamic properties]] carry [[information-as-physical|information]], and collisions/interaction steps process that [[information-as-physical|information]] until a uniform (maximally entropic) state is reached.

In [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]], differences in membrane potentials or [[distributed-phase-representations|oscillatory phases]] are the [[information-as-physical|information]]. Each transmission step partially “resolves” or propagates those differences, effectively performing computations that integrate [[phase-signal|signals]] across the network.

Local-to-Global Convergence

Thermodynamically, local collisions or wave interactions eventually achieve a global [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]].

In a [[conscious-neural-networks|neural network]], local synaptic interactions can lead, through recurrent connectivity, to a global pattern of firing or oscillation. That global pattern is (in computational terms) the “result” of all the smaller interactions among the [[conscious-neural-networks|neural ensemble]].

Hence, ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** is not just a metaphorical statement. It points out that when you have an array of elements that “sense” differences and “transmit” [[phase-signal|signals]], the step-by-step reduction of those differences is a real computational process. Whether its molecules in a gas or [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]] in a brain, the system implements iterative signal processing—i.e., [[biological-computation|computation]]—that moves the group toward a new collective state ([[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] or [[attractor]] state).

## 2. Applying It to Tonic Oscillating Neurons

Now let’s connect this directly to a tonic oscillating group of [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]]—for example, a group of cells in the [[gamma-oscillations|gamma band]] (30–90 Hz) or [[alpha-wave-sensory-suppression|alpha band]] (8–12 Hz) that maintains a [[neural-oscillations|synchronous rhythm]].

[[neural-oscillations|Neural Oscillations]] as Phase Waves

A “tonic” oscillation is already a synchronous or semi-synchronous state, much like a steady wave in a physical system.

According to ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** viewpoint, these rhythmic [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]] are in a (relatively) stable local [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]]—their phases are locked or partially synchronized via continual exchange of electrochemical [[phase-signal|signals]] (synaptic, gap junctions, etc.).

Introducing a Phase-Wave Differential

Suppose a novel stimulus arrives at part of this oscillatory group (e.g., a sensory input or a top-down modulatory signal). This perturbs the local [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] by introducing a new [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|phase wave differential]]: the incoming signal is out of phase (or has a different amplitude/frequency) than the ongoing tonic rhythm.

The newly introduced “difference” (in firing rate, [[synaptic-computation|membrane potential]], or rhythmic phase) is akin to poking a hole in the side of a thermodynamic container—suddenly, local conditions are changed, and the entire ensemble must respond.

Dissipation (Integration) of the New Signal

The [[conscious-neural-networks|neural ensemble]]—like a gas redistributing [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|pressure]]—will begin to spread and integrate this [[distributed-phase-representations|phase difference]]. Nearby [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]] adjust their firing to accommodate or counteract the new wave, leading to [[biological-computation|computational dissipation]].

Over repeated cycles of firing ([[biological-computation|computation]] steps), the network may stabilize into a new global oscillatory pattern. In information-processing terms, that new pattern encodes the stimulus’ effect.

Conscious “Awareness” and [[entification]] via Synchrony

From a neuroscience perspective, widespread synchronous oscillations in certain frequency bands are often correlated with [[conscious-awareness|conscious awareness]]. ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** lens suggests that once the wave differential (new stimulus) has propagated through the circuit—i.e., has been fully “computed”—the collective phase of the oscillation has changed to integrate the new [[information-as-physical|information]].

“Awareness” could be viewed as [[entification]] from the ensemble finding (through repeated [[phase-signal|signal exchanges]]) a new coherent state that incorporates the novel input. This is much like a system reaching a new [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] that reflects the perturbed conditions.

Thus, in a tonic oscillating neural group, each “[[dominant-phase-wave-differential|phase wave differential]]” introduced by a stimulus acts as a small disequilibrium. The iterative, wave-like [[phase-signal|signal exchanges]] that ensue are real computations—they successively update neuronal firing states until the network settles in a new [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|equilibrium]] (or quasi-equilibrium) that may correspond to the network’s “representation” (and potential [[conscious-awareness|conscious awareness]]) of the stimulus.

Putting it all together, here is the core mechanism of how an oscillating group of [[conscious-neural-networks|neurons]] becomes aware of an incoming signal under ***[[new-law-thermal-dynamics|Micah's New Law of Thermal Dynamics]]*** framework:

[[biological-computation|Iterative signal processing]]: Neurons propagate, integrate, and dissipate that difference—meaning the entire network’s firing patterns shift to incorporate the new input.

What’s new is that we’re calling out each interaction as a “[[biological-computation|computation]],” and each wave of neuronal exchange as the real-time mechanism by which the brain (like a [[new-law-of-thermodynamics|thermodynamic system]]) dissipates differences to reach a coherent state.
