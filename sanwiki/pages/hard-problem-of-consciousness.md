It visually presents oscillatory binding, phase coherence, multimodal rendering, and entification as a completed bridge across the explanatory gap. None of those mechanisms is established as sufficient for phenomenal experience.

The **hard problem of consciousness** asks why and how physical or computational activity has subjective character: why there is something it is like to see a color, feel pain, or occupy a body. It is different from explaining discrimination, report, integration, memory, attention, or action, although those functions constrain any biological theory.

## What neuroscience can establish

Experiments can identify neural states that covary with reported content, decode distinctions, test causal necessity, and compare theories. They can also separate perception from report, attention, confidence, and motor preparation. These results progressively narrow the mechanism.

They do not by themselves prove that a decoded or causally necessary state is identical to experience. That is the transition a theory must argue rather than assume.

## The SAN proposal

SAN proposes that the explanatory target is a distributed observer-action process, not a representation delivered to a hidden subject. Receptors and cells register changes; learned morphology and network state select consequential combinations; recurrent pathways maintain a body-world model; and the system's later sampling, report, autonomic response, and action are changed. On this account, the observing is performed by the network itself.

NAPOT, tonic/phasic differentiation, GCS, NDCA, and entification are candidate components of that account. Calling their aggregate a **render** does not close the hard problem. The stronger identity claim - that a particular distributed causal organization is phenomenal experience - must be stated separately from the functional mechanism and tested against rival accounts.

## Four claims that must not be collapsed

1. **Representation:** a neural state varies with a content.
2. **Decoding:** an investigator can recover that content from measurements.
3. **Causal use:** the organism uses the state in flexible control.
4. **Phenomenal identity:** that organized activity is the experience itself.

SAN gains explanatory force by making each transition explicit. Failure to decode is not proof of absence; successful decoding is not proof of identity; and a correlation with oscillation or recurrence is not proof of sufficiency.

## Research burden

For each proposed mechanism, define a content, scale, receiver, causal route, alternative explanation, selective perturbation, expected loss, and rescue. Compare SAN predictions prospectively with recurrent processing, global workspace, higher-order, predictive, and integrated-information accounts.

The 2025 COGITATE adversarial study is an important model: it preregistered divergent predictions and found observations that both supported and challenged leading theories rather than declaring a winner or a completed solution ([Nature, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1)).
