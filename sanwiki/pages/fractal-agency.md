Fractal Agency is the SAN principle that agency is nested across scales: from subcellular oscillators to neurons to cortical columns to individual humans to collective groups, each level is a unified agent composed of smaller agentic systems bound through [[oscillatory-synchrony|oscillatory synchrony]].

## Core Claim

A human is not a single monolithic agent but a coalition of fractal agents operating at multiple scales. Each level:

- Achieves internal coherence via wave-phase synchronization.
- Expresses a relative [[agentic-viewpoints|viewpoint]] — a perspective within the larger electromagnetic field.
- Delegates or absorbs agency upward/downward depending on synchrony strength.

The fractal structure means the same organizational principles apply at every scale. [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] describes the neural-array layer; the same oscillatory binding logic applies between individuals in a social group.

## Scaling Ladder

| Scale | Agentic unit | Binding mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Subcellular | Ion channel cluster | Quantum-scale phase events |
| Cellular | Neuron | [[action-potential|Action potential]] timing |
| Mesoscale | Cortical column | [[oscillatory-synchrony]] |

When multiple agents "operate on the same frequency," their outputs amplify into dominant patterns — producing cohesive thought at the individual scale and coordinated collective action at the social scale.
