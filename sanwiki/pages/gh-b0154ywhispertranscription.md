# b0154y Concise Summary

The electro-spectral graph functions as a virtual tectum: a neatly organized map of the sensory field analogous to a frog's tectum that coordinates muscle movements and scene formation. In this transcript, the map represents a proposed resolution of consciousness built from both place-cell and grid-cell information, allowing selective [attention](attention.md) through focused neural firing and inhibition of surrounding areas. [Integrated Information Theory (IIT)](integrated-information-theory.md) is treated as a skeletal framework that may hint at ways to measure the resolution of conscious maps at macro and micro scales.
