## A Sensor Does More Than Record

The first problem is easy to miss. Light does not arrive in the brain as a finished picture.
Photoreceptors change biochemical and electrical state, retinal circuits compare signals across
space and time, and ganglion cells send transformed outputs onward. Experiments show that distinct
inhibitory retinal pathways can jointly construct receptive-field surround computations at
different spatial scales.

The same general job recurs after the retina:

```text
receive a structured pattern
-> change it according to local circuitry, prior state, and context
-> project a new structured pattern
```

The 2021 "fractal eyeball" recording uses the eye to make this recurrence visible. Neural arrays
receive structured views of other neural arrays. Dendritic, synaptic, excitatory, and inhibitory
state changes what each array passes onward. The entorhinal-hippocampal system then contributes
scene, location, object, temporal-context, and memory relations. Human measurements show that
entorhinal and CA1 activity patterns can be re-expressed when temporal context is remembered.

The source family becomes more explicit in `a0282z.md`, publicly expanded on August 17, 2022.
It describes one array of receivers presenting a changed pattern to another array, then assigns
capture-like, transport-like, and projection-like roles to dendrites, axons, and terminals. The
same passage immediately says that literal light is not traveling through the brain. This is the
public bridge between the 2021 eye image and the later cell-and-array receive-transform-project
architecture.

SAN extends these established components with a cross-scale hypothesis: receive-transform-project
organization is functionally self-similar across cells, circuits, arrays, and larger loops, and that
recurrence helps implement distributed neural rendering and NAPOT. This is stronger than saying
only that the hippocampus supports memory.

The comparison stops before anatomical identity. The lens, retina, hippocampus, thalamus, and
Layer 6 are not assigned one-to-one equivalence. "Fractal" names recurring functional organization
unless a formal scaling law is separately demonstrated. "Functional isomorphism" requires an
explicit mapping of the relations that are preserved.

## One Cell Has More Than One Consequential Output State

An all-or-nothing spike abstraction is useful for some models, but it hides part of the biological
problem. A neuron receives input at different dendritic locations and times while its channels,
membrane voltage, inhibition, recent activity, and synapses are already in a changing state.

In plain language:

```text
the same nominal input
-> reaches a different cellular state or dendritic location
-> produces a different local transformation
-> changes spike timing, burst structure, waveform, release, or downstream recruitment
```

State-dependent dendritic experiments show that synchronous clustered and asynchronous distributed
input can engage different integration modes. Other experiments show that cortical action-potential
waveform carries information about recent conductance history, and that presynaptic waveform can
change calcium-current and transmitter-release timing.

The four-level LED recording is an early attempt to teach that output is not one timeless bit.
The useful operation survives even though the cell is not a literal LED and does not emit one
universal four-level scalar.

SAN extends this operation through Neural Tuning, the Action Potential Magnitude Effect, and PWD:
a learned receiver state transforms an arriving pattern into a receiver- and route-dependent
consequence. The hypothesis must identify the measured waveform variables, terminal, receiver,
time window, uncertainty, and downstream effect. It does not claim that spike height plus duration
is a universally conserved quantity or deterministic vesicle count.

Research routes:

- [State-dependent dendritic computation in hippocampal CA1 pyramidal neurons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16481442/)
- [Stimulus history reliably shapes action potential waveforms of cortical neurons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15944394/)
- [Timing constraints of action-potential-evoked calcium current and transmitter release](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30872753/)

## Bioelectric Computation Is Built From Maintained Differences

Cells spend energy maintaining unequal ion concentrations across membranes. Channels, pumps,
receptors, water, proteins, and membrane geometry turn those differences into changing voltage,
current, chemical state, and signaling. Nervous systems build computation from these living
gradients rather than from abstract electrical symbols alone.

The "metal robots" recording uses sodium, potassium, calcium, and magnesium to make this physical
dependence memorable. The complete operation also includes chloride, protons, metabolic chemistry,
and glial regulation. Tissue carries ionic current; it is not a metal wire.

The same source compares neural networks with mitochondria. Its recoverable point is organizational:

```text
the organism supplies metabolism, circulation, protection, sensors, and effectors
-> the neural and glial subsystem supplies adaptive sensing, regulation, learning, prediction, and action
-> body and network become mutually dependent
```

Mitochondria are descendants of an endosymbiotic lineage and are now integrated organelles.
Neurons and glia are organismal cells rather than foreign endosymbionts. That boundary does not
erase the functional-symbiosis comparison: the analogy concerns evolutionary integration and
reciprocal benefit across organizational levels.

SAN extends the operation by asking how ion-gradient-dependent cellular state, receptor learning,
oscillation, and intercellular coordination scale into COT and Super Evolution Theory. That joined
extension remains a hypothesis requiring measurable state variables, inheritance or learning rules,
perturbations, and cross-scale predictions.

Research route:

- [Mitochondrial genome evolution and the origin of eukaryotes](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10690412/)
- [The intrinsic electrophysiological properties of mammalian neurons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3059497/)

## A Prospective Scene Can Guide The Next Action

Rank 249 begins with an ordinary planning event. A destination, route, object, time, and expected
future condition are assembled from partial memory. The current environment either confirms the
scene or surprises the planner, and the mismatch changes the next plan.

```text
partial memory and current goal
-> sparse possible scene
-> expected route, object, and event
-> observation and mismatch
-> revised scene
-> selected action
```

Research on episodic future thinking connects imagined future episodes with decision making,
prospective memory, emotion regulation, spatial navigation, and planning. The recording then
externalizes the operation in software: objects and state changes become time-indexed 3D events
that can be replayed, scrubbed, compared, or edited.

SAN extends this into prospective neural rendering. A remembered relation is not merely replayed;
it contributes to a possible scene that can be compared with current input and used to choose an
action. This provides a bridge from memory to prediction to movement.

The comparison stops before literal file storage. The recording is an autobiographical example,
not a controlled experiment, and the brain is not asserted to store scene files, animation arrays,
or one representational format.

Research route:

- [Episodic Future Thinking: Mechanisms and Functions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29130061/)

## Rendering Is Selective And Perspective-Bound

An XR system cannot spend equal resources rendering every possible viewpoint at maximum detail.
It predicts and updates the view that can presently affect the user's perception and action.
Moving the head or body produces a sequence of new samples.

That engineering history helps explain a later SAN question:

```text
current body, gaze, task, and expectation
-> selective sensory sampling
-> context-sensitive reconstruction
-> action
-> changed viewpoint and next sample
```

The internal-AR recording adds the read/write direction. If an interface can reproduce a suitable
neural pattern, a perceptual addition need not appear on external glasses. Closed-loop BCI research
already demonstrates bounded cases in which patterned cortical stimulation contributes artificial
sensory feedback and task learning.

SAN and NerveGear extend those components toward multimodal, pattern-specific, adaptive neural
read/write. The extension requires explicit forward and inverse models, spatial and temporal
precision, uncertainty, held-out tests, reversibility, consent, local disable controls, and
behaviorally meaningful feedback.

The comparison stops before claiming that the brain runs a commercial graphics engine or that
current interfaces can synthesize a complete arbitrary world. Selective rendering is an operational
comparison. Full internal AR remains an engineering hypothesis.

- [Distributed cortical feedback facilitates brain-machine-interface learning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37738340/)

## A Successor Can Continue A Project Without Settling Identity

A future system might preserve a person's records, goals, memories, preferences, or behavioral
patterns. That would establish some forms of informational and project continuity. It would not
by itself answer whether the original first-person subject continues.

The practical questions are separate:

- What information and behavior were preserved?
- Is there causal and bodily continuity?
- Did the source person consent?
- Can the successor be modified, copied, represented, or terminated?
- How does a successor diverge through new embodiment and experience?

Artificial Neurology preserves this distinction. Functional success, behavioral resemblance,
memory continuity, consciousness, and numerical identity require different evidence. "Resurrection"
is not used as a scientific conclusion merely because a successor can continue an agenda.

## Performance Depends On Person, Task, Body, And Environment

The performance discussion resists a simple moral label. Persistence can change with the task,
expected value, learned strategy, sleep, stress, mood, health, nutrition, social support, and
material conditions.

This does not make every health explanation correct. It means that "grit" is an outcome description
until the relevant state, task, and environment are specified. SAN emotion and agency models can
use that contextual operation without diagnosing a person or importing condition-specific medical
claims from an informal multi-speaker discussion.

The book, Wiki, visual atlas, WebXR environment, physical institute, and distributed collaborators
perform different jobs:

- the book creates a coherent path through the argument;
- the SAN Wiki preserves depth and connected mechanisms;

capability can escape accountability, while operational release can increase misuse. Responsible
disclosure can publish concepts, evidence, evaluations, and safety methods while separately

## What T16 Does Not Establish

T16 does not establish:

- a strict mathematical fractal or one-to-one eye-brain anatomy;
- a universal scalar neural-output code;
- arbitrary cellular nanomachines, organ redesign, full internal AR, or immortality;
- a literal wire-like metal conductor inside neural tissue;
- neural endosymbiotic ancestry;
- one medical cause of persistence, attention, or learning performance;
- mind uploading, identity transfer, or resurrection;
- sentient robots or a sufficient machine-consciousness test;
- a biological implementation of a commercial graphics engine;
- scientific validity from chronology alone.

These stopping boundaries occur after the recoverable operations. They do not replace them.
