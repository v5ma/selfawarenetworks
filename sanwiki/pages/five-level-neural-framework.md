The Five-Level Neural Framework is [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]]'s hierarchical scale model organizing neural phenomena — and by extension SAN theory — across five levels of biological organization, from subcellular processes to multi-agent ecosystems.

## The five levels

| Level | Label | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Ecosystem | Personal + interpersonal experience; multi-agent dynamics; frequency coupling between brains |
| 4 | Macroscale | Whole-brain coordination via [[thalamic-cortical-loops]] |
| 3 | Mesoscale | Individual and group [[oscillators]]: cortical columns, hippocampus, brain regions |
| 2 | Microscale | Neurons, glia, neural circuits |
| 1 | Subcellular | Proteins, molecules, ions; potential quantum effects |

## Why this matters for SAN

SAN theory claims consciousness is not a property of any single level but an emergent product of cross-level oscillatory integration. The framework explicitly situates [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) at the boundary between Levels 2–4: individual neuron arrays (L2) project phase patterns that form cortical column representations (L3) that are globally coordinated by thalamocortical feedback (L4).

The Ecosystem level (L5) opens SAN toward **inter-brain synchronization** — frequency coupling between individuals — which extends the [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling network beyond a single skull.

## Synchronization as the core mechanism

The framework foregrounds "SYNC": high-amplitude, low-frequency oscillations cause simultaneous reactions across network components. [[oscillators]] act as "photocopiers" — magnifying and broadcasting patterns to downstream neural layers. This is the mesoscale (L3) mechanism through which consciousness achieves spatial-temporal integration described in [[cortical-column-oscillatory-synchronization]].

## Quantum boundary

The framework places quantum effects firmly at L1 (subcellular) while noting that consciousness operates at L2 (cellular scale), despite quantum interactions occurring within neurons. This is a deliberate positioning: SAN does not require quantum computation for consciousness; quantum effects are substrate-level, not mechanism-level.
