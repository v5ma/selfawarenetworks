# Predictive Coding

> **Boundary note (2026-07-18):** Predictive coding and predictive processing are mainstream algorithmic models, not settled proof of any one neural substrate, rigid laminar implementation, or SAN phase-wave carrier. SAN can map prediction/error loops onto phase-wave differentials, cortical traveling waves, and neural rendering, but those are stronger substrate hypotheses that require circuit-specific evidence. See [[phase-language-firewall]], [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries]], and [[cognitive-map]].

**Predictive Coding** is, in [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md), the algorithmic framing -- inherited from [predictive processing](predictive-processing.md), [free-energy](free-energy-principle.md) formulations, and [Hawkins-style](hierarchical-temporal-memory.md) hierarchical prediction -- that SAN absorbs as a compatible high-level account but reformulates at the substrate level through [phase-wave differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md), [oscillatory](neural-oscillations.md) error signaling, and [continuous neural rendering](continuous-neural-rendering.md). Predictive coding describes *what* the brain computes (top-down predictions versus bottom-up errors); SAN's [rendering-engine](brain-as-rendering-engine.md) picture supplies the *how* -- phase-coded prediction errors propagating across [cortical-columns](cortical-columns.md) in real time.

## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream predictive-coding literature, SAN takes three positions. First, it accepts the Friston / [Hawkins](hierarchical-temporal-memory.md) framing as broadly correct *at the algorithmic level* (`03san--md`, line 18, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md); `07san--md`, line 248, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md)). Second, it specifically denies that predictive coding is the substrate-level explanation: per [08san](gh-08san.md) line 29 the SAN account does not "supplant" predictive coding but situates it inside the [phase-wave](phase-wave-differentials.md) machinery, recovering the same input/output behavior through differential phase activity rather than literal prediction-error subtraction in dedicated neural populations. Third, where mainstream PC predicts canonical L2/3-L5 error/prediction routing, SAN's response to the 2024 challenge (`04san--md`, line 1, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md)) is to predict that disrupting predictive performance will track [gamma](gamma-oscillations.md) / [theta](theta-oscillations.md) coherence and the [dominant phase-wave differential](dominant-phase-wave-differential.md) -- independent of which sensory [cortex](cortex.md) is the seat of "the" prediction error. The 2024 preprint [summary](gh-b0049y.md) (`dl-oct6-2025--2024.10.02.616378v1.full`, line 59, note, 2024) -- that PC explains cortical responses but with empirical caveats -- is exactly the wedge SAN's relational [synchrony](synchrony.md) account was designed to handle.
