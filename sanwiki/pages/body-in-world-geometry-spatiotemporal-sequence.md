# Body-In-World Geometry And Spatiotemporal Sequence

## The problem

The eyes move, the head turns, the body tilts, objects change position, and memory predicts what
may happen next. Yet a person normally experiences one body at a location in one changing world.

Saying that the brain "represents space" leaves the main operation unexplained. A useful account
must say how visual, vestibular, somatic, memory, and action signals are related over time, how
self-location remains inside that changing relation, and how the result affects the next action.

## The operation in plain language

```text
visual, vestibular, somatic, and environmental change
-> spatially distributed and temporally ordered neural activity
-> learned relations among body, self-location, objects, and surroundings
-> recurrent body-in-world state
-> remembered or predicted sequence
-> report, plan, or action
-> changed sensory return
```

Different populations need not contain one miniature picture. They can carry partial relations:
head direction, body tilt, object position, expected movement, remembered order, and the
consequence of an action. Recurrent interaction can keep those relations mutually usable while
the scene and body change.

The 2021 source calls this a "four-dimensional matrix." In ordinary language, it means
three-dimensional body-and-world relations changing over time. It does not require a literal
four-dimensional lattice in neural tissue.

## What established research supports

Multisensory and vestibular processing contributes to self-location and first-person perspective.
Ionta and colleagues altered visuotactile and visuovestibular relations and connected the
resulting changes in self-location to temporoparietal mechanisms
([2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2011.03.009)).

Neural activity also carries spatial and temporal organization:

- hippocampal theta sequences can organize portions of spatial experience
  ([Gupta et al., 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3138));
- human medial-temporal neurons can show sequence-related firing at different theta phases
  ([Reddy et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-25150-0));
- hippocampal ensembles can encode and reactivate relationships among extended nonspatial events
  ([Elias et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28057-6));
- perturbing oscillatory timing can alter memory encoding under specific conditions
  ([Robinson et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-021-00919-0)).

These findings make oscillatory coordination and sequence learning legitimate mechanisms to test.
They do not supply one universal phase code. In a different human working-memory task, theta phase
related to item position while phase order did not reproduce item order
([Liebe et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-01893-7)).

## Where the comparison stops

- Multisensory self-location research does not show that oscillations alone create spatial qualia.
- A phase relationship found in one task, region, species, or frequency band is not a universal
  neural protocol.
- Spatial and temporal firing order does not imply a literal geometric grid in tissue.
- Longer learned sequences can preserve more relations, but length by itself is not intelligence,
  consciousness, truth, or creative value.
- Introspection identifies the stable body-in-world phenomenon; it does not identify the complete
  mechanism.

## The SAN extension

SAN proposes that relative timing and phase are not merely correlates of a finished spatial model.
They may help coordinate the changing relation among populations carrying visual, vestibular,
somatic, memory, prediction, and action variables.

The discriminating prediction is:

> A model using relative neural timing and phase will predict and causally track changes in
> self-location, spatial perspective, remembered continuation, and action better than matched
> models using only average activity, spectral power, anatomy, or static connectivity.

The proposal should be rejected or narrowed if phase-aware variables add no reproducible
predictive or causal value.

## Learned sequences connect perception to action

The recording also preserves an early operation later developed into the Memory Prediction
Rendering Sequence:

```text
partial current relation
-> learned sequence recruitment
-> remembered or predicted continuation
-> updated body-world state
-> candidate action
-> returned sensory consequence
```

This is a genealogy relation, not a backdated acronym. The exact source was recorded on September
17, 2021; **MPRS** was named later. The later **MPRS** name is not backdated into the recording.

## The physics branch remains visible

The same source asks why living and engineered systems can maintain organized activity and how
information relates to physical change.

The useful comparison is layered:

- living organisms are open, energy-consuming, self-maintaining systems;
- hurricanes are familiar dissipative structures;
- machines are engineered physical organizations;
- galaxies and atoms are persistent physical organizations but are not automatically the same
  thermodynamic class;
- information processing is physically implemented and can have energetic constraints.

Prigogine provides the dissipative-organization route
([1977 Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1977/prigogine/lecture/)).
Landauer provides a bounded relation between logically irreversible information processing and
physical dissipation
([1961](https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.53.0183)).

That research does not decide whether information is ontologically prior to physics. The stronger
SIT question remains a separate theoretical and mathematical program.

## Machines are physical; consciousness is a separate test

A system does not leave physics because it was assembled rather than grown. But origin alone does
not answer whether it is conscious. Machine consciousness needs operational indicators,
perturbation, competing explanations, and welfare-aware governance. This route connects to the SAN
machine-consciousness measurement proposal without attributing its later criteria to the 2021
recording.
