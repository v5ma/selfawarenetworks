A taxonomy of [[brain-computer-interface|brain-computer interface]] (BCI) technologies organized by modality and invasiveness, compiled by [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]] as background research for [[self-aware-networks]].

## BCI Device Categories

BCI technologies divide across three axes: **imaging vs. stimulation**, **implanted vs. external**, and **single-modality vs. combined**. The source document covers four major branches:

- **Implanted imaging & stim** — Neuralink, NeuroString, ECoG, DBS, Stentrode
- **External stimulation** — TMS, tDCS, AVS/tACS, ECS (electroconvulsive)
- **External imaging** — EIT, EEG (+ VR, fNIRS, MEG combinations), fMRI
- **Data pipelines** — Neuropype (Tim Mullen) for unstructured neural data; PCA for feature reduction

## Relevance to SAN Theory

[[self-aware-networks]] posits that [[neural-oscillations|neural oscillations]] encode information through [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography). BCI devices that read or write to the brain are potential windows into — or interventions on — the network's [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling mechanism. External imaging modalities (fNIRS, EEG) provide non-invasive access to the oscillatory patterns SAN theory studies. See [[neomindcycle]] for how cross-scale neural cycles relate to BCI feedback loops, and [[xmark]] for the role of [[attention]] in neural signal interpretation.
