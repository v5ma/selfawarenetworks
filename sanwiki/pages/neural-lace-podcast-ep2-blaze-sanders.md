Episode 2 of the [[neural-lace-podcast]] features Blaze Sanders — CTO of SpaceVR, former AR engineer on the Meta 2 glasses, and ex-NASA roboticist — exploring the medical, business, and robotics applications of [[neural-lace|neural lace]] technology.

Sanders brings a multi-domain engineering perspective: having worked at the intersection of spaceflight, [[augmented-reality|augmented reality]] hardware, and robotics, he grounds [[neural-lace|neural lace]] discussion in practical implementation constraints. The conversation covers how existing AR and robotics tooling informs the design space for brain-computer interfaces.

Key themes include the feasibility of non-invasive [[neural-lace|neural lace]], the crossover between AR wearable sensors and neural monitoring, and how lessons from robotics (closed-loop feedback systems, embodied cognition) translate to [[bci]] design principles.

This episode sits alongside [[neural-lace-podcast-ep3-eric-matzner]] (nootropics and human enhancement) and [[neural-lace-podcast-4-andre-watson]] (genetic nanomedicine and synapse physiology) in the podcast's broader project of defining the practical and theoretical boundaries of [[neural-lace-podcast|neural lace]] as a technology.
