**Tonic-phasic canvas of consciousness** is a historical SAN name for the idea that changing neural
events are registered relative to an ongoing state. The medically bounded mechanism is maintained at
[[tonic-phasic-canvas|Tonic and Phasic Roles: Canvas and Ink]].

## Current interpretation

- A tonic context is a relatively maintained receiver state over a declared interval.
- A phasic difference is a transient change that alters the receiver and its downstream effects.
- The same distributed tissue receives, transforms, re-expresses, and uses the change.
- Consciousness is a further SAN interpretation, not a property granted by the labels tonic or phasic.

Tonic activity is not necessarily slow, high-amplitude, synchronized, contentless, or aware.
Phasic activity is not necessarily fast, burst-like, information-rich, or conscious. A return toward
baseline need not erase a representation; it can reflect adaptation, inhibition, recurrent
dynamics, movement, or many other processes.

Reports under salvia, alcohol, psychedelics, anesthesia, or other altered states cannot establish
one canvas/ink mechanism without controlled measurements and alternatives. Nor does uniform activity
imply no percept in every circuit.
