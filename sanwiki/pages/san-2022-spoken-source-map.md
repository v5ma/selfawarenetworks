# SAN 2022 Spoken Source Map

The 2022 Self-Aware Networks videos are a public first-party explanation of how Micah Blumberg connected NAPOT, memory scaling, neural rendering, oscillatory coordination, and distributed observer-action. They establish the development and intended meaning of SAN concepts. They do not, by themselves, establish that every proposed biological mechanism is correct.

## Review coverage

All four recovered transcript bodies have received bounded segment-level review: July 28, 28/28 segments; July 30, 135/135; July 31, 492/492; and September 18, 879/879. The combined review covers 1,534/1,534 segments and records 89 source-genealogy atoms. Those atoms document what was publicly said, joined, attributed, qualified, or proposed. They are not automatically 89 medical validations or 89 priority verdicts.

## The four-video sequence

1. [The Self Aware Networks Institute for Neurophysics, Artificial Neurology & Bio-Synthetic Interfaces — July 28, 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VTBNyUM47Zg) introduces the research program and connects synaptic memory with its later expression in whole-brain activity.
2. [Self Aware Networks: A longer explanation of my research, my past work, and what I have built — July 30, 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKbl0ryKRoY) places neural rendering, memory amplification, and the distributed neural observer within the longer SAN genealogy.
3. [One-hour explanation of NAPOT as the central thesis of SAN — July 31, 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fLp-yTQ6pSM) joins memory scaling, neural arrays, selective reception, transformed output, oscillation, 3D reconstruction, and perception into one architecture.
4. [Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography Theory, fifth revision — September 18, 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vixhppNAKPs) develops the receiver-transmitter, tonic/phasic, inhibition, rendering, and observer-action account in greater detail.

## What the videos clarify

### NAPOT is not only a visual-rendering hypothesis

The July 31 explanation calls NAPOT the central thesis of SAN and gives it two connected jobs:

- explain how distributed neural activity can maintain and update a usable model of body and world; and
- explain how learned changes at synaptic and cellular scales can participate in later circuit- and brain-scale memory reconstruction.

This is why [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] must be described across micro, meso, and macro scales rather than illustrated as one generic neuron containing a complete percept.

### Reception and re-expression are functional roles

The videos compare dendrites and receptors with cameras or microphones, and neural output with pixels, screens, speakers, or transmitters. These are explicitly functional comparisons, not claims that literal media devices exist inside neurons.

The modern biological translation is:

```text
selective reception
-> state-dependent dendritic and somatic integration
-> transformed cellular output
-> distributed downstream re-expression
```

The analogy therefore identifies candidate operations that can be measured. It should neither be literalized nor dismissed as decorative language.

### The observer is the distributed network

The videos do not require a smaller person or inner eye watching a neural picture. Neural populations participate in receiving differences, changing state, transmitting consequences, selecting actions, and receiving the results of those actions. Their coordinated activity collectively performs the discrimination and action attributed to an observer.

This is the observer-action interpretation developed on [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], [[entification-observer-problem|Entification and the Distributed Observer]], and [[passive-observer-fallacy|The Passive Observer Fallacy]].

### Tonic canvas and phasic ink are proposed operations

The 2022 explanations distinguish ongoing tonic activity from relatively irregular phasic departures. In modern bounded language:

- tonic population state supplies an ongoing receiver context or canvas;
- phasic changes supply differentiated updates or ink;
- the effect of an update depends on timing, anatomy, coupling, receiver state, and recurrent consequences.

The videos contain provisional frequency and amplitude assignments that require empirical checking. The operational distinction should be tested rather than reduced to a literary metaphor. See [[tonic-phasic-canvas|Tonic Canvas and Phasic Ink]].

### Memory scales through reinstatement, not enlargement of a tiny picture

The videos move from synapses and dendrites to neurons, arrays, local circuits, distributed activity, and behavior. A source-faithful modern account does not say that a literal miniature image grows larger. Learned local structure changes which larger state can be reconstructed when a partial cue returns.

This is the direct 2022 precursor to [[telescoping-network-neighborhood-reinstatement|Telescoping Network-Neighborhood Reinstatement]].

### COT and NAPOT form a proposed scale bridge

The July 28 and July 30 videos present Cellular Oscillating Tomography (COT) and NAPOT as distinct but connected programs. COT concerns cellular receptor learning, computed structure, and a proposed evolutionary extension. NAPOT concerns array-to-array projection, reconstruction, memory scaling, and observer-action. Their source-faithful connection is a scale bridge from learned cellular structure to larger receive-transform-project dynamics. The evolutionary extension remains a hypothesis requiring independent developmental and evolutionary evidence.

### Choice joins three attributed component theories

The July 30 explanation explicitly joins Peter Tse's criteria causation, György Buzsáki's inhibitory pathway-selection material, and Steven Strogatz's oscillator synchronization. SAN does not originate those donor components. Its proposed integration is that learned criteria, alternative pathways, inhibition or bifurcation, and oscillatory coordination participate in selecting a thought or action and thereby change the next observer-action cycle.

## Established component science versus SAN interpretation

### Established component science

Primary neuroscience supports state-dependent dendritic integration, local dendritic nonlinearities, probabilistic synaptic release, recurrent excitation and inhibition, phase-dependent neural coding in some systems, memory-related replay, distributed engrams, and behaviorally consequential activity in sparse populations.

### Proposed SAN/NAPOT integration

SAN proposes that these components form a multiscale receive-transform-redistribute cycle in which tonic context and differentiated phasic updates help a distributed network reconstruct a usable body-world model and produce observer-action.

### Not established by the videos

The videos do not establish a universal four-symbol vesicle code, a universal cortical-column algorithm, fixed semantic meanings for frequency bands, unlimited propagation of every perturbation, or literal CT-style tomography in neural tissue. Those are separate empirical questions.

## Claims that require discriminating experiments

1. Does a learned dendritic configuration carry held-out information about a structured spatial relationship beyond generic excitability or feature selectivity?
2. Does phase or timing add causal reconstruction value beyond spike count and rate?
3. Can perturbing a prepared cell or assembly predictably change which larger network state is reinstated?
4. Does inhibition select among candidate reconstructions in the relevant circuit, or merely normalize activity?
5. Can a multiscale SAN model outperform recurrent, attractor, predictive-coding, and HTM alternatives on held-out reconstruction and action data?
