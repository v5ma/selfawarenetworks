# Phasic-Tonic Relationship

The **phasic-tonic relationship** is the receiver-relative interaction between a maintained state
and a consequential departure from that state. It is the mechanism behind SAN's tonic-canvas /
phasic-ink mnemonic. The full scale and evidence boundaries are maintained at
[[tonic-phasic-canvas|Tonic and Phasic Roles: Canvas and Ink]].

## The relation, not two substances

**Tonic** and **phasic** name roles relative to a declared receiver and time window. Tonic context
is the comparatively maintained state against which a change can matter. A phasic event is a
transient departure that changes what the receiver does next. The same event can be phasic at one
scale and part of a tonic context at another.

This distinction prevents three common errors:

- tonic does not mean one universal slow-frequency carrier;
- phasic does not mean gamma activity or excitation alone; and
- neither role is meaningful until the receiver, measurement, and comparison window are named.

Established neuroscience supports state-dependent excitability, adaptation, gain, phase-sensitive
timing, and transient versus sustained responses in specific circuits. SAN adds the interpretation
that the ongoing state is an active comparison context and the consequential change is the update
registered by that living receiver. The stronger claim that this relation helps constitute
observation or qualitative experience remains an open SAN hypothesis.

## Cellular translation

A presynaptic event is not transmitted intact across a chemical synapse:

1. somatodendritic state influences axon-initial-segment output;
2. the action potential propagates along the axon;
3. terminal waveform and calcium dynamics influence probabilistic release;
4. transmitter, receptor state, dendritic voltage, inhibition, and recent history shape the postsynaptic effect;
5. the receiver integrates that effect with many other inputs.

Potassium channels can influence waveform and excitability, but they are one family among many
conductances and modulators. Action-potential duration does not determine a universal vesicle count,
and vesicle count does not by itself encode a qualia value. Pacesetter potentials, plateau
potentials, bursts, and action potentials are different physiological events, not three universal
levels of one SAN code.

## Population translation

At population scale, the relationship may involve transient changes in firing, phase, synchrony,
traveling-wave structure, gain, or population geometry relative to ongoing state. A valid claim
must identify the downstream receiver and show causal value beyond correlated variables.

## Scale-specific examples

| Scale | Candidate tonic context | Candidate phasic change | Receiver that must be tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | Membrane voltage, conductance, recent synaptic history, or dendritic readiness | Synaptic event, dendritic spike, burst, waveform change, or inhibition | Named dendritic compartment, soma, axon initial segment, terminal, or postsynaptic cell |
| Meso | Local inhibition, recurrent gain, or maintained ensemble state | Phase reset, traveling-wave passage, or ensemble transition | Named local or downstream circuit |
| Macro | Distributed brain-body state maintained across recurrent loops | Sensory evidence, memory reinstatement, prediction error, or action feedback | Named systems whose later control, report, or behavior changes |

The repeated abstract form does not imply one mechanism at every scale. A cellular waveform, a
local-field phase shift, and a distributed state transition are different measurements and need
different causal tests.

## Minimal experimental test

1. Define the tonic reference state and its time window.
2. Define the candidate phasic variable without combining phase, power, rate, waveform, and release
   into one label.
3. Identify the receiving cell or population and the output predicted to change.
4. Compare the phasic variable with matched rate, power, movement, arousal, and history models.
5. Perturb the proposed relation while preserving the main alternatives, then test for rescue.

A successful result would show that the declared tonic-phasic relation predicts and causally alters
a receiver-specific transformation. It would support that bounded mechanism, not by itself prove a
universal PWD code, neural rendering, or consciousness.
