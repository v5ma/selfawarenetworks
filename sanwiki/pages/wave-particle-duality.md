## Position

[[binary-cosmology-particles-waves|Binary Cosmology: Particles as 1, Waves as 0]] is the load-bearing piece: particles are the "on-shell," localized read-out (the bit-1 case), waves are the off-shell, distributed propagation (the bit-0 case), and what classical physics calls duality is the cosmos-level encoding alphabet. [[particle-interaction-time-density]] and [[particle-gravity-time-density]] complete the bridge: particle-ness and wave-ness are local time-density signatures, not separate ontological categories. [[gravity-quantum-diffusion-ficks-law|Gravity as quantum-particle diffusion]] follows automatically. [[soliton-wave-mechano-neural-signaling|Soliton-wave mechano-neural signaling]] makes this explicit by treating the [[action-potential|action potential]] as a localized soliton — a particle-shaped wave — that travels with conserved profile until dissipation.
