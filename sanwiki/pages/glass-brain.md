# Glass Brain

## Position

Glass Brain has two functions inside the SAN corpus, neither of which is a metaphysical claim. First, it is the *visualisation prior art* against which SAN's own [[brain-as-rendering-engine|brain-as-rendering-engine]] proposals can be benchmarked: Glass Brain renders source-localised EEG activity onto a 3D reconstructed [[cortex]] in real time, showing what time-resolved cortical dynamics look like when you stop reducing them to scalp topographies. SAN's framing of [[consciousness]] as [[continuous-neural-rendering|continuous neural rendering]], [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering|persistent rendering]], and [[neural-rendering-of-qualia|qualia rendering]] inherits the *idea* that the brain's activity is renderable spatiotemporal content, with Glass Brain serving as a partial external proof-of-concept (`a0121z--md`, line 5). Second, it is part of the *multimodal-time-sync* prior art that [[micah-blumberg|Micah]]'s [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] / [[neo-mind-cycle-neurofeedback|neurofeedback]] / [[eeg-vr-biofeedback|EEG-VR biofeedback]] work points toward: Neuropype's same-timecode fusion of EEG, EKG, eye-tracking, and pupil dilation sensors (`a0121z--md`, line 5) is precisely the architecture an [[eeg-xr-neurofeedback-bci|EEG + XR neurofeedback BCI]] needs to read [[brainwaves|brainwave]] state in context. The associated network — Tim Mullen at Intheon and UCSD, the Phong / Android Jones / Microdose-VR connection at VRLA 2016 / AWE 2017 (`a0249z--md`, lines 49–55, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0249z.md)) — is the social context in which [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s [[consciousness-as-neurofeedback-loops|neurofeedback-loops]] research interfaced with VR/AR practice in the 2010s.
