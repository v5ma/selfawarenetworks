# Wave Phase Dissipation

**Wave Phase Dissipation** is, in [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]]'s [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] writings, the SAN-internal name for the way an oscillatory wave loses *phase* — not amplitude — over time, with the resulting phase drift treated as a signal-bearing quantity rather than a nuisance. ## Position

Pending acquisition, the SAN-aligned reading is that *phase* dissipation is to *amplitude* dissipation what timing-information is to energy: the brain (and any [[brain-as-dissipative-system|dissipative system]] of comparable structure) leaks coherence not by getting quieter but by getting out of step with itself. SAN treats this leakage as the quantity that the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|dominant phase-wave differential]] is *measuring* — a phase-difference signal that supplies the read-out for [[neural-oscillation-rendering|oscillation rendering]] and [[neural-oscillatory-tomography|oscillatory tomography]]. [[inhibitory-decay-oscillation-frequency|Inhibitory decay]] supplies one fine-grained mechanism for controlled phase dissipation: the time-course of inhibition sets how fast a local [[oscillator]] can drift before it is re-synchronized by the next [[gamma-oscillation|gamma cycle]] or [[oscillatory-binding-problem|oscillatory binding event]]. Cosmologically, the same vocabulary is used in [[fractal-dissipating-cosmos|fractal dissipating cosmos]] to describe how phase, not energy, is the relevant currency in the [[wave-dissipation-universality|wave-dissipation-universality]] argument.
