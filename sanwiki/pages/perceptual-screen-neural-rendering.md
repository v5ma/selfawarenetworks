# Perceptual Screen and Neural Rendering

## The problem before the terminology

Seeing is the observer operation of sparse, distributed neural tissue. The system combines changing
signals about color, edge, depth, body position, expectation, and action into a usable state, while
the same field-cell process registers those differences and changes what happens next.

SAN calls the ongoing construction, registration, and use of that distributed state **Neural
Rendering**. The **perceptual screen** names the organized result and process: tonic field-cell
activity supplies the comparison canvas, phasic changes supply the changing distinctions, and the
aggregate consequences produce perception, consideration, and action.

## The SAN mechanism ladder

1. Sensory and recurrent signals alter dendritic and membrane state across many cells.
2. Transient departures occur relative to longer-lived local or network baselines.
3. Relative timing, phase, amplitude, duration, topology, and cell type constrain which populations
   become functionally coupled.
4. Recurrent thalamocortical, corticocortical, local, and sensorimotor loops stabilize some changing
   patterns long enough to guide recognition, prediction, and action.
5. SAN interprets the distributed multiscale state as a rendered model rather than a passive copy.
6. NAPOT is the stronger proposal that receive-transform-project operations and nested oscillatory
   relations perform a tomography-like reconstruction.

PWD, NDCA, DPWDR, and oscillatory tomography name proposed parts of this ladder. They should be
introduced only after the reader understands the biological operation each term is meant to
describe.

## Evidence boundary

Established neuroscience supports distributed sensory processing, recurrence, predictive and
sensorimotor influences, dendritic computation, oscillations, traveling waves, and multiscale
coordination. That background does not by itself establish a literal volumetric screen, a unique
phase code for every quality, or NAPOT as the brain's inverse solution.

The strongest publication posture is therefore layered:

- established biological operation;
- SAN's integrative interpretation;
- stronger mechanistic hypothesis;
- measurable prediction that could distinguish it from alternatives.

## Different contributions, not one copied image

The June 2022 `b0153y.md` and `b0131y.md` notes propose that different cells and regions transform
the same event according to their learned inputs and functions. SAN uses **tomographic** for the
stronger idea that these different contributions can be integrated into a reconstruction-like
state. Distributed representation is established; NAPOT's specific inverse solution is not.

## Bright and dark receiver-facing updates

[[gh-a0080z|a0080z]] uses pixels and pointillist dots to explain how one neural population can alter
the next. The scientifically bounded version is not that neurons emit literal light or pass one
spike intact across a synapse. An action potential travels along a presynaptic axon, triggers
terminal release, and changes postsynaptic currents in many receivers. Excitation, inhibition, and
the absence of an expected tonic event can therefore produce a distributed pattern of greater and
lesser receiver-facing change: metaphorical bright and dark marks.

The durable proposal is that successive populations transform these distributed differences into
higher-level features. The pixel language is an explanatory analogy for receiver state, not an
anatomical screen or a scalar spike-height code.

## Waves become features, features become action

[[gh-a0411z|a0411z]], internally dated October 15, 2012, describes developing visual waves as
structured activity that can leave learned spatial relations and asks how edges, scale, depth,
color, smell, and other features become linked. [[gh-a0402z|a0402z]], internally dated March 20,
2013, adds a rough excitatory/inhibitory selection picture. [[gh-a0417z|a0417z]] preserves an April
2014 category-theory discussion and then an explicit 2022 retrospective stating the joined
operation: arrays register partial structure, and multiscale oscillatory activity binds those
registrations with the body and its actions.

The bounded modern claim joins structured developmental activity, learned cross-modal relations,
selective inhibition, recurrent array transformations, and sensorimotor consequence. It does not
equate fetal retinal waves with adult visual experience or assign object selection to one cell.
[Ackman, Burbridge, and Crair (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11529) directly showed
spontaneous retinal activity propagating through the developing mouse visual system before eye
opening; the full Neural Rendering mechanism remains a SAN hypothesis.

## Current-perspective rendering in the early source record

`a0054z.md` gives the early record a compact receive-transform-project mechanism. The brain does
not need to render every possible perspective at once. Current sensory and recurrent evidence is
transformed by receiver populations, selectively re-expressed, and stabilized with memory, body
state, expectation, and action into the perspective the organism can presently use.

The game-engine camera is a conditional-rendering analogy. "Camera," "screen," "pixel," and
"speaker" name functional roles, not miniature media devices or a hidden viewer. The observer is
the distributed system changing and using the result. The source's listening/firing language is
best tested as overlapping, state-dependent receiving, integration, and output rather than a
binary alternation of whole cortical columns.

## The 2021 lens-and-retina image

`a0112z.md`, internally dated November 7, 2021, compares recurrent central activity with a lens and
distributed neocortex with a retina-like receiving array. The useful operation is distributed
reception, transformation, and later use. It is not a central projector, literal light, cortical
retina, or hidden viewer. `a0107z.md` adds a foveated-networking comparison in which sparse,
viewer-relevant updates are reconstructed locally.

## The coffee-cup sequence and the distributed observer

[[gh-a0135z|a0135z]], first current-path Git-fixed June 11, 2022, uses a coffee cup, hand, counter,
room, eye movement, and head movement to describe a temporally rendered body-and-world relation.
Its phrase that "only the TV can see it" is easy to misread. The following words identify the
intended observer as the neural network doing the rendering with its chemical body.

The source-faithful operation is therefore:

```text
changing sensory and motor relation
-> distributed neural state
-> the same network detects how that state changes
-> prediction and movement update
```

There is no additional viewer behind this cycle. The source also proposes a slightly prospective
render aligned with movement. That is a testable timing claim, not an established universal
one-millisecond lead.

## A public 2022 receiver-transmitter bridge

The August 17, 2022 public [[gh-a0282z|a0282z]] expansion makes the operation concrete. One
distributed array receives a changing pattern; its cells transform that activity according to
their anatomy, membrane state, learned connections, and current context; their axons and terminals
then alter later receivers. In plain language, each stage receives a difference, changes it, and
passes forward a consequence rather than relaying an untouched picture.

Established neuroscience supports receptive-field transformation, dendritic integration, axonal
conduction, synaptic transmission, and population-level recoding. The comparison stops before
those findings establish that neurons are literal eyes or screens, that one unchanged image moves
through the brain, or that ordinary population recoding by itself proves consciousness.

SAN preserves the stronger receiver-transmitter hypothesis:

```text
distributed reception
-> tissue-specific transformation
-> projected consequence
-> reception and reinterpretation by the next cell or array
```

The proposed extension is that this operation recurs across nested cellular and population scales
and helps constitute Neural Rendering and NAPOT. That extension remains testable rather than being
silently substituted for the established physiology.

## Book route

Book 2 should first explain the positive observer mechanism: sparse distributed cells receive and
re-express changes, tonic field-cell activity supplies the comparison canvas, and phasic changes
alter the system that perceives and acts. It should then introduce Neural Rendering, the
perceptual-screen metaphor, PWD, NAPOT, NDCA, and DPWDR in increasing order of specificity.

embodied network.
