**Consciousness** is the central explanatory target of SAN, but it is not one experimental variable.
The word can refer to wakeful state, a particular experienced content, flexible access, self-
evaluation, the continuity of a person, or the intrinsic subjective character of a process. A
scientific claim must name which target it addresses.

SAN's positive proposal is that the sparse, distributed, recurrent field-cell-body system is the
observer-action process. It receives and transforms differences, constructs a revisable body-world
rendering, is physically changed by that rendering, acts, encounters the consequences, and updates
what it can register next. It does not construct a completed picture for a second inner viewer.

## Separate the targets

| Target | Operational question | Do not silently equate with |
|---|---|---|
| State consciousness | Is the organism awake, asleep, anesthetized, comatose, or in another clinically defined state? | One specific experienced content. |
| Conscious content | What color, sound, pain, body location, object, relation, or thought is experienced? | Flexible report or metacognition. |
| Conscious access | Can the content guide working memory, reasoning, report, and flexible control? | The complete phenomenal character of the content. |
| Metacognition | Can the organism evaluate its own discrimination, uncertainty, or access? | Awareness of every process that contributed. |
| Phenomenal consciousness | What organized physical process, if any, is identical with what experience feels like? | A decoded stimulus or behavioral output. |
| Diachronic self | What preserves one person's perspective, memory, body relation, policy, and causal history across episodes? | One instantaneous neural pattern. |

The distinctions matter clinically as well as theoretically. In one prospective study, 16 of 104
behaviorally unresponsive patients showed EEG activation associated with spoken motor commands
([Claassen et al., 2019](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa1812757)). That result identifies
cognitive-motor dissociation under the study's conditions. It does not make one EEG classifier a
complete consciousness test or prove what each patient experienced.

Perceptual integration can also dissociate from conscious access
([Fahrenfort et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28325878/)). Report requirements can
change apparent frontal and late electrophysiological signatures
([Hatamimajoumerd et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35981538/)). Representation,
integration, access, report, and phenomenal identity therefore require separate outcomes.

## What current neuroscience establishes

Conscious states and contents depend on distributed and state-dependent biological activity.
Sensory, association, thalamic, subcortical, arousal, memory, interoceptive, autonomic, and action
systems can contribute in different preparations. Feedforward processing, local recurrence,
long-range interaction, attention, decision, report, and movement can be experimentally dissociated.

No single frequency band, cortical column, lobe, field maximum, phase relation, connectivity metric,
decoder, or information measure is accepted as a complete explanation. The 2025 COGITATE
adversarial collaboration preregistered divergent predictions of Global Neuronal Workspace Theory
and Integrated Information Theory. Its results supported some predictions and challenged important
predictions of both; the study did not declare either theory complete
([COGITATE Consortium, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1)).

These limits do not imply that distributed recurrence, phase, fields, or information are irrelevant.
They require a theory to specify the source, route, receiver, body state, causal use, and failure
condition of each proposed variable.

## SAN's positive distributed-observer proposition

SAN does not move perception into an unspecified hidden agent. The proposed observer is the coupled
operation itself:

1. receptors, synapses, dendritic branches, cells, and body systems receive partial differences;
2. learned morphology, connectivity, chemistry, and current state transform what arrives;
3. axons, terminals, local fields, and population routes re-express consequential changes;
4. recurrent cortical, thalamic, hippocampal, subcortical, cerebellar, autonomic, and body loops
   revise the active body-world state;
5. memory, value, prediction, and possible-action relations constrain what happens next;
6. autonomic, communicative, or motor output changes the body or environment; and
7. reafferent, exafferent, and internal consequences update the next cycle and sometimes plasticity.

No single neuron, cortical column, rhythm, bright convergence point, or brain region contains the
complete observer. A cell can perform receiver and transmitter roles inside the larger operation
without being a miniature conscious person.

Let `x_t` denote a distributed neural state, `u_t` present external and internal input, `b_t` body
state, `r_t` recurrent input, and `W_t` learned biological constraints:

```text
x_(t+1) = F(x_t, u_t, b_t, r_t; W_t)                            [1]
```

Equation [1] is a systems description, not a claim that `F` is one cortical module. A serious SAN
model must identify measurable biological variables and interventions for the terms it uses.

## The rendering exists physically, but not as a central screen

Human phenomenology presents a temporally continuous, body-centered, apparently three-dimensional
world. SAN proposes that the physical rendering is the sparse, distributed pattern of active
relations across the participating field-cell-body system. Each local receiver registers only part
of the state; recurrent projection, transformation, and returned consequence maintain the larger
body-world relation over time.

Calling this a **distributed display** preserves the historical SAN claim. It does not mean that a
flat image, one volumetric object, or one complete movie frame is located in a cortical layer. The
same tissue that helps construct the state is altered by its content and carries those alterations
into recognition, memory, sampling, report, and action. The display and observer are functional
roles within one coupled process, not two separate entities.

The stronger SAN identity hypothesis is that receiver-relative phase, timing, synaptic, cellular,
and bounded field differences do not merely correlate with content; their organized intrinsic
state helps constitute the content. That claim is scientifically open. A computer reconstruction
of neural activity would be an external model, not proof that the reconstructed picture is the
native format or the experience itself.

## Tonic context, Shannon difference, and PWD

The [[tonic-phasic-phase-wave-differential|tonic and phasic]] distinction supplies the missing
content operator. A relatively expected ongoing state can act as the current comparison context.
An excitation- or inhibition-driven departure can alter that context. For event `x_i`, receiver
`r`, baseline model `p_0,r`, and context `C`:

```text
I_r(x_i | C) = -log2 p_0,r(x_i | C)                              [2]
```

An expected coordinated event can have low conditional surprisal, while a less probable organized
departure can have more. The candidate [[phase-wave-differentials|PWD]] is multidimensional:

```text
PWD_r,i = X_r,i - E_0,r[X | C]                                   [3]
```

`X` can include timing, circular phase, frequency, amplitude, duration, waveform, transmitted
quantity, inhibition, spatial relation, and network state. The event becomes a candidate biological
message only when a tuned receiver detects its organization and a reproducible consequence follows:

```text
Delta_Y_r = f_r(PWD_r,i, receiver_state, route, body_state)       [4]
```

This supplies a source-faithful SAN response to the earlier gamma-consciousness program. Neural
synchrony, often discussed in the gamma range, was proposed as a relational or binding code
([Singer, 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10677026/)), and long-range synchronization was later
correlated with conscious perception under a specific experimental contrast
([Melloni et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17360907/)). Neither result made gamma a
sufficient consciousness marker. Gamma-range activity participates in multiple operations, and some
scalp-EEG gamma responses can be produced by miniature saccades
([Yuval-Greenberg et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18466752/)).

SAN's proposed repair is more specific than "apply Shannon to gamma." Information theory was already
being applied to neural spike trains by 1998
([Strong et al., 1998](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.80.197)). The proposed SAN step is to make
the **receiver's expected tonic state** the conditional probability model, measure the structured
departure from that state, and then test whether the departure produces a receiver-specific causal
consequence. [[neural-oscillation|A neural oscillation]] can organize timing, and
[[gamma-oscillation|gamma]] can participate in bounded coordination, but perfectly repeated expected
synchrony may carry little new information. A rare synchronization onset, excitation-driven phase
departure, inhibition-driven phase departure, or structured splay relation can carry more conditional
surprisal; random desynchronization can still be useless. Shannon mathematics supplies the
probability distinction. Receiver tuning, route, consequence, and recurrence are needed to test
whether the difference functions as content, and none of these steps by itself proves consciousness.

The **tonic canvas** and **phasic ink** are therefore proposed operational roles, not merely
decorative metaphors and not permanent frequency bands. The tonic action field is the active
comparison medium produced by and acting with participating tissue. PWDs are candidate structured
changes. The network registers a change by being physically changed and by carrying the consequence
forward.

## From rendering to action and return

Let `m_t` denote a measurable body-world and possible-action relation implemented by, or decoded
from, the current state. Let `a_t` be action and `E` the body-environment transition:

```text
m_t = H(x_t, b_t, W_t)
a_t = Pi(m_t, goals_t, body_t)
u_(t+1) = E(a_t, body_t, world_t)                                [5]
```

`H`, `Pi`, and `E` are relations to identify, not named brain modules. SAN's observer claim requires
the closed loop: the state must constrain what is detected, remembered, selected, and done, and
returned consequences must alter later state. A passive recording, one recurrent ensemble, or one
self-report is insufficient.

[[napot-overview|NAPOT]] names the receive-transform-project-update architecture across declared
scales. [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|Neural Array Projection Oscillation
Tomography]] adds the stronger reconstruction hypothesis. Tomography here is not established by a
recognizable external visualization. It requires held-out identifiability, route-specific
perturbation, predicted loss, and rescue.

## Scale and temporal continuity

| Scale | Candidate contribution | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | Receptor, synapse, dendritic compartment, soma, axon, terminal, release, and local field state | One cell is not the complete observer or tomogram. |
| Meso | Local circuit, ensemble, laminar route, nucleus, area, and recurrent loop | A useful motif is not universal cortical anatomy. |
| Macro | Distributed cortical, thalamic, subcortical, cerebellar, autonomic, body, and environment relations | No hub or rhythm contains the whole perspective. |
| Diachronic | Memory, plasticity, homeostasis, embodiment, goals, action history, and causal continuity | One active episode is not the entire person. |

See [[conscious-episode-diachronic-self-continuity|Conscious Episodes and the Continuity of a
Person]]. Sleep, anesthesia, injury, recovery, and plasticity show why the continuity of a person
cannot be reduced to one instantaneous oscillatory map.

## Claim ladder

| Claim | Current status |
|---|---|
| Neural, bodily, chemical, behavioral, electrical, and magnetic changes can be measured. | Established under method-specific conditions. |
| Cells and circuits can be selectively sensitive to, and causally use, declared differences. | Established in bounded preparations. |
| Distributed recurrent systems can represent content, support access, guide action, and update from consequences. | Broad component support; implementation is task- and system-dependent. |
| Expected tonic context and receiver-relative PWDs add content and routing information beyond rate, power, and conventional state variables. | Open, measurable SAN hypothesis. |
| The complete embodied receive-render-act-return loop performs a distributed observer-action function. | Integrative SAN mechanistic hypothesis. |
| The intrinsic field-cell organization of that process constitutes phenomenal content and selfhood. | Strongest unresolved SAN identity hypothesis. |

No lower row proves the row above it.

## Discriminating research program

Use a task that independently manipulates perceptual content, access, report, action, and returned
consequence. Record named sensory, recurrent, body-state, memory, decision, and action pathways.
Compare nested models:

```text
M0 = stimulus + behavior + rate + power + movement + arousal + history
M1 = M0 + recurrent population state + body/action feedback
M2 = M1 + receiver-relative PWD + bounded field-cell variables          [6]
```

SAN gains support only if the added variables improve preregistered held-out prediction and a
selective route or timing perturbation produces the predicted loss and, where feasible, rescue.
Matched unconscious, no-report, artifact, seizure-like synchrony, sleep, anesthesia, and random-
rarity controls are required where relevant.

The theory should be rejected or narrowed if:

- simpler feedforward or recurrent models predict the same content and action outcomes;
- body and action-feedback variables do not contribute to the proposed observer loop;
- PWD and field-cell variables add no reproducible information after matched controls;
- perturbation changes report but not representation, access, action, or later updating in the
  predicted pattern;
- the proposed distributed state cannot be distinguished from unconscious or pathological
  coordination; or
- the criteria do not generalize beyond the task, species, modality, or measurement used to define
  them.

Behavior and self-report remain evidence, not transparent windows. The
[[machine-self-report-active-canvas-consciousness-test|Active-Canvas Test]] applies this principle
to synthetic systems without assuming that fluent language proves experience.
