## The Governing Question Was Explicit In 2021

Rank 204 states the problem before the mature SAN terminology existed:

```text
communication among living cells
-> changing distributed neural patterns
-> seeing, hearing, feeling, tasting, and touching
-> an experienced body and world
-> the organism's next action
```

The problem is not solved by saying that the brain "processes information." A useful account must
identify what each biological stage receives, how it changes that input, what it sends onward,
how multiple routes become one action-usable state, and how behavior changes the next input.

The same recording explains why brain-computer interfaces belong in the investigation. External
sensors, Utah arrays, deep-brain stimulation, optical measurements, and other instruments expose
different portions of the chain. None observes the whole process, but together they constrain
what a complete mechanism must explain.

The mature SAN terms should therefore come after the problem and operation. **Neural Rendering**
names the distributed construction and use of the current body-and-world state. **NAPOT** names a
more specific proposal about how receiving, transforming, and projecting arrays participate in
that construction.

## Body-In-World Geometry Uses Relations That Change Over Time

Rank 199 begins with a familiar problem. The eyes move, the head turns, the body tilts, and objects
change, but a person normally experiences one body located in one structured world. Balance is
part of that operation rather than an afterthought.

The source's strongest recoverable sequence is:

```text
visual, vestibular, somatic, and environmental change
-> spatially distributed and temporally ordered neural activity
-> learned relations among body, self-location, objects, and surroundings
-> recurrent body-in-world state
-> remembered or predicted continuation
-> report, plan, or action
-> changed sensory return
```

Established research supports important components. Multisensory and vestibular processing
contributes to self-location and first-person perspective. Hippocampal populations preserve
spatial and nonspatial sequence relations, and oscillatory timing can matter under specific
encoding conditions.

The comparison stops before a universal code. One task, region, species, or frequency band does
not establish that oscillations alone create spatial experience. The source's "four-dimensional
matrix" means three-dimensional relations changing over time, not a literal lattice in neural
tissue. Longer sequences may preserve more structured relations, but length alone is not
intelligence or consciousness.

SAN's additional hypothesis is measurable: relative timing and phase among populations carrying
visual, vestibular, somatic, memory, and action variables should predict and causally track
self-location, spatial perspective, learned continuation, and action better than matched models
using only average activity, spectral power, anatomy, or static connectivity.

The same source contains a separate physics branch. Its likely reference to Ilya Prigogine is
recovered as a comparison with open, self-maintaining physical organization. Hurricanes are useful
dissipative-structure examples; galaxies and atoms are retained as persistent-organization
analogies, not assigned automatically to the same thermodynamic class. Machines remain physical
when they are assembled rather than grown, but consciousness requires a separate measurement
program. Landauer-style physical information constraints do not settle SIT's stronger ontology.

The source was recorded September 17, 2021, first publicly Git-fixed June 8, 2022, and moved to its
stable `b0191y.md` route August 28, 2022. The later **MPRS** name is not backdated into the
recording.

## The System Produces And Reads Its Own State

Ranks 197 and 206 explain the no-inner-viewer operation in plain language:

1. sensory and internal events change activity in some neural populations;
2. other populations are selectively sensitive to parts of that changed activity;
3. their own state changes in response;
4. they project altered signals to additional populations and motor systems;
5. the changing network state affects what the organism can discriminate, predict, and do.

The same distributed system is therefore both producer and receiver. There is no extra observer
behind the cells. Camera, microphone, screen, and speaker comparisons refer only to functional
roles: capture, transform, project, and respond.

The 2021 phrase "Hertz rendering" emphasizes timing and frequency. It is retained as genealogy,
not as a claim that consciousness has one measurable frequency. "Solitone" is treated as a likely
transcription of "soliton," but no neural soliton mechanism is inferred from that word.

Volumetric video is also explicitly an analogy. A computer can maintain a larger world model while
rendering the viewpoint needed for the current camera. SAN asks whether biological systems solve
an analogous selection problem using changing body position, gaze, attention, memory, and action.
The brain does not need to represent every possible viewpoint at equal resolution at once.

## Active Perception Builds Nested Object Knowledge

Rank 205 begins with an ordinary event: turning the head changes where a box falls in the visual
field. The box has not jumped across the room. The nervous system must update the relation among
eyes, head, body, object, and surrounding scene.

The recording then decomposes the object:

```text
edges, markings, color, material, and condition
-> object identity
-> location and orientation relative to the body
-> relation to other objects and the room
-> possible movements and expected consequences
```

Repeated co-activity can strengthen relations among feature, object, context, and action
populations. Recurrent activity can reinstate part of a learned sequence; a partial cue can recruit
additional elements; and imagined outcomes can help organize a future movement.

This is not a one-neuron-per-video-frame theory. "Frame" describes a sampled relation in a changing
stream. The physical representation is distributed, overlapping, recurrent, and dependent on
coordinate transformations. The later SAN phrase **tempo-spatial pattern** names this joined
spatial-and-temporal structure after the operation has been explained.

## Attention Selects What Can Guide Action

Rank 194 asks why a detailed world model needs selection. An organism has more available sensory,
memory, and action information than can dominate behavior simultaneously. Attention changes the
relative influence of some populations while inhibition suppresses competitors.

The retained operation is:

```text
available body-and-world state
-> task and expectation
-> excitation of relevant routes
-> inhibition of competing routes
-> selected object, movement, or question
-> action and sensory return
```

The optic tectum is used as an analogy for a sensory map coupled to orienting action. Graziano's
Attention Schema Theory is a close comparison because it asks how a system models and controls
attention. SAN adds its own distributed rendering and action-route proposal, but that difference
must be assessed after each theory is represented from primary sources.

"Resolution" here means discriminative and action-usable detail. It is not a literal `32K` screen,
not a score derived from brain size, and not a direct synonym for IIT's integrated information.
A future measure would need to test how much structured distinction a system preserves and uses
under controlled perturbation.

## Cell Selection And Neural Choice Are Related But Distinct

Rank 195 asks what scientists mean when they say that a cell "decides." A cell does not need an
inner deliberator. Its receptors, signaling networks, gene expression, membrane state, morphology,
and environment jointly constrain which transition occurs next.

```text
external and neighboring signals
-> receptor and intracellular transformation
-> current cell state
-> one of several possible state transitions
-> changed outputs to neighboring cells
-> a new multicellular state
```

This is physical nonlinear computation in a functional sense. Calling the transition a "choice"
is useful only if the scale and alternatives are specified.

Neural choice adds operations not present in every cell transition: learned action routes,
inhibitory gating, prospective simulation, value, body state, report, and social responsibility.
The recording's final question about "imagined data" is therefore important. A counterfactual
scene can become a physical neural input to the next decision without becoming nonphysical.

## A Maintained State And Its Informative Updates

Ranks 202 and 207 reject the idea that better cognition means turning everything on. A network
that activates all routes equally loses the differences needed for selection. Useful operation
requires maintained context, transient updates, inhibition, recovery, and task-relative balance.

The washboard metaphor describes a transient deviation that gradually loses influence or becomes
integrated into a maintained population state. Two oscillatory groups may remain coordinated
without becoming one group. Their phases and rates can influence one another over repeated cycles,
including across frequencies.

The scientifically usable distinction is:

- a **maintained context** persists long enough to constrain interpretation and action;
- an **informative update** is a receiver-relevant change that alters that context.

SAN later calls these role-relative operations tonic and phasic, and calls a structured
receiver-relative update a candidate Phase Wave Differential. The roles are not permanently
assigned to named frequency bands. Broadband activity does not mean "slow tonic," and a phasic
event can itself contain oscillatory structure.

## Receptors, Body State, Emotion, And Drug Effects

Ranks 201 and 207 supply a bounded causal chain for psychoactive and affective change:

```text
drug, hormone, transmitter, or other ligand
-> receptor-subtype and cell-state interaction
-> ion-channel and intracellular signaling change
-> altered excitability, firing probability, timing, rate, or waveform
-> changed population dynamics
-> changed cognition, emotion, behavior, and bodily return
```

This explains why an emotion can be paired with a detectable electrophysiological shift without
reducing the emotion to one wave or one frequency band. It also explains why a person can
misattribute drug-related irritability to the current situation: the internal state has changed,
and the self-model must infer why.

The chain is a measurement program, not medical advice. Serotonin compounds differ by receptor
subtype and mechanism. Treatment of a disorder differs from enhancement of a healthy state.
Acute benefit does not establish long-term safety, and receptor effects outside a selected brain
region can matter.

## The BCI Is Also A Learned User Interface

Ranks 196, 203, and 204 connect the Neo Mind Cycle and WebXR history to a practical BCI design
question: even if a decoder and stimulator exist, how will a person learn what the coupled system
can do?

The 2021 proposal uses familiar channels as scaffolding:

- words and internally generated language;
- images, visual preferences, and spatial objects;
- sound, rhythm, and voice;
- XR scenes and body-relative controls;
- a graph of related memories, media, programs, and AI operations.

The recordings compare implanted electrodes, optogenetics, engineered tissue, fNIRS, holographic
optics, and ultrasound. These are not interchangeable. They differ in temporal precision, spatial
precision, depth, invasiveness, causal-write ability, and inverse problems. Predictions about
human optogenetics or whole-brain optical decoding remain speculative.

media are not the same as a running interactive experience. Likewise, stored neural data is not

## The Conscious Episode And The Longer-Lived Person

A changing nervous system creates a practical identity problem. Sleep, learning, injury, body
state, and new sensory input alter present activity, while memory, skills, commitments,
embodiment, and characteristic responses can persist.

The source's strongest operation is:

```text
durable learned neural and bodily organization
-> present recurrent self/world state
-> sensory and remembered differences
-> prediction and action
-> bodily and environmental consequence
-> returned evidence and plastic change
-> later recurrent episode
```

Only after that operation is clear are two terms useful. A **conscious episode** is the currently
enacted period of experience and access. **Diachronic self-continuity** is the relation that
connects one person across different episodes and physical changes.

Established sleep research shows that conscious experience can occur in REM and NREM sleep and
that effective cortical integration changes across sleep and wake states. Ensemble-reactivation,
plasticity, lesion, split-brain, and local field-cell studies support other bounded components.
They stop short of a complete identity theory: ordinary sleep does not erase the person, one
engram or field map is not the whole self, split-brain research does not provide one uncontested
subject count, and local field effects do not establish a global field self.

SAN preserves a testable extension. Personal continuity should be measured through memory,
embodiment, perspective, causal recurrence, plastic persistence, and characteristic policy rather
than exact repetition of one instantaneous state. The Field-Cell Self is the stronger hypothesis
that cellular and field-cell variables add explanatory power to that recurrent process.

The December 13, 2022 recording is a later private clarification, not pre-ChatGPT priority
evidence. Public SAN sources from June through November 2022 already fix the self-learning,
distributed observer, cellular rendering, memory, prediction, and action genealogy. The
recording's spacetime analogy remains a separate SIT branch and does not validate the neural
mechanism.

## Choice, Repair, And Cognitive Liberty

Ranks 198 and 200 begin with a practical problem. If every action has causes, it can sound as
though learning, restraint, treatment, and responsibility no longer matter. But a caused system
need not be a fixed lookup table.

```text
current conditions and learned criteria
-> new evidence or internally represented alternative
-> changed competition among responses
-> action, withholding, or redirection
-> consequence
-> changed criteria for a later choice
```

A person can encounter new evidence, imagine alternatives, inhibit a prepared response, alter a
habit, and change what becomes available next time. Eagleman and Correro's neurolaw work supplies
a bounded comparison: biological explanation need not mean exculpation, and causal understanding
can support individualized prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation. Experimental work on
intentional withholding and counterfactual representation establishes narrower component
operations.

The comparison stops before a metaphysical verdict. Premovement neural activity does not by
itself prove that choice is illusory. Brain science does not diagnose every harmful action,
determine legal responsibility, or prescribe one repair for every person. The SAN extension is
Choice Refinement: learning, internally represented alternatives, inhibition, action, and
returned consequences may change the physical criteria and effective routes that govern a later
choice.

The source also preserves the recipient of harm. Its animal comparison is useful as a
vulnerable-recipient ethical question, not as nutritional evidence. Its 2021 technology example
raises a second design problem: a system intended to detect severe harm can also affect privacy,
due process, and control over personal data. Informal prevalence and dietary-health claims are
not used.

A read/write neural system must likewise preserve mental privacy, consent, reversibility, and the
right to self-directed thought. Protection from harm and cognitive liberty are coexisting design
requirements; neither can be dismissed by calling the person a deterministic machine.

This public ethical lane predates the later SAN terminology. An August 2, 2018 multi-speaker
*Neural Lace Podcast* discussion connected neural inference and brain-computer interfaces with
privacy, identity, freedom, abuse, and regulation. Micah's March 2021 public article then joined
neuro-hacking, neuromarketing, cognitive nudging, consent, privacy, public safety, human rights,
and enforceable legislation. Rank 198 adds private authorial genealogy about self-directed
thought; it is not the public or scientific basis for the claim.

The private source body remains private. It supplies genealogy and candidate questions, not
biological proof. Eagleman's arguments, legal responsibility, clinical policy, animal welfare,
and surveillance governance retain their own evidence boundaries.

## Collective Intelligence Is Not Automatically One Mind

Rank 193 proposes that communication can magnify collective intelligence. Local observations can
alter group behavior, and network scale and connection rate can increase coordination.

That does not establish one phenomenal observer. A colony, online community, temporary
human-machine coupling, and individual brain differ in persistence, access, routing,
translation, recurrent closure, ownership, and action. Those variables must be measured before
making an identity claim.
