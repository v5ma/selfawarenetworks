## Main result

This tranche strengthens existing SAN architecture rather than establishing a wholly separate new
mechanism. Four recoveries are especially valuable:

1. `a0277z.md` states the persistent re-rendering problem: a stable percept must be maintained by
   repeated distributed updates, while the same brain both constructs and uses the state.
2. `a0282z.md` gives a compact August 2022 NAPOT exposition: neural arrays repeatedly
   sense, transform, project, and re-sense changing patterns across modalities.
3. `a0284z.md`, internally dated August 21, 2012, supplies a ten-step Neo Mind Cycle loop from EEG
   measurement through transformed light and sound, returned brain state, learned association,
   and subsequent feedback.
4. `a0296zWhisker.md` turns the whisker system into a concrete SAN testbed joining internal state,
   synchrony and desynchronization, dendritic integration, sensory-motor loops, and qualia/PWD
   hypotheses.

The whisker route may support a future experimental methods paper or a major section in PWD,
Qualia, or NAPOT. It should not receive a separate paper number until nonduplication and a
preregisterable experiment are complete.

## Persistent rendering without a hidden viewer

`a0277z.md` argues that sustained experience cannot be one neural event followed by nothing. A
usable place model must be renewed through many partially overlapping events. This aligns with the
existing sparse sequential rendering model:

```text
partial sensory and recurrent evidence
-> receiver-specific updates
-> memory- and prediction-constrained state
-> recognition or action
-> new evidence and another update
```

The phrase "the brain watches itself" is governed as causal sensitivity: one population changes
the state and behavior of other populations, and the network uses those changes. It does not add a
little viewer, a literal video, or a universal frame rate.

## Simplified NAPOT exposition

`a0282z.md` is not another numbered NAPOT revision. It is a later exposition that joins several
already developing parts:

```text
sensory transduction
-> neural arrays receive a patterned change
-> each array transforms and projects consequences
-> later arrays receive a different, context-dependent pattern
-> recurrent cycles jointly maintain perception and action
```

The eye/camera, dendrite/receiver, terminal/light, ear/microphone, and speaker metaphors are
functional analogies. Photons do not travel through the optic nerve, interneurons do not literally
see or hear, apical dendrites are not output screens, and phase variation alone has not been shown
to constitute mind or qualia.

## Neo Mind Cycle's ten-step loop

`a0284z.md` is a multi-speaker discussion between Mark Andrews and Micah Blumberg. Micah's response
lays out a concrete cycle: measure EEG, transform it into sound and isochronic stimulation, return
light and sound, measure the changed EEG, and allow the participant to learn associations between
inner state and feedback.

This supports an early **brain-self-decoded feedback** architecture. It does not establish that the
system released healthy brain chemicals, improved neuroplasticity, produced long-term happiness,
or outperformed other neurofeedback. Those claims require controlled trials and current medical
review.

## The whisker system as a SAN testbed

The source's strongest move is to combine component findings into a discriminating experimental
program. The underlying primary studies support:

- quiet wakefulness and active whisking can differ in nearby layer 2/3 membrane-potential
  correlation, LFP/EEG synchronization, and spike signal-to-noise;
- distal dendrites and layer-1 circuits can participate in fast sensory integration;
- barrel and vibrissal motor cortex form reciprocal, layer-specific sensorimotor loops; and
- whisker-evoked S1 and S2 population responses can occur in close succession under a declared
  preparation.

They do not establish that thalamus sends identical copies to all layers, that every
desynchronization is a PWD, that tonic activity "absorbs" sensory distinctions, or that the whisker
system performs NAPOT tomography.

A strong SAN test would measure and manipulate four levels separately:

```text
behavioral state and whisker movement
-> cellular and dendritic input/output
-> local coherence, dispersion, and receiver-relative change
-> object discrimination, action, and reported or inferred sensory consequence
```

The SAN extension survives only if its phase-relational or receiver-relative variables add held-out
prediction and causal control beyond movement, membrane state, firing rate, power, ordinary
connectivity, and standard sensorimotor-loop models.
