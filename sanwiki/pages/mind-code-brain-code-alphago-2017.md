## Position

The essay's core conceit is the *Go-board metaphor* for cortical computation: black pieces represent excited blocks, white pieces represent inhibited blocks (or the reverse), and the *shape* of a thought is therefore set as much by what is *inhibited* as by what is *excited* — "the shape of our thoughts, in tempo-spatial patterns is represented not only by what is excited in our brains but also by what is inhibited in our brains" (`a0250z--md`, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2017-06-30, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/main/a0250z.md)). The metaphor is the seed of several SAN cluster constructs: [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves|criterial tempo-spatial waves]], [[beliefs-as-tempo-spatial-sequences|beliefs as tempo-spatial sequences]], [[inhibitory-error-correction|inhibitory error correction]], [[inhibitory-interneurons-plasticity-gating|inhibitory interneurons / plasticity gating]], [[receptor-inhibition-pattern|receptor-inhibition-pattern]], and the [[layer-6-inhibitory-intuition|layer-6 inhibitory intuition]] page. The essay also explicitly names the *ionotropic-vs-metabotropic* dual-brain framing that the later SAN cluster ([[ionotropic-signaling]], [[metabotropic-signaling]], [[synaptic-ion-channel-tone]], [[tonic-phasic-neural-oscillation]]) elaborates: "we have an ionotropic brain (electromagnetic waves interacting with electromagnetic waves) and a metabotropic brain (the chemical interacts of the brain that interact with other chemical interactions in the brain)" (`a0250z--md`, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2017-06-30, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/main/a0250z.md)). ## Post-SAN

Mainstream 2017-era discourse around AlphaGo focused on the ML-architecture story (deep convolutional networks + Monte Carlo tree search + [[gh-a0206z|self]]-play); the [[consciousness]]-and-cognition implications were largely deferred. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s essay takes the opposite move — treating AlphaGo as the empirical-ML-anchor for an essentially *speculative* thesis about cortical computation, unconscious-conscious gating, multi-band oscillatory processing, and the prospect of intracerebral measurement ([[neural-lace|Neural Lace]]) being able to discriminate among the candidate hypotheses. The essay's enduring SAN-side commitments are three: first, that the *contrast structure* of [[consciousness]] — what is inhibited matters as much as what is excited — is constitutive, not decorative ([[inhibitory-error-correction]], [[inhibitory-decay-oscillation-frequency]]); second, that the [[cortex]] runs *concurrently* on at least two timescales (fast ionotropic + slow metabotropic), with the slow side providing the state context for the fast side ([[tonic-phasic-canvas-of-consciousness]]); third, that the *[[gh-a0206z|self]]* is a feedback-loop construct hosted in rich-club circuits rather than a localised seat ([[self-as-oscillatory-construct]], [[neural-strange-loop-self-referential-consciousness]]). The essay's final paragraph also names the SAN-style claim that *information has physical existence in the brain* — "experiences, are located in our brains, as electromagnetic waves, with spatial and temporal characteristics, frequency patterns with charge, spin, and all the other properties that can be assigned to highly coordinated electromagnetic waves in a brain" — a 2017-dated prefiguration of [[electromagnetic-theory-of-feeling]], [[electromagnetic-emotions]], and [[consciousness-energy-wave-differences]] (`a0250z--md`, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2017-06-30, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/main/a0250z.md)).
