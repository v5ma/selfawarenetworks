# Protein Synthesis

**Protein Synthesis** in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] is the molecular act by which transient electrical and calcium events at a synapse are converted into durable structural change — new receptors, new dendritic-spine geometry, and altered protein composition — and is thus the substrate that turns short-lived oscillatory activity into long-term [[engram|engrams]]. SAN treats it as the bridge layer between [[hebbian-learning|Hebbian]] firing rules at the [[chemical-synapse|synapse]] and the [[ltp-structural-memory-encoding|long-term potentiation]] story that underwrites memory, and (in NAPOT-aligned readings) further proposes that the new proteins themselves are calibrated by [[magnetic-phase-field|magnetic spin]] state.
