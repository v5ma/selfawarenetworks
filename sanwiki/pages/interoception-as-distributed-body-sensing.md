# Interoception as Distributed Body Sensing

> **Scientific boundary:** Interoception is not one vagal cable, one insular map, one arousal value, or one consciousness signal. It includes multiple organ-specific measurements, spinal and cranial routes, humoral and immune influences, reflex control, bodily feelings, and context-dependent interactions with perception and action.

Interoception concerns sensing and regulating conditions inside the body. Heartbeat and blood pressure, airway closure and inflation, stomach stretch, gut osmolality, bladder filling, temperature, inflammation, and metabolic need are different biological problems.

## Established component architecture

Zhao and colleagues found that vagal sensory populations distinguish organ, tissue layer, and stimulus modality along partly separable dimensions in mice ([2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04515-5)). The vagus is therefore a heterogeneous family of routes rather than a generic internal-status wire.

Defined pathways illustrate why the distinctions matter:

- cardiac vagal neurons can drive the Bezold-Jarisch reflex in mice ([Lovelace et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06680-7));
- PIEZO2-expressing airway neuroepithelial cells and a defined vagal pathway are required for a closure-evoked gasping reflex in mice ([Schappe et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07144-2));
- stomach-distension signals participate in negative feedback controlling ingestion in mice ([Kim et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2167-2)); and
- hepatic-portal-area signals contribute to pre-absorptive thirst satiation ([Ichiki et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04359-5)).

A powerful reflex is not the same as a consciously felt heartbeat, breath, fullness, or thirst. Receptor activity, physiological regulation, urge, report, and phenomenal quality occupy different evidence levels.

## The insula is important but not an inner body screen

Khalsa and colleagues studied a rare participant with extensive bilateral insula and anterior-cingulate damage. Ordinary heartbeat awareness was impaired, while strong pharmacologically induced cardiovascular arousal could still produce vivid heartbeat sensation, supporting additional routes including somatosensory input ([2009](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2411)).

This case does not make the insula unnecessary. It rejects the stronger claim that all interoceptive awareness must pass through one indispensable cortical center.

## Internal and external evidence interact

Prestimulus heartbeat-evoked responses predicted later visual detection in one human paradigm ([Park et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3671)). Pharmacologically changing cardiorespiratory state can also change bodily sensation and insular activity ([Hassanpour et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/npp.2017.154)).

These findings do not show that the heartbeat contains visual information or that cardiac timing is a universal gate for consciousness.

## SAN interpretation

SAN can model interoception as part of distributed observer-action: specialized receptors register internal changes; peripheral, spinal, brainstem, autonomic, endocrine, immune, thalamic, cortical, and bodily processes transform them at different scales; each receiver interprets an update relative to maintained state; some updates alter reflexes without report while others affect urge, attention, memory, valuation, ownership, or action; and the changed organism produces the next conditions.

In tonic/phasic language, tonic activity is the receiver-specific physiological and task context; phasic activity is a consequential departure registered against it. This is a proposed operational interpretation, not evidence for one global electromagnetic body canvas or universal frequency code.

## Scale map

- **Micro:** channels, receptors, sensory endings, epithelial cells, immune signals, synapses, and cellular state.
- **Meso:** organ-specific afferent pathways, spinal and brainstem reflex circuits, hypothalamic, thalamic, insular, somatosensory, autonomic, and recurrent systems.
- **Macro:** the organism coordinating physiological regulation, felt bodily state, valuation, perception, and action.

## Measurement contract

Declare the organ and physical variable; receptor or transducer; afferent and central route; receiving population and reference state; physiological, neural, behavioral, and report endpoints; expected lag; matched autonomic, movement, arousal, and exteroceptive controls; selective perturbation; component-specific loss; rescue; and failure condition.

No single heartbeat task, insular BOLD response, vagal manipulation, spectral band, or subjective rating can establish the entire chain.
