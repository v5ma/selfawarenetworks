The **[[gh-a0206z|self]]** is not the brain, body, memories, or beliefs — it is the active feedback loop
between three physical components: [[senses]], [[brainwaves]], and [[actions]].

## Core Model

The loop runs in one direction, continuously:

> Senses pull in information → form brainwaves → trigger muscles (movement, sound) →
> which are sensed again → updating brainwaves → completing the loop.

This is a **learning loop** because each pass updates the statistical model used to
predict what happens next. Those predictions *are* thoughts. The loop does not merely
process; it models itself — hence the term [[self-awareness]].

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s 2011 [[gh-formulation|formulation]] (verbatim):

> "There are three components to the loop of you: your senses, your brainwaves, and
> your [[actions]]. Your senses pull in information to form brainwaves which trigger your
> muscles to make movement and sound which get pulled in by your senses to update your
> brainwaves creating a loop. This is the loop of you."

## Key Properties

- **Hardware-independent.** The loop requires senses, brainwaves (a predictive model),
  and [[actions]] — not any specific biological substrate. Mechanical machines can instantiate it.
  [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] cites Numenta (Jeff Hawkins' [[memory-prediction-framework]]) as a working example.
- **[[gh-a0206z|Self]]-referential by definition.** The loop learns itself, and that [[gh-a0206z|self]]-reference is
  what the word "[[gh-a0206z|self]]" points to. See Hofstadter's *I Am a Strange Loop* for parallel
  philosophical treatment.
- **Physical.** [[Brainwaves]] are measurable (EEG); thoughts are physical electrical events,
  not mystical. [[Neurofeedback]] gives direct experience of the loop.
- **Species-general.** Dogs and some other animals have a sufficient loop to count as
  [[gh-a0206z|self]]-aware; simpler organisms (worms, crabs) likely lack the predictive layer.

## Relation to Memory-Prediction

The loop's substrate is a [[memory-prediction-framework]]: experiences are sorted into
statistical memory → used to generate predictions → predictions manifest as brainwaves.
This aligns with Hawkins' Hierarchical Temporal Memory model and anticipates SAN's later
[[NAPOT]] framing of oscillatory [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling.

## Distinction from "No-Self" Views

The loop *can believe it doesn't exist*, but the belief is itself a product of the loop.
The loop is still there. "No-[[gh-a0206z|self]]" is a belief-based interpretation of experience, not a
disproof of the loop's existence.

## History

This [[gh-formulation|formulation]] appeared in a public online discussion (pre-Sep 2011) and represents
an early, conversational articulation of what later became a formal SAN theoretical
pillar. The three-component vocabulary (senses / brainwaves / [[actions]]) maps directly
onto the input / internal-state / output triad in the SAN whitepaper.
