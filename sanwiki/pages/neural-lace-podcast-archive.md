# Neural Lace Podcast Archive

The Neural Lace Podcast Archive is the source index for Micah Blumberg's 2017-2020 public
conversations about brain-computer interfaces, VR, neurophysics, neural computation and medical
imaging. The archive is no longer an unacquired placeholder: eleven transcript files, public
recording routes, per-episode proof pages and several processed episode summaries are present on
disk.

## Coverage and gaps

The recovered dates run from April 11, 2017 to May 12, 2020. The old “2017-2019” bundle name is
incomplete because `nlps2e3.md` is a 2020 optical-imaging episode. No `nlp6.md` appears in the
downloaded family, so Episode 6 remains a recovery question rather than an inferred missing text.

## Use rules

- Use the public recording for exact wording and speaker attribution.
- Use the raw transcript for local full-text search.
- Use the proof page for date, URL, bundle identity and preserved extraction.
- Use processed SAN pages for interpretation and cross-concept navigation.
- Do not treat a transcript date as biological validation or merge guest and host statements.
