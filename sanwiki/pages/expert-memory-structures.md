## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature on expertise and skill memory (Ericsson, Chase, Simon, Bilalić) treats expert performance as the result of stored chunks, retrieval structures, and long-term working memory. SAN agrees that experts have access to fast, structured patterns; it reframes the *substrate* of those patterns as fractal oscillator subnetworks rather than as a chunk-store.
