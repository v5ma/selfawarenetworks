## Position

Phase Space Encoding tightens the [[oscillatory-neural-coding]] picture in one specific direction. The neural-coding question — what does a population's activity *encode*? — is answered, in the SAN reading, by pointing at where the population's joint state currently sits in its own [[phase-space-dynamics]] manifold. A single neuron's [[action-potential-phase-space]] traces a Hodgkin–Huxley loop; a coupled population's state lives in a higher-dimensional manifold whose coordinates are the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential]] / [[phase-wave-differentials]] between member [[oscillators]]; and "encoding" means: the *position* in that manifold is the message, and the [[neural-phase-pattern-propagation]] dynamics over time are the language. Within this picture, [[criterial-causation]] readers do not count spikes — they recognise points (or short trajectories) in phase space against a [[criterial-thresholds]] template, and [[neural-ensemble-phase-reset-communication]] events are how upstream populations *write* a new position into the downstream manifold via a [[phase-reset]] pulse. The [[brain-as-spectral-graph]] reading then re-states the whole thing as encoding-on-the-Laplacian-spectrum, with [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators]] supplying the multi-scale structure mainstream rate-coding models tend to flatten. The [[holographic-brain-distributed-memory]] page is the storage-side companion: a memory is not a stored pattern at a location, it is a region of phase space the system can re-enter.
