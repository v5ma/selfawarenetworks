# a0058z

Original source note timestamp: Sep 3, 2012, 3:43 AM.

The source note describes [Neo Mind Cycle](neo-mind-cycle.md) as a personal "brain optimization" program that combines [brainwave entrainment](brainwave-entrainment.md), neurofeedback, mind-machine hardware, and responsive light-and-sound feedback. It says the system lets a person change light and sound beats through feelings and thoughts, and it presents the intended results as stronger internal connections, intelligence, creativity, confidence, and performance. These statements are preserved as the 2012 source author's claims, not as independently verified medical or therapeutic claims.

The note also says the process can bring psychological material to the surface, including developmental issues and family-pattern material. The author describes the year of use as therapeutic and personally maturing, while also saying that the technology should be used with a strong supportive therapist. Claims about autism, ADHD, PTSD, addiction, or other health conditions remain source claims that would need separate clinical support before being treated as medical evidence.

The described setup uses a head-worn unit to measure brainwaves and send the data into software that controls isochronic light-and-sound patterns. According to the note, changes in mood, attention, or imagined future events may alter qualities such as brightness, pitch, reverb, or beat speed.

Electroencephalogram, or EEG, is described as an integral part of the custom process. The source contrasts ordinary EEG interfaces that show graphs, sounds, colors, or games with the author's own combination of EEG feedback and [brainwave entrainment](brainwave-entrainment.md). It describes the entrainment as dynamic because the light-and-sound patterns change in response to EEG data, especially within [Neo Mind Cycle](neo-mind-cycle.md).

The author states that the settings are adjustable and that the system was improved after additional self-directed study of the brain. The note presents the service as an in-person offering in San Francisco that combines multiple pieces of equipment, software, techniques, tested procedures, and the author's education and experience.
