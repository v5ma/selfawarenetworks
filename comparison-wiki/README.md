## Scope and chronology

Certification here is the Comparison Wiki project’s bounded source/editorial protocol, not human scientific peer review or validation of an entire theory. The selected cards do not replace the larger private evidence ledger or imply that unselected work has no matches.

The Git commit records when this snapshot was made public. It does not backdate any statement or establish first publication of the underlying ideas. Private working data, source PDFs, chat exports and operational review files are excluded.
