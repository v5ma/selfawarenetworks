It depicts narrative, volumetric visual, and audio-body streams being bound by one thalamocortical phase process into a unified conscious scene. There is no established central binder or universal phase-locking event that performs this operation.

The **multimodal rendering loop** is SAN's proposal that a biological system maintains a usable body-world model by repeatedly coordinating modality-specific evidence, memory, prediction, valuation, and action.

## Multimodal does not mean one fused screen

Vision, audition, somatosensation, proprioception, interoception, olfaction, and vestibular processing begin with different receptors and pathways. Their signals are transformed across cortical and subcortical systems and related through learned, task-dependent interactions. Some information is combined; some remains modality-specific; some never becomes reportable.

The thalamus is not one scene compositor. Its nuclei have heterogeneous relay, state, attention, and recurrent roles, and olfactory pathways are a major exception to the simple pre-cortical thalamic relay story. Cortical association, hippocampal, basal-ganglia, cerebellar, brainstem, and body loops also contribute.

Narrative is not a universal prerequisite for consciousness. Infants, nonlinguistic animals, and nonverbal human experiences make clear that language-like stories are at most one higher-level process within a broader model.

## SAN interpretation

The rendering metaphor becomes useful when translated into operations:

- receptors and local circuits provide partial evidence;
- recurrent populations preserve and revise relations;
- body coordinates constrain possible interpretations and actions;
- memory and prediction supply learned context;
- downstream systems use the resulting distinctions; and
- new action and sensory consequences update the next cycle.

The network is both producer and user of these relations. No separate viewer or final scene surface is added.

NAPOT proposes cross-population reconstruction, NDCA proposes continuity-preserving approximation, and GCS proposes circuit-specific timing and routing. None is established as the unique multimodal binding mechanism or a completed solution to phenomenal consciousness.

## Tests

Choose one cross-modal relation, such as audiovisual timing or visuo-proprioceptive body location. Declare the contributing pathways, measured variables, receivers, behavioral consequence, and matched unimodal alternatives. Perturb one route and predict both impairment and rescue. A phase-aware model must add held-out explanatory value beyond stimulus statistics, attention, movement, and recurrent connectivity.
