# Oscillatory Dynamics

**Oscillatory Dynamics** is the time-varying, frequency-structured behavior of the brain's electromagnetic activity — the [[brainwave]] regime treated as a computational primitive rather than a side effect. Within [[self-aware-networks]] oscillatory dynamics is the load-bearing observable: it is what [[predictive-coding]] runs on, what [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] reads out, what [[cortical-traveling-waves|traveling waves]] propagate, and what [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] reinterprets at the field-theoretic level. The SAN claim is not that oscillations correlate with cognition but that they constitute the [[consciousness-as-oscillatory-feedback-loop|oscillatory feedback loop]] of cognition itself.
