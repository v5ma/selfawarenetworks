## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the planetary-scale slug is the natural upper limit of a single move applied recursively: at every scale at which an [[oscillating-network-of-oscillators]] forms, the [[brain-as-spectral-graph]] coupling story applies, and the [[distributed-phase-representations]] / [[distributed-cognition]] / [[distributed-consciousness]] consequences track. At the within-brain scale the coupling produces a single observer's [[phenomenological-consciousness]]. At the cross-agent scale ([[oscillatory-coupling-multi-agent]], [[inter-brain-eeg-synchronization]], [[brain-to-brain-eeg-synchronization]], [[holonic-rhythm-social-bonding]]) it produces the kind of group-level integration that [[neural-synchronization-bonding-analogy]] formalises and that [[artificial-telepathy-via-oscillatory-synchrony]] takes as a constructive target. Planetary Scale Networks is what you get when those scales are not treated as separate: a network of communicating biological brains, AI agents, and the [[cellular-communication-graph-neural-network]]-style infrastructure that links them, all participating in [[cross-scale-oscillation-communication]] dynamics.
