This SAN plate maps phasic burst firing as an informative deviation from tonic baseline: a fast burst cluster perturbs the slower tonic canvas, contributes to phase-wave differentials and slow-wave potentials, then relaxes back toward tonic rhythm.

**Phasic Burst Firing** is the [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks.md) term for short, fast, irregular firing events that *deviate* from a neuron's regular [tonic-oscillation](tonic-oscillation.md) baseline, and within SAN it is treated not as noise but as one of the three coordinated signals — alongside the [phase-wave-differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md) and the [slow-wave potentials](slow-cortical-potentials-napot-revision-3.md) — by which the [brain-as-rendering-engine](brain-as-rendering-engine.md) renders its own state to itself. It is the dynamic companion of [tonic-phasic-neural-oscillation](tonic-phasic-neural-oscillation.md) and the close kin of [burst-event](burst-event.md), [burstlets](burstlets.md), [neural-burst-backpropagation](neural-burst-backpropagation.md), and [neuronal-sequences-population-bursts](neuronal-sequences-population-bursts.md).

## Post-SAN

Mainstream neurophysiology already distinguishes tonic from phasic / burst firing modes — the thalamic burst-vs-tonic literature (Sherman, Llinás), the burst-coding work in cerebellar climbing fibres, and the bursting literature in [dopamine](dopamine.md) and noradrenergic systems are all canonical. The [Self](gh-a0206z.md)-Aware Networks reading converges on those distinctions but does two things the published literature tends not to. First, it treats the *deviation from tonic* as the representational primitive rather than the burst itself: *"The brain renders its phasic burst firing patterns as deviations from its tonic firing, demonstrating its capacity to process and represent information at different scales."* (`documents-legacy--ChatGPT2025June17`, line 242669, note, 2025) — the rendering frame rather than the burst-count frame. Second, the [gh-a0048z](gh-a0048z.md) thermodynamic-cost argument is sharper than mainstream burst-coding accounts: SAN claims that slower oscillations are *necessarily disturbed* by phasic bursts because fast non-oscillating information is thermodynamically expensive, and that this disturbance is itself part of the rendering mechanism rather than an artefact to be averaged out. The [gh-a0309z](gh-a0309z.md) / `dl-oct6-2025--part_1` (paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md), 2023) electric-fish frequency-band example — tonic at 0–400 Hz, phasic burst at 150–600 Hz — is the kind of concrete, falsifiable comparison case that puts the SAN reading on testable ground: the prediction is that burst-band power should be *consistently above* the tonic upper edge across species in which sensory-burst coding is documented, and that the deviation magnitude should track the rendering load on the slower bands. This page does not yet ground that prediction beyond the 25 packet quotes; widening the Lane B corpus is the next step.
