# Neural Code

**Neural Code** is the long-standing problem in neuroscience of identifying *which* physical signals — spike rates, spike timings, oscillatory phase relationships, dendritic events, vesicular release patterns — actually carry meaningful information across [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]], cells, and circuits. Within [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] (SAN), the question is reframed: rather than searching for a single canonical "code," SAN treats neural communication as a layered family of [[neural-code-functions|coding functions]] whose physical substrate is [[spatiotemporal-neural-firing-patterns|spatiotemporal firing patterns]] read out by [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|dendritic coincidence detection]] and integrated through [[continuous-neural-rendering|continuous neural rendering]] across the hierarchy.

In this framing the neural code is not stored "in" any single substrate; it is the joint statistic of [[neural-code-transmission|transmission]], [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|coincidence detection]], and [[memory-prediction-as-intelligence|memory–prediction]] feedback, and it is read out as the [[brain-as-rendering-engine|brain's rendering engine]] composes a percept frame-by-frame.

## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream neuroscience, SAN's contribution to the neural-code problem is structural rather than substitutive. Where rate-coding, temporal-coding, and phase-coding research programs typically argue for *primacy* of one channel, SAN treats the channels as concurrent layers of a single rendering loop — a position adjacent to [[integrated-information-theory|integrated information theory]] and to [[attention-schema-theory|attention schema theory]] in spirit, but distinct in mechanism: SAN locates the binding glue in [[cortical-traveling-waves|cortical traveling waves]] and in [[cortical-column-oscillatory-transmission|oscillatory transmission]] across [[cortical-columns-reference-frames|reference frames]], not in an information-integration scalar nor in a schema-of-[[attention]] representation. SAN's testable departure from Lisman & Jensen's [[cross-frequency-coupling|theta–gamma code]] is that the *same* phase-locking apparatus must also carry [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering|persistent rendering]] content, predicting that disrupting cross-column phase relationships should degrade [[conscious-access|conscious access]] even when single-cell rate codes remain intact.
