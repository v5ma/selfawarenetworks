# Nested Oscillating Expert Data Structures

This historical SAN phrase names the proposed nesting of learned cellular transformations inside
oscillating groups and larger arrays. The governed treatment is
[[expert-data-structures|Expert Data Structures]].

The nested claim is stronger than ordinary dendritic computation. It predicts that declared
relations remain usable across a bounded change in carrier, timing, phase, viewpoint, or scale.
That prediction must be tested against point-neuron, population-code, recurrent-network, and
state-space baselines. It does not follow merely from synchrony, dendritic nonlinearity, or the
existence of extracellular vesicles.

The earlier statement that sleep clears short-term memory is not retained. The cited source was a
model of activity-regime-dependent synaptic-weight dynamics, not evidence that sleep erases human
short-term memory.
