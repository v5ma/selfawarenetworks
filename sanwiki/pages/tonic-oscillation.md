A **tonic oscillation** is sustained or recurring neural activity treated as a reference over a
declared time window. In SAN, tonic language helps describe the ongoing conditions against which a
phasic change becomes consequential. It does not name one universal rhythm or the ground of pure
awareness.

## Biological definition

The relevant ongoing state may include membrane potential, synaptic conductance, firing
probability, adaptation, inhibition, neuromodulation, population activity, oscillatory timing, and
the extracellular fields produced by participating cells. Some neurons fire tonically in particular
states; others are silent, irregular, bursting, or changing mode. A brain-wide shared tonic phase is
not required.

## SAN interpretation

SAN calls the relatively maintained field-cell state a **tonic context** or **tonic action field**.
A phasic event changes that context, and the same distributed system carries the consequence into
later recognition, prediction, autonomic control, sampling, or action. The observer claim belongs to
that complete receive-transform-use loop, not to tonic oscillation alone.

## What must be measured

A tonic-oscillation claim should name:

1. the cells, circuit, and behavioral state;
2. the recorded signal and reference;
3. the frequency range and duration;
4. whether the rhythm is local, propagated, coherent, or merely common-input driven;
5. the receiver and consequence of a departure;
6. alternatives such as arousal, movement, waveform shape, and aperiodic activity.

## Scale firewall

- **Micro:** sustained membrane or firing mode in a named cell or compartment.
- **Meso:** relatively maintained population or circuit state.
- **Macro:** distributed brain-body context inferred from multiple measurements.

The same word does not make these states one oscillator.
