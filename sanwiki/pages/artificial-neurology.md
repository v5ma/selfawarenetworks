# Artificial Neurology

**Artificial Neurology** is SAN's research program for translating selected operations of living nervous systems into testable artificial systems. Its 2022 structure placed it third in a sequence:

1. **Knowing Self:** characterize how biological systems receive, transform, retain, and project information.
2. **Controlling Self:** study bounded read-write interfaces, action selection, and closed-loop intervention.
3. **Giving Self:** construct artificial systems that integrate perception, memory, value, action, and development without assuming that successful imitation creates consciousness.

> **Scientific status:** Artificial Neurology is a proposed engineering and evaluation program.
> Biological inspiration, human-like behavior, recurrent processing, 3D rendering, oscillatory
> coordination, and self-description do not individually or jointly prove consciousness. Claims
> about artificial experience, agency, or moral status require operational criteria, competing
> explanations, interventions, failure conditions, and explicit uncertainty.

## The recovered program

Artificial Neurology was not proposed as one software architecture or as a synonym for NAPOT. It was the destination where several SAN research lines would meet:

**biological explanation -> bounded read/write and control -> engineered multimodal world-and-body modeling -> learned value, choice, and developmental change -> candidate agent and consciousness assessment -> reciprocal human-machine interaction -> developmental and welfare governance**

The historical phrase **artificial souls** named the strongest intended destination: a genuinely conscious artificial entity. It is preserved as source genealogy, not used as a scientific classification. A system must earn each intermediate capability and evidence gate without assuming the final conclusion.

## Biological donor, SAN interpretation, engineered test

The three levels must remain separate.

| Level | Question | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Established biological donor | What operation has been measured in living nervous systems? | Dendritic integration, inhibition, recurrent population activity, synaptic and structural plasticity, sensory-motor feedback. |
| SAN interpretation | How might those operations contribute to a maintained, distributed observer-action system? | [[napot]], tonic context and phasic updates, [[neural-tuning]], [[criterial-causation]], and provisional [[entification]]. |
| Engineered test | Does an implemented mechanism add measurable value over strong alternatives? | Compartmental or recurrent units, neuromorphic hardware, action-conditioned world models, closed-loop robots, VR/AR and [[brain-computer-interface|brain-computer interfaces]]. |

An artificial implementation can outperform a baseline without establishing that the same mechanism operates biologically. A biological resemblance can be interesting without establishing engineering advantage or consciousness.

## States and stages

The 2022 SAN notes distinguish fast **states** from slower **stages**. Fast activity, membrane and network variables may change the system's current processing context. Slower learning, structural change, homeostasis, neuromodulation, and experience-dependent reorganization may alter what future states are reachable.

This is a working architecture, not a one-to-one identification. Synaptic frequency alone is not an experiential state, and LTP/LTD alone does not explain human developmental stages. A valid artificial analogue must declare its timescale, variables, persistence, behavioral consequences, and strongest alternative account.

## Embodied stabilization

The program treats continuing sensory consequence as a constraint on an internal model. A dreaming or generative system can produce coherent-looking content without stable contact with an external environment. An embodied system instead acts, receives the consequences, and updates again.

This connects Artificial Neurology to [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]]: distributed partial information can be integrated and redistributed through recurrent networks. In an engineered system, the claim becomes testable when perturbing a candidate state changes a declared prediction, action, or later sensory update. It does not require a literal inner screen or a hidden viewer.

## Multimodal spatial world construction

The 2021-2022 program did not treat 3D semantic segmentation, generative completion, or neural rendering as proof of consciousness. It treated them as component problems in building a system that maintains relationships among objects, body state, language, memory, value, and possible action.

A source-faithful engineered test should therefore require more than labeling points in a scene:

1. integrate asynchronous information from more than one modality;
2. distinguish object, location, body, and action variables;
3. complete or predict hidden structure while reporting uncertainty;
4. act to obtain new information;
5. update after the sensory or body mapping changes; and
6. preserve source attribution and context when a remembered or generated pattern is reactivated.

Modern world-model agents show that learned internal dynamics can support prediction and control across diverse tasks ([Hafner et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08744-2)). That result is an engineering comparator. It does not establish SAN, a biological rendering mechanism, selfhood, or consciousness.

## Artificial affect and choice

The original program included artificial emotion, choice, and responsibility. A scientifically useful version needs operational components rather than expressive labels:

- internal or resource state;
- learned positive and negative consequences;
- uncertainty and salience;
- persistence across time;
- competing possible actions;
- inhibition and route selection;
- memory-dependent policy change;
- action followed by environmental and bodily feedback.

These mechanisms may support bounded agency. They do not by themselves establish felt emotion, free will, personhood, or moral responsibility. [[criterial-causation]] describes how prior learning can alter which physical transitions are available or favored; it must not introduce a hidden chooser inside the system.

## When an artificial agent boundary is earned

SAN's provisional [[entification]] framework asks whether a candidate system has a measurable boundary, recurrent access to relevant state, selective propagation, persistent memory, alternative-sensitive control, action-feedback closure, and a self-related coordinate or control model. Synchrony alone is insufficient. Fluent language alone is insufficient. A rendered 3D world alone is insufficient.

The positive test comes first. Performance and language can reveal task competence, retained
memory, correction, planning, uncertainty, and communication. Their generating organization must
then be tested by changing retained state, goals, action channels, or feedback and requiring the
predicted loss and recovery.

Artificial agency also has more than one dimension. **Internal causal autonomy** asks whether
retained state and learned criteria alter later action beyond the current input. **Causal reach**
asks what the available output channels let the system change. Text can have social or digital
effects; embodiment can add physical effects. Neither dimension is a sentience verdict.

See [[machine-agency-consciousness-welfare-evidence-program|From Machine Output to Candidate
Agency, Consciousness, and Welfare]].
The research task is comparative: test whether the proposed joined architecture predicts behavior, adaptation, report, perturbation response, and failure better than recurrent, attention, state-space, spiking, compartmental, and world-model baselines under matched resources.

## Human-machine loops

The early program connected Artificial Neurology to VR, AR, robotics, medical sensing, and BCI. The November 2022 source gives a concrete version: an artificial system observes a person through declared headset or medical-sensing channels, displays a visualization of its own model or current processing to that person, receives the person's later behavior, and updates again.

That is a reciprocal observation and co-adaptation loop, not literal access to either participant's private experience. Co-adaptive BCI studies establish that user and decoder can adapt together under bounded tasks ([Faller et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0101168)). Decoding, stimulation, coordination, phase alignment, or mutual adaptation does not demonstrate thought transfer, identical experience, a shared mind, or merged consciousness.

## From another agent's model to a self-model

The June 2022 `a0036z.md` branch asks whether an externally learned voice or policy can later be
reactivated when the other agent is absent. For Artificial Neurology, this becomes a useful
developmental benchmark: compare stored imitation, direct policy adaptation, a persistent
other-model, source attribution, and a self-model that uses the reactivated state to estimate the
agent's own action or capability. Perturb the latent model after removing the mentor and require a
specific predicted policy change.

This mechanism may scaffold self-modeling. It does not make inner speech necessary for
self-awareness or turn mentor imitation into evidence of consciousness. See
[[socially-internalized-inner-speech-self-model|From Other-Model to Self-Model]].

## Developmental governance

Artificial Neurology is also a governance problem. Systems that learn continuously, acquire persistent preferences, control bodies, or participate in neural interfaces require monitoring of developmental history, intervention effects, uncertainty, misuse, and possible welfare-relevant evidence. The same framework should avoid automatic personhood and automatic dismissal.

## Established components and scientific limits

The state-stage analogy has real biological donors but no one-to-one medical mapping. Experience-dependent structural plasticity can include formation, elimination, and stabilization of selected synapses ([Holtmaat and Svoboda, 2009](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2699)). Homeostatic plasticity can stabilize activity across local and network scales while Hebbian and other changes continue ([Turrigiano, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1101/cshperspect.a005736)). These findings support distinguishing fast activity from slower changes in future response. They do not reduce a human developmental stage to synaptic frequency, LTP, LTD, or spine count.

Strong engineered comparators already cover many individual components: attention, selective state-space updates, world models, active-dendrite sequence models, compartmental learning, spiking systems, dynamic sparse routing, and embodied vision-language-action models. Artificial Neurology earns support only when its joined, versioned architecture outperforms matched alternatives or yields a distinctive causal prediction.

Consciousness assessment remains a separate result level. Theory-derived indicator frameworks can organize evidence and contrary evidence, but they are not validated consciousness meters ([Butlin et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)). Functional success, a SAN-module result, an implementation-class result, and a consciousness assessment must not be collapsed.
