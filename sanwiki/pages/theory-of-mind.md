# Theory Of Mind

**Theory Of Mind**, in this wiki, names the [Self Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) *Theory of Mind* -- [Micah Blumberg](micah-blumberg.md)'s mechanical-philosophy-of-mind framework for explaining how brains generate [consciousness](consciousness.md) through oscillatory, [phase-wave-differential](phase-wave-differentials.md), and [NAPOT](napot-overview.md)-tomographic mechanisms. This usage is distinct from the cognitive-science notion of "theory of mind" as the *mentalizing* capacity to attribute beliefs to others; SAN's "Theory of Mind" is a substrate-level mechanical theory of how *any* mind works, in the philosophy-of-mind sense, with the mentalizing capacity being one downstream consequence among many.

## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream "theory of mind" usage, SAN's *Theory of Mind* operates at a different level. First, the academic ToM literature (Premack & Woodruff 1978 lineage; mentalizing-network [neuroscience](neuroscience.md)) treats ToM as a *capacity* -- the ability to attribute mental states -- and the SAN page does *not* claim to be that. Second, SAN's framework is in the lineage of *mechanical* / *unified* theories of mind ([Hawkins](hierarchical-temporal-memory.md) PRTM, Grossberg unified theory, Friston [free-energy](free-energy-principle.md) formulations, [OIT](information-theory.md)) -- `01san--md`, line 1, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md), explicitly positions SAN as a "branch of OIT". Third, SAN's distinguishing commitments -- [Backpropagation Through Oscillation](backpropagation.md), [Fractal Oscillatory Predictive Coding](predictive-coding.md), [NAPOT](napot-overview.md), the [dominant phase-wave differential](dominant-phase-wave-differential.md) -- make falsifiable predictions about oscillatory structure and [cortical column](cortical-columns.md) dynamics that the broader unified-theory family does not. The Kuhn 2024 paper-external entry (line not specified in packet) shows the type of "mind" landscape SAN is contributing to, and the 2017 history-of-SIT entry shows that SAN's specific name and content date from a documented mid-2022 coinage rather than retrospective rebranding.
