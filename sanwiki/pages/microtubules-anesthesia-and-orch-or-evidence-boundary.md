# Microtubules, Anesthesia, and Orch OR: Evidence Boundary

This bridge separates established neuronal microtubule physiology from three stronger proposals: microtubule memory, a biologically used quantum channel, and [[orch-or|Orch OR]] as a theory of consciousness. The same experiment can be relevant to several proposals without proving all of them.

## The claim ladder

| Level | Claim | Representative evidence | Do not infer yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Physical structure | Neurons contain dynamic microtubules | Cell biology and imaging | Memory, computation, or consciousness |
| 1. Cellular function | Microtubules support transport, polarity, morphology, and remodeling | Established neuronal physiology | A content-bearing code |
| 2. Plasticity participation | Dynamic microtubules can transiently enter some actin-rich spines and affect postsynaptic organization | [Jaworski et al., 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2008.11.013); [Hu et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2445-11.2011) | An autonomous microtubule memory store |
| 3. Preparation-specific biophysics | Microtubule preparations show defined ionic oscillation, energy-migration, or collective-emission effects | [Cantero et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30453-2); [Kalra et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.2c01114); [Babcock et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.3c07936) | An intact-neuron code, a source of scalp EEG, or in-vivo neural quantum computation |
| 4. Anesthetic association | Microtubule-modulating interventions change rodent isoflurane loss-of-righting-reflex measures in some experiments | [Khan et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0291-24.2024); [Li et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-02956-9); [Huang et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropharm.2026.110834) | Direct measurement of conscious content or objective reduction |
| 5. Biological quantum channel | A state persists in living neurons and is selectively read to change neural computation | Open causal test | Orch OR or phenomenal identity |
| 6. Orch OR mechanism | Orchestration and objective reduction occur as proposed and are causally necessary | Open causal test | A complete account of consciousness |
| 7. Consciousness explanation | The mechanism explains a declared state, content, access, metacognitive, or phenomenal target | Open theory comparison | Other consciousness targets or scales |

## Anatomical correction

Dendritic spines are predominantly actin-rich postsynaptic structures. Dynamic microtubule plus ends can transiently invade a subset of spines; they should not be illustrated as stable bundles filling every spine. In vertebrate preparations, axonal microtubules are predominantly plus-end-out while dendrites contain both orientations ([Baas et al., 1988](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.85.21.8335); [Yau et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2430-15.2016)). A generic diagram must not imply one polarity pattern throughout every species, neuronal type, or compartment.

## Electrical oscillations and the EEG bridge

Cantero and colleagues found a prominent 39 Hz fundamental in voltage-clamped rat-brain microtubule bundles under intracellular-like solution conditions and recorded related oscillatory activity in membrane-permeabilized cultured hippocampal neurites ([Scientific Reports, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30453-2)). The numerical overlap with the gamma band is hypothesis-generating, not source identification.

EEG and local field signals reflect superimposed extracellular fields produced by transmembrane currents, with measured magnitude depending strongly on cellular geometry and temporal synchrony ([Buzsaki, Anastassiou, and Koch, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn3241)). A direct microtubule-to-EEG account must therefore demonstrate the complete bridge:

`microtubule oscillation in an intact neuron -> coupling to membrane current -> aligned population current dipoles -> synchronized tissue-scale field -> detectable extracranial signal`

The 2018 preparation did not test that chain. A 2025 paper developed a multiscale electrokinetic transmission-line model informed by this research program; it is a model of possible ionic propagation and oscillation, not an independent in-vivo replication ([Scientific Reports, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-24920-w)).

## Fractal time-crystal proposal

Hameroff, Bandyopadhyay, and Lauretta propose that microtubules form nested self-similar oscillatory systems across multiple frequency scales and describe them as "fractal time crystals" ([Journal of Consciousness Studies, 2026](https://doi.org/10.53765/20512201.33.1.211)). This is a peer-reviewed theoretical synthesis. The time-crystal classification, the assignment of frequency bands to particular microtubule subsystems, and the proposed bridge to consciousness require independent experimental replication and explicit operational tests. They should not be stated as established medical or neuroscience findings.

## Anesthesia is not one measurement

Anesthesia changes molecular targets, synaptic transmission, network dynamics, behavior, and reportability. Loss of righting reflex is a practical rodent endpoint, but it is not a direct measurement of subjective content. A microtubule-targeting drug can also change transport, morphology, pharmacokinetics, inflammation, or general neural function. The reported drug effects differ by compound, regimen, species, and study. The bounded conclusion is that microtubules may contribute to anesthetic action; the stronger Orch OR inference remains a separate hypothesis.

## SAN interpretation across scales

- **Micro:** microtubules can change cellular structure, cargo delivery, local plasticity, and readiness. This is established or testable cell biology.
- **Meso:** altered cellular readiness may change local circuit recruitment, timing, and recurrent dynamics. This requires circuit-level measurement and selective perturbation.
- **Macro:** conscious state and content involve distributed cortical, thalamic, subcortical, body, and action systems. A molecular result does not transfer to this scale without a causal chain.

SAN's observer-action account resides in the distributed organization that receives, transforms, re-expresses, and uses differences. A microtubule, quantum event, neuron, or cortical area is not a hidden viewer. Tonic context and phasic change become relevant only through identified receivers and consequences in the larger network.

## Decisive experiment pattern

A strong experiment would:

1. define the proposed microtubule state and its lifetime;
2. measure it in living neural tissue under physiological conditions;
3. identify the cellular reader and downstream circuit route;
4. perturb the state selectively while preserving transport, viability, and ordinary synaptic function;
5. measure a declared neural representation and behavioral or report target;
6. distinguish arousal, motor impairment, memory, access, and conscious content;
7. rescue the predicted state and consequence; and
8. test a classical cellular explanation, a network explanation, and the Orch OR explanation against the same observations.
