# Global Workspace Theory

**Global Workspace Theory (GWT)** is Bernard Baars's external account in which conscious content arises when locally processed information is broadcast to a brain-wide "workspace," and inside [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] it is treated as one of the four headline mainstream theories — alongside [[integrated-information-theory|Integrated Information Theory]], [[attention-schema-theory|Attention Schema Theory]], and Higher-Order Thought Theory — against which SAN must articulate its [[napot-overview|NAPOT]]/[[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave-differential]] alternative. SAN endorses the *distributed-information* claim of GWT but rejects the workspace metaphor for a more specific [[consciousness-as-oscillatory-feedback-loop|oscillatory-mechanism]] reading.

## Position

SAN's position composes three claims that turn GWT-alignment into a stronger, mechanism-providing variant. First, the GWT distribution claim is *correct in form* but *under-specified in mechanism*: `04san--md`, line 483 makes the alignment explicit (information is broadly distributed) and the SAN supplement explicit (the distribution is "a more specific oscillatory mechanism"). The mechanism in question is the dominance of [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] across [[cortical-column|cortical columns]] — `04san--md`, lines 487–497 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/04san.md)) connect this to global-oddball detection, sparse encoding, prefrontal involvement, and oscillatory coherence as the testable observables that distinguish a *dominant* phase-wave-differential pattern (conscious) from a non-dominant one. Second, GWT is the *macro-scale instance* of a [[fractal-pattern|fractal]] hierarchy in which the same recursive-distribution dynamic plays out at each level: `a0039z--md`, line 71 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0039z.md)) writes "Your own personal simulation of reality, from your own viewpoint is a fractalization of a neuron, a neural column, and a global workspace with high level [[attention]] coordinated by the hippo-campus." This embeds GWT inside [[fractal-consciousness|fractal consciousness]] — the workspace is one rung in a [[fractal-brain-scale-hierarchy|fractal scale hierarchy]] where the same operation (recursive distribution of information across an [[neural-array-projection|array]]) repeats from the single neuron up. Third, GWT is positioned *against* the [[attention-schema-theory|Graziano Attention Schema]] approach: `a0039z--md`, line 75 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0039z.md)) records "What I notice is that he is differentiating his argument from Global Workspace theory, by arguing that looking at an Apple, making a mental model of an Apple, and allowing that Apple model to exist in the global workspace of the mind isn't enough, you also need a second model, a model of your [[attention]]." SAN reads both GWT and Graziano's extension of it as describing *what the brain does* without explaining *how the doing is physically implemented*; the SAN difference is "[[consciousness]] is rooted in all cells when they entify via oscillatory synchronization" (`a0039z--md`, line 75) — i.e. [[entification]] is constitutive, not contingent on a model-of-[[attention]].

The position has consequences. If GWT is correct that conscious content is distributed but [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] are how the distribution is physically realised, then global-oddball-detection paradigms (`04san--md`, lines 489–495) become tests for the SAN reading rather than for GWT alone — sparse encoding in sensory [[cortex]] and stronger prefrontal responses fit the dominant-phase-wave-differential prediction, and the missing piece in published GWT studies is direct phase-relationship measurement (`04san--md`, line 495). And if [[entification]] across all cells is what ground-truths [[consciousness]], then GWT's reliance on a *global broadcast* across high-order brain areas alone is necessary-but-not-sufficient: the SAN claim is that a [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|column-level]] oscillatory [[entification]] has to be in place for a global-workspace broadcast to *be* a conscious content rather than a mere routing pattern.

## Post-SAN

GWT and the Dehaene-line global-neuronal-workspace literature have provided extensive empirical scaffolding for the *distribution* claim — global ignition, prefrontal involvement, late-wave evoked responses to consciously perceived stimuli — and SAN's reading at `04san--md` line 483 takes the distribution claim as compatible. Where SAN parts company is in the *mechanism specification*: GWT typically invokes a workspace metaphor and a winner-take-all broadcasting dynamic; SAN insists the underlying mechanism is oscillatory-phase coherence among [[neural-array-projection|neural-array]] projections, with the [[phase-wave-differentials|dominant phase-wave-differential]] reading the broadcast as a specific phase-coherence pattern rather than an abstract "global access."
