**Holonic rhythm** ([[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]) is the claim that what observers describe as human tribal bonding
is more precisely understood as **multi-spatial, multi-tempo-scale neuronal oscillatory synchrony** —
different brain-wave frequencies linking across individuals to form nested (holonic) coordination
structures.

## Core Claim

> "What you are observing as innate tribalism I am observing as an innate multi-spatial-multi-tempo-scale
> holonic rhythm. Humans link together because different neuronal oscillations link together." — [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]], [[gh-a0274z|a0274z]]

The reframe: *tribalism* is an evolutionary-behavioural label placed on an underlying neurophysiological
reality. That reality is [[neural-oscillatory-synchronization]] extended beyond skull boundaries —
individuals whose oscillatory patterns resonate across frequencies and timescales experience the pull
of group cohesion.

## Holarchy in Neural Terms

A *holon* is simultaneously a whole and a part of a larger whole. Applied here:

- **Cell level**: individual neurons phase-lock within a local assembly.
- **Column level**: local assemblies couple into cortical columns.
- **Network level**: columns bind via gamma/beta/theta into brain-scale networks.
- **Social level**: co-present individuals entrain inter-brain oscillations (inter-brain sync).

Each level is a complete oscillatory loop *and* a component of the next. The same [[fractal-pattern]]
structure that [[napot]] identifies inside a single brain extends outward into social space.

## Connection to NAPOT

[[napot]] describes perception as projection from a neural array through a sequence of oscillating
arrays. Social holonic rhythm extends this: a group of individuals forms a *meta-array*, projecting
shared representations through synchronized inter-brain oscillation. Tribalism becomes the subjective
phenomenology of participating in a higher-order [[napot]] projection.
