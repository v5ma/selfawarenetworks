## Joined Constructive Result

The tranche strengthens one continuous mechanism:

```text
external, bodily, or remembered difference
-> receptor and dendritic sampling
-> state-dependent ionic and branch-level transformation
-> contextualized spike, waveform, and population timing
-> convergence, inhibition, dispersion, and recurrent reexpression
-> multimodal object, body, and world relation
-> prospective alternatives and valuation
-> selected cognitive or motor route
-> action and sensory feedback
-> changed memory, expectation, and later readiness
```

This is a distributed operation. The network that receives differences, reconstructs relations,
predicts consequences, selects actions, and receives feedback performs the observing operation.
It does not send a finished picture to a second inner viewer.

## The BCI Measurement Problem

Ranks 98 and 106 preserve a durable distinction:

```text
recording specificity
!= source-localization confidence
!= semantic-decoding confidence
!= stimulation specificity
!= learned percept
!= phenomenal consciousness
```

The “microphones in a dark room” analogy asks how several mixed observations constrain hidden
sources. More electrodes can improve those constraints without guaranteeing single-neuron
identity. A useful read/write system needs longitudinal calibration, uncertainty estimates,
bounded perturbation, neural and behavioral readback, and a learnable relation for the user.

The 2012 neurofeedback loop is an important ancestor: an EEG-derived feature changed an external
light or sound, allowing the participant to learn a relation between an internal action and an
external consequence. That establishes a learnable closed loop, not transparent access to a
thought.

## The PWD Problem Before The Name

Ranks 99, 100, 110, and 111 first explain the missing operation in plain language. A neural spike
is real, but a context-free event or count leaves out distinctions that can change its
consequence:

- timing relative to ongoing population activity;
- phase relation;
- waveform and duration;
- sending population;
- receiving population and receptor state;
- concurrent excitation and inhibition;
- the change produced in prediction, route availability, plasticity, or action.

A maintained population relation supplies the comparison context. A consequential event changes
that relation. **Phase Wave Differential (PWD)** names the typed, receiver-relevant change. It
does not replace spikes and is not merely another name for phase coding, coherence, or traveling
waves.

Tonic and phasic are relative roles:

- tonic is the maintained context for a selected system and interval;
- phasic is a consequential update relative to that context;
- either role can occur at different frequencies under different conditions.

Fast transients can rapidly recruit distant anatomical targets. Slower rhythms can coordinate
broader networks through repeated interactions over time. This is the source-faithful
two-timescale distinction; it does not claim that broadband high-frequency LFP activity spreads
farther through tissue than slow rhythms.

See [[tonic-phasic-phase-wave-differential]],
[[continuous-dynamics-discrete-neural-information]],
[[state-relative-differential-neural-write-in]], and
[[receptive-inceptive-dendritic-projection]].

### Rank 110: "Not Spikes Alone"

The title of `b0003y` is easy to misread as a denial of action potentials. The source immediately
says that a spike occurs, then asks what change it produces in connected cells. Its strongest
source-faithful operation is:

```text
contextualized spike or burst
-> transformed axonal and synaptic transmission
-> state-dependent receiver response
-> changed population relation and consequence
```

Phase-of-firing, concurrent-code, ongoing-state, and waveform-dependent-release research supplies
bounded footholds. The comparison stops before phase-only coding, universal tonic frequencies,
semantic phase labels, consciousness, PWD, or NAPOT.

SAN preserves a stronger receiver-relative hypothesis: timing, phase, waveform, route, release,
receiver state, and later consequence should jointly improve held-out prediction over matched
count-, rate-, timing-, or phase-only models. This is an immediate operational precursor. The
exact `phase wave differential` phrase belongs to a separate August 23, 2022 source and is not
backdated.

See [[contextualized-spike-receiver-relative-transformation]].

## From Coincidence To Choice And Action

Ranks 99, 103, 110, and 111 connect timing to selection:

```text
prospective alternatives
-> attention to currently relevant evidence
-> learned and body-state-dependent valuation
-> inhibition of competing routes
-> coordinated recruitment of a selected route
-> descending motor command
-> neuromuscular transmission
-> muscle-fiber calcium release
-> ATP-powered contraction
-> sensory consequence and model update
```

Concurrent phase-compatible neural inputs can help a neuron or motor pool cross threshold. The
neural voltage does not supply muscular fuel; it triggers the physiological chain whose
actin-myosin contraction uses cellular ATP.

Choice remains physically caused while learned reasons remain causally effective. Person-level
responsibility requires separate judgments about capacity, knowledge, control, coercion, and
foreseeable consequence; neither determinism nor noise settles those judgments by itself.

## Bidirectional Scale Magnification

Rank 107 begins with a practical problem: a receptor, dendrite, or cell changes locally, but
perception and action depend on distributed populations. The population context must also return
to change what its participating cells do next.

The recording title's "Oct 22" date agrees with its embedded
`2021-10-23T00:23:43Z` creation time: at UTC-07:00, that instant was still October 22. The transcript
was first publicly Git-fixed on June 8, 2022 as
[`b0102y ctpr.txt`](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/9324fe74e40cf4134395edfc4cc3320fa99e18be/b0102y%20ctpr.txt).

```text
local receptor, dendritic, or cellular difference
-> state-dependent integration and output
-> divergent excitation, inhibition, or modulation
-> changed population state
-> recurrent and feedback influence
-> changed local excitability, timing, and response
```

SAN calls the increased functional footprint of a difference **Neural Magnification**. The
historical "photocopier," "enlarger," and "megaphone" metaphors were attempts to name that scale
change. The operation is not an exact enlargement: it preserves selected relations while changing
carrier, receiver, location, dimensionality, timing, and scale.

Established research supports the component operations. Brief thalamocortical trains can recruit
widespread recurrent cortical activity; layer-6 feedback can excite thalamic relay cells directly
and inhibit them indirectly; prefrontal activity can alter motor-cortex ensembles; and task events
can change transitions among cortical population states. The comparison stops before one
universal scale-conversion algorithm, identical dynamics at every scale, consciousness, or NAPOT
is inferred.

The SAN extension is a testable bidirectional scale transformation: perturb a bounded local state,
measure the population trajectory, perturb the population context, and test whether local response
and task-relevant reconstruction change as predicted. The joined multiscale model should improve
held-out prediction, reconstruction, or action decoding over a single-scale model.

Rank 107 also preserves a separate reconstruction operation:

```text
partial samples from several moments or viewpoints
+ learned regularities and prior state
-> bounded scene or object estimate
-> current action-relevant view
-> movement supplies another sample
-> estimate is tested and updated
```

Medical-image super-resolution and NeRF demonstrate constrained reconstruction, learned priors,
and viewpoint-specific rendering as engineering operations. They do not establish that cortex uses
the same representation or algorithm. Measured input, learned completion, and uncertainty remain
separate.

## Molecular-To-Oscillatory Bridge

Rank 111 first earns the operation in plain language. One spike is a regenerative event, but the
living terminal and receiving cell transform it according to their current states:

```text
recent cellular and terminal state
-> sodium, potassium, and other conductances shape a spike waveform
-> the terminal waveform shapes calcium-current timing and amount
-> calcium and release machinery shape vesicle-fusion statistics
-> transmitter, receptor, dendrite, and receiver state shape the next response
-> many transformed responses alter population timing and effective routes
```

Sodium, potassium, calcium, action-potential duration, spike rate, oscillatory frequency, and
relative phase are related but distinct variables. Studies support preparation-specific links from
potassium-channel state and presynaptic waveform to calcium entry and release. MVR and multiple
release sites further expand the outcome distribution beyond a one-vesicle-or-failure model.

The source's "spatial information" intuition survives as a measurable question. Release-site
arrangement, fusion timing, transmitter diffusion, receptor organization, and dendritic position
can change the spatial-temporal footprint seen by a receiver. The comparison stops before claiming
that vesicles collide with one another, that MVR automatically reaches more anatomical synapses, or
that this footprint already proves PWD.

The `1/f` passage also survives as a generative cross-scale question. Population spectra often
contain an aperiodic power-frequency relation plus oscillatory peaks. A test can ask whether
cellular waveform, event-duration, calcium, release, and receptor variables improve a model of that
population spectrum after firing rate, synaptic currents, anatomy, filtering, and recording
geometry are controlled. It is not necessary to assert one universal inverse law across microscopic
spikes and macroscopic fields.

Connectomics supplies the possibility graph. Inhibition, neuromodulation, membrane state, timing,
task, and learning select a changing functional or effective graph on that structure. SAN's later
phase-graph extension asks whether receiver-relative waveform and phase variables add reproducible
prediction of which route becomes effective.

## Artificial Neurology And Temporary Coupling

Ranks 101, 104, and 110 preserve three boundaries:

- SAN is a versioned model whose mechanisms should improve when evidence improves;
- a temporary communication or BCI loop can form a shared cognitive cycle without turning two
  persistent people into one permanent person;
- an artificial system's sensing, body model, prediction, persistent preferences, action, and
  feedback should be measured separately from fluent output.

Hidden, compelled, irreversible, or harvested coupling creates an ethical breach. Designing a
potentially sentient system to prefer labor also raises autonomy and welfare questions.
