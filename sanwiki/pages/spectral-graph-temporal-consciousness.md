The brain functions as a **spectral graph** in which consciousness arises from temporally synchronized invariant patterns — not from spatial organization of neural regions.

## Core Claim

Oscillations are not themselves conscious; they serve as **frames** — discrete synchronization windows that allow cortical columns separated in space to integrate information by sharing invariant features at the same temporal moment. This is the mechanism underlying [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography).

## Learned vs. Innate Space

A central premise of [[gh-b0171y|b0171y]] is that spatial awareness is **learned**, not hardwired. The brain constructs a spatial model of the world through accumulated patterns of activity, comparable to how a computer has no intrinsic spatial representation. This aligns with [[self-aware-network]] theory's emphasis on learned [[gh-a0206z|self]]-models.

Key [[gh-formulation|formulation]]: *"Space itself, or space-time, was also the content of the mind and not the mind itself"* — consciousness is a contentless medium; spatial structure is one of its constructed contents.

## Evidence from Altered States

Experiences with Salvia and DMT are cited as natural experiments: dissolving the learned spatial construct eliminates the subjective sense of spatial separation, supporting the view that space perception is a maintained construction rather than a fundamental given.
