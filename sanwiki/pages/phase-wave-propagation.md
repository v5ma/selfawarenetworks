## Post-SAN

The packet contains zero `paper-external` evidence -- mainstream literature does not use "phase wave propagation" as a named construct, but the underlying phenomenon is well-studied. Cortical-traveling-waves work (Muller et al. 2018, Davis et al. 2020) shows that [brainwave](brainwave.md) activity propagates as waves with measurable speeds and directions across [cortex](cortex.md). Neural-field theory (Bressloff, Coombes) treats propagating bumps and waves as solutions to spatially extended Wilson-Cowan-style PDEs. SAN's distinctive contribution is *what propagates*: not a generic activity bump, but a *content-bearing* [phase wave differential](phase-wave-differential.md) whose spread carries the rendered information forward. The testable consequence is that modulating the propagation step -- disrupting the phase-shifting "subtle" coupling between neighbouring [neurons](single-neuron-phase-cascade.md) in an oscillating group while leaving local firing intact -- should specifically degrade the *transport* of conscious content without abolishing the originating events, in contrast to a rate-coding null hypothesis. This is adjacent to but more specific than mainstream traveling-wave-disruption experiments, which typically measure activity rather than reportable content.
