Three-dimensional time is a theoretical reformulation of spacetime in which time has three independent dimensions — **length-time**, **width-time**, and **height-time** — rather than the single monolithic time dimension of standard 3+1 spacetime.

## Core claim

Standard physics describes spacetime as "3 blue 1 brown" (3 spatial dimensions + 1 time dimension). This theory proposes a "**3 blue 3 brown**" structure: 3 spatial dimensions paired with 3 independent temporal dimensions. Each temporal dimension has distinct properties defined by its [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation]] ([[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]]) state.

## Why three time dimensions

The minimum data required to describe a dimension is the key question. If time itself is variable and field-like — similar to the electromagnetic field — then a single time coordinate may be insufficient at quantum scales. Each temporal axis could independently dilate, especially under extreme quantum conditions such as within [[bose-einstein-condensate]] or [[fermionic-condensate]] systems.

## Connection to QGTCD

Each of the three time dimensions carries its own [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] state, leading to different time-dilation properties. This extends [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation]] from a modifier of a single time axis to a tensor-valued field operating across all three temporal dimensions. The three temporal axes are "almost like three kinds of particles" — distinct in how they interact with quantum mechanics and spacetime curvature.

## Brain as 4D structure

The same source grounds this in neuroscience: the human brain is a 3D neural network that changes over time — a 4D structure. The [[entity-module]] concept (a [[gh-a0206z|self]], consisting of oscillating virtual phase patterns) lives in this 4D tissue layout. The three-dimensional-time framework raises the possibility that neural computation itself exploits temporal degrees of freedom beyond a single time axis.

## Relation to Quantum Field Theory

Treating time as a field analogous to the EM field has implications for [[quantum-field-theory]] (QFT): particle trajectory probabilities, quantum gravity corrections, and the arrow of time would all be reframed under a 3+3 spacetime.
