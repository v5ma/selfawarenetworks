It treats awareness as feedforward detection, attention as one selection stage, consciousness as loop closure, and Phi as output resolution. None of those assignments is an established biological hierarchy, and Phi is not a measure of image resolution.

This page uses **feedback** for endogenous recurrent and body-world loops. **Neurofeedback** should be reserved for an intervention in which measured neural activity is returned to a participant so that the participant can learn to alter it.

## The SAN claim

SAN proposes that observer-action depends on closed causal cycles linking sensation, learned neural state, recurrent processing, body state, prediction, and action. Loop closure matters because the consequences of an action or internal update can revise the next state. It does not follow that every closed loop is conscious or that loop closure alone explains phenomenal experience.

## Attention, awareness, and access

Awareness is not merely feedforward detection, and attention is not always a step that precedes it. Experiments can dissociate attention, perceptual awareness, integration, and report. A useful feedback model must therefore specify which operation is affected rather than label each arrow with a mental category.

## IIT comparison

Integrated Information Theory does not define Phi as the visual resolution or fidelity of a conscious render. IIT proposes an intrinsic causal structure and uses integrated information in a specific formal theory. SAN may disagree with its assumptions or biological implementation, but the comparison must use IIT's actual claim.

Similarly, the cerebellum's large neuron count and distinctive architecture do not by themselves prove that it is nonconscious or that synchrony is irrelevant. Lesion, stimulation, anatomy, and functional evidence must be interpreted at the level of the tested capacity.

The 2025 COGITATE adversarial study directly tested divergent IIT and GNWT predictions. It found some aligned results and substantial challenges for both; it did not show that Phi is merely resolution or that recurrent feedback has replaced both theories ([Nature, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1)).

## Synchronization is contextual

Synchronization and desynchronization are not creativity and repetition modes. Their effects depend on the measured variable, circuit, timescale, receiver, and task. SAN should predict a specific receiver consequence rather than interpret freedom from synchrony as consciousness.

## Test burden

A feedback-loop claim should identify the forward route, return route, state variable, controlled outcome, and expected effect of selectively interrupting and restoring the loop. A consciousness claim must additionally separate access, report, metacognition, and phenomenal interpretation.
