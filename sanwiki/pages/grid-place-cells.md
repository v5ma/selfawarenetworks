## Position

In [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the hippocampal-entorhinal system is the canonical *reference-frame engine* — the cellular substrate that supplies the [[frame-of-reference|frame of reference]] every other cortical operation reads from. Grid Place Cells names the joint substrate this engine runs on. Three commitments follow. First, *both populations are oscillators*: the [[grid-cells-place-cells-oscillators|grid-and-place-cells-as-oscillators]] page makes the SAN-canonical claim that these populations should be read primarily as elements of the [[neural-oscillation-hierarchy|oscillator hierarchy]] rather than as ad-hoc representational devices, which is what allows their activity to participate in [[oscillator-environmental-encoding|environmental encoding]] and to be readable by [[napot-theory|NAPOT]]. Second, *they encode tempo-spatial sequences*: the [[beliefs-as-tempo-spatial-sequences|beliefs-as-tempo-spatial-sequences]] and [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves|criterial tempo-spatial waves]] readings make grid/place activity the substrate not only of physical-space navigation but of belief-space and concept-space navigation, which is what generalizes [[hippocampal-reference-frames|hippocampal reference frames]] into the [[cortex]]-wide [[reference-frames-cortical-knowledge|cortical reference-frames-for-knowledge]] reading. Third, *they implement separation-by-phase*: the [[spear-hippocampal-phase-offset|SPEAR]] and [[hippocampal-encoding-retrieval-separation|encoding-retrieval-separation]] readings tie grid/place activity to phase-offset mechanics, which is the SAN-canonical way of separating encoding from retrieval inside the same circuit. The cumulative SAN position is that grid place cells are the *cellular substrate of the reference-frame movability* SAN treats as central — the cellular-level site at which the [[frame-of-reference|frame of reference]] can be re-anchored, scaled, or re-positioned.
