# SAN Knowledge Architecture Synthesis

This page summarizes the structure that now appears across the SAN Wiki, the medically reviewed SAN figure set, and the separate SAN Book 2 expansion map. It is an architectural reading of the project, not a new scientific validation claim and not an edit to the Book 2 manuscript.

## The central problem

SAN is strongest when it begins with a biological control problem:

> A living body must maintain a usable model of itself and its surroundings from partial, delayed, noisy, and differently routed signals, then act before complete information is available.

From that problem, the SAN concepts form a sequence rather than a pile of acronyms:

1. **Receive:** receptors, dendrites, synapses, and recurrent pathways gather partial state.
2. **Differentiate:** incoming changes alter an already active neural and bodily context.
3. **Integrate:** cells and populations transform converging input under their current state.
4. **Render:** distributed activity maintains a working model usable by perception, memory, valuation, and action.
5. **Redistribute:** axonal, recurrent, thalamic, corticocortical, subcortical, and body-linked pathways carry the changed state onward.
6. **Act and return:** motor, autonomic, endocrine, and sampling consequences change the next input.
7. **Learn:** plasticity changes which future differences matter and which routes become effective.

[[napot-overview|NAPOT]], [[ndca-non-linear-differential-continuous-approximation|NDCA]], [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|GCS]], [[criterial-causation]], and [[entification]] should be introduced as proposed parts of this cycle. They should not be introduced as independent labels whose relationship the reader must infer.

## Five architectural layers

| Layer | Established target | SAN contribution | Main boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biological substrate | Receptors, membranes, dendrites, synapses, axons, cell types, plasticity, circuits, body feedback | The substrate participates in computation rather than merely carrying abstract symbols | Biological complexity alone does not prove SAN's interpretation |
| Active reference state | Ongoing membrane, synaptic, population, field, and body state | Tonic activity is treated as a context or canvas against which changes become consequential | Tonic does not mean one frequency, one global clock, or one universal synchronized state |
| Difference and transformation | Sensory transduction, synaptic input, dendritic integration, recurrent error and novelty responses | Phasic changes and phase-wave differentials are candidate receiver-relative update variables | A phase difference is not automatically information, prediction error, free energy, or conscious content |
| Multi-scale reconstruction | Recurrent local and distributed integration across specialized pathways | NAPOT proposes gather-integrate-redistribute motifs at cellular, circuit, and network scales | Repeated motifs do not prove literal tomography or scale invariance |
| Distributed observer and action | Perception, valuation, motor control, autonomic regulation, memory, and feedback emerge from interacting systems | The sparse distributed field-cell system itself is the observer operation; GCS and entification propose coordination mechanisms | There is no separate viewer, but distributed processing alone does not prove the constitutive consciousness claim |

The most important new bridge is therefore [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries|the SAN scale ladder]]. It prevents a single-neuron motif, a canonical local circuit, and a distributed working model from being drawn or described as one biological object.

## What the image work revealed

The reviewed figure set did more than illustrate the theory. It exposed recurring conceptual defects that prose can hide.

### NAPOT

The first NAPOT designs made one neuron appear to receive complete named feature streams and project directly to several high-level targets. That confused a bounded cellular receive-integrate-project motif with population and network reconstruction. The corrected architecture uses three explicit scales:

- **cellular candidate motif:** dendritic and synaptic reception, integration, thresholding, and axonal projection;
- **mesoscopic proposal:** recurrent local populations gather and redistribute partial updates;
- **distributed proposal:** specialized cortical, thalamic, hippocampal, subcortical, cerebellar, brainstem, and body-linked systems update a working model.

### Active vision and embodiment

An early active-vision plate placed V1 at the anterior pole implied by its brain orientation. That was an anatomical error, not a labeling preference. The corrected visual established a rule for the whole wiki: pathways must be drawn with explicit orientation, modality-specific relays, and distinct recurrent and motor routes. "The brain renders" cannot be represented as a single arrow into or out of prefrontal cortex.

### GCS

The first GCS figures assigned fixed frequency bands and layer functions too categorically. The corrected account separates measured rhythms from the SAN interpretation, treats laminar circuits as canonical schematics rather than universal templates, and includes parallel proprioceptive, vestibular, interoceptive, sensory, motor, and efference-copy routes. GCS is most useful as a proposed coordination pattern, not a universal frequency-to-function chart.

### OCA, BOT, and COT

The medically reviewed plates exposed three different category errors:

- OCA had treated phase sign as if it directly determined excitation, inhibition, voltage, calcium, and vesicle release.
- BOT had visually collapsed synchrony, prediction mismatch, entropy, information, and equilibrium into one progression.
- COT had blurred receptors with channels and presented a bounded cellular cascade as universal.

The corrections separate measured variables, proposed SAN variables, causal interventions, and excluded conclusions. That same separation now belongs in the prose.

### Observer language

The figure review also clarified that Micah Blumberg's account does not posit a hidden viewer. The intended claim is positive: cells and populations have complementary receive and re-express roles inside an active tonic field-cell context; phasic changes physically alter that context; the distributed system registers, evaluates, and acts through those changes. The camera/screen and microphone/speaker analogies name internal functional roles, not literal devices or a spectator inside the brain.

See [[screen-metaphor-no-inner-viewer|Distributed Observer and the Screen Metaphor]].

## The four lenses the wiki now needs on every mechanism page

### 1. Scale

Every page should state whether its main claim is molecular, subcellular, cellular, local-circuit, cortical-column, distributed-network, bodily, behavioral, computational, or phenomenological. Cross-scale links should be described as hypotheses unless a measurement actually spans the levels.

### 2. Evidence status

Each important statement should be recognizable as one of four kinds:

- established anatomy or physiology;
- a measured but interpretation-dependent phenomenon;
- a SAN/NAPOT mechanistic hypothesis;
- a metaphor or explanatory analogy.

The page does not need bureaucratic labels on every sentence. It does need enough local wording that a reader cannot mistake a proposal for a medical fact.

### 3. Variable identity

Related quantities must not be collapsed. Phase, coherence, power, firing rate, membrane potential, calcium, release probability, prediction error, surprisal, variational free energy, entropy, information, and conscious content are different variables. A proposed relationship needs a mapping and a test, not a shared color or arrow.

See [[phase-language-firewall]].

### 4. Causal discrimination

Correlation is insufficient for the strongest SAN claims. A useful page should identify the alternative models and the intervention that would distinguish them: common input, volume conduction, arousal, firing-rate changes, power changes, task timing, recurrent connectivity, and motor consequences are common confounds.

## The remaining structural gap

The largest remaining gap is the **agency and valuation bridge**. GCS, Layer 5 output, prediction, and proprioceptive correction currently risk carrying jobs performed by several interacting systems. The next bridge should join:

- interoception and homeostatic regulation;
- salience, affect, and learned value;
- basal-ganglia action selection;
- cerebellar prediction and correction;
- cortical and thalamic recurrence;
- brainstem, spinal, autonomic, and endocrine consequences;
- distributed executive control rather than one central executive.

Its purpose would not be to give every region a SAN label. It would show how the proposed rendering cycle becomes goal-directed biological action without reducing agency to prefrontal command or to one oscillation band.

## Recommended Book 2 story

Book 2 can tell a larger story by using the architecture above as its spine:

1. **The control problem:** acting from partial, delayed sensory and body signals.
2. **The living substrate:** why dendrites, synapses, cells, fields, and plasticity matter.
3. **The active canvas:** tonic context and phasic difference without frequency essentialism.
4. **The update cycle:** NAPOT as receive-integrate-redistribute across explicitly labeled scales.
5. **Continuity:** NDCA as a proposed route from discrete events to usable changing models.
6. **Embodied agency:** GCS, proprioception, motor prediction, action, and returning consequences.
7. **The distributed observer:** the field-cell system itself registers and uses changes.
8. **Evidence and alternatives:** what is established, what is proposed, and what could falsify the proposal.
9. **Genealogy and comparison:** only after the reader understands the mechanism, show chronology and bounded equivalences.
10. **Engineering implications:** BCI, artificial neurology, and NerveGear requirements derived from the biological account.

This order prevents comparison evidence from substituting for explanation and prevents metaphors from carrying anatomy they were never meant to carry.

## One-sentence synthesis

> SAN is a multi-scale proposal for active biological rendering in which living neural and bodily systems maintain context, register consequential differences, reconstruct working state across recurrent pathways, and convert that state into perception, learning, and action without a separate inner observer.

That sentence is a theory summary, not a statement that every proposed mechanism has been established.
