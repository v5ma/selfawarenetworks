# Early a0135-a0155 Obscure Argument Map

This page maps a fixed semantic-review queue from the downloaded SAN repository. It preserves what
each source contributes without treating every sentence, link, analogy, or historical health claim
as current scientific evidence.

## The strongest recovered chain

```text
changing body-and-world relation
-> temporally rendered distributed state
-> receiving population detects and transforms the pattern
-> ensemble timing and phase can be reset
-> successive states form a neural trajectory
-> inhibitory and excitatory routes select a consequence
-> the next population, thought, or action receives the result
```

This chain is distributed. The phrase in `a0135z.md` that "only the TV can see it" is not a hidden
viewer. The intended operation is that the neural system that carries the changing state is also
the system that detects and transforms it.

## Recovered concepts

### Neural trajectories and local expansion

`a0149z.md` and the nearby July 2022 notes ask how a sequence of changing neural states can trace a
curve, object, feeling, or action. The governed interpretation is now
[[neural-phase-trajectories-taylor-series-proposal|Neural Phase Trajectories and the Taylor-Series Proposal]].
It preserves the original idea while distinguishing a fitted local expansion from a demonstrated
biological Taylor calculator.

### Ensemble receiver

`a0136z.md` and `a0137z.md` move the receive-transform-project problem from one dendrite to an
oscillating population. Established phase reset and entrainment make the scale transition
testable. SAN's additional claim is that a population can act as a receiver when its collective
state changes in a reproducible, downstream-usable way.

### Choice before the name

`a0153z.md` provides a 2012 ancestor: candidate patterns compete, inhibition suppresses
alternatives, experience changes pathway strength, and one selected route coordinates the body.
`a0149z.md` later names Choice as the neuropath taken through a distributed circuit. Neither source
requires an uncaused inner chooser.

### Self as active organization

`a0139z.md` asks what remains when no single cell, memory, limb, or body component is the whole
self. Its useful contribution is an embodied, plastic, distributed organization that can adopt an
artificial limb or body through changed control relations. The later Field-Cell thesis adds a
specific mechanistic identity hypothesis; it is not backdated to the 2012 discussion.

## Primary-science reconciliation

- [Churchland et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11129) supports structured neural
  population trajectories in motor control.
- [Lakatos et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1154735) supports attention-dependent
  oscillatory entrainment and response-gain changes.
- [Dickey et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2107797119) supports widespread cortical ripple
  co-occurrence and phase locking.
- [Adam et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2022.111139) supports a derivative-like
  corticosubthalamic control operation in one locomotor task.
- [Li et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.ade3874) supports pathway-specific inhibitory
  routing of similar visual threat input toward different behavioral consequences.
- [Jones and Kording 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroscience.2021.07.036) provides a modeled
  test of dendritic computational capacity under biological constraints.

These papers support components. They do not validate the joined SAN architecture automatically.

## What this review did not prove

- that the brain implements a literal multidimensional Taylor series;
- that phase, frequency, amplitude, duration, waveform, and power are interchangeable;
- that oscillatory synchrony is sufficient for awareness or consciousness;
- that a field without cells decodes meaning;
- that every cortical column performs one universal algorithm;
- that a Raspberry Pi implementation is conscious;
- that internal dates establish public priority;
- that a composite PDF independently confirms its embedded files.
