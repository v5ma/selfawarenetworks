# Synchrony

**Synchrony** in [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) is the relational alignment of [oscillations](neural-oscillations.md) across neurons, [cortical columns](cortical-columns.md), and brain regions whose precise [phase relationships](phase-wave-differentials.md) -- not absolute amplitudes -- carry information, bind features into perceptual objects, and produce the [dominant phase-wave differential](dominant-phase-wave-differential.md) that SAN identifies with conscious experience. Where mainstream [neuroscience](neuroscience.md) treats synchrony as one signature among many, SAN promotes it to a constitutive role in the [rendering pipeline](brain-as-rendering-engine.md) and in inter-brain [entrainment](brainwave-entrainment.md).

> **Scientific status:** The first paragraph states the SAN hypothesis, not an established
> consciousness mechanism. Phase relations can covary with attention, communication, perception,
> and coordinated action in defined preparations, but synchrony is not automatically information,
> binding, meaning, rendering, or consciousness. Common input, reference choice, volume conduction,
> waveform shape, movement, arousal, and filtering must be controlled. Interbrain similarity does
> not establish merged fields or minds. A SAN claim requires a named receiver, route, scale,
> consequence, selective perturbation, and comparison against simpler and capacity-matched models.

## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream [neuroscience](neuroscience.md), SAN's treatment of synchrony makes three distinct moves. First, it accepts the Engel/Fries/Singer "binding-by-synchrony" lineage (`dl-current--ARUs_Bio_Comp_Paper`, line 1572, paper-external) and the modern [predictive-processing](predictive-processing.md) reframing in which "Phase synchrony ... represents the precision (inverse variance) of these beliefs" (`04san--md`, line 331, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md)). Second, it strengthens the claim from correlation to constitution: synchrony is not merely *correlated* with conscious perception but is the relational substrate by which the [dominant phase-wave differential](dominant-phase-wave-differential.md) is computed across [cortical columns](cortical-columns.md) -- so SAN predicts that disrupting inter-columnar phase coupling should produce specifically conscious-level deficits, not merely behavioral ones, in line with the [TMS](transcranial-magnetic-stimulation.md) interventions [Micah](gh-resnote04.md) cites elsewhere. Third, where the recent connection-density literature (`dl-oct6-2025--2507.03631v2`, line 45, paper-external) treats "the emergent relationship between connection density and both oscillation frequency and synchrony" as a network-architecture fact, SAN reads the same finding as evidence that synchrony is a [dissipative](dissipative-system.md) equilibrium [attractor](attractor.md) -- [Micah](gh-resnote04.md)'s [a0048z](gh-a0048z.md) frames living systems as "multiple cooperating [dissipative systems](dissipative-systems.md) in chaotic synchrony" (`a0048z--md`, line 15, on github), making synchrony a thermodynamic signature of life itself rather than a circuit-level coincidence. The Strogatz "[Sync](oscillatory-synchrony.md)" lineage is acknowledged explicitly: "principles of [oscillatory synchrony](oscillatory-synchrony.md) as outlined in the book Sync by Steven Strogatz" (`a0254z--md`, line 15, on github) -- SAN inherits the cross-domain coupled-[oscillator](oscillator.md) math but routes it specifically through neural [phase-wave differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md) to recover [qualia](qualia.md).
