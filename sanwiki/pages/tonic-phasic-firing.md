## Position

In SAN, the tonic-phasic distinction is not two different *kinds* of activity but two roles played by the *same* spike train. A neuron has a tonic rate — its baseline firing in concert with its [[oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration|phase-locked]] population — and it has phasic deviations from that rate, when an [[action-potential]] arrives early, late, or out of band relative to the [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]] of its peers. The combined pattern is *tonic-[[phasic-firing|phasic firing]]*: the cell never stops oscillating, but particular spikes are flagged as informative because they are out of phase. The Lane A quote at `(GPT2023X--md, line 13482, [[blob_permalink|on github]])` makes this concrete for memory recall — "A memory initiated with a phasic [[action-potential|action potential]] continues to oscillate with tonic [[phasic-firing|phasic firing]] rates, representing activated areas in the tonic firing graph and quieter, inhibited areas" — i.e., when a memory is *triggered*, the tonic-[[phasic-firing|phasic firing]] pattern of an entire population is what carries the memory's content forward, with the phasic component marking which subpopulations are "lit up" against the tonic background. This is the spike-level realisation of the [[tonic-phasic-canvas-of-consciousness|canvas-and-content]] story: the canvas is the population's tonic, the content is the phasic deviations, and the [[coincidence-detection|coincidence-detection]] arithmetic — the [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence-as-a-bit]] reading — runs on the joint pattern. The framing connects upward to the [[brainwave|brainwave]] / [[gamma-oscillation|gamma-band]] population picture, and downward to the per-cell [[threshold-state]] picture: a cell sitting near threshold and oscillating with its peers is, by construction, in tonic-[[phasic-firing|phasic firing]] mode.
