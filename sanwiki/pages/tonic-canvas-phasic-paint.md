# Tonic Canvas, Phasic Paint

**Tonic canvas, phasic paint** is a historical SAN mnemonic for the receiver-relative relationship
between ongoing state and consequential change. The current scientific explanation is maintained at
[[tonic-phasic-canvas|Tonic and Phasic Roles: Canvas and Ink]].

## What the metaphor preserves

- A receiving system is already active before a new event arrives.
- A new event matters by changing that ongoing state.
- The change can be re-expressed to other receivers and alter the next cycle.
- The same distributed tissue constructs, registers, and uses the result; there is no inner viewer.

## What the metaphor does not establish

The canvas is not one slow shared oscillation or pure awareness. The paint is not a fixed burst,
phase, waveform, or 0/1/2/3-vesicle symbol. Synaptic release is probabilistic and target-specific,
and each postsynaptic cell combines the event with many other inputs. A tonic/phasic relationship
must name its scale, time window, biological receiver, measured variable, and causal consequence.

This page preserves the phrase and its provenance without maintaining a second competing mechanism.
