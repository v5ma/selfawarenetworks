# SAN Wiki Random 100-Page Visual Audit - 2026-08-01

This audit is a reproducible random sample of the complete indexed SAN Wiki corpus. It was used to find presentation defects that a source-only link audit cannot reveal.

- Indexed population: **2,372 pages**
- Random sample: **100 pages**
- Seed: `san-wiki-random-100-20260801-v1`
- Screenshot viewport: **1440 x 1000**
- Pages without detected browser or presentation signals: **100**
- Pages with detected signals after repair: **0**

## Repairs Triggered by the Sample

- Replaced the old all-wikis/internal SAN reader with the SAN-only canonical interface.
- Removed private-tool navigation and internal product-cluster metadata from the SAN reader.
- Reorganized the page browser around eight featured SAN concept groups while retaining full-corpus search and direct routes.
- Repaired malformed source wikilinks and corrupted auto-link titles found by the sample.
- Expanded the empty OCA Glossary into a source-bounded SAN navigation glossary.
