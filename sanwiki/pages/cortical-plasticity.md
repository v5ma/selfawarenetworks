# Cortical Plasticity

![Cortical Plasticity](../assets/generated/san/cortical-plasticity/cortical-plasticity-20260702-v1.png)

This image frames cortical plasticity as experience-driven retuning across scales: NMDA-gated Ca2+/CaMKII signaling changes dendritic spines through LTP/LTD, inhibitory and dopamine signals gate the plasticity window, and cortical-column plus M1-striatal loops convert those local changes into retrained neural-array readouts.

**Cortical Plasticity** is the capacity of cortical synapses, dendrites, and circuits to change their connectivity and excitability in response to experience, stimulation, or injury. In the [[Self-Aware-Networks|Self-Aware Networks]] (SAN) corpus the term sits at the intersection of [[brain-plasticity]], [[neural-plasticity]], [[neuroplasticity]], [[synaptic-plasticity]], and [[hebbian-plasticity]] — all readings the project takes as candidate substrates for how the [[cortex]] re-tunes its [[Cortical-columns|cortical columns]] and supports [[Engrams|engrams]] across the [[brain-state]] trajectory of a learner.

The single direct evidence quote in the corpus describes invasive induction of plasticity in a corticostriatal motor loop: "Like they actually put in tungsten electrodes to actually induce some kind of cortical plasticity between M1 and the striatum" (original_archived_b0302ytimecode--md, line 4073), and the same statement reappears verbatim in (b0302ywhisper--md, line 4128). M1 is the primary motor cortex and the striatum is the input nucleus of the basal ganglia — a corticostriatal loop the project repeatedly invokes when it discusses motor learning, the [[predictive-motor-self-model]], and the [[self-as-motor-sensory-rendering|self as motor-sensory rendering]] story. The use of [[Neuralink]]-style electrode implants to actively shape this loop ties cortical plasticity directly to the SAN account of [[BrainGate]], [[brain-computer-interface|brain–computer interfaces]], [[brain-computer-interface-neural-readwrite|read/write brain–computer interfaces]], [[bci|BCI]] systems, and the [[neuralink-ethics-panel-b0303y|Neuralink ethics-panel]] critique that follows from the same b0302/b0303 conversations.

At the molecular and circuit grain, the corpus repeatedly grounds cortical plasticity in [[Hebbian-Learning|Hebbian learning]] mediated by [[NMDA-receptor]] / [[NMDA-receptors|NMDA-receptor]] activation, [[Long-Term-Potentiation|long-term potentiation]] (with the related [[ltp-ltd|LTP/LTD]] balance, [[ltp-protein-synthesis|protein-synthesis-dependent LTP]], [[ltp-bouton-synaptic-memory-traces|bouton-level synaptic memory traces]], [[ltp-structural-memory-encoding|structural memory encoding]], [[ltp-tempo-spatial-rhythmic-memory|tempo-spatial rhythmic memory]], and [[camkii-microtubule-ltp|CaMKII-microtubule LTP]] readings), [[ltd]], [[ltd-inhibitory-pattern-sharpening|inhibitory pattern sharpening]], [[bistable-synaptic-plasticity|bistable synaptic plasticity]], [[hebbian-engrams|Hebbian engrams]], and [[Dendritic-compartmentalization|dendritic compartmentalization]] inside [[apical-dendrite|apical dendrites]] of [[pyramidal-cells|pyramidal cells]]. The project also pairs plasticity with [[dopamine]], [[dopamine-as-pattern-selector|dopamine as pattern selector]], and [[dopamine-associative-memory|dopamine-mediated associative memory]] as the gating signals that decide which patterns get reinforced — a story that sits next to the [[plasticity-stability-problem|plasticity–stability problem]] and the [[inhibitory-interneurons-plasticity-gating|inhibitory-interneuron gating]] of when plasticity windows open.

At the system level, cortical plasticity is what the corpus appeals to when it explains how [[NAPOT]]-style [[neural-array]] readouts can be retrained, how [[Neurofeedback]], [[non-invasive-brain-stimulation|non-invasive brain stimulation]], [[eeg|EEG]]-based and [[fmri|fMRI]]-based protocols reshape cortical circuits, and how the [[memory-prediction-framework|memory-prediction framework]] of [[Jeff-Hawkins]] / [[Jeff Hawkins]] depends on continuously plastic [[cortical-column|cortical columns]] and the [[cortical-columns-reference-frames|reference frames]] those columns store.
