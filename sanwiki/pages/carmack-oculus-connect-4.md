John Carmack, CTO of Oculus, was interviewed by [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]] at Oculus Connect 4 (October 2017). The interview spans two video segments covering VR hardware, software performance, and the trajectory of spatial computing.

## Topics Covered

The interview is documented via embedded video (transcript unavailable in text form). Known topics from metadata and tags:

- **Samsung Gear VR** and mobile VR performance constraints
- **Oculus Rift** hardware roadmap
- Carmack's engineering philosophy on graphics and frame-rate optimization
- Near-term and long-term VR platform directions

## Relevance to SAN

Carmack's work on eliminating latency and maximizing perceptual fidelity in VR is adjacent to the [[self-aware-networks]] thesis: both treat the human perceptual system as a high-bandwidth, low-latency channel whose constraints determine the design envelope for immersive computing. His comments on presence and perceptual grounding inform the SAN framing of [[virtual-reality]] as a tractable substrate for testing theories of [[consciousness]].
