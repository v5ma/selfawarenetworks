## Primary-source corrections

- [Modi et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.80263) analyzed state-dependent coupling among
  multiple hippocampal oscillations. It supports a multivariate state-space method, not the claim
  that one phase graph is conscious content.
- [Stetson, Fiesta, and Eagleman, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0001295) found
  retrospective duration expansion without improved temporal resolution during frightening free
  fall. Subjective duration and online temporal resolution must remain separate.
- [Han et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2012.12.015) reported enhanced plasticity and
  learning after human glial-progenitor engraftment in mice. It does not establish a general
  intelligence hierarchy among species or substrates.
- [Payeur et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-021-00857-x) developed a burst-dependent
  plasticity mechanism in hierarchical models. It does not show that the brain implements ordinary
  machine-learning backpropagation.
- [Cabral et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-022-00950-y) derived metastable modes in a
  delay-coupled connectome model. It is a useful comparison, not a finished mechanism for all brain
  rhythms or EDS.
