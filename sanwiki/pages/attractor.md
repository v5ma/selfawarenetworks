Tonic rhythms define the readiness landscape, phasic bursts perturb it, and recurrent cortical-thalamic dynamics settle into coherent dominant phase-wave states that support conscious rendering.

**Attractor** is a dynamical-systems concept central to the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] framework's account of how the brain generates stable [[conscious-awareness|conscious representations]] from continuous, noisy [[neural-oscillation|oscillatory activity]]. In the SAN corpus, the brain is modeled as a deterministic dynamical system — "including oscillatory networks and attractor models — that can reproduce complex cognitive phenomena" and that supports the idea that brain dynamics, and by extension [[consciousness]], can arise from physical laws ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 75).

The SAN account grounds attractor dynamics explicitly in Hopfield's foundational work: "Hopfield's work on attractor neural networks is foundational for understanding how collective properties, pattern completion, and content-addressable memory can emerge" from networks of recurrently connected units ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 505). Recurrent [[neural-circuits|neural networks]] that settle into stable attractor states effectively perform [[oscillatory-binding|pattern completion]] and serve as content-addressable [[attractor-memory|memory]] systems, and "this concept of attractor dynamics supports SAN's idea that stable representational states can emerge from the collective activity of [[neural-arrays|neural arrays]]" ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 730). The SAN/[[napot-overview|NAPOT]] framework extends this idea beyond static weight-based storage by embedding attractors in the [[neural-oscillation|oscillatory physics]] of real cortical tissue.

Within [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]], the [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillations]] of local field potentials create stable, low-frequency attractor basins — "akin to a mental rendering substrate — while incoming or internally generated [[phasic-bursts|phasic bursts]] represent high-frequency pattern inputs" that are absorbed into or repel from those basins ([[gh-12san|12san]]--md, line 46). The tonic [[brain-rhythms|oscillatory band]] thus acts as the attractor landscape into which phasic burst events write new [[engrams]] or retrieve existing ones. [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|Dominant phase wave differentials]] (DPWDRs) in the [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] framework act as stable attractor states crucial for [[conscious-awareness|conscious rendering]]; the key computational principle is "attractor-like dynamics; pattern stabilization" ([[gh-09san|09san]]--md, line 128–129). Higher frequency [[brain-rhythms|powerbands]] further serve as "binding attractors that absorb discordant oscillating states (like a de-resonating attractor that essentially dissipates the resonating oscillations of burst firing events including sharp wave ripples)" into the higher-magnitude [[neural-oscillation|oscillatory]] background (a0007z--md, line 534).

The [[nonlinear-differential-continuous-approximation|Non-linear Differential Continuous Approximation]] (NDCA) framework — SAN's computational kernel — operates explicitly on cross-sections of unified attractor states: perceptual representations are described as "computational renderings in neural arrays computed via the cross sections of unified attractor states via oscillatory physics, binding together [[coincidence-detection|neural coincidence detections]] ([[predictive-coding]])" ([[gh-09san|09san]]--md, line 100). A body schema or postural representation, for example, would be "represented by its own complex, learned phase patterns or attractor states," with multi-modal binding occurring when phase patterns representing distinct modalities become synchronized and coherently integrated ([[gh-10san|10san]]--md, line 41; [[gh-10san|10san]]--md, line 43). The attractor thus provides the stability that allows the [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] rendering loop to maintain a coherent perceptual state across time despite the rapid fluctuation of individual [[neural-array|neural array]] activity.

At the cellular level, ions themselves function as attractors in the SAN vocabulary: "the ion is the moving material of neural dynamics, of neurophysics, it is an attractor and a state manager" ([[gh-a0002z|a0002z]]--md, line 32). Incoming sensory input drives the cortex toward "an attractor state of readiness, the ground of awareness, the ground of being" (a0004z--md, line 46), linking the mathematical concept of a basin of attraction to the phenomenological notion of background [[ground-of-being|ground of being]] awareness. The SAN notes draw the personal implication directly: "If my memories are attractor states, in synaptic connections, it means that I am, through my memories, literally creating myself, growing my mind, and building via new [[protein-synthesis|protein synthesis]] my simulation of reality" (a0010z--md, line 131). Attractor networks more generally "generate states for robust representation, integration, and memory" (a0010z--md, line 140), providing the substrate for both [[episodic-memory]] and the stable [[predictive-coding|predictive models]] that the brain uses to interpret ongoing [[sensory-processing|sensory input]].

The SAN corpus explicitly recognizes attractor networks alongside [[oscillatory-binding|sparse coding]] and [[predictive-coding]] as mainstream neuroscience frameworks that SAN-specific explanations must be situated within ([[gh-08san|08san]]--md, line 29; [[gh-09san|09san]]--md, line 27). Section 3.1 of [[gh-08san|08san]] is titled "Internal Attractor States in Cortical-Thalamic Systems for Rapid Shape Encoding," and the answer to how the brain encodes object shape rapidly is precisely the role of "internal attractor states in the RNN-like cortical-thalamic system" realized through NDCA ([[gh-08san|08san]]--md, line 88). This convergence — from Hopfield-style discrete attractors through continuous attractor manifolds to oscillatory [[thalamus|thalamocortical]] settling dynamics — is the conceptual backbone linking [[neuroscience]] of memory to the [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] account of [[conscious-awareness|conscious perception]].
