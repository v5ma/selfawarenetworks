# Free Energy Principle

![Free Energy Principle](../assets/generated/san/free-energy-principle/free-energy-principle-20260702-v1.png)

> **Scope boundary:** This visual depicts a proposed SAN interpretation of the Free Energy Principle, not the standard FEP formalism and not an established neural implementation. Variational free energy, sensory surprisal, prediction error, measured neural oscillations, and SAN phase-wave differentials are related but non-identical quantities.

This visual frames SAN's candidate phase-realignment interpretation of prediction and action: tonic reference activity meets phasic sensory updates, mismatch may alter local dynamics, and NAPOT proposes that distributed updates contribute to an active working model. Whether those dynamics implement variational free-energy minimization is an empirical SAN hypothesis.

The **Free Energy Principle (FEP)** is Karl Friston's external mathematical framework. Variational free energy is a tractable bound on sensory surprisal under a generative model; active inference extends the framework across perception, learning, and action. SAN compares FEP with [[napot-overview|NAPOT]], [[ndca-non-linear-differential-continuous-approximation|NDCA]], and [[super-information-theory|Super Information Theory]], but FEP does not itself entail tonic/phasic canvas language, phase-wave differentials, a perceptual screen, or a fixed cortical rhythm. SAN proposes that those physical dynamics may supply one implementation; that proposed identification is not yet established neuroscience.

## Origin
The clearest internal statement of the FEP in the SAN GitHub corpus appears in `03san.md`, the chapter that situates Friston alongside [[entification]], [[neural-strange-loop-self-referential-consciousness|strange loops]], synchrony, and the [[fractal-consciousness|fractal nature of consciousness]] (`03san--md`, line 106, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/03san.md)): "Karl Friston's work on active inference and the free energy principle proposes that the brain is constantly trying to minimize the difference between its predictions of sensory inputs and the actual sensory data it receives." The next paragraph of `03san.md` ties the FEP directly to SAN's [[cortical-column|cortical-column]] architecture, casting the [[prefrontal-cortex|PFC]]-generated Beta waves as top-down predictive modulation of sensory-[[cortex]] Gamma waves, with [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] supplying the substrate over which "this dynamic interaction between Beta and Gamma waves forms a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-regulating system that refines its model of reality through active inference" (`03san--md`, lines 108–114, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/03san.md)). Because Friston's published FEP (Nature Reviews Neuroscience, 2010) predates the SAN manuscript's explicit naming of it, SAN does not claim chronological priority over the FEP itself; what is SAN-original is the *integration* — the claim that FEP-style variational minimisation is what the brain's oscillatory phase-realignment is *physically* implementing. The packet's 50 evidence quotations across 16 sources confirm that FEP is a load-bearing external concept SAN engages with repeatedly across the manuscript and supporting notes.

## Position
SAN's position composes three integration hypotheses that attempt to connect the FEP's statistical description to a wave-mechanical implementation. First, the brain's [[deterministic-wave-based-consciousness|deterministic, wave-based]] [[consciousness]] is a *physical analog* of FEP-style free-energy reduction: as `07san--md`, line 67 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)) puts it, "This idea aligns with frameworks like Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle, which says the brain is always trying to reduce the difference between its predictions and sensory inputs (i.e. reduce 'surprise' or entropy) in a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organizing way ... SAN theory's wave-based [[consciousness]] suggests the brain is constantly realigning phases to reconcile discrepancies (a physical analog of reducing free energy)." Where Friston's text gives a variational-Bayes formalism, SAN supplies the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave]] mechanism and the [[oscillatory-binding-problem|oscillatory binding]] dynamics by which the realignment happens. Second, FEP fits inside SAN's tomographic stack: `08san--md`, line 157 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/08san.md)) writes that "Integrating NDCA's explicit link to 'predictive coding' with SIT's alignment with Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle (FEP) offers a powerful explanatory framework. The FEP posits that biological systems, including brains, act to minimize their variational free energy, which is a proxy for prediction error or 'surprise.' In this light, each 'instantiation of the screen' ... can be conceptualized as a transient, locally optimal solution to the brain's continuous, dynamic effort to predict its sensory inputs and minimize this free energy." This proposes the [[perceptual-screen|perceptual screen]] as a candidate time-step in an FEP-style minimisation. Any fixed screen-burst duration, rhythm, or convergence signature requires direct measurement and should not be inferred from the metaphor alone. Third, the FEP fits SAN's broader physics ambitions: `09san--md`, line 158 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/09san.md)) writes "NDCA's link to 'predictive coding' and SIT's alignment with the Free Energy Principle suggest each screen instantiation is a transient, optimal solution in the brain's effort to predict sensory inputs and minimize free energy. The timing reflects moments of model convergence, and the shape is the content of this converged predictive model." The screen's *timing* tracks model-convergence events; the screen's *shape* is the content of that converged predictive model — both co-determined.

A fourth, more speculative move appears in the personal notes. `a0103z--md`, line 28 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0103z.md)) places the FEP in continuity with [[super-dark-time|dark-time]] and harmonic-oscillator physics: "Anything that isn't participating in the harmonic oscillation between entropic decay & extropic information (like the free energy principle) is like a bad point in the harmonic oscillator of space." This is SAN's gesture at promoting FEP from a brain-specific principle to a universal participation criterion in [[qgtcd-overview|QGTCD]]'s entropic/extropic harmonic exchange — a stronger metaphysical reading than Friston's own.

## Post-SAN

Friston's published FEP is a proposed unifying account of action, perception, and learning; it does not by itself settle a theory of consciousness or select one unique neural implementation. SAN's reading converges on the same minimisation but parts company on substrate. Where Friston's published treatment is comfortable with abstract generative models and posterior approximations (a probabilistic-inference framing), SAN insists the actual physical implementation is *phase realignment among coupled oscillators* — Kuramoto-like dynamics played out on [[neural-array-projection|neural arrays]], with [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|cortical-column oscillatory collaboration]] doing the hierarchical message-passing FEP describes only abstractly. The companion paper trail in `07san.md` makes the connection explicit by citing Strogatz on coupled oscillators alongside Friston, and by reading both through the lens of [[oscillatory-binding-problem|oscillatory binding]]. SAN also extends the FEP outside the brain in two directions external work has only partially explored: Friston, Levin, Sengupta and Pezzulo's 2015 free-energy-and-pattern-regulation paper (cited in `07san.md`'s reference list at line 116) anticipates the bio-extension; SAN's own [[criterial-causation|criterial causation]] and [[dark-time-theory|Dark Time Theory]] threads push further toward a physics-level reading. A testable SAN-specific hypothesis is that a model-update event should covary with a receiver-relative change in neural phase organization. A gamma-band coherence peak is one candidate signature, not a required consequence of FEP and not yet evidence for a discrete perceptual-screen instantiation.
