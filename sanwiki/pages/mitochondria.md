# Mitochondria

**Mitochondria** are the eukaryotic-cell organelles that produce [ATP](atp.md) via oxidative phosphorylation; in standard neuroscience they are treated as the metabolic powerhouse and calcium-buffer of the neuron, with growing 2010s/2020s evidence for mitochondrial-membrane dynamics, mitophagy, and neuron-glial mitochondrial transfer playing roles in synaptic function and plasticity. Within [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) mitochondria carry a *substantially stronger* role than the standard metabolic-substrate reading: they are the proposed *receivers* of action-potential-emitted electromagnetic waves and the *effectors* of a SAN-original *electromagnetic reinforcement-learning* mechanism that drives [ATP](atp.md) release, [protein synthesis](neuron-rank-protein-synthesis.md), spine growth, new receptor insertion, and glial signalling on the millisecond-to-hours timescale that bridges spike events and structural plasticity.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream literature treats mitochondrial signalling as biochemical (ROS, Ca²⁺, mtDNA-derived peptides) and treats neuronal EM emissions as *epiphenomenal* of synaptic activity. SAN's commitments depart from each. First, the *EM-receiver* claim is testable and falsifiable: mitochondrial respiration rate, [atp](atp.md) production, or [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md) should be measurably modulated by spike-frequency-band EM stimulation in isolated mitochondria, and the SAN line predicts a *specific* frequency-band sensitivity ("a certain type" of EM wave, `a0101z--md`, line 5, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0101z.md)) that is in principle measurable. Second, the *reinforcement-learning* claim reframes a metabolic-effector role as an *information-processing* role — the mitochondrion is treated as the cellular RL update operator, not just as the energy source for plasticity. This places mitochondria inside the [protein-synthesis-rank](neuron-rank-protein-synthesis.md) / [spine-temporal-memory](dendritic-spine-temporal-memory.md) / [structural-memory-encoding](ltp-structural-memory-encoding.md) cluster as the *triggering* rather than *enabling* component. Third, the SAN line explicitly puts mitochondrial stimulation upstream of [dopamine-mediated pattern selection](dopamine-as-pattern-selector.md) and the [criterial-causation / ATP-neurons](criterial-causation-atp-neurons.md) reading of decision-making — a stronger architectural claim than mainstream "mitochondria support synaptic plasticity" framings. None of this displaces the standard biochemical literature; it relocates mitochondria as *receivers and operators* of the EM-substrate that the [phase-wave-differential](phase-wave-differentials.md) / [NAPOT](napot-neural-array-phase-tomography.md) cluster argues is the [cortex](cortex.md)'s primary information variable.
