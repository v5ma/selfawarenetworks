![The SAN Wiki evidence-to-certification route: complete evidence donors bind to one stable, versioned atom; six review lenses protect its meaning; independent decisions and whole-page coverage lead to exact-hash certification; local, reviewed, accepted, and public states remain separate.](assets/generated/san/san-wiki-evidence-to-certification/san-wiki-evidence-to-certification-001.png#compact-html)

The SAN Wiki is source-faithful only if a reader can recover the strongest complete SAN argument,
the evidence used to evaluate it, and the exact review state of the current page. A matching phrase,
citation, title, graph edge, or file hash proves useful custody facts, but none of those alone proves
that the right claim was understood or that its scientific burden was checked.

This method therefore works at two levels:

1. each substantive claim is reviewed as a stable, versioned **atom**; and
2. the exact current page is certified only after every substantive line is covered by accepted,
   nonoverlapping atoms and the rendered reader is rechecked.

Certification is an auditable editorial and evidence state. It is not blanket proof of SAN, a
medical approval, a publication receipt, or authorization to make a local artifact public.

## Start with the complete argument

Atomization begins only after the complete source neighborhood has been read. The reviewer
reconstructs the original problem, definitions, examples, causal chain, qualifications,
dependencies, date, vocabulary, and intended operation. A short quotation or compressed paraphrase
cannot replace that reconstruction.

For historical SAN material, the review keeps at least four things distinct:

- what Micah wrote or said in the dated source;
- what the complete source context shows he was trying to explain;
- how the same operation can be stated precisely with current vocabulary; and
- which parts are established science, SAN synthesis, SAN-specific extension, or a proposed test.

Book 2 and its Evidence, Bridge, and Visual Atlases may donate chronology, source routes, claims,
references, bridge candidates, and visual requirements. They remain read-only evidence donors and
do not confer acceptance, certification, or authority on an atom merely because they contain it.

## One stable atom, one exact meaning

A substantive atom is the smallest review unit that can preserve one complete intended operation
without turning it into a slogan. Its stable identity survives review history; a new version records
a changed formulation or evidence binding without erasing the earlier record.

Each atom binds:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity | Stable atom key and explicit version |
| Locator | Exact page span and exact text |
| Meaning | Complete intended operation, not a neighboring label |
| Context | Source neighborhood and anti-strawman reconstruction |
| Genealogy | Dated sources and terminology boundaries |
| Science | Same preparation, scale, receiver, variable, conditions, endpoint, and claim burden |
| Evidence | Structured evidence identities with custody and purpose |
| Decision | Independent accept or reject record with rationale |

An exact excerpt and page hash are necessary custody evidence. They are not semantic acceptance. A
label attached to the wrong neighboring passage fails even when every byte matches. Likewise, a
citation attached at the wrong biological scale fails even when the paper is real and correctly
identified.

## Six checks protect each atom

Every active atom must pass all six lenses against the same claim:

1. **Source fidelity:** exact wording, date, author, custody, and intended operation agree.
2. **Full-context reconstruction:** definitions, causal chain, examples, qualifications, and
   dependencies are present.
3. **Strongest-form no-strawman:** the complete argument is reviewed rather than a quotation,
   compressed synopsis, or weaker substitute.
4. **Scientific fidelity:** preparation, scale, receiver, variable, conditions, endpoint,
   alternatives, and claim class match the literature being used.
5. **Narrative preservation:** the reader-facing explanation retains the original operation and
   necessary vocabulary instead of reducing it to database labels.
6. **Protocol and release integrity:** identity, version, evidence, decision, privacy, image status,
   exact bytes, and local-to-public boundaries remain explicit.

A pass in one lens cannot compensate for failure in another. Scientific checking does not replace
source recovery, and source faithfulness does not waive a scientific evidence boundary. The aim is
the apex semantic position where the strongest source-faithful SAN meaning and the exact scientific
result are represented together at the correct scale and claim class.

An apparent SAN-versus-science conflict is not an admissible terminal verdict. It keeps the review
open for context recovery, ontology translation, scale correction, variable separation, or causal
decompression until both meanings are represented without strawman compression. The reader page
then states the aligned component science, SAN synthesis, remaining hypothesis, and decisive test.

Use [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries|SAN Scale Ladder and Claim Boundaries]] and
[[consciousness-claim-and-evidence-ladder|Consciousness Claim and Evidence Ladder]] to prevent
evidence from moving silently across biological scale or claim level.

## Challenges cannot rewrite history

A later challenge, critique, or proposed correction begins as a non-operative submission. It does
not suspend, downgrade, replace, or erase accepted SAN content. Before it can enter review, it must
itself prove complete source reconstruction, strongest-form anti-strawman treatment, source
fidelity, same-scale scientific fidelity, narrative preservation, and protocol integrity.

Only after those gates pass may an independently reviewed proposal add a new atom version,
decompress a causal arrow, separate variables, add evidence or alternatives, or make an atom-local
clarification. Prior source wording, accepted decisions, rejected proposals, and genealogy remain
preserved so later reviewers can see exactly what changed and why.

This rule applies to PWD as it does to every SAN concept. [[phase-wave-differentials|PWD]] remains a
**core SAN concept**, not a secondary concept: a typed, receiver-relative departure from a declared
tonic pattern, with excitation, inhibition, and their interaction as coequal possible departures.
Neither sign defines PWD, and the differential must not be compressed into excitation minus
inhibition. This methods page explains review procedure; it does not rank or redefine SAN concepts.

## Page certification is a separate gate

Accepted atoms do not automatically certify a page. Whole-page certification checks the exact
current bytes and proves that:

- the entire page, including frontmatter, prose, tables, diagrams, captions, references, and
  attribution, was read;
- every substantive claim belongs to one active atom;
- atom locators and exact text match the current file;
- substantive coverage has no omissions or incoherent overlaps;
- every active atom has six passing checks and an independent decision;
- no unresolved proposal or admitted challenge remains in the certified set; and
- the exact page hash, links, privacy boundary, assets, desktop rendering, and mobile rendering pass.

Any page-byte change breaks exact-hash custody and requires a new bounded page check. Unchanged
atoms may retain their stable identities and prior history, but the new page bytes still need a new
whole-page receipt. Status is never inherited merely from a neighboring atom, prior page version,
search result, graph edge, generated page, or attractive infographic.

## Fast recheck without a corpus rescan

The ledger exists so routine verification can use indexed exact lookups rather than repeatedly
searching the complete data stack. A fast page recheck follows this path:

1. look up the page slug and compare its live SHA-256 with the certified SHA-256;
2. retrieve the active stable atom IDs, versions, locators, and exact text hashes;
3. verify the six check records and their structured evidence identities;
4. verify the latest independent atom decisions and whole-page certification receipt;
5. inspect any admitted challenge or pending proposal attached to those exact identities; and
6. rerun bounded reader, link, asset, privacy, desktop, and mobile checks for the exact page.

Aggregate counts describe the cached ledger state and use different denominators. Page inventory is
not page certification; pending proposals are not active accepted atoms; check rows are not pages;
evidence records are not atom-to-evidence links; and pages awaiting certification are not failures.
The infographic's line widths are therefore explanatory, not a proportional Sankey.

## Medical-grade image gate

Biological or clinical-looking artwork has a separate exact-image review. A model or human reviewer
may medically certify a frozen image for bounded SAN project use when the complete protocol passes:
source-faithful concept mapping, anatomy and physiology at the depicted scale, labels, routes,
laterality, preparation, units, caption, uncertainty, prohibited implications, and exact pixel hash.
Any pixel, label, caption, source, or claim-scope change requires a fresh review.

That certification means the exact SAN project image passed its declared medical-art checks. It does
not imply diagnostic, therapeutic, clinical, regulatory, or whole-theory approval. Third-party
logos, memes, television stills, comments, and unlicensed artwork are evidence or inspiration, not
SAN scientific artwork.

## Evidence state is not release state

The ledger keeps four release states distinct:

| State | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Local | Exact files exist on the D drive |
| Reviewed | Checks and decisions have been recorded |
| Accepted | The exact atom or page hash passed its required gate |
| Public | A separately authorized public projection exists |

Local files, accepted atoms, certified pages, commits, pushes, deployments, and public publication
are different events. No earlier state implies a later one. The current infographic and this page
remain local until a separate owner-authorized release action occurs.
