# Copyright Argument Jurisdiction Strategy

**Copyright Argument Jurisdiction Strategy** is the umbrella concept SAN uses to organise *which* legal venue and *which* body of intellectual-property doctrine should be invoked when defending priority and authorship of [[selfawarenetworks-github-wiki-pages]] material. It sits next to, but is distinct from, the patent track represented by [[gset-patent]], [[provisional-gset-patent]], [[provisional-patent-application-5]], and the [[san-patent-corpus]] — copyright governs the *fixed expression* of the work, while the patent track governs the *underlying claimed inventions* surfaced in the [[official-claim-set-non-provisional]] and rehearsed in the [[gset-attorney-presentation]].

The jurisdictional dimension matters because SAN's public record — including the [[selfawarenetworks-changelog-public-record]], the [[selfawarenetworks-github-chronology]], and the upstream [[selfawarenetworks-welcome-page]] — accrues in venues (GitHub, arXiv mirrors, blog posts, video transcripts) whose servers, terms-of-service, and DMCA hooks each pull copyright disputes toward different forums. A coherent strategy therefore picks a primary jurisdiction in advance, lines up the relevant deposit copies, and decides whether parallel filings (US Copyright Office registration, Berne-treaty automatic copyright, EU sui-generis database protections) are worth the cost given the live priority disputes documented in [[sit-vs-figshare-priority-dispute]] and [[popular-mechanics-gravity-priority]]. The point is not to litigate but to keep the option of litigating credible, so that [[plagiarism-equivalent-legal-distinction]] — the doctrine that academic plagiarism and legal copyright infringement are *not* the same cause of action — can be invoked deliberately rather than reactively.

In practice the strategy threads three workstreams that already have their own staging pages. The [[pendetta-legal-strategy]] sets the broader playbook (when to send a takedown, when to publish a rebuttal, when to escalate to counsel) and is the natural home for jurisdictional [[choice]]-of-forum decisions. The [[sit-history-corpus-svgn]] and the parent [[sit]] page — together with [[math-sit]] and [[history-of-sit]] — supply the dated technical record copyright would protect, including [[sit-hydrogen-first-principles-graphic]] and the long-form [[sit-hydrogen-part1]] derivation. And the [[selfawarenetworks-link-review-backlog]] feeds back the housekeeping signal: dead links, missing deposits, and unverified mirrors that would weaken any copyright claim before a tribunal. Together these threads define what "argument" actually means here — a documentary argument, built from timestamped artifacts, *before* any court is asked to choose a jurisdiction.
