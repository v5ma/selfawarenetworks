The *Layered Consciousness Model* is a SAN working hypothesis proposing that consciousness emerges through a hierarchical sequence: intelligence precedes [[attention]], which precedes base sensory awareness, which is followed by temporally persistent oscillating learning patterns that generate goals and [[gh-a0206z|self]]-identity.

## The Sequence

1. **Intelligence** — the foundational layer; present even in simple cells without [[gh-a0206z|self]]-awareness.
2. **[[attention]]** — selective focus mechanism, grounded in tectum mapping (see [[attention-schema]] / Graziano).
3. **Base Consciousness** — raw sensory awareness; cells exhibit "conscious perception without [[gh-a0206z|self]]-awareness," with internal representations limited to sensory feelings and no contextual identity.
4. **Temporal Oscillating Patterns** — sustained learning patterns that accumulate across time.
5. **Goals and [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Identity** — the top layer, emergent from persistent oscillatory patterns.

## Key Claims

- All of reality is a computation on a universal oscillating system; the brain reads and writes oscillations.
- Space exhibits a dual nature: as a fractal computer system and as a continuous liquid — both perspectives hold depending on observational velocity.
- [[sharp-wave-ripple]] sequences encode memory-prediction rendering; they implement the "reading" step in the oscillation cycle.
- **Dominant Phase Wave Differential Rhythms** govern discrete information processing across layers.
