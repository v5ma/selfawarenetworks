## Core Mechanism

In standard general relativity, mass curves spacetime. Here the mechanism is reversed: it is the **rate and intensity of particle interactions** that creates local time dilation, and mass is itself a downstream consequence of that accumulated interaction density. Areas of higher interaction density become "denser in time," drawing more particles inward — a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-reinforcing loop that explains planet formation from dust clouds as an emergent accumulation effect.

This positions gravity as an **emergent phenomenon** rather than a fundamental force tied to mass alone.
