## Position

In [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the frame of reference is what binds an [[agentic-viewpoints|observer's viewpoint]] to a particular slice of the [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|nested-scale stack]]. The frame-of-reference concept is what allows SAN to talk about [[quantum-super-time-position|quantum super-time-position]] and [[spacetime-geometry-observer|observer-dependent spacetime geometry]] without invoking a privileged inertial frame, and it is what allows the [[distributed-observer-vs-consciousness-module|distributed-observer]] reading of [[consciousness]] to coexist with [[split-brain-modular-observer|modular-observer]] readings: each module occupies a movable frame, and conscious experience is the reconciliation of frames.
