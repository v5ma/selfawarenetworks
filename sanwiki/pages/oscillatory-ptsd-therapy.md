**Oscillatory PTSD therapy** is a first-person SAN-informed therapeutic practice in which
trauma and stress triggers are reframed as oscillatory wave patterns and counteracted by
deliberately activating competing oscillatory patterns from other brain regions.

## Technique

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]] describes the method in a0127z: when a stressful thought arises, visualize
it as an oscillating circle representing its neural wave pattern. Then consciously generate
"oscillating waves from other parts of my brain" — effectively invoking competing
[[neural-oscillatory-synchronization]] patterns to interfere with and damp the stress pattern.

The therapeutic logic: if trauma is a persistent oscillatory [[attractor]] (a [[phase-attractor]]
the network re-enters under cue), then introducing counter-phase signals can disrupt the
[[attractor]] and prevent lock-in.

## SAN Theoretical Basis

[[napot]] frames perception and emotion as oscillatory projections from neural arrays. A
traumatic memory is, in SAN terms, an entrenched oscillatory representation that projects
itself when cued. Mindfulness meditation activates prefrontal regulatory arrays that project
counter-patterns — reducing the amplitude of the trauma oscillation.

The practice operationalizes the [[phase-wave-differential]] principle: what we experience
is the *difference* between competing wave patterns. By raising the amplitude of calm counter-waves,
the differential shifts even if the trauma signal persists.

## Relation to Established Therapies

This framework maps onto:
- **EMDR**: bilateral stimulation creates competing oscillations that reduce trauma response
- **Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction**: sustained [[attention]] trains regulatory arrays
- **Mirror Box Therapy** ([[body-ownership-statistical-matching]]): competing sensory signal
  cancels phantom pain pattern

In each case, a correctly calibrated counter-oscillation interferes with and weakens an
entrenched aversive pattern — consistent with SAN's oscillatory model.
