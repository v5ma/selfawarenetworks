The **fractal oscillatory tree** is the claim that reality organizes itself as a nested hierarchy of oscillators — from electrons to atoms, cells, cortical columns, brains, planets, galaxies — each level containing and being contained by oscillating systems, forming a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar branching structure.

## Core claim

Oscillation is not a property specific to neurons or to physics: it is a universal organizational principle. The same [[attractor]] dynamics that produce a neuron's [[action-potential|action potential]] produce planetary orbits and galactic rotation. Consciousness enters as the branch of this tree that models the tree itself — humans are "branches of an oscillating universe, effectively the universe experiencing consciousness."

## Synaptic pattern recognition within the tree

At the microscale, individual dendrites function as pattern recognition units. Each synapse represents a distinct input channel; inhibited synapses gate out unwanted signals, letting neurons respond selectively to specific pattern combinations while filtering others. When one channel is inhibited, signal energy concentrates in open channels, producing amplified selective responses. This local selection is the micro-instance of the tree's general organizing logic: oscillating branches that suppress noise and amplify signal.

## Memory mechanisms

Multiple storage substrates are proposed across the hierarchy:
- **Long-term** — stable synaptic configurations (structural)
- **Short-term** — temporary synaptic inhibition patterns (functional)
- **Speculative** — acoustic standing waves in microtubules (sub-cellular)
