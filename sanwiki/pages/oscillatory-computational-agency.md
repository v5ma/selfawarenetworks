**Oscillatory Computational Agency** is the umbrella framework in [[self-aware-network]] theory that spans three nested scales of oscillatory computation: [[cellular-oscillating-tomography]] (COT) at the cell, [[napot]] at the neural array, and Biological Oscillatory Tomography (BOT) at the whole-brain level.

## The three-level hierarchy

| Scale | Framework | Core claim |
|---|---|---|
| Micro (cell) | [[cellular-oscillating-tomography]] (COT) | Each cell encodes phase-wave differentials; cell-level Hebbian learning |
| Meso (neural array) | [[napot]] | Arrays of neurons project oscillatory patterns; tomographic reconstruction across columns |
| Macro (brain/body) | BOT | Global spectral map integrating neural and immune signals into coherent organismal state |

Intelligence is not localized at the macro level; it emerges from interactions across all three tiers simultaneously.

## Agentic brain: each cell as autonomous agent

The [[agentic-brain|agentic brain]] posits that every cell is a semi-autonomous learning unit — it can receive, encode, and transmit information independently of top-down instructions. Cognition and consciousness are *emergent* from the aggregation of millions of these micro-agents synchronizing and desynchronizing through phase transitions. This dissolves the traditional view that cognition resides solely in higher cortical structures.

## Deterministic oscillatory consciousness

A key claim: consciousness arises from **deterministic** oscillatory processes, not from irreducible emergence. Repetitive cycles of synchronization and desynchronization are inherently measurable mechanical phenomena. The brain's local oscillations, seeking equilibrium, weave into thought and awareness through feedback loops across scales — the same wave mechanics that govern cellular signaling govern cortical dynamics.

## Self-organization across scales

Feedback loops at the smallest scales (molecular oscillations, calcium influx, receptor modulation) coalesce into coherent large-scale patterns. Cortical columns mirror the same [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organizational process that individual cells perform — local configurations merge into broader cognitive states. This is a [[fractal-pattern]]: the same agentic logic repeats at cell, column, and whole-brain scale.
