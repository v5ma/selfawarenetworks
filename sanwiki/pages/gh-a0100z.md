# a0100z

This SAN note connects Micah's oscillator-spacetime hypothesis with a 2021 report on cosmological coupling and black-hole growth. The Phys.org article, published on 3 November 2021, summarizes a paper in *The Astrophysical Journal Letters* proposing that black holes could gain mass as the universe expands, without requiring new stellar-formation physics. Micah's original note reads that result as indirect support for the idea that oscillators participate in creating spacetime; the cited article supports the black-hole-growth premise, while the oscillator-spacetime interpretation remains Micah's hypothesis.

Source citation: [Phys.org, "New study proposes expansion of the universe directly impacts black hole growth"](https://phys.org/news/2021-11-expansion-universe-impacts-black-hole.amp). Research citation: Kevin S. Croker et al., "Cosmologically Coupled Compact Objects: A Single-parameter Model for LIGO-Virgo Mass and Redshift Distributions," *The Astrophysical Journal Letters* (2021), DOI: [10.3847/2041-8213/ac2fad](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ac2fad).
