# Soliton Mechanical Waves

**Soliton Mechanical Waves** are non-dispersive travelling pulses on neuronal membranes and through synaptic fluid that, in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], form one of several co-running signalling channels — alongside chemical, electrical, and electromagnetic waves — that participate in [[soliton-wave-mechano-neural-signaling|mechano-neural signalling]] and the [[concurrent-soliton-consciousness-channels|concurrent soliton-consciousness channels]] hypothesis. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s writing treats the soliton as a real biophysical carrier integrated with the [[membrane-potential|membrane potential]] and the [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|action-potential waveform]], not merely a metaphor for the spike.

## Position

Inside [[self-aware-networks|SAN]], soliton mechanical waves are positioned as one mechanical leg of a [[multi-medium-soliton-fields|multi-medium soliton field]] that runs in parallel with the standard neural-spike picture. The [[gh-veca|vecA]] line 10533 passage ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecA.md)) uses a camera/projector framing — "the camera system is each cell, and the projector system is each cell" — and reads soliton mechanical and electromagnetic waves as both sensing and transmitting channels of the cellular tomography apparatus, tying them to the same projection-tomography stack that carries [[bot-biological-oscillatory-tomography|BOT]] and [[neural-tomography|neural tomography]]. The [[gh-b0077y|b0077y]] line 271 enumeration places them on equal footing with chemical, electronic-field, and magnetic-field waves, consistent with [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s broader [[multi-medium-soliton-fields|multi-medium]] argument that the brain is not a single-channel signalling system. The [[membrane-potential|membrane potential]] and [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|action-potential]] pages capture the standard electrical view; this page captures the parallel mechanical-soliton view that runs underneath it.

## Post-SAN

In published neurophysiology the soliton model (Heimburg–Jackson, 2005 onward) competes with the Hodgkin–Huxley electrical model rather than running alongside it; [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s SAN treatment is distinctive in *combining* both — the [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|action-potential]] electrical event and the soliton mechanical pulse co-exist on the same membrane and contribute to signalling and to the [[brain-as-rendering-engine|rendering-engine]] account of perception. The testable consequence implied by [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s framing — that mechanical solitons carry information that the spike alone does not, and that this information is integrated by [[dendritic-leaky-membrane-coincidence-filter|dendritic membrane filtering]] and the broader [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]] machinery — diverges from spike-only neural-coding accounts and converges with the small but growing experimental literature on mechanical correlates of action potentials and on near-infrared optical signatures of spike events ([[near-infrared-action-potential-detection|near-infrared action-potential detection]]).
