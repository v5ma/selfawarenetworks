It presents gamma phase matching as a binding mechanism, theta-nested gamma cycles as universal working-memory slots, and action-potential decay as the source of a population gamma clock. These are distinct, circuit-dependent hypotheses rather than one established mechanism.

**Gamma oscillation** usually refers to a narrowband or rhythmic population signal in an approximate 30-100 Hz range. The exact range, peak frequency, duration, generator, and behavioral relation vary by species, area, layer, stimulus, task, and recording method. Gamma is not one universal 40 Hz brain clock.

## Distinguish the measurements

- **Narrowband gamma** appears as a spectral peak or rhythmic burst and can arise from interacting excitation, inhibition, membrane properties, and network delays.
- **Broadband high-frequency activity**, often called high gamma, can closely track local population spiking and sharp transients without being a sustained oscillation.
- **Spike-field locking**, **phase coherence**, **power**, and **cross-frequency coupling** are different measurements. One cannot substitute for another.

Ray and Maunsell dissociated visual cortical gamma rhythm from broadband high-gamma activity and showed that broadband high gamma tracked local firing more closely ([PLOS Biology, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1000610)).

## What gamma can do

Gamma activity can mark transient periods of coordinated excitability, sensory processing, attention, or memory in particular circuits. Bosman et al. found attention-related gamma synchronization between macaque visual areas ([Neuron, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2012.06.037)). Lundqvist et al. found brief, variable gamma and beta bursts during working-memory encoding and reactivation rather than a continuous slot clock ([Neuron, 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2016.02.028)).

These results do not establish that gamma binds every percept, carries all current content, or causes consciousness.

## SAN and GCS interpretation

In SAN, gamma can be treated as one candidate fast timing mode within the [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|Gamma Consideration Sandwich]]. The medically bounded version asks whether a gamma-band event changes receiver-specific communication or control within a declared pathway. It does not assign all sensory ink to gamma or all top-down context to slower bands.

The causal unit must name the source population, target population, conduction and synaptic delays, measured signal, task, and downstream consequence. A phase relation is informative only if the receiving population can use it.

## Tests

Separate oscillatory peaks from broadband power, spikes, evoked transients, muscle artifact, and line noise. Compare gamma-aware models against firing-rate, recurrent-state, stimulus, attention, and movement models. Then perturb a candidate generator or route and predict both the specific loss and its rescue.
