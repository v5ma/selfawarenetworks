**Inhibitory Neurons** are, in [Self](./gh-a0206z.md)-Aware Networks, the GABAergic cell population -- including parvalbumin- and somatostatin-positive [interneurons](./inhibitory-interneurons-plasticity-gating.md) -- that the SAN account treats as the *encoding substrate of the [tonic oscillation](./tonic-oscillation.md)* and the *bifurcation engine* of cortical signal flow, rather than as a subtractive control system. The SAN reading is most clearly stated in [a0206z--md](./gh-a0206z.md) line 43, where the [exit-terminal](./napot-revision-2-exit-terminal-sdr.md) [phase field](./phase-attractor.md) "becomes encoded inhibitory neurons that create the oscillating tonic [brainwave](./brainwave.md) patterns that unify cell assemblies to recurrent waves of expectation" ([a0206z--md](./gh-a0206z.md), line 43, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0206z.md)).

## Position

Inhibitory Neurons hold three positions in [Self](./gh-a0206z.md)-Aware Networks. *They are the tonic substrate*: per [a0206z--md](./gh-a0206z.md) line 43, the [exit-terminal](./napot-revision-2-exit-terminal-sdr.md) phase field is what gets *encoded into* inhibitory-neuron populations, which then *generate* the oscillating [tonic-phasic canvas](./tonic-phasic-canvas-of-consciousness.md) that unifies [cell assemblies](./hebbian-engrams.md) into "recurrent waves of expectation" ([a0206z--md](./gh-a0206z.md), line 43, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0206z.md)) -- a much stronger claim than "inhibition keeps excitation in check." *They are the bifurcation switches*: [a0364z--md](./gh-a0364z.md) line 14 reads inhibitory neurons as "train switches" implementing *[choice](./choice.md) bifurcation*, so that the [cortex](./cortex.md) can produce "a high number of possible signal patterns" precisely because inhibitory [choice](./choice.md) is binary at each junction ([a0364z--md](./gh-a0364z.md), line 14, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0364z.md)) -- same metaphor and same engineering function the [inhibitory interneuron system](./inhibitory-interneuron-system.md) page treats systemically. *They are the impulse regulator*: [a0577z--md](./gh-a0577z.md) line 38 names "the inhibitory neurons in the neocortex that regulate impulses from the lower brain structures, in addition nearby neurons, carving out a highly" structured signal ([a0577z--md](./gh-a0577z.md), line 38, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0577z.md)) -- the executive face of the same population. The bookkeeping note in [a0414z--md](./gh-a0414z.md) line 13 -- "a sizable portion of neurons are inhibitory neurons" ([a0414z--md](./gh-a0414z.md), line 13, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0414z.md)) -- fixes the population scale at the canonical ~20% of cortical cells, which the SAN account treats as the populational reservoir from which all three positional roles are sourced.

## Post-SAN

The Inhibitory Neurons page sits squarely on top of the modern PV/SST/VIP literature (Klausberger and Somogyi; Rudy, Fishell; Cardin; Sohal) and on top of Freund and Buzsaki's 1996 axoaxonic/perisomatic-targeting account, which [a0007z--md](./gh-a0007z.md) line 1013 quotes verbatim ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0007z.md)). Where the mainstream picture has interneurons setting gamma- and theta-rhythm timing for downstream computation, SAN agrees with the *rhythm-generation* claim and adds two specific testable extensions. First, [a0206z--md](./gh-a0206z.md) line 43's encoding claim predicts that the *spatial/cell-identity pattern* of tonically-active inhibitory neurons should be readable as a phase-field signature of the immediately-prior [exit-terminal SDR](./napot-revision-2-exit-terminal-sdr.md) -- falsifiable via paired calcium imaging of pyramidal exit terminals and PV/SST populations during predictable-vs-novel sensory streams. Second, [a0364z--md](./gh-a0364z.md) line 14's "[choice](./choice.md) bifurcation" framing predicts that targeted inhibitory-neuron silencing should not just degrade rhythm fidelity but *collapse the branching factor* of accessible signal patterns -- separating SAN's switching account from rhythm-only accounts. The SAN book chapter [04san--md](./gh-04san.md) (line 59) also notes a negative result -- "No involvement of specific inhibitory neurons: Neither parvalbumin-positive nor somatostatin-positive interneurons showed significant global oddball detection" ([04san--md](./gh-04san.md), line 59, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/04san.md)) -- which SAN reads not as evidence against the encoding claim but as evidence that *global oddball detection* lives elsewhere (apical [dendritic computation](./dendritic-computation.md), layer-1 NMDA), exactly where the SAN [apical-dendrite](./apical-dendrite.md) account places it. The packet's external-paper coverage is light, so the comparative wiring above is structural rather than citational.
