## Post-SAN

Mainstream functional-information theory (Hazen, Szostak, Walker and Wong) measures functional information as the log-probability that a random configuration achieves a specified function. Adjacent neuroscience literature on temporal coding, neural-population coding, and optogenetic readout converges on the temporal-feature claim from a different direction. SAN's distinct move is to fuse these two strands: the *thing* that the optical readout recovers and the *thing* that directs cellular response are the same kind of object, and both reduce to temporally structured coincidence-bits.
