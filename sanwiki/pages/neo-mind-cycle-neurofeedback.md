# Neo Mind Cycle Neurofeedback

Neo Mind Cycle was an exploratory San Francisco neurofeedback program developed by [[gh-resnote04|Micah Blumberg]] during the early 2010s. Surviving records describe scalp EEG, changing visual and auditory output, custom software mappings, participant reports, equipment problems, and a social salon setting. They establish a hands-on feedback practice, not a clinical treatment or proof that detailed thoughts were decoded.

## What the apparatus did

The documented operating idea was:

```text
brain, body, and environment
    -> scalp voltage plus artifacts
    -> selected feature and software mapping
    -> changing light or sound
    -> perception, strategy, movement, and expectation
    -> changed brain, body, and environment
```

Different source records describe different equipment, tracks, and audiovisual mappings. They should not be compressed into one universal three-modality protocol. Claims that a particular imagined movement controlled pitch, reverb, or another parameter describe intended or reported mappings unless synchronized raw data and controls establish reliable control.

## What scalp EEG measures

A scalp electrode does not record one neuron, one cortical column, or one thought. The measured voltage is a weighted mixture shaped by neural sources, tissue, source geometry, volume conduction, electrode position, reference, contact, eyes, muscles, movement, electronics, filters, and feature extraction. Recovering candidate generators is an inverse problem, not direct inspection of semantic content ([Nunez and Srinivasan, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780195050387.001.0001)). EEG and MEG reporting guidance therefore requires explicit acquisition, reference, signal-quality, artifact, and analysis information ([Keil et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.12147)).

The software mapping is also part of the experiment. If one extracted value changes brightness in one condition and pitch in another, the biological measurement may be the same while the learned task and sensory consequence differ. Apparent control could arise from the intended neural strategy, another neural strategy, eye or facial-muscle activity, posture, breathing, contact drift, instruction, expectation, or a changed threshold.

## Closed-loop learning

The durable engineering contribution is the contingent loop: a measured feature changed the participant's sensory environment, and that returned consequence could change the next measurement. Stronger implanted work has shown that biological activity, behavior, and a decoder can co-adapt during brain-machine-interface learning ([Orsborn et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2014.04.048)). That result supports the general need to model a changing user and interface. It does not validate Neo Mind Cycle, scalp-EEG thought decoding, or the historical explanation that loss of control reflected greater metabolic efficiency.

## Efficacy and safety boundary

The surviving records do not establish target-specific neural regulation, durable behavioral transfer, increased intelligence, personality integration, treatment of a disorder, whole-brain synchronization, or clinical benefit. Historical wellness and benefit claims remain part of the source record rather than accepted medical conclusions.

A modern reconstruction would need contingent, yoked or sham, and active-comparison conditions; synchronized raw EEG and output logs; prespecified targets and artifact rules; declared latency and mapping; participant blinding and expectancy measures where feasible; behavioral outcomes separated from reports; adverse-event and stopping procedures; and held-out transfer tests. The CRED-nf checklist separates target-specific effects from trainer, expectation, repetition, technology, cognitive practice, and natural change ([Ros et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaa009)). EEG-BIDS provides a standard route for preserving acquisition, electrode, event, and participant metadata ([Pernet et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0104-8)).

## SAN interpretation

Neo Mind Cycle is a historical and engineering precursor to SAN because it made a recurrent receive-transform-return loop tangible. It does not demonstrate NAPOT, a tonic canvas, phasic ink, a neural attractor change, a self-model, or consciousness. The bounded SAN proposal is that a living system may learn from transformed consequences of its own activity while the same distributed body-brain network supplies meaning and action. No software display or hidden inner viewer receives a completed mental picture.
