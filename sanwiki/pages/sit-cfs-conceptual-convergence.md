**SIT–CFS Conceptual Convergence** refers to the independent parallel development of core ideas between [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s [[super-information-theory]] (SIT) and Felix Finster's Causal Fermion Systems (CFS), where SIT established conceptual priority on several foundational claims later adopted in CFS publications (2024–2025).

## Key Convergences and Priority Timeline

### Spacetime as Emergent from Information
Both theories claim spacetime and fields emerge from a more primitive informational structure — correlations between quantum states in CFS, and the dynamics of the coherence field $\psi$ and time-density field $\rho_t$ in SIT. SIT articulated this from 2017 onward via the [[neural-lace-podcast]]; CFS adopted equivalent framing from its collaborators in April–July 2025.

### Coincidence Patterns → Web of Correlations
Blumberg introduced the **bit as coincidence pattern** of neural events in his 2017 [[neural-lace|Neural Lace]] Podcast — a relational, event-based definition of information. CFS adopted the "web of correlations" framing in 2025. See [[coincidence-as-a-bit]].

### Distributed Observation → Dynamical Collapse
[[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) presented observation as an intrinsic distributed process within an informational network — effectively solving the quantum measurement problem without an external observer. Finster published an equivalent "effective dynamical collapse" in CFS in 2024. See [[super-time-position]].

### Pervasive Oscillations → Continuous Underlying Field
Blumberg's 2017 framework of everything linked by pervasive oscillations (the bridge from [[self-aware-networks]] theory of mind to [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation]] theory of gravity) parallels Finster's later invocation of an underlying continuous field as spacetime substrate.

### Discrete Bits → Spacetime Emergence
The [[binary-cosmology-particles-waves]] claim (particles=1, waves=0) from 2017 predates CFS's explicit treatment of discrete elementary structures from which continuous spacetime emerges (post-2017 in Finster's publications).

## Status of Convergence

The convergence is one of **independent validation**, not derivation — Finster has stated he is unaware of SIT. The fact that two distinct mathematical programs reached functionally equivalent solutions on multiple foundational questions is taken as strong corroboration of the underlying correctness of both frameworks, with SIT holding conceptual priority.

## Not in SIT (as of Aug 2025 recording)

Finster's lack of supersymmetry treatment, his octonion-derived particle structure, and certain cosmological inputs (baryogenesis, dark matter pathways) remain CFS-specific. SIT's [[information-energy-density-law]] (Aug 2025) is absent from CFS.
