A speculative cosmological framework in which gravity, spacetime expansion, and
thermodynamic time are all emergent effects of a fractal hierarchy of oscillating
bodies — from sub-atomic particles to galaxies — each bending nearby configuration
space by concentrating synchronized oscillations.

## Core Claim

Every mass is a bundle of oscillators. When particles synchronize into atoms, molecules,
planets, or galaxies, their collective oscillation warps the surrounding field: space
contracts *inside and around* the oscillator (gravity) while space expands in the
interstitial regions *between* oscillators. These two effects are linked, relative, and
inverted — a single oscillator dynamics seen from two vantage points.

This is the cosmological extension of [[path-integral-neural-connectivity]]: just as
[[dendrite]] spines grow toward co-firing neurons along the shortest path through
[[configuration-space]], particles and large masses drift toward each other because
oscillation densifies the field lines between them, creating more probable paths inward.

## Heat as Faster Local Time

In this framework, *heat is not a stored quantity but a temporal rate*: heated space
is moving faster in the temporal dimension than cool space. Particles that are in phase
(same oscillation frequency) can occupy the same region of space, creating a
Bose-Einstein-like condensate that registers as a local increase in phase cycles per
interval — experienced by neighboring matter as heat and gravitational attraction.

This connects thermodynamic arrows of time to oscillator density: regions with more
synchronized oscillators age faster, aligning with general relativistic time dilation
near massive bodies.

## Galaxy Filaments and Large-Scale Structure

Hot field lines between two massive oscillators (galaxies) carry higher phase density in
both directions simultaneously, folding the interstitial space and making particle
trajectories more likely to curve toward those filaments. Over cosmic time this produces
galaxy filaments and sheets — the web of large-scale structure — as a natural output of
the same path-minimization principle operating at the neural scale.

Beyond a mass threshold, the horizontal divergence of energy between oscillators becomes
a repulsive effect, explaining the apparent accelerating expansion of space between
galaxy clusters as the elastic reaction of configuration space to extreme oscillator
concentration.

## Fractal Scale Symmetry

The mechanism operates identically at every scale:

- **Sub-atomic**: electrons/protons settle into orbitals whose phase states prevent
  collision (different phase frequencies create paths that orbit rather than merge).
- **Atomic/molecular**: constructive phase interference along bond axes forms stable
  molecules.
- **Neural**: [[path-integral-neural-connectivity]] — dendrites grow along phase
  gradients between co-firing cells.
- **Planetary/stellar**: mass concentrations bend spacetime via collective oscillation.
- **Cosmic**: galaxy filaments, voids, and the overall web form from the same
  [[attractor]] geometry.

This scale-invariance is a core [[fractal-pattern]] in SAN — the thesis that one
principle threads quantum, biological, and cosmological phenomena.
