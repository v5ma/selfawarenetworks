# Functional Connectivity

**Functional Connectivity** in the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] reading is the *temporary, phase-locked coherence* between distant neural groups — the dynamic complement of static structural connectivity, in which "distant neural groups form temporary coherent circuits by locking phases" through [[tonic-oscillation|tonic-phase]] re-settling after input ingestion ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 26). The corpus is explicit that it is *established by neural tuning*: "when synaptic frequencies are changed, this is the topic of neural (synaptic) tuning combined with functional connectivity, because functional connectivity is established by neural tuning. It's analog computing in…" ([[gh-02san|02san]]--md, line 291) — making functional connectivity the *output* of the [[synaptic-computation-ltp|synaptic]] / [[synaptic-ion-channel-tone|ion-channel-tone]] tuning machinery rather than a separate physical layer. The phenomenon scales: "ors, pairs of receptors, dendrites, soma's, neural circuits, [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]], and cortico-thalamic functional connectivity have discreet thresholds, phase wave differential rhythms have discrete information" — the same connectivity logic recurs from receptor pairs to [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]] to cortico-thalamic loops, always carrying [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differential]] content.
