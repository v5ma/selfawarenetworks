## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the primitive that gets recognised, stored, transmitted, and modulated is not a spike, not a rate, and not even a pure phase — it is a *phase-temporal-spatial pattern*: a structure in which the phase relationships between coupled cells unfold over time and across the cortical sheet in a particular geometry. The [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves]] page handles the readout side: a [[criterial-causation]] reader recognises a pattern of this kind by threshold-matching against a stored template. The [[spatiotemporal-neural-firing-patterns]] page handles the firing-side: the propagating wave of action-potential timing is the observable trace of the underlying phase pattern. The [[neural-phase-pattern-propagation]] page handles the dynamics: how the pattern travels across the [[brain-as-spectral-graph]] under the constraints of [[cortical-traveling-waves]], [[gamma-oscillations]], and [[theta-oscillations-background-scripts]] coupling. The [[ltp-tempo-spatial-rhythmic-memory]] page handles the storage: the [[long-term-potentiation]] / [[ltp-structural-memory-encoding]] machinery is what *memorises* a phase-temporal-spatial pattern by reshaping [[dendritic-morphology-memory]] so the same pattern can re-form on the same population later. And the [[beliefs-as-tempo-spatial-sequences]] / [[belief-as-spatiotemporal-sequence]] pages identify what the pattern *is* at the cognitive level: a belief, a percept, an episode, an emotion ([[emotions-as-spatiotemporal-patterns]]) is a *trajectory* through this pattern space rather than a stored proposition.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream neuroscience has been moving toward spatiotemporal-pattern accounts of neural representation for two decades — the cell-assembly tradition descending from Hebb, the hippocampal-replay literature on temporally-precise sequences, the [[cortical-traveling-waves]] empirical programme, and the broader recognition that timing structure carries information rate-codes miss. The [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks reading converges on those results but extends them in a specific direction: the relevant primitive is not "spike sequence" or "firing-rate timecourse" but *phase pattern across coupled oscillators*, with [[phase-wave-differentials]] as the formal coordinate. The [[dpwdr]] formal language and the [[brain-as-spectral-graph]] reading then re-frame the spatiotemporal pattern as the dynamics of a wave on a graph rather than a sequence of localised events. The testable wedge is that SAN predicts that what mainstream studies call "spike sequence" should map cleanly onto a [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm]] when both are measured simultaneously — a prediction the rate-coded and pure-spike-sequence accounts do not make.
