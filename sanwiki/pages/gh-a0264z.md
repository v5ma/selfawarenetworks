What I have written to VFW so far, and their responses/questions:
I would like to contribute to the research and development of new medical tools by combining [[virtual-reality|VR]], [[artificial-intelligence|AI]], and [[bci|BCI]]. To start, we can bring the community of [[neurotechnology|neurohackers]] from [[hack-days-neural-lace-noisebridge-2017|Noisebridge]], but I am also a member of the global neurohacking community called NeurotechX, which is larger than Noisebridge. NeurotechX has 21 chapters globally, but we do not yet have an office for NeurotechSF, so we have been working out of Noisebridge. That has been great, but space is very limited because many other projects are also happening there, including woodshop work, sewing, music creation, and other projects. Those projects are valuable, but they leave very little room for neurohacking, meaning the development of new medical tools.

Our current project combines a new portable EEG device with [[webxr-bci-integration|WebVR]], an [[oculus-go-standalone-vr|Oculus Go]], TensorFlow for [[artificial-intelligence|AI]], and a Raspberry Pi to act as a local server. Since we are focused on fundamental technology research, our product is open to the community, so companies and other medical researchers are allowed to use it to make products that could benefit people with serious medical conditions. The prototype works, and we can demo it now, but some features still need to be implemented, such as FFT, AI, and the Pi, so it is not finished yet.

The next project is going to involve EIT, or electrical impedance tomography, again with [[webxr-bci-integration|WebVR]], another [[virtual-reality|VR]] headset, volumetric video, and [[3d-cross-hair-cnn|3D Cross-hair Convolutional Neural Networks]]. The goal is to create or acquire a 3D experience, acquire 3D [[3-medical-imaging-technologies-2018|medical imaging]] with good spatial resolution beyond what EEG can offer, closer to fMRI and perhaps improved beyond fMRI, and then use object segmentation through [[3d-cross-hair-cnn|3D Cross-hair convolutional neural networks]] to correlate [[brainwave|brainwave]] patterns with patterns in the 3D experience and establish next-generation nerve correlations.

We could benefit from a much larger space, and possibly help with applying for grants to get funding for this project. I would very much like to help real people such as the people at the Veterans of Foreign Wars.

I am also a member of another team called The Vision Agency, which combines EEG, ECG, and other sensor technology with [[virtual-reality|VR]]. One of our products has a website at MicrodoseVR.com and was featured at the Smithsonian this past summer and at CES 2018 by Intel and HTC. MicrodoseVR is intended to be the kind of app that we hope doctors will someday prescribe to their patients for a variety of illnesses, including PTSD, pain relief, anxiety relief, and more. The MicrodoseVR team has been looking to create an alternative health center for a long while. We would like to have people use our VR products equipped with [[eeg-vr-biofeedback|biofeedback sensor technologies]]. At the same time, we want to keep doing fundamental research to create new technologies or new applications from novel combinations of existing technologies.

Sheridan mentioned that the VFW group might be interested in having a neurohackers group use the empty space at the Veteran's building in SF.

We would like to do that by building a [[neurotechnology|Neurotech Research]] facility with computers to develop EEG and EIT into [[virtual-reality|VR]], virtual-reality software development stations, and VR experience stations for veterans and the public.

We have some hardware to start with, we have a number of EEG machines, at least 1 pc, maybe more soon, we have an EIT medical imagining device that is the size of a mini refrigerator. 

We also have the ability to invite the people from [[hack-days-neural-lace-noisebridge-2017|Noisebridge]] to share the space and bring their [[virtual-reality|VR]] station if they wish. We have four VR headsets.

So that's the first thing, we would like to use the space in the Veteran's building to move our operations into that.

The second thing would be that we would like to apply for grants, or funds to acquire more equipment, and even a paid staff to build technology and test it on whomever wants to come through the doors to try out our demos.

I am also an event and community organizer, so with that space we can host regular events, hackathons, and conferences, inviting Bay Area university researchers and Silicon Valley companies to give talks, demonstrate new research and technologies, and potentially become collaborators with us.

I would love to be able to attract University researchers to come work with us.

So the space would first be for [[neurotechnology|neurohackers]] and developers to work on creating novel combinations of existing technologies, such as combining [[bci|BCI]] with [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] and [[virtual-reality|VR]], and to develop new core technologies or teachable paths for using core technologies in new ways. This is the goal of NeurotechSF and its parent organization, NeurotechX.

Second it would be to engage with the community to develop applications for these core technologies, hackathons, classes, and conferences with talks and demos. (San Francisco Virtual Reality is a meetup group that has been doing this for years)

We can also bring in MicrodoseVR, which is a product designed with well-being in mind that incorporates EEG and ECG into [[virtual-reality|VR]].

So the first step is to see that space, to make our pitch to use that space, to win over folks and move our equipment to that space to start using it.

We share and publish our research code on our [[selfawarenetworks-github-repository|GitHub]] account, which is currently at github.com/micah1/neurotech.

With an official space we will be able to grow, and apply for funding, and potentially help create tools that medical professionals can use, and tools that can help the Veterans of Foreign Wars who will be able to visit the Veterans building to try anything that we are working on.
