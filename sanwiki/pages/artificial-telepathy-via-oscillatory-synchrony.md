# Artificial Telepathy Via Oscillatory Synchrony

**Artificial telepathy via oscillatory synchrony** is a historical SAN hypothesis recovered from
the 2022 recording *Towards artificial telepathy?* The recording explores how two people with a
shared history can develop mutually predictive timing models. It also briefly entertains a
stronger possibility in which a remote change in one system affects an unmeasured global signal
available to the other.

Those are different scientific claims. The first can be studied through ordinary perception,
memory, prediction, and joint action. The second requires a physical communication channel that
has not been established.

## The Source-Faithful Distinction

### Model A: Shared-history prediction

Two people repeatedly exchange visible, audible, tactile, linguistic, or device-mediated cues.
Each nervous system separately learns temporal regularities about the interaction. Later, a delayed
message, changed cadence, altered expression, or missing expected event produces a prediction
error inside one person.

The bounded operator is:

**reciprocal interaction -> learned temporal model in each person -> later cue or omission ->
receiver-specific expectation mismatch -> memory retrieval and inference -> report or action**

This can feel intuitive or "telepathic" because the learned prediction is fast, implicit, and
partly unavailable to verbal introspection. No signal has to travel directly from one brain to the
other at the moment of inference.

### Model B: Direct remote coupling

The recording also asks whether previously coordinated oscillations could remain coupled after the
people are physically separated, so that a state change in one person alters a global signal
detected by the other.

That claim cannot be explained merely by saying that both people once oscillated together. A
post-separation effect requires:

1. a declared physical variable;
2. a source and transmission medium;
3. a distance and shielding relationship;
4. a receiver and coupling mechanism;
5. timing precise enough to distinguish the event from chance;
6. prospective trials including failures; and
7. controls for communication, shared schedules, prior expectations, public events, and selective
   memory.

No direct interbrain oscillatory channel currently meets those requirements. This stronger branch
remains a source-preserved SAN hypothesis, not an established mechanism.

## What Established Research Supports

Research on joint action supplies a real scientific foothold for Model A:

- [Dumas and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0012166) measured behavioral and EEG
  coordination while pairs spontaneously imitated one another.
- [Kourtis and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22671131/) found evidence that people
  represent a partner's action while preparing a joint action.
- [Sanger and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35029645/) reported interbrain
  synchronization related to pianists' compatible temporal plans during a silent pause, while
  explicitly testing shared sensorimotor and surrogate-pair alternatives.
- A common informative stimulus can synchronize
  [brain, heart, and eye measurements across viewers](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36712806/).
  Similar measurements therefore do not automatically imply direct coupling.

These studies show mutual adaptation, shared task structure, temporal anticipation, and correlated
measurements. They do not show one brain perturbing another at a distance, a shared neural
attractor spanning two skulls, transfer of memory, or a merged observer.

## What "Oscillatory Synchrony" Can Mean Here

The phrase must be scale- and measurement-specific:

- **Within one nervous system:** neural populations can exhibit phase relationships, rhythmic
  coordination, and state-dependent excitability.
- **Between people during interaction:** behavior, speech, movement, autonomic signals, and EEG
  summaries can become statistically aligned through reciprocal cues and common task structure.
- **After separation:** similar expectations or schedules can persist independently in memory.
- **Direct remote coupling:** would require a measured cross-person causal channel, not merely
  correlated rhythms or similar reports.

The 2022 recording's statement that a small pattern "scales up" is best treated as a recruitment
and influence hypothesis: a learned local pattern can participate in progressively broader
within-brain activity and behavior. It does not establish that one synaptic pattern is copied
unchanged into a whole-brain waveform or into another person.

## Attention, Expectation, and the Later PWD Interpretation

The recording connects attention with the increased influence of a selected pattern. A bounded
interpretation is that attention changes which representations guide prediction, report, and
action. When expected social timing fails, the mismatch changes the receiver's own recurrent state.

Later SAN language can describe that receiver-relative departure as a phase-wave differential
(PWD), provided the later term is not backdated into the recording. The measurable question is
whether a multidimensional timing and state description predicts the person's next update better
than elapsed time, explicit expectation, arousal, ordinary prediction error, and behavioral cues.

## Microtubules Are a Separate Branch

The recording speculates that microtubules could preserve backups of synaptic configurations. That
proposal is not required for the shared-clock operator. Long-term memory already has established
synaptic, cellular, and systems-level mechanisms, while storage of complete synaptic-state backups
in microtubules has not been demonstrated.

The microtubule proposal should therefore be tested and sourced on its own page. It must not be
used as the missing causal bridge between two people.

## A Discriminating Experiment

Use familiar pairs and compare:

1. live reciprocal interaction;
2. a time-matched recording of the partner;
3. a yoked recording from another pair;
4. temporally scrambled cues;
5. expected-contact omissions;
6. physically separated, fully blinded no-cue trials; and
7. preregistered sham event times.

Measure behavior, confidence, explicit expectation, autonomic state, each person's neural signal,
and all ordinary communication channels. Analyze separately:

- within-person prediction;
- interpersonal behavioral coordination;
- similarity between recorded signals;
- incremental prediction from relationship history; and
- any claimed remote event effect.

Model A is supported if relationship-specific temporal learning improves prediction when
informative cues or omissions exist and the effect survives matched controls. Model B requires a
prospective, time-locked effect during fully blinded no-cue trials, replication across laboratories,
and a measurable physical channel. Failure of that stronger test does not invalidate the
shared-history model.

## Two Distributed Observers

Each person remains a separate distributed observer-action system. Similar timing, coordinated
behavior, or correlated EEG does not create a single field, memory, subject, or identity. The
relevant observable is how each nervous system transforms its own inputs, history, and current
state into its next perception and action.

## Why the Image Failed

The quarantined figure tried to show shared training, persistent expectation, and mismatch
detection. Those are useful ideas. Its arrows nevertheless showed a perturbation in Mind B causing
a mismatch in Mind A after both were linked through a central "shared oscillatory attractor." That
visual asserted the remote causal channel that the caption denied.

A medically and scientifically bounded replacement would use two panels:

1. **During interaction:** public sensory and behavioral arrows connect the people while each brain
   independently learns a temporal model.
2. **After separation:** no arrow connects the people; an expected cue, observed omission, or
   preregistered test event updates only the receiving person's internal model.

A third, clearly dashed experimental panel could show the unestablished remote-coupling hypothesis
with question marks and the required channel, shielding, timing, and sham controls. No replacement
art is generated while image work remains paused.

## Source Genealogy

The primary source is the
[Whisper transcript publicly fixed October 31, 2022](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/dab2de5c4a1d23936662d28cecc224205165d2fa/b0105ywhisper.md).
Its original recording date remains unresolved. The transcript explicitly says the proposed
connection may be "telepathy that isn't telepathy at all," grounds part of the effect in learned
cadence and expectation, and then moves into a stronger remote-oscillation speculation. The present
page preserves both branches instead of making the stronger branch silently govern the weaker one.

The 2026 generated page added a shared attractor spanning two people, a direct perturbation path,
global-pattern detection, and microtubule-backed shared-clock persistence as if they formed one
mechanism. Those additions were not established by the source or the literature. The July 2026
scientific warning was directionally correct but did not remove the contradictory prose or figure;
the current repair does.
