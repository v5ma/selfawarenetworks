Gravity as gradient time dilation is a SAN-adjacent theoretical position proposing that gravitational attraction is not a primary force but an emergent consequence of time dilation gradients operating at the particle and atomic scale.

## Core claims

The framework proposes several layered hypotheses (each explored in separate notes in the selfawarenetworks repository):

1. **Gravity = gradient time dilation**: Gravitational effects arise because time runs at different rates at different mass densities. The gradient of that rate differential is experienced as force.
2. **[[gravity-time-wave-electrical-force|Gravity as time wave in electrical force]]**: Gravity is the inverse-mass component of a time-wave embedded in the electromagnetic (electrical) force — suggesting a deep unification between electromagnetism and gravitation.
3. **Quantum gravity from oscillator-flow space warping**: At quantum scales, gravity emerges from space warping around [[oscillator-flows]], linking gravitational theory to SAN's oscillatory-network framework.
4. **Quantum gradient time crystal dilation**: Time-crystal-like periodic structures at the quantum scale produce the dilation gradient that macroscopic gravity represents.
