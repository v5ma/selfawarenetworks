Current evidence does not establish one anatomical gate, one phase pattern, or one render surface that converts subpersonal processing into consciousness.

**Conscious access** refers to information becoming available for flexible report, working memory, reasoning, planning, and downstream control. It is narrower than consciousness as a whole and stronger than the mere presence of stimulus information in neural activity.

## What access does not mean

- A classifier decoding stimulus information does not show that the subject consciously accessed it.
- A late frontal or parietal response can include report, confidence, decision, and motor preparation.
- Early sensory activity can carry substantial information without flexible access.
- Access is not automatically identical to phenomenal experience.

Studies using masking, attentional blink, and no-report designs show that stimulus processing, perceptual organization, awareness, and reportability can dissociate. The relevant neural operations are therefore distributed and task-dependent rather than one universal threshold in prefrontal cortex.

## SAN interpretation

SAN proposes that access occurs when receiver-relative changes are preserved and re-expressed across enough recurrent pathways to affect memory, comparison, report, and action. [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-wave differentials]] and [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] are candidate descriptions of this propagation, not established names for the access mechanism.

The term **render** is acceptable only as an operational shorthand for a distributed, action-ready state. It is not a screen and does not create an inner viewer. The same participating cells and circuits that carry and transform the content are part of the system that later uses it.

## Testable formulation

For a specific content, declare:

1. the sensory or internally generated source;
2. the neural variable and timescale;
3. the receiving populations;
4. the recurrent or long-range routes;
5. the downstream capacities gained when access occurs; and
6. a perturbation predicted to remove those capacities while preserving earlier processing.

The phase-aware model must outperform matched rate, connectivity, recurrent-state, confidence, and motor-preparation models on held-out trials.

## Primary anchors

- Dehaene and Naccache formulated global availability as an experimentally tractable access problem ([Cognition, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0010-0277(00)00123-2)).
- Fahrenfort et al. found perceptual integration that remained decodable despite impaired conscious access ([PNAS, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1617268114)).
- Hatamimajoumerd et al. compared report and no-report masking conditions and found that apparent frontal involvement depends partly on measurement design ([Current Biology, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.07.068)).
