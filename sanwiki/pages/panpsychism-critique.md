# Panpsychism Critique

**Panpsychism Critique** is the position, articulated across the [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks corpus, that [[consciousness]] is not a fundamental property attached to every bit of matter but a specific dynamical regime — an [[observer-mechanism]] — that arises only when the right kind of [[oscillating-network-of-oscillators]] closes a [[consciousness-as-temporally-active-loop]]. It sits opposite both classical panpsychism and its modern integrated-information descendants, and it is the explicit alternative to the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness]] framing that motivates panpsychism in the first place.

## Position

The [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks reading of [[consciousness]] is mechanistic and local: an observer is what a [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine]] produces when its [[tonic-phasic-neural-oscillation]] dynamics close a [[consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop]] over a [[brain-as-rendering-engine]] state, and the [[observer-as-aggregate-expectation]] page formalises which population statistic counts as the observer at each moment. That commitment leaves no room for proto-[[consciousness]] as a property of arbitrary matter. The Panpsychism Critique is the negative half of that move: claims that "everything is a little bit conscious," that micro-experiences combine to produce macro-experiences, or that integrated information above some threshold automatically *is* phenomenal experience are read as smuggling the explanandum into the explanans rather than mechanising it. The [[hard-problem-hidden-emergent-properties]] page makes the same critique against the constructive-emergence move, and the [[integrated-information-theory]] page records SAN's specific objections to IIT's identification of phi with experience. Where panpsychism dissolves the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness]] by declaring it solved by fiat at the substrate level, the SAN critique reads the dissolution as evasion — the question is not "why is there experience at all" but "what mechanism, in this brain, at this time, *renders* this experience," and the [[neural-rendering-of-qualia]], [[oscillatory-qualia-imprinting]], and [[consciousness-persistent-rendering]] pages are the constructive answers [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] substitutes for the panpsychist move.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream [[consciousness]] studies has been hospitable to panpsychism in recent years — Goff's constitutive panpsychism, Strawson's monism, Chalmers's "naturalistic dualism" sympathy, and the Tononi–Koch reading of [[integrated-information-theory]] as metaphysically committed to experience as fundamental — and the [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks position is in explicit disagreement with that drift. SAN accepts the descriptive claim that integration and oscillatory coupling matter; it rejects the metaphysical claim that those quantities *are* experience. The [[observer-mechanism]] account predicts that experience appears only when a [[minimum-observer-circuit]] closes — that is, only when [[criterial-causation]] readout, [[neural-rendering-of-qualia]] phase patterns, and a [[consciousness-as-temporally-active-loop]] feedback regime are all simultaneously present — and that prediction is empirically distinct from the IIT prediction that any system above a phi threshold has experience. Where panpsychism makes [[consciousness]] explanatorily cheap by spreading it everywhere, the SAN critique makes [[consciousness]] explanatorily expensive but mechanistically trackable.
