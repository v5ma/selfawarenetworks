Within the [[qgtcd-dark-time-theory]] framework, neural oscillators exhibit **bidirectional magnification**: small patterns scale upward through the cortical hierarchy (bottom-up amplification) and large patterns impose structure downward (top-down shaping). ## The magnification analogy

A single neuron's activation, when synchronized with neighbors, magnifies into a meso-scale inhibitory or excitatory wave — the "photocopier enlargement" of a fine-grained pattern into a coarser population-level pattern. [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] proposes the same temporal expansion mechanism governing gravity (time-area differentials around mass) also governs this magnification: the temporal density of oscillatory activity increases near cortical columns acting as local "mass centers."

## Bidirectional fractal cascades

Unlike unidirectional feedforward models, [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] oscillatory magnification is bidirectional:
- **Ascending**: synaptic patterns → cortical columns → thalamus → global phase
- **Descending**: global phase → column reset → synaptic modification

This bidirectionality is what distinguishes [[self-aware-network]] from a simple feedforward classifier: the network can modulate its own lower levels using its higher-level state, implementing the real-time [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modification central to [[napot]].
