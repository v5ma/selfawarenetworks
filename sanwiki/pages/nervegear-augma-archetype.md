The **Nervegear / Augma archetype** is a cluster of science-fiction technologies that serve as the explicit design target for [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s BCI research. All share the property of being **non-invasive, full-fidelity** brain-computer interfaces capable of two-way signal exchange with the living brain.

## The archetype defined

Two fictional devices anchor the archetype:

- **NerveGear** (Sword Art Online): a VR headset worn during sleep that transfers full consciousness into a virtual world. Non-invasive implication — no surgery. Speculative real-world analogue: laser-Doppler holography + ultrasound, or laser fNIRS holography (cf. Openwater.cc).
- **Augma** (SAO: Ordinal Scale): NerveGear's successor, capable of sending signals to the brain while **awake** — used for [[augmented-reality|augmented reality]], not just full-immersion VR. This is the direct ancestor of the [[artificial-cortex]] vision.

## Why Micah cites these

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] named nervegear/augma-level BCI as one of his three life goals (2012). The archetype defines the *bar*: a device that can read and write neural patterns at sufficient fidelity to merge biological cognition with an [[artificial-cortex]] or a [[self-aware-network]] of other minds. This is the applied goal that SAN theory is meant to eventually enable.
