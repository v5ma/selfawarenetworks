# NAPOT Revision Genealogy

> **Boundary note (2026-07-18):** This genealogy preserves historical revision deltas; it is not a ladder of equally validated mechanisms. Older keyboard, printing-press, anti-spike, inceptive-field, and quantum-biological language must be read through corrected dendrite/axon physiology, scale labels, and current evidence posture. See [[receptive-inceptive-dendritic-projection]], [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries]], and [[phase-language-firewall]].

Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography (NAPOT) was not written once and merely renamed. Successive versions changed the level of resolution and the problem being addressed: arrays, synapses, inhibition, dynamic subnetworks, multiscale routing, motor consequences, quantum-biological branches, nested agency, sensory reconstruction, and neural manifolds.

## Why the revision history matters

Flattening all NAPOT material into one summary hides the intellectual work. The revision ladder shows which question each version added, which concepts continued into the modern neuroscience line, and which branches became separate physics or evolution programs.

The stages are not ten equally validated theories. They are dated changes in a developing model.

## Before the numbered revisions

`b0153y.md`, Git-fixed on June 19, 2022, distinguishes transformed neural slices from a complete
image copied everywhere and develops the neural-oscillatory-tomography wording.

The `b0131y` family supplies an even earlier operation with layered dates. Its October 14, 2021
recording asks how repeated partial descriptions, transformed cortical passage, multisensory
coincidence, recurrent scale influence, and coupled assemblies can jointly constrain a model. A
lower-quality transcript was publicly Git-fixed on June 8, 2022. The substantially expanded June
21 `b0131y.md` adds tonic context, rare deviations, a Shannon bridge, location-tolerant
distributed reconstruction, and stronger biological and cross-domain hypotheses. The current
route stabilized on August 28.

Together these sources establish a pre-revision reconstruction problem:

```text
partial view
-> receiver-specific transformation
-> recurrent comparison with other views
-> stable-enough current model
```

They are pre-revision genealogy anchors, not retroactive NAPOT stages. The stronger claim that
neural relations implement an identifiable inverse solution remains a hypothesis with its own
measurement and perturbation burden. See
[[tonic-context-rare-deviation-tomographic-language]].

`a0036z.md` is a separate early named-NAPOT bridge. Its June 8 source already contains the
external-to-inner-voice and absent-other-model proposal. The July 29 expansion explicitly names
NAPOT and describes one neural array rendering transformed sensory or conceptual information for
a later array. This belongs before the numbered ladder without being assigned a retroactive
revision number.

The June Cascade sources add another pre-revision operator. [[gh-a0141z|a0141z]] and the source
renamed from `a0332z ctpr.txt` to [[gh-a0159z|a0159z]] describe successive arrays detecting,
transforming, and re-expressing patterns under changing head position and orientation. This helps
explain what the later receive-transform-project loop does internally. It is preserved as
[[cascaded-array-perspective-transformation|Cascaded Array Perspective Transformation]], not given
a retroactive revision number.

Two additional pre-revision ancestors are now linked. [[gh-a0348z|a0348z]], internally dated May
13, 2011, joins partial temporal-spatial sensory contributions, pattern completion, prediction, and
one continuously updated multisensory picture. [[gh-a0337z|a0337z]], first Git-fixed June 12,
2022, describes a local learned pattern unfolding into a larger distributed phase pattern as
different arrays contribute transformed relations. Neither source receives a retroactive NAPOT
revision number.

### The pre-revision ensemble receiver

[[gh-a0136z|a0136z]], first current-path Git-fixed June 11, 2022, already names NAPOT and describes
an output population receiving many signals, transforming them through learned synaptic and
dendritic state, and emitting a new population update. [[gh-a0137z|a0137z]], internally dated July
12, 2021 but first Git-fixed in 2022, adds a mesoscopic phase-reset proposal: enough coordinated
excitation or inhibition changes an ensemble's collective timing and therefore its effect on
another ensemble.

This is a substantive bridge from a dendritic receiver to a population receiver. It is not given a
retroactive revision number. Established phase reset and entrainment make the bridge testable, but
do not establish whole-brain awareness, a universal cortical-column oscillator, or the claim that
slower activity is always more powerful.

## Current-perspective and external-tomography bridge

`a0054z.md` supplies a pre-revision receive-transform-project account in which successive
populations construct the perspective currently usable by the organism. `a0069z.md` distinguishes
external array tomography, which reconstructs prepared tissue, from the proposed intrinsic NAPOT
operation performed by living neural arrays. The analogy motivates experiments; it does not make
the mechanisms identical or create an eleventh numbered revision.

## The branches, not just the sequence

- NAPOT 2 and 4 deepen cellular and synaptic resolution.
- NAPOT 3 makes inhibition and below-baseline events first-class information questions.
- NAPOT 5 and 6 connect multiscale routing to output, action, and functional reconfiguration.
- NAPOT 7 is an outward quantum-physics branch toward QGTCD, not the main modern neuroscience line.
- NAPOT 8-10 broaden the account toward nested agency, reconstruction, manifolds, and action.

This branching structure is more accurate than treating every stage as a linear replacement of the previous stage.

## Date boundaries

Revisions 2-6 are routed by repository changelog and source notes from August through November 2022. NAPOT 7 was developed in the December 2022 branch but entered later Git collections. NAPOT 8-10 were explicitly reassigned around January 2, 2024 in the recovered collection.

Event date, claimed development date, first Git date, publication date, and scientific validation remain different facts.

## Recovered NOAT-to-NAPOT bridge

[[gh-a0043z|a0043z]], Git-fixed on June 10, 2022, says the proposal had previously been called
**Neural Oscillating Array Tomography (NOAT)** before becoming NAPOT. It compares overlapping
anatomical sensor/transmitter arrays with functional edge communities while explicitly noting that
functional connectivity is not anatomical connectivity. This records a name and problem-history
transition; it does not validate neural tomography or anatomical arrays. The later spacetime and
programmable-matter discussion in the same note belongs to the QGTCD/SIT history branch.

## Why NAPOT 11 is not registered as stable

One collection asks for NAPOT 11, 12, and 13 in eight words and associates them with emotion, choice and social cognition, and BCI. Elsewhere the source explicitly reassigns versions 8-10. The 11-13 labels may belong to a book-cycle numbering scheme rather than a settled scientific revision ladder.

A stable NAPOT 11 requires at least one of:

1. an authored source explicitly titled as revision 11;
2. a dated transcript defining its delta from revision 10; or
3. a changelog entry joining the number to a specific mechanism.

Until then, candidate material remains preserved in the graph without manufacturing a false sequence.
