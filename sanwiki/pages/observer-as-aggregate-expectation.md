# Observer as Aggregate Expectation

**Observer as aggregate expectation** is an early SAN formulation in which distributed learned
predictions help define the system's current self-world relation. The phrase should not be read as
saying that every neuron predicts, that prediction is the whole observer, or that an expectation is
a separate entity watching perception.

## Current bounded interpretation

1. Learned cellular and circuit structure constrains expected input and possible action.
2. Ongoing neural and bodily state supplies a context for evaluating new evidence.
3. Sensory and recurrent differences alter that expectation.
4. The altered distributed state changes recognition, sampling, autonomic response, or action.
5. Returning consequences update the next expectation.

The observer-action operation is the whole receive-transform-use loop. Expectation is one important
component, not a synonym for consciousness.

## What is established and what SAN adds

Established neuroscience supports predictive influences, recurrent processing, sensorimotor
expectations, error-sensitive learning, and distributed self-related processing. SAN adds the
interpretation that the sparse field-cell system registering differences against aggregate
expectation is itself the observer process.

The stronger identity and phenomenal claims remain hypotheses. Better prediction alone does not
show greater awareness, and a prediction error does not automatically become experienced content.

## One observer is a useful level of description

Human behavior often supports one coordinated point of action and report, but the implementation is
distributed and can partially dissociate. Split-brain studies show substantial specialization and
task-dependent disconnection without resolving every question about the number or unity of conscious
subjects. Hemispherectomy and hemispherotomy outcomes show remarkable plasticity in selected cases,
not ordinary function after simply removing half of a typical adult brain.

- [Pinto et al. (2017), divided perception and preserved unity after split-brain surgery](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/aww358)
- [Kliemann et al. (2019), functional connectivity with one cerebral hemisphere](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.10.067)

These findings constrain localization claims. They do not prove the SAN observer mechanism.

## Relation to self-models

A self-model is a learned, revisable relation among body, memory, goal, action, and returning
evidence. The embodied organism extends beyond that model, and the model remains incomplete. SAN
therefore rejects both a permanent ego-object and a hidden metaphysical witness as neural mechanisms.

## Test requirements

A useful test must manipulate a defined expectation while separately measuring sensory
representation, confidence, report, autonomic response, action, and no-report physiology. The SAN
claim gains support only if a receiver-relative field-cell variable predicts and causally changes
the loop beyond ordinary predictive, recurrent, movement, and arousal accounts.
