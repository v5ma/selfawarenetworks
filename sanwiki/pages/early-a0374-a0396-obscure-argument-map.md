## Internally cued memory is more than a lookup table

The fuller `a0382z.md` source asks whether active dendrites and recurrent loops let one internally
reconstructed state become the cue for another. The mature operation is:

```text
external or internal partial cue
-> state-dependent dendritic and circuit transformation
-> associative reconstruction
-> active prediction, memory, or concept state
-> new internal cue and route bias
-> next reconstruction or action
```

This is **autogenous associative retrieval**: the process is physically caused, but its next cue
need not be a new environmental event. The source's "information-driven" language becomes
testable when the preceding represented state predicts the next transition beyond sensory input,
arousal, movement, and a conventional recurrent-network baseline.

`a0382z.md` and `a0131z.md` are source variants rather than two independent discoveries. The former
preserves more of the discussion; the latter edits and names the Self-Activating Content-
Addressable Memory branch. Both were first Git-fixed in June 2022, while the internal conversation
date remains a separate provenance claim.

## A break in rhythm can be the informative event

`a0383z.md` adds an operation missing from coincidence alone:

```text
repeated timing relation establishes an expectation
-> a coincidence, omission, delay, or changed relation violates it
-> thalamic and cortical receivers selectively transform the departure
-> the changed population state updates perception, attention, memory, or action
```

Modern auditory work distinguishes repetition suppression from genuine deviance responses across
midbrain, thalamus, and cortex. Visual work identifies a cooperative pulvinar, VIP, and SOM circuit
that selectively amplifies unexpected stimuli in V1. These studies establish candidate operations
for a SAN rhythm-break test. They do not establish one universal thalamic comparator or prove the
specific PWD code.

## Multisensory space and the observer inside the loop

The internally dated `a0377z.md` treats sight and hearing as spatial representations and extends the
same geometric intuition to smell, taste, body state, and thought. `a0378z.md` then describes a
chain of sensing circuits that recursively changes itself and constructs an observer-self inside
the loop.

The constructive SAN descendant is a distributed model whose components encode relations and are
themselves altered by the pattern they help construct. No represented apple, table, screen, or
single neuron becomes a miniature observer. The later spiritual language in `a0377z.md` is retained
as philosophy and is not used to validate the neural mechanism.

`a0395z.md` and `a0354z.md` preserve another source-variant lineage. The shorter file contains the
physical-dependence, quantum-soul, recursive-movie, and piano-key discussion; `a0354z.md` preserves
additional comments and date detail. Their overlap strengthens textual recovery, not the count of
independent evidence sources.

## Volumetric rendering as a BCI design ancestor

The May 2017 public `a0374z.md` route asks how light-field capture, volumetric video, point-cloud
streaming, interactive scene reconstruction, and GPU rendering might inform a future Neural Lace.
The reusable architecture is:

```text
partial multi-view capture
-> geometric and appearance reconstruction
-> observer-relevant interactive rendering
-> compressed transmission
-> perceptual display and action
```

For BCI research, the display becomes a measurement-and-intervention problem: estimate a
person-specific neural and body state, infer the relevant perceptual relation, deliver a bounded
write signal, and verify the predicted perceptual or behavioral consequence. `a0379z.md` adds
structural imaging, tractography, computation, and metabolic cost as separate constraints.

## A measurement is not its visualization

`a0393z.md` distinguishes three layers that must remain visible in every SAN instrument claim:

```text
sensor output
-> calibrated signal features
-> inverse model or visualization
-> biological interpretation
-> held-out behavioral or perturbational test
```

An EEG graph and a 3D activity visualization can derive from the same measurements while making
different assumptions. fMRI measures hemodynamic signals related to neural activity rather than a
brainwave directly. The strong SAN use is a demand for declared forward models, calibration,
spatial and temporal resolution, uncertainty, and validation at every inference layer.

## Orch OR and the machine-recognition question

The April 2013 `a0384z.md` debate asks whether microscopic quantum processes could contribute to
thought and how consciousness could be recognized in a machine. It predates the mature SAN
measurement proposal but does not supply an operational definition. Its modern value is to expose
two requirements:

1. every proposed microscopic event must cross an explicit scale-translation path into membrane,
   circuit, behavior, or a declared conscious endpoint; and
2. machine-consciousness assessment must use architecture, perturbation, integration, memory,
   action, report, and counterfactual controls rather than substrate vocabulary alone.
