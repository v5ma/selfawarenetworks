# SAN Brain Summary Source Map

This page organizes the dated SAN recordings and public source pages that explain how local neural reception, transformation, transmission, and memory could scale into distributed neural rendering and action. It is a source map, not evidence that SAN or NAPOT has been experimentally established.

The new large-v3-turbo retranscriptions were used internally to recover the intended operations. They are not public citations, and any quotation still requires comparison with the original audio.

## Recovered Organizing Question

The source family asks how a network can construct and maintain a usable model of the body and world without moving perception into a hidden inner viewer.

The recurring operation is:

**many local receivers -> state-dependent integration -> transformed neural output -> recurrent array-to-array exchange -> persistent distributed state -> memory-guided perception and action**

In this account, a neuron is not literally a camera, microphone, screen, or speaker. Those analogies identify receiving and transmitting roles. The proposed observer is the distributed network process itself: many cells register, alter, and re-express partial state while ongoing activity supplies context for later differences.

## What Established Neuroscience Supports

- Dendrites perform passive and active, spatially structured integration rather than merely collecting scalar inputs. See [Stuart and Spruston, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26605882/).
- EEG, MEG, ECoG, and local field potentials arise from transmembrane currents whose measured form depends on cellular geometry, population coordination, distance, tissue, and the recording method. See [Buzsaki, Anastassiou, and Koch, 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22595786/).
- MEG provides millisecond-scale measurements of magnetic fields associated with neuronal currents, but inferring the underlying sources requires a constrained inverse solution. See [Baillet, 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28230841/).
- Some neurons have intrinsic oscillatory or resonant membrane properties. This does not make every neuron a pacemaker or prove a universal neural clock. See [Llinas, 1988](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3059497/).

These findings support component mechanisms. They do not by themselves establish the complete SAN architecture.

## SAN Hypotheses Recovered From the Sources

### Distributed Observation

Neural receiving, transformation, and transmission are distributed across the network. No single cell or cortical location contains the finished percept or watches an internal screen.

### Array-to-Array Scaling

Local activity may be transformed and re-expressed through successive populations so that a learned local pattern can participate in a larger network state. This is not literal magnification of a microscopic picture.

### Tonic Context and Phasic Difference

Ongoing population state supplies a receiver-relative context or canvas. Relatively differentiated phasic events supply changing information or "ink." The scientific target is a measurable change in receiver access, timing, routing, or consequence, not the metaphor by itself.

### Sensory Stabilization

Recurrent sensory evidence may constrain an internally generated model across time. SAN proposes that dreams, waking perception, memory, and artificial agents can be compared by how external and internal signals stabilize or fail to stabilize a continuing state.

### Memory-to-Action Continuity

The recovered sources repeatedly connect perception and memory to coordinated movement. A useful model must therefore specify how distributed state changes influence motor planning, vocalization, or other action, and how returned sensory consequences update the next cycle.

## Corrections Without Erasure

- **Sensor/transmitter** becomes a receive-integrate-project motif, not literal audiovisual hardware inside a neuron.
- **Whole image** becomes a distributed, task-usable population state, not an optical image stored in one cell.
- **MEG map** becomes a model-dependent estimate of neural current sources, not a distortion-free photograph of brain activity.
- **Scale** means a proposed transformation across cellular, circuit, and distributed-network levels, not a universal assignment of frequencies to cortical layers.
- **LTP and LTD** are families of activity-dependent synaptic change, not simple synonyms for remembering and forgetting.
- **Tomography** requires declared latent variables, projection operators, reconstruction baselines, identifiability, and perturbation. Otherwise the surviving claim is recurrent integration.

## Scale Check

- **Micro:** synapses, dendritic branches, membrane state, spikes or bursts, axonal transmission, and synaptic release.
- **Meso:** local recurrent circuits, inhibition and disinhibition, oscillating assemblies, cortical and thalamocortical interactions, and population state.
- **Macro:** distributed body/world/action models, memory-guided behavior, report, and agency.

The three scales may constrain one another, but they are not interchangeable descriptions of one generic neuron.

## Source Roles

- Direct author recordings and dated author pages are genealogy sources.
- AI summaries are navigation aids and must not replace the underlying author text or audio.
- External papers can support, constrain, or falsify component mechanisms; they do not date SAN claims.
- Generated Fit/Jigsaw clusters are retrieval evidence only. They do not establish that every co-clustered page belongs to one scientific concept.
