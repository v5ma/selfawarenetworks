Fractal Cooperative Oscillations is the SAN thesis that consciousness is "a fractal body of cooperative oscillations" containing [[expert-data-structures]] inside a globally aware [[self-aware-network]] — and that life itself, as a dissipative system, is defined by oscillation.

## Core Claim

"To be alive is to oscillate." Life is a dissipative system drawing parallels to fractal structures observed at cosmic, planetary, and neural scales. Consciousness is not separate from this oscillatory substrate — it is the highest-order [[attractor]] within it.

## Nested Hierarchy

The fractal structure spans from ions and neurotransmitters → synapses → dendrites → neurons → microcolumns → cortical columns → large-scale networks. At each scale, cooperative oscillation — not individual activity — is the functional unit. This is the basis for [[napot]]'s array architecture.

## Key Property: Spatial Blindness

Neurons operate as synchronized sensory units without awareness of their spatial position relative to one another. The global pattern (the "fractal body") emerges from local coupling alone — no central coordinator. This is structurally analogous to [[fractal-consciousness]] and distinguishes SAN from global workspace accounts that require a broadcasting hub.

## Sensory Modalities as 4D+ Systems

Auditory, visual, and somatosensory pathways converge via thalamic structures into at least four-dimensional spatiotemporal representations. The extra dimension beyond 3D space is the oscillatory phase dimension, consistent with [[neural-timing-encoding]].

## Orch-Or Connection

The text references Orchestrated Objective Reduction ([[orch-or]]) — quantum microtubule collapse as the physical substrate of oscillatory binding. SAN holds a compatible but broader position: oscillatory cooperation is the mechanism regardless of whether the substrate is quantum or classical.
