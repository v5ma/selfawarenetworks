This image maps the page's core SAN reading: the brain remains coherent by operating as an open, far-from-equilibrium system. Energy and sensory drive flow in, entropy/noise/metabolic waste flow out, and oscillatory neural networks maintain ordered phase structure rather than relaxing to inert equilibrium.

The brain is a **dissipative system** — an open, far-from-equilibrium physical structure that continuously imports energy and exports entropy, analogous to hurricanes and galaxies. Unlike closed thermodynamic systems, the brain maintains its organized complexity by constantly cycling through sensory input and behavioral output.

This framing positions [[consciousness]] as a natural physical phenomenon rather than a domain separate from natural law. Neural activity follows deterministic physical laws; the rich structure of [[self-aware-networks]] emerges from this dissipative organization, not in spite of it.

## Key Claims

- **Unified with physics**: mind and matter are not separate — [[choice]] and [[intention]] emerge from underlying physical processes rather than originating outside them.
- **Connection density**: the density of synaptic connections in the human brain is comparable in scale to the number of visible stars, supporting the hypothesis that consciousness achieves a form of universal integration.
- **Symphony analogy**: neural populations function like instruments in a [[neural-symphony]], each following local rules whose global result is coherent experience.
