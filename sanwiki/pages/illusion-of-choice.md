**[choice](choice.md) is a predictive artifact**, not an independent volitional act. Within SAN theory the mind is a [prediction-machine](prediction-machine.md) -- a [self](gh-a0206z.md)-similar structure that automatically gathers information and generates the best prediction it can from available inputs. There is no observable mechanism of independent [choice](choice.md); behavior is the output of a causal chain, not a separate choosing agent.

The concept of [choice](choice.md) is itself a prediction -- a model the mind constructs to explain divergent behavior in others. When another person acts unexpectedly, the observer predicts a "[choice](choice.md)" as the cause. This framing has social utility but does not describe an independent faculty.

## Prediction-altitude

In place of [choice](choice.md), SAN introduces [prediction-altitude](prediction-altitude.md): as awareness expands and information quality improves, a person's predictions -- and therefore their apparent choices -- automatically improve. The balloon analogy: "No balloon can choose to stop rising toward the ceiling. The only reason some balloons rise higher is that their corner of ceiling is higher." Apparent differences in decision quality reflect differences in prediction-altitude, not degrees of freedom.

## Self-similar mind

The SAN framing grounds this in [self-similar-structure](self-similar-structure.md): the mind operates the same way at every level, from brainstem reflexes to abstract reasoning. Each level makes predictions. The "you" that seems to be choosing is a network of electrochemical reactions, a [self-as-collective](self-as-collective.md) -- a coordinated chorus of cells producing the prediction "I am."

## Relationship to free will debate

This position takes the illusion-of-free-will claim further than standard physicalism by identifying prediction as the one thing the mind does "in [summary](gh-b0049y.md)." It directly engages the subject/object distinction raised in phenomenological counter-arguments (Stark, [a0357z](gh-a0357z.md)): even the sense of effortful choosing is itself a prediction the system produces.

## History

First articulated in a 2011 forum exchange ([a0357z](gh-a0357z.md)). The prediction-machine framing and prediction-altitude metaphor pre-date the formal [NAPOT](napot-theory.md) framework but are consistent with it.
