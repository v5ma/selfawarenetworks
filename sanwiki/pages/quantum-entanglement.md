## Post-SAN

Compared with the mainstream-physics treatment of entanglement as a phenomenon described by quantum-mechanical product states with no preferred frame, SAN's distinguishing move is to take the "more dimensions than what are visible" framing seriously and to derive entanglement-like correlations as projections of higher-dimensional [[high-dimensional-coherence|coherence]]; this is the load-bearing claim of [[qgtcd]] and the [[quantum-supertimeposition]] / [[super-dark-time]] machinery in [[super-information-theory]]. Where the published quantum-cognition literature (see the companion entry [[quantum-cognition]]) typically uses entanglement either as a Hilbert-space modeling primitive or as a brain-substrate proposal à la [[orch-or-and-quantum-cognition|Orch-OR]], SAN's position is closer to a *geometric* reading: entanglement is what coherence looks like when you only see a slice of the higher-dimensional structure, and [[entanglement-phase-lock|phase-lock]] across neural [[oscillators]] is the macroscopic shadow of the same geometry. The TESTABLE [[prediction]] this framing makes that the standard treatment does not is that entanglement-correlation strength should be measurably modulated by [[gravitational-modulation-of-entanglement|gravitational time-dilation]] in a way standard QM does not predict — see [[gravitational-modulation-of-entanglement]] for the dedicated entry. Where the framing is, by [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s own acknowledgement, a notational/geometric reformulation of standard correlations rather than a new physical law, this article does not claim priority — only that the SAN/QGTCD framing makes the geometry explicit.
