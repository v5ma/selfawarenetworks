**Neural Rendering** is SAN terminology for the ongoing distributed construction and revision of a
body-world working model. The model is used for recognition, prediction, imagination, autonomic
regulation, sampling, and action. Rendering is an operation performed by the participating system,
not an internal picture delivered to a hidden viewer.

## The operation before the metaphor

1. Receptors, synapses, and dendrites receive partial, already transformed evidence.
2. Cells and local circuits combine that evidence with ongoing state and learned structure.
3. Recurrent sensory, thalamic, hippocampal, association, subcortical, cerebellar, and motor loops
   revise the current model.
4. Axonal and synaptic output redistributes consequential updates to other receivers.
5. Action changes the body or environment, and returning consequences constrain the next update.

No single neuron, cortical column, V1 map, field maximum, or bright integration point contains the
complete render. The render is the time-varying relation across the sparse participating network and
body.

## Tonic canvas and phasic ink

SAN uses **tonic canvas** for the relatively maintained comparison conditions of a receiver and
**phasic ink** for a consequential departure from those conditions. Baseline, phase, magnitude,
frequency, gain, and time window are receiver- and preparation-dependent. A phasic event is not
automatically conscious content, and a tonic rhythm is not automatically an observer.

Phase-wave differentials remain a bounded SAN hypothesis: timing and phase may add useful
information in some circuits, but a page must identify the biological receiver, show value beyond
correlated rate, power, waveform, movement, arousal, and history, and demonstrate causal use.

## Scale declaration

| Scale | What can be rendered | Biological denominator |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | A local transformed output, not a complete percept | Dendritic compartment, soma/AIS, axon, terminal, postsynaptic receiver |
| Meso | A local population update or task variable | Named recurrent circuit, population, layer motif, or thalamocortical loop |
| Macro | A revisable body-world working model | Distributed sensory, memory, body, action, and regulatory systems |

The term **tomography** is strongest at meso or macro scale, where multiple partial constraints may
support a reconstruction that no one input contains. At micro scale, one neuron illustrates a
receive-transform-project motif; it does not perform literal tomography merely by integrating
dendritic input.

## Established physiology and SAN claims

| Claim class | Content |
|---|---|
| Established neuroscience | Distributed sensory processing, nonlinear dendrites, recurrence, feedback, prediction, plasticity, population dynamics, and sensorimotor control |
| SAN interpretation | These components jointly perform a rendering-like receive-transform-re-express-update cycle |
| Stronger NAPOT hypothesis | Partial projections and oscillatory relations implement an identifiable reconstruction process |
| Consciousness hypothesis | A declared subset of the field-cell operation constitutes observer-action or phenomenal experience |

Evidence for one row does not automatically establish the rows below it.

## Relation to NAPOT, NDCA, and GCS

- [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] asks whether many partial states are combined and redistributed through a
  tomography-like multiscale cycle.
- [[ndca|NDCA]] names the continuity problem: maintaining useful derivatives and trajectories despite
  sampling, noise, delay, movement, and representational drift.
- [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|GCS]] is SAN's more specific historical proposal about sensory,
  contextual, action, and feedback timing. Its alpha, beta, and gamma assignments must be tested in
  named circuits rather than treated as universal anatomical departments.

## What would count as evidence

A useful test should specify the represented variable, scale, participating circuit, receiver,
behavioral role, and null alternatives. It should evaluate held-out reconstruction or prediction,
then selectively perturb a proposed route or timing variable and test whether the relevant model or
action consequence changes. A recognizable decoder output alone shows recoverable structure under
that measurement pipeline; it does not reveal the brain's native format or a literal internal movie.
