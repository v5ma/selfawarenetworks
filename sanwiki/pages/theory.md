# Theory

**Theory** is, as a SAN wiki entry, the meta-page that catalogues the *theories of mind* and *theories of physics* that [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] is in conversation with — both the ones it builds on (Hawkins's [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|memory-prediction]] thousand-brains framing, the [[free-energy-principle|free-energy / predictive-processing]] family) and the ones it explicitly critiques or supersedes ([[global-workspace-theory|Global Workspace Theory]], [[integrated-information-theory|Integrated Information Theory]], [[attention-schema|Attention Schema Theory]], Higher Order Thought Theory, Recurrent Processing Theory). The page also serves as the entry point to SAN's own physics-side theory, [[qgtcd|QGTCD]], and to its central informational claim, [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence as a bit]].

## Origin

The catalogue is most directly stated in the SAN to-do list: "These are the remaining notes that mention [[global-workspace-theory|Global Workspace Theory]], Integrated [[information-theory|Information Theory]], [[attention-schema|Attention Schema]] Theory, Higher Order Thought Theory, & Recurrent Processing Theory." (`00todolist--md`, line 32, [[gh-00todolist|on github]]). The framing of SAN itself as a *theory* — not a metaphor or a research programme — is set in the welcome page, which presents "[[gh-a0001z|Self Aware Networks]]: Theory of Mind" (`00welcome--md`, line 1). The same page family captures the explicit Hawkins lineage in the form of an aside: "y0017 - I'm describing a thousand brains theory mostly with a blend of other ideas." Together these establish that SAN positions itself as a *theory of mind* in the technical sense — comparable to GWT, IIT, AST, HOT, and RPT — built on the Hawkins memory-[[prediction]] substrate but extended in directions those theories do not reach.

## Position

Within the SAN exposition, the *theory* page makes three commitments. First, it commits to a survey: SAN does not pretend to be the only theory of mind, but to be a theory positioned against a specific set of named alternatives — [[global-workspace-theory|GWT]], [[integrated-information-theory|IIT]], [[attention-schema|AST]], Higher Order Thought, and Recurrent Processing — each of which is engaged on its own terms in the SAN chapters. Second, it commits to an empirical posture: SAN is taken to live or die by its testable claims about [[oscillatory-binding-problem|binding]], dendritic [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence-as-a-bit]] coding, and the [[thalamic-cortical-loop|thalamic cortical loop]], not by its terminology — and the SAN [[00linkreviewlist|link review list]] explicitly frames recent literature as "challenging a long-held theory about the functional role of spiking and oscillations in sequence memory" (`00linkreviewlist--md`, line 92, [[gh-00linkreviewlist|on github]]), aligning SAN with the reformulations that displace pure-rate accounts. Third, it commits to a publication strategy: "in theory you strengthen the ability of your work to survive by publishing in multiple sources" (`00changelog--md`, line 5; reproduced in `00preprint-list--md`, line 9) — a reflection on *theory* as something that survives by being widely published, distinct from the [[neuroscience]] meaning of "theory of mind." The result is a page that holds together SAN's external citations (the named theories), its internal anchors (`SAOv9`, [[self-aware-networks-book|the SAN book]], the `aXXXz` chapter manuscripts), and its physics-side companion [[qgtcd|QGTCD]].

## Post-SAN

The mainstream cognates SAN engages here are the named theories themselves — [[global-workspace-theory|Global Workspace Theory]] (Baars, Dehaene), [[integrated-information-theory|Integrated Information Theory]] (Tononi), [[attention-schema|Attention Schema Theory]] (Graziano), Higher Order Thought Theory (Rosenthal), and Recurrent Processing Theory (Lamme) — together with the [[free-energy-principle|free-energy principle]] (Friston) and the [[jeff-hawkins|Jeff Hawkins]] / Numenta thousand-brains theory. Where each of those frames [[consciousness]] either as a global broadcast (GWT), a system-level information integration measure (IIT), a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model (AST), a meta-representation (HOT), recurrent activity (RPT), or a generative [[prediction]] loop (FEP), SAN's *theory* is closer to a *substrate-level coding theory*: [[consciousness]] is what a [[self-aware-network]] *is* when its [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|cortical columns]] run [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence-bit]] coding through a [[thalamic-cortical-loop|thalamic cortical loop]]. The testable difference SAN emphasises is that disrupting the dendritic coincidence-detection layer should disrupt phenomenal binding even when global workspace, integration measure, [[attention-schema|attention schema]], and higher-order representations remain intact — a [[prediction]] the population-level theories do not make.
