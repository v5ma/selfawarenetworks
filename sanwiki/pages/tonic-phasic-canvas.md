# Tonic and Phasic Roles: Canvas and Ink

The **tonic canvas / phasic ink** model is SAN's functional description of how an ongoing neural
state can supply the conditions against which a consequential change is registered. The ongoing
state is the canvas; the change is the ink. The same participating network receives the change,
is physically altered by it, and carries its consequences into later perception, control, and
action. There is no separate listener or viewer.

## The medically bounded definition

**Tonic** and **phasic** are relative roles, not permanent frequency bands:

- **Tonic context** is a relatively maintained state over a declared time window. It may include
  membrane potential, synaptic conductance, firing probability, adaptation, neuromodulatory state,
  population activity, and the extracellular fields produced by participating cells.
- **Phasic difference** is a transient event or departure that changes a receiver's state and what
  that receiver can do next.

A signal can be tonic at one scale and phasic at another. A slow population change can be phasic
relative to a day-long state; a spike burst can be tonic within a short burst epoch. The labels do
not imply that tonic activity is always slow or high-amplitude, that phasic activity is always fast
or low-amplitude, or that all cells share one clock.

## The receive-transform-use chain

1. A receiving cell or population has an ongoing state.
2. Synaptic, sensory, recurrent, or bodily input changes that state.
3. Dendritic, somatic, circuit, and neuromodulatory dynamics transform the change.
4. Axonal output and synaptic release re-express some consequences to downstream receivers.
5. Recurrent and body-world feedback changes the next tonic context.

This chain is the biological translation of canvas and ink. The ink is not a substance painted on
a screen. It is a receiver-relative change in living tissue.

## What established neuroscience supports

Neurons and populations exhibit sustained and transient response modes. Oscillatory phase, spike
timing, bursts, adaptation, gain, and population state can influence processing in named circuits.
Those relations are preparation- and task-dependent. For example, the phase of thalamic firing
relative to slow cortical activity varies with thalamic nucleus and the exact ongoing cortical
pattern, while causal timing perturbation can selectively affect hippocampal memory encoding:

- [Bartho et al. (2011), nucleus-specific thalamocortical timing](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3375-10.2011)
- [Quirk et al. (2021), causal theta-timing perturbation during memory encoding](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-021-00919-0)

These findings support context-sensitive timing as a biological variable. They do not establish a
universal tonic carrier, a fixed phasic alphabet, or a consciousness code.

## The SAN interpretation

SAN adds the proposal that the coordinated **field-cell** state is an active comparison context.
Phasic cellular and synaptic events change both the participating cells and the spatially structured
electrical activity they produce. The network registers a difference by being changed by it and by
using that change in later recognition, prediction, autonomic regulation, sampling, or action.

The stronger claim that this operation is constitutive of observation or phenomenal consciousness
is a SAN hypothesis. It is not established by the existence of oscillations, extracellular fields,
or decodable phase relations alone.

## Scale ladder

| Scale | Candidate tonic context | Candidate phasic difference | Required receiver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | Membrane, synaptic, dendritic, and excitability state | Synaptic event, dendritic spike, burst, waveform change, or release event | Named dendritic compartment, soma/AIS, terminal, or postsynaptic cell |
| Meso | Local population state, inhibition, recurrent gain, or oscillatory relation | Ensemble transition, phase reset, traveling-wave passage, or routed population update | Named local or downstream circuit |
| Macro | Distributed brain-body working state | Sensory update, prediction error, action consequence, or state transition | Named distributed systems that alter later behavior or report |

The same abstract relationship can recur at several scales, but that does not show that a single
neuron performs tomography or that every local oscillator is conscious.

## What the historical model must not imply

- A neuron at rest does not necessarily fire regularly or tonically.
- Neighboring neurons do not generally share one perfectly synchronized baseline.
- A phasic event is not automatically a pattern-recognition event or conscious content.
- Synchronization restoration is not automatically integration, perception, or entropy dissipation.
- Inhibitory interneurons are not a separate audience listening to excitatory performers.
- A fixed 0/1/2/3-vesicle value is not a universal qualia code.
- Anesthesia does not erase one universal canvas while leaving all phasic variables independently intact.
- Cosmic, zero-point-field, magnetic-attraction, piano, and seismic language is analogy, not neural anatomy.

## When a biological difference becomes a code

A firing pattern, phase relation, waveform, burst, or vesicle count becomes a code claim only when
the page identifies:

1. the sender and receiver;
2. the recoverable distinction;
3. the advantage beyond correlated rate, power, movement, arousal, and history;
4. the receiver's causal use of the distinction;
5. the conditions under which the relationship fails.

Without those elements, the observation establishes biological richness, not semantic encoding.
