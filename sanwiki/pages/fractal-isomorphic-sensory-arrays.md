Fractal isomorphic sensory arrays is the observation that data-collection architecture repeats [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similarly from the single neuron up through cortical columns, whole sensory organs, and full body-surface arrays — a cross-scale fractal of function rather than merely of form.

## Core Claim

Across hands, eyes, ears, tongue, and smell the collection of data operates through arrays of receptors. At each scale — [[dendrite]], neural column, sensory epithelium, full organ — the same logical structure recurs: an array that spatially samples an environment and passes phase-encoded signals inward. This is the "hand-eye-neuron" fractal: not that these structures look alike, but that they perform isomorphic computational operations.

## SAN Framing

In [[self-aware-network]] theory, this fractal recurrence is not coincidental — it is proposed as a necessary consequence of [[napot|neural array phase oscillation tomography]]. Each array level reads the phase patterns produced by the level below and passes its own phase pattern upward. The result is a hierarchy of [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar phase-reading arrays that ultimately constructs conscious experience.
