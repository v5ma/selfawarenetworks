**Sharp Wave Ripple** (SWR / SWP) is the [[self-aware-networks|Self Aware Networks]] (SAN) corpus's name for a [[cortical-traveling-waves|traveling-wave]] event that begins as a [[burstlets|burstlet]] cascade and propagates across the brain as a binding signal — what `02san--md` line 27 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/02san.md)) calls "a burst of burstlets or waves [that] become a sharp wave ripple that ripples across the 3D pond of your mind as it causes other neurons to represent patterns." SAN treats the SWR as one of the operational mechanisms by which the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave-differential]] substrate distributes a locally-initiated pattern across the wider [[fractal-neural-hierarchy|fractal neural hierarchy]].

## Position

First, *binding*: `a0018z--md` line 95 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0018z.md)) explicitly couples the SWR to "scaling & magnification notes & SWP Sharp Wave Ripple binding" — placing the SWR as a binding event in the [[fractal-pattern-scaling-brain|fractal-scaling]] story. Second, *pattern propagation*: `02san--md` line 27 describes the SWR as the mechanism by which a localized burstlet-cascade "ripples across the 3D pond of your mind as it causes other neurons to represent patterns" — i.e. the SWR is the operator that transports a pattern from its initiation site to a wider population, which connects directly to [[neural-burst-backpropagation|neural-burst-backpropagation]] and [[memory-consolidation|memory consolidation]] readings. Third, *inhibitory gating*: `a0053z--md` line 26 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0053z.md)) embeds the SWR inside the [[phasic-tonic-relationship|Phasic-Tonic Relationship]], where "the high phasic spike … creates a burstlet, that creates an inhibitory wave (a sharp wave ripple across the brain) that starts with [its] direct exit terminal array, the phasic spikes are …" — making the SWR an *inhibitory* wave initiated at the exit terminal, not just an excitatory propagation. The exit-terminal mechanism is reinforced in `a0309z--md` line 9 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0309z.md)): "[the oscill]ation sets the timing of the [[neural-array|neural array]] defined by [its] exit terminal that is inhibited after the Sharp wave ripple" — i.e. the SWR closes a phasic-tonic timing window and the post-SWR state is one of inhibition. Together these three roles position SWR as the *binding-and-gating* event in SAN's account of how local pattern activity gets distributed and then closed off, with the [[napot-revision-2-exit-terminal-sdr|exit-terminal SDR]] mechanism specifying where the wave fires from. SAN also signals a non-linear-replay reading: the corpus describes how "old learned patterns can be triggered in a novel order, a nonlinear activation each part of a sharp wave ripple" — bringing the SAN account into explicit contact with the [[hippocampus|hippocampal]] memory-replay literature.
