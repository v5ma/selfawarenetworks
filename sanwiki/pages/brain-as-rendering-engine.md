# Brain as a Rendering Engine

![Brain as a rendering engine: learned structure, maintained readiness, partial cue, and distributed pattern re-expression](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/brain-as-rendering-engine/brain-rendering-maintained-readiness-pattern-reexpression-medically-reviewed-20260722-v1.png)

*Bounded medical-anatomy schematic (reviewed 2026-07-22; source-fidelity review 2026-07-28). The
earlier tonic-cache figure is preserved in the failed-medical-review archive. This replacement
separates learned structure, ongoing readiness, partial evidence, recurrent completion, distributed
re-expression, and feedback rather than placing complete scripts in one frequency band. Its
neurons are examples, not one universal circuit, and arrows between panels denote multistep
functional routes rather than direct monosynaptic connections.*

Calling the brain a **rendering engine** means that perception is actively constructed and revised,
not passively copied from a sensory surface. Receptors transform physical stimulation into neural
signals; neural and bodily systems combine those partial signals with context, memory, expectation,
eye and body movement, and action consequences; the resulting working state changes what the
organism does next.

There is no literal movie, screen, or localized viewer. The sparse, distributed process that builds
and uses the state is the observer-action operation proposed by SAN.

## The established biological loop

For vision, the relevant system includes retina, lateral geniculate nucleus, V1, extrastriate visual
cortex, parietal and temporal pathways, pulvinar and other thalamic routes, attention, memory, eye
movements, motor planning, and body-state feedback. V1 is in occipital cortex and contributes an
important early recurrent transformation; it does not contain the complete percept or sit inside
prefrontal cortex.

Comparable distributed loops apply to audition, somatosensation, proprioception, vestibular input,
interoception, and action. Feedforward and feedback processes can propagate with different temporal
and spatial structure. In awake mice, for example, visual stimulation has produced interacting fast
feedforward and slower feedback traveling waves across visual and higher-order cortex:

- [Aggarwal et al. (2022), visual-evoked feedforward and feedback traveling waves](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32378-x)

That result supports distributed recurrent coordination in a named preparation. It does not show
that the waves are a conscious picture or that one frequency mapping is universal across species.

## Rendering as an operation

1. Sensory receptors and body systems supply partial, transformed evidence.
2. Local cells and circuits combine it with ongoing state and learned structure.
3. Recurrent pathways revise boundaries, depth, identity, relevance, and expected consequences.
4. Distributed populations maintain an action-ready body-world model.
5. Sampling and action change the body or environment.
6. Reafference and other feedback update the next cycle.

The working model need not resemble an image file or a computer graphics buffer. "Render" names the
ongoing construction and use of structured relations.

## Why the current-view comparison remains useful

A graphics system cannot render every possible perspective at maximum detail at once. It preserves
or can regenerate a wider scene model while allocating work to the current camera, gaze region,
task, and likely next view. Gaze-tracked foveated rendering is a concrete example
([Patney et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1145/2980179.2980246)).

Biological vision solves a related bounded-resource problem. Eye and body movement change the
available evidence; attention changes competition among signals; memory and expectation influence
what is selected; recurrent processing updates an action-usable current state. The normalization
model of attention formalizes one established part of this selective operation
([Reynolds and Heeger, 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2009.01.002)).

The useful comparison is therefore:

```text
wider latent or environmental structure
-> current viewpoint and task
-> selective high-resolution processing
-> action
-> returned evidence
-> next current view
```

The comparison stops before implementation identity. SAN does not require cortex to contain a GPU,
framebuffer, camera pipeline, NeRF, or literal movie. Its stronger hypothesis is that distributed
neural and bodily systems implement a perspective-conditioned reconstruction whose changing state
is used directly by the same network for observation and action.

## Tonic context is not, by itself, a complete prediction cache

Recent activity, adaptation, working memory, synaptic state, neuromodulation, and learned structure
can bias what happens next. SAN calls the relatively maintained comparison conditions a tonic
context or tonic action field. A phasic event is a consequential departure from those conditions.

This does not justify treating theta activity as an always-on store of complete habits, thoughts,
or scenes. Nor does it establish that common states are metabolically cheap "shadow scripts" that
become conscious by amplification. Any cache claim must identify the substrate, content, receiver,
retention interval, energy measurement, causal perturbation, and alternative explanations.

The stronger medically compatible interpretation is that several different substrates can leave a
system ready to re-express a pattern. Learned connectivity and dendritic or synaptic state can
preserve relatively latent constraints. Recurrent population activity, inhibition, adaptation,
neuromodulation, and oscillatory phase can alter present excitability. A partial cue can then recruit
some of the learned ensemble, and recurrent interactions can complete or re-express more of the
distributed pattern. These processes operate at different scales and times; none is a miniature
scene stored inside a theta wave.

Examples of the component evidence include:

- Human perturbation experiments have recovered item-specific information from an
  activity-silent working-memory state: [Wolff et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4546).
- In awake mouse visual cortex, imprinted neuronal ensembles could recur and undergo pattern
  completion after stimulation of a single member: [Carrillo-Reid et al. (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaf7560).
- After repeated training on a visual sequence, mouse V1 activity could regenerate omitted
  sequence elements, demonstrating learned prediction in a named circuit without establishing a
  whole-brain render: [Gavornik and Bear (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3683).
- In humans, prestimulus oscillatory phase predicted trial-by-trial visual detection, showing that
  ongoing state can bias reception without demonstrating that the oscillation stores a complete
  percept: [Busch et al. (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0113-09.2009).

## Energy-efficient re-expression and thresholds over time

The phrase **energy-efficient rendering engine** should not be discarded merely because the
original shadow-script wording was too literal. It can be restated as a measurable SAN hypothesis:
learned structure plus maintained readiness may reduce the additional drive, latency, or
physiological cost needed to recruit a familiar distributed pattern and its appropriate action,
compared with an unprepared or novel pattern under matched task conditions.

Established findings do not supply one universal economy law. Repetition priming has sometimes
combined faster behavior with reduced activity in task-relevant regions
([Wig et al., 2005](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn1515)), while other repetition paradigms show
enhancement, suppression, fatigue, or later representational sharpening depending on stimulus,
stage, task, and measurement ([Cui et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1788-24.2025)).
Neural energy costs are also distributed across synaptic signaling, action potentials, resting
potentials, and maintenance; a smaller EEG, MEG, fMRI, or fNIRS signal is not by itself a direct
measurement of lower ATP use.

For a declared target pattern \(R\), define an operational re-expression cost rather than assuming
one:

\[
C_R = \text{additional measured drive or physiological expenditure required to reach } R
\]

A fair test would compare prepared and unprepared trials while matching input, behavior, and
accuracy; measure the minimum perturbation needed, recruitment latency, spike and synaptic load,
and metabolic or hemodynamic proxies; and ask whether a named tonic-state variable predicts
\(C_R\) beyond learned connectivity, adaptation, firing rate, attention, and generic recurrence.

The 2022 phrase **thresholds of the mind over time** adds a second, slower hypothesis: repeated
recruitment may cross mechanism-specific thresholds for adaptation, plasticity, consolidation,
habit, or reorganization. Those processes span different timescales and substrates. They should not
be collapsed into one accumulating theta wave or a fixed frequency-to-duration table.

The same source described observation as "always on." The medically recoverable operation is
continuous body-world monitoring and action readiness during ongoing behavior, not proof of
uninterrupted phenomenal consciousness or of theta as a permanent observation layer.

## Recovered 2022 SAN proposal

The rejected figure did not invent its central concern. It over-literalized a dated SAN source
lineage. In June 2022, `a0444z` described tonic activity as being *like* shadows of recent patterns
that remain ready for activation, and `a0050z` described tonic organization as a state of readiness.
By September 2022, `a0007z` and `a0310z` had expanded the idea into learned synaptic structure,
ongoing excitation and inhibition, cue-dependent recruitment, persistent or reactivated patterns,
and expectations that bias later detection and action.

The recovered operation is therefore:

```text
learned cellular and synaptic structure
-> maintained receiver-relative readiness
-> partial cue or phasic departure
-> selective recruitment, inhibition, and recurrent completion
-> distributed pattern re-expression
-> perceptual, memory, and action consequences
-> sensory and bodily feedback
```

SAN's distinct, unresolved hypothesis is that the tonic field-cell state supplies a distributed
receiver-relative comparison context, while phasic phase-wave differentials recruit and reshape
that context so learned patterns can be re-expressed as part of an action-usable render. A decisive
test must show that the declared tonic/phasic variables add held-out predictive or selective causal
value beyond learned connectivity, firing rate, generic recurrence, neuromodulation, and ordinary
state-dependent excitability. The energy-efficiency extension additionally predicts reduced
incremental re-expression cost for a prepared familiar pattern, not simply lower bulk activity.

## Established physiology and SAN interpretation

| Status | Claim |
|---|---|
| Established | Sensory processing is transformed, distributed, recurrent, context-sensitive, and linked to action |
| Bounded empirical support | Population state, attention, feedback, oscillatory timing, and traveling waves can alter sensory processing in named preparations |
| SAN interpretation | These processes form a receive-transform-recruit-re-express-update architecture that maintains a working render |
| Unresolved SAN claim | The tonic field-cell state and its phasic differences are constitutive of the observer or phenomenal experience |

## Historical analogies

The older page connected neural rendering to cosmic finite-state machines, quantum entanglement,
and oscillatory natural selection. Those are cross-domain analogies, not medical support for the
brain mechanism. They belong on separately labeled physics or comparison pages and cannot be used
as evidence that neural rendering is correct.
