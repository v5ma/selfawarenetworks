## Position

SAN's substantive claim is composable in three steps. First, expectation is *encoded* as a tonic-oscillation phase pattern — not as a stored vector or a synaptic weight, but as a continuing wave configuration carried by the [[neural-oscillations|neural-oscillation]] substrate: `a0149z--md`, line 85 names this the "baseline of consciousness, or a ground of being, a tonic wave pattern" ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0149z.md)). Third, expectation is *loaded into working memory* by phasic action potentials whose tonic continuation IS the held expectation: `a0007z--md`, line 508 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0007z.md)) — "initiated with a phasic [[action-potential|action potential]] continues to oscillate with tonic oscillations, like a new expectation has been loaded into working memory".
