**Episodic Memory** in [[self-aware-network|Self-Aware Networks]] is the *volumetric reconstruction* of a past moment — sensory, spatial, and emotional — re-rendered as a [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] sequence over the [[entorhinal-cortex|hippocampal–entorhinal]] loop, with [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave-ripple]] traffic as the cognitive biomarker of encoding and retrieval. SAN treats episodic memory not as a stored video file but as a re-instantiation of the same tomographic dynamics that produced the original moment, drawn against the [[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic baseline]] by the [[continuous-neural-rendering|continuous-rendering]] loop.

## Position

## Post-SAN

The mainstream episodic-memory literature (Tulving, Buzsáki, Eichenbaum, Norman, Squire) supplies hippocampus-as-temporal-binder, sharp-wave-ripple replay, theta-gamma coupling during encoding, and the medial-temporal-lobe / retrosplenial / prefrontal network. SAN agrees with the substrate and reframes the *content*: the recovered episode is a re-rendering of a [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave-differential]] pattern over the [[hippocampal-reference-frames|hippocampal-entorhinal reference frame]], not the read-out of a stored synaptic [[engram]]. Specific SAN-side predictions: (i) episodic-memory replay should show recoverable phase signatures beyond firing-rate replay; (ii) [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave-ripple]] events should mark crossings into [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|dominant-rhythm]] regime for the recalled scene, with measurable cross-frequency coupling to gamma; (iii) the retrosplenial-[[cortex]] layer-1 projection (`a0403z--md`, line 69) should carry the *rendered* version of the episode rather than just an index pointer.
