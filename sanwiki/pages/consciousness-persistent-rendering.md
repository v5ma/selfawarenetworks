## Render and Watch

The brain simultaneously:
1. **Renders**: cortical columns fire in learned sequences, projecting patterns to
   downstream arrays ([[neural-array-projection]]).
2. **Watches**: [[predictive-coding]] â€” neurons listen to the activity of other neurons,
   comparing rendered output to expected patterns. Mismatches update predictions.

This dual role (renderer + observer) produces the [[self-aware-network]] character of
consciousness: the system models its own rendered output.
