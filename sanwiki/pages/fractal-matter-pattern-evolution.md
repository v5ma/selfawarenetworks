**Fractal matter-pattern evolution** is an early SAN cosmological thesis (2011) proposing that matter and anti-matter, interacting via uneven spacetime and random quantum decay, produce [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar fractal patterns of increasing complexity — the same dynamic that produces patterns of thought in the neo-cortex.

The core mechanism: matter and anti-matter repel each other and continuously split. Because spacetime is uneven, the routes each particle must travel before recombining vary in length. Longer routes mean longer lifetimes before decay, causing matter to accumulate in lumps. These lumps further redirect future routes, producing spiral/fractal accumulation patterns at every scale.

The result is **natural selection of matter patterns**: random quantum ranges (entropy, "yang") drive the chaos; the extropy side ("yin") — the tendency for patterns to increase in complexity through this navigation — is the universe's intrinsic counter-tendency. The two together produce novel, increasingly complex structures: atoms, stars, cells, cortex.

## Analogy to Neural Pattern Formation

The note explicitly maps this physics to [[neural-array-projection]] and cortical dynamics:

- Random particle decay ↔ the chaotic external world an organism navigates
- Extropy accumulation ↔ the neo-cortex's ability to build increasingly complex learned patterns
- Natural selection of matter patterns ↔ Hebbian / evolutionary selection of neural firing patterns

This positions [[fractal-consciousness]] as not merely metaphorical but as the *same process* operating at different scales — a scale-free [[fractal-pattern]] spanning cosmological, biological, and cognitive levels.

## Relation to SAN Theory

In later SAN formulations, this early sketch grounds the claim that [[self-aware-network]]s are a natural outcome of physical law rather than a special biological invention. The universe "began to think" when fractal matter-pattern complexity crossed the threshold required for [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modelling.

The "Very Specific Relativity" label in the source (`a0386z`) treats particle decay rate as the more precise substitute for the speed of light constant in E=mc², reflecting the note's pre-formal status. This is a SAN theoretical seed, not a physics claim to defend.
