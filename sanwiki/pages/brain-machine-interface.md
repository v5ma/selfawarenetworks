# Brain Machine Interface

**Brain Machine Interface** (BMI) is, in the [[self-aware-networks]] corpus, the umbrella technology category that links neural readout and stimulation hardware — from [[neuralink]] and [[braingate]] to [[neurofeedback]] devices and [[fmri]] — to the SAN programme of decoding and writing the [[neural-arrays]] that underlie [[consciousness]]. SAN treats BMI not as a peripheral application but as the engineering counterpart of [[biological-oscillating-tomography]] and [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]]: where BOT and NAPOT describe how the brain reconstructs volumetric experience, BMI describes how external instruments tap into that reconstruction process and, eventually, become a reciprocal channel into it.

The SAN sources frame BMI in terms of next-generation goals well beyond current command-and-control prosthetics. The [[gh-a0223z|a0223z]] page lists the prerequisite explicitly: "One of the first steps to accomplish Neural Gear, [[neural-lace-journal|Neural Lace]], or the next generation Brain Machine Interface is that we need to correlate 3D volumes of data from the real world" ([[gh-a0223z|a0223z]]--md, line 11), tying BMI directly to [[3d-semantic-segmentation]], [[point-cloud]] correspondence, and the [[frame-of-reference]] alignment between sensor space and [[cortex]]-space. [[gh-a0227z|a0227z]] foregrounds [[polina-anikeeva|Polina Anikeeva]]'s "Rethinking the Brain Machine Interface" as a touchstone — "the talk was also called: '[[polina-anikeeva|Polina Anikeeva]] "Blurring the Boundaries between the Brain and Machines"'" ([[gh-a0227z|a0227z]]--md, line 20) — and [[gh-a0636z|a0636z]] reports the same community organising around BMI: "This meetup designed to unite the Brain Machine Interface Neuro Hacking community with [[augmented-reality|Augmented Reality]] and Virtual Reality developers, and Machine Learning Deep Learning developers" ([[gh-a0636z|a0636z]]--md, line 35), placing BMI in the same room as [[augmented-reality]], [[virtual-reality]], [[deep-learning-neural-networks]], and [[convolutional-neural-networks]] practitioners.
