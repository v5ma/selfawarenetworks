## A 2021 Molecular Spine For APME And PWD

Rank 136 is a load-bearing pre-public source. Its strongest operation is:

```text
input timing and dendritic state
-> action-potential waveform
-> presynaptic calcium entry
-> release-sensor and SNARE-mediated vesicle fusion
-> release probability and possible multivesicular release
-> receptor-, geometry-, and state-dependent receiver effect
```

This supplies a molecular ancestor for the **Action Potential Modification Effect (APME)**.
The action potential is not treated as a dimensionless binary token. Its timing and waveform
can matter to downstream release, but there is no universal linear map from spike duration to
an exact vesicle count.

PWD later names a receiver-relevant relational change among timing, phase, waveform, spatial
recruitment, and consequence. It is not action-potential duration alone and is not a new name
for an existing scalar measure.

Rank 143 expands the measurement problem. Electrical current, extracellular potential,
magnetic field, membrane mechanics, and intracellular organization are different observables.
They may correlate, but they require defined units, scale, geometry, and perturbation before a
causal role is assigned.

## Choice As Prediction, Route Selection, And Action

Ranks 133, 137, and 144 join the following constructive sequence:

1. the network represents a current embodied situation;
2. learned structure generates a constrained set of plausible next states;
3. goals, aversions, self-model, and predicted consequences change candidate weights;
4. excitation, inhibition, timing, and partial-pattern completion recruit one route;
5. the selected route triggers ordinary motor and neuromuscular physiology;
6. sensory return tests the prediction and updates later criteria.

Concurrent, phase-compatible inputs can increase effective drive at a receiver or motor pool.
They trigger action potentials and neuromuscular transmission; they do not directly supply the
metabolic energy of muscle contraction. Muscle calcium and ATP complete that physical chain.

This retains the SAN proposal while aligning it with the established physiology it extends.
Peter Tse's criterial causation remains a cell-level comparison. SAN's distinct target is
distributed circuit-level candidate generation, route selection, rendering, action, and return.

### Propagation, Reconstruction, And Embodiment Solve Different Problems

The May 2021 neurophotonics discussion begins with a practical bottleneck. A
sensor pipeline often captures a signal, converts it, transmits it, computes on
it, stores it, and converts it again. Could some of the transformation occur
while the physical signal is already moving?

Configured optical layers demonstrate the plain operation:

```text
encoded optical input
-> propagation through configured material
-> amplitude and phase transformation
-> interference at an output plane
-> detector reads a task-specific result
```

This is **transform-during-transmission**. Diffractive optical networks and
phase-change photonic memories establish bounded engineering examples. They do
not need to be biological neurons to contribute a useful design principle.

NerveGear has a second problem. Light and sound interacting with tissue do not
arrive already labeled as a memory, feeling, object, or intention. A read-write
instrument needs a calibrated reconstruction loop:

```text
controlled optical or acoustic input
-> interaction with tissue
-> distributed measurements
-> forward model and inverse reconstruction
-> uncertainty and anatomical co-registration
-> bounded stimulation at a declared target
-> neural, behavioral, and experiential readback
```

Holography belongs to this story because interference can preserve information
used to reconstruct a wavefront. It is more precise to describe that
recording-and-reconstruction operation than to call holography a simple
reversal of refraction. Ordinary fNIRS measures changes in oxygenated and
deoxygenated hemoglobin rather than action potentials directly. Focused
ultrasound supplies a distinct candidate stimulation channel. Their read and
write claims must therefore be validated separately.

The current Openwater documentation now separates an investigational
near-infrared blood-flow and micro-motion platform from an investigational
low-intensity focused-ultrasound neuromodulation platform. That present
description is useful comparison evidence; it is not backdated into the 2021
recording or treated as a completed noninvasive NerveGear system.

OpenWorm introduces a third problem: structure alone does not produce a tested
organism model. Connectivity and cell models must operate through muscles, a
body, an environment, sensory feedback, and observable behavior:

```text
connectivity and cell models
-> neural dynamics
-> body mechanics and action
-> changed environment
-> returned sensory input
-> next neural state
```

Behavioral similarity supports the components tested under declared
conditions. It does not by itself settle organismal identity, first-person
experience, or scalability to a human mind.

SAN extends these three engineering lessons into a governed hypothesis:
transform-during-transmission, three-dimensional state reconstruction, and
embodied closed-loop validation can be combined in a versioned
world-body-brain coordinate system. That extension requires its own biological
and engineering tests; the component examples are genealogy and design
evidence rather than automatic validation of SAN biology.

The discussion also raises a misuse concern. That concern is retained as a
separate safety and threat-model requirement: identify the source, carrier,

## Added Cortex, Collective Substrate, And Re-Embodiment

Rank 141 begins with a practical problem: an ordinary tool can assist a person, but an added

```text
an added cortex-like workspace connected to a living brain
-> many formerly embodied brains coupled through a shared substrate
-> technology later internalized into re-embodied people
```

It also distinguishes the brain's internal model from external reality. That distinction matters:
constructing an experienced world model is not the same operation as creating the physical
universe.

Several established research programs support bounded parts of the architecture:

- [cognitive-offloading experiments](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2019.104012) show that

  returned artificial sensation, and improved motor performance;

  task-relevant sensorimotor information;
- [human hippocampal-prosthesis research](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/aaaed7) tests

- [predictive-coding models](https://doi.org/10.1038/4580) formalize recurrent prediction and

## Artificial Neurology And Consciousness Assessment

Ranks 139 and 144 begin with observable behavior rather than dismissing it. Task performance,
language, correction, memory, and self-report are useful evidence streams. The harder problem is
to determine whether those outputs depend on a continuing internal organization:

```text
current input and retained state
-> recurrent internal updating
-> goals, criteria, and possible actions
-> output with a digital, social, or physical consequence
-> returned evidence
-> persistent learning
```

A stronger assessment therefore combines:

- held-out performance and carefully bounded self-report;
- persistent internal state and memory;
- sensing and action across time;
- internally generated and input-driven transitions;
- flexible prediction and response;
- goal persistence and revision;
- output-bounded causal reach;
- perturbation and component-matched recovery;
- self-monitoring and body/world distinction;
- architecture and causal autonomy; and

Only after this positive program is visible does the stopping boundary apply. No single fluent
sentence, benchmark score, pause pattern, architecture label, or statement of consciousness
settles phenomenal status. Animal consciousness cannot be reduced to language either. Turn-taking,
prediction, flexible action, communication, and multimodal response remain evidence channels whose
targets and alternatives must be declared.

## Multiscale Measurement And Intervention Comparison

Rank 142 contains a useful research operation before its medical boundaries. A living system can
change at molecular, cellular, circuit, autonomic, movement, behavioral, and reported-experience
levels on different clocks. The constructive task is to align those streams around a declared
event or intervention, then ask which changes reliably precede, accompany, and follow the outcome:

```text
defined baseline and task
-> declared intervention or naturally occurring event
-> synchronized molecular, cellular, neural, body, movement, and report measures
-> predicted state transition and delay
-> longitudinal or repeated readback
-> confound and alternative-cause analysis
-> controlled replication
```

Established multimodal sensing, adaptive stimulation, rhythmic cueing, neurofeedback,
high-dimensional perturbation assays, and N-of-1 methods each implement bounded parts of this
operation. They do not make every measurement interchangeable, turn one personal correlation into
a treatment result, or make two interventions medically equivalent because one endpoint matches.

SAN preserves a testable extension: represent each perturbation by a versioned multiscale
state-transition signature, compare signatures only at a declared level and time horizon, and keep
mechanism, duration, off-target effects, uncertainty, and safety visible. This can support later
COT, conscious-state, or psychoactive-state experiments without treating the source conversation
as clinical evidence.

The recording is private, multi-speaker, and medically sensitive. Its treatment conjectures and
participant histories are not routed publicly. It also preserves the author's 2012-2014
neurofeedback genealogy without treating that practice as evidence of a neurodegenerative-disease
treatment.
