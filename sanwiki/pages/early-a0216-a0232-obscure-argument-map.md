This map records the fixed semantic-review tranche after `a0215z.md`, excluding the already
governed `a0223z.md`. It distinguishes Micah-authored mechanisms, public articles, multi-author

## Main result

The tranche produced one newly governed concept:
[[sensory-to-symbol-neural-grounding|From Sensory Difference to Symbol: Learned Neural Grounding]].
Its principal 2012 note asks how a color, word, object, or person becomes a reusable concept
without requiring another hidden symbol reader. The governed answer is distributed and learned:

```text
sensory differences + body and action consequences + repeated association
-> a reactivatable multiscale neural disposition
-> recognition, prediction, communication, or action
```

The historical note's single-neuron and single-dendrite language is preserved but narrowed. A
whole concept is not assigned to one cell. The later broccoli example supplies the cross-person
recognition and BCI test: different internal encodings can support the same referent-sensitive
behavior, while evoking a label is not equivalent to reconstructing a percept or qualia.

## Important distinctions recovered

### Field readout, field causation, and field identity

`a0224z.md` contains an unresolved but productive tension. One branch treats EEG or related
brainwaves as a residual, aggregate readout, or sign of underlying neural processes. Another
assigns local tonic and phasic extracellular dynamics an active computational role. These claims
must not be collapsed:

1. a measured field can correlate with neural activity;
2. a local endogenous field can causally alter nearby membrane dynamics under bounded conditions;
3. the field pattern can be constitutive of represented content or the observer process.

Established electrophysiology strongly supports the first and provides evidence for limited local
ephaptic effects. The third is SAN's stronger testable hypothesis, not an established inference
from EEG visibility.

### Oscillator, resonator, and symbol

`a0217z.md` supports the view that neurons can be intrinsic oscillators or frequency-selective
resonators whose response depends on membrane conductances and recent state. `a0225z.md` asks how
those physical responses become learned concepts. Oscillation is therefore a carrier and state
constraint, not semantic meaning by itself. Grounding additionally requires stimulus relations,
learning history, context, action, and consequence.

### BCI ancestry and capability boundaries

The public 2018 and 2021 articles show that SAN's later neural-rendering program grew through
multimodal imaging, XR, semantic scene models, and biological read/write ambitions. They document
development. They do not prove that any listed instrument can decode arbitrary thoughts, write a
complete percept, infer mental states without construct validation, or achieve NerveGear.

### Recurrent-flow dream provenance

## Primary-source corrections

- [Llinas (1988)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.3059497) supports intrinsic neuronal
  autorhythmicity and resonance. It does not make every neuron one stable-frequency oscillator or
  establish semantic content from frequency alone.
- [Buzsaki, Anastassiou, and Koch (2012)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22595786/) explains how
  transmembrane currents, geometry, synchrony, and volume conduction shape extracellular fields.
  A measured EEG or LFP is not a direct image of one computation.
- [Anastassiou et al. (2011)](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.2727) demonstrated bounded
  ephaptic coupling in cortical tissue. This does not establish a global electromagnetic content
  field or SAN's identity thesis.
- [Harnad (1990)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789(90)90087-6),
  [Binder and Desai (2011)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22001867/), and
  [Pulvermuller (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2013.06.004) bound the symbol-grounding and
  distributed semantic-memory comparisons. They support the problem and several components, not
  SAN's complete phase/rendering mechanism.
- [Shilts et al. (2021)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-80464-1) found no evidence that
  CD147 is a direct SARS-CoV-2 spike receptor. Current
  [CDC treatment guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/covid/treatment/index.html) does not support the
  historical supplement prescription in `a0222z.md`.
