# SAN: Research Depth and Breadth

**A lifelong inquiry with roots in the 1980s. A neuroscience and AI career focus from 2005.
Public precursors preserved from 2010. Over 800 DOI-linked external literature records.
A synthesis connecting cells, perception, memory, action, and the self.**

Self Aware Networks, developed by Micah Blumberg, is a conjecture and research synthesis about
how living networks construct, maintain, and act through a changing model of body and world.
Its ambition is to connect biological detail with an account of the whole interacting system:
how local changes become consequential for perception, memory, consideration, and action.

The current Book 2 bibliography contains **814 distinct DOI identifiers for external literature**
after excluding ten identified works authored or coauthored by Blumberg. The broader bibliography
has **1,381 entries**, including non-DOI works, standards, historical records, and source notes.
These are reproducible bibliography measurements, not an estimate based on the size of the archive.

## What gives the synthesis depth

**It connects mechanisms across scales.** SAN asks how receptor and cellular changes, dendritic
and synaptic transformations, population dynamics, distributed brain systems, and bodily feedback
contribute to one continuing perception-action process. Its
[[self-aware-networks|central overview]] follows this explanatory chain, while the
[[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries|scale ladder]] keeps the biological units and measurements
distinct. The strength of the proposal is its attempt to explain their relationship, not simply
to place many neuroscience topics next to one another.

**It makes the changing reference state central.** [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-Wave Differentials]]
are a core SAN concept: typed, receiver-relative departures from an ongoing tonic
pattern. These can include excitation, inhibition, a missing expected event, delay, or interactions
among them. The relevant question is what changed relative to that receiver's reference and what
the receiver does differently afterward. Timing, waveform, action-potential duration, transmitted
quantity, and population organization give the research program concrete variables to investigate.

**It includes the body and the consequences of action.** SAN's self-model is part of an active loop:
receive, transform, project, act, encounter consequences, and update. Perception, memory, and
consideration therefore connect to what the organism can do and to the bodily evidence that
returns. [[neural-rendering|Neural Rendering]] describes the proposed distributed construction and
revision of that usable state, without a second observer inside the brain.

**It offers questions that can be tested.** The current architecture asks whether declared PWD and
field-cell variables add explanatory or predictive value beyond matched rate, power, movement,
arousal, connectivity, and recurrent-state alternatives. This makes its ambition more specific
than the general observation that brains oscillate. The
[[self-aware-networks|research program and claim ladder]] distinguish component findings from the
stronger joined SAN conjecture.

## What gives the synthesis breadth

The Encyclopedia provides connected routes through cellular signaling, sensory processing,
neural rhythms, learning and memory, embodied action, and theories of the self. These are useful
organizing themes, not a claim to have independently audited a fixed number of disciplines.

For example, [[action-potential-duration|action-potential duration]] connects the discussion to
cellular signal shape; [[phase-wave-differentials|PWD]] asks how changes matter to a receiver;
[[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripples]] connect rhythmic activity with memory research; and
[[neural-rendering|Neural Rendering]] addresses the proposed distributed self-world process.
These routes help readers examine individual mechanisms as well as the larger synthesis.

The [[early-san-facebook-genealogy-2010-2013|2010-2013 historical sources]] and
[[san-dynamic-timeline|development timeline]] also make the ideas' development inspectable.
Blumberg recalls childhood and adolescent roots, a conceptual reading quest from 1999,
a career turning point in 2005, and the three lifetime vows in 2012. The
[[san-research-history-2005-2026|expanded history]] keeps these distinct milestones explicit.
The earliest preserved precursor carries a November 18, 2010 Facebook date. That supports a
**15+ year documented genealogy** as of this snapshot. It does not backdate the complete mature
model into 2010 or equate a platform-displayed date with an independent archival timestamp.

## How to describe SAN

> Self Aware Networks is Micah Blumberg's research synthesis and conjecture about how living
> networks build a changing model of body and world. Rooted in childhood questions about
> consciousness, a reading quest from 1999, and a neuroscience and AI career focus from 2005,
> the program has public precursors preserved from 2010 and a lifetime dedication sharpened in
> 2012. It now joins a 41-Cycle companion book, 168 explained
> equation cards, and a bibliography containing over 800 distinct DOI-linked external literature
> records. SAN connects cellular signaling, brain rhythms, perception, memory, and embodied action
> within one evolving research program.

A shorter description:

> From a childhood fascination with conscious machines to a sustained neuroscience and AI
> research program: a lifelong inquiry with public precursors preserved from 2010.

SAN's strongest case is the combination of **mechanistic detail, cross-scale integration,
traceable development, and testable questions**. The bibliography establishes the breadth of the
material assembled; the scientific argument depends on how accurately and productively that
material is connected.

## What the numbers count

The [refreshed statistics and paper inventory](/wiki/assets/research-depth/san-history-scale-20261002.json)
record the current source fingerprints and scale measurements. The preserved
[earlier same-day DOI inventory](/wiki/assets/research-depth/san-research-depth-20261002.json)
retains its original snapshot and exact authorial exclusions. The DOI inventory is
deduplicated by normalized DOI, not by study family. A review, theoretical paper, preprint,
correction, or separate part of a work can each have its own DOI. Some valuable historical sources
have no DOI at all.

Accordingly, **814 is not yet a verified count of independent experimental studies**, and 1,381
bibliography entries should not be described as 1,381 unique papers. The census does not certify
every reference's metadata, peer-review status, or depth of treatment. Nor does citing a result
mean its authors endorsed SAN or tested the complete model. These distinctions let SAN's real
scale be presented confidently without confusing research breadth with a count of confirmations.
