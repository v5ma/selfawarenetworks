# Phi Integrated Information

**Phi Integrated Information** (Φ) is Giulio Tononi's [[integrated-information-theory|Integrated Information Theory]] (IIT) measure for the irreducibility of a system's cause–effect structure to the union of its parts — proposed (in IIT 2.0/3.0/4.0, Tononi 2004 onward) as the *quantity* of [[consciousness]] a physical system possesses. Within [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] this construct is referenced as the closest mainstream cousin of SAN's [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythms]] and [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] framings, but the SAN reading rejects Φ as the *substrate-level* measure and re-routes the same intuition through [[phase-wave-differential|phase-wave-differential]] dynamics on [[brainwave|brainwave]] [[oscillator|oscillators]].

## Post-SAN

Where IIT treats Φ as a *substrate-irreducibility* measure on the system's cause–effect structure, SAN's distinctive prediction is that *substrate alone* under-determines phenomenal content — what determines content is the [[phase-wave-differential|phase-wave-differential]] structure *on* the substrate, modulated by [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]]. The two frameworks make different empirical predictions in the same experimental setting: IIT predicts that a high-Φ inactive system is conscious; SAN predicts that a high-Φ inactive system is *not* conscious because it lacks dominant phase-wave-differential rhythms. The IIT-styled "intrinsic existence" axiom maps in SAN onto the [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]] reading of which oscillator-states *count*, with the difference that SAN treats counting as a dynamic, top-down property of the [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|memory-prediction machine]] rather than an axiomatic property of the system's cause–effect graph. Mainstream literature beyond IIT — global-workspace theory, higher-order theories, predictive-processing accounts, and reentrant-[[cortex]] theories — already captures parts of the SAN reading; SAN's contribution is to insist on the [[phase-wave-differential|phase-wave-differential]] level as the right measurement substrate, not the cause–effect-graph level.
