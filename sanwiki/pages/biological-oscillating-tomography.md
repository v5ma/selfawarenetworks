# Biological Oscillating Tomography

**Biological Oscillating Tomography** (BOT) is the conceptual framework within [[self-aware-networks]] that describes how the brain — and, by extension, the whole body — constructs spatial and temporal representations of experience through phase-wave differentials in [[oscillatory-dynamics]]. BOT generalizes the oscillatory integration principle to whole-brain scale, sitting alongside Cellular Oscillating Tomography and [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]] (NAPOT) as a tier of [[oscillatory-tomography]] that yields the volumetric substrate of [[consciousness]] and [[qualia]] ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 39).

BOT proposes that groups of neurons in [[cortex]] iteratively reconstruct three-dimensional representations the way tomographic imaging reconstructs a 3D volume from many 2D slices, with each slice corresponding to a phase of an oscillation rather than a literal physical cross-section (draft1--md, line 250; draft2--md, line 130). The brain therefore behaves as a [[neural-rendering]] engine in which travelling waves across [[neural-arrays]] supply the projections, and the differentials between phases supply the geometric register that lets [[cortical-columns]] in regions like the [[hippocampus]], [[prefrontal-cortex]], and [[auditory-cortex]] integrate coincident detections into a coherent volumetric percept (draft3--md, line 189; draft4--md, line 283; [[gh-veca|vecA]]--md, line 1219). In this way BOT positions itself as the biological mechanism behind both [[3d-semantic-segmentation]] inside the brain and the felt-quality registration that [[predictive-coding]] alone leaves underspecified, taking a direct stance on the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness]].

Mechanistically, BOT couples macroscopic [[brainwaves]] — including [[gamma-oscillations]] and [[theta-oscillation]] activity — to microscopic events in [[apical-dendrites]] and [[dendritic-spines]], where [[nmda-receptor]] gating, [[gaba]]-mediated [[inhibitory-circuits]], [[acetylcholine]] modulation, and [[camkii]] signalling jointly determine whether a coincident phase alignment crosses the [[action-potential]] threshold and produces [[neural-spikes]] ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 357; rexnote02--md, line 1623). The oscillatory framework therefore serves as a [[bridging-molecular-mechanisms]] bridge: BOT explains how [[hebbian-learning]] and [[hebbian-plasticity]] — expressed as [[long-term-potentiation]], shaped by [[synaptic-vesicle-release]], and stabilized by [[microtubules]] within [[engrams]] — are timed and addressed by the same wave geometry that defines the percept itself ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 489). [[Criterial-Causation]] and the [[agentic-viewpoint]] then become naturally legible inside this picture, because the phase-locked [[attractor]] that fires the spike is also the [[attractor]] that selects which content is represented.

Within the broader [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks (SAN) corpus, BOT is the parent principle from which NAPOT specializes: NAPOT explicitly connects volumetric processing to the Fourier Slice Projection Theorem and to Non-linear Differential Continuous Approximation ([[ndca]]), treating the brain's tomographic reconstruction as a continuous, non-linear analogue of medical imaging mathematics (rexnote02--md, line 1623; rexnote12--md, line 2583). [[gh-vecc|vecC]] frames the same idea functionally — the brain "uses Biological Oscillating Tomography to interpret and represent sensory stimuli, integrating oscillatory patterns across an entified neural net" ([[gh-vecc|vecC]]--md, line 757) — anchoring [[attention]], [[memory-persistence]], and the [[attractor]] dynamics of phase-locked assemblies inside one tomographic substrate. Conceptual lineages cited alongside BOT include [[advaita-vedanta]] for the non-dual reading of the unified percept, [[category-theory]] for the morphism between projection and reconstruction, and adjacent thinkers such as [[jeff-hawkins]] and [[michael-levin]], with [[neuralink]] and [[braingate]] sitting on the engineering side as readout instruments for the very [[action-potential-waveform]] BOT predicts ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]]--md, line 8687; [[gh-veca|vecA]]--md, line 1226).
