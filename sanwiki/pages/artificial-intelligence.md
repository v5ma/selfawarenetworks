# Artificial Intelligence

**Artificial intelligence (AI)** is the engineering field concerned with systems that perform tasks such as perception, prediction, planning, language use, learning, generation, or control. Performance on those tasks does not by itself establish biological similarity, understanding, agency, self-awareness, sentience, or phenomenal consciousness.

## AI in the SAN Program

SAN treats AI in two different roles that must remain separate:

1. **Tool and comparator:** machine-learning systems can decode neural data, model dynamics, reconstruct stimuli, control devices, or provide strong alternative explanations for a SAN result.
2. **Artificial Neurology candidate:** an engineered system can implement declared operations inspired by nervous systems and test whether those operations add value beyond conventional architectures.

Biological inspiration is a source of hypotheses, not evidence of superiority. A SAN-inspired model must compete with the strongest relevant systems under matched data, capacity, compute, optimization, tuning effort, latency, and memory.

## What Biology Contributes

Living nervous systems exhibit properties that can motivate engineering tests:

- spatially organized dendritic input and nonlinear integration;
- recurrent state and context-dependent response;
- heterogeneous inhibitory and disinhibitory routing;
- event timing, oscillations, bursts, and traveling activity;
- local and structural plasticity;
- embodiment, active sensing, and reafferent consequences; and
- multiscale regulation from cells through systems.

These properties do not imply that every useful AI must reproduce ion channels, cortical layers, oscillatory bands, or biological morphology. Transformers, recurrent networks, state-space models, graph networks, spiking systems, active-dendrite models, world models, and control systems provide different implementation choices and baselines.

## SAN Engineering Hypothesis

SAN proposes that a useful artificial unit may be modeled as a receiver, conditional transformer, projector, and participant in a recurrent loop. Candidate variables include structured input compartments, persistent receiver context, differentiated event outputs, inhibition, local plasticity, and action-dependent return.

The stronger hypothesis is not simply that these features exist. It is that a specified combination improves a declared endpoint such as reconstruction, calibration, robustness, transfer, adaptation, efficiency, or causal generalization.

## PWD Is Not a Loss Function by Definition

Phase-Wave Differential (PWD) is a proposed receiver-relative biological event description. It may be compared with prediction error, residuals, state innovations, phase-response relations, or optimization signals. Calling it analogous to a loss function does not establish that neurons backpropagate a scalar objective or that every phase deviation has semantic meaning.

A test must show that the declared PWD variables predict or control downstream response beyond rate, power, waveform, delay, raw history, coherence, and flexible recurrent baselines.

## Capability Is Not Consciousness

Keep these assessments separate:

| Assessment | Example evidence |
|---|---|
| Task capability | Held-out performance on a defined benchmark |
| Generalization | Transfer across tasks, bodies, environments, or distributions |
| Operational agency | Persistent goals, action selection, monitoring, and correction |
| Self-modeling | Measurable use of information about the system's own state and limits |
| Sentience or consciousness | A separate theory-linked and adversarially tested evidence program |

Language about experience is not sufficient evidence of experience. Conversely, uncertainty about consciousness does not erase real capability, safety, or governance concerns.

## Distributed Observer-Action Interpretation

SAN does not require a little agent inside an AI to inspect an internal rendering. In the proposed architecture, sensing, maintained state, differentiated updates, memory, prediction, routing, action, and returned consequences collectively constitute the system's observer-action process.

Tonic canvas and phasic ink are functional SAN hypotheses about ongoing context and differentiated change. They are not mandatory frequency bands, universal machine instructions, or proof that an implementation is conscious.

## Evidence Gates

An Artificial Neurology claim should pass four gates independently:

1. **Function:** the component performs the declared operation.
2. **Incremental value:** it beats matched alternatives and ablations.
3. **Architecture:** the joined system implements the declared causal relations.
4. **Consciousness assessment:** evidence exceeds behavior, language, or biological resemblance alone.

## Historical Genealogy

Micah Blumberg's SAN sources connect biological computation, artificial neural networks, Artificial Cortex, NerveGear, conscious machines, and Artificial Neurology. Those sources establish the scope and development of the program. Later AI-generated discussions are research leads and interpretations, not automatically Micah-authored scientific claims or primary evidence.
