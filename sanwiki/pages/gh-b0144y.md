## Material added to the 2022 Git note

The following items were not spoken in the short 2021 recording. They were appended to the public
notebook later:

1. a proposal to use TMS when estimating threshold states;
2. the 2022 preprint later published as Aberra et al. (2023), on rapid estimation of activation
   thresholds in computational TMS-neuron models; and
3. Kumar, Rotter, and Aertsen's 2010 discussion of Perkel and Bullock's four neural-code functions.

The public Git history first fixed `b0144y` on June 8, 2022, renamed it on June 16, and modified it
again on August 28. The appended items must not be attributed to the October 2021 audio.

## Scientific boundary

The HTTP/FTP language is an engineering analogy for a future designed interrogation interface. It
is not a claim that native neural communication uses Internet protocols. The TMS paper estimates
thresholds in computational neuron models from simulated electric-field distributions; it does not
decode memories or query a living brain's semantic data structures.
