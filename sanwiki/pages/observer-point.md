## Position

In the [[self-aware-networks]] account, an **observer point** is the invariant that survives change-of-scene: rooms change, contents of perception change, but the [[frame-of-reference]] from which they are registered does not. The SAN reading puts this in dialogue with three nearby concepts. First, [[contentless-observer]] — the observer point is the locus to which the [[contentless-observer|contentless observer]] corresponds, drained of perceptual content but still anchored. Second, [[attention-schema]] / [[attention-schema-theory]] / [[attention-schema-holographic]] — the SAN claim is that the system models its own [[attention]], and the model includes a consistent reference point for "where the attending happens"; that model-token is the observer point. Third, [[minimum-observer-circuit]] — the smallest [[self-aware-network]] capable of registering experience contains, as one of its irreducible parts, an observer-point register; this is what makes [[awareness-as-predictive-myth]] still tractable rather than circular. The observer point is therefore not a homunculus inside the head but a [[consciousness-as-frames]]-level invariant of the [[brain-as-rendering-engine]], specifically the part that [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering]] keeps re-instantiating each cycle so the rendered scene has somewhere to be rendered "to". In SAN, this also explains why the [[brain-as-self-watching-movie]] framing works without a literal homunculus: the watcher is reduced to an observer-point register inside the [[neural-strange-loop-self-referential-consciousness]].
