**Brain State** is, in SAN's vocabulary, the *whole-brain* phase pattern at an instant — the fractal, oscillation-defined configuration that the global [[gh-a0206z|self]] emerges from rather than the static EEG-band label clinical neuroscience uses. The motif of "fractal-like organization in both [[napot-theory|NAPOT]] and brain state waves is critical here" — NAPOT envisions the brain's structure as a repeating fractal of phase-based oscillations ([[gh-05san|05san]]--md, line 78) — and the same essay routes the discussion through Part 2's "[[phase-wave-differentials|Phase Wave Differentials]] and Brain State Dynamics" ([[gh-05san|05san]]--md, line 122). In Blumberg's terms, brain state is what the [[self-aware-networks-theory|self-aware network]] *is doing right now*, expressed as a phase topology on top of [[Brainwaves|brain waves]].

A brain state is multiscale and emergent. Networks form, "those meso-agents in turn synchronize to enable the macro-agent (the global brain state, or the '[[gh-a0206z|Self]]') to emerge -- all while the lower levels continue to do their independent work" ([[gh-07san|07san]]--md, line 82); the macro-agent is built from coherent meso-agents, not imposed from above. At the experiential timescale, "in each interval of time your brain state, and your brain waves representing one facet of your brain state are changing to represent that next moment in time" (a0001z--md, line 198) — each *frame* of conscious experience corresponds to a distinct brain-state vector, with brain waves as readouts of facets. AI/ML cross-mapping work attempts to "correlate changes in the brain state at a macro level with speech processing in an artificial neural network" (a0039z--md, line 21), making brain state the natural unit at which biological cognition can be aligned to model activations.

The state is held *near criticality* by tonic dynamics: "the [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]] also serves to maintain at brain state that is always ready, or a brain state that is at the edge of criticality, primed with tonic expectations" ([[gh-a0050z|a0050z]]--md, line 12). This makes brain state an [[attractor-memory|attractor]] phenomenon — "the phase topology as a brain state is an [[attractor]] point for the mind's oscillations" (a0149z--md, line 39) — and ties brain-state transitions to slow-potential phenomena, where slow potentials have been associated with "brain-state transitions and readiness potentials, and pathological phenomena, such as spreading depression, stroke, and epilepsy" (a0288zslow--md, line 60). Critically, brain state is *constructed*, not measured-by-mirror: "the measured brain state is a construction not a reflection that includes the users recent history" ([[gh-a0244z|a0244z]]--md, line 45), so any [[BrainGate]]/EEG decoder reads a model-laden estimate, not a transparent window.

Within SAN, brain state therefore sits at the intersection of [[Cortical-columns|cortical columns]], [[Brainwaves]], and [[Consciousness|consciousness]]: a coherent brain state requires that "different frequency oscillations must synchronize to form a coherent brain state" ([[gh-05san|05san]]--md, line 224), echoing the [[Phase-Wave-Differential|Phase Wave Differentials]] frame in which travelling phase fronts (sensory cortex propagating through memory/association cortex) "[create] a unified brain state that incorporates both new sensory data and stored memories" ([[gh-05san|05san]]--md, line 181). The page connects upward into [[napot-theory|NAPOT]], [[Self-Aware-Networks|Self-Aware Networks]], and [[selfawarenetworks-gpt|GPT]]-style alignment work cited in the [[gh-map-brain-interface|brain-interface map]], and downward into [[3d-semantic-segmentation]] and [[advaita-vedanta|Advaita Vedanta]]-flavored framings of the [[gh-a0206z|Self]]; the [[Concepts|Concepts]] index lists brain state alongside [[Category-theory|Category theory]] as one of the structural rubrics organizing the rest of the wiki.
