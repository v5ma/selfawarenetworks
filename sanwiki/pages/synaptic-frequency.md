## Post-SAN

Mainstream synaptic [[neuroscience]] has long known that individual synapses can sustain particular firing-rate regimes (tonic vs phasic) and that population [[gamma-oscillations|gamma]] / theta / beta bands carry information; what the SAN account adds is the claim that *each individual [[synaptic-configuration|synaptic configuration]]* embodies a *specific* frequency that is part of how it encodes content — i.e. that synaptic frequency is not just a population-level statistic but a per-[[synapse]] property. The [[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]] consolidation states the claim cautiously: "synaptic frequency state changes can also be imagined as like pairs of speakers & microphones, or pairs of television pixels & eyes" (`GPT2023X--md`, line 10879, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2023X.md)).
