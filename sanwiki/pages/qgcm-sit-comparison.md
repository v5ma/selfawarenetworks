The comparison is therefore not a proof of equivalence and not a refutation — it is a **protocol-disciplined** map of where the two theories overlap (single-pole exponential memory → KG linearized sector) and where they don't (primitives, mass derivation, GR limit, BH predictions, EM/measurement structure).
