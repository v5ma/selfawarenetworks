Population bursts are periods when groups of neurons fire in rapid succession; the
precise **sequence** of firing within each burst carries specific information about
sensory input or cognitive state. This finding maps tightly onto [[napot]]
([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillatory Tomography), where each neural array acts as
both sensor and projector of phasic signals.

## SAN Alignment

**Sequential firing ↔ oscillatory propagation**: NAPOT posits that high-phasic
([[action-potential|action potential]]) and tonic (inhibitory/memory) waves propagate across neural arrays.
Population-burst sequences are the empirical correlate — the traveling waves are
observable as ordered neuronal firing.

**Population bursts as projection containers**: In [[self-aware-network]] theory,
activity at one array constitutes a rendered "projection" consumed by downstream arrays.
A population burst is therefore an information packet propagated upward through the
cortical hierarchy, consistent with [[neural-array-projection]].

**Oscillatory encoding**: Burst sequences interact with [[tonic-phasic-oscillation]]
cycles: tonic states gate which neurons are available to fire; phasic spikes produce
the ordering. Phase synchronization is thus an enabling condition for information-
bearing sequences.

**Plasticity**: Synaptic changes induced by burst activity (Hebbian / STDP) contribute
to long-term pattern storage, linking burst-sequence encoding to [[memory-prediction-cycle]]
in SAN.

## Implications

Burst-sequence encoding supports the claim that the brain computes via *temporal order*,
not merely firing rate — consistent with [[napot]]'s rendering model where the *phase*
of oscillatory waves, not their amplitude alone, carries representational content.
