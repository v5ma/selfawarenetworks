**Preference** is a persistent modal strategy — a recurring [[attractor]] pattern in a brain's decision-making that reasserts itself across varying contexts. **Personality** is the downstream social contract: the identification with a strategic path through many cycles of [[synaptic-computation-ltp|synaptic sequence memory]], such that the path becomes the "[[gh-a0206z|self]]."

Different brains take different strategic paths through experience. When a brain identifies with its path repeatedly — reinforcing it through each cycle of [[gh-a0206z|self]]-representing synaptic connections — that identification crystallizes into personality. Personality is not a fixed structure but the ongoing selection and reinforcement of the same modal [[attractor]].

## Connection to self-aware networks

In SAN terms, personality is the brain's [[self-modeling]] output: the network modeling its own habitual firing patterns as "me." The social contract aspect is key — personality is partly performed and partly real, maintained both internally by synaptic reinforcement and externally by social feedback loops.

This framing situates personality within the [[response-able-causation]] framework: the strategies that define personality were selected by a reactive system, not chosen from outside causality. Understanding this dissolves rigid [[gh-a0206z|self]]-identification.

## EEG / brainwave dimension

The note references [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]] and EEG as tools for making one's own modal strategies legible. EEG listens to the brain's electromagnetic emissions without affecting it — a read-only interface to the [[attractor]] patterns that constitute preference and personality.
