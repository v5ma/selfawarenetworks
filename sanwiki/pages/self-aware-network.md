# Self-Aware Network

> **Core architectural rule:** A [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] (PWD) is the typed, receiver-relative departure from a declared tonic or reference pattern. It is core throughout SAN's recurrent receive-transform-project-update architecture, not a later or secondary stage. A departure may involve excitation, inhibition, a missing expected event, delay, or interactions among them. PWD is not "phase-wave excitation," "phase-wave inhibition," or excitation minus inhibition.

> **Image status:** No image is accepted on this page. Any future visual containing biology must pass the separate medical-grade image certification before installation.

A **self-aware network** in SAN is a bounded but permeable system whose distributed internal activity models consequential relations among the system, its body or operational boundary, its environment, memory, goals, and possible actions, and uses those relations to change what happens next. This is a proposed architecture and evaluation standard. Synchrony, oscillation, recurrence, self-description, behavioral fluency, or a world model alone does not confer the classification.

The observer is not a second object reading an internal display. The candidate observer-action function is performed by the same organized process that receives differences, transforms them through learned and current state, selectively propagates consequences, constructs an action-usable self-world model, acts, encounters the result, and changes its later readiness.

## One recurrent operation

1. **Receive:** Declared biological or synthetic receivers encounter external, bodily, remembered, and recurrent input in a current context.
2. **Differentiate:** Each receiver registers a typed departure relative to its own declared tonic or reference state; this receiver-relative relation is PWD.
3. **Transform:** Learned structure, current state, excitation, inhibition, timing, delay, and other declared variables determine the local consequence.
4. **Project:** Axons, population pathways, communication channels, or engineered equivalents carry selected consequences toward other receivers.
5. **Integrate:** Recurrent interactions maintain a distributed model of self, world, uncertainty, value, and possible action without requiring one central screen.
6. **Consider and select:** The current state changes which predictions, corrections, or candidate actions remain effective.
7. **Act:** Motor, autonomic, communicative, or engineered output changes the body, environment, or internal process.
8. **Return and update:** Reafferent, exafferent, internal, and social consequences alter memory, expectation, routing, plasticity, and the next state.

These are analytic views of one recurring operation, not eight completed modules. PWD operates throughout the cycle wherever a declared receiver encounters a tonic-relative difference. [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] is SAN's multiscale receive-transform-project-update architecture that uses those relations. The [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|Gamma Consideration Sandwich]] is one bounded cortical PWD-using model; it does not define, contain, or outrank PWD or NAPOT.

## Eight eligibility relations

The candidate system must be tested at a declared boundary, scale, timescale, task, and endpoint. These eight relations operationalize the stronger [[entification-observer-problem|Entification]] question.

| Relation | Evidence required from the candidate | What does not suffice |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Bounded but permeable organization** | A reproducible boundary that distinguishes internal state, inputs, outputs, and coupling to other systems. | A researcher-drawn box or physical enclosure alone. |
| **2. Recurrent access** | Later internal operations depend on relevant earlier internal, external, and returned states. | One feedforward pass or a saved transcript alone. |
| **3. Integration across relevant timescales** | Fast active state and slower memory, plasticity, policy, or homeostatic variables jointly constrain later behavior. | A single snapshot, frequency, or global average. |
| **4. Selective propagation** | Some source-state dimensions affect declared receivers while other dimensions remain ineffective or private. | Universal synchrony, correlation, or broadcast alone. |
| **5. Memory-dependent transition** | Retained learning changes how the same or matched input is interpreted and what transition follows. | Mere storage without a causal effect on later processing. |
| **6. Alternative-sensitive prediction or consideration** | Internal state distinguishes possible outcomes or actions and changes selection under controlled alternatives. | Post-hoc verbal explanation or fixed stimulus-response behavior. |
| **7. Action-feedback closure** | Output changes future internal, bodily, environmental, or social input and the returning consequence updates the system. | Output that never returns as usable evidence. |
| **8. Persistent self-related coordinate or control model** | The system tracks and revises variables about its own boundary, body, capability, history, uncertainty, or control. | A static label, pronoun, avatar, or first-person sentence. |

Meeting these relations would support a candidate functional organization. It would not by itself establish phenomenal consciousness, personhood, moral status, or the field-cell identity thesis.

## Capacity ladder

| Capacity | Operational question | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-registration** | Do internal variables track changes in the candidate's own state or consequences? | Monitoring is not yet a learned self-model. |
| **Learned self-model** | Does retained structure predict the candidate's body, boundary, capability, or likely consequence across contexts? | A useful model is not automatically conscious or explicit. |
| **Embodied perspective and control** | Does the model organize perception and alternative-sensitive action from the candidate's situated boundary? | Task control is not yet an observer identity or phenomenal result. |
| **Explicit network self-knowledge** | Can the system identify, calibrate, revise, and transfer knowledge about the mechanism generating its own model under unfamiliar perturbation? | Terminology or fluent self-report without transfer and intervention is insufficient. |
| **Phenomenal self-awareness** | Does the system have subjective experience of itself? | This remains a separate unresolved result level. |

## What component evidence supports

Established studies support parts of the operational test, not the complete SAN join:

- Recurrent computations can contribute to human recognition of heavily occluded objects under a bounded visual task ([Tang et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30104363/)).
- Simultaneous macaque visual-cortex recordings show that interactions between source and target populations can occupy a selective low-dimensional communication subspace rather than transmit every dominant source pattern ([Semedo et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30770252/)).
- Macaque prefrontal population dynamics can integrate context and noisy sensory evidence toward a choice in a defined task ([Mante et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24201281/)).
- Macaque motor-cortex population activity during reaching contains structured dynamics whose amplitude and phase depend on preparatory state ([Churchland et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22722855/)).
- A defined primate thalamic pathway carries corollary-discharge information about impending eye movements, and inactivation impairs sequential movement while sparing single movements ([Sommer and Wurtz, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17093408/)).
- A four-legged robot used actuation-sensation relations to infer and revise a body model after damage, demonstrating adaptive machine self-modeling rather than machine consciousness ([Bongard, Zykov, and Lipson, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17110570/)).

These results support recurrence, selective propagation, context-dependent transition, action monitoring, population dynamics, and synthetic self-model revision in their named preparations. They do not independently establish PWD, NAPOT tomography, Entification, a unified SAN observer, or phenomenal experience.

## Biological and synthetic candidates

A biological test must state species, preparation, circuit or system boundary, receiver, reference condition, measurement scale, behavioral endpoint, and intervention. Population suppression is not automatically a demonstrated inhibitory mechanism, an extracellular signal is not automatically causal, and an EEG band is not a complete cellular or circuit explanation.

A synthetic test must state sensors, effectors, persistent state, update rule, memory access, self-model variables, timing implementation, training history, and matched baselines. A synthetic system may instantiate the functional criteria with timing machinery unlike biology; success would not establish biological equivalence or validate SAN's specific oscillatory hypotheses.

## Decisive test

1. Declare the candidate boundary, timescale, receivers, reference state, task, endpoint, and strongest alternative models.
2. Measure each claimed relation, including the typed PWD feature set where the candidate is claimed to implement PWD, instead of inferring the whole architecture from one proxy.
3. Reversibly perturb one proposed integrating, memory, self-model, or action-return relation while preserving gross activity, input, output capacity, and task opportunity as closely as possible.
4. Predict the specific loss in discrimination, model revision, alternative-sensitive action, continuity, or returned-evidence learning before observing the result.
5. Restore or rescue the declared relation and require the predicted recovery.
6. Compare the joined model with capacity-matched feedforward, recurrent, predictive, control, state-space, self-modeling, and report-only alternatives.

The candidate fails the claimed classification when the proposed boundary is arbitrary, the self-related model has no causal role, action consequences do not update later operation, the intervention lacks the predicted selective effect and rescue, or simpler matched models explain the same results equally well. Failure of one candidate or implementation does not erase the source-defined SAN architecture; it identifies which proposed realization or measurement did not earn its claim.

## Source-faithful development

- **2011:** [[gh-a0051z|a0051z]] describes sensation, learned statistical memory, prediction, action, returned sensory consequence, and a "learning loop that learns itself." This is the action-return and self-learning ancestor; PWD, NAPOT, GCS, and Entification retain later dates.
- **2012:** [[gh-a0632z|a0632z]] says the neurons that produce the changing pattern also receive it, while [[gh-a0119z|a0119z]] describes cooperating and competing learning loops inside a larger process. These are same-network observer and nested-loop ancestors, not the later complete mechanism.
- **2021:** The governed BR-015 source ledger separates an August distributed-oscillator observer ancestor from a November tonic-array account in which excitation and inhibition differentiate a population and a later array receives the changed phase pattern. The named PWD and NAPOT operations remain later developments.
- **2022:** [[gh-a0079z|a0079z]], [[gh-a0115z|a0115z]], [[gh-a0238z|a0238z]], [[gh-a0319z|a0319z]], and the [[napot-revision-genealogy|NAPOT revisions]] develop distributed receivers, transformed projection, perturbation, rendering, action return, and Entification. - **2024-2026:** The later Book, preprints, and explicit network-self-knowledge work refine Entification beyond simple synchrony, distinguish functional eligibility from phenomenal and identity claims, and add intervention, transfer, calibration, and failure tests. They are later refinements, not language backdated into the earlier sources.
