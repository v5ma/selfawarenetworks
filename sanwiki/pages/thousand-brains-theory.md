# Thousand Brains Theory

**Thousand Brains Theory** is [[jeff-hawkins|Jeff Hawkins]]'s and Numenta's account of [[cortex]] as a population of largely independent [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]] that each learn complete sensorimotor models of objects via [[reference-frames-cortical-knowledge|reference frames]], with perception emerging by [[cortical-column-voting|voting]] across thousands of columnar models. In the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] catalogue it is the most direct precursor to SAN's own [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|memory-prediction]] account of [[cortex]], and the SAN literature explicitly frames itself as building on this base — "I'm describing a thousand brains theory mostly with a blend of other ideas" (`00abstractbump--md`, line 15, [[gh-00abstractbump|on github]]).

## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the relationship to Thousand Brains Theory is *built-on, not assumed*. SAN inherits the core claim — that each [[cortical-columns|cortical column]] learns a complete sensorimotor model rather than a feature, and that perception is the joint output of thousands of such columnar models — and then extends it on three axes Thousand Brains alone does not address. First, where Hawkins's account leaves the binding mechanism implicit, SAN supplies one: [[oscillatory-binding-problem|temporal-synchronicity binding]] across [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|columnar oscillations]] is what makes "voting" between thousands of columns coherent in real time. Second, where Hawkins explicitly rejects holographic distribution — Hawkins's answer to [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] was "no, the idea is that variations of the same patterns occur in different [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]]" (`a0045z--md`, line 5, [[gh-a0045z|on github]]) — SAN treats *both* mechanisms as compatible: distinct columns hold distinct reference-framed models *and* those models are duplicated in [[fractal-isomorphic-sensory-arrays|self-similar fractal copies]] across the cortical sheet, producing a hybrid Thousand-Brains-plus-holography picture. Third, the b0302y session extends the theory to the [[brain-computer-interface|BCI]] frontier — "the Thousand Brains theory is saying implicitly that … the [[cortex]] is using the same algorithm that it's been repeating, … there's [[reference-frames|reference frames]] that are being computed" (`b0302ywhisper--md`, line 4410) — making explicit what Numenta's own publications usually leave to inference: the algorithmic uniformity claim that any successful BCI decoder should exploit.
