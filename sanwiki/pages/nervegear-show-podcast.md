The NerveGear Show is a media series hosted by [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]] ([[neural-lace|Neural Lace]] Podcast) covering the convergence of [[neuroscience]], [[artificial-intelligence]], [[virtual-reality]], and [[brain-computer-interfaces]] — the four pillars of the [[self-aware-networks]] research agenda.

The November 2017 edition (SFVR Novem event) featured Sara Shashkes of **Virtual Bytes**, an education and research collective using HTC Vive, Oculus, and WebVR to demonstrate how combining VR tech and neuroscience knowledge can influence human experience. Matterport released an open-source deep learning algorithm for 3D spatial mapping, illustrating how [[fractal-pattern]] spatial data becomes a tool for cognitive modeling.

The show reflects [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s conviction — core to SAN theory — that [[neuro-rendering]] (immersive environments that mirror neural architectures) and [[self-modeling]] systems will converge as VR hardware becomes indistinguishable from perception itself.

- Neuroscience-informed VR design as proto-[[napot]] application
- BCI + WebVR as a path toward [[self-aware-network]] human-machine loops
