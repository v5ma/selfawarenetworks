# Agentic Viewpoint

**Agentic Viewpoint** is the foundational interpretive stance within [[Self-Aware-Networks]] (SAN) theory holding that neural subsystems — [[Cortical-columns]], localized oscillatory circuits, individual [[neural-circuits]], and even subcellular compartments — operate as semi-autonomous agents that carry out distinct computational functions while coordinating their outputs through [[oscillatory-binding|oscillatory signals]] (draft1--md, line 583). Rather than treating the brain as a monolithic, centrally directed processor, the agentic viewpoint reconstructs neural dynamics as an emergent collective produced by the competitive and cooperative interactions of many specialized sub-agents, each contributing a local computation to a global [[Consciousness|conscious]] state. This frame connects directly to the ambition of [[Self-Aware-Networks]] to bridge [[biological-computation|biological computation]] and [[artificial-general-intelligence|AI architecture design]].

The concrete mechanism through which agentic coordination is realized in SAN is [[NAPOT]] ([[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]]): each [[neural-array]] is treated as an independent agent that receives a tomographic projection from upstream arrays, performs local encoding, and emits an [[oscillatory-phase-patterns|oscillatory phase pattern]] that downstream agents decode as sensory or conceptual content (draft1--md, line 583). The [[Oscillatory-tomography|oscillatory tomography]] achieved by this layered agent network yields a [[neural-tomography|neural tomographic]] rendering in which [[Consciousness]] is not located at a privileged locus but arises from the coordinated outputs of the entire distributed agent collective. [[cellular-oscillating-tomography]] extends this logic to the cellular scale, treating individual neurons as agents performing their own internal tomographic computation through [[dendritic-compartmentalization|compartmentalized dendritic]] processing. The [[cortical-column-oscillatory-transmission|oscillatory transmission between cortical columns]] is the macroscopic signature of these inter-agent communications, and [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|oscillatory collaboration across columns]] generates the high-dimensional phase space in which perceptual objects are encoded.

The agentic viewpoint resonates with [[distributed-cognition]] frameworks and [[oscillatory-coupling-multi-agent]] models in which no central command coordinates behavior — global coherence emerges instead from local [[oscillatory-coupling]] negotiations between pairs and ensembles of neural agents (draft1--md, line 583). Each agent communicates through trains of [[action-potential|action potentials]] whose phase and timing within a [[Brainwaves|brainwave]] cycle carry informational weight that goes beyond simple rate coding, a point developed in the SAN treatment of [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|action potential waveform encoding]] and [[action-potential-phase-space|action potential phase space]]. [[brainwave-entrainment|Brainwave entrainment]] across regions reflects the degree to which distant agents have achieved phase-locked coordination, and disruptions to entrainment correspond to failures of [[consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop|multimodal conscious rendering]]. The [[Predictive-Coding]] hierarchy can be read through the agentic lens as a vertical negotiation protocol: each cortical level is an agent that generates predictions for lower agents and receives prediction errors in return, all mediated by [[oscillatory-coupling]] and [[agent-phase-sync-feedback-loop|phase-sync feedback loops]] that enforce temporal coherence across the hierarchy.

At the cellular level, [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|dendritic coincidence detection]] is the biophysical implementation of local agentic decision-making: an individual pyramidal neuron acts as an agent that integrates spatiotemporally coincident inputs across its [[dendritic-computation-network-detection|dendritic computation network]] and fires only when internal threshold criteria are satisfied (draft1--md, line 583). [[apical-dendrites|Apical dendritic]] compartments in particular serve as intra-neuronal gating mechanisms that weigh top-down contextual signals against bottom-up feedforward drive, giving each neuron a two-stage agentic deliberation process. [[Criterial-Causation]] provides the theoretical grounding: agents fire according to intrinsic criteria — threshold conditions, phase states, synaptic weights — rather than as passive relays, endowing the collective with genuinely causal [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organizing structure. [[Inhibitory-circuits]] regulate competitive dynamics among agents by suppressing runaway synchrony and preserving the diversity of agentic contributions to [[Consciousness]], an idea formalized in the [[inhibitory-logic-gate-pattern-cascade|inhibitory logic-gate pattern cascade]] model. The long-term architecture of the agent network is shaped by [[Hebbian-Learning]] principles — repeated co-activation between coordinating agents reinforces their mutual coupling and consolidates stable representational coalitions through synaptic weight updates, linked to [[dendritic-spine-temporal-memory|dendritic spine temporal memory]] mechanisms that anchor learned inter-agent relationships in physical synaptic structure.

[[cells-as-pattern-recognition-devices]] captures the agentic viewpoint at the microcellular scale: every cell is understood as a pattern-recognition agent whose response properties define its contribution to the collective computation, and [[coincidence-detection-neural-bit|coincidence detection neural bits]] are the elementary informational acts of these micro-agents. The [[adaptive-resonance-theory-napot-comparison|comparison of adaptive resonance theory with NAPOT]] illuminates how the agentic coordination logic in SAN differs from earlier connectionist models: where ART relies on top-down template matching to stabilize representations, NAPOT relies on oscillatory phase entrainment among agents to produce a [[consciousness-as-temporally-active-loop|temporally active conscious loop]] rather than a static [[attractor]] state. The [[agentic-brain]] concept in SAN generalizes the viewpoint to the whole brain as an ecology of interacting cognitive agents, each with its own timescale, spatial extent, and representational domain. Finally, [[functional-connectivity]] patterns measured across brain regions by fMRI or EEG reflect the current coordination topology of this agent network, and task-dependent reconfigurations of that topology are how the collective repurposes its agentic resources for different computational demands.
