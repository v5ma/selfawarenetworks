# Rich Club Network

**Rich Club Network**, in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], names the densely interconnected hub topology of the [[cortex]] — the same construct mainstream connectomics calls the *rich club* — that SAN treats as one of three canonical broadcast routes for mesoscopic activity, alongside the [[default-mode-network]] and the [[thalamocortical-network|thalamo-cortical loops]]. ## Post-SAN

Mainstream connectomics has heavily developed the rich-club construct, particularly in work tying hub topology to brain-wide integration, cognitive control, and disorders of [[consciousness]]. SAN's distinctive commitment, beyond reading the rich club as a topological curiosity, is to treat it as a *physical broadcast pathway* whose oscillating groups are concretely involved in the moment-to-moment assembly of conscious experience (`b0131y--md`, line 74, [[gh-b0131y|on github]]; `b0005y--md`, line 15, [[gh-04san|repo path]] under `b0005y.md`).
