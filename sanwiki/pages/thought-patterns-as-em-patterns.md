## Position

Within [[self-aware-networks]] the move from "thought" to "EM pattern" is the move from a folk-psychology category to a physical observable. Each [[cortical-column]] generates a measurable electromagnetic signature when its neurons fire in concert; the population-level signal is what an [[eeg|EEG]] records and what is partitioned into [[brainwave|brainwave]] bands. SAN takes that observation and presses it: the *content* of a thought — not just its presence — is held in the structure of the EM pattern, in the same way that the content of a memory is held in the structure of a [[magnetic-phase-field]] in the [[action-potential-magnetic-phase-field]] account. This is why the framework treats [[emotions-as-spatiotemporal-patterns|emotions as spatiotemporal patterns]] rather than as inner labels: the affective and the cognitive are different shapes of the same kind of physical thing. The framing dovetails with [[oscillatory-convergence-memory|oscillatory convergence memory]] — converging EM patterns are what get bound, retrieved, and recognised — and with [[coincidence-detection|coincidence detection]] / [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence-as-a-bit]] arithmetic, which is the per-event rule by which one EM pattern registers as relevant to another. The thalamic side of the same picture is carried by [[thalamocortical-network]], [[thalamic-bridge-consciousness-portal]], [[thalamic-convergence-zone]], and [[thalamus-as-display-screen]]; "thought patterns as EM patterns" is the cortical-side companion to the thalamus-side hub story.
