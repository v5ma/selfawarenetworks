# Attention Schema Holographic

![Attention Schema Holographic distributed phase self-model plate](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/attention-schema-holographic/attention-schema-holographic-distributed-phase-self-model-20260630-v1.png)

This plate renders the page's central SAN mechanism: the attention schema is not a local module but a phase-coded self-model distributed across cortical columns, with inhibitory envelope shaping, thalamic timing, and phase-aligned binding producing the attended foreground.

**[Attention Schema](./attention-schema.md) Holographic** is a synthesis concept in the [Self-Aware Networks](./self-aware-networks.md) framework combining Michael Graziano's [Attention Schema Theory](./attention-schema-theory.md), described by Graziano and Webb's 2015 mechanistic account of subjective awareness ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25954242/)), with the holographic distributed-representation model of cortical processing. Where the standard [Attention Schema Theory](./attention-schema-theory.md) posits that the brain constructs a simplified internal model of its own [attention](./attention.md) state -- and that this [self](./gh-a0206z.md)-model is what gives rise to the subjective sense of [Consciousness](./consciousness.md) -- the holographic extension argues that this schema is not localized to any single cortical region but is instead encoded as a phase-distributed pattern across the entire [Cortical columns](./cortical-columns.md) network, analogous to the way a hologram encodes information redundantly across its whole surface.

In [NAPOT](./napot-overview.md), distributed holographic encoding is a fundamental property of [neural oscillations](./neural-oscillations.md): each [cortical column](./cortical-columns.md) carries a phase-coded fragment of a global pattern, and the full representation emerges only from the interference pattern of all participating columns. Applied to the [attention schema](./attention-schema.md), this means that the brain's [self](./gh-a0206z.md)-model of its own attentional state is not a discrete "module" readable from a single electrode but a standing wave relationship across the [cortex](./cortex.md). [Brainwaves](./brainwaves.md) in the beta and gamma bands are the primary carriers of this inter-columnar coherence, with [inhibitory circuits](./inhibitory-circuits.md) (particularly [GABA](./gaba.md)-ergic interneurons) shaping the spatial envelope of phase-locked assemblies that constitute the schema at any moment. The [thalamus](./thalamus.md) acts as the timing hub that synchronizes these assemblies across distant cortical areas, broadcasting a global reference frame against which local [action potential](./action-potential.md) bursts are measured for their phase relationship.

The holographic framing resolves a difficulty in the original [Attention Schema Theory](./attention-schema-theory.md): if the schema is merely a local neural representation, it is unclear how it can exert control over the diffuse attentional process it models. In the holographic account, the schema and the attentional process are co-extensive -- the same distributed phase pattern both encodes what is being attended and constitutes the attentional selection itself, via top-down re-entrant signals from [prefrontal cortex](./prefrontal-cortex.md) that modulate column-level gain. This is continuous with [predictive coding](./predictive-coding.md) architectures, where high-level representations send predictions downward and receive prediction-error signals upward; the [attention schema](./attention-schema.md) functions as a meta-level predictive model that predicts the behavior of the attentional filter, allowing the system to correct attentional drift before [sensory processing](./sensory-processing.md) degrades. [Working memory](./working-memory.md) is the functional correlate of this schema maintenance: the content held in working memory is precisely the representation that the [attention schema](./attention-schema.md) is currently flagging as task-relevant, and degradation of schema fidelity -- as in mind-wandering -- corresponds to working-memory content becoming uncoupled from sensory input.

The connection to [Consciousness](./consciousness.md) is direct in this framework. [Self modeling](./self-modeling.md), including the capacity to think about one's own cognitive states, depends on the [attention schema](./attention-schema.md) being legible to other cortical processes; the holographic encoding ensures this legibility is distributed rather than gated through a single bottleneck, which aligns with [global workspace theory](./global-workspace-theory.md)'s claim that consciousness requires broadcast across a wide workspace. The [binding problem](./oscillatory-binding-problem.md) is addressed similarly: the holographic interference pattern intrinsically binds features that are phase-coherent, so unified [sensory processing](./sensory-processing.md) is a consequence of schema coherence rather than an additional binding step. [Micro columns](./micro-columns.md) within a cortical column are the fine-grained units that contribute individual phase samples to the holographic interference pattern, while the [glass brain](./glass-brain.md) visualizations derived from dense [neural oscillations](./neural-oscillations.md) recordings offer an empirical window into the large-scale coherence structures that the theory predicts. In [artificial intelligence](./artificial-intelligence.md), replicating the [attention schema](./attention-schema.md) holographically would require moving beyond localized transformer [attention](./attention.md) heads toward architectures that maintain a globally coherent, phase-modulated [self](./gh-a0206z.md)-model of their own attentional allocation -- a challenge that current [deep learning neural networks](./deep-learning-neural-networks.md) have not yet solved.
