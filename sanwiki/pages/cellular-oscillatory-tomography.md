# Cellular Oscillatory Tomography

**Cellular Oscillatory Tomography** (COT) is, in [[self-aware-networks]], the cellular tier of [[oscillatory-tomography]] — the claim that ordinary cells, not only neurons, behave as oscillatory tomographic computers in their own right and so participate as agents in the same phase-driven reconstruction process that [[biological-oscillating-tomography]] (BOT) and [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]] (NAPOT) describe at higher levels. As a SAN-internal "New Theory of Evolution," COT extends [[hebbian-learning]] beyond the synapse into the general physics of receptor-bearing cells.

The theoretical content is laid out across the SAN drafts. draft1 places COT inside the rhythm-and-cognition story: "the critical role of brain rhythms in cognition. This perspective is further extended through Cellular Oscillatory Tomography (COT), which elucidates the ways in which individual cells compute and adapt" (draft1--md, line 125), so COT is the way [[brain-rhythms]] turn into per-cell computational events. draft2 connects the same machinery to the broader oscillatory neuroscience tradition: "in line with findings by György Buzsáki and Steven Strogatz, and extends this view via Cellular Oscillatory Tomography (COT) to connect individual cellular computations with global cognitive phenomena" (draft2--md, line 26). The theory therefore sits in a Buzsáki-/Strogatz-grade tradition and explicitly couples cellular [[oscillatory-dynamics]] to cognition.

draft3 and draft4 then anchor COT inside SAN's tomographic hierarchy: "advanced tomographic models such as [[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography (NAPOT) and Cellular Oscillatory Tomography (COT). NAPOT explains how synaptic memories become conscious through phase-encoded" representations (draft3--md, line 92; draft4--md, line 157). The pairing makes the division of labour clear — NAPOT operates over [[neural-arrays]] using projection geometry over [[gamma-oscillations]] and [[theta-oscillation]] phase, while COT operates over individual cells using their receptor and channel kinetics. The receptor side is foregrounded in the SAN definition itself: cells "function like neural networks, in part because receptors have thresholds" (paraphrasing draft1's framing), so [[nmda-receptor]] activation, [[gaba]]-mediated [[inhibitory-circuits]], [[acetylcholine]] modulation, [[dopamine]] gating, [[camkii]] cascades, and [[microtubules]]-mediated cytoskeletal dynamics jointly constitute the per-cell tomographic reconstruction of incoming signals.

In the broader SAN architecture COT is the bridge between molecule and mind. It tells [[bridging-molecular-mechanisms]] *what to do with* the signalling cascades — the cell is not a passive integrator but an oscillatory tomographic agent — and it tells [[predictive-coding]] *where* its hierarchical residuals can be physically realized below the cortical column. By extending tomographic computation to non-neural tissue, COT also overlaps with the [[body-as-chemical-neural-network]] reading and with [[glial-cells]]-driven contributions to [[brain-complexity]].
