# Brain as Hard Drive: Legacy VRMA 2017 Route Note

> **Provenance correction:** This slug was generated in April 2026 from a zero-evidence automation
> packet and described a separate “VRMA 2017 conference talk.” No slide deck, abstract, recording,
> transcript, or contemporary notice was attached to that claim. It must not be counted as an
> independent 2017 event.

The verified source is **The Brain as a Special Kind of Hard Drive**, written at AWE on June 7,
2017 and published June 11 alongside Neural Lace Podcast Episode 7 with Android Jones. This route
is retained so older links continue to resolve, but the source-bounded concept page is
[[brain-as-hard-drive|The Brain as a Special Kind of Hard Drive]] and the episode record is
[[neural-lace-podcast-s1e7-android-jones-2017|Neural Lace Podcast Episode 7]].

The verified 2017 material proposes:

- a biological read/write-medium analogy rather than passive file storage;
- measuring a present neural pattern and returning only the difference toward a desired pattern;
- three EEG-XR uses: state estimation, interface control, and direct sensory biofeedback; and
- an iterative loop in which feedback changes the next measured state.

The source does not establish arbitrary memory upload or download, semantic thought decoding from
scalp EEG, clinical efficacy, or the later PWD, NAPOT, and Neural Tuning formulations.
