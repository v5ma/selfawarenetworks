# Brain As Sensory Processor

![Brain as macroscopic sensory processor distributed cortex diagram](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/brain-as-sensory-processor/brain-as-sensory-processor-distributed-cortex-20260629-v1.png)

This diagram maps the page's core SAN claim: four sensory channels converge through thalamic and hippocampal-entorhinal hubs, then distribute across the neocortex as a whole-brain sensory comparison surface. The cortical-layer inset keeps conventional anatomy separate from the SAN interpretive claim that deep feedback loops support macroscopic pattern comparison.

The **brain-as-macroscopic-sensory-processor** model proposes that the entire neocortex is fundamentally a sensory processing hierarchy, not merely the three primary sensory cortices. Four sensory modalities — visual, auditory, somatosensory, and gustatory — enter the brain like an elephant's four legs, converging through the thalamus, hippocampus, and entorhinal cortex before spreading across all neocortical areas.

## The Elephant Model

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s "elephant" metaphor maps:
- **Four legs → four senses** (vision, hearing, touch, taste/smell)
- **Elephant's body → thalamus + hippocampus + entorhinal cortex** (relay and integration hub)
- **Trunk/top → neocortex** (distributed sensory surface)

Under this model, even the **prefrontal cortex** may function as a gustatory/olfactory sensory cortex, leaving the standard three primary sensory divisions (visual, auditory, somatosensory) plus PFC as a complete quadrant map of neocortical sensory territory.

## Neocortical Layer Hierarchy

Layer 1 handles incoming sensory data. **Layer 6 — the outermost layer — processes macroscopic patterns** and generates high-level decisions. This inverts the naive assumption that higher cognition is localized to the prefrontal cortex alone; instead, the whole sixth layer acts as a distributed macroscale comparator, integrating micro-, meso-, and macro-scale pattern comparisons.

Decision-making is proposed as a deterministic mechanical process over predicted futures: the brain computes a range of plausible futures and selects toward the most desirable outcome. See [[deterministic-decision-making]] and [[napot]].

## Learned Spatial Self

Spatial [[gh-a0206z|self]]-awareness is not innate — it is a pattern the brain learns from the world. VR embodiment experiments (e.g., having a subject operate as a robot positioned behind their physical body) demonstrate that the sense of "where I am" can transfer to a non-local locus. This grounds [[non-local-self]] and supports [[self-aware-network]] theory: [[gh-a0206z|self]] is a learned network state, not a fixed anatomical property.
