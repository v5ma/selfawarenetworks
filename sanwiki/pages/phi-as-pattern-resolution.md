Phi (Φ), the metric at the center of [[integrated-information-theory]], does not measure consciousness — it measures the **resolution** of the internal pattern representations that constitute conscious experience. This distinction is the core of [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s critique of IIT as articulated in b0092y.

## Core claim

IIT claims that sufficiently integrated information *is* consciousness. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] argues instead:

> Phi is the resolution of the patterns inside a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-aware network]] — not the quantity of consciousness. Consciousness is the *looping* of patterns.

The resolution of a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-aware system sets the detail and complexity of what it can render; consciousness itself arises from the [[strange-loop]] feedback dynamic (after Hofstadter's *Gödel, Escher, Bach* and *I Am a Strange Loop*) in which sensory patterns, motor outputs, and [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model reinforce one another across time.

## Three distinct faculties

[[attention]], awareness, and consciousness are separate things. Medical imaging shows the brain tracks stimuli that [[attention]] misses (e.g., the gorilla in a visual-[[attention]] task). IIT conflates these, treating integrated complexity as equivalent to conscious experience.

## Pattern resolution gradient

Pattern resolution scales with [[neural-oscillation-complexity]] and brain architecture. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] uses video-game graphics as an analogy:

| Entity | Pattern resolution analogue |
|---|---|
| Puppy / young child | Pac-Man, 8-bit (simple, sparse) |
| Adult dog | Streets of Rage 2, 16-bit |
| Human adult | Grand Theft Auto 5, modern 3D |

The gradient is not just graphical complexity but also *temporal resolution* — how far back and forward a mind can link memories into coherent narrative.

## Desynchronization enables pattern novelty

[[inhibitory-interneuron-networks]] produce the desynchronization necessary for novel pattern formation in cortex. By contrast, synchronized networks (heart, cerebellum) excel at movement coordination but do not generate the varied firing patterns that underlie representation. Desynchronization is cognitively essential; synchronization serves motor and rhythmic functions.

## Self-awareness as circuit design

[[gh-a0206z|Self]]-awareness is not a product of information quantity. It emerges from the *cyclic configuration* of the circuit — the feedback loop in which learned patterns of sensory input, proprioception, and motor output form a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model. A [[gh-a0206z|self]]-driving car has feedback loops but lacks the [[temporal-spatial-frequency]] of biological oscillations that produces human-like [[gh-a0206z|self]]-awareness.

## Phi reinterpreted

Under this view, Phi is a useful diagnostic for the *potential resolution* of consciousness (how detailed and integrated the internal pattern matrix is), not a quantity of consciousness. A person in a DMT state might have high Phi but be dissociated from their actual environment — demonstrating that Phi and awareness-of-surroundings can decouple.
