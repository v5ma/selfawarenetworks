# Tensegrity Memory

**Tensegrity Memory** is the [[self-aware-network]] hypothesis that durable memory traces in neural tissue are stabilised the way [[cytoskeletal-signal-routing|tensegrity structures]] are stabilised — by a balanced field of distributed tensions across many compartments rather than by point-localised storage at any single [[synapse]] or cell. The term joins Buckminster Fuller's *tensional integrity* — geometric forms held together by the equilibrium of compression members suspended inside continuous tension — to the SAN account of memory as a [[brainwave-vortex-attractor|distributed attractor]] in oscillatory and structural state. It sits adjacent to [[holographic-brain-distributed-memory]] in the SAN catalogue: both reject single-locus storage, but tensegrity-memory specifically picks out the *mechanical* and *geometric* basis for distribution rather than the wave-interference one.

## Position

Read as a definitional consequence of its title in the SAN context, **tensegrity memory** describes the proposition that an [[engram]] is not a localised mark but a globally tensioned configuration: a set of dendritic, cytoskeletal, and oscillatory states that hold each other in a particular shape the way the cables and struts of a tensegrity sculpture hold each other in a particular geometry. Removing or perturbing one component does not erase the memory; it redistributes the tension across the remaining components, which is consistent with the [[holographic-brain-distributed-memory]] observation that lesions degrade rather than delete specific traces. In the SAN frame this distributed stabilisation is expected to operate at multiple scales — across [[cytoskeletal-signal-routing|microtubule]] networks inside a cell, across the [[apical-dendrites|apical dendritic]] arbor of a [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|cortical column]], and across the standing-wave field of a [[brainwave-vortex-attractor]]. The same scale-invariant move that lets [[fractal-pattern|fractal patterns]] of activity recur at every level of the brain lets a tensegrity stabilisation principle apply at every level too, which is why the concept is grouped with [[oscillatory-memory-invariance]] and [[biomagnetic-memory-storage]] rather than with [[synapse]]-only accounts.
