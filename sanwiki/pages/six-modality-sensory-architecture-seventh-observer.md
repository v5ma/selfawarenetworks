# Six-Modality Architecture and the Proposed Seventh Observer

The **six modalities and seventh observer** scheme is a historical SAN taxonomy, not an accepted map
of sensory neuroanatomy or brainwave function. Its useful intuition is that observer-action is not
located inside one sensory stream. Its original band assignments and quantum mechanism should not
be presented as medical facts.

## Established sensory organization

Biological sensing includes multiple partially overlapping systems: vision, audition, olfaction,
gustation, somatosensation, proprioception, vestibular sensing, interoception, nociception, and
visceral and chemical regulation. Their pathways use different receptor classes, nerves, brainstem
and thalamic routes, cortical and subcortical targets, and body-control loops.

These systems cannot be assigned one-to-one to gamma, beta, alpha, theta, delta, or a
"neuromodulator band." Frequency bands occur in many regions and change with task, state, species,
layer, and measurement. Neurotransmitters and neuromodulators are chemical signaling systems, not a
seventh frequency band.

## Current SAN interpretation

SAN can preserve the cross-modal insight in bounded form:

1. different sensory and body systems contribute partial state;
2. recurrent circuits combine those contributions with memory, goals, and possible action;
3. no one stream contains the complete body-world model;
4. the sparse distributed system registering and using the combined state performs observer-action.

There is no substrate-free seventh entity floating between six modalities. The proposed observer is
the aggregate operation of the participating cells, fields, pathways, body states, and consequences.

## Corrections to the historical mechanism

- **Quantum/thermal is not a standard sensory modality.** Thermal sensation has identified peripheral
  receptors and somatosensory pathways; generic quantum events do not form a gamma sensory channel.
- **Olfaction and emotion are not a beta department.** Olfactory and affective processing use
  distributed circuits and several temporal modes.
- **Vision, audition, and language are not one alpha soliton pathway.** They have distinct and
  interacting biological routes; ordinary chemical synapses are not established acoustic soliton gaps.
- **Proprioception is not magnetic theta sensing.** Muscle spindles, Golgi tendon organs, joint and
  cutaneous signals, spinal circuits, cerebellum, thalamus, and cortex contribute across timescales.
- **LTD is not established spacetime contraction or quantum decoherence.** LTD refers to measured
  reductions in synaptic efficacy produced through preparation-dependent molecular and circuit mechanisms.
- **Cortical layers are not a universal photocopier magnification stack.** Laminar pathways vary by
  area, cell type, species, development, and task.

## Modality invariance needs a receiver

Braille and visual reading can converge on shared language and semantic systems, but that does not
make early sensory codes identical or substrate-independent. A modality-invariant representation
claim must identify where cross-modal generalization occurs, what decoder or receiver uses it, and
what changes when one pathway is perturbed.

## Relation to GCS

[[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|GCS]] is a separate historical proposal about sensory,
contextual, action, and feedback timing. Its alpha, beta, and gamma assignments remain
preparation-specific hypotheses. They cannot be used to restore the six-modality band table as a
universal anatomy.
