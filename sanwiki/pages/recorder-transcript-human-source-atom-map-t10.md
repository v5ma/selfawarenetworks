## Joined Constructive Result

T10 preserves a continuous operation that predates the mature 2022 terminology:

```text
changing external or bodily condition
-> sensory-array and dendritic sampling
-> local learned transformation
-> temporal, spatial, phasic, and waveform-relative event
-> recurrent projection into other receiving populations
-> integration with stable context, memory, value, and predicted next states
-> transient distributed rendering for the current viewpoint
-> attention and criteria change candidate action routes
-> selected motor or cognitive action
-> sensory return and revision of the next rendering
```

The system that receives these changes is also the system changed by them. There is no little
viewer watching an internal display. "Camera," "screen," "graphics processor," and "frame"
language identifies capture, projection, construction, and update problems; it does not add a
hidden observer.

## The Dynamic Canvas Problem

Rank 154 supplies a particularly strong plain-language problem:

1. neural tissue is continuously active, moving, plastic, and noisy;
2. the participating cells in a representation can drift;
3. the head, eyes, and body change viewpoint;
4. obsolete perceptual content must fade;
5. nevertheless, the world appears spatially stable and usable.

The proposed answer is a relational and transient canvas. Current patterns can be reinstated by
changing populations, move within an embodied coordinate frame, recruit neighboring resources,
and dissipate when they no longer fit the current view. Stability belongs to the maintained
relations, not to permanent biological pixels.

The source explicitly contrasts tonic, inhibitory, and phasic roles. Tonic and phasic are
relative functions, not fixed frequency labels. Inhibition shapes contrast and routing rather
than serving as literal darkness. PWD later names a receiver-relevant change within this
maintained relational activity.

[[memory-state-drift-virtual-mind]], [[memory-prediction-rendering-sequence]],
and [[coherence-dispersion-sculpting-hypothesis]].

## Pattern Threshold, APME, And PWD

Ranks 149 and 153 preserve an August 2021 question that later became more formal:

```text
partial temporal-spatial coincidence
-> dendritic integration
-> threshold crossing
-> action-potential timing and waveform
-> receiver-specific downstream consequence
```

The source asks whether input far above threshold can alter the outgoing event in a way that
preserves information about the preceding dendritic computation. It does not claim that the
answer is already known. Conventional spikes are regenerative, so somatic amplitude is not a
simple linear measure of excess input. The constructive research space includes spike timing,
rate, bursts, waveform variation in bounded cell types and compartments, axonal state,
presynaptic calcium, release probability, multivesicular release, and postsynaptic response.

The source also makes the terminology problem visible. Spatial location, event time, oscillation
frequency, phase relative to a reference, amplitude, and duration must be defined separately.
PWD is valuable only if it specifies their joined receiver-relevant relation rather than
renaming phase coding or traveling waves.

## Receive, Transform, Project, And Listen Again

Ranks 151 and 155 state the no-homunculus operation directly:

- a participating neural element receives a bounded pattern;
- learned cellular and synaptic state changes its response;
- its outgoing event carries the consequence of that local transformation;
- other cells receive and transform that projected event;
- recurrent output becomes new input to the same larger network;
- the aggregate network can therefore generate and detect its own changing activity.

"Talking to itself" means recurrent self-availability. It does not imply a second person inside
the brain. Structural plasticity prepares paths that can reinstate learned sequences, while
sparse distributed recruitment prevents one fixed group from becoming the permanent screen.

Rank 152 adds the tomographic ancestor: repeated, slightly different projections can jointly
constrain a model. NAPOT later joins convergence, transformation, distributed projection, and
recurrent reconstruction.

The operation is easiest to understand through the source's cube example. A receiver can hear
one side described at a time, retain relations among the partial descriptions, and build a
stable-enough whole even though no transmission contains the complete cube. In the neural
version, each receiving population changes what it receives before projecting onward. The
reconstruction therefore depends on relations among differently transformed views, not on one
complete image copied everywhere.

The term "tomographic" identifies the reconstruction problem. A literal computed-tomography
algorithm or uniquely solved neural inverse problem remains a stronger hypothesis. The associated
tonic/phasic language is relative: maintained context and transient departure are roles at a
declared scale, not permanent assignments to universal frequency bands.

## Rank 152: Coupling Without Merger

The same source asks how interacting assemblies can affect one another without losing their
boundaries. Established neural and mathematical work supports selective synchronization,
phase-locked clusters, and state-dependent changes in effective connectivity. That is enough to
preserve the operation:

```text
internally coordinated population A
<-> selective or phase-offset coupling
<-> internally coordinated population B
```

The comparison stops before every representation is called an oscillator or synchrony is equated
with consciousness. SAN's added hypothesis is that coherent subassemblies and structured
dispersion jointly organize routing: compatible timing supports communication, while phase
separation, inhibition, or competition preserves distinctions.

The source's Sumerian, orca, clinical, and stellar examples remain part of the intellectual
genealogy. They are routed rather than erased: repetition and deviation; embodied
state-to-vocal-expression; coordination pathology; and cross-scale oscillator analogy. Their
literal linguistic, cetacean, clinical, and gravitational claims require separate evidence.

## XR, Active Vision, And Semantic Segmentation

Ranks 148 and 157 clarify how earlier BCI and computer-graphics work fed SAN:

```text
current viewpoint and body orientation
-> partial sensory samples
-> learned scene and object relations
-> prediction of what should appear next
-> correction by current input
-> action-ready rendering
```

The Tetris aftereffect shows that current visual input is not required for every experienced
visual pattern. Blind spots, eye movements, scene stability, and object recognition pose a
construction problem. Three-dimensional semantic segmentation supplies an engineering
comparison for grouping pixels or points into persistent objects with learned properties across
views. It does not prove that cortex uses the same algorithm.

The important bridge is functional: XR renders the current camera view rather than every possible
viewpoint at once. SAN proposes that biological perception likewise prioritizes the embodied
view and detail needed for present prediction and action.

## Three-Dimensional Phase Relations And Bounded Propagation

Rank 158 links sensory arrays, volumetric representation, and coupled oscillators. A local
deviation from an established oscillatory relation can perturb connected cells. The transcript
explicitly notes that a small change may not reach the whole network. Propagation therefore
depends on coupling, topology, attenuation, state, and signal magnitude.

This is the correct constructive version of the firefly analogy:

- relative coordination supplies a maintained context;
- deviation carries novelty relative to that context;
- receiving cells change their state and output;
- repeated compatible activity can recruit a larger assembly;
- weak or incompatible perturbations may remain local.

Neural phase, mathematical phase space, extracellular electromagnetic fields, optical light
fields, and quantum fields remain different constructs. The speculative electron and
fundamental-space passages are genealogy for later physics work, not evidence for SAN biology.

## Artificial Neurology And Machine Consciousness

Rank 147 makes a useful methodological move: replace an undifferentiated consciousness label
with specific capacities and tests. Micah's proposal is that an artificial system should build
internally available multimodal representations, use them over time, and report or act on their
contents.

A serious assessment keeps separate:

- wakefulness or active state;
- perceptual discrimination;
- internally maintained representation;
- attention and selective access;
- memory and temporal continuity;
- self/world/body distinction;
- goal-directed action and revision;
- perturbation and recovery;
- subjective report;
- architecture and causal autonomy.

Competence, novelty, fluent language, or a claim of consciousness is not sufficient by itself.
An internal 3D model is a candidate component, not proof of phenomenology.

## Embodiment, Interoception, And Choice

Ranks 150, 159, and 160 connect local control to whole-organism agency:

- an octopus arm or enteric circuit can perform local control without automatically becoming a separate person;
- body-computer interfaces can expose otherwise weakly accessible physiological state;
- repeated feedback can improve discrimination of selected internal signals;
- bottom-up evidence is constrained by top-down goals and regulation;
- awareness of a recurring behavior and its physiological context can create another opportunity to redirect it;
- action changes the next sensory state and closes the loop.

Neurofeedback does not diagnose a disorder from one waveform, and there is no universal EEG
signature for OCD. Expanded awareness can increase practical choice without making action
independent of physiology.

## Field-Cell And Cellular Boundaries

Rank 156 asks whether electric, magnetic, mechanical, ionic, biochemical, and metabolic
processes form reciprocal carrier channels. The bounded scientific form is:

- membrane and synaptic currents contribute to extracellular potentials;
- extracellular fields can sometimes affect membrane excitability;
- cellular motion and tissue mechanics can affect mechanosensitive processes;
- ionotropic and metabotropic signaling operate on different timescales;
- all of these interactions consume and transform physical energy.

Those observations motivate a multicarrier measurement program. They do not establish separate
electric, magnetic, acoustic, or chemical conscious beings. They also do not make immune-cell
chemotaxis a neural message protocol without additional evidence.
