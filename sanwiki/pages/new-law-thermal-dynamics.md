[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s New Law of Thermal Dynamics reframes entropy as a sequential computational process: thermodynamic equilibrium is reached by dissipating [[phase-wave-differential]]s across arrays of sensing-and-transmitting components, step by reducible step.

## Core Statement

> "Entropy can be viewed as the computational dissipation of signals—differences in thermodynamic properties (gas, heat, light, chemical, mechanical, electromagnetic)—across arrays of 'sensing and transmitting' components, such that repeated interactions progressively eliminate those differences and drive the system toward uniform equilibrium."

Unlike the first three classical laws (conservation, entropy increase, absolute zero), this law describes the **mechanism**: the sequential wave-interaction path by which entropy increases. Computation is not metaphorical — each collision or synaptic exchange is a discrete step reducing a property differential.

## Key Claims

- **Anti-emergence**: all thermodynamic and neural computations are reducible to component-level signal exchanges; emergence is a viewpoint artifact, not a genuine physical category.
- **Universal scope**: gas molecules, oscillating neurons, fireflies, and clocks all operate under the same signal-dissipation principle.
- **Equilibrium = synchronous oscillation**: when all [[phase-wave-differential]]s are minimized, the system enters a synchronized steady state analogous to [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]].
- **Perturbation restarts the cycle**: a new disturbance (hole in container; novel sensory stimulus) creates fresh differentials that propagate until a new equilibrium is reached.

## Connection to NAPOT and Consciousness

Tonic oscillating neural groups are in relative equilibrium. A sensory stimulus introduces a [[phase-wave-differential]] — an out-of-phase perturbation — that the ensemble dissipates iteratively through synaptic exchanges. When the differential is fully integrated, a new coherent oscillatory state emerges that is proposed as the substrate of [[phenomenological-consciousness]] of the stimulus. This grounds [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) in thermodynamic principle: the brain is a signal-dissipating system under this law, not merely analogous to one.
