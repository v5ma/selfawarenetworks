## Position

Pending acquisition, the SAN-aligned reading is that "volumetric predictive rendering" combines three already-evidenced SAN moves into a single product. First, the perceptual scene is *volumetric* — three-dimensional and stabilized in egocentric and allocentric frames — as the [[sensory-stabilized-volumetric-rendering|sensory-stabilized volumetric rendering]] entry already articulates. Second, the rendering is *predictive* — generated forward in time from a [[memory-prediction-cycle|memory–prediction cycle]] running over [[tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame|tonic-oscillation reference frames]] — the [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|brain-as-memory-prediction-machine]] thesis. The composite — volumetric × predictive × continuous — is what SAN means by the rendered scene of conscious experience, and it is the substrate for [[neural-rendering-of-qualia|neural rendering of qualia]] in the strict sense.
