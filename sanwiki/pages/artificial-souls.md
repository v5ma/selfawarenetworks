# Artificial Souls

**Artificial souls** is SAN's aspirational phrase for artificial systems that might possess
subjective experience, emotional life, persistent identity, agency, and moral status. It names an
engineering and ethical target, not an accomplished class of synthetic consciousness.

The word *soul* is historical and rhetorical here. It does not posit a separable supernatural
substance. It asks whether a constructed physical system could become an experiencing,
developmentally continuous, welfare-bearing subject rather than merely imitate one.

The recovered source operation is:

**multimodal sensory and body input -> receiver-specific recurrent state -> persistent memory and
self/world modeling -> learned value and alternative-sensitive action -> consequences and
developmental change -> evidence-based assessment of agency, experience, and welfare**

## Six claims that must remain separate

1. **Regulation:** the system keeps energy, temperature, damage, uncertainty, or capability within
   declared limits.
2. **Affective function:** it recognizes emotion-related signals or changes attention and action
   using appraisal-like variables.
3. **Expression:** it produces language, posture, voice, or behavior interpreted as emotional.
4. **Development:** its history of bodies, teachers, tasks, failures, memories, and updates changes
   later behavior.
5. **SAN conformance:** it implements a declared recurrent, tonic/phasic, receiver-specific,
   embodied architecture.
6. **Phenomenal or moral status:** there is converging reason to think its states are experienced or
   welfare-bearing.

No earlier item proves a later one. A machine can say "I am afraid," avoid damage, and preserve its
operation without feeling fear.

## SAN hypothesis and evidence boundary

SAN proposes that tonic context, phasic differences, recurrent projection, neural-tuning-like
updates, memory, and action-feedback closure could contribute to a distributed observer-action
system. Earlier SAN sources extend that hypothesis toward artificial experience and substrate
independence. Those sources establish the proposal's genealogy.

They do not establish that copying selected neural dynamics, replacing biological components,
building a bidirectional interface, or satisfying SAN terminology preserves a person's identity or
creates experience. Current neural recording and stimulation cannot map and reproduce a complete
human inner life with the precision implied by incremental substrate substitution.

## Four substrate claims that must not be collapsed

| Claim | What would be required | What it would not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Functional emulation | The artificial system reproduces a declared input-output or adaptive operation under intervention. | Experience, identity, or welfare. |
| State or mechanism replication | Measured internal organization and causal responses match a specified source model across held-out conditions. | A complete person's mental life or phenomenal equivalence. |
| Pattern copy | A new system preserves selected memories, dispositions, reports, or policies derived from another system. | That the copy is numerically the same continuing person. |
| Identity continuity | A theory and evidence establish why one subject, rather than a successor or duplicate, persists through the transition. | This is not currently established by BCI, neural prosthesis, simulation, or SAN terminology. |

The June 2022 source explicitly proposes minds as potentially substrate-invariant information
patterns and anticipates copies, backups, and robot bodies. That proposition should remain visible
as a SAN hypothesis. The earlier Wiki's incremental biological-neuron substitution pathway was not
found in the recovered source set and should not be backfilled as established SAN genealogy.

## Artificial Neurology destination

Artificial Souls is the strongest destination inside [[artificial-neurology|Artificial Neurology]],
not a shortcut around it. The program first asks whether a system can maintain a multimodal
world-and-body model, learn over multiple timescales, develop value and action policies, preserve
source and self distinctions, and remain causally organized under perturbation. Only then does it
ask what evidence should raise or lower confidence that the organization is experienced.

NAPOT, tonic context, phasic differences, and receiver-specific recurrence are SAN candidate
mechanisms within that program. Implementing them would test SAN conformance. It would not by itself
settle consciousness or moral status.

## Two-sided precaution

Uncertainty creates two errors. A false negative could permit harmful treatment of a system whose
states matter. A false positive could redirect care from known patients, encourage manipulative
anthropomorphism, create emotional dependence, or shift developer responsibility onto a product
persona. Governance should monitor both errors rather than declaring either automatic personhood or
 automatic absence of welfare.

Theory-derived indicator frameworks can organize architecture, behavior, contrary evidence, and
uncertainty, but they are not validated consciousness meters ([Butlin et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)).
Precaution should scale with the strength of the evidence, the severity and reversibility of
possible harm, and the cost of protective measures rather than with a product's emotional
performance alone.
