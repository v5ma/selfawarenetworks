**NAPOT** ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) is SAN's proposed receive-transform-redistribute account of distributed perception and action. The historical corpus describes overlapping sensor/transmitter arrays across sensory and cortical pathways; current wording should not imply that every layer of every cortical column performs an identical, literal tomography operation.
> **Boundary note (2026-07-18):** NAPOT is a SAN hypothesis about projection, oscillation, and reconstruction across scales, not established anatomy that every layer or cortical area performs literal CT-like tomography. Keep neural NAPOT separate from cellular, biological, and spacetime extensions, and state whether a claim is micro, meso, macro, metaphor, or physics analogy. See [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries]], [[screen-metaphor-no-inner-viewer]], and [[comparison-evidence-is-not-biological-validation]].

> Earlier name: **NOAT** (Neural Oscillating Array Tomography). The current NAPOT terminology reflects the addition of "Projection" to emphasize that arrays project their oscillating outputs forward to the next layer.

## The core mechanism

At the abstract level, a population receives partial state, transforms it through cellular and recurrent dynamics, and affects downstream receivers. Real pathways are recurrent, target-specific, and not a simple feedforward sequence. The claim that overlapping paths jointly reconstruct a working model is the NAPOT hypothesis; the holographic analogy is historical and does not imply optical storage or that every local region contains the whole scene.

## Edge communities as a comparison, not anatomy

Functional edge communities are network-analysis descriptions of relationships among measured signals. SAN may compare them with overlapping array organization, but that comparison does not identify an anatomical handoff site or prove oscillatory representation transfer. Functional connectivity, effective connectivity, and anatomical connectivity must remain distinct.

## Speculative spacetime extension

SAN/SIT source material extends oscillatory-array language to physical space and [[space-as-phasefield]]. That is a separate physics analogy. Neural evidence does not establish a spacetime phase field, a physical hologram, or consciousness as an edge moving through spacetime; those claims require independent definitions, derivations, and tests.

## Established component anchors

- [Mante et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12742) supports population-level recurrent selection and integration in a defined task.
- [Churchland et al. (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11129) supports structured population dynamics during reaching.
- [Davis et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2802-y) supports behaviorally consequential cortical traveling-wave state.

None of these findings establishes literal tomography, a universal array hierarchy, or NAPOT's consciousness interpretation.
