# Pragmatic Agency and Consequence-Sensitive Meaning

## The problem

An organism never has a complete description of reality. It must act using partial measurements,
memories, learned rules, bodily needs, and uncertain predictions. The practical question is how
a physical difference becomes relevant enough to alter choice, learning, or action.

## The operation in plain language

Two neighboring calendar days can have nearly the same duration but very different consequences.
On one day a ballot can still be cast; on the next day the deadline has passed. The relevant
difference is not a large increase in energy. It is the relation among time, rule, memory, goal,
and remaining action.

```text
measured difference
-> current body, environment, and memory state
-> learned rule or goal
-> available actions
-> predicted consequences
-> selected action
-> returned result
-> updated state and future policy
```

After this loop is clear, **pragmatic agency** means acting with the best available model without
first resolving ultimate metaphysics. **Consequence-sensitive meaning** means that a difference
matters to a system partly because it changes prediction, available action, expected outcome, or
learning.

## A compact proposal

Let `d_t` be a measured difference, `s_t` the current state and history, `g_t` active goals and
rules, `A_t` available actions, and `P(o | a, s_t)` predicted outcomes.

```text
R_t(d) = F(d_t, s_t, g_t, A_t, P(o | a, s_t))
```

`R_t` is not proposed as a conserved physical quantity. It is an operational relation describing
how one difference changes prediction, policy, or learning for a particular receiver at a
particular time.

## Established footholds

Reward-prediction-error research supplies one bounded neural example in which a difference between
expected and obtained outcome changes learning
([Schultz, Dayan, and Montague, 1997](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.275.5306.1593)).
Sensorimotor studies likewise connect agency judgments to motor command, expected consequence,
sensory return, and context
([Blakemore, Goodbody, and Wolpert, 1998](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.1998.0404)).

Avatar experiments show that manipulated virtual self-representations can alter behavior in
bounded tasks
([Yee and Bailenson, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-2958.2007.00299.x)).
Virtual environments can therefore serve as experimental spaces for changing representation,
available action, and consequence.

Moore demonstrated that some exactly defined dynamical systems contain undecidable long-term
questions
([Moore, 1990](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.64.2354)).
Prediction limits can arise from a system's own dynamics.

Shannon information quantifies statistical uncertainty and transmission while bracketing
semantics
([Shannon, 1948](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x)).
Landauer connects logically irreversible information processing to physical dissipation
([Landauer, 1961](https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.53.0183)).
These results help separate a physical difference, statistical information, reliable
transmission, learned meaning, and action consequence.

## The 2021 conversation donor

A December 2021 multi-speaker discussion joins simulation imagery, repeated practice, virtual
identity, consent, conscious and nonconscious action, information, coincidence, and pragmatic
evaluation. Its strongest public use is the operation above, not a literal simulation or
retrocausal claim.

The conversation was first fixed in the SAN Git repository in November 2024 together with a later
AI-written relevance summary. The 2021 event, unidentified speaker contributions, 2024 public
fixation, and later SAN synthesis remain separate. The source is not used to claim Micah priority
for SIT, Field-Cell Self, or a physics of meaning.

## Where the comparison stops

- A useful simulation or world model is not evidence that reality is externally simulated.
- Prediction limits do not imply external programming.
- Reward-prediction error is not a complete theory of meaning, choice, or consciousness.
- Shannon information does not specify semantics.
- Landauer's principle does not establish SIT, PWD, NAPOT, or a physics of meaning.
- Virtual rehearsal does not guarantee durable transfer or clinical benefit.
- Personal coincidence is not evidence of retrocausality.
- Godel incompleteness is not a measure of psychological wholeness.
- Unidentified speakers are not assigned to Micah.

## The SAN extension

SAN proposes that receiver-relative relevance should be tested as a joined relation among
measured difference, current state, memory, goal, action availability, predicted consequence, and
returned outcome.

Later SAN work can ask whether PWD-like phase, timing, duration, waveform, and magnitude variables
improve held-out prediction of that relevance beyond rate, power, statistical surprise, task
state, and flexible recurrent models. PWD remains a later term and is not backdated to the 2021
conversation.

## Discriminating prediction

A context- and consequence-sensitive model should predict choice and learning better than signal
magnitude or rarity alone, especially when the physical stimulus stays similar but the rule,
deadline, goal, or available action changes.
