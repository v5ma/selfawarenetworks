# Brain Optimization

![Brain Optimization oscillatory intervention loop](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/brain-optimization/brain-optimization-oscillatory-intervention-loop-20260706-v1.png)

This plate shows Brain Optimization as SAN reframes it: audio/visual entrainment, neurofeedback, and ATP-linked cellular support form an intervention loop whose real evidence would be measurable oscillatory change, phase coherence, and dissipative settling rather than self-report alone.

**Brain Optimization** is the umbrella term [[micah-blumberg]] uses across the [[self-aware-networks]] corpus for the cluster of practices and instruments — [[brainwave-entrainment]], [[neo-mind-cycle-neurofeedback|neurofeedback]], mind machines, and cognitive nutrition — that the [[neo-mind-cycle]] / [[neomindcycle-program]] business sold from 2012–2014, and that the later SAN whitepapers re-read as direct interventions on the brain's oscillatory machinery ([[gh-a0086z|a0086z]], line 5; [[gh-a0333z|a0333z]], line 2). The SAN reframing matters because it converts a wellness-services category into a concrete claim about [[neural-oscillation-hierarchy|neural oscillations]], [[brain-as-dissipative-system|dissipative dynamics]], and the assessability of cognitive change.

The defining recipe appears repeatedly and converges across sources: "[[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] is a 'brain optimization' program, that involves [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]], neurofeedback, mind machines, and basically it allows a person to change l…" ([[gh-a0058z|a0058z]], line 3); "[[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] features a brain optimization packages: these involves combinations of [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]], neurofeedback, mind machines, and cognitive nutrition" ([[gh-a0068z|a0068z]], line 25); and the [[neomindcycle-cognitive-audio-control|cognitive-audio-control]] companion is described as "brain optimization with an audio & visual system" ([[gh-a0352z|a0352z]], line 6). The 2014-onward statements add an instrument-level detail — "[[brain-optimization|Brain Optimization]] is new and it incorporates a combination of neurofeedback + [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]] with light and sound to adjust the entr…" ([[gh-a0229z|a0229z]], line 6) — and a service-business statement — "I make a living doing brain optimization research, and services including a brain optimization program that allows people to visit my office regularly for weekly sessions of Neurofeedback driven [[brainwave-entrainment|Brainwave Entrainment]]" ([[gh-a0591z|a0591z]], line 30). The claimed long-term effects are uniform across the corpus: "long term effects after several sessions include brain optimization, more ability to focus, relax, greater creativity and problem solving" ([[gh-a0031z|a0031z]], line 5; restated verbatim at [[gh-a0083z|a0083z]], line 6), and the broader benefit set is summarised as "increased personal creativity, intelligence, memory, and success, via new vehicles of expanded [[gh-a0206z|self]] understanding, including more connections between regions of the mind" ([[gh-a0491z|a0491z]], line 29; [[gh-a0502z|a0502z]], line 28).

The corpus also extends Brain Optimization into nutrition: a "high fat low protein low carb core diet" is treated as the substrate intervention that "optimize[s] cellular fitness and [[atp|ATP]] production" while doubling as "an anti-aging diet, an anti-alzheimer's diet, and anti-diabetes diet, an anti-obesity diet" ([[gh-a0491z|a0491z]], line 25; [[gh-a0492z|a0492z]], line 17; [[gh-a0502z|a0502z]], line 24). That move re-reads Brain Optimization through the [[criterial-causation-atp-neurons|ATP-and-criterial-causation]] register — energy supply at the cell wall as the bottleneck on cognitive performance — and lines up with the broader SAN preference for [[brain-as-dissipative-system|dissipative-system]] framings of cognition. Origin notes give the personal lineage: "Thresholds of the Mind is why I got into Brain Optimization, and Neurotechnology" ([[gh-a0086z|a0086z]], line 99; [[gh-a0333z|a0333z]], line 161), and "From 2012 to 2014 with [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] I considered myself to be in the business of brain optimization" ([[gh-a0333z|a0333z]], line 2).

The most consequential thread for SAN is the explicit acknowledgement that Brain Optimization runs into a measurement problem. The retrospective in b0042y is blunt — "the goal was to optimize brains but of course I couldn't figure out how to prove that brain optimization had happened" (b0042y, line 59) — and the same source supplies SAN's proposed answer: "perception and memory are inherently intertwined with the brain's oscillatory mechanisms" (b0042y, line 17), so any honest measurement of optimization should look at oscillations, not at [[gh-a0206z|self]]-report. The [[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]] / y0031sci summaries make the link to next-generation tooling explicit, framing the work as "the challenges of quantifying brain optimization and interpreting the vast data produced by the human brain" ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]], line 25822; y0031sci, line 107) and projecting it forward into "the vision for advanced [[brain-computer-interface|BCIs]], the challenges of brain optimization, and the idea of creating artificial brains" ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]], line 25882). The [[gh-veca|vecA]] reformulation closes the loop by noting that the [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] "while currently a brain optimization program, embodies key principles that could contribute to the development of next-generation BCIs" ([[gh-veca|vecA]], line 8827) — i.e., the 2012–2014 services frame is, on the SAN reading, an early prototype for the [[brain-computer-interface-neural-readwrite|read/write BCI]] target the whitepapers later commit to.
