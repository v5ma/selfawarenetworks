# Micro Columns

**Micro-columns** (also written *minicolumns* or *microcolumns* in the SAN corpus) are the sub-millimeter [[columnar-organization|columnar units]] inside a [[cortical-column|cortical column]] — clusters of microcircuits one level below the canonical cortical macrocolumn — that [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] treats as the actual locus of millisecond-scale [[coincidence-detection|coincidence detection]] and short-term-memory activity in the [[cortex]]. In the SAN catalogue they sit between the [[dendritic-architecture|dendritic architecture]] of single neurons and the macroscale [[cortical-column-network-node|cortical-column network nodes]], filling the role of the *fast* compartment in the column–microcolumn–neuron stack.
