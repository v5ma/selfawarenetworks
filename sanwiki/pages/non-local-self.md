## Position

Pending acquisition, the SAN-aligned reading is that "non-local" here is doing two distinct kinds of work, both of which need to be sourced before being asserted as [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s settled view. The weaker claim — well-supported by adjacent pages — is *intra-brain* non-locality: the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is not in a single module but is the joint statistic of many [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]] and oscillator populations participating in [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering|persistent rendering]] and [[consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop|multimodal rendering loops]]. The stronger claim — *inter-brain* non-locality, brushing against [[brain-to-brain-eeg-synchronization|brain-to-brain]] coupling, [[artificial-telepathy-via-oscillatory-synchrony|oscillatory-synchrony telepathy]], or [[global-oscillator-entanglement-mediator|global oscillator entanglement]] — must be sourced carefully if it is to be attributed to SAN at all, since SAN generally distinguishes empirical synchronization from metaphysical non-locality.

The SAN reading also intersects [[advaita-vedanta|Advaita Vedanta]] and related traditions in which non-local identity is a phenomenological starting point; SAN's contribution is to ground that intuition in [[self-as-oscillatory-construct|oscillator dynamics]] rather than in metaphysics, leaving open the question of whether the felt non-locality of [[gh-a0206z|self]] is best described as distributed-but-still-local rendering or as something stronger.
