This SAN plate maps the four-state neuron alphabet as a biological readout chain: inhibited and tonic states define the baseline, low-phase sub-threshold deviations contribute to coincidence detection, and high-phase spikes or bursts broadcast the dominant phase-wave differential.

**Four-Phase Neuron States** is the [[self-aware-network|Self-Aware Networks]] discretisation of a neuron's instantaneous status into four functionally distinct states — *inhibited*, *tonic*, *low-phase*, and *high-phase* — that together form the state-space within which [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differentials]] are detected and against which dominance is measured. The construct supplies SAN with a clean, low-dimensional alphabet for talking about neural activity at the single-cell scale, and connects directly to the [[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic-phasic canvas]] and the [[graded-neural-firing|graded neural firing]] line.

## Position

The four-phase discretisation does four jobs in SAN. First, it supplies a *baseline / signal contrast*. The *tonic* state is the SAN baseline carrier — a steady-state oscillatory drive against which everything else is read — corresponding to the [[tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame|tonic oscillation as predictive reference frame]] and to the [[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic-phasic canvas]] reading of [[consciousness]]. The *inhibited* state is the cleanly-quiet baseline — the neuron held below its [[neuron-electrical-firing-threshold|firing threshold]] by [[inhibitory-decay-oscillation-frequency|inhibitory tone]]. Together, *inhibited* and *tonic* define the *no-information* end of the state space.

Second, the four-phase discretisation supplies a *signal axis*. The *low-phase* state is a sub-threshold phase deviation — a [[graded-neural-firing|graded]] / dendritic-level perturbation that contributes to [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|dendritic coincidence detection]] but does not by itself drive a spike. The *high-phase* state is the supra-threshold deviation — the spike or [[burst-event|burst event]] that broadcasts the neuron's contribution into the wider network. The two phase states together carry the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave-differential]] content that makes a neuron informative; they are the *signal* end of the state space. The [[action-potential-phase-space|action-potential phase space]] page elaborates the ion-channel mechanics that implement the high-phase state, and [[action-potential-magnitude-via-duration|action-potential magnitude via duration]] specifies how the encoding works.

Third, the discretisation gives SAN a *clean read-out alphabet*. With only four states the [[recursive-neural-array-perception|recursive neural-array]] can compute over a low-cardinality vocabulary, which is what makes [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence as a bit]] work — a neural bit fires when the right multi-cell combination of phase states co-occurs, not when a continuous-valued combination crosses a fuzzy threshold. The [[coincidence-detection-neural-bit|coincidence-detection-neural-bit]] reading depends on this discretisation: bits live in the joint pattern over four-state cells, not in the firing rate.

Fourth, the discretisation specifies *what dominates what*. A [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|dominant phase wave differential rhythm]] is a coherent pattern of *high-phase* activity that out-competes the surrounding *tonic* and *low-phase* activity — this is precisely the dominance criterion that the four-phase frame makes formally clean. Without the four-state alphabet, the dominance claim would be a continuous signal-to-noise statement; with it, dominance is a categorical change in which state most cells are in, across the [[recursive-neural-array-perception|neural array]].

These four pieces compose to a single position: SAN treats the neuron not as a continuous voltage device but as a *four-state device* whose macroscopic patterns ([[dpwdr]], tonic-phasic canvas) are the legible coarse-graining of the four-state activity across an array. The discretisation is what lets [[neural-rendering-of-qualia|qualia rendering]] read out a finite-cardinality message rather than a high-dimensional voltage map.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream electrophysiology often distinguishes resting / sub-threshold / supra-threshold / hyperpolarised regimes for individual neurons, and Hodgkin–Huxley dynamics naturally produce a small number of qualitatively distinct regimes. SAN's four-phase discretisation overlaps with that practical taxonomy and reframes the *role* of each state: in SAN, the four states are not just biophysical regimes but the alphabet over which [[coincidence-as-a-bit|neural information]] is computed. Specific SAN-side predictions: (i) coincidence-detection performance should depend categorically on which of the four states neurons sit in, with the largest information-theoretic contribution coming from the high-phase / low-phase combination over a tonic baseline; (ii) [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|DPWDR]] onset should correlate with categorical four-state transitions in the relevant array, not just with firing-rate increases; (iii) [[graded-neural-firing|graded firing]] effects should be explainable as transitions among the four states rather than as continuous voltage modulation.
