# Bci

**BCI** — short for [[brain-computer-interfaces|brain computer interface]] — is, in the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] corpus, a class of [[neurotechnology]] that lets a machine and a [[nervous-system|nervous system]] talk to each other directly, so that the [[cortex|brain]] can be read, written, decoded, and ultimately networked into [[artificial-intelligence|AI]], [[virtual-reality|VR]], and [[augmented-reality|AR]] experiences. The author frames it as the working substrate for [[nervegear]]-class devices, [[artificial-telepathy]], and the long-term goal of standing up an [[artificial-cortex]] capable of hosting a [[sentient-self-aware-neural-network]].

The earliest sketches in the corpus describe BCI in concrete engineering terms: a "napkin sketch stage blueprint for a machine that reads the contents of the human brain by communicating with it, via a bci interface that plugs into your nervous system" ([[gh-a0038z|a0038z]]--md, line 857). That blueprint is explicitly multi-modal — "Nerve Gear: [[neuralink|Neuralink]], Openwater, [[eeg|EEG]], MRI, FNIRS, OPEN EIT + [[deep-learning-neural-networks|Deep Learning]]. Multi-modal bci approaches" ([[gh-a0048z|a0048z]]--md, line 37) — meaning the read channel mixes [[neuroimaging]] streams ([[fmri|fMRI]], [[fnirs-biofeedback|fNIRS]] biofeedback, [[eeg|EEG]], MEG, DTI) and feeds them through [[convolutional-neural-networks|convolutional neural networks]] to recover [[spatial-information]] about ongoing [[neural-oscillation]]. This is consistent with a later, more elaborated description in which a real-time vector field map represents "what the bci is detecting with meg eeg fnirs fmri and dti + neuralink" ([[gh-a0412z|a0412z]]--md, line 55), aligning the BCI directly with [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] and the broader [[oscillatory-tomography|neural oscillatory tomography]] program.

The author treats BCI as a life-goal-scale project rather than a single device: "Please help me to achieve my life goals of creating nervegear/augma level bci tech, conscious robots, and [[artificial-cortex|artificial cortex]]" ([[gh-a0087z|a0087z]]--md, line 116), and announces a 300-page synthesis covering "Digital Fashion, Art, NFT, AR, VR, WebXR, 3D Deep Learning, BCI, and the future native digital economy" ([[gh-a0087z|a0087z]]--md, line 158). [[nervegear|Nerve Gear]] is then defined explicitly as "a next generation brain computer interface (BCI) and the development of this technology means reading and writing to the human brain as if the brain was just a special kind of hard drive" ([[gh-a0525z|a0525z]]--md, line 21), framing BCI as a bidirectional channel — not just sensing — that supports [[memory-consolidation]], [[memory-prediction-cycle]] integration, and the storage and replay of [[engram|engrams]] / [[hebbian-engrams]] across the [[connectome]]. The cultural reference point is the [[sword-art-online|Sword Art Online]] universe, where "people put it on like a VR headset while they are sleeping and their minds are transported into a VR game" ([[gh-a0534z|a0534z]]--md, line 19), which the author cites alongside the 1999 Playstation 9 commercial as touchstones every "bci enthusiast should know" ([[gh-a0534z|a0534z]]--md, line 25).

Inside the [[self-aware-networks-theory|Self-Aware Networks theory]], BCI is the deployment surface for the [[self-aware-neural-network]] itself: "I speculate about building a [[sentient-self-aware-neural-network|Sentient Self Aware Neural Network]] on an FPGA, a GPU, a TPU, with a memory buffer, or with a 3D program, as a part of a BCI Brain Computer Interface" ([[gh-a0320z|a0320z]]--md, line 17). The theoretical bridge runs through [[criterial-causation]] and [[criteria-causation|criteria causation]] — Peter Tse's account of how [[neural-circuits]] set [[criteria]] that gate [[action-potential|action potentials]] — combined with [[hierarchical-temporal-memory|Hierarchical Temporal Memory]] (the [[jeff-hawkins|Jeff Hawkins]] / [[cortical-column|cortical column]] / [[micro-columns|micro-column]] tradition) and a [[predictive-coding|predictive coding]] view of the [[cortex]]. The BCI's job is to externalize the [[phase-pattern|phase patterns]], [[phase-field|phase fields]], and [[phase-precession]] signatures of the [[brain-state]] so that [[3d-semantic-segmentation|3d semantic segmentation]] and [[neural-rendering]] can reconstruct the [[mental-space]] occupied by [[qualia]] and [[phenomenological-consciousness]].

The corpus also threads BCI through clinical and applied work. Slow Cortical Potentials read off the [[motor-cortex]] illustrate the canonical control loop: "These brain signals can be [[gh-a0206z|self]]-regulated by both healthy users and paralyzed patients to control external devices by means of a BCI. SCP shifts can be used to move a cursor and select the targets presented on a computer screen" (a0288zslow--md, line 23), with the caveat that "longer training is required to use SCP-based BCI and it is likely that users will need continuous practice for several months" (a0288zslow--md, line 29). Pyramidal-tract neurons offer a finer-grained channel — "Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI): Research in BCIs focuses on utilizing the signals from PT cells to control external devices, aiding individuals with motor impairments" (a0403z--md, line 494) — connecting BCI to [[apical-dendrite|apical dendrites]], [[dendritic-spike|dendritic spikes]], [[dendritic-spines]], [[dendritic-compartmentalization]], [[coincidence-detection]], and the [[nmda-receptor|NMDA receptor]] / [[long-term-potentiation|LTP]] machinery underlying [[hebbian-learning|Hebbian learning]] and [[synaptic-vesicle-release]] (including [[multi-vesicle-release]] and modulation by [[acetylcholine]], [[dopamine]], [[gaba|GABA]], and other [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]]). Open hardware shows up in the form of an [[braingate|OpenBCI]]-style "headset that we could stick open BCI sensors in" ([[gh-b0267y|b0267y]]--md, line 85), and [[neurofeedback]] tools are flagged as the path through which "potential BCI applications through neurofeedback also aligns with SAN, particularly the [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] initiative, which focuses on bridging brain-com[puter interfaces]" (b0042y--md, line 20).

A second cluster of evidence binds BCI to extended reality and [[affective-computing]]. The author repeatedly bundles BCI with WebXR, [[augmented-reality|AR]], [[virtual-reality|VR]], digital fashion, and NFTs — "Digital Fashion, Art, NFT, AR, VR, WebXR, 3D Deep Learning, BCI, and the future native digital economy" ([[gh-a0220z|a0220z]]--md, line 7) — and notes that combining "Cryptovoxels with BCI & Eye tracking the computer could do what is called [[affective-computing|affective computing]]. That means predicting your emotions, intentions, and perhaps ev[en thoughts]" ([[gh-a0214z|a0214z]]--md, line 190). Meetups for "SF VR + NeurotechSF: BCI + XR (VR+AR) + AI/DL: Lets Meet Share Present & Build" ([[gh-a0636z|a0636z]]--md, line 33) and the slogan "BCI + AI/DL + XR" ([[gh-a0636z|a0636z]]--md, line 21) make explicit that BCI is treated as the binding layer between [[artificial-perception]], [[artificial-general-intelligence|AGI]] research, and immersive interfaces — the place where [[expectation]], [[intention]], [[attention]], and [[prediction]] become legible to a machine. The Vision Agency framing — "(field, tomography) Vision Agency BCI" ([[gh-a0264z|a0264z]]--md, line 3) — anchors BCI to [[tomography]] and to the [[neural-array]] / [[neural-arrays]] / [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]] (NAPOT) reconstruction pipeline.

The author also raises the ethical and speculative frontier directly. A research-paper [[gh-b0049y|summary]] describes "the ethical considerations surrounding the use of BCI, including the potential for enhancing human capabilities and the question of whether using BCI to connect the brain to external devices is preferable to death or a limited quality of life," along with "the challenges and possibilities of creating new input interfaces for BCI and the potential use of psychedelic drugs in altering neural pathways" (b0313ySci--md, line 17), naming [[transcranial-magnetic-stimulation|TMS]] limits, [[neuroplasticity]], and [[nootropics]]-adjacent interventions as part of the same design space. Brain-to-brain BCI is explicit — "we're going to be making holograms of the tomograms and use them for telecommunication, right? Or tomogram extraction & implantation for brain to brain BCI?" (b0041y--md, line 389) — and the author flags "research into possible ways of transferring experience thought perspective and emotion with the aid of bci tech" ([[gh-a0624z|a0624z]]--md, line 68) as the long arc, alongside "bci hacking the brain, and artificial brains" (a0597z--md, line 192). Together these positions place BCI at the intersection of [[neuroscience]], [[computational-neuroscience]], [[bioelectronics]], [[biological-computation]], [[information-theory]], [[category-theory]], and the [[advaita-vedanta|Advaita Vedanta]] / [[ground-of-being]] / [[strange-loop]] / [[self-awareness]] frame the corpus uses to reason about [[consciousness]], [[theory-of-mind]], and [[qualia]].

Operationally, the BCI in SAN is therefore characterized by: multi-modal sensing across [[brain-rhythms|brain rhythms]] / [[brainwave|brainwaves]] / [[brainwaves]] / [[neural-oscillation]] / [[theta-oscillations]] / [[tonic-oscillation]] / [[phasic-burst|phasic bursts]] and [[phasic-burst-firing]]; reconstruction via [[fourier-slice-theorem|Fourier slice theorem]] and [[oscillatory-tomography|oscillatory tomography]] into a [[glass-brain|glass brain]] / [[point-cloud|point cloud]] / [[sparse-distributed-representation]] of the live [[functional-connectivity]]; semantic decoding tied to [[receptive-field|receptive fields]], [[inceptive-field]], [[grid-cells|grid cells]] in the [[entorhinal-cortex|entorhinal cortex]], [[hippocampus]] [[episodic-memory]], the [[thalamus]], [[prefrontal-cortex]], [[auditory-cortex]], [[visual-cortex]], [[amygdala]], and [[motor-cortex]]; and write-side modulation that touches [[membrane-potential]], [[action-potential-waveform|action-potential waveform]], [[action-potential-duration|action-potential duration]], [[neural-spikes]], [[neural-coincidence-detection]], [[synaptic-frequency]], [[synaptic-connections]], [[synapse]] dynamics, [[mitochondria]], [[microtubules]], [[glial-cells]], the [[glymphatic-system]], and downstream [[distributed-memory]] and [[working-memory]] systems. The intended deliverables — [[artificial-telepathy]], [[brain-optimization]], [[3d-neural-network-brain|3D neural-network brains]], [[neural-rendering]] of [[mental-space]], and a stable [[frame-of-reference]] for [[senses]] / [[sensory-processing]] — define the success criteria for any SAN-aligned BCI program.

## Related concepts

- [[nervegear]] — SAN's flagship next-gen BCI form factor; the BCI category exists largely to make Nerve Gear buildable.
- [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]] — the reconstruction pipeline that turns multi-modal BCI sensor data into a usable phase-space model of the brain.
- [[criterial-causation]] — the theoretical mechanism (Peter Tse) BCIs must respect when reading or writing neural decisions.
- [[hierarchical-temporal-memory]] — the [[jeff-hawkins|Jeff Hawkins]] / [[cortical-column|cortical column]] view of how cortex represents information that BCIs aim to decode.
- [[self-aware-neural-network]] — the SAN target architecture a BCI is meant to read from, write to, and eventually host.
- [[neuralink]] — invasive electrode-array exemplar of contemporary BCI hardware.
- [[neurofeedback]] — closed-loop, non-invasive training paradigm that overlaps with BCI use cases.
- [[affective-computing]] — emotion / intention prediction layer that BCI + eye tracking + XR are expected to enable.
- [[artificial-telepathy]] — the long-horizon BCI capability of routing thought between minds.
- [[oscillatory-tomography]] — the inverse-problem framework BCIs in SAN rely on to recover spatial structure from oscillation.
