Galactic filaments — the large-scale structure of synchronously oscillating galaxies — exhibit dynamics analogous to [[long-term-potentiation]] (LTP) in biological neural networks. This is a named [[fractal-pattern]] in SAN: the same oscillatory strengthening mechanism operates at cosmic scale.

## Core Claim

Synchronous oscillations between galaxies heat the intergalactic medium, shaping spacetime curvature, attracting matter, and participating in the creation of atoms from empty space. In SAN's fractal framework, this mirrors how synchronized [[neural-oscillations|neural oscillations]] modulate [[synaptic-weight]] via LTP at the biological scale.

The proposed mechanism links galactic-scale oscillation to:
- The [[higgs-field]] (particle mass emergence from oscillatory field density)
- The [[path-integral-formulation]] (quantum amplitude summing over all paths, shaped by field geometry)
- Cosmological structure formation (filaments as attractors of matter)

## Fractal Connection to LTP

In SAN theory, [[long-term-potentiation]] is the local instance of a scale-invariant oscillatory strengthening process. The galactic filament pattern suggests this process is not unique to biology — it is a universal feature of [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organizing oscillatory networks at any scale.
