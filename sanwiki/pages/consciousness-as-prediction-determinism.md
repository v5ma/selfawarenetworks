# Consciousness as Prediction and Determinism

This plate renders a historical SAN interpretation: distributed prediction and competition contribute to action, feeling, thought, and a compressed conscious summary. It does not establish that consciousness is only a read-out, that all neural causation is deterministic, or that one anatomical cascade produces every conscious state.

In this SAN formulation, consciousness is the *news headline* - a surface [[gh-b0049y|summary]] of distributed unconscious processing. The stronger claims that the cascade is entirely deterministic and that free will is an illusion are SAN philosophical positions, not settled results of neuroscience.

> **Medical boundary:** The early note uses a simplified upward-and-downward pathway. Actual perception and action are distributed across receptor pathways, thalamic and cortical circuits, basal ganglia, cerebellum, hypothalamic and brainstem systems, spinal pathways, and recurrent body feedback. The historical wording should not be read as a literal route in which sensory signals generally travel through neocortex to hypothalamus and then return to cause all action.

## Core claim (Micah, July 2011)
The early SAN note summarizes the brain's task as **making predictions** and describes a simplified ascending-and-descending cascade. That sentence records the historical model; it is not a complete anatomical account. Sensory, interoceptive, autonomic, memory, valuation, and motor systems interact through multiple parallel and recurrent routes. Consciousness — the felt sense of a "you" making decisions — is not the author of this cascade but its headline output.

> *"Consciousness is the news headline, the [[gh-b0049y|summary]] of a mountain of unconscious thought."*

This framing positions consciousness as a proposed **distributed summary operation** rather than a separate controller. In the mature SAN account, the sparse distributed tonic field-cell system itself receives, differentiates, updates, and acts; [[entification]] names the temporary unification of those consequences.

## Choice as altitude, not control

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] proposes that what we mistake for *[[choice]]* is better understood as **prediction-altitude**: as awareness expands through life, the system automatically makes better-calibrated predictions. There is no deliberate selection — only increasingly refined automatic responses to incoming information. This connects to [[competitive-neural-pattern-dominance]], where the "[[choice]]" between competing patterns is determined by their relative strength, not by an agent choosing.

## Determinism vs. quantum fluctuations

The early 2011 framing acknowledges quantum-scale fluctuations inside the brain but treats them as chaotic noise within an overall deterministic chain of cause and effect. Later SAN theory introduces [[qgtcd]], which gives quantum-scale processes a more structured causal role — revisiting whether pure determinism holds at the micro-scale.

## Consciousness as fractal self-similarity

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] describes the brain as "a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar structure throughout" — a tree, a fractal, made of cells reaching automatically for nutrients and light. Consciousness emerges at the top of this fractal hierarchy as its most compressed [[gh-b0049y|summary]]. This connects directly to [[intro-fractal-consciousness]] and the fractal-pattern grounding of SAN.
