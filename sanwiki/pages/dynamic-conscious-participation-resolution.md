# Dynamic Conscious Participation and Resolution

## The idea in plain language

A neuron does not need a permanent label saying "conscious" or "unconscious." It can participate in
a conscious process at one moment and remain outside the active coalition at another.

An early SAN note described this boundary as **tidal**. The number and arrangement of participating
cells can expand or contract with the task, current brain state, effective connections, and energy
limits.

## Resolution is not existence

Another note predicted that a measure such as IIT's `Phi` might be more useful for describing the
resolution of an organized conscious state than for deciding whether consciousness exists.

A 2022 spoken source expands the point: attention, awareness, intelligence, consciousness, causal
participation, and representational resolution should not be collapsed into one score. It also
contrasts differentiated consideration with coordinated motor output, but this remains a
context-dependent SAN hypothesis rather than a universal synchrony rule.

That is a proposal, not a validation of `Phi`. It points to a broader measurement rule: keep these
questions separate.

`a0098z.md`, first Git-fixed on June 10, 2022, records an earlier attention-awareness distinction.
It correctly routes to Watanabe et al.'s 2011 binocular-suppression study: attention robustly
modulated V1 BOLD, while target visibility did not produce a significant V1 BOLD effect. The result
supports separating attention from awareness; it does not prove that one rich-club network creates
consciousness.

The source's "long distance interneurons" phrase is corrected. Most long-range cortical and
corticothalamic projections are excitatory projection neurons, while cortical interneurons are
mainly local with specialized exceptions. A cortical column is also not one literal movie frame.

- Which parts causally participate?
- How integrated are they?
- How many distinct states can they form?
- How finely can their content be discriminated or reconstructed?

## Why this matters

External report speed cannot by itself reveal the amount or structure of internal processing. The
same applies to machines: fluent output is not a direct measurement of internal causal
participation or experiential resolution.

## Dynamic-self source variants

[[gh-a0467z|a0467z]] and [[gh-a0473z|a0473z]] share a core proposal: the current self is an
on-the-fly population calculation using dynamic, context-sensitive representations. One source
adds body and avatar material; the other supplies a shorter article framing. They are variants of
one source family. Established recurrent-dynamics research supports flexible context-dependent
population computation, while the conscious-participation extension remains a SAN test.
