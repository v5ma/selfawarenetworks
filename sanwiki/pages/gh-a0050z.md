The low-frequency, high-magnitude [tonic oscillation](tonic-oscillation.md) has a wide-area [entification](entification.md), or unification effect, that synchronizes large oscillating groups of cells and readies them at the critical point so they can react to incoming stimulus.

The higher frequency phasic firing neurons then inscribe the contents of the mind, that the mind is aware of, they formulate from everything you see, taste, hear, smell, touch, they are the words in your mind, the songs in your soul, the hallucinations of people touching you when no one is around after a night of drinking Absinthe.

I was not the first person to suggest that EEG-spectrum tonic brainwaves are related to consciousness. This is an old idea. After reading Peter Tse's book The Neural Basis of Freewill: [Criterial Causation](criterial-causation.md), I am making a narrower distinction: the phasic firing pattern of neurons is something that the entire oscillating cortical-column sack of neurons can observe, notice, learn, and reproduce with different sets of neurons in the firing pattern. I am arguing that the oscillating sack or cortical column learns patterns that are neuron-invariant, because the electric, electromagnetic, and magnetic properties of dipoles and oscillators allow unique phasic firing patterns to disturb every neuron in the oscillator. The neurons in that oscillator will work to slow the firing neurons back to a tonic firing rate, preparing them for future firing.

Neurons also have to take breaks after firing, so if the same pattern appeared to you, to your brain many times really fast, faster than a millisecond, it would have to be represented by different sets of neurons, in different places, because the neuron that represented it the first time might be on a break, well a neuron fires and that takes about a millisecond.
