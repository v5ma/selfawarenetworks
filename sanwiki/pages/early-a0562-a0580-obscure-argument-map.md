# Early a0562-a0580 Obscure Argument Map

This page recovers sixteen SAN source artifacts without allowing an old typo, compressed analogy,
or incomplete physiological description to erase the problem Micah was trying to solve. Dates,
speakers, copied sources, later terminology, current evidence, and correction history remain
separate.

## The problem before the terminology

An emotion is experienced as a change in the whole organism: attention shifts, muscles prepare,
breathing and heart activity change, memories and possible actions become easier or harder to
reach, and electrical population activity changes. A person can often feel that transition from
inside, while an instrument records only selected physical correlates from outside.

The scientific problem is not whether an emotion is physical. It is how to connect these levels
without reducing the emotion to one electrode value:

```text
subjective state and bodily context
-> changing neural-population dynamics
-> limited EEG, MEG, optical, or physiological measurement
-> feedback that makes one correlate perceptible
-> learned discrimination and partial self-regulation
-> changed attention, choice, action, and returned consequence
```

The early Neo Mind Cycle notes describe this as learning to recognize one's own brainwave patterns.
Current language should say that a person learns associations between experience and selected
electrophysiological feedback. The feedback is real, but it is not the whole emotion and does not by
itself prove a treatment.

## What it means to detect an emotional shift

Micah's 2012 report in `a0569z.md` makes a specific and defensible observation: when his internal
state changed, the light and sound driven by his EEG also changed, and repeated observation helped
him associate aspects of subjective experience with aspects of the feedback.

The source-faithful modern statement is:

> Emotions and shifts in internal state are accompanied by changing electrophysiological and bodily
> dynamics. A person can detect some transitions directly through interoception and can learn
> additional associations when selected EEG or physiological features are converted into contingent
> feedback. The measured feature is a correlate of the changing state, not the state in its
> entirety.

This preserves the original finding without claiming a one-to-one emotion frequency, direct thought
readout, or universal treatment effect.

## Choice begins with distributed evidence

The 2011 `a0575z.md` note uses literal ions, holes, pressure, and pathways. Its strongest operation is
not the ion arithmetic. It is the sequence:

```text
different inputs carry different learned implications
-> spatial and temporal coincidence increases effective drive
-> inhibition and pathway state alter which evidence can propagate
-> a threshold is crossed
-> one action route becomes effective
```

`a0577z.md` adds the distributed competition between excitation and inhibition. This is an early
ancestor of the later SAN Choice mechanism. The mature name is not assigned to 2011, but the
coincidence, weighting, threshold, and route-selection atoms are present.

## The spike is a stage, not the whole channel

`a0573z.md` objected to saying that neurons communicate "by action potentials" when the action
potential travels within one cell. The literal objection is too strong, because propagation to the
terminal is an essential part of neural communication. The underlying question was right: the
spike train alone does not exhaust the channel.

```text
dendritic and somatic state
-> spike initiation and propagation
-> branch and terminal invasion
-> calcium-dependent, probabilistic vesicle release
-> transmitter and cotransmitter mixture
-> receptor- and state-dependent postsynaptic transformation
-> later firing, plasticity, inhibition, or modulation
```

Experiments show that connections made by the same axon can differ according to target, strength,
release dynamics, and recent history. That is a strong physiological bridge to SAN's later interest
in receiver-relative, state-dependent neural output. It is not evidence that an action potential is
irrelevant.

## Structural plasticity was not just strong versus weak

`a0570z.md` was reaching for a structural alternative to a purely scalar synaptic-weight story.
Adult circuits can form, retract, stabilize, and eliminate dendritic spines and synapses, while also
changing efficacy at existing connections. The repaired statement therefore keeps both:

```text
functional plasticity: an existing connection changes how it responds
structural plasticity: the local geometry or existence of a connection changes
```

This is a richer account than either "strong versus weak" or "connected versus disconnected."

## From brute-force search to learned representation

`a0576z.md` belongs in the SAN history of the perceptron and deep learning. Deep Blue searched a
hand-engineered chess problem at enormous scale. The Google experiment trained a designed neural
network on unlabeled image crops and found units sensitive to higher-level features such as faces,
cat faces, and bodies. It did not label those images during feature learning, but researchers still
selected the architecture, objective, data, optimization, and tests.

The lasting SAN question is not whether one machine was magical. It is which computational
organization can learn reusable categories from sensory structure, bind them to action and bodily
consequence, and continue developing after deployment.

## Measurement devices see projections, not the whole state

The copied EEG source in `a0562z.md` and the later OPM-MEG intake in `a0578z.md` establish a useful
instrument boundary:

- scalp EEG measures voltage differences produced by spatially summed current sources and distorted
  by head conductivity;
- MEG measures magnetic fields and has a different source geometry and inverse problem;
- wearable OPM-MEG can place sensors close to the scalp and tolerate movement;
- no one modality directly reports the complete cell-by-cell content of emotion, thought, or
  consciousness.

That boundary strengthens the SAN instrument program. It explains why multiple sensors, explicit
perturbations, individual calibration, and returned behavior are needed.
