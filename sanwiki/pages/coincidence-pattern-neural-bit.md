# Coincidence-Pattern Neural Bit: The 2021 Ensemble Extension

The **coincidence-pattern neural bit** is SAN's 2021 extension from a local, receiver-relative
coincidence event to a learned population pattern. Selected cells become active in a reproducible
spatial-temporal relation; activity-dependent changes alter how that population responds later; and
a partial cue may help recruit a related learned ensemble. The proposal concerns a distributed
pattern and its use by another receiver. It does not make one spike, one synapse, one neuron, one
cortical column, or one oscillation intrinsically equal to one Shannon bit.

## Source recovery: what the 2021 recording proposed

[[gh-b0180y|The source recording b0180y]] was first recorded on **September 29, 2021**, according
to the M4A creation metadata. Its rough transcript was publicly Git-fixed on June 8, 2022 and
explicitly warned that the audio transcription needed repair. A 2026 large-v3-turbo transcription
recovered the source more clearly, but it remains discovery material until checked against the
audio.

The source was exploratory rather than categorical. It repeatedly said that the proposed
microtubule mechanism might or might not occur, that its author did not know the upscaling
mechanism, and that an appropriate paper might answer the question. It also corrected itself from
neurons learning the "same exact patterns" to neurons learning **different versions of the same
pattern**. The prior Wiki page lost those uncertainty and distributed-representation boundaries.

The recoverable operation is:

`receiver-relative events -> coactive spatial-temporal ensemble -> activity-dependent tuning ->
partial completion or re-expression -> context- and value-sensitive downstream use`.

This is distinct from [[coincidence-as-a-bit|the 2017 origin]], which defines the local information
event, and [[coincidence-detection-neural-bit|the 2018 refinement]], which adds subthreshold,
decoder, and information-theory qualifications. The 2021 source asks how many such local
distinctions become a learned ensemble representation.

## Event, pattern, and analytical bit

Let `P_W` denote a measured population pattern during a declared window `W`:

`P_W = {(i, t_i, y_i)}`, where `i` identifies a measured cell, `t_i` is event timing, and `y_i`
describes the measured event or state.

The pattern may include spikes, bursts, silence, subthreshold events, or another declared response.
A decoder can define a binary analytical readout:

`b_D(P_W) = 1` when pattern `P_W` meets decoder criterion `D`; otherwise `b_D(P_W) = 0`.

That readout is one binary partition created for an experiment. It does not imply that the complete
population state is binary or that every occurrence carries exactly one bit of information.
Information depends on the alternatives, probabilities, measurement resolution, receiver, and
context.

## Established evidence for learned ensembles

Primary experiments establish bounded versions of several component operations:

- [Carrillo-Reid et al. (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaf7560) repeatedly stimulated
  selected neurons in layer 2/3 of awake mouse visual cortex and produced ensembles that recurred
  after imprinting and could be recalled under the study conditions.
- [Carrillo-Reid et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2019.05.045) showed that targeted
  activation of a small number of identified pattern-completion neurons could recall behaviorally
  relevant ensembles in mouse visual cortex and alter a learned visual behavior.
- [Nakazawa et al. (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1071795) found that a CA3 NMDA-receptor
  manipulation impaired associative recall from partial cues in mice, whereas
  [Mei et al. (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0019326) found preserved partial-cue
  recall under different inducible and pharmacological NMDA-receptor manipulations. Pattern
  completion therefore cannot be assigned to one universal receptor rule.

These results support coactive ensembles, activity-dependent imprinting, and pattern completion in
specific preparations. They do not show that every participating neuron stores a complete invariant
copy, that every coactive population is a memory, or that one cortical column is the brain's
universal learning matrix.

## Recovering the cortical-column analogy

The 2021 recording called a cortical column a grid or "mini orchestra." Read literally, the prior
page turned that analogy into universal anatomy. Read functionally, the useful proposal is that a
local population can contain cells with different roles whose coordinated activity forms a
reproducible ensemble state.

A cortical column, barrel, layer-specific population, hippocampal subfield, nucleus, or other local
circuit can be the declared **mesoscopic study unit** when its anatomy supports that choice. No one
container should be imposed across all cortical areas, species, tasks, or subcortical systems.
Cells can participate in overlapping ensembles and can contribute different aspects of a learned
relation rather than each holding the same complete pattern.

## Recovering the microtubule question

The source's medically recoverable question is not whether microtubules are simply "the memory" or
whether protein synthesis occurs on a microtubule. It is an **interface question**:

`intracellular state -> membrane or synaptic consequence -> altered cellular output -> measurable
population effect`.

Microtubules provide intracellular structure and transport routes and participate in activity-
dependent spine dynamics. [Hu et al. (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3074-08.2008)
observed activity-dependent microtubule invasion of dendritic protrusions, and
[Jaworski et al. (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2008.11.013) linked dynamic microtubule
ends to spine morphology and synaptic plasticity in cultured neurons. Proteins are synthesized by
ribosomes; microtubules can help organize and transport cellular cargo. Disrupting microtubules can
affect neuronal health and particular forms of plasticity, but it does not follow that all LTP fails
through one pathway or that microtubules store a complete population pattern.

Any conventional or quantum microtubule proposal must identify the variable, cellular receiver,
membrane or synaptic transform, population readout, and perturbation-rescue result. The 2021 source
posed that bridge burden; it did not solve it. See [[quantum-microtubule-hypothesis]] for the
separate hypothesis family.

## Value influences use; it does not label a destination

Learned value can change neural responses and behavior.
[Paton et al. (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04490) measured positive- and negative-value
coding in primate amygdala neurons during learning, and
[Redondo et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13725) switched the valence associated with a
mouse contextual-memory engram under specific experimental conditions. Selective coordination can
also influence interareal communication; for example,
[Bosman et al. (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2012.06.037) measured attention-related
selective synchronization between macaque visual areas.

These results do not show that each pattern carries one scalar valence tag that determines which
brain region receives it. Downstream recruitment depends jointly on anatomical projections,
synaptic weights, cell and receptor state, inhibition, neuromodulation, timing, current task,
learned value, and bodily context.

The source-faithful SAN hypothesis is narrower: a learned pattern's spatial-temporal signature and
its present affective or motivational significance interact with receiver tuning and network state,
changing which available downstream ensembles are recruited and what action becomes more likely.

## Tonic context, phasic pattern, and distributed use

The ongoing state of cells and circuits supplies a [[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic receiver context]].
A measured coincidence pattern is a phasic population departure only relative to that context. It
becomes a candidate [[phase-wave-differentials|PWD pattern]] only when its reference state, typed
changes, routes, receivers, and downstream consequences are specified.

- **Micro:** synapses, dendritic branches, somata, axons, and terminals produce receiver-relative
  events and plastic changes.
- **Meso:** an identified local population expresses, completes, separates, or suppresses an
  ensemble pattern.
- **Macro:** distributed recurrent, bodily, and motor systems use many population changes in
  perception, memory, choice, and action.

The same abstract receive-distinguish-update relation may recur across scales, but the biological
receiver and measurement must be redefined at each step. A local ensemble does not contain an inner
viewer. The participating receivers, recurrent pathways, bodily feedback, and motor consequences
jointly constitute the observer-action system.

## Distinctive SAN hypothesis and test

The distinctive 2021 proposition is that learned spatial-temporal relations among receiver-relative
events form distributed ensemble representations, and that the participating cells are tuned toward
different versions or fragments of that relation. Partial reactivation can then help re-express a
related population state whose downstream use depends on current context and learned value.

A decisive test should:

1. define one cell class, circuit, task, time window, and candidate ensemble;
2. measure cellular events, ensemble recurrence, connectivity or plasticity, and downstream output;
3. compare rate-, count-, timing-, fixed-window-, and ensemble-state models on held-out trials;
4. perturb selected ensemble members, plasticity mechanisms, microtubule dynamics, and value inputs
   separately rather than treating them as one cause;
5. test whether partial cues complete a specific learned state and whether matched restoration
   rescues the state or behavior; and
6. determine whether receiver-relative timing and typed PWD variables add predictive and causal
   value beyond conventional population covariates.

Failure of the ensemble model to outperform simpler decoders, failure of targeted perturbation to
selectively alter completion, or failure of the proposed intracellular variable to mediate a
cellular and population consequence would count against the stronger SAN interpretation.
