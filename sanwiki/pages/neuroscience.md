## Post-SAN

External neuroscience has converged on parts of the SAN picture without committing to its strongest version. The 2026 *Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews* paper on analog rhythmic units (`dl-current--ARUs_Bio_Comp_Paper`, paper-external, 2026) explicitly questions "the persistence of the digital-and-discrete computation metaphor in neuroscience" — territory SAN has been arguing through the [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|oscillatory-collaboration]] and [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|waveform-encoding]] frame for over a decade. Where mainstream [[computational-neuroscience|computational neuroscience]] treats [[predictive-coding]] as an algorithmic story about precision-weighted error signals, SAN reads the same observations as evidence that prediction *is* the [[brainwave]] phase structure — the mainstream view is consistent with SAN, but doesn't commit to the stronger ontological claim. The [[gh-06san|06san]] chapter is honest about this gap: SAN extends beyond "current empirical findings" but is structured to be testable against them (`06san--md`, line 51, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/06san.md)). Concrete divergences include SAN's treatment of [[microtubules]] and [[penrose-hameroff|Penrose-Hameroff]]-adjacent quantum substrates as candidate carriers of the oscillatory signal — a position mainstream neuroscience treats as fringe but which SAN ties to specific observable predictions about [[phase-coherence]] across [[cortical-column]] networks.
