## Post-SAN

Mainstream neuroscience has historically treated the [[hippocampal-reference-frames|hippocampus]] as anatomically distinct (allocortex, three-layer rather than six-layer), and the "hippocampus is just another [[cortical-column|cortical column]]" reading is non-trivial — Hawkins-style columnar accounts have been pushing in this direction in recent years, and the [[grid-cells-place-cells-oscillators|grid-cell]] / [[reference-frames-cortical-knowledge|reference-frame]] convergence supports it.
