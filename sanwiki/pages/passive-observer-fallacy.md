# Passive Observer Fallacy

**Passive Observer Fallacy** is the cross-referenced label, used by other [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] pages, for the mistaken intuition that observation is a *passive window* onto reality rather than an active, constructive, [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|memory-prediction]] operation. Within the SAN frame the fallacy collapses once perception is treated as a [[consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop|multimodal rendering loop]] whose contents are generated by [[consciousness-as-prediction-determinism|prediction]] rather than received by it.
