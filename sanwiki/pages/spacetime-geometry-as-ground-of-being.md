The idea that all things — thoughts, emotions, objects, photons, information bytes, living beings — are expressions of a single underlying [[spacetime]] geometry unfolding in time while simultaneously existing as a static, faceted shape outside of time.

## Core claim

The geometry of spacetime is sufficient explanation for the existence of observers, complexity, and consciousness. Just as the geometry of water explains oceans and gravity explains galaxies, spacetime geometry may explain why there are beings that ask why they exist.

Three perspectives on the same geometry:

- **Temporal view**: complexity appears to increase.
- **Thermodynamic view**: entropy appears to increase.
- **4D view**: nothing moves; the entire pattern is a static shape.

## The observer

The observer is part of the geometry, not external to it. "Is the observer reality? Yes." The observer does not *create* reality — it *is* a feature of the spacetime geometry expressing itself through local perception. This frames [[observer-dependent-reality]] as a physical consequence, not a philosophical add-on.

## Information and matter

The note treats "byte of information" on the same ontological footing as photons, atoms, and people. This prefigures SAN's later treatment of [[information-as-physical]] — information has geometric standing in the world, not merely instrumental standing.

Matter-antimatter asymmetry produces a slow drift toward complexity via a "viciously feeding circle of chaos and attraction." Complexity and entropy feed each other, producing the fractal-like elaboration seen at every scale.

## Relation to SAN

This 2010 note seeds the later SAN position that [[fractal-consciousness]] and [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) emerge from the same physical substrate. The geometry of the brain is continuous with the geometry of spacetime; the mind's [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling is the universe observing itself through a local aperture.
