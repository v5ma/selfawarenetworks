# Observer-Observed Entification

**Observer-observed entification** is SAN's proposal that a distributed system can form a bounded,
causally consequential self-world relation without creating a second internal viewer. The observer
and observed are complementary roles within one recurrent operation: part of the system carries a
working differentiation, and the same system is changed by and acts through that differentiation.

## What entification must mean operationally

An entified process requires more than synchrony or a visually unified pattern. A candidate must
show:

1. **boundary:** which cells, circuits, body variables, and environmental relations participate;
2. **differentiation:** which internal alternatives the system can distinguish;
3. **integration:** how distributed variables constrain one another without becoming identical;
4. **recurrence:** how consequences return and alter the next state;
5. **memory and continuity:** how current state is linked to prior learning and future action;
6. **causal use:** how the relation changes report, control, autonomic response, or behavior.

No single perspective point, canvas, array, or cortical region is created by definition.

## Relation to the perceptual screen

The [[perceptual-screen-neural-rendering|perceptual screen]] is a metaphor for the organized working
state of the distributed process. Observer-observed entification does not explain first-person
experience by inserting a first-person location into that state. It proposes that the system's own
receiver-relative organization supplies the perspective through its body, history, goals, and
possible actions.

## Established physiology and SAN hypothesis

Distributed representation, recurrent processing, multisensory integration, self-modeling, and
sensorimotor control are established research domains. SAN adds the claim that their field-cell
organization forms one observer-action entity and may be constitutive of experience. That identity
claim remains unresolved and must be tested separately from report or behavioral coordination.

## Falsification direction

The claim fails if entification adds no predictive or causal value beyond ordinary recurrence,
workspace access, predictive processing, body modeling, and action control, or if its proposed
field-cell boundary cannot be identified consistently across perturbations and state changes.
