
## The Self Model Without A Little Viewer

Ranks 122-125 contain a constructive solution to the observer problem:

```text
bodily and sensory evidence
-> prediction of possible consequence
-> learned relation between action and return
-> distributed self/world model
-> recurrent selection and observation
```

Rank 123 is especially useful because the source tests and rejects a thalamic "screen." A screen
does not explain perception unless the receiving operation is also explained. The replacement
is a cascade of cells whose states are changed by projected activity and whose outputs generate
the next distributed state.

Rank 124 states that the self-model combines body state, goals, remembered relations, predicted
consequences, and representations of other agents. There is no additional self behind that

## Memory, Reexpression, And Scale

Ranks 115, 120, 122, and 125 distinguish four ideas that should not be collapsed:

- **storage constraints:** maintained synaptic, dendritic, cellular, and network relations;
- **reactivation:** a current cue recruits part of a learned relation;
- **reexpression:** another population preserves selected relations in a changed carrier;
- **scale:** micro, meso, and macro descriptions expose different dependencies.

This is not a claim that one exact pattern is enlarged like a photocopy. A cell can participate
in an engram without storing the entire memory. A population can preserve an object relation
without reproducing the same activity in every sensory or motor system. Retrieval can update a
memory, but it does not always strengthen it, and reconsolidation is conditional.

Physical size, representational granularity, and update rate are separate variables. Similar
motifs across scales do not establish literal fractal identity or a universal frequency ladder.

[[memory-prediction-rendering-sequence]], and

## Tonic Context, PWD, Coherence, And Dispersion

Ranks 115, 121, 127, and 128 recover the operation before and after its mature name:

1. a population maintains a context over a selected interval;
2. a new event changes timing, phase, waveform, recruitment, or receiver consequence;
3. compatible inputs can coordinate or reinforce one another;
4. inhibition and structured phase dispersion preserve alternatives and redirect routes;
5. the changed relation becomes available to memory, perception, choice, or action.

**Phase Wave Differential (PWD)** names this receiver-relevant relational change. It is not a
replacement for action potentials, and it is not simply a new name for phase coding, coherence,
traveling waves, or gamma power.

Gamma is neither necessary nor sufficient by itself for consciousness. A conscious episode can
include coordination, dispersion, slow context, fast bursts, and recurrent exchange at several
scales. Tonic and phasic remain relative roles rather than fixed frequency bands.

## Choice, Emotion, And Action

Ranks 118 and 119 preserve two related research programs.

The emotion-electrophysiology route begins with the author's report that emotional shifts are
paired with bodily or electrophysiological changes he can notice. That observation can generate
testable hypotheses, but it does not identify the source or causal direction. A useful protocol
would align self-report and task events with EEG, ECG, respiration, skin conductance, EMG,
movement, and behavior, then test which relations survive perturbation and replication.

The choice route treats an ongoing behavior and a goal-supported alternative as competing
assemblies. Attention, learned value, body state, inhibition, and timing change which route is
recruited. The selected neural route then triggers ordinary motor and neuromuscular physiology;
neural voltage initiates the chain but does not provide the ATP used by muscle contraction.

## EEG In XR And The External Cortex

Rank 126 documents a 2021 reflection on the 2018 Noisebridge EEG-in-XR work. The route joined
OpenBCI-style signals, an immersive headset, microcontroller or web-server transport, and a
browser-based spatial display. It belongs in the Neo Mind Cycle and Neural Lace genealogy as
real amateur neuroscience and interface engineering.

The later external-cortex idea adds reciprocal adaptation:

```text
person changes interface
-> interface measures behavior or physiology
-> interface changes what it presents
-> person changes attention or action
```

The source also contains a useful current-view principle: preserve a wider world and personal
state, allocate detail according to viewpoint and task, prepare likely next states, act, and use
the returned result to update the next view. This is functionally comparable to foveated rendering,
selective attention, cognitive offloading, and adaptive BCI without claiming a literal graphics
pipeline in cortex.

For several users, the constructive proposal is a temporary shared task model:

```text
distinct person loops
-> shared objects, goals, roles, and uncertainty
-> coordinated but attributable action
-> returned consequence
-> individual learning and reversible separation
```

Communication coupling, interbrain synchrony, and collective performance make parts of this
operation measurable. They do not establish transferred qualia, permanent fusion, or one conscious
global subject. SAN preserves the stronger question as a testable task-level collective-controller
hypothesis.
