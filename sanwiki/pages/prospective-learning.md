# Prospective Learning

![Prospective learning: established retino-geniculo-cortical anatomy, a bounded SAN phase-gradient hypothesis, future-state prediction residuals, and fast versus slow learning timescales](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/prospective-learning/prospective-learning-phase-gradient-future-state-test-20260713-v2.png)

This SAN mechanism card frames prospective learning as a future-oriented memory-prediction loop: past sensory context and engram libraries generate candidate future trajectories, criterial causation selects action, sensory consequences return a match/error signal, and Hebbian updates reshape the learner toward future task distributions.

**Prospective Learning** is, as a definitional consequence of the title within [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], the SAN-aligned framing of learning oriented toward *future* tasks rather than only retrospective fitting — the family of training regimes in which the predictor is evaluated on its ability to generalize to as-yet-unseen task distributions, and in which the learner's [[engrams|engram]] library is updated with that forward-task generalization as the optimization target. SAN's stake in this construct is direct: the [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|brain treated as a memory-prediction machine]] is, by construction, a prospective learner — every [[hebbian-learning|Hebbian update]] is implicitly weighted by how it will sharpen future predictions, not by how it would have improved the past.
