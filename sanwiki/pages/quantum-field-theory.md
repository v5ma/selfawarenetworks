# Quantum Field Theory

**Quantum Field Theory** (QFT) is the relativistic-quantum-mechanics framework in which particles are excitations of underlying fields and dynamics are computed via path-integral sums over field configurations — and within [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] it serves as the **physics-side reference frame** that [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]], [[sit|SIT]], and Super Dark Time are positioned against, with the SAN-original move being a **time-density modification** ρ_t(x) of the standard QFT-in-curved-spacetime framework. SAN does not propose a competing QFT; it proposes a *time-density-modulated* extension of QFT in which the metric structure that QFT lives on is itself driven by an informational time-density field, with the standard QFT recovered in the flat-spacetime / decohered-fields limit (per `dl-current--Super_Information_Theory76beta2` line 430 et al., "Quantum Field Theory Limit: Flat Spacetime and Decohered Fields" — appearing as Appendix E.2 across SIT 76/77/78/79).

## Post-SAN

Mainstream QFT after roughly 2010 has moved along three lines: (a) the renormalization-group / effective-field-theory paradigm (Wilson, Polchinski, Weinberg) treating QFTs as effective theories with cutoffs; (b) the holographic / AdS-CFT line treating gravity as emergent from a boundary QFT; and (c) the curved-spacetime QFT line (Wald, Hollands-Wald, Fuentes) treating QFT on a fixed curved background. SAN's QFT engagement is **adjacent to all three** and proposes a specific differentiator: **time-density modulation as a new variable on top of standard QFT**, with concrete consequences for vacuum energy, holography, and the renormalization-group structure. The SAN-original predictions are testable: (i) the cosmological-constant / vacuum-energy mismatch (`raynote06--md`, line 54; `QGTCDArReduct2--md`, line 1482) should be partially absorbed by the time-density field if the mechanism is real; (ii) holographic properties should be present in QFT proper, not just in the AdS/CFT correspondence (`b0322ySci--md`, lines 279, 2531); (iii) extension of QFT to curved spacetime should incorporate ρ_t(x) modulation (`rawnote05--md`, line 270). Where SAN converges with the mainstream is on the underlying QFT machinery — particles as field excitations (`raynote20--md`, line 104; `b0324y--md`, line 3295: "quantum field theory is based on particles"), the energy-frequency relation E = ℏω (`QGTCDArReduct1--md`, line 470), the path-integral [[gh-formulation|formulation]], the Dirac equation for relativistic spin-½ particles (`QGTCDSuperReduction--md`, line 701). Where it diverges is on whether spacetime structure is a fixed background or an active informational field — SAN says active, mainstream QFT says fixed.

Where SAN makes a sharper testable claim than the mainstream is in the **AdS/CFT extension** (`rawnote05--md`, line 1616): if the boundary CFT is influenced by time-density fluctuations (`rawnote00--md`, line 187), there should be a measurable signature in the boundary correlation functions that standard AdS/CFT does not predict. The [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] legacy file `QGTCDlegacy--md`, line 94, [[gh-qgtcdlegacy|on github]] also engages Kletetschka's three-time geometry as "a three-time geometry can merge quantum field theory with general relativity, potentially eliminating divergences and anomalies," positioning SAN's time-density modification as one of several active proposals for QFT-GR unification.
