# NAPOT Overview

**Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography (NAPOT)** is SAN's proposal that distributed neural
systems repeatedly gather partial signals, transform them through learned and current biological
state, redistribute the result, and update perception and action without a separate inner viewer.
Each declared receive-transform-project-update relation is an expression of SAN's core
[[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-Wave Differential]] (PWD) architecture.

NAPOT is a hypothesis, not an established name for a known anatomical pathway. The strongest version must be tested at declared cellular, circuit, and distributed-network scales.

> [!important] PWD is core to NAPOT, not a later stage
> PWD names the typed departure registered by a declared receiver relative to a declared tonic or
> reference pattern as the NAPOT operation unfolds. The departure may involve excitation,
> inhibition, or their interaction. NAPOT uses this relation throughout its recurrent operation; it
> does not define PWD as a sixth step, final output box, gamma-only signal, or arithmetic
> excitation-minus-inhibition result.

This page is the concise entry. The canonical operational model and falsification tests are on
[[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|the NAPOT technical mechanism page]]; the exact
2012-2024 source sequence is on [[napot-revision-genealogy|NAPOT Revision Genealogy]].

## The operation in one cycle

1. **Receive:** a declared dendritic, cellular, or population receiver encounters incomplete sensory,
   bodily, memory, and recurrent signals relative to its maintained context.
2. **Transform:** receptor state, dendritic integration, excitation, inhibition, neuromodulation,
   recent history, and network dynamics determine the receiver's typed departure from that context.
3. **Project:** axons and population pathways carry the consequences of that PWD-bearing receiver
   state to selected downstream receivers, each with its own reference and transform.
4. **Integrate:** recurrent populations recruit, suppress, combine, and re-express overlapping partial
   information as further receiver-relative PWD relations.
5. **Update:** those relations change further sampling, memory, prediction, communication, bodily
   state, and movement, which changes the reference conditions of the next cycle.

These are analytic views of one recurrent PWD-bearing operation, not five completed stages followed
by PWD. The next cycle begins from the changed state. No single input, neuron, column, or brain area
contains the complete percept.

Many inputs can converge at one local receiver and one receiver can affect many targets. Across the
larger network, the cycle is recurrent and many-to-many rather than one global many-to-one-to-many
bottleneck.

## Three scales that must remain separate

### Micro

- **Candidate SAN motif:** One neuron gathers many synaptic effects, registers typed departures
  relative to its current state, and projects an axonal consequence to declared targets.
- **Scientific boundary:** This is one cellular realization of a PWD-bearing
  receive-transform-project operation, not literal tomography by one neuron.

### Meso

- **Candidate SAN motif:** A local circuit or cortical population combines excitatory, inhibitory,
  timing, and state-dependent departures and redistributes a transformed population relation.
- **Scientific boundary:** Local recurrent computation is established; a universal cortical-column
  algorithm or gamma-only PWD layer is not.

### Macro

- **Candidate SAN motif:** Distributed cortical, thalamic, association, memory, motor, autonomic,
  sensory, and body systems exchange partial state through recurrent PWD-bearing relations.
- **Scientific boundary:** Distributed recurrent processing is established; a complete NAPOT
  reconstruction mechanism, localized final PWD box, or inner viewer remains hypothetical.

## Memory completion inside the cycle

During recall, a local learned state need not contain or transmit a complete scene. A partial cue can change selected receivers; direct projections, recurrent excitation, interneuron-mediated inhibition, and present context can then recruit a larger distributed pattern. [[neural-magnification|Neural Magnification]] names the bidirectional local-to-population and population-to-local scale transition inside that cycle. [[oscillating-group-completion|Oscillating-Group Completion and Re-expression]] describes the 2022 SAN route, while [[telescoping-network-neighborhood-reinstatement|Telescoping Network-Neighborhood Reinstatement]] separates its 2012 structural-transfer ancestor, 2022 local-to-network mechanism, and later geometric analogy.

This is a candidate NAPOT memory operation, not an established universal retrieval circuit. A
particular phase, timing, waveform, excitatory, inhibitory, or interaction feature set earns
incremental empirical support only if it predicts or controls reinstatement beyond cue identity,
firing rate, learned connectivity, inhibition, and ordinary recurrent sequence models. That test
does not determine PWD's architectural rank: failure would narrow the tested operationalization,
not demote PWD to a secondary NAPOT stage.

## Why call it tomography?

In medical tomography, many partial measurements constrain a reconstruction that no single measurement contains. NAPOT uses that as a computational analogy: multiple time-varying neural projections may jointly constrain a working body-world model.

The Encyclopedia calls that candidate distributed, action-ready working state the
[[san-tomogram|SAN Tomogram]]. NAPOT names the proposed operation that repeatedly revises it; the
Tomogram is not a second object located behind the operation.

The stronger SAN interpretation is that the sparse, distributed body-world state is the experienced
rendering itself, not a code presented to a smaller observer. That identity claim remains an open
mechanistic hypothesis and must be tested separately from ordinary neural decoding.

The analogy does **not** imply that the brain performs a CT scan, stores an optical hologram, reconstructs Euclidean 3D space in one location, or gives a complete image to an inner observer. See [[napot-tomography-not-holography|NAPOT: Tomography, Not Holography]].

## Tonic context, phasic difference, and PWD

SAN uses **tonic**, **phasic**, and **PWD** as receiver-relative roles and relations:

- tonic or reference activity supplies a maintained comparison context;
- phasic or differentiated activity supplies a consequential departure from that context, including
  excitatory, inhibitory, missing-expected, delayed, and interaction effects; and
- a [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-Wave Differential]] names the typed relation between that
  declared receiver departure and its declared tonic or reference pattern. It is the departure
  relation itself, not a separate semantic payload carried after an otherwise completed update.

These words do not permanently assign one function to one frequency band. A traveling wave is not
automatically a semantic message, synchrony alone does not establish binding or consciousness, and
the later position of this section does not make PWD secondary to NAPOT. See
[[phase-language-firewall|Phase-Language Firewall]] and
[[tonic-phasic-canvas|Tonic and Phasic Roles: Canvas and Ink]].

## Cellular transmission is multivariate

The historical NAPOT material links potassium conductance, action-potential duration, calcium entry, and vesicle release. The medically bounded sequence is:

1. membrane and dendritic state influence action-potential initiation and waveform;
2. a spike propagates along the axon;
3. terminal waveform and voltage-gated calcium dynamics influence release probability;
4. transmitter, receptor state, postsynaptic voltage, and dendritic location determine the receiver's consequence.

This supports variable, state-dependent transmission. It does not establish a universal vesicle-count alphabet or prove that one spike waveform encodes a complete spatial object. See [[action-potential-magnitude-via-duration|Action-Potential Magnitude via Duration]], [[multi-vesicular-release|Multivesicular Release]], and [[synapse|Synapse]].

## Established components, core SAN architecture, and open tests

Established neuroscience supports nonlinear dendritic integration, recurrent population computation,
structured population dynamics, traveling cortical waves, and state-dependent synaptic transmission.
SAN's core architectural interpretation is that receiver-specific transformation and recurrent
projection are PWD-bearing relations whose distributed consequences help update the system itself.
Particular biological variables and the stronger rendering identity remain open empirical claims.

Component evidence includes:

- [Polsky, Mel, and Schiller (2004)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15156147/) used focal
  stimulation and imaging in identified thin dendrites of rat neocortical pyramidal neurons to show
  branch-local nonlinear integration in that preparation.
- [Mante et al. (2013)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24201281/) recorded macaque prefrontal
  populations during a context-dependent sensory-choice task and modeled selection and integration
  as recurrent population dynamics.
- [Churchland et al. (2012)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22722855/) found structured population
  dynamics, including a brief oscillatory component, in macaque motor cortex during reaching.
- [Davis et al. (2020)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33029013/) found that spontaneous traveling
  waves in extrastriate visual cortex of awake, behaving marmosets predicted target-evoked responses
  and perceptual detection.

These studies constrain candidate cellular and population implementations. None demonstrates NAPOT,
the complete PWD relation, the SAN rendering-identity claim, or consciousness, and none makes PWD a
secondary concept assembled only after those components.

## Source boundary

The September 2012 neuron-as-program proposal, the April 2017 present-versus-desired difference
protocol, and the 2021 partial-view reconstruction sequence are operation-level ancestors. They do
not use the later NAPOT/PWD/NDCA vocabulary. The named receive-transform-project architecture is
publicly integrated in the August 2022 SAN Whitepaper; later 2022 sources explicitly join
tonic-relative differentiation, excitation, inhibition, recurrence, rendering, and action. The
public September 18, 2022 video explains NAPOT Theory, Fifth Revision. Later terminology is not
backdated into the earlier sources, but its source-defined architectural role is not demoted by its
later name. See the genealogy page for immutable sources and date boundaries.
