Within SAN, **NDCA (Non-linear Differential Continuous Approximation)** is a proposed account of how
time-varying neural populations could preserve usable distinctions while sensory and body-state
signals remain nonlinear, noisy, delayed, and continuously changing. It is not an established
neuroscience term or a completed mathematical operator. See [[ndca]] for the canonical boundary.

## Scientific and scale boundary

Established neuroscience supports nonlinear dendritic and circuit dynamics, recurrent population
activity, and the use of state-space trajectories or manifolds to analyze neural data. SAN adds the
hypothesis that related operations participate in a multi-scale continuity process supporting an
action-ready working model. Evidence for the established components does not by itself validate
NDCA, NAPOT, phase-wave differentials, semantic readout, or consciousness.

- **Micro:** synaptic and dendritic integration are possible component mechanisms, not proof that
  each cell performs NDCA.
- **Meso:** population trajectories and attractor-like dynamics are measurable, but a claimed NDCA
  transform must be defined for a named circuit and task.
- **Macro:** continuity of a distributed body-world model is the SAN interpretation to be tested,
  not a property already demonstrated by the word "manifold."

A scientific NDCA model still needs an explicit state variable, update or differential rule,
measurement mapping, cross-scale relationship, alternatives, and failure conditions. Its relation
to QGTCD is a separate speculative extension, not part of the neuroscience evidence.

## Core mechanism

The bounded SAN proposal is a candidate cycle rather than an established cortical algorithm:

1. Receptors, cells, and populations sample partial, time-varying state.
2. Nonlinear cellular and recurrent dynamics transform that state.
3. Population activity follows a trajectory that may preserve task-relevant distinctions over time.
4. Downstream receivers use the changed state to update prediction, selection, or action.

A manifold is ordinarily an analytical description of population activity. The stronger claim that
cortical columns literally compute attractor cross-sections, extract semantics, or render a percept
requires circuit-specific measurements and a defined readout.

## Relation to predictive coding

NDCA can be compared with predictive-processing and dynamical-systems models, but it should not be
identified with them by definition. Coincidence detection is an established family of cellular and
circuit phenomena; prediction error is a model-level quantity. Equating the two is a SAN hypothesis
that needs an explicit mapping and tests against firing-rate, recurrent-state, and control accounts.
