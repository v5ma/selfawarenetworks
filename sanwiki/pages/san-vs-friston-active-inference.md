## Position

Within the SAN system, the proposed distinction is between levels of description: active inference supplies a variational account of inference and action, while SAN proposes that receiver-relative oscillatory dynamics may provide an additional physical implementation. The current evidence does not establish that Bayesian belief updating and oscillatory phase are mutually exclusive, or that SAN has identified the unique substrate. The SAN chapter `07san.md` states this alignment plainly: the [prediction](./prediction.md)-error engine "aligns with frameworks like Karl Friston's [free-energy-principle](./free-energy-principle.md), which says the brain is always trying to reduce the difference between its predictions and sensory inputs (i.e. reducing surprise)" (`07san--md`, line 67, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)) — and reframes Friston's principle as "a brain-specific [formulation](./gh-formulation.md) of a very similar idea" to the broader [self](./gh-a0206z.md)-aware-networks claim about coupled [oscillators](./oscillators.md) "from pendulums to firing neurons" (`07san--md`, line 108, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)). The mechanism that supplies the missing substrate is the pair [ndca-non-linear-differential-continuous-approximation](./ndca-non-linear-differential-continuous-approximation.md) and [napot-theory](./napot-theory.md): chapter `08san.md` argues that "integrating [ndca](./ndca.md)'s explicit link to [predictive coding](./predictive-coding.md) with SIT's alignment with Karl Friston's [Free Energy Principle](./free-energy-principle.md) (FEP) offers a powerful explanatory framework" in which the [perceptual-screen](./perceptual-screen.md) becomes "the brain's momentary best solution in an ongoing process of minimizing [prediction](./prediction.md) error" (`08san--md`, line 157, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/08san.md); `08san--md`, line 218, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/08san.md)). Chapter `09san.md` finishes the move by treating each instantiation of the [perceptual-screen](./perceptual-screen.md) as "a transient, optimal solution in the brain's effort to predict sensory inputs" (`09san--md`, line 158, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/09san.md)), threading [awareness-as-predictive-myth](./awareness-as-predictive-myth.md), [predictive-motor-self-model](./predictive-motor-self-model.md), and [tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame](./tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame.md) into the same [prediction](./prediction.md)-minimization loop that Friston handles abstractly. SAN therefore need not contradict active inference. It proposes a more specific oscillatory implementation involving [gamma-oscillations](./gamma-oscillations.md), [cortical-traveling-waves](./cortical-traveling-waves.md), and [cortical-column](./cortical-column.md) dynamics. Active-inference process theories already propose neural dynamics, so the scientific task is to distinguish SAN's predictions from those alternatives rather than describe the comparator as implementation-free.

## Post-SAN

Compared to Friston's published programme, SAN keeps the [prediction](./prediction.md)-minimization core and adds three commitments that the [free-energy-principle](./free-energy-principle.md) alone does not entail. First, the carrier is oscillatory: the [perceptual-screen](./perceptual-screen.md) is updated via [cortical-traveling-waves](./cortical-traveling-waves.md), [gamma-oscillations](./gamma-oscillations.md), and [oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration](./oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration.md) rather than via abstract belief updates over Markov blankets — a substrate claim that makes [neural-oscillation-synchrony-deviation](./neural-oscillation-synchrony-deviation.md) a directly testable error signal. Second, the discrete-event step is supplied by [criterial-causation](./criterial-causation.md) in the Tse sense: a neuron's threshold crossing is the mechanism by which a [prediction](./prediction.md) "wins" against sensory input, which active inference treats as variational message passing. Third, the global story extends to physics: the SIT papers recast active inference as "mesoscopic limits of the same action" in which "neuronal synchrony corresponds to regions where the gauge phase" stabilizes (`dl-current--SIT77`, line 3566, paper-[micah](./gh-resnote04.md)), connecting the comparison to [qgtcd](./qgtcd.md) and [adelic-foundations-super-information-theory](./adelic-foundations-super-information-theory.md) in a way Friston's framework does not. SAN's catalog of cross-domain neighbors — [jeff-hawkins](./jeff-hawkins.md) on [hierarchical-temporal-memory](./hierarchical-temporal-memory.md), [michael-levin](./michael-levin.md) on bioelectric inference, the broader [brain-as-memory-prediction-machine](./brain-as-memory-prediction-machine.md) tradition — is the same neighborhood Friston's work occupies, and chapter `08san.md` treats them as compatible accounts of the same target (`08san--md`, line 218, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/08san.md)). Where SAN goes further, it goes by specifying the carrier and the threshold rather than by replacing the variational picture.
