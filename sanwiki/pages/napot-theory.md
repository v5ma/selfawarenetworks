# NAPOT Theory: Scope and Reading Route

This page preserves the widely linked **NAPOT Theory** route while preventing several overlapping
pages from acting as competing definitions.

**Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography (NAPOT)** is SAN's proposal that distributed neural
systems repeatedly receive partial signals, transform them through learned and current biological
state, project changed consequences to selected receivers, and thereby update a body-relative
working model used in perception and action. Every declared receive-transform-project-update relation
in that architecture is PWD-bearing: a [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-Wave Differential]] (PWD) is
the typed departure registered by a declared receiver relative to its declared tonic or reference
pattern.

NAPOT is a current SAN term. It is not an obsolete alias, a literal CT scan performed by one neuron,
or an established anatomical name.

> [!important] PWD is core, not one optional NAPOT component
> A PWD may involve excitation, inhibition, a missing expected event, delay, or an interaction among
> these effects. NAPOT uses that receiver-relative relation throughout reception, transformation,
> projection, recurrence, integration, memory, rendering, and action. NAPOT does not contain, define,
> or outrank PWD. Page order, list order, scale, revision number, and empirical feature-set performance
> do not make PWD a later stage or secondary concept.

## Core theory boundary

The neural theory is easiest to navigate as four coupled operational views within a PWD-bearing
architecture:

1. **Receiver-specific PWD registration:** the consequence of an input depends on the receiving cell or population, its learned structure, present state, excitation, inhibition, neuromodulation, and recent history. The typed departure that receiver registers relative to its declared tonic or reference pattern is its PWD relation.
2. **PWD-bearing projection:** neural output changes declared downstream receivers through axons, synapses, and population pathways; it is not a dendrite broadcasting as a second axon or a context-free PWD packet moving unchanged between receivers.
3. **Distributed PWD reconstruction:** overlapping recurrent receiver-relative updates jointly maintain a usable body-world state without one cell, column, hub, or hidden viewer containing the whole percept.
4. **Observer-action through PWD consequences:** the network performs observation by changing its own subsequent sampling, memory, selection, report, and movement.

These are not four completed stages followed by PWD. Each downstream receiver establishes a new
receiver-relative PWD relation. Tonic context provides a declared comparison condition; neural
rendering, memory re-expression, and embodied action name uses or consequences of the recurrent
PWD-bearing architecture when their receiver, scale, variable, route, and predicted consequence are
declared. PWD is not permanently assigned to one frequency band.

## Historical roots without backdating

- The September 2012 neuron-as-program proposal is an ancestor involving morphology-conditioned recognition and structured output. It does not use the later NAPOT name.
- Neural Lace Podcast 1 in April 2017 proposes transmitting a present-versus-desired neural-pattern difference. It is a difference-and-write-back ancestor that does not use the later PWD or NAPOT names.
- The 2021-2022 `b0131y` sequence develops partial-view, receiver-specific recurrent reconstruction.
- The August 2022 SAN Whitepaper publicly integrates the named receive-transform-project architecture.
- On August 23, 2022, [[gh-a0306z|a0306z]] names the **Phase Wave Differential** while developing a multidimensional receiver-relative update account. The later name is not backdated into earlier sources.
- The numbered NAPOT revisions then add synaptic resolution, inhibition and absence, dynamic subnetworks, multiscale reconfiguration, action-side consequences, and later branches.

See the [[napot-revision-genealogy|governed genealogy]] for exact event dates, first public fixation,
immutable source routes, speaker boundaries, and what each source does not establish.

## How to use the revision record

The revision record is a provenance map, not a sequence in which each later number erases the
earlier mechanism or determines architectural rank. The first numbered revision records an early
named receive-transform-project formulation; PWD is core throughout the current synthesis and its
later explicit naming does not place it after that operation. Revisions 2-4 increase
cellular, synaptic, inhibitory, and dynamic-routing resolution. Revisions 5-6 connect the mechanism
to multiscale recurrence, memory, reference frames, and action. Revision 7 is a separate molecular
and quantum-biological branch. Revisions 8-10 extend the proposal toward nested integration,
three-dimensional rendering, neural manifolds, NDCA, choice, and behavior.

PWD is core throughout the current synthesis. Its explicit August 2022 naming and its later position
in some source or reader sequences do not make it an operation added after receive-transform-project,
a final output, or a lower-ranked concept.

The [[napot-revision-genealogy|revision-to-current-mechanism map]] routes every revision record to the
Encyclopedia page that now owns its operation and states the relevant evidence boundary.

## Extensions are not the core neural mechanism

Historical SAN material extends the receive-transform-project language into [[cellular-oscillating-tomography|COT]],
[[bot-biological-oscillatory-tomography|BOT]], artificial neurology, NDCA, and a later molecular or
physics branch toward QGTCD. Those routes remain visible because they are part of SAN's development.
They do not make spacetime physics, quantum biology, or one scale-invariant biological operator part
of the evidence required to understand the core neural NAPOT proposal.

## Evidence boundary

This route page does not treat evidence for dendritic integration, axonal projection, recurrent
population dynamics, traveling waves, or plasticity as independent validation of complete NAPOT,
complete PWD, SAN's rendering-identity hypothesis, or consciousness. The
[[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|canonical technical mechanism]] owns the
preparation-specific scientific anchors, operational equations, and decisive model-comparison tests.
That empirical boundary classifies support; it does not make PWD secondary.
