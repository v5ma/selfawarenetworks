## Main result

1. `a0054z.md` is the tranche's strongest Neural Rendering source. It describes a current-
   perspective model assembled through successive receiving, transforming, and projecting neural
   arrays. The durable reading is sparse, receiver-relative reconstruction by the whole system,
   with no little viewer and no literal screen inside the brain.
2. `a0045z.md` adds a multimodal transformation ladder across electrical, chemical, mechanical,
   thermal, and magnetic correlates. Established coupling among these variables supports a
   multichannel measurement problem; it does not make every correlate an independently decoded
   neural message.
3. `a0059z.md` asks whether ordinary cells perform pattern-sensitive coordination. Neutrophil
   chemotaxis supplies a concrete established example of intercellular signal relay, amplification,
   and collective migration. COT's extension from stateful coordination to learning, tomography,
   or consciousness remains a separate test.
4. `a0089z.md` is an important Action Potential Magnitude Effect (APME) and PWD ancestor. Its
   strongest modern form is that presynaptic waveform and duration can change calcium-current
   timing and transmitter release. A universal inverse frequency-duration law, direct EEG
   summation rule, or scalar neural "magnitude" is not established.
5. `a0069z.md` distinguishes external array tomography from SAN's proposal that neural arrays
   themselves receive, transform, and re-express structured activity. It also preserves a broad
   BCI instrument program whose modalities must retain separate forward models and uncertainties.
6. `a0092z.md` supplies a September 2013 Neo Mind Cycle and Choice ancestor joining criteria,
   noise, synchronization, neurofeedback, and plasticity. It is program and idea genealogy, not a
   clinical-efficacy result.
7. `a0066z-a0150z.pdf` is a composite custody artifact containing 213 pages and 85 apparent source

## Sparse current-perspective rendering

`a0054z.md` asks how a brain can construct a usable three-dimensional perspective without storing
or displaying every possible view at once. Its constructive chain is:

```text
current sensory and recurrent input
-> receiver-specific dendritic and cellular transformation
-> sparse population update
-> successive array-relative re-expression
-> recurrent binding with body, memory, expectation, and action
-> current usable perspective
```

The game-engine and camera language is an engineering analogy for conditional rendering: update
what the organism can presently sense, predict, or act upon. Neurons are not literal pixels,
screens, cameras, or speakers. Those terms refer to capture-like and projection-like functional
roles inside a distributed physical system. The observer operation is the system's state change
and later use of the result, not a hidden agent watching the render.

The source also proposes that groups alternate between listening and firing. The defensible form
is state- and phase-dependent receiving, integration, and output across overlapping populations;
whole cortical columns do not need to switch as synchronized binary blocks. Likewise, a one-
millisecond "pixel" and soliton language remain hypotheses until tied to measured variables.

## Multimodal cellular transformation

`a0045z.md` treats the neuron as a hierarchy of transformations rather than a one-frequency
cartoon. Membrane voltage, ionic current, calcium, transmitter release, mechanical change,
metabolic heat, and local electromagnetic fields can covary because they arise from coupled
cellular processes. This motivates a multichannel experiment:

```text
controlled input
-> simultaneous electrical, ionic, chemical, mechanical, thermal, and field measurements
-> estimate which variables predict downstream effect beyond the electrical signal alone
-> perturb the candidate additional channel
-> test whether downstream information or behavior changes
```

Covariation does not by itself establish an independent communication channel. Magnetic and
thermal signals may be consequences, constraints, or measurement correlates rather than separately
decoded messages. That distinction is central to the governed multimodal-neuron concept.

## Ordinary cells, relay, and COT

`a0059z.md` asks whether non-neural cells can detect structured conditions and recruit other cells
into a coordinated response. Primary neutrophil studies provide a concrete mechanism: stimulated
cells release leukotriene B4, which amplifies polarization and relays chemotactic information over
distance; in tissue, that relay helps organize swarming around damage. Later work shows pulsatile,
self-limiting relay waves rather than unlimited diffusion.

This supports the following bounded COT ladder:

```text
receptor state + local signal + cell history
-> intracellular transformation
-> secreted relay and neighbor recruitment
-> population-scale coordinated behavior
```

It does not show that neutrophils possess neural representations or conscious experience. SAN's
further questions concern whether some non-neural systems learn persistent transformations,
reconstruct distributed states, or satisfy operational consciousness criteria.

## Action-potential waveform and effective magnitude

`a0089z.md` correctly points toward more than spike count. Presynaptic action-potential waveform
can change the timing and magnitude of calcium current and transmitter release. Potassium and
sodium channel dynamics help shape that waveform, while release also depends on terminal type,
calcium coupling, vesicle state, recent activity, temperature, and neuromodulation.

For SAN, "magnitude" should therefore be a declared measurement, not an undefined sum of height
and duration. Candidate quantities include waveform area, delivered charge, calcium-current
integral, released-vesicle distribution, postsynaptic current, or downstream state change. Which
quantity matters is an empirical question. The note's broader inverse frequency-duration and EEG
amplitude claims remain hypotheses that require within-system measurement rather than analogy
across scales.

## External tomography and neural tomography are different claims

`a0069z.md` uses established array tomography as an external microscopy method, then asks whether
the brain performs a functionally analogous many-to-one and one-to-many transformation on its own
activity. External array tomography reconstructs tissue structure and connectivity from prepared
serial sections. NAPOT is a proposed biological computation. Shared use of the word "tomography"
does not make the two mechanisms identical.

The source's imaging and stimulation list belongs in the BCI Instrument Atlas. EEG, MEG, EIT,
fNIRS, HD-DOT, fMRI, tractography, microscopy, ultrasound, and stimulation each measure or alter
different variables at different scales. A valid multimodal reconstruction must preserve those
forward models, latencies, uncertainties, and safety boundaries.

## Choice and Neo Mind Cycle before the mature terminology

`a0092z.md`, internally dated September 11, 2013, joins criteria, noisy neural transitions,
oscillator synchronization, neurofeedback, and plasticity inside the Neo Mind Cycle program. It is
an early bridge toward the later Choice model:

```text
current state + learned criteria + variable input
-> context-sensitive neural transition
-> reinforced or inhibited route
-> action, no action, or revised state
-> feedback and later plasticity
```

The metronome analogy supports phase coordination as an intuition, not proof that all neural
choice is global synchronization. The multi-speaker source preserves Matthew Bendyna's
contributions separately, and the Neo Mind Cycle description records an intended research and
training program rather than demonstrated treatment efficacy.
