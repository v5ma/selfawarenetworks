# Fermionic Condensate

The mainstream physics literature on fermionic condensates is settled: BCS theory for Cooper-pair superconductivity, the BCS-BEC crossover, atomic-Fermi-gas experiments (Jin, Ketterle, Zwierlein), and the relation to spin-imbalanced systems.
