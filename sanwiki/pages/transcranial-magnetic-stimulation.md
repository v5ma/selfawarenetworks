# Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation

**Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS)** is the noninvasive technique for inducing focal cortical activity by external magnetic fields, and within [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] it is the canonical perturbation tool — alongside [[eeg|EEG]], [[fmri|fMRI]], and [[optogenetics]] — for testing SAN's [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave]] / oscillatory hypotheses about [[consciousness]] and [[qualia]].
