# SAN Scale Ladder and Claim Boundaries

This page is a reader-facing bridge between the cellular mechanism pages, the NAPOT and OCA theory
pages, medically reviewed images, and comparison evidence. It separates three dimensions that must
not be collapsed into one ladder: the biological denominator being measured, the empirical strength
of a claim, and the historical custody of the source wording. None of those dimensions is a ranking
of which SAN concept matters most.

SAN often uses the same receive-transform-project-update motif at multiple biological levels. That
is useful as a unifying story, but it also creates a risk: a diagram of one neuron can accidentally
look like a claim about a whole visual system, and a whole-network comparison can accidentally look
like a claim about one literal synapse. The scale label has to travel with the claim.

> [!important] PWD is core SAN architecture, not a secondary rung
> A [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] (PWD) is a typed, receiver-relative
> departure from a declared tonic or reference pattern. Depending on the declared receiver and
> measurement, the departure may be expressed through excitation, inhibition, or their interaction.
> PWD is not excitation minus inhibition, not a synonym for gamma, and not a later stage that begins
> only after cellular or circuit processing. The Gamma Consideration Sandwich (GCS) is one bounded
> cortical model that uses PWD relations; it does not contain, define, or outrank PWD.

## Three biological scales and one evidence label

| Scale label | What is being described | Established background | PWD / SAN interpretation | Common failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | One neuron, dendritic branch, spine, bouton, synapse, or local biochemical compartment | Dendrites receive synaptic input; active dendrites support nonlinear transformation; axons transmit spikes and influence downstream release | A declared cell or compartment can be treated as a receiver whose current state departs from a stated tonic reference and changes projected output | Treating one generic neuron as if it directly receives all feature streams, or treating one voltage difference as the whole PWD architecture |
| Meso | Named local circuit, cortical column, laminar motif, hippocampal microcircuit, or thalamocortical loop | Recurrent excitation and inhibition, laminar pathways, dendritic integration, and oscillatory coordination shape population computation | Named populations may transform and redistribute typed receiver-relative departures through recurrent and phase-sensitive relations | Drawing one canonical laminar circuit as universal across cortex and species, or reducing PWD to one frequency band |
| Macro | Distributed cortical, thalamic, cerebellar, basal-ganglia, sensory, motor, body-state, and association networks | Perception and action use distributed, recurrent, multisensory, and sensorimotor loops | NAPOT organizes repeated receive-transform-project-update operations; PWD remains the proposed core relation by which declared receivers register differentiated updates | Detaching the observer from the distributed process, assigning the complete percept to one location, or presenting PWD as a late add-on |
| Comparison / genealogy | A dated conceptual atom across Micah sources and other works; this is an evidence label, not a biological scale | Historical source comparison can identify shared atoms, chronology, named priors, and residual differences | Comparison can preserve when tonic-relative differentiation and related functions entered SAN without backdating the mature PWD term | Confusing priority/equivalence evidence with empirical validation, or compressing an early functional ancestor into the complete later mechanism |

## Scale and evidence status do not determine architectural rank

Micro, meso, and macro identify the denominator of a biological claim. Comparison/genealogy
identifies a different kind of evidence operation. Moving down this page does not move toward a more
important SAN concept, and moving from component physiology to a discriminating PWD experiment does
not make PWD newly architectural. PWD is already core to the source-defined SAN synthesis; the open
empirical question is whether a preregistered PWD feature set adds reproducible explanatory or
predictive value beyond matched rate, power, connectivity, movement, arousal, and recurrent-state
alternatives.

## Micro NAPOT: candidate cellular motif

At micro scale, the safer wording is:

> A single neuron can illustrate a candidate receive-transform-project motif: many synaptic inputs
> arrive across dendritic branches, branch-local and somatic mechanisms transform their effects, and
> the axon sends an update outward.

This is not literal tomography by one neuron. A micro-scale image should show receptors, spines,
dendritic branches, local calcium or voltage compartments, soma/AIS integration, and axonal output.
It should not imply that color, sound, object identity, striatum, prefrontal cortex, and visual cortex
all directly converge on one generic neuron.

Active dendritic events can contribute to perception in a bounded mouse somatosensory task
([Takahashi et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28008068/)), and local dendritic spikes can
sharpen orientation selectivity in mouse visual cortex
([Smith et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24162850/)). These studies support active,
location-dependent dendritic transformation. They do not independently establish PWD, cellular
tomography, or a complete SAN observer.

## Meso NAPOT: local circuit or column-scale motif

At meso scale, the safer wording is:

> A local circuit or cortical column can gather many neuron-level updates, use recurrent excitation,
> inhibition, dendritic integration, and thalamocortical or corticocortical loops to form a population
> state, then redistribute that state through downstream projections.

This is the right scale for canonical laminar diagrams, but they should be explicitly labeled
canonical, illustrative, or preparation-bounded. A cortical-column plate should not imply that every
brain area has the same circuit, the same layers, the same cell mix, or the same computational role.

Quantitative macaque visual-cortex tracing supports preparation-bounded feedforward and feedback
laminar counterstreams ([Markov et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23983048/)). Bounded
preparations also show excitatory propagation, inhibition-dependent changes in oscillatory spatial
dynamics, and suppressive population waves
([Huang et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15525774/);
[Xiao et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22805065/);
[Chemla et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30886010/)). These findings motivate typed
meso-scale measurements; a suppressive population wave is not automatically a GABAergic inhibitory
wave, and none of these studies establishes the complete PWD operator.

## Macro NAPOT: distributed perceptual and action update

At macro scale, the safer wording is:

> Distributed sensory, motor, memory, emotional, language, and association systems contribute partial
> state. Recurrent loops combine those partial states into an integrated working model, then the
> update is redistributed so the next perceptual and action cycle begins from a changed state.

This is the scale where NAPOT's tomography analogy is strongest as a system-level description.
The CT-scan analogy is still conditional: it becomes literal only if identifiable partial projections,
reconstruction operators, and causal intervention evidence are shown. Until then, it is a proposed
mechanism and a disciplined analogy, not established neuroscience.

Recurrent processing, selective communication dimensions, context-dependent causal channels, and a
defined corollary-discharge pathway provide bounded component support
([Tang et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30104363/);
[Semedo et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30770252/);
[Javadzadeh and Hofer, 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35690063/);
[Sommer and Wurtz, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17093408/)). They do not independently
establish NAPOT tomography, the complete PWD operator, observer identity, or phenomenality.

## Comparison scale: atom equivalence is not mechanism validation

Comparison pages can show that two works share a bounded functional atom, a causal sequence, or a
later movement toward Micah's axis. That matters for provenance and priority. It does not prove that
the brain implements SAN/NAPOT.

Use this boundary:

- A comparison match can support "Micah had this conceptual atom earlier."
- A comparison match can support "another work later used a similar causal structure."
- A comparison match cannot support "SAN is medically established."
- A comparison match cannot support "the rival had intent, access, or misconduct" unless separate
  evidence establishes those claims.

## Source-faithful anchor

The early sources contain functional ancestors that must be preserved in their own wording. They
must not be rewritten as if the mature acronym and mathematical formulation were already present.
The 2011-2017 sequence establishes learning feedback, a neural-process observer, transformed output,
distributed spatial computation, and recursive sensory-action relations. The public 2022 sequence
then joins cellular reception and transformed projection with oscillatory coordination, tonic
context, differentiated content, recurrent array-to-array rendering, prediction, and motor
consequence. In particular, [[gh-a0310z|a0310z]] and [[gh-a0319z|a0319z]] are the mature dated anchors
for tonic-relative differentiated content and distributed rendering. Later PWD formalization may
clarify those claims but cannot be backdated verbatim into them.

The [SAN Claim Genealogy, 2011-2022](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/san/sources/san-claim-genealogy-2011-2022.md)
controls wording and chronology. It establishes what Micah argued and when; it does not by itself
validate the biology. A revision is acceptable only after the original passage and its surrounding
argument have passed source-genealogy and anti-strawman review, followed separately by the
scientific-literature check.

## Claim-boundary language to reuse

Use these phrases when writing or correcting SAN pages:

| Risky wording | Safer wording |
|---|---|
| "The neuron performs tomography." | "The neuron illustrates a candidate receive-transform-project motif; tomography is a network-level or system-level hypothesis unless a cellular reconstruction operator is specified." |
| "The cortical column does NAPOT." | "A cortical column or local circuit is a candidate meso-scale NAPOT unit, depending on the declared circuit, input/output pathways, and intervention test." |
| "The brain renders a screen." | "The sparse distributed field-cell system maintains, registers, and uses a working model; screen language names that observer operation rather than a separate display." |
| "Gamma is the signal." | "Gamma-band activity can be a measured circuit operation or routing component; SAN's phase-wave differential is a receiver-relative update hypothesis, not a synonym for gamma." |
| "PWD comes after the lower-level mechanism." | "PWD is core SAN architecture across declared micro-, meso-, and macro-scale receiver relations; the scale label specifies the biological denominator, not PWD's rank." |
| "PWD is excitation minus inhibition." | "Excitation, inhibition, and their interaction can each produce typed departures from a declared tonic pattern; PWD names the receiver-relative departure, not one arithmetic subtraction rule." |
| "PWD is Level 3, so it is secondary." | "A distinct incremental PWD contribution is an open empirical claim; that test status is separate from PWD's core role in the SAN architecture." |
| "GCS contains PWD." | "GCS is one bounded cortical model that uses PWD relations for sensory, top-down, consideration/proprioceptive, action, and returned-body coordination; it does not define or outrank PWD." |
| "Comparison evidence validates NAPOT." | "Comparison evidence supports provenance or conceptual convergence; empirical validation requires separate neuroscience experiments." |

## How this page should guide future diagrams

Every SAN image should carry a scale label:

- Micro: spine, dendrite, soma, AIS, axon, bouton, receptor, vesicle, local calcium/voltage.
- Meso: named local circuit, laminae if relevant, cell classes, recurrent loops, inhibitory timing.
- Macro: named brain systems, pathways, loops, body/sensory/action context, distributed latent state.
- Comparison: two works, dated anchors, shared atom, residuals, and explicit non-claims.

PWD should remain visible as the typed tonic-relative relation at every biological scale where it is
claimed. It should not be drawn as a single color, a gamma-only band, an excitation-only pulse, or a
late box downstream of otherwise completed SAN processing.

Every image containing biology must pass SAN medical-grade image certification before acceptance.
That review must verify the named structures, cellular morphology, pathway direction, scale,
species/preparation boundary, labels, and caption claims against appropriate anatomical and
physiological sources. Artistic color and composition may be adapted from the supplied infographic
set, but style cannot substitute for biological accuracy. Near-duplicate brain-and-robot concepts
should share one factual diagram contract rather than silently multiplying anatomical claims.

The same visual motif can recur across scales only when the caption says what changed. The central
rule is: **same motif, different biological denominator; core PWD relation, explicitly declared
receiver and reference**.

## Book and Atlas evidence-donor boundary

SAN Book 2 and its Evidence, Bridge, and Visual Atlases are read-only evidence donors for this Wiki.
They can supply candidate claims, chronology, citations, bridge requirements, and visual needs, but
they are not blanket authority and are not edited through this page. A donated item enters canonical
Wiki prose only after source-context, anti-strawman, scientific-literature, narrative, and release
checks. The reader should never have to infer whether a statement concerns cellular physiology,
local-circuit computation, distributed rendering, or historical comparison evidence.
