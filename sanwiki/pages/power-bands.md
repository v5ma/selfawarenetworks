## Post-SAN

Mainstream [[neuroscience|neuroscience]] partitions [[eeg]] into delta, theta, alpha, beta, gamma, and high-gamma "bands" as a measurement convention — the partitioning sits in textbooks like Buzsáki's *Rhythms of the Brain* (2006), which `GPT2023X--md`, line 12145 and `NotebookML_6_Collection--md`, line 130 cite directly. Power Bands as defined in [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks differs from that mainstream usage in three concrete, falsifiable ways. First, SAN claims the band centers are not arbitrary cuts but are spaced by an approximately constant ratio (≈2.17 in `b0103ywhisper--md`, line 195) generated by [[cortical-columns|cortical column]] geometry — a structural prediction the textbook framing does not make and which is tractable against multi-species [[eeg]] / LFP datasets. Second, SAN claims the bands behave as [[attractor|attractors]] (`a0027z--md`, line 9; `a0234z--md`, line 23) that physically converge information and concentrate electrons, casting them as something closer to [[dissipative-system|dissipative]] limit cycles than as Fourier bins; this aligns with the cross-frequency-coupling and theta-gamma nesting findings (Penttonen, cited in `a0244z--md`, line 24 and `b0308y--md`, line 476) but goes further by predicting band stability under perturbation. Third, SAN reads the bands as a [[oscillatory-tomography|tomographic projection set]] for [[cot-cellular-oscillating-tomography|Cellular Oscillating Tomography]] (`a0199z--md`, line 10; `vecA--md`, line 1520) — i.e. the bands are the angular sweep from which the brain reconstructs [[3d-semantic-segmentation|volumetric semantic content]], which is a stronger and more specific claim than the published binding-by-synchrony literature.

Where SAN converges with mainstream work is on the spectrolaminar motif — `GPT2024Jan1toJan22--md`, line 1086 explicitly notes the alignment between the "different power bands in different cortical layers" hypothesis and the published cross-species spectrolaminar findings — and on the role of [[inhibitory-interneuron-system|inhibitory interneuron]] networks in maintaining the band structure. Where it diverges is in treating the band partitioning as a derived consequence of [[oscillator|oscillator]] physics rather than as an empirical observation; that is a testable difference, not a notational one.
