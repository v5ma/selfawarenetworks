# Temporal Interleaving and Conscious Access

Self Aware Networks proposed that neural representations could be interlaced rapidly enough
to appear simultaneous, that observation involves recurrent detection and rendering, and that
conscious content depends on selective dominance within distributed activity. These arguments
are present in Micah Blumberg's source versions dated 2022, with an earlier recording lead
catalogued in September 2021.

A new primate study offers a concrete experimental parallel. The connection is the temporal
organization of representation and conscious access, beyond the general observation that
brains oscillate. The dated sources below make that comparison inspectable.

## What the 2026 study reports

Hesse and colleagues' preprint, [Rapid alternation of conscious and suppressed stimulus
representations in the primate brain](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.15.759299v1),
was posted on October 16, 2026. Its abstract reports that inferotemporal and prefrontal
populations alternate about 15 times per second between representations of consciously
perceived and suppressed faces during binocular flash suppression, while stimulus and percept
remain stable. The corresponding face can be reconstructed from each type of micro-epoch,
and feature tuning is stronger during conscious epochs.

This is a preprint. The comparison here follows the reported abstract-level result; it does
not claim an independent replication or a full audit of the experimental methods.

## June 8, 2022: interleaving beneath apparent simultaneity

In the rough Pixel transcript **Oscillation is just for the canvas of consciousness**,
preserved in a June 8, 2022 commit, Micah proposes:

> "You could interlace them so that they don't appear at the same time ... maybe as if they are overlayed instead of interlaced"

[Original transcript, 04:04, line 21](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/09b8306fbb54536e20ba1071e91dff0cb8a4770f/b0192y%20ctpr.txt#L21).

The surrounding argument concerns sound, visual patterns, and representations at different
scales. Their components could occur in rapid succession while the resulting experience
seems overlaid. The qualification "maybe" belongs to the original proposal. This passage
concerns multisensory interleaving; it does not describe the later two-face experiment.

The [[recorder-transcript-human-source-atom-map-t14|reviewed recording catalogue]] dates the
underlying talk, `b0192y`, to **September 17, 2021**. That is a recording-catalogue date;
June 8, 2022 is the date attached to the Git version that preserves the quoted text. The
August 2022 Markdown filename reflects a rename. The quotation is from the preserved rough
transcript, and the audio has not been newly checked for this comparison.

## November 28, 2022: a recurrent observer

The November 28, 2022 version of Micah's working synthesis describes:

> "an observer is a strange loop of detection frames interlaced with rendered frames"

[Original source, line 13](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/4e0cf1ee0975c01dc57182368497d3f0cd864030/0abstractbump.md#L13).

In its full context, rendering changes the network, detection registers the changed state,
and that change participates in the next update. The coordinated network performs the
observer operation. [[detection-rendering-interlace|Detection-rendering interlace]] therefore
adds a recurrent functional relationship to the temporal proposal. Detection and rendering
are not automatically the same pair of states as suppressed and conscious face content.

## November 28, 2022: selective conscious dominance

In the original NAPOT 6 draft, Micah writes:

> "those things that are perceived consciously are the dominant phase wave differential rhythms."

[Original NAPOT 6 source, line 99](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/4e0cf1ee0975c01dc57182368497d3f0cd864030/a0325zNAPOT6.md#L99).

The source also describes the maintained context as:

> "the synchronous tonic low information brainwave oscillations"

[Original NAPOT 6 source, line 103](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/4e0cf1ee0975c01dc57182368497d3f0cd864030/a0325zNAPOT6.md#L103).

Read across the surrounding argument, dominance includes relative persistence, distributed
recurrent pathways, and spatial-temporal organization. It is not simply a label for the
largest instantaneous amplitude in every neuron or region. This connects
[[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|dominant phase-wave differential rhythms]] with
[[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic context and changing content]], while preserving the distinction
between an active representation and its participation in conscious experience.

## The connection, stated precisely

| Earlier SAN proposal | Relationship to the new finding |
|---|---|
| Rapidly interlaced representations can appear simultaneous. | A conceptual antecedent for temporal separation beneath a stable percept. The original example involves different sensory contents. |
| Observation involves recurrent detection and rendering. | A mechanism proposal for how a changing distributed representation participates in an observer's continuing state. The experimental states have different definitions. |
| Conscious content involves selective dominance and persistence within a tonic context. | A related account of why representation and conscious access must be distinguished. It is not a direct measurement of the reported face-code purity or tuning gain. |

The specific empirical addition is the reported alternation between reconstructible conscious
and suppressed face codes in IT/PFC, with a measured switching rate and tuning difference.
The selected SAN passages establish an earlier conceptual architecture; they do not specify
that complete experimental result. A local switching rate also does not, by itself,
establish one universal frame rate of consciousness.

Git author and committer dates, recording-catalogue dates, current public source availability,
and independently archived historical publication dates are distinct forms of evidence.
