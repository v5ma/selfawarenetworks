Gravity as information processing is the theoretical claim, central to [[super-information-theory]], that gravitational attraction is not a fundamental force but an emergent phenomenon arising from coherence-regulated time-density gradients in an underlying information field.

The core move: mass warps the local density of time (scalar field ρt), and objects follow gradients toward higher time density — what we experience as gravitational attraction. This reframes gravity as a consequence of computational structure rather than a geometric property of spacetime in the standard Einsteinian sense.

## Key constructs

- **Time-density scalar field ρt(x)**: local density of time as a measurable physical quantity; gradient determines effective gravitational attraction.
- **Coherence field ψ(x)**: oscillatory coordination field that regulates information propagation and couples to ρt.
- Both primitives are unified under [[super-information-theory-v76|Super Information Theory]].
