**Neural Ensemble Phase Reset Communication** is SAN's proposed ensemble-scale analogue of
[[dendritic-coincidence-detection|dendritic coincidence detection]]. It interprets a sufficiently
large change in a population's oscillatory phase distribution as an information-bearing event that
can alter the response of downstream populations.

![Neural ensemble phase-reset communication medical boundary plate](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/neural-ensemble-phase-reset-communication/neural-ensemble-phase-reset-communication-medically-bounded-20260714-v1.png)

The plate separates measured physiology from the SAN interpretation. Event-related synaptic input
can transiently reorganize the phase of ongoing population activity, and the resulting excitability
window can enhance or suppress a later response in specific circuits. A local field potential,
EEG/MEG phase estimate, or increase in inter-trial phase concentration does **not** mean that every
neuron simultaneously adopts one phase; an additive evoked response can also produce a
phase-consistent average. SAN adds the proposed threshold/message interpretation and the 4D
tempo-spatial phase graph. Local ephaptic effects have experimental support under some conditions,
but they do not establish long-range field-mediated messaging between arbitrary ensembles.

**Medical accuracy boundary:** ordinary axonal and synaptic pathways remain the principal
communication route shown here. The all-neuron reset, ensemble-awareness, long-range field-message,
and self-sustaining inner-voice soliton claims are SAN hypotheses rather than established
physiology. See [Lakatos et al. (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2006.12.011),
[Makeig et al. (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1066168), and
[Anastassiou et al. (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2727).

In the SAN mechanism, phasic firing or inhibited firing in a subgroup is proposed to perturb the
population oscillator. If that perturbation crosses a threshold, the population phase distribution
is proposed to reorganize; the event is then treated as a message that can be detected through
ordinary projections by a downstream population. This is a population-level model, not a claim
that all constituent neurons reset simultaneously.

## Awareness at ensemble scale

SAN extends [[dendritic-coincidence-detection]] upward in scale: just as a [[dendrite]] can detect
coincident inputs and change its output, the theory proposes that an ensemble detects a sufficiently
large population perturbation and changes its phase distribution. Applying that operation to
"ensembles of ensembles" is a theoretical scale-up, not an established universal cortical rule.

This is the SAN model of **[[gh-a0206z|self]]-awareness at scale**. The stronger claim that every
oscillatory level is sensitive to every other level's field remains proposed and is not implied by
the local phase-reset or ephaptic-coupling evidence.

## 4D tempo-spatial phase graph

The cumulative output of distributed ensemble-state changes is what [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] calls
the "4D tempo-spatial phase graph" of consciousness — a proposed four-dimensional description of
population phases across space and time that could in principle be represented in a physics
simulation. The proposed pattern is simultaneously:
- **Information-producing**: ensemble resets generate new phase configurations
- **Information-driven**: incoming phase configurations trigger the next round of resets

This [[gh-a0206z|self]]-referential loop is the SAN account of [[consciousness-persistent-rendering]] and the basis for arguing that consciousness could theoretically run inside a game engine (Unity, WebXR) if the physics simulation is sufficiently faithful to neural dynamics.

## Inner voice as soliton

A separate speculative SAN extension models the inner voice as a **moving soliton wave** — a
localized wave packet traveling through neural tissue. This has not been established as the
biological mechanism of inner speech and is not part of the medically supported phase-reset core.
Within SAN it connects to [[concurrent-soliton-consciousness-channels]] and
[[tonic-phasic-neural-oscillation]].

## SAN grounding

Phase-reset communication is a proposed mechanism in [[napot-theory]]: SAN uses it to describe how
phasic changes perturb a tonic population state and influence downstream populations. Evidence for
circuit-specific phase resetting and phase-dependent response gain does not by itself establish
SAN's every-scale, synapse-to-whole-brain interpretation. See also
[[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration]] and [[oscillatory-neural-coupling]].
