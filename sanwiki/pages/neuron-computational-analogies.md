The neuron has been compared at different times to a **telegraph** (discrete signal relay), a **clock** (oscillatory timekeeper), and a **Geiger counter** (threshold detector of random arrivals). These analogies appear in Strogatz's *Sync* and Lieff's *The Secret Language of Cells*.

Each analogy captures one aspect of neural function while obscuring others. SAN theory requires all three simultaneously — neurons relay signals, entrain to oscillatory rhythms, and detect stochastic coincidences — and goes further by treating them as [[criterial-causation]] agents that rewrite their own response criteria.
