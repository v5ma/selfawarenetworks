# Nonlinear Differential Continuous Approximations

**Nonlinear Differential Continuous Approximations** is a plural SAN phrasing, also glossed in the
source corpus as "Equilibriations" (equilibrium plus calibrations). It proposes that related
receive-transform-update operations recur across biological scales. The recurrence is a research
hypothesis, not proof that every cell computes an expectation error or that one identical algorithm
runs throughout biological tissue.

## Scientific and scale boundary

Cells do maintain regulated states, integrate nonlinear inputs, and communicate consequential
changes. SAN interprets those facts through prediction, phase-wave, entification, and agentic
viewpoint language. Those interpretations require separate support: an ordinary homeostatic or
synaptic change is not automatically an NDCA, a phase-wave differential, a viewpoint, or an act of
conscious rendering.

Micro, meso, and macro uses must define different state variables and receivers. A scientific model
must specify the approximated quantity, expected state, residual, dissipation term, update rule,
units, cross-scale composition, and an experiment that could reject the model.

The sections below preserve source genealogy and historical SAN formulations rather than certifying
the mechanism.

## Origin

NDCA enters the SAN repository as [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s named operation for the cross-scale processing engine that runs *after* [[napot-overview|NAPOT]]'s tomographic projection has produced array-level signals: "passed around bound together with [[neural-array-projection|neural array projection]] oscillation tomography and processed with nonlinear differential continuous approximations up neurons and cortical hierarchies, resulting in new phase wave differential patterns" (`b0054y--md`, line 67, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0054y.md)) — fixing NDCA's location *after* NAPOT in the processing chain and giving the operation explicit responsibility for generating new [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differential]] patterns as the signal climbs the cortical hierarchy. Note `vecC` then formalises NDCA as the cell-level unit of computation: "It performs NDCA Nonlinear Differential Continuous Approximations as a fractal Unit of Computation at multiple scales of biology" (`vecC--md`, line 443, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecC.md)) — making the operation *fractal* in the [[fractal-pattern|fractal-pattern]] / [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|fractal-scale-hierarchy]] sense and tying the same mechanism to neurons, glia, and other cell populations under the [[cellular-communication-graph-neural-network|cellular-communication]] framing. The Equilibriations gloss is given in `resnote05` and reappears in `vecB`: "Nonlinear Differential Continuous Approximations (or Equilibriations: Equilibrium + Calibrations) connect, bind, and entify into larger entities" (`resnote05--md`, line 27, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/resnote05.md); same construction at `vecB--md`, line 1526, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecB.md)) — naming the dual reading: NDCA is what *equilibrates* by *calibrating* against [[expectation]], and the residual structure is what binds, entifies, and propagates upward.

## Position

Inside the SAN architecture NDCA is the engine that converts NAPOT's array-level projections into the [[agentic-brain|Agentic Brain]] viewpoints that bind into larger entities. The viewpoint [[gh-formulation|formulation]] is given directly in `resnote05` and `vecB`: "[[agentic-brain|Agentic Brain]] rendered viewpoints, emerging from these Nonlinear Differential Continuous Approximations (or Equilibriations: Equilibrium + Calibrations) connect, bind, and entify into larger entities" (`resnote05--md`, line 27, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/resnote05.md)) — making NDCA the source of viewpoint emergence and tying it to the [[agentic-viewpoints|agentic-viewpoints]] / [[agentic-viewpoint|agentic-viewpoint]] notes elsewhere in the SAN wiki. The cell-level statement positions NDCA inside [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s [[awareness-as-predictive-myth|prediction-driven]] account: each cell anticipates an expected state and computes a continuous, nonlinear differential against actual incoming signal, dissipating "extra energy as [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differentials]] or cellular communications" (`vecC--md`, line 443, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecC.md)) — yielding the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave-differential]] outputs that are the load-bearing currency of [[neural-rendering|Neural Rendering]] and the [[consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop|multimodal rendering loop]]. The processing-chain statement in `b0054y` makes the role explicit: NDCA is the *upward* pass through "neurons and cortical hierarchies" that produces new phase wave differential patterns from the incoming bound signals, so the chain is roughly NAPOT-projection → bound multimodal signal → NDCA at each cell → new [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differentials]] → next stage of NAPOT. As a *fractal* unit of computation NDCA is run at neuron, [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|cortical-column]], population, and tissue scales — each with its own scale-appropriate equilibration target — connecting the operation to the [[oscillatory-binding-problem|oscillatory binding-problem]] account and to the [[neural-distinction-consciousness|distinction-as-root-of-perception]] story in which every distinction is a viewpoint. NDCA-as-equilibrium-calibration also reads naturally onto the [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]] threshold layer: a cell's equilibration error is the input to the criterial threshold determining whether the resulting phase wave differential crosses into [[oscillator-consciousness-threshold|conscious]] aggregation or stays subthreshold.

## Chronology

The earliest dated evidence for the explicit NDCA / Equilibriations terminology in the present packet is **2025** — the same June-2025 ChatGPT working notes (`documents-legacy--ChatGPT2025June17`, line 279273, note) that are central to several other late-2025 SAN constructs. The Lane A `b0054y`, `resnote05`, `vecB`, and `vecC` notes carrying the term sit inside the same window, and the `dl-oct6-2025` corpus mirrors the same passages — suggesting NDCA crystallised as a named operation in late 2024 / early 2025 as [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] was consolidating the post-NAPOT processing story. The construct is *not* present in the earliest SAN whitepaper-era writings; it is part of the more recent cluster that pairs [[agentic-brain|Agentic Brain]] viewpoints with [[entification|entification]] mechanics and [[fractal-pattern|fractal]] cross-scale [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|hierarchical]] computation. As of the captured corpus there is no external paper using the exact "Nonlinear Differential Continuous Approximations" phrasing, so the construct is currently SAN-internal and not yet engaged with mainstream literature under this name.
