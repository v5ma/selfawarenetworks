SuperTimePosition (STP) is [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s extension of [[super-dark-time]] that replaces the Transactional Interpretation's retrocausal waves with **time density gradients and phase synchronization** as the explanation for quantum coherence, wavefunction collapse, and Bell's inequality violations — without invoking nonlocality or faster-than-light communication.

## Core Mechanism

In the Transactional Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics (TIQM, Cramer 1986; extended by Ruth Kastner as PTI), quantum events are "handshakes" between retarded (forward-time) and advanced (backward-time) waves, implying future events influence the past.

SuperTimePosition rejects retrocausality. Instead:

1. **Time density governs quantum update rate**: regions closer to mass have higher time density; quantum states in these regions update more slowly. What appears as instantaneous collapse is actually a phase synchronization event — the particle "reaching" the observer's time density level.
2. **Phase-locked vortices replace handshakes**: each particle is an oscillating quantum vortex. Coherence occurs when local time densities align — not via bidirectional time-traveling fields.
3. **Bell violations without nonlocality**: entangled particles share a phase relationship; apparent nonlocal correlations arise because the vortices evolved together under the same time-density field, not because they signal each other faster than light.

## Relationship to Super Dark Time

[[super-dark-time]] is the broader theory (time has density gradients, mass is an emergent accumulation of quantum vortices trapping each other). SuperTimePosition is the quantum-measurement extension: it specifies how the collapse/coherence mechanism works within that time-density framework.

Neither requires retrocausality, nonlocality, or hidden variables. Both aim for a **fully deterministic** account of quantum mechanics rooted in time as a structured field.

## Comparison to Entropic Gravity

Erik Verlinde's Entropic Gravity treats gravity as a thermodynamic emergent effect of information. SDT/STP instead grounds gravity in **quantum vortex synchronization across a time-density field** — each particle trapping adjacent vortices, gravitational attraction being the collective phase-coherence of these oscillators. The mechanisms diverge at the quantum level.
