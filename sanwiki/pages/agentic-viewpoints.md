Agentic Viewpoints is the SAN thesis that every physical distinction — from a particle to a brain to a collective — constitutes a **viewpoint**: a relative differentiation from the whole electromagnetic field that possesses some degree of agency.

## Core Claim

A viewpoint is any region of the electromagnetic field that has achieved internal coherence via oscillatory binding. At the cellular scale, groups of neurons produce rendered sub-viewpoints through [[napot|NAPOT]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography). These sub-viewpoints are then unified into a singular perspectival whole via [[nonlinear-differential-continuous-approximations|Nonlinear Differential Continuous Approximations]] (NDCAs, also called Equilibriations).

## Hierarchy of Viewpoints

1. **Particle/wave** — a bare distinction from the cosmic EM field; the minimal viewpoint.
2. **Cellular coalition** — a group of oscillating sensor-transmitters rendering a local sub-image via NAPOT.
3. **Organism** — millions of sub-viewpoints [[entification|entified]] by oscillatory synchrony into a unified first-person perspective.
4. **Collective** — inter-organism frequency matching assembles group-scale agency with emergent coordinated behavior.

Each level is a **fractal agent**: a single sensor-transmitter system made of smaller sensor-transmitter parts, bound by phase synchrony.

## Functional Connectivity Rule

Agents within a mind connect when they are "on the same frequency" — frequency matching gates which rendered sub-viewpoints are integrated into the dominant [[phase-wave-differential|phase wave differential]]. Synergistic (same-frequency) agent outputs are amplified, yielding conscious thought and coordinated action.

## Relation to Entification

[[entification|Entification]] is the mechanism by which many smaller agentic viewpoints bind into one larger singular viewpoint. The oscillatory synchrony of NDCAs is the glue. This makes agentic viewpoints and [[entification]] two sides of the same process: viewpoints are the units; [[entification]] is the binding operation.
