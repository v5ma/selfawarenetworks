# Three Simultaneous Neural Wave Modalities (a0286z)

(synap, oscillat, [dendrite](dendrite.md), electromagnetism, cortex, soliton, vector, dissipation)

# Connect notes a0177z, a0286z, a0039z

Three types of simultaneous soliton wave transmission are considered alongside Hodgkin-Huxley dynamics. The note frames the oscillatory dynamics of a neuron as extending from [dendritic computation](dendritic-computation.md) to multi-vesicle release.

At a higher level, neuron firing could be seen as creating a magnetic arrow inside a four dimensional electromagnetic phase space created by the rest of the brain, essentially a vector written into negative space. In this hypothesis, the [action potential](action-potential.md) is an electrical event that may create an electromagnetic wave in the brain, while the simultaneous creation of a mechanical wave and a magnetic wave from the electrical wave may be valuable for motor signals. Moving the human body requires more signalling power than a single neuron transmits, so soliton waves created by action potentials could potentially create motor power. The dissipation of those electromagnetic waves would probably increase the chances of oscillator behavior emerging when a larger group of neurons receives the same signal at the same time, or close to the same time.

The synapse potentially transmits three types of soliton waves simultaneously. Each exit terminal might send a smaller soliton wave fractal of the [action potential](action-potential.md)'s mechanical vibration wave, a smaller soliton wave fractal of its electrical wave via chemical transmission, and a smaller soliton wave fractal of its magnetic wave. This would allow each neuron to correlate the three types of waves, with the [dendrite](dendrite.md) serving as a detector or pooler that creates the four dimensional soliton waveform. Neurons on the receiving end would use that waveform to compile their own picture, or potentially a four dimensional pattern representation, of the transmitted signals. If this is true, then a neural firing sequence in the [auditory cortex](auditory-cortex.md) could represent a musical note at each interval, or at the firing of each neuron.

Note that a soliton wave transmission is described here as a phase with specific topological characteristics in four dimensions, so its four dimensional surface morphs over time. The next neuron may use its dendritic collection to shrink four dimensional wave patterns from other neurons into micro-voltage events over time. These events would likely co-occur with micro-vibration events and micro-magnetic events that shake and pressurize the mechanical wall of the cell, which is held together by electromagnetism. That process may allow the cell to expand so transmitters can flood in, like a balloon sucking in neurotransmitters slowly, until it needs to release them with the [action potential](action-potential.md) event.

Essentially, the waveform of the [action potential](action-potential.md) by itself is a frequency with amplitude. The multi-possible microvesicle release, with options between 0, 1, 2, or 3 vesicles being released depending on the waveform of the [action potential](action-potential.md), would mean that both the amplitude and frequency of the [action potential](action-potential.md) are transmitted in a smaller form across the synapse to other [dendrite](dendrite.md) computers.

If the amplitude & frequency transfer does not include soliton waves, then at the very least patterns could still be computed at the network level, because you would have a situation where neurons could at least monitor the phase patterns of other neurons knitting together 4 dimensional pattern representations by themselves, but they would be distributed to other neurons through multiple firing events instead of with a single firing event. If the soliton wave hypothesis combined with Hodgkin & Huxley + Chemical Neurotransmitter are both true, then a single neuron would be able to observe, learn, and transmit 4D patterns to its network. If only the chemical neurotransmitter theory is true, then at least neurons can communicate both their amplitude & frequency via multi-vesicle-release, which by itself is going to transmit a temporal pattern that has spatial characteristic into the network. In both situations the neurons are using their dendrites to track 4D patterns (at least via the build up of ions in the branches & body that create the electromagnetic charge that distributes transmitters and opens up ion channels in the exit synapses) and their synapses to transmit patterns over time to the network. A tempo spatial pattern can be as simple as a time series of voltages, so the synapse firing can create & track a time series of voltages, so can the [dendrite](dendrite.md) through ion charge build up, including dendritic action potentials, the soma, where the cells primary [action potential](action-potential.md) event happens is a signal convergence event. The signal output could be a vector representing the frequency or phase or voltage or a wave, with one vector representing its frequency & amplitude (the magnitude of its chemical distribution) which are inversely related. The chemical pressure in the synaptic cleft could be increasing, which could lead to sodium ions being pushed into the next cell, even as the excitation of the membrane of the next cell is causing the next cell to expand and pull in new neurotransmitters like a balloon.

The model also suggests an electromechanical wave that could represent a sound, a light pattern, or a touch sensation that is heard, felt, or seen by the post-synaptic [dendrite](dendrite.md). In this hypothesis, these mechanical waves are symbolic of the quantity of ions being let into the neuron by the neurotransmitters.

# Multi-Vesicle -> Multi-scale Magnitude Neuro Transmitter Release (referenced in b0123y and b0067y.md)

So imagine that a low amplitude high frequency phasic [action potential](action-potential.md) spike transmits both neurotransmitters and a mechanical wave that is received as sodium channels open on the other side of the synapse. The delta (or amount) of the mechanical wave ought to correlate 1:1 with the delta of the synaptic transmission.

# The magnitude of the wave, determined by Potassium (see Synaptic Unreliability article a0221z.md) is passed via duration (calcium channels open longer)

This translates to a range of vesicle counts, with 0, 1, 2, or 3 vesicles transmitted in each interval of [action potential](action-potential.md) firing. In the note's model, those intervals represent oscillation phases for four neuronal phase states: inhibition, tonic firing, excited firing, and really excited firing. This allows the neuron to store the meaning of a phase event as a charge resulting from ion buildup. Each event sets an interval for the neuron's clock, which is essentially counting until it fires, making it an oscillator that learns patterns by changing the configuration of its synapses or by having those synapses reconfigured by other neurons.
