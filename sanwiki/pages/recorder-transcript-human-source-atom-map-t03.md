## Rank 35: Shared Coordination Does Not Require Identity Merger

The 2021 recording begins with a practical problem. Several people might one day use neural
interfaces to participate in the same artificial environment. Sharing a world, however, does not
answer who sensed, remembered, chose, acted, learned, or can disconnect.

The recovered operation is:

```text
person-specific sensing, memory, and action
-> voluntary registered connection
-> shared artificial environment
-> avatar or body reference
-> coordinated activity with attributable consequences
-> reversible separation and independent continuation
```

The avatar supplies a location and action reference inside the shared environment. It is not an
inner viewer. Each person's own biological network remains the system receiving, rendering, and
using that person's situated information.

Established work supplies bounded footholds. BrainNet demonstrated low-bandwidth neural mediation
among three people in one collaborative task
([Jiang et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-41895-7)).
Virtual-reality studies show that first-person perspective and multisensory congruence can alter
body ownership and self-location
([Petkova et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00035);
[Maselli and Slater, 2014](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00693)).
Functional-connectivity profiles can preserve measurable individual structure across sessions
([Finn et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4135)), while hyperscanning can measure
interpersonal phase coordination during joint action
([Sanger et al., 2012](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2012.00312)).

The comparison stops before collaboration becomes mind merger, synchrony becomes identity, an
avatar becomes an observer, WebXR becomes neural read/write, or shared data becomes shared qualia.
An external cortex coupled to a biological person also remains distinct from an autonomous
artificial mind.

SAN preserves a **retained-personhood hypothesis**: temporary coupling can preserve distinct
participants when memory continuity, sensorimotor contingencies, action attribution, independent
error correction, values, consent, revocation, and successful post-disconnection operation remain
measurable.

The recording event is November 2021; public Git fixation begins in June 2022. The separate
`a0080z.md` note adds cellular pixels, tonic and phasic activity, and layer-to-layer rendering in
2022. That later scale bridge is linked but not backdated.

## Rank 39: Meaning Changes When The Available Consequence Changes

The source's simulation debate contains a more useful operation than its unresolved metaphysics.
An organism must act with partial information. It interprets a difference through current body and
task state, memory, learned rules, available actions, and expected consequences.

```text
measured difference
-> receiver state and history
-> goal, rule, and available action
-> predicted consequence
-> action
-> returned result
-> later learning
```

The conversation's calendar example makes this plain. Two adjacent days may be physically similar,
yet a ballot can still change an outcome on one day and not the next. The difference matters
because the rule and available action changed, not because the second day contains a special
increase in energy.

Reward-prediction-error and sensorimotor-agency research provide bounded neural footholds for
expected-versus-observed consequence
([Schultz, Dayan, and Montague, 1997](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.275.5306.1593);
[Blakemore, Goodbody, and Wolpert, 1998](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.1998.0404)).
Avatar experiments show that virtual self-representation can alter behavior in controlled tasks
([Yee and Bailenson, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-2958.2007.00299.x)).
Some formally specified systems also contain undecidable long-term questions
([Moore, 1990](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.64.2354)).

The comparison stops before a useful model proves external simulation, prediction difficulty
identifies an external programmer, reward prediction explains all meaning, or virtual rehearsal
guarantees durable change. Shannon information brackets semantics, and Landauer's physical
principle does not establish SIT or a physics of meaning.

SAN preserves a receiver-relative hypothesis: a model joining measured difference, state, memory,
goal, action availability, expected consequence, and returned result should predict choice and
learning better than signal magnitude or rarity alone. Later PWD variables may be tested for
additional value, but the mature term is not backdated to this conversation.

The source is a non-diarized multi-speaker donor. The 2021 event, unidentified speaker
contributions, 2024 public Git fixation and AI summary, and later SAN synthesis remain separate.

## The Eye Analogy Names A Recurring Biological Operation

Rank 42's eye, retina, lens, and fractal language belongs to a longer source family. Its strongest
meaning is operational rather than anatomical:

```text
receive a structured difference
-> transform it according to the receiving tissue and its prior state
-> project a changed relation to another receiving system
```

The eye makes this process easy to picture. Light does not arrive in the brain as a finished
image. Photoreceptors change state, retinal circuits combine excitation and inhibition across
space and time, and ganglion cells send selected output patterns onward. Roska, Molnar, and
Werblin directly measured how those distributed retinal relations generate cell-type-specific
spiking output ([Roska, Molnar, and Werblin, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16510780/)).

SAN asks whether a related receive-transform-project operation recurs at other scales. A
dendritic branch receives a selected pattern; cellular state transforms it; an axon and its
terminals influence later receivers. Arrays of cells likewise receive partial relations,
transform them through local architecture and memory, and reexpress changed relations to other
arrays.

This genealogy is staged:

- the November 2021 `b0062y` recording preserves the cross-scale eyeball operation and was first
  fixed in Git on June 8, 2022;
- `a0282z.md`, publicly expanded on August 17, 2022, maps receiver, cable, and output roles from
  arrays of eyes to dendrites, axons, and terminals while explicitly denying literal light
  transmission through the brain; and
- the private October 2022 rank-42 monologue joins that operation to transformed multimodal
  representations, tonic context, recurrent reception, and the action-consequence self-loop.

The comparison stops before anatomical identity. Retina, cortex, thalamus, hippocampus,
dendrites, and terminals are not copies of one another. `Fractal` means functional self-similarity
unless a formal scaling law is demonstrated. `Functional isomorphism` requires an explicit map of
the preserved inputs, transformations, outputs, and relations.

The SAN extension remains a testable cross-scale hypothesis: cells, circuits, arrays, and larger
loops may compose distributed Neural Rendering by repeatedly receiving, transforming, and
projecting structured relations. A test must declare the variables and receiver at each scale.

## New Input And Existing Circuitry Build Function Together

Rank 42's compressed `eye into ear socket` story names a real developmental-plasticity result.
The plain operation is:

```text
a new patterned input reaches a developing recipient circuit
-> afferent activity reorganizes part of that circuit
-> recipient anatomy and developmental history constrain the transformation
-> a useful but nonidentical new representation emerges
```

Sur, Garraghty, and Roe redirected retinal projections into auditory thalamus and cortex in
newborn ferrets and recorded visually driven responses in the normally auditory pathway
([Sur, Garraghty, and Roe, 1988](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2462279/)). Sharma, Angelucci,
and Sur later found orientation modules in rewired auditory cortex, while also finding that their
organization differed from normal visual cortex
([Sharma, Angelucci, and Sur, 2000](https://www.nature.com/articles/35009043)).

The comparison stops before cortical interchangeability. The work did not plug an adult rat eye
into an ear socket, erase recipient circuitry, or show arbitrary adult reassignment. It shows
that arriving activity and recipient structure jointly influence function.

SAN preserves **receiver-conditioned transformation**: a signal's effective meaning depends on
the tissue that receives it. That matters for Neural Tuning and NerveGear. A neural write-in
system cannot assume that copying a source signal reproduces the source experience; it must model
the receiving circuit's morphology, state, history, and feedback.

See [[neural-tuning]], [[neo-mind-cycle-neurofeedback-bci-genealogy]], and
[[world-body-brain-semantic-coregistration]].

## A Changing Population State Can Have Geometry

The `XYZ` image in rank 42 is a first explanation, not a final coordinate claim. At each interval,
many cells differ in physical location, activity, timing, tuning, body relation, task relevance,
and learned context. Together those variables define a population state. A later interval defines
another state, and the sequence traces a trajectory.

Visual cortical population responses to natural images are experimentally high-dimensional and
smoothly structured ([Stringer et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31243367/)).
Recurrent computation also contributes to the evolving representation of partial and difficult
objects ([Tang et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30104363/);
[Kar et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31036945/)).

The comparison stops before an `XYZ` plot becomes the neural code or a population manifold becomes
consciousness. Physical space, feature space, body coordinates, semantic relations, affective
dimensions, and task variables must be distinguished.

SAN preserves a larger NAPOT question: do successive arrays transform partial population
relations into a body- and world-centered state that can guide prediction and action? That
composition must outperform alternative recurrent and population models on held-out neural,
behavioral, and perturbational data.

## Context And Update Are Relative Roles

Rank 42 joins three earlier comparisons: Hawkins's reference-frame problem, Tse's tonic/phasic
discussion, and the philosophical `ground of being` image. The resulting operation is simple:
some coordinated activity persists long enough to supply context, while another event supplies a
consequential change.

```text
maintained object, body, task, expectation, or population relation
-> incoming or recurrent difference
-> comparison, amplification, suppression, routing, or learning
-> revised maintained relation
```

Frequency-specific cortical research supplies bounded components. In macaques, gamma influences
were relatively stronger in feedforward directions while alpha/beta influences were relatively
stronger in feedback directions
([Bastos et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25556836/)). Behavioral-timescale
synaptic plasticity supplies a different bridge by allowing dendritic plateau events to alter
place-related firing over intervals of seconds
([Bittner et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28883072/)).

The comparison stops before feedforward always means gamma, feedback always means alpha/beta, or
tonic means one slow band, layer, region, or conscious carrier. Broadband, aperiodic, cellular,
synaptic, and biochemical state also participate.

SAN preserves tonic and phasic as **relative functional roles**. The tonic role is the maintained
receiver context for the chosen population and interval. The phasic role is the consequential
departure. PWD names a proposed receiver-relevant differential spanning phase, timing, magnitude,
duration, waveform, location, chemistry, and prior state.

## The Network Receives Its Own Transformed Output

The rank-42 pixel and television images solve a composition problem. No single synapse contains
the scene. Many local synaptic, dendritic, cellular, and population changes contribute to a
larger relation. Recurrent pathways make the system's changed output part of what later cells and
arrays receive.

The operation is:

```text
many local state changes
-> distributed population relation
-> recurrent projection
-> later reception and comparison
-> changed recognition, prediction, or action
```

Recurrent visual computations support pattern completion and difficult object recognition
([Tang et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30104363/);
[Kar et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31036945/)). Those results establish
component recurrent operations, not an internal display or complete consciousness theory.

The comparison stops before synapses become fixed pixels, spikes carry little pictures, or a
screen requires a hidden viewer. The system is the observer insofar as its distributed state is
changed, carried forward, used to predict, and made effective in action.

SAN preserves the distributed-observer hypothesis: Neural Rendering is the ongoing construction,
registration, and use of the field-cell state; NAPOT is a proposed receive-transform-project
architecture. A causal test must predict what downstream rendering, discrimination, or behavior
changes when a specific local or array-level contribution is perturbed.

## Photons as measurement, perturbation, and biological output

Rank 34 starts with a practical problem: light can interact with neural tissue in several
different ways, and the measurement chain must say which interaction is being used.

In plain language:

```text
light can illuminate or excite a target
-> a molecule or tissue state changes
-> emitted, transmitted, or reflected light reaches a detector
-> an inference is made about calcium, metabolism, structure, or activity
```

That sequence contains distinct operations:

| Optical role | Established operation | What remains to be tested |
|---|---|---|
| measurement carrier | focused excitation and detected fluorescence can report an indicator's state | how accurately that proxy recovers the neural variable of interest |
| causal input | light can activate or suppress cells prepared with photosensitive molecular machinery | how spatially and temporally selective the perturbation is in the living circuit |
| intrinsic optical output | swelling, scattering, oxygenation, flavoprotein state, and NAD(P)H can change with neural or metabolic activity | which physical source dominates at each scale and condition |
| ultraweak photon emission | neural tissue emits photons associated with metabolism, oxidation, stimulation, and other state changes | whether any emission acts as a functional message received and decoded by another neural component |
| optical-field reconstruction | phase-resolved methods can characterize a controlled ultrafast laser pulse at its focal plane | whether a different instrument and forward model can reconstruct endogenous neural-tissue fields |

Large-field two-photon systems show why simultaneous observation matters for distributed neural
activity ([Clough et al., 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-26737-3)).
Calcium fluorescence is a useful activity-related proxy whose kinetics and expression remain part
of the measurement model ([Heider et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21079806/)).
Engineered opsins permit temporally precise optical control
([Gunaydin et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20081849/)).
Activity-dependent flavoprotein autofluorescence supplies a label-free metabolic observable
([Shibuki et al., 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12730344/)).

The natural-photon question remains scientifically open rather than erased. Ultraweak photon
emission has been measured from brain tissue and related to metabolism, oxidative state, EEG, and
stimulation ([Kobayashi et al., 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10498336/)).
Glutamate-dependent spatiotemporal emission has also been reported in mouse brain slices
([Tang et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24454909/)). More recent work asks whether
these signals can become a useful optical marker of brain state while explicitly retaining
uncertainty about communication and waveguiding claims
([Casey et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40124516/)).

The SAN extension is therefore a test program, not a vocabulary substitution:

```text
identify an endogenous emitter
-> identify a physically plausible receiver
-> measure spectrum, timing, path, attenuation, and noise
-> separate emission from metabolic and hemodynamic covariates
-> perturb the proposed optical channel
-> test whether downstream neural information or behavior changes
```

Emission alone does not establish message transmission, but the absence of a completed receiver
model does not justify retiring the hypothesis. The correct status is a source-gated,
falsifiable optical propagation term inside a broader multimodal account.

The same recording supplies a separate dendritic result: inputs arriving at the same locations in
different temporal orders can produce different responses. Patterned glutamate-uncaging work
demonstrates sequence-sensitive dendritic integration
([Branco, Clark, and Hausser, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20705816/)).
This established operation motivates later SAN work on coincidence, temporal-spatial pattern,
PWD, and Taylor-sequence ancestry without by itself proving those larger compositions.

## From mechanical force to biological electrical state

Rank 43 contains a compressed tree, sound, electricity, and fungal-network comparison. Its
strongest recoverable operation is simpler than the metaphor:

```text
pressure or vibration deforms living tissue
-> mechanosensitive structures change ion conductance
-> membrane voltage, calcium, or another bioelectric variable changes
-> the changed state can propagate or alter later cellular behavior
-> electrodes or impedance imaging measure part of the consequence
```

Plant mechanosensitive channels directly convert membrane tension into ion flux. MSL10 is
particularly relevant to the recording because it responds to oscillatory membrane stretching
([Tran et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33372153/)). Plant wound experiments also
connect mechanosensitive-channel activity with long-distance electrical and calcium signaling
([Moe-Lange et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34516872/)). Electrical impedance
tomography has been tested as a non-destructive method for monitoring plant-root development
([Corona-Lopez et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6528207/)).

Fungal electrophysiology likewise supplies a real measurement target. Action-potential-like
events were recorded in *Neurospora crassa* decades ago, although their function was unresolved
([Slayman, Long, and Gradmann, 1976](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/130926/)). More recent
differential-electrode work found mycelial voltage fluctuations related to growth and altered by
biocide treatment while emphasizing the need for reproducible methods
([Buffi et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41035690/)).

This is where the established comparison stops. The studies do not show that sound and
electricity are the same signal, that fungal networks perform brain-equivalent computation, or
that animal sounds make trees cause rain. EIT can reveal a changing conductivity distribution;
it does not identify the causal mechanism by itself.

The preserved SAN/COT extension is a controlled experiment:

```text
specified acoustic or mechanical stimulus
-> simultaneous direct electrical recording and impedance imaging
-> sham, environmental, species, and tissue controls
-> measured latency, propagation, frequency response, and recovery
-> perturbation of the proposed mechanotransduction route
-> test of the predicted cellular or organismal consequence
```

That extension is a falsifiable cross-scale bioelectric measurement hypothesis, not a retired
analogy. See [[cellular-oscillating-tomography]],
[[neural-lace-podcast-s2e1-jean-rintoul-openeit]], and
[[multimodal-neural-state-reconstruction-perturbation]].

## A maintained process, a selected view, and an action-source self

Rank 43 also joins three ideas that should be explained before their SAN names:

1. A living system stays recognizably continuous while exchanging matter because it repeatedly
   maintains constraints, memories, and control relations.
2. A resource-limited observer samples and constructs the body/world relation needed for its
   current viewpoint and action rather than representing every possible viewpoint equally.
3. The system can attribute predicted consequences to the recurring body-centered source of its
   own motor commands.

The first idea is a **process-identity model**: persistence depends on organized causal
continuity, not on retaining every molecule. The second is a **selective-rendering comparison**
drawn from vision and XR. The third connects to established distinctions between body ownership
and agency, and to experiments in which prospective motor planning and action-outcome structure
alter the sense of authorship
([Moore et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2010.05.021);
[Seghezzi et al., 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7458439/)).

These comparisons do not establish that an organism is literally one rotating vortex, that
perception is a video-game renderer, or that action authorship is the whole personal self.
Memory, embodiment, interoception, social continuity, and conscious availability remain separate
requirements.

SAN preserves a joined, testable extension:

```text
maintained body and neural process
-> selective body/world/action rendering
-> predicted motor consequence
-> attribution to a body-centered action source
-> action, returned sensory evidence, and model revision
```

The proposed self is the distributed system performing this loop; no additional inner viewer is
inserted. A test must perturb one stage and predict a selective change in agency, body/world
representation, action, or feedback learning. See [[field-cell-self-bioelectric-information-entity]],
[[perceptual-screen-neural-rendering]], [[distributed-observer-vs-consciousness-module]], and
[[agency-valuation-action-feedback]].

## Tonic context, phasic difference, and accumulated drive

Rank 41 supplies a vivid perturbation model: a coordinated population establishes a current
context; a local event changes that relation; recurrent interaction either absorbs, amplifies,
routes, or suppresses the difference.

Tonic and phasic remain **relative functional roles**, not permanent names for fixed bands:

- tonic is the maintained reference state for the selected population and interval;
- phasic is a consequential update relative to that state;
- a Phase Wave Differential is the structured, receiver-relevant difference involving phase,
  timing, magnitude, duration, waveform, location, chemistry, and prior state;
- compatible concurrent inputs can accumulate effective drive at a receiver and help recruit an
  output pathway.

Neural drive does not replace the metabolic energy of muscle. Coincident inputs can recruit
motor neurons and motor pools; neuromuscular transmission, calcium regulation, and ATP-powered
actomyosin contraction produce movement.

## From a transient difference to selective plasticity

Rank 41 also asks how a brief population difference can influence what persists. In plain
language, one selected response can excite some cells, inhibit or delay others, and leave another
group near its prior rhythm. That changed relation may recur, decay, recruit a different assembly,
or create repeated opportunities for activity-dependent synaptic change.

Established research supplies several parts of this operation:

- electrical and synaptic coupling can alter phase locking, with the result depending on coupling,
  cell type, input regime, and phase-response properties
  ([Mancilla et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17314301/);
  [Sohal and Huguenard, 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16339306/));
- synchronous activation of nearby inputs can support cooperative, input-specific potentiation
  ([Weber et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27098773/));
- potentiation can be accompanied by activity-dependent remodeling of an individual stimulated
  dendritic spine ([Matsuzaki et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15190253/)); and
- competition for molecular resources can contribute to different growth or shrinkage outcomes
  among neighboring spines ([Eggl et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39217140/)).

The comparison stops before claiming that spines literally seek the fastest-firing neuron, that
LTD is merely nutrient withdrawal, or that a higher event rate produces relativistic spacetime
dilation. Those literal readings are not needed to preserve the source's operation:
appropriately timed and repeated activity can bias which relations persist and which pathways are
remodeled.

SAN's additional hypothesis is mesoscopic. A perturbation is represented by the relation among
excited, inhibited, delayed, silent, and still-tonic cells; recurrent interaction can make that
differential less aligned with one group and more aligned with another. The paired
Group Short-Term Depression and Group Short-Term Potentiation terms name this proposed
population-level transition. An activity-clock interpretation counts consequential events per
external interval; it does not require a general-relativistic claim.

[[oscillating-group-completion]], [[neural-phase-trajectories-taylor-series-proposal]], and
[[extended-brain-thought-density-hypertime]].

## Nonlinear approximation and NAPOT

Rank 37 states the problem before naming the solution: every receiving array sees a partial,
transformed relation, yet a useful percept or action requires continuity across successive
states. The proposed operation is:

```text
partial input at one interval
-> nonlinear transformation by the current array
-> projected state with retained history
-> another partial view and another transformation
-> recurrent approximation of a stable object, trajectory, or action relation
```

NAPOT names the multiscale receive-transform-project architecture. The Taylor-series language
names a candidate mathematical description of successive corrections, not a claim that neurons
literally execute symbolic polynomial software.

Tomography, photogrammetry, semantic segmentation, and volumetric rendering remain engineering
comparisons that help formulate the biological inverse problem. They are not substituted for
biological evidence.

## Measurement, perturbation, and BCI

Ranks 43, 44, 46, and 47 convert the theory into a measurement program:

- structural anatomy constrains the possible routes;
- electrophysiology measures fast population and cellular relations at different scales;
- optical, hemodynamic, impedance, and magnetic methods provide different observables;
- calcium imaging, optogenetics, Patch-seq, and pharmacological perturbation can connect cell
  identity, state, and consequence;
- XR supplies a controlled world/body/action environment;
- closed-loop behavior tests whether a decoded or induced state has the proposed function.

The BCI genealogy spans EEG and neurofeedback, ECoG and intracortical arrays, DBS and noninvasive
stimulation, fNIRS, holographic and optical proposals, ultrasound, EIT, and multimodal fusion.
These methods differ in resolution, latency, invasiveness, and evidentiary reach. Listing them as
one portfolio does not imply that every combination is currently validated or clinically ready.

## From cellular intelligence to the physics program

Ranks 35, 40, and 48 preserve a legitimate genealogy without collapsing distinct theories:

```text
cells selectively receive and transform information
-> organized populations maintain and alter relational state
-> recurrent systems render body/world/action structure
-> non-neural cells may also compute and coordinate through cellular signaling
-> COT and SET ask how widely this organizational logic extends
-> later QGTCD and SIT ask whether related informational constraints apply in physics
```

The recordings establish the problem transition, not a proof that one neuroscience mechanism
solves quantum gravity. The later physics theories have their own equations, evidence burdens,
and dates.

## Source-faithful repair rules

| Compressed or ambiguous wording | Public-safe retained operation |
|---|---|
| a neuron is a literal camera, microphone, screen, or speaker | dendrites and cells perform selective capture and transformation, while outputs reexpress changed state to other receivers; the device terms are functional analogies |
| an action potential carries one scalar bit | regenerative spikes remain discrete events, while timing, rate, burst organization, waveform, release, target, and context can carry additional consequences |
| synchronized cells are automatically conscious | coordination can support integration and effective drive; consciousness additionally requires explicit criteria, alternatives, perturbations, and behavioral consequences |
| electrical valence supplies muscular energy | coincident neural input can recruit motor output; calcium-regulated ATP-powered contraction supplies muscular work |
| one frequency is always tonic and another always phasic | tonic and phasic name state-relative roles; frequency bands can play different roles across regions, tasks, and intervals |
| every cell performs the same algorithm | cells share physical operations but differ by morphology, receptors, channels, connectivity, history, and role |
