# Hebbian Engrams

**Hebbian [[engrams]]** are the cell-assembly-style memory traces predicted by Donald Hebb's 1949 theory — repeatedly co-active neurons become wired into associated assemblies whose collective activity *is* the stored memory. Inside [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] they are the *classical-cell-assembly* baseline that SAN's [[engrams|holographic-engram]] reading extends, with [[hebbian-learning|Hebbian learning]] supplying the connection-strength dynamics and [[single-cell-hebbian-assembly|single-cell Hebbian assembly]] supplying the per-cell version that scales up to ensemble-level [[engrams|engrams]].
