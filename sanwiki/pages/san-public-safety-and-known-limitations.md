# SAN Public Safety and Known Limitations

The SAN Wiki contains neuroscience education, historical source recovery, engineering proposals, and unconfirmed scientific hypotheses. It is not medical advice, a clinical decision system, or a substitute for independent scientific or ethical review.

## Scientific status

Each page should separate established research from SAN's interpretation and from SAN's novel proposals. A citation can support a component without validating the complete SAN architecture. A chronological comparison can establish documentary order without proving biology, physics, mathematics, consciousness, or priority over every possible source.

Medical, biological, physical, and mathematical review improves clarity and catches errors. It is not the same as independent peer review, clinical validation, replication, regulatory approval, or expert consensus.

Use the [[consciousness-claim-and-evidence-ladder|Consciousness Claim and Evidence Ladder]] and [[comparison-evidence-is-not-biological-validation|Comparison Evidence Is Not Biological Validation]].

## Medical and neurotechnology boundary

The Wiki does not authorize self-treatment, medication changes, brain stimulation, invasive experimentation, or clinical interpretation of an individual's data. EEG, fNIRS, MRI, ultrasound, electrical stimulation, optical systems, and other BCI technologies have modality-specific limits, artifacts, risks, and regulatory requirements.

NerveGear, artificial sensory write-in, thought-density hypertime, complete experience reconstruction, and general-purpose neural read/write remain research objectives or hypotheses. Current measurements do not provide unrestricted access to a person's thoughts or a complete copy of subjective experience.

## Consciousness boundary

Neural decoding, behavioral competence, report, metacognition, integrated information, and phenomenal consciousness are not interchangeable. A successful classifier or benchmark does not by itself establish consciousness. A proposed consciousness measure must declare its target, controls, causal perturbations, uncertainty, and possible rival explanations.

Conversely, the absence of a current measurement does not justify treating a possibly conscious biological or artificial system without ethical consideration. Machine-consciousness claims require conservative testing and governance.

## Privacy and consent

BCI and neuroaffective systems can expose sensitive information even when their inferences are incomplete. Public SAN work should preserve:

- informed and revocable consent;
- data minimization and purpose limitation;
- clear separation between raw signals and inferred mental content;
- security against unauthorized stimulation or model access;
- the right to refuse monitoring and to leave a coupled system;
- special protection for medical, emotional, identity, and developmental data.

Hidden, compelled, irreversible, or exploitative human-machine coupling is outside the acceptable research boundary.

## Copyright and source handling

The SAN Wiki should summarize, cite, and link to external research rather than republishing full copyrighted works without permission. Historical screenshots and source figures require provenance, captioning, license review, and a declared public-use basis before release.

Private AI chats, private email, unpublished collaboration material, credentials, local disk paths, and provider-control files are excluded from public pages unless the owner deliberately releases an appropriate public derivative.

## Known v1 limitations

The candidate SAN Wiki v1 is a bounded public core, not the complete SAN archive.

- The governed concept registry and reviewed source maps cover the current high-value spine, not every source atom.
- B-series transcript triage and selective re-transcription remain incomplete.
- Some older transcripts are noisy and must not carry load-bearing quotations until checked against audio.
- External links can change after verification.
- Source recovery can establish an older atom without establishing the mature later theory.
- Comparison Wiki verdicts remain governed separately and are not silently imported into SAN.
- Reader rendering and local route validation do not prove a hosted public deployment.

## Correction rule

When a sentence appears medically or scientifically wrong, first test whether it is a typo, transcription error, compressed analogy, or outdated wording by reading the underlying SAN source and earlier versions. Preserve the intended innovation where a source-faithful formulation is scientifically defensible. Record substantive corrections rather than silently changing the narrative.
