prompts. These pages preserve the outline and its current editorial routing; they are not chapter
drafts, medical evidence, or a requirement that the book devote equal space to every prompt.

## How to use this map

The cycle pages answer **where an idea appeared in the planning source**. Concept pages answer
**what operation the current SAN account proposes**. Evidence pages answer **what established
science supports, leaves open, or contradicts**. Experiment pages answer **how a claim could be
distinguished from alternatives**.

That separation prevents three common errors:

1. treating an AI-generated planning prompt as a settled Micah claim;
2. treating a historical metaphor as established anatomy or physics;
3. treating SAOv9's fourteen cycles as the current 31-chapter book.
