# Synaptic Cleft

> **Medical-accuracy correction (2026-07-19):** The synaptic cleft is a transient extracellular reaction and diffusion space, not a demonstrated temporal abacus, pressure bus, soliton channel, or phase-pattern emitter. The earlier image remains in the asset archive but is not embedded here.

The **synaptic cleft** is the narrow extracellular space separating pre- and
postsynaptic membranes at a chemical synapse. Vesicular release creates a transmitter
concentration transient shaped by quantal release, diffusion, receptor binding and
unbinding, uptake, enzymatic breakdown where applicable, and cleft geometry. The
postsynaptic cell does not read a free-standing count stored in the gap; receptors and
membrane state transform the concentration history into conductance and voltage
([Clements et al., 1992](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1359647/)).

## SAN hypothesis and test boundary

Release quantity, timing, and spatial organization can influence downstream response,
but any additional SAN timing claim must name a molecule, receptor, geometry,
timescale, receiver, intervention, and alternative explanation. Mechanical pressure,
soliton transfer, a temporal tensor, and binding-by-cleft-count remain proposals.

## Position — the temporal abacus

## Position — splay state and asynchronous release

Two passages connect the cleft to the macro-state of the network. `GPT2023X--md` line 7103 and `NotebookML_6_Collection--md` line 1895 both record that "[i]n a splay state, neurons fire asynchronously, leading to a decrease in glutamate release in the synaptic cleft" ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2023X.md), [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/NotebookML%206%20Collection.md)). `GPT2023X--md` line 7119 elaborates: asynchronous firing "leads to less coordinated and efficient synaptic transmission, contributing to the process of synap[tic depression]." The cleft therefore reads out the network's coherence state directly — when arrays fall out of phase, the cleft sees less [[neurotransmitter]], and the post-synaptic [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|coincidence detector]] reads that as a weakening signal. This is one of the simplest places SAN says the [[oscillatory-binding-problem|binding problem]] is *physically* resolved: in the cleft, by the count.
