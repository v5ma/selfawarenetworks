## Branch 1: the splay-state research lead

The first file only points to Zou and Zhan's mathematical study of splay states in rings of coupled nonlinear oscillators. In a formal splay state, population phases are distributed around an oscillatory cycle. This is not automatically the same as:

- two oscillators in anti-phase;
- transient desynchronization;
- random neural noise; or
- quantum decoherence.

[Zou and Zhan (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1137/09075398X) analyzed stability under different coupling ranges. Their paper did not establish SAN's later claims about neural differentiation, LTP or LTD, rendering, or qualia. Those SAN proposals and the required timing vocabulary are governed in [[coherence-dispersion-sculpting-hypothesis|Coherence-Dispersion Sculpting Hypothesis]] and [[oscillatory-coherence-architecture|Oscillatory Coherence Architecture]].

## Branch 2: the magnetoreception and training question

The second file connects three observations into a question:

```text
magnetic material or a magnetically responsive molecule
-> a neural response to a weak field
-> active sampling through movement and attention
-> feedback and perceptual learning
-> possible behavioral use or conscious access
```

This ordered question is the source-faithful operation. The note then proposed air-traffic controllers and other sky watchers as a group that might acquire such a skill. No evidence recovered here demonstrates that occupational prediction. It remains an experimental suggestion.

## What later evidence changes

The relevant findings must remain separate:

- Human postmortem brain tissue contains magnetite, with regionally varying remanent magnetization ([Gilder et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-29766-z)). Presence does not identify a receptor, neural pathway, behavior, or conscious percept.
- Human CRY2 restored light-dependent magnetic behavior in a transgenic fly assay ([Foley et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms1364)). That establishes molecular capability in the fly system, not human magnetoreception.
- A controlled human EEG study reported alpha-band responses to selected Earth-strength field rotations ([Wang et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0483-18.2019)). The participants did not report conscious field perception, and the result does not establish useful behavior.
- A study of 34 men reported light-dependent forced-choice orientation under restricted experimental conditions ([Chae et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-12460-6)). It inferred a radical-pair mechanism, whereas Wang et al. inferred a ferromagnetic transducer. The difference is unresolved.
- A 2025 study from an overlapping research group reported geomagnetic-field effects in a probabilistic choice task without conscious field perception ([Oh et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2025.1497021)). This is not independent replication of the earlier studies.

A current physiological review concludes that the human evidence is promising but still requires independent replication ([Nordmann et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1152/physrev.00032.2025)). None of these later papers should be backdated into the wording of the 2022 source.

## The medically bounded SAN interpretation

Active sensing and perceptual learning can improve discrimination when a sensory channel already supplies usable information. They do not prove that training can create a magnetic transduction channel. SAN's narrower proposal is testable: repeated movement, comparison, feedback, and action might make an existing weak neural response behaviorally useful.

The full sham-controlled protocol and its rejection conditions are in [[human-magnetoreception-active-sensing-training|Human Magnetoreception as an Active-Sensing and Training Hypothesis]].

## Scale boundary

- **Molecular:** magnetite particles, cryptochrome chemistry, or another transducer.
- **Cellular and circuit:** conversion into neural activity and routing through a measurable pathway.
- **Network:** distributed discrimination, confidence, and sensorimotor updating.
- **Behavioral:** performance above sham and chance on held-out trials.
- **Conscious:** reportable experience, which must not be inferred from EEG alone.
