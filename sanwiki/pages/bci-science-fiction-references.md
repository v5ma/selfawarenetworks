A curated set of science fiction works that explore [[brain-computer-interface]] concepts, serving as cultural scaffolding for [[self-aware-networks]] theory. These stories map onto real BCI modalities ranging from invasive surgery (Neuralink-class) to non-invasive holographic sensing (Openwater-class).

## Key works

**Nexus Trilogy** (Ramez Naam) — nanomachines attach to neurons enabling two-way wireless transmission of thoughts, feelings, and memories between participants. Closest fictional analog to [[artificial-telepathy]] and [[neural-oscillation-synchrony]].

**Sword Art Online / NerveGear** — non-invasive headset transporting consciousness into VR while sleeping. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] speculates this maps to Laser-doppler holography + ultrasound or Laser fNIRS + holography (Openwater). Contrast with Matrix-style surgery.

**Augma** (SAO: Ordinal Scale) — AR evolution of NerveGear that operates while users are awake. Directly relevant to [[augmented-reality-bci]] and the v5ma [[impossible-spaces]] design goal: AR spatial cognition without invasive hardware.

**Neuromancer / Sprawl Trilogy** (William Gibson) — foundational cyberspace fiction. Influenced *The Matrix*, which along with "Simulacra and Simulation" (Baudrillard) and "Out of Control" (Kevin Kelly) shaped the philosophical frame SAN inhabits.

**I Am a Strange Loop** (Douglas Hofstadter) — listed alongside fiction; bridges to SAN's [[self-reference]] and [[strange-loop]] concepts in consciousness theory.
