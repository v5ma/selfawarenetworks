## Post-SAN

Mainstream neuroscience already separates tonic from phasic firing modes in many systems — thalamic relay neurons, [[dopamine]] and noradrenergic regulation, hippocampal replay vs. ongoing theta — but it does so as a within-cell or within-system distinction rather than as a global representational primitive.
