MIL-PUB-SHAPE-2022 — Thoughts have a pattern of activity and inhibition

SAN record 2021-09-19 → 2022-12-01 target document. Partial operational match; target-family firstness remains open.

Source: Mind Code, paragraph d2a1. Target: Working Memory Is Complex and Dynamic, Like Your Thoughts, PDF page 5 (printed page 21), Top–Down Control by Spatial Computing.

Article metadata says published 30 June 2017 and modified 14 August 2018. The captured text is not individually backdated to either metadata date. Crossref records 1 December 2022; journal volume 35 issue 1 is 2023. DOI creation on 2 November 2022 is not a publication date. The 2021 SAN archive predates either 2022 date.

Full SAN paragraph: avoid compressed paraphrases

I think of the game of Go as being related to the code of the human mind if the black pieces represent the excited blocks of our minds and the white pieces represent the inhibited blocks in our minds (or visa versa). So that the shape of our thoughts, in tempo-spatial patterns is represented not only by what is excited in our brains but also by what is inhibited in our brains.

Target context: Pages 4–5 describe broad alpha/beta-versus-gamma patterns controlling the expression of finer-grained neural representations. Their location changes with task demands.

Lens 1 · Operations and structure

  • Function: d2a1 describes the spatial form of thoughts using excitation and inhibition. The selection function is supplied by f236 in the same article, not inferred from d2a1 alone. The target instead specifies spatial control of memory expression. The shared operation is partial.
  • Behavior: Both describe simultaneous higher activity in selected regions and inhibited activity elsewhere. The target adds specific task-dependent readout and control behavior.
  • Operation: Spatially organized excitation/inhibition changes which neural content is expressed. The target assigns distinct roles to beta, gamma, spiking and item location; the SAN source does not specify that entire implementation.
  • Math: No equation-to-equation equivalence is demonstrated in these passages. No formal numerical score or isomorphism proof is assigned.
  • Program: An informal selection/gating subroutine can be translated between the descriptions. There is no executable implementation or verified program equivalence here.
  • Form: A spatially and temporally differentiated neural pattern is shared; SAN thought-shape and target control-pattern are not treated as identical representational levels.

Lens 2 · Causal architecture

selection or task context → spatially differentiated excitation/inhibition → selective expression or access to neural content

The SAN causal selection step is explicitly bound to neighboring article paragraph f236; d2a1 alone is representational. Together they support spatial excitation/inhibition -> selected content expression. This does not equate thought content itself with the target's coarse control map.

Bound SAN context: d2a1, f236.

Lens 3 · Reconstruct the comparison

The SAN source independently documents the two compared formulations before the selected 2022 and 2023 publication records. Different vocabulary does not erase the shared gating operation.

The target-family January 2021 Hot-Coal preprint already presents spatial activation maps and beta gating (P5–P8), before the September 2021 SAN archive capture. Earlier SAN article metadata remains visible but is not sufficient to date each sentence.

Residual: SAN's passage concerns the proposed spatial shape of thoughts. The target describes working-memory control with specified frequency bands and separates coarse control from fine-grained item coding. Those additional claims are not contained in this SAN sentence.

Listed authors: Timothy J. Buschman, Earl K. Miller. These are coauthorship links, not separate proofs or accusations.

MIL-PUB-MASK-2022 — Selection changes what the network can express

Full SAN paragraph: avoid compressed paraphrases

In the brain when someone is trying to focus on something we see a lot of inhibition in some areas of the brain and a lot of excitation in other areas of the brain, the idea here is that what is happening is an attention gain modulation, just like audio gain modulation, the parts of you that are paying attention to something you are looking at become excited and that means they exhibit more activity, more excited cellular activity, more information transfers between cells in some areas, and perhaps in part because the brain must maintain it’s overall electrical equilibrium you will see that other areas of the brain become inhibited at the same time, these other parts of the brain will exhibit less neural activity, less blood flow, less electrical activity that might result from mass action potential firings, the inhibit parts of the brain should correspond to everything you are not thinking about during that window of time. The inhibited parts of the brain should feature the unconscious material.
