# Self-Aware Networks Book and Encyclopedia Overview

The Self-Aware Networks book and the SAN Encyclopedia are related, but they do different jobs.
The book gives readers one ordered scientific journey. The Encyclopedia lets readers stop at any
concept, recover its dated sources, examine its biological scale and evidence boundary, and follow
its links into mechanisms, experiments, applications, and later revisions.

## Scientific boundary

The book program does not require one neural code to exclude every other code. SAN's mature claim
concerns receiver-relative timing, phase, duration, waveform, transmitted quantity, and network
context alongside firing rate and spike count. Historical hologram language remains a question and
translation aid; [[napot-tomography-not-holography|NAPOT is tomography, not a claim of established brain holography]]. Likewise, the later QGTCD physics branch is not a prerequisite for the
neuroscience argument.

The current book and Encyclopedia therefore use four labels consistently:

1. **Established finding:** supported independently of SAN.
2. **Bounded compatibility:** established findings leave room for a SAN interpretation but do not prove it.
3. **SAN hypothesis:** a specific proposed mechanism or synthesis.
4. **Discriminating test:** an observation or intervention that could support, weaken, or reject that hypothesis.
