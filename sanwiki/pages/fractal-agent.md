# Fractal Agent

**Fractal Agent** is the [[self-aware-network|Self-Aware Networks]] term for a *unit of agency* that recurs at every scale of the [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|fractal neural scale hierarchy]] — from the dendritic compartment, to the column, to the area, to the brain, to the social swarm — with each scale running an analogous *predict-act-confirm* loop and aggregating the lower-scale agents into the higher-scale one. The construct is the agency-side counterpart of the [[fractal-modular-consciousness|fractal-modular consciousness]] picture and of the [[fractal-cooperative-oscillations|fractal cooperative oscillations]] substrate, and it gives SAN a single vocabulary for talking about cells, columns, brains, and swarms without forcing a substrate-specific story for each scale.

## Position

Fractal Agent inherits four load-bearing properties from its definition. First, it is *scale-invariant in form*: the same predict-act-confirm loop runs at every scale of the [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|fractal neural scale hierarchy]] and the [[fractal-brain-scale-hierarchy|fractal brain scale hierarchy]]. A dendritic agent runs the loop over [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|dendritic coincidence detection]] and a column-level agent runs the loop over [[cortical-column-voting|column voting]] and [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|column collaboration]]; a brain-level agent runs the loop over the entire [[recursive-neural-array-perception|recursive neural array]]; a swarm-level agent runs the loop over the [[swarm-agi|swarm AGI]] / [[holonic-rhythm-social-bonding|holonic-rhythm]] coupled population. The *form* is invariant; the *substrate* and *time-scale* are not.

Second, it is *cooperatively-coupled*: each fractal agent is composed of lower-scale fractal agents and itself participates as a sub-agent in a higher-scale fractal agent. This is the SAN-specific reading of *holonic* organisation — every agent is a whole-and-part — and it sits underneath the [[fractal-cooperative-oscillations|fractal cooperative oscillations]] picture and the [[expert-memory-structures|expert memory structures]] vocabulary. The substrate of the coupling is the [[oscillating-network-of-oscillators|oscillator network]] at every scale, with [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators|multiscale fractal neural oscillators]] supplying the cross-scale phase locking that lets a higher-scale agent's read-out be sensitive to lower-scale activity.

Third, it is *predictively-driven*: each fractal agent runs a [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|memory-prediction]] loop — the [[life-as-memory-prediction|life-as-memory-prediction]] page generalises this from the [[cortex]] out to whole organisms. The agent generates expectations, tests them against incoming activity, and updates either the model or the action — which is why [[consciousness-as-prediction-determinism|consciousness-as-prediction]] applies at every scale of the fractal hierarchy, not only at the cortical scale. The corresponding [[memory-prediction-as-intelligence|memory-prediction-as-intelligence]] reading places intelligence itself at the fractal-agent level rather than the species level.

Fourth, it is *causally-efficacious in the SAN sense*: a fractal agent's behaviour is governed by [[criterial-causation|criterial causation]] — a higher-scale criterion (the agent's running [[gh-b0049y|summary]]) selects among the lower-scale dynamics that satisfy it, and the [[observer-as-aggregate-expectation|observer-as-aggregate-expectation]] reading explains why the criterion can be efficacious without being an additional substance. The [[self-as-distributed-criterial-wave-summary|self as distributed criterial wave-summary]] page makes this explicit at the brain-level fractal agent.

These four pieces compose to a single position: SAN treats *agency itself* as a fractal phenomenon, with the same machinery — predictive coupling over an oscillator network, criterial-causation read-out, holonic embedding — running at every scale, and with a single label (*fractal agent*) for whatever unit is doing it. The construct gives SAN a way to talk about [[agentic-brain|agentic brain]], [[agentic-viewpoints|agentic viewpoints]], and [[agentic-general-intelligence|agentic general intelligence]] under one continuous frame.

## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature has several adjacent constructs. *Holonic agency* (Koestler 1967, then taken up in distributed AI and multi-agent systems) gives the whole-and-part picture; SAN's fractal agent is compatible with the holonic frame and adds the [[oscillator-environmental-encoding|oscillator-encoded]] coupling story that holonic accounts have lacked. *Active inference* (Friston) gives the predict-act-confirm loop at the brain scale and recently at the cellular scale (Levin); SAN's fractal agent agrees on the loop and reframes the *substrate* as fractal oscillator coupling rather than Markov-blanket factorisation — testable consequence: cross-scale active-inference signatures should show specific phase-locking signatures that the Markov-blanket account does not predict. *Multi-scale agency* (Levin's bioelectric work, Bach's *Computational Theory of Mind*, Friston's free-energy generalisations) gives the cross-scale picture; SAN's fractal agent is the SAN-specific version of that picture, with the [[fractal-cooperative-oscillations|cooperative-oscillations]] substrate replacing the bioelectric or generative-model substrate.
