## Position

The 2017 Fifer-Garbesi-co-hosted EEG/VR talk does four jobs in the SAN intellectual lineage. First, it is the *earliest public articulation* of [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s [[neural-lace|neural lace]] vision in his own voice — predating the [[neural-lace-podcast|Neural Lace Podcast]] series by two years and the formal SAN GitHub corpus by five years. The brain-as-hard-drive framing in 2017 is substrate-neutral (treats the brain as storage), and the intellectual history of SAN between 2017 and 2022 is the transition from that storage metaphor to the process-first metaphor of [[napot-theory|NAPOT]] / oscillatory tomography.

Second, the 2017 talk introduces the *EEG-as-mirror* protocol: making one's own [[brainwaves]] visible and audible so that the user can "form concepts about how their thoughts and emotions are [[brainwave]] signals." This is the direct precursor of the SAN [[eeg-biofeedback-self-reflective-loop|EEG biofeedback self-reflective loop]] and of the [[consciousness-as-neurofeedback-loops|consciousness-as-neurofeedback-loops]] page; the conceptual move it makes — that consciousness is *[[gh-a0206z|self]]-observable oscillatory pattern* — is the seed of the [[recursive-neural-array-perception|recursive neural-array perception]] reading.

Third, the 2017 talk identifies the *EEG-in-XR pipeline* as a real product line: Android Jones's Microdose VR built Muse EEG into HTC Vive headbands at AWE 2017, and [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] documents this as evidence that EEG-driven XR was already shipping. The SAN-side descendants are [[eeg-vr-biofeedback|EEG and VR biofeedback]], [[eeg-xr-neurofeedback-bci|EEG + XR neurofeedback BCI]], and [[brainwave-driven-photic-stimulation|brainwave-driven photic stimulation]], each of which formalises a piece of what the 2017 talk only sketched.

Fourth, the talk cites three then-current neuroscience advances — [[connectome]]-specific harmonic waves, facial identity assembly, wireless transcranial magnetic stimulation — as the scientific context that informed the developing framework. The [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|cortical traveling waves]] line and the [[oscillator-environmental-encoding|oscillator environmental encoding]] page are the SAN-side absorptions of the [[connectome]]-harmonic-wave material; the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differentials]] primitive and the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|DPWDR]] construct are how those harmonic-wave findings get reinterpreted as carriers of conscious content rather than as substrate-level descriptions.

## Post-SAN

The 2017 article sits *upstream* of much of the published EEG-in-VR / consumer-BCI literature, so the "Post-SAN" relation here runs forward in time rather than backward. The [[connectome]]-specific harmonic-wave research the 2017 article cited (Atasoy and colleagues) has since been absorbed into mainstream neuroscience; the SAN-side reformulation in [[oscillator-environmental-encoding|oscillator environmental encoding]] and [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|cortical traveling waves]] remains the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-relational]] reading rather than the harmonic-amplitude reading.
