# Microtubule Quantum Effects

**Microtubule quantum effects** refers to quantum-mechanical or quantum-optical behavior studied in tubulin and microtubule preparations. The phrase is not equivalent to "quantum computation in the brain" and does not by itself establish [[orch-or|Orch OR]] or consciousness.

## What has been measured

Kalra and colleagues reported wavelength-dependent electronic energy migration in microtubules and reductions in the measured effect after exposure to isoflurane or etomidate in an in-vitro preparation ([ACS Central Science, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.2c01114)). Babcock and colleagues reported collective ultraviolet-emission behavior in large tryptophan networks assembled in biological architectures including microtubules ([Journal of Physical Chemistry B, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.3c07936)).

These are physical findings about particular preparations, excitation regimes, observables, and timescales. They do not yet show that the measured states occur with the proposed function in intact neurons, survive at the required scale in vivo, alter a defined neural representation, or generate conscious experience.

## Classical electrical oscillations are a separate finding

Cantero and colleagues measured spontaneous ionic-current oscillations in voltage-clamped rat-brain microtubule bundles, including a prominent fundamental near 39 Hz, and related activity in membrane-permeabilized cultured hippocampal neurites ([Scientific Reports, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30453-2)). This is important microtubule biophysics, but it is not by itself evidence for quantum computation. Nor does frequency overlap establish that microtubules generate intact-brain gamma synchrony or scalp EEG.

A later multiscale electrokinetic transmission-line model explored how surface ionic layers and nanopore coupling could support propagation and oscillation ([Scientific Reports, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-24920-w)). Because it develops the same experimental research line, it should be treated as mechanistic modeling rather than independent in-vivo confirmation.

## Fractal time-crystal proposal

The 2026 proposal that microtubules are "fractal time crystals" assembles reported oscillatory phenomena across hertz-to-terahertz scales and connects them to Orch OR ([Journal of Consciousness Studies, 2026](https://doi.org/10.53765/20512201.33.1.211)). Its publication establishes a citable theoretical proposal, not the empirical validation of a biological time crystal, an intact-neuron decoder, or a consciousness mechanism.

## Required translation chain

To support a neural computation claim, research must establish:

`physical state -> persistence in living neuron -> selective cellular readout -> circuit consequence -> behavioral or report consequence`

To support Orch OR, the evidence must additionally identify the proposed orchestration and objective-reduction event. To support a SAN interpretation, it must show how the resulting cellular difference enters a receive-transform-re-express-use pathway. Neither framework gains support merely because the word "quantum" applies to the underlying matter.

## Scale and observer boundary

A quantum-optical event is a micro-scale physical event. It is not a microscopic observer. SAN's observer-action account remains distributed across biological receivers, recurrent networks, body state, and action. Any microtubule contribution must be traced into that larger causal organization.
