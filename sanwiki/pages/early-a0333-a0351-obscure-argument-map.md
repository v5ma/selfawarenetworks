## A 2011 distributed multisensory rendering ancestor

The May 13, 2011 `a0348z.md` note begins with an engineering analogy: a stereographic or 3D camera
does not merely store one undifferentiated object. Different sensors contribute different parts and
relations, and a learning system uses their temporal-spatial sequences to recognize and predict.
Micah then applies the same problem to biological perception:

```text
different sensory receptors and neural populations
-> different temporal-spatial feature contributions
-> learned cross-layer relations and partial-pattern completion
-> prediction of the larger pattern
-> one continuously updated multisensory state
-> observer-level recognition and action
```

The phrase "camera" is an analogy for capture and transformation, not a claim that one neuron
records a photograph. The observer is the distributed system whose state and action change when
the sensory relations change. The note therefore anticipates the later SAN account of cells as
internal receivers and transmitters, while avoiding a little viewer behind the resulting pattern.

The internal date establishes an owner-held chronology claim. The first tracked Git fixation is
June 12, 2022, commit `ac553d2f536baadb0c70115976153d990ea0a8cb`; that commit does not prove
public custody in 2011.

## Coincidence becomes a content-bearing update

The November 23, 2014 `a0338z.md` note proposes that inputs arriving close together can be treated
as one recognized pattern and that the resulting transient event can circulate through a larger
network. A modern SAN statement keeps the constructive mechanism and turns its fixed historical
numbers into measured variables:

```text
learned dendritic and membrane state
+ multiple signed presynaptic contributions
+ receiver-specific arrival-time and phase relation
-> nonlinear cellular response
-> phasic population update
-> downstream recognition, prediction, or route recruitment
```

The later PWD model adds waveform, duration, magnitude, relative phase, receiver state, and tonic
context. "High gamma" is one candidate regime rather than the only possible carrier, and "soliton"
is a physical hypothesis requiring propagation evidence. The historical note is important because
the coincidence-to-propagated-update operation predates the mature vocabulary.

When this process recruits action, the physiological chain remains explicit:

```text
phase-compatible neural coincidence
-> effective population drive and motor-pool recruitment
-> motor-neuron action potentials
-> acetylcholine release at neuromuscular junctions
-> muscle-fiber action potentials and calcium release
-> ATP-powered actin-myosin contraction
-> movement or vocal action
```

Neural timing supplies the control and recruitment signal. Muscle ATP supplies the mechanical
energy. Vocal behavior additionally coordinates respiratory, laryngeal, and articulatory pools.

## Procedural rendering with state and history

In the November 9, 2013 discussion preserved by `a0345z.md`, Micah proposes that the same input can
recruit procedures that generate a similar temporary memory or brain canvas. He also describes
parts of a world model as generated when needed rather than permanently displayed in full detail.

Jordan Tribby's replies add requirements that improve the resulting theory: reconstruction depends
on present state, learning history, available resources, and the observer-selection problem. The
joined modern model is therefore:

```text
current input + learned structure + body and task state + reconstruction history
-> bounded procedural reinstatement
-> temporary perceptual or memory state
-> prediction and action
-> returned evidence that updates the next reconstruction
```

This is a stronger Neural Rendering route because it specifies why nominally identical inputs need
not generate identical internal states. The source is multi-speaker; Micah's and Jordan's claims
remain separately attributed.

## Intracellular patterns require a circuit receiver

`a0334z.md` shows that SAN was already comparing CaMKII and microtubule-memory proposals with
criterial causation in 2014. Craddock, Tuszynski, and Hameroff modeled a geometric match between
extended CaMKII kinase domains and microtubule lattice neighborhoods. That paper proposes a
molecular encoding route; it does not establish long-term memory storage or conscious readout in
living brains.

SAN's useful addition is the scale-translation contract:

```text
candidate intracellular pattern
-> identified molecular or membrane receiver
-> ion-channel, voltage, burst, or plasticity change
-> circuit recruitment
-> behavior or declared conscious endpoint
```

This permits microtubules to matter without making them the exclusive source of consciousness and
without erasing the larger dendritic, cellular, circuit, and field-cell architecture.

Primary route:

- [Craddock, Tuszynski, and Hameroff, 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22412364/)

## BCI prediction, reciprocity, and full-field sensing

`a0340z.md`, internally dated July 15, 2012, proposes training Numenta- or Google-Brain-like models
against EEG and fMRI measurements, using model error to improve BCI control, and eventually forming
reciprocal links among people and machines. The modern engineering form is a closed-loop prediction
problem:

```text
multimodal measurement -> person-specific state estimate -> predicted response
-> controlled feedback or stimulation -> measured consequence -> model update
```

`a0351z.md` asks whether laser Doppler measurements and holographic imaging could be combined for
faster full-field observation. The cited 2021 paper validates amplitude and phase reconstruction
of structural surface vibration using high-speed digital holography, with scanning laser Doppler
vibrometry as a reference. It provides an engineering operation and validation design. A brain BCI
would still need optical penetration, safety, tissue-motion, hemodynamic, skull, neural-specificity,
and in-vivo validation.

Primary route:

- [Meteyer et al., 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-86371-3)

## Connectome and artificial-synapse comparison inputs

`a0344z.md` points to rich-club architecture: highly connected hubs can participate in a large
fraction of network communication. This gives SAN a structural routing comparison, not one central
observer or one fixed global workspace. Scale, tractography limits, directionality, and effective
connectivity remain explicit.

`a0347z.md` preserves a third-party report on a magnetic Josephson-junction artificial synapse.
The primary paper demonstrates a trainable superconducting device with embedded state, spiking,
and sub-attojoule event energy under cryogenic conditions. That is a serious hardware comparison
for Artificial Neurology, while biological synaptic plasticity and the SAN receive-transform-
project architecture remain different explanatory levels.

Primary routes:

- [van den Heuvel and Sporns, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3539-11.2011)
- [Schneider et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5786439/)
