# Prediction

![Prediction memory-prediction-rendering in Self-Aware Networks](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/prediction/prediction-memory-prediction-rendering-20260630-v1.png)

This plate renders the page's SAN mechanism: prediction is memory-prediction-rendering, where engram traces unfold as expected sensory and motor trajectories, phase deviations against a tonic reference rhythm carry prediction error, and criterial threshold crossing updates attention and consciousness.

**Prediction** is the load-bearing computational primitive of [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] — the operation by which a [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|brain treated as a memory-prediction machine]] continuously generates expected sensory states and motor outcomes from its [[engrams|engram]] library, then renders the world in terms of those expectations rather than reading raw input. Within SAN it is *not* a single feed-forward signal but a [[fractal-pattern|fractal]], hierarchical, [[neural-oscillation-hierarchy|oscillatory]] process distributed across [[cortical-traveling-waves|cortical traveling waves]] and [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]], with [[expectation]], [[attention]], and [[consciousness]] all defined as derived quantities of the prediction loop.

## Position

Prediction in SAN is **memory-prediction-rendering**: the brain selects a stored pattern (an [[engram]]) and lets it unfold as a predicted sensory or motor trajectory, where the rendering "shrinks until it is in sync and thus indistinct from the canvas upon which some other memory-prediction-rendering happens" (`02san--md`, line 77, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/02san.md)). This is the SAN-internal definition of [[consciousness-persistent-rendering|persistent rendering]] — predictions don't "stop" so much as fall into phase-coherence with neighbouring renderings, which is why [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering|consciousness presents as continuous]] rather than as a discrete sequence of frames. The chapter on Friston situates prediction as the engine of [[predictive-motor-self-model|the predictive self-model]]: "this mechanism of prediction helps maintain an internal model of the world, contributing to how we experience perception, cognition" — an internal model whose mismatches with sensory input drive learning and whose [[expectation|expectations]] modulate the very [[senses|sensory]] streams they are predicting (`03san--md`, line 20, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/03san.md)). At the architecture level, "higher-level [[fractal-isomorphic-sensory-arrays|sensory arrays]] manage more abstract cognitive functions like memory and prediction. The synchronization of waves across these hierarchical levels allows the brain to integrate sensory" inputs into coherent percepts (`05san--md`, line 280, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/05san.md)). And at the encoding level, prediction errors are carried not as isolated spikes but as *deviations from a baseline rhythm*: "that deviation can carry a signal (for example, indicating a stimulus change or a prediction error). The theory argues that such phase-based encoding is richer than binary spike codes" (`07san--md`, line 54, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)). Prediction is therefore inseparable from [[neural-phase-pattern-propagation|phase-pattern propagation]] and [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|dominant phase wave differentials]] — it lives in the [[tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame|tonic oscillatory reference frame]] rather than in spike rasters.

## Post-SAN

The chapter-four engagement with *Stimulus history, not [[expectation]], drives sensory prediction errors in mammalian [[cortex]]* is the cleanest external-paper anchor in the available evidence: SAN's response is to fold the stimulus-history finding into a Fractal Oscillatory Predictive Coding (FOPC) framing, in which prediction error is not subtracted against a fixed [[expectation]] but against a [[fractal-pattern|fractally]] structured baseline of [[fractal-cooperative-oscillations|cooperative oscillations]] (`04san--md`, line 3, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/04san.md)). Compared with mainstream predictive coding — in which prediction errors propagate up a [[cortical-columns|cortical column]] hierarchy as residuals — SAN's distinguishing claim is that prediction is encoded in *phase relationships* across [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|traveling neural oscillations]] and [[gamma-oscillation|gamma]]/theta substructure, so that the same neuron's contribution to a prediction depends on its phase relative to a [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillatory]] reference (`07san--md`, line 54, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)). This makes a TESTABLE prediction the published binary-spike formulations do not: phase-locked perturbations of the reference rhythm should bias prediction-error magnitudes in a sign-controlled way, even when net spike counts are matched. Where SAN overlaps with Friston-style [[awareness-as-predictive-myth|predictive accounts of awareness]], it diverges by tying prediction strength explicitly to [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]] thresholds rather than to free-energy minimization alone.
