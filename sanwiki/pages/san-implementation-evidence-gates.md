# SAN Implementation Evidence Gates

This page defines what an artificial SAN or NAPOT experiment may claim. It prevents a successful
demonstration, a familiar metaphor, or conformance to SAN terminology from being promoted into a
biological or consciousness result.

## Gate 1: Functional performance

The system reconstructs a declared body or world variable, predicts an action consequence,
controls an effector, transfers to a held-out setting, or adapts after a perturbation. Report the
exact endpoint, uncertainty, resource use, and failure cases. Do not rename competent control as
self-awareness.

## Gate 2: Causal module support

A candidate mechanism must beat or complement a strong matched replacement. Comparisons preserve
information access and match parameter count, state dimension, compute, data, tuning opportunity,
and calibration as closely as possible. Separately retrain the replacement and intervene on the
already trained candidate; those tests answer different questions.

A null result has a declared consequence. If generic recurrent state matches tonic local context,
retain recurrence and retire the unit-local necessity claim. If attention matches adaptive routing,
use the simpler explanation. If an equal-dimensional vector matches named event fields, retire the
special interpretation for that task.

## Gate 3: Versioned SAN conformance

A conformance profile declares:

- receiver boundaries and local state;
- route sources, targets, delays, gains, and update order;
- event dimensions, units, missing values, and normalization;
- body, sensor, effector, and capability estimates;
- action and returned-consequence pathways;
- learning, topology-change, and rollback rules; and
- mandatory performance, calibration, resource, safety, and replication criteria.

Conformance is architectural description, not empirical support. A supported profile must also
pass the preregistered matched comparisons, interventions, held-out worlds, precision thresholds,
and sealed replication.

## Gate 4: Consciousness assessment

Neither behavior, self-description, recurrence, oscillation, synchrony, neural resemblance,
three-dimensional rendering, self-modeling, nor SAN conformance is sufficient evidence by itself.
Assessment must compare indicators derived from multiple consciousness theories, adverse and null
evidence, uncertainty, possible welfare consequences, and competing non-conscious explanations.

[Butlin and colleagues' computational-indicator report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708) is one
provisional framework, not a consensus meter and not SAN validation.

## Evidence design

Use generated world families whose hidden causes are known but unavailable to the agent. Freeze
training, validation, and confirmation worlds before final evaluation. Treat independently trained
runs and generated worlds as evidence units; episodes and frames are repeated observations, not
independent replications. Report a metric vector and Pareto frontier rather than one flattering
score.

- [Procgen environment-distribution benchmark](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/cobbe20a.html)
- [Calibration of modern neural networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html)
- [Statistical evaluation of deep reinforcement learning](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/f514cec81cb148559cf475e7426eed5e-Abstract.html)
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1)

## Medical and biological boundary

Biological studies can motivate a candidate variable, such as dendritic location, inhibition,
timing, waveform, or plastic structure. The artificial experiment must still show that the modeled
variable corresponds to the biological operation and adds engineering value. Biological
resemblance is not engineering superiority, and engineering success is not biological validation.
