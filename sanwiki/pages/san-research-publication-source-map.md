# SAN Research Publications and SVGN/VRMA Source Map

Micah Blumberg's [public research index](https://pub.svgn.io/) currently lists 89 numbered papers.
SVGN.io and the historical VRMA/Medium archive contain an additional source family of articles,
interviews, podcast notices, BCI reporting, and SAN explanations. This page maps those sources into
the Encyclopedia without pretending that every publication belongs to SAN or that a shared keyword
proves conceptual coverage.

## What counts as representation

A paper or historical article is represented only when the Encyclopedia records:

1. the exact source identity and date;
2. a direct public source link;
3. the SAN concept or historical stage to which it contributes;
4. whether it is a primary Micah source, later synthesis, external evidence, or cross-domain work;
5. any scientific, medical, provenance, or privacy boundary; and
6. at least one reader-visible route from the relevant concept page.

A title on a publication list is **inventory coverage**, not concept coverage. A page that happens
to use similar words is **candidate topic overlap**, not a source binding.

## The cortical-traffic example

The January 2024 article
[Cortical Dynamics & Neurocircuitry](https://www.svgn.io/p/cortical-dynamics-and-neurocircuitry)
is now bound to [[flow-of-information-in-the-brain|The Flow of Information in the Brain]]. That page
connects the March 2021 recording to the later `a0403z` traffic map and then routes the idea into
[[neural-tuning|Neural Tuning]] and the
[[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|Gamma Consideration Sandwich]].

This binding preserves the historical proposal while distinguishing its teaching geometry from
current anatomy. Laminar, feedforward, feedback, corticothalamic, corticofugal, and reafferent
traffic are heterogeneous and circuit specific. The source's vertical/horizontal and front/back
language is retained as a proposed map, not promoted into a universal cortical wiring law.

## Historical SVGN and VRMA routes

High-value source families include:

- the 2017-2020 [[neural-lace-podcast-source-genealogy|Neural Lace Podcast]] and BCI articles;
- [[brain-as-hard-drive-vrma-2017|The Brain as a Special Kind of Hard Drive]];
- spatial reconstruction and artificial-perception articles, including 2D-to-3D shape recovery;
- fNIRS, EEG, EIT, Stentrode, neurofeedback, and closed-loop interface reporting;
- synaptic unreliability and biological-computation articles;
- the 2024 cortical-traffic, analog biological backpropagation, wave-life motif, and hidden 3D phase-field articles; and
- later neuroscience reviews and SAN source-history articles.

These sources should be mapped by exact article identity after semantic review. Duplicate SVGN,
VRMA, Medium-export, and preserved historical routes must be treated as multiple custody paths for
one work where appropriate, not automatically counted as multiple intellectual sources.

## Current completion boundary

The 89-paper list is authoritative as an inventory, but paper-to-concept mapping is not yet
complete. The current SAN corpus directly names or links only a small subset of those exact paper
identities even though many underlying topics already have substantial pages.

The historical article archive also has unresolved count and identity differences among its story
backlog, article index, and hosted HTML routes. Until duplicate and retained-file reconciliation is
complete, the Encyclopedia cannot honestly claim that every SAN- or neuroscience-relevant SVGN and
VRMA article has been mapped.

The completion task is finite: reconcile article identities, classify relevance, bind each accepted
source to one or more concept pages, create a bridge only when no adequate route exists, and assign
every candidate a terminal mapped, cross-domain, duplicate, source-gated, private, or out-of-scope
disposition.
