# Temporal Interference Patterns

**Temporal Interference Patterns** is a [[self-aware-networks]] term that does double duty: it names the technical phenomenon of multiple oscillatory signals interfering in time to produce a focal envelope (the basis of [[non-invasive-brain-stimulation|non-invasive temporal-interference stimulation]] and the [[neural-lace-podcast-s2e1-jean-rintoul-openeit|EIT-style conductivity-mapped]] deep-brain targeting program), and it names the SAN phenomenological claim that phenomenal experience itself IS a temporal interference pattern in the brain's coupled [[oscillator]] field. The two senses are linked: the same wave-superposition principle that produces a focal stimulation envelope at depth is the principle the SAN reading insists is doing the substrate-level work of conscious experience inside the [[cortex]].

## Position

The SAN position has three components. First, the technical wave-superposition principle is real and independent of the SAN program: two or more sinusoids at slightly different frequencies sum to an amplitude envelope at the difference frequency, and when carried into a conductive medium with multiple injection points the envelope localizes spatially to the region where the carriers reach equal amplitude. The [[non-invasive-brain-stimulation]] article handles the engineering implementation. The [[neural-lace-podcast-s2e1-jean-rintoul-openeit|OpenEIT]] entry handles the conductivity-mapping prerequisite that the [[gh-a0291z|a0291z]] note names: without a per-subject conductivity model you cannot place the envelope precisely. The SAN reading takes this technical apparatus as a working demonstration that interference patterns in time are the ontologically primary phenomenon, not derived effects of point-source neuronal firing.

Second, the same principle operates endogenously in [[cortex]]. The [[wave-interference-attractive-repulsive-forces]] article handles the broader interference-as-force reading; the [[phase-wave-differentials]] and [[dominant-phase-wave-differential]] articles handle the SAN-specific phase-wave-differential primitive that the temporal-interference-patterns slug makes the substrate-level commitment about. The [[cortical-traveling-waves]] and [[wavefront-collision]] articles handle the propagation-and-collision phenomenology in published work. The [[dpwdr]] article handles the dominant-phase-wave-differential read-out that the SAN tomography stack consumes. Read this way, temporal interference is not just a stimulation technique but the substrate-level mechanism by which [[coincidence-as-a-bit]], [[coincidence-detection]], and [[dendritic-coincidence-detection]] do their information work: the bit is detected because the right interference envelope crested in the right dendritic locus at the right phase.

Third, the phenomenological reading collapses the explanatory ladder. Where standard philosophy of mind treats phenomenal experience as something to be explained by identifying its physical correlate, the SAN reading treats the temporal interference pattern AS the phenomenal experience: "phenomenal experience IS the temporal interference pattern — there is nothing additional to explain beyond the pattern's [[gh-a0206z|self]]-referential structure" ([[humans-as-metal-robots-thesis]]). This is the position [[neural-rendering-of-qualia]] and [[oscillatory-qualia-imprinting]] develop further, the [[consciousness-canvas-wave-differential]] and [[fourier-transform-in-consciousness]] articles support, and the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness]] page positions the SAN program against. The [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] reading is the formal statement of what the pattern's "[[gh-a0206z|self]]-referential structure" means in tomographic terms: the pattern is read out by the same array-projection mechanics that produce it.

## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature for the technical sense is the temporal-interference-stimulation (TIS) program associated with Grossman et al. and the Boyden lab at MIT, plus the conductivity-mapping and EIT programs that the [[neural-lace-podcast-s2e1-jean-rintoul-openeit|OpenEIT]] entry connects to. The SAN reading agrees with the engineering content of those programs and adds the substrate-level claim that the same interference-envelope principle operates endogenously in [[cortex]] without external injection — a position the standard TIS literature is silent on but does not rule out. For the phenomenological sense the most directly adjacent literature is the predictive-coding [[consciousness]] program ([[memory-prediction-framework]] tradition), the Tononi integrated-information [[consciousness]] program, and the field-theoretic [[consciousness]] literature (McFadden's CEMI). The SAN reading converges with field-theoretic [[consciousness]] on the substrate-level commitment and diverges on the format: the carrier is not an electromagnetic field per se but the phase-and-frequency interference pattern in the coupled-[[oscillator]] field, with [[ephaptic-coupling|ephaptic-coupling]] as one but not the only inter-cell mechanism. Where this framing makes a falsifiable [[prediction]] the standard reading does not, it is that controlled phase-scrambling of the cortical oscillation pattern — even when total power and spike rates are preserved — should disrupt the structured aspects of phenomenal experience that the temporal-interference-patterns reading attributes to the pattern itself.
