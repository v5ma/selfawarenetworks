The *[[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks* GitHub repository (`v5ma/selfawarenetworks`) is the primary source archive for [[self-aware-network]] (SAN) theory — an academic, science-based theory of mind and consciousness applicable to both biological and [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] systems.

## Repository Purpose

The repo hosts:
- The full [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) theoretical framework, with the 5th Revision released 18 Sep 2022.
- A whitepaper and wiki with the core SAN theoretical structure.
- Notes spanning 2011–2015, audio transcriptions (b####y format, auto-transcribed with known errors), and assorted research fragments.

## Intended Applications

SAN theory is presented as foundational for:
- Scientific research on organic and artificial neurology.
- [[brain-computer-interface|Brain-computer interface]] (BCI) development.
- Life-extension technologies.
- Broader challenges in AI alignment and consciousness science.

## Content Caveats (as of 2022)

The welcome document explicitly notes the raw, unpolished state of the repository: notes require updating, transcription errors exist throughout, and the material "resembles a brain dump." The author flagged November 2022 as a revision target.
