**[[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation|Quantum Gradient Time Crystal Dilation ([[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]])]]**, also called **[[dark-time-theory|Dark Time Theory]]**, proposes that spacetime expansion is proportional to local mass density and that time density — not exotic repulsive force — is the fundamental variable unifying gravity and electromagnetism.

## Core thesis

Mass concentrations increase local time density. Particles near high-mass regions experience more "frames" of time per unit of coordinate time, effectively moving through denser temporal fabric. At the extreme, black hole interiors exhibit time density so great that internal particles move faster than light from an external perspective, rendering them informationally isolated — the event horizon marks where "time density is so dense that a particle's energy is at maximum."

## Hubble Tension resolution

[[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] competes with two mainstream cosmological proposals:

- **Dark Energy** — accelerating space expansion via repulsive force
- **Cosmological Slowing Time** — time itself slowing since the Big Bang

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] argues these two cannot coexist: one requires time to have been slower in the past, the other claims it is slower now. [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] dissolves the contradiction by localizing temporal variation to mass density gradients rather than imposing a global time rate, and claims to better fit Hubble constant measurements across object classes.

## Electromagnetism as gravity

A key unification move: magnetism is reframed as a relational effect of relative motion between charged objects, sharing the same temporal distortion mechanism as gravity. This collapses the electromagnetic/gravitational distinction into a unified temporal geometry.

## CMB reinterpretation

The Cosmic Microwave Background's apparent insufficiency to generate observed large-scale structure (CMB lensing deficit) is attributed to temporal dynamics affecting gravitational interactions rather than dark energy suppression. [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] argues the CMB permits multiple valid three-dimensional interpretations.
