# Neural Spikes

**Neural Spikes** are, in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], the multi-dimensional discrete events whose timing, magnitude, frequency, and phase together carry information — explicitly *not* the binary "flips" that mainstream AI summaries reduce them to. They are the discrete observables on which [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differentials]], [[neural-oscillation|neural oscillation]] phase locking, and [[criterial-causation|criterial causation]] are computed, and the substrate that Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP) reads out into [[synaptic-plasticity|synaptic plasticity]] changes.
