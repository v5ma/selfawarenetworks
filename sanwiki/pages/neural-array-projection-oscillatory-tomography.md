# Neural Array Projection Oscillatory Tomography

> **Boundary note (2026-07-18):** The Oscillatory variant is a naming/revision emphasis, not a separate proof that all relevant processing is wave-based or that glia, immune cells, neurons, cortex, and physics extensions share one identical tomography operation. Treat it as a continuous-dynamics framing of NAPOT, then declare scale, cell type, circuit, measured oscillatory variable, and alternative explanations. See [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries]], [[phase-language-firewall]], and [[static-map-vs-active-render]].

**Neural Array Projection Oscillatory Tomography** is the Revision-5 naming of the NAPOT hypothesis. "Oscillatory" emphasizes continuous and recurrent population dynamics, but it does not mean that all relevant signaling is a traveling wave or that neurons, glia, immune cells, cortical areas, and physics extensions implement one identical tomography process. Current use must name the cell type or population, measured variable, scale, receiver, and alternative account.

## Origin

The Oscillatory rebranding is recorded in the changelog as a discrete authoring event: "a0310z created 'NAPOT 5: [Neural Array Projection](neural-array-projection.md) Oscillatory Tomography Revision 5'" (`00changelog--md`, line 349, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/00changelog.md)) — fixing the term to a numbered fifth revision and locating it inside note `a0310z` of the SAN repository. The crystallisation of the "Oscillatory" word [choice](choice.md) itself is traced by [Micah Blumberg](micah-blumberg.md) back to György Buzsáki's neurophysiology work: a later note explains that "'Cortical Pyramidal Neurons' was a key paper in my thinking that crystalized or kernelized [NAPOT: Neural Array Projection Oscillatory Tomography](napot-overview.md). Specifically the 'Oscillatory' part of NAPOT" (`b0232y--md`, line 7, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0232y.md)) — anchoring the rename to a deliberate alignment with EEG-scale oscillation literature and to the tonic/phasic framing already running through [NAPOT's tomography-not-holography](napot-tomography-not-holography.md) argument. [Micah Blumberg](micah-blumberg.md) states the thesis-claim status of the Oscillatory variant directly when responding to an external researcher: "Your paradigm describes part of NAPOT. [Neural Array Projection](neural-array-projection.md) Oscillatory Tomography. Which is my thesis" (`a0287zstar--md`, line 107, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0287zstar.md)).

## Historical position in the source corpus

The Oscillatory variant frames perception as a brainwide reconstruction operation: "with NAPOT [Neural Array Projection](neural-array-projection.md) Oscillatory Tomography I'm arguing that the human brain is doing an operation similar in concept to Sparse 3D reconstruction" (`a0004z--md`, line 66, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0004z.md)) — equating subjective experience with a continuously-running [neural-tomography](neural-tomography.md) estimator over [recursive neural arrays](recursive-neural-array-perception.md) rather than with a localized image-buffer or [holographic store](napot-tomography-not-holography.md). Crucially, the Oscillatory revision widens NAPOT's scope beyond neurons proper: "NAPOT [Neural Array Projection](neural-array-projection.md) Oscillatory Tomography includes communication with [astrocytes](astrocytes.md), Oligodendrocytes, T-Cells, Microglia, and basically all cellular partners" (`a0011z--md`, line 88, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0011z.md)), folding glia and immune-resident cells into the tomographic substrate that the [COT](cot-cellular-oscillating-tomography.md) and [BOT](bot-biological-oscillatory-tomography.md) descendants then generalize across [all cellular life](cellular-oscillating-tomography.md). Within [the SAN book](self-aware-networks-book.md) the Oscillatory framing is positioned as the [prediction-driven](awareness-as-predictive-myth.md) tomographic engine behind the [distributed-observer account](screen-metaphor-no-inner-viewer.md) and the [multimodal rendering loop](consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop.md), with [criterial causation](criterial-causation.md) selecting which oscillatory phases cross into [conscious experience](oscillator-consciousness-threshold.md). The note set explicitly extends the construct to optical-physics analogues: NAPOT "can potentially incorporate birefringence principles to map signal bifurcation and oscillatory dynamics" (`b0049y--md`, line 31, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0049y.md)) — letting the [fractal](fractal-pattern.md), [cross-scale oscillatory](fractal-oscillation-cosmos.md) vocabulary of NAPOT travel from neuroscience into [QGTCD](qgtcd-overview.md) and [Super Information Theory](super-information-theory.md) without changing its tomographic skeleton.

## Chronology

The earliest dated evidence for the Oscillatory variant in the present clone is 2023 — the NAPOT-5 / Revision-5 marker — although the underlying [NAPOT theory](napot-theory.md) (the "Oscillation" form) had already accumulated several earlier revisions by the time of the rename. By the `GPT2023X` notebook the Oscillatory phrasing is being used to compare NAPOT against fractal retinal organization and other [retinotopic-mirroring](fractal-retinotopic-mirroring.md) work: "the retina throughout its entire structure aligns with the concept of fractal organization and NAPOT ([Neural Array Projection](neural-array-projection.md) Oscillatory Tomography), which is central to the [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) theory" (`GPT2023X--md`, line 26, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2023X.md)). The 2024 GPT thread re-states the Sparse-3D claim verbatim and links NAPOT to [QGTCD](qgtcd-overview.md) integration: "[QGTCD](qgtcd-overview.md) also integrates with the [Neural Array Projection](neural-array-projection.md) Oscillatory Tomography (NAPOT) theory, which deals with how the brain integrates sensory information into coherent models" (`GPT2024Jan1toJan22--md`, line 4012, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2024Jan1toJan22.md)) — positioning the Oscillatory framing as the cognitive bridge between SAN and the [Super Dark Time](super-dark-time.md) / [Dark Time](dark-time-theory.md) physics extensions.

## Established component anchors

- [Takahashi et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-020-0677-8) supports cell-type-specific active dendritic contributions to descending cortical output.
- [Davis et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2802-y) supports behaviorally relevant traveling cortical-wave state.
- [Mante et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12742) supports recurrent population computation in a defined task.

These findings do not validate biology-wide tomography, phase qualia, or a physics extension.
