# Self Awareness

**[Self](gh-a0206z.md) Awareness** in [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks.md) is the closed sensorimotor feedback loop that runs through the brain's [oscillatory](brain-rhythms.md) activity — "the loop we call [self](gh-a0206z.md) awareness, that's what the word '[self](gh-a0206z.md)' is referring to, this active loop" (`a0072z--md`, line 86, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0072z.md)). It is operationalized through [neurofeedback](neurofeedback.md)-style information return, threaded with [strange-loop](strange-loop.md) / Hofstadter framing and Prigogine-style dissipative-systems claims, and grounded in [napot-theory](napot-theory.md)'s account of how [phase-wave-differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md) over neural arrays construct the [perceptual-screen](perceptual-screen.md) and the felt [self](gh-a0206z.md) that watches it.

## Position

Within [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks.md) the position decomposes into three claims. First, *[self](gh-a0206z.md)-awareness is a closed loop, not a thing*: chapter `a0072z.md` makes the active-loop claim explicit (`a0072z--md`, line 86, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0072z.md)), and `Hex_7.md` extends it computationally — "exists in a feedback loop, with sufficient memory, [self](gh-a0206z.md) awareness can be computed, rendered like neural rendering, and the [self](gh-a0206z.md) begins to take form, inside a nervous system that is also a neural network" (`Hex_7--md`, line 1157, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/Hex 7.md)). Second, *the loop is realized as a [strange-loop](strange-loop.md) over a dissipative substrate*: chapter `a0339z.md` reads "we are the [dissipative system](dissipative-system.md) (like tornado) that facilitates this cycle of [self](gh-a0206z.md) aka [self](gh-a0206z.md) awareness" (`a0339z--md`, line 8, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0339z.md)), and `Hex_7.md` lines this up explicitly with Prigogine and Hofstadter — "We are all [dissipative systems](dissipative-systems.md), as Ilya Prigogine argued, and we are all Strange Loops as Douglas Hofstader argued" (`Hex_7--md`, line 493, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/Hex 7.md)). ## Post-SAN

Compared to mainstream literatures on [self](gh-a0206z.md)-awareness, the SAN position has three distinguishing commitments. First, against [attention](attention.md)-schema accounts ([attention-schema-theory](attention-schema-theory.md), Graziano), SAN does not reduce [self](gh-a0206z.md)-awareness to a single [self](gh-a0206z.md)-model running on top of brain activity; it treats it as a [holographic-attention-schema](holographic-attention-schema.md) over [distributed-phase-representations](distributed-phase-representations.md), where the [self](gh-a0206z.md) is the rendered interference pattern of [cortical-traveling-waves](cortical-traveling-waves.md) and [gamma-oscillations](gamma-oscillations.md). Second, against default-mode-network accounts of [self](gh-a0206z.md)-referential cognition (which the corpus quotes — "the [default mode network](default-mode-network.md)... is involved in [self](gh-a0206z.md)-awareness and [consciousness](consciousness.md)" — `NotebookML_6_Collection--md`, line 839, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/NotebookML 6 Collection.md)), SAN treats DMN activity as one expression of the broader oscillatory loop that [napot-theory](napot-theory.md) specifies. Third, against Hofstadter's purely symbolic [strange-loop](strange-loop.md) reading, SAN keeps the recursive structure but anchors it in physical [phase-wave-differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md) and [criterial-causation](criterial-causation.md) thresholds — Hofstadter's loop becomes computable in [napot-sentient-ai-architecture](napot-sentient-ai-architecture.md) only when it is rendered like neural rendering (`Hex_7--md`, line 1157, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/Hex 7.md)). Where SAN goes furthest is the testable prediction that disrupting [neurofeedback](neurofeedback.md) return — or any of the [oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration](oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration.md) mechanisms — should attenuate the felt loop without removing the [connectome](connectome.md) structure that supports it; conversely, neurofeedback training should expand the loop's bandwidth (`a0062z--md`, line 20, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0062z.md); `core-set--Chicken_Cloud`, line 35, transcript, 2012).
