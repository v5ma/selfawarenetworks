## Crossmodal relations before the name

Different receptors produce different signals. The useful question is how a learned relation such
as letter order, object shape, location, or expected action can remain usable when its carrier
changes from vision to touch, sound, or another sensory route.

```text
different receptor and pathway
-> local sensory representation
-> learned relation to object, sequence, or action
-> receiver-specific transformation
-> re-expression through recognition, prediction, or movement
```

Only after that operation is clear does the Wiki name it **Crossmodal Pattern Invariance and
Re-Expression**. The term does not mean identical activity across modalities. It marks the joined
SAN claim that task-relevant relations can survive biologically constrained transformation and be
made usable again downstream.

## Sparse foveated networking as an engineering ancestor

`a0107z.md` proposes sending only the behavior updates relevant to a viewer's position, gaze,
head direction, and proximity. A local model reconstructs intervening behavior, graphics are
rendered locally, and live events are separated from historical state.

This design clarifies an engineering solution to the same resource problem faced by active
perception: a system can maintain a useful current perspective without transmitting or rendering
every detail at equal resolution. It does not establish that neural tissue uses peer-to-peer
shards, the same loss function, or the same data packet.

## Lens, retina, and field-cell imagery

`a0112z.md` is internally dated November 7, 2021. Its lens-and-retina comparison is best preserved
as a functional image: recurrent central structures can generate changing signals while spatially
distributed cortical populations receive, transform, and use them. There is no cortical retina,
central projector, literal light, or hidden viewer. The observer operation remains the distributed
system's sensitivity to and use of its own changing state.

## Active hardware and identity

The later section of `a0116z.md` proposes that selfhood depends on cycling activity across active
components and asks what happens when new hardware becomes functionally integrated. This supports
three separable questions:

1. Which components causally participate in a current conscious episode?
2. When does a coupled external system become part of a temporary cognitive process?
3. When does extension alter, suppress, or replace the longer-lived identity supported by memory
   and embodied continuity?

The source does not settle those questions, but it supplies an early joined genealogy for the
Field-Cell Self, Extended Brain, and virtual-mind routes.

## Artificial labor and ecological control

`a0109z.md` is valuable because it notices that a benevolent objective can damage a dynamic system
when optimization ignores adaptation, predator-prey relations, resource limits, or downstream
effects. The Artificial Neurology route should therefore distinguish an agent's stated goal from
its model of the system, intervention policy, feedback, uncertainty, and corrigibility.
