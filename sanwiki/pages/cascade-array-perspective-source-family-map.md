-> local array detection
-> state- and reference-dependent transformation
-> downstream re-expression
-> recurrent correction during movement
-> memory or action consequence

prediction of these transformations beyond firing rates and generic recurrence.

## Claim boundary

The archive establishes that Micah recorded a cascade-of-arrays proposal in June 2022. It does not
show that arrays form a literal holodeck, that synchrony alone produces consciousness, or that the
speculative physics branches are established.
