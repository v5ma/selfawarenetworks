# TCNR

It stands for Tomographic Coherence-based [[neural-rendering|Neural Rendering]], sits as a successor [[gh-formulation|formulation]] to [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography]] in the [[oca-overview|Oscillatory Computational Agency]] line, and treats [[high-dimensional-coherence]] as an active integrator rather than an epiphenomenal signature of activity.

## Position

The core formal model treats the substrate as a graph G of coupled [[oscillators]] with band-limited phases and runs three operators jointly: a generative path `yt = HΘ(st) + ηt` that takes hidden state `st` to measurements `yt` through a possibly-nonlinear projection HΘ where dim(st) >> m is underdetermined, a stitching operator S that aligns phases across nodes and bands by minimizing a wrapped phase-field functional Rφ that tolerates lawful traveling-wave gradients while penalizing incoherent misalignment, and a render step `ŝt = argmin_s ||yt − HΘ(s)||² + λ₁Rcoh(S) + λ₂Rcf` that performs variational inversion under a coherence prior and a counterfactual prior. A substrate "realizes TCNR" when it repeatedly computes `ŝt` while sustaining sufficient multi-band coherence to achieve both low projection error and reliable control or [[prediction]]. This is the position from which [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography]] (NAPOT array projections), [[phase-wave-differentials]] and [[coincidence-as-a-bit]] (the stitching operator S and the coherence reward Jcoh), [[cortical-traveling-waves]] and [[wavefront-collision]] (the wrapped phase functional Rφ with delay field b^(ω)), [[cellular-oscillating-tomography]] (local renders within `ŝt` and counterfactual surrogate), and [[bot-biological-oscillatory-tomography]] (coupling HΘ with coherence-gated evidence and policy evaluation) are mapped onto a single formal scaffold.

The Tomographic [[consciousness]] Index (TCI) is the dimensionless cross-substrate score the framework defines for comparing a system against surrogate nulls: `TCI(t) = α·fMI_Δ(ŝt; yt:t+Δ) + β·Σω wω·log det(C^(ω)_t + εI) + γ·Ĉ(Θ|ŝt) − δ·fPE(t)`, with all components standardized against three null classes (phase-scrambled, time-shifted, coupling-decoupled) and a default decision rule that non-zero [[consciousness]] is claimed in a window if TCI(t) > 0. The fMI predictive-mutual-information term reads render-versus-future-projections agreement; the log det C^(ω) term reads band-limited multi-site coherence as a log-volume; the Ĉ(Θ|ŝt) counterfactual-capacity term reads the local [[attractor]] or policy repertoire and admits three preregisterable options (`log|Aκ(ŝt)|`, `rankτ(Jf(ŝt))`, or `Div(π(·|ŝt))`); and the −fPE term subtracts normalized [[prediction]] error. The position is that the joint score, not any single component, is what discriminates conscious rendering from coherent-but-blind synchronization.

The Six Necessary Conditions (SNC) gate `G6(t) = min(x1…x6)` is the second formal layer and lists C1 band-limited coherence (log det C^(ω) exceeds null), C2 lawful traveling-wave routing (phase-gradient directionality and entropy, PGD/PGE), C3 closed feedback loops (transfer entropy ŝt → yt:t+Δ), C4 memory integration via hysteresis (MHI plus [[atp]] exceeding null), C5 agency or counterfactual capacity (local policy or [[attractor]] repertoire non-trivial), and C6 dynamic inference (predictive MI minus PE exceeds null). The conditions are framed as "jointly necessary, not sufficient," which means a single-mode reflex scoring near zero on C5 blocks SNC even with high coherence and prevents the index from collapsing into trivial-synchrony false positives. The companion paper [[computational-proof-of-god]] reuses a parallel six-part gate but labels it "Six Conditions for Subjective Conscious Experience" and treats those conditions as jointly sufficient operational [[criteria]]; the TCNR slug holds the stricter "necessary but not sufficient" reading.

The [[super-information-theory|SIT]] integration layer adds the [[coherence-field|coherence field]] φ^(ω)(r,t) and pairwise coherences C^(ω)_ij(t), the time-density field ρ(r,t) for local rate of informational events, the informational energy εSIT(t) = ζ · Rcoh(t) · ρ²(t) coupling structure to rate, the path-dependent memory-as-hysteresis Mem(t) = H[{φ^(ω)(·,τ)}_{τ≤t}], the Memory Hysteresis Index MHI as cumulated coherent work, the Coherence Accumulation-Transport Product [[atp]], and the forward arrow of time as the direction of net positive coherence-flux dissipation. The Telos and Embedded Agency layer adds `TH(R) = E[Σ γt (αTCI(t) + βValence(t) − λDissipation(t))]` with `Dissipation(t) = η₁·Jcoh² + η₂·ρ²`, the Telos-TCI monotonicity claim that policy steps increasing TH also increase expected TCI, the Alignment Index ALI as `corr(TCI(t), Valence(t))` on held-out windows, and the embedded-agency claim that local renderers — brains, biofilms, AIs — optimize Telos through coherence-field hysteresis updates. Dendritic Vector Embeddings (DVE) implement similarity-gated access to the [[coherence-field|coherence field]] and are the SAN-side connection to [[semantic-tokens-as-vector-geometry|AI token generation]], which is read as a discrete trace of the same render loop.

## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature most directly adjacent to TCNR is the [[free-energy-principle]] tradition exemplified by Karl Friston's variational-inference reading of perception as approximate posterior inference on a generative model; the predictive-coding traditions descended from Rao-and-Ballard and Bastos-et-al; and the [[consciousness]]-measurement programs centered on phi-style integrated-information indices. TCNR converges with the variational tradition on the form of the render step (`ŝt = argmin_s ||yt − HΘ(s)||² + λ₁Rcoh(S) + λ₂Rcf` is recognizably a variational inversion under a structured prior) and disputes it on three specifics: it makes the prior explicitly a coherence prior over phase fields rather than a generic complexity prior; it adds a counterfactual prior `Rcf` and a counterfactual-capacity term in the score that the standard variational reading does not require; and it commits to underdetermined projections (dim(st) >> m) and multi-band oscillatory phase as substrate-level requirements rather than implementation details. Where TCNR makes a falsifiable cross-substrate [[prediction]] the standard reading does not, the source spells out concrete plant predictions (P1 hydrotropism-defense coupling: TCI rises with band-limited coherence along the conduction path; P2 perturbational tomography: rotating line-integral stimuli yield phase-locked traveling waves) and microbial/biofilm predictions (M1 coupling attenuation reduces long-range synchronization and TCI; M2 synthetic coherence imposes weak spatially patterned phase-locking without changing mean power and raises TCI), with adversarial controls handling volume conduction, power mimics, synthetic synchrony without repertoire, and trivial global limit-cycle synchrony. The "TCNR is not panpsychism" stance is operational: it requires three jointly necessary substrate conditions (underdetermined projections, multi-band coherent integrator, nontrivial counterfactual capacity) that the [[prediction]] set explicitly probes for in plants, biofilms, and adversarial controls.
