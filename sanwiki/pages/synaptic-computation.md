# Synaptic Computation

**Synaptic Computation** is the [Self-Aware Networks](./self-aware-networks.md) claim that the synapse is not a passive relay or a stochastic gate but an active computational element whose specific [chemical synapse](./chemical-synapse.md) dynamics -- [multi-vesicle release](./multi-vesicle-release.md) timing, receptor-insertion plasticity, action-potential waveform shaping, and cell-specific frequency variation -- constitute a unique-per-cell computational signature. It pairs with [dendritic computation](./dendritic-computation.md) under a single chapter heading in the SAN draft series, "An Addressable Coefficient of Variation: Unique Cellular Fingerprints in Dendritic & Synaptic Computation," and that pairing is the structural anchor for SAN's reading of [dendritic coincidence detection](./dendritic-coincidence-detection.md) and [coincidence as a bit](./coincidence-as-a-bit.md) as cell-individuated rather than population-averaged primitives.

## Position

SAN's position is that the addressable coefficient of variation (CV) -- the ratio of standard deviation to mean for a cell's spiking against the local rhythm -- is the right per-cell observable for reading synaptic computation, and that this CV is sculpted jointly by [dendritic computation](./dendritic-computation.md) and synaptic dynamics rather than by either alone. The draft1 statement of the position is explicit: "Dendrites carry out non-linear summation of incoming signals, multiply coincident signals to highlight crucial inputs, and filter out noise or unrecognized stimuli. Such active processing enables dendrites to encode a spatial representation of learned experiences, while synapses -- through activity-dependent plasticity -- modulate the temporal dimension" (`draft1--md`, line 260, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/draft1.md)). The [dendritic spine temporal memory](./dendritic-spine-temporal-memory.md) and [bistable synaptic plasticity](./bistable-synaptic-plasticity.md) mechanisms are then treated as the substrate on which [hebbian plasticity](./hebbian-plasticity.md) acts to install per-cell fingerprints in the [ltp bouton synaptic memory traces](./ltp-bouton-synaptic-memory-traces.md) sense.

A second position, made sharpest in vecB, is that synaptic computation is deterministic where mainstream literature reads it as probabilistic. The vecB exchange contrasts the [multivesicular release synaptic unreliability](./multivesicular-release-synaptic-unreliability.md) reading -- which treats apparent vesicle-release variance as gradient-estimating noise in the Seung 2003 sense -- with the SAN reading that vesicle release is "a deterministic computation correlated with a change in potassium release, representing what the neuron is sensing and transferring" (`vecB--md`, line 1218, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecB.md)). Under this framing the CV is informative rather than noisy, and [multi-vesicle release spatial encoding](./multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding.md) and [multivesicular release spatial information](./multivesicular-release-spatial-information.md) become the formal carriers of the per-cell signature.

The third position couples the synapse-level computation to the action-potential-waveform layer above it. Draft1 ties the addressable CV to potassium dynamics: "Potassium levels and [action potential duration](./action-potential-duration.md) (APD) also play critical roles in shaping this individuality. By influencing the amount of time that voltage-gated calcium channels remain open, potassium channels help dictate how many vesicles of [neurotransmitter](./neurotransmitter.md) will be released during an [action potential](./action-potential.md)" (`draft1--md`, line 262, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/draft1.md)). That coupling makes [action potential duration](./action-potential-duration.md), [action potential waveform encoding](./action-potential-waveform-encoding.md), [action potential magnitude](./action-potential-magnitude.md), and [action potential magnetic phase field](./action-potential-magnetic-phase-field.md) structural neighbors of synaptic computation rather than separate topics, and it ties the synaptic level to the [phase wave differentials](./phase-wave-differentials.md) and [cortical traveling waves](./cortical-traveling-waves.md) phenomena one scale up.

A fourth position generalizes the per-cell fingerprint claim across cell types. Draft1 frames [place cells](./place-cells.md) in the [hippocampus](./hippocampus.md) and fibroblasts with distinct calcium-wave oscillations as instances of the same per-cell variation principle, with the consequence that "no two cells ever align perfectly in their oscillatory behavior" (`draft1--md`, line 266, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/draft1.md)). The coincidence-operated tomography (COT) extension then treats this fingerprint as legible at the network level via [biological oscillating tomography](./biological-oscillating-tomography.md) and [NAPOT neural array phase tomography](./napot-neural-array-phase-tomography.md), so synaptic computation is the bottom of a stack whose top is [neural array projection](./neural-array-projection.md).

## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature most directly adjacent to this concept is the gradient-estimation reading of synaptic stochasticity, exemplified by the Seung 2003 and Fiete-et-al 2007 line of work that vecB explicitly engages: those papers treat probabilistic [multi-vesicle release](./multi-vesicle-release.md) as a substrate for finite-difference gradient estimation across the synapse, which lets a network approximate gradient descent if it can wait long enough. The SAN position concedes the formal result but disputes the interpretation, arguing that what the gradient-estimation reading sees as noise is in fact a per-cell deterministic computation whose CV is addressable and informative -- a position that converges with the [multivesicular release synaptic unreliability](./multivesicular-release-synaptic-unreliability.md) and [ltp bouton synaptic memory traces](./ltp-bouton-synaptic-memory-traces.md) reframings. Where SAN's position makes a falsifiable [prediction](./prediction.md) the gradient-estimation reading does not, it is that controlled perturbation of [synaptic ion channel tone](./synaptic-ion-channel-tone.md) and potassium dynamics should produce CV shifts that are predictable from the cell's individual [dendritic morphology](./dendritic-morphology.md) and [dendritic pattern recognition](./dendritic-pattern-recognition.md) history, not just from population statistics. The [synaptic computation LTP](./synaptic-computation-ltp.md) page handles the [LTP](./ltp.md)-specific corollaries; this page is the umbrella claim that synaptic computation is per-cell, deterministic, and CV-addressable.
