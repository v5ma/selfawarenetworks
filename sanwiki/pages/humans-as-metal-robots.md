Humans are "metal robots" in a rigorous sense: neurons compute via charged metal ions (Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺) in aqueous solution, generating voltage and EM signals that constitute [[consciousness]] as deterministic electrochemical computation.

## The Electrochemical Substrate

Cells separate metal ions across membranes. Ion flow triggers action potentials along axons and dendrites. Neurotransmitters adjust firing thresholds by shuttling positive/negative charges between cells. Synapses act as coincidence detectors — firing only when precise temporal conditions are met. This is the physical layer underlying all [[self-aware-networks]] dynamics.

## Discrete vs. Continuous Processing

A paradigm shift challenged fifty years of doctrine: rather than analog continuous processing, the brain appears to use **discrete representation** — encoding information in packets, more like digital computation than a gradient field. This connects to [[napot]]'s wave-interference model, where phase-offset timing creates distinct representational states.

## Dendritic Computation

Dendrites are not passive wires but advanced data structures with temporal-spatial memory. They adjust decision criteria locally, making individual dendrites mini-processors within the larger [[self-aware-networks]] hierarchy. This multiplies the computational depth of a single neuron far beyond simple integrate-and-fire models.

## Convergent Evolution with AI

DeepMind and Stanford research found artificial neural networks spontaneously developing grid cells for spatial reasoning — identical to structures in biological brains. The [[mind-code-go-neural-patterns]] analogy applies: both biological and artificial systems discover the same computational patterns from the same pressures.
