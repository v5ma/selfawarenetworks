# SAN Knowledge Graph

The SAN Wiki is the discovery layer between the complete source archive and reader-facing books,
papers, comparisons, and experiments. Its purpose is to make every important SAN idea findable
from the problem it addresses, the terminology later attached to it, the exact sources in which it
developed, and the works that use it.

This graph treats every SAN source as part of the golden corpus. It ranks placement and routing,
not worth.

## Current graph layers

1. **Exact source census:** every file in the governed downloaded SAN repository receives a stable
   source node, family, byte count, SHA-256, title, Git chronology when available, and candidate
   concept mentions. Local custody paths remain in private build records rather than public prose.
2. **Path-stable wiki graph:** wiki page identity is its relative path, not only its basename. This
   prevents pages named `index.md` or `README.md` in different folders from overwriting each other.
3. **Curated concept registry:** reviewed concept hubs carry aliases, source anchors, dates, roles,
   book destinations, and Comparison Wiki routes.
4. **Candidate discovery edges:** lexical matches broaden recall but remain visibly unreviewed.
5. **Reader routes:** concept hubs explain the problem in ordinary language before introducing SAN
   terminology.
6. **Reviewed source-family maps:** coherent source families receive exact episode or document
   roles, dates, processed reader routes, attribution limits, and unresolved identities.
7. **Reviewed concept routes:** source-family anchors connect to governed concept nodes only after
   a human semantic pass; the edge records relevance, not scientific validation.

## What is complete and what is not

The source census makes the complete downloaded repository discoverable. The sixty-one governed
hubs are a curated semantic layer selected because source recovery exposed them as high-value but
weakly routed. They do not exhaust SAN.

Current 2026-07-22 registry state: 1,036 source files, 2,341 indexed reader pages, 377 curated Wiki
pages, 61 governed concepts, 64 reviewed source maps, and 395 files in the semantic-review queue.
The focused registry audit reports zero missing governed concept files, source-map reader files, or
indexed concept/source-map reader routes. Candidate edges and unresolved source identities remain
review work rather than accepted scientific claims.

The next semantic tranches must cover at least:

- GCS, quantitative PWD/DPWDR metrics, and the deeper APD/MVR claim family;
- FRACTAL, NDCA, TCNR, and the stronger molecular or universal EDS extensions beyond the governed
  dendritic/synaptic state bundle;
- remaining sensory systems and the motor/proprioceptive loop beyond the new taste/hearing bridge;
- NerveGear requirements, VR, and artificial neurology beyond the reviewed Neo Mind Cycle,
  states/stages, BCI instrument, and Neural Lace Podcast family spines;
- the remaining Perceptron/Metatron and SAOv9 semantic queues, the wider concept-edge queue, and a
  reference implementation beyond the governed source spine;
- the outward branches to SET, QGTCD, SDT, SIT, and companion papers;
- people, recordings, public events, source versions, scientific references, counterarguments,
  experiments, and publication packages.

A file can be inventory-complete while still semantically unreviewed. The generated coverage report
keeps that difference visible.

- The generated human-readable coverage report remains an internal build receipt until it has a
  sanitized reader projection.
- `knowledge-graph/source-map-registry.json` - reviewed source-family placements and live-reader
  routes, including reviewed concept relevance edges

## Governing boundaries

- A source proves what was recorded, not that its scientific claim is true.
- A Git date proves repository chronology, not event date, influence, or validation.
- A lexical edge proposes a route; it does not establish a semantic relation.
- Comparison Wiki verdicts remain owned by the Comparison lane and are linked, not silently
  recomputed here.
- The book should consume reviewed graph routes and still perform its own narrative and evidence
  work.

## Operating contract

Read [[golden-corpus-contract|SAN Golden Corpus Knowledge Graph Contract]] before extending the
graph. It defines placement roles, accepted versus candidate edges, date discipline, completeness
levels, and the memory-wipe test.

Before correcting a scientific or medical statement, also read
[[source-faithful-correction-intent-recovery-protocol|Source-Faithful Correction and Intent
Recovery]]. The protocol requires preservation of the pre-correction page, recovery of Micah's
probable intended operation from the dated corpus, separate treatment of established evidence and
SAN extension, and retroactive old-versus-new review of earlier correction waves.

The first byte-and-source case study is
[[source-faithful-retro-audit-grid-reference-wave|Retroactive Correction Audit: Grid and Reference
Frames]]. It also recovered a June 2022 magnification/vortex/three-dimensional-grid source branch
that the first scientific rewrite had omitted.
