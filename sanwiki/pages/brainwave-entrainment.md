Brainwave entrainment is the process by which an external rhythmic stimulus — auditory, visual, or electrical — drives cortical oscillations to synchronize at the stimulus frequency. It is one of the oldest non-invasive tools for modulating [[oscillatory-neural-coupling]] and has 50+ years of research behind it.

## Core mechanism

The brain's tendency to match external periodic input is an expression of the same oscillatory synchronization that underlies functional network formation (see [[oscillatory-neural-coupling]]). When an auditory beat at a target frequency is sustained, local networks entrain — their phase locks to the stimulus cycle. This is how **binaural beats** work: a ~10 Hz beat perceived in the [[auditory-cortex|auditory cortex]] when left and right ears receive tones that differ by 10 Hz.

Frequency–function correlates (approximate):

| Band | Range | Associated state |
|---|---|---|
| Delta | 0.5–4 Hz | Deep sleep, slow-wave memory consolidation |
| Theta | 4–8 Hz | Memory encoding, hippocampal replay |
| Alpha | 8–13 Hz | Relaxed [[attention]], sensory gating |
| Beta | 13–30 Hz | Active cognition, focused arousal |
| Gamma | 30–100 Hz | High-level binding, conscious perception |

Sabotaging theta specifically impairs memory formation — a finding that motivated [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s early entrainment experiments and the [[neo-mind-cycle|NeoMindCycle]] project (2012–2014).

## Delivery modalities

- **Binaural beats** — requires headphones; each ear receives a different pure tone
- **Isochronic beats** — monaural amplitude-modulated pulses; no headphones needed
- **Solfeggio / tone sequences** — wide-spectrum tones (e.g., 3-6-9 Hz) without directional encoding
- **tACS** (transcranial alternating current stimulation) — direct electrical entrainment, bypassing the auditory pathway; stronger and more frequency-precise than acoustic methods

## SAN relevance

In [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] theory, information is phase-coded: a neural array both sends and receives phase patterns, and the brain's reading of its own phase state constitutes perception. Entrainment is an external write to that phase register — demonstrating that the phase state is modifiable from outside and that it has causal downstream effects on cognition and affect. That makes entrainment a practical test bed for NAPOT predictions.

## NeoMindCycle lineage

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s [[neo-mind-cycle|NeoMindCycle]] company (2012–2014) applied entrainment protocols (delta→theta→alpha→beta→gamma→back) combined with tACS and tDCS, and eventually brought EEG into WebVR — visualizing live brainwave activity as 3D spheres inside a VR environment.
