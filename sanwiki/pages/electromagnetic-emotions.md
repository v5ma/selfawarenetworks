## Position

Within [[self-aware-networks|SAN]], emotions are not extra data structures painted on top of cognition; they are *configurations of the same electromagnetic substrate* that carries perception. The substrate is the field of [[brainwaves|brainwaves]] — the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] whose [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|dominant rhythms]] (DPWDRs) win pattern competition on the [[tonic-phasic-canvas-of-consciousness|tonic-phasic canvas]]. An emotion is then a *valence-coloured* DPWDR: the same dominance structure that selects perceptual content also carries a charge — felt-good, felt-bad, felt-arousing, felt-quiet — and that charge is what makes the rhythm an *emotion* rather than a neutral percept. This reading is consistent with [[phase-differential-rendering|phase-differential rendering]] (the rendering operation produces qualia, including affective qualia, from differentials), with [[neural-rendering-of-qualia|neural rendering of qualia]] (the rendering pipeline acts on emotional content the same way it acts on sensory content), and with [[consciousness-energy-wave-differences|the energy-wave-difference reading]] of the canvas.

Three implications follow. First, emotions are *physical* in the strong sense — they are electromagnetic moments, with measurable extracellular consequences, not metaphysically separate from oscillatory dynamics. Second, valence is *positional in the phase field*: an emotion's "polarity" corresponds to a phase-shift signature that the substrate can carry, which is why [[gh-a0271z]]'s phrase *electromagnetic wave valence* is doctrinally apt and why [[oscillator-level-awareness]] and [[oscillator-consciousness-threshold]] generalize to affect as well as percept. Third, the *temporal extent* of an emotion is the duration of its DPWDR — short flashes are micro-affects, sustained moods are persistent rhythms — which is the [[emotions-as-spatiotemporal-patterns|spatiotemporal-pattern]] reading expressed in electromagnetic terms.

The chemical side of affect — dopamine, serotonin, adrenaline — is not displaced by this reading; it is *modulatory*. [[dopamine-as-pattern-selector|Dopamine as pattern selector]] biases which patterns become dominant; [[dopamine-effort-motivation]] and [[adderall-dopamine-dynamics]] describe the energetics of that biasing; but the felt emotion *itself* is the resulting electromagnetic configuration, not the chemical that biased the selection. The architecture therefore preserves affective neuroscience's chemistry while relocating the *felt* component to the same wave-difference substrate that carries percept.

## Post-SAN

Against published affective neuroscience the picture is a re-expression rather than a refutation. Constructionist accounts of emotion (Barrett-style) — which deny natural-kind affect categories and instead treat emotions as construals — translate into the SAN frame as the claim that DPWDR signatures are *not* fixed-locus event types but *patterns over the canvas*, with category boundaries drawn by the brain's own context model. Affective valence research that locates polarity in approach/withdrawal asymmetries can be re-expressed as a phase-shift signature in the [[phase-wave-differentials|differential field]]. And classical limbic accounts that anchor emotion in [[amygdala|amygdala]]-and-friends translate into the SAN frame as a statement about *which oscillator populations* most reliably carry the emotional DPWDR — without making those populations the sole site of emotion. Engineering corollaries follow for [[affective-computing]]: the right read-target for emotion is the dominant electromagnetic signature, not a single chemical or a single anatomical region.
