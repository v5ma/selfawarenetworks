# Next-Generation Neuroimaging

Several emerging systems aim to make neural measurement more wearable, mobile, dense, or affordable. Their value must be stated at the level actually demonstrated: a prototype, a particular preparation, a reconstruction result, or a validated clinical measurement. A product goal is not evidence that the system can read thoughts or reconstruct a percept.

## Technology families

**Optically pumped magnetometer MEG (OPM-MEG)** places magnetic sensors close to the head and can permit more movement than conventional fixed-array MEG. It still measures weak magnetic consequences of population currents and retains environmental-noise, forward-model, inverse-model, and localization limits.

**Electrical impedance tomography (EIT)** applies small probing currents, measures boundary voltages, and estimates conductivity or impedance change. Fast neural impedance changes are small. Experimental peripheral-nerve reconstructions do not establish noninvasive whole-brain functional imaging.

**Diffuse optical systems** estimate hemodynamic or optical-property changes. Higher optode density can improve reconstruction, but depth, scalp physiology, motion, and inverse inference remain important.

**Microwave brain imaging** is an engineering research direction involving tissue dielectric contrasts and electromagnetic propagation. Safety standards and device concepts do not establish neural decoding performance.

## SAN experimental relevance

SAN needs measurements that can relate fast population dynamics, anatomy, body state, and behavior without treating any one modality as a complete view. The strongest design is multimodal and hypothesis-specific: define the predicted variable, scale, direction of interaction, behavioral consequence, and expected cross-modal lag before selecting instruments.

AR or VR can supply controlled sensory and motor tasks while wearable sensors record the participant. That creates an experimental setting; it does not make the headset a bidirectional neural interface unless stimulation or validated decoding is actually present.

## Evidence boundary

- Demonstrated: wearable OPM-MEG can record brain activity during movement under controlled conditions.
- Demonstrated: EIT can reconstruct evoked impedance changes in bounded peripheral-nerve preparations.
- Proposed engineering directions: wearable optical, microwave, and multimodal reconstruction systems.
- Not demonstrated by these facts: whole-brain thought reading, direct percept reconstruction, or confirmation of SAN/NAPOT.
