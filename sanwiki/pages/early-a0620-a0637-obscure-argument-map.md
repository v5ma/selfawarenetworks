# Early a0620-a0637 Obscure Argument Map

Sixteen notes in this tranche ask a practical question:

> How can a physical network receive a difference, select a route, reconstruct a learned pattern,
> act on it, and remain the observer of that cycle without adding a hidden viewer?

The notes approach the problem through choice, inhibition, memory, prediction, emotion, shared
experience, consumer neurotechnology, disease research, and artificial minds. This page states each
useful operation in plain language first, then connects it to SAN terminology and current evidence.

## First the problem: signals need routes

A nervous system constantly receives more signals than it can turn into one action. The same
incoming activity can have different consequences depending on:

- which cells are excitable;
- which inhibitory cells are active;
- which dendritic branches are available;
- what was learned before;
- what the body needs now;
- what happened in the preceding cycle.

The system therefore needs more than signal strength. It needs a way to open some routes, close
others, and change that routing as context changes.

`a0631z.md` describes inhibitory cells as train switches. The analogy is useful when translated
carefully:

```text
incoming activity
-> excitation, inhibition, and disinhibition at branch and circuit targets
-> alternative assemblies become more or less reachable
-> one prediction, representation, or action wins relative support
-> feedback changes the next routing state
```

Modern evidence shows that molecularly distinct interneurons can selectively inhibit pyramidal
cells or other interneuron classes. SAN adds a circuit-level Choice proposal: momentary inhibitory
state helps determine which learned route carries a pattern into thought or action. This belongs to
[[neural-path-selection-interneurons-choice]] and
[[inhibitory-routing-signal-processing-grid-graph]].

It is not necessary to call the operation backpropagation. In a living circuit it is better
described as state-dependent gating, disinhibition, predictive suppression, and route selection.

## A prediction must become something another part can receive

Saying that the brain predicts is incomplete. A prediction must be physically expressed and made
available to another receiver.

The later annotation in `a0632z.md` makes this explicit:

```text
learned state forms a prediction
-> the prediction is re-expressed as a signal pattern
-> a later array receives and transforms that pattern
-> match, mismatch, valuation, and action alter the next cycle
```

This is a major bridge from the April 2012 discussion to
[[memory-prediction-rendering-sequence]]. The operation is plain before SAN names are added:
learned structure generates a current pattern that another part of the system can use. Later SAN
calls this repeated physical re-expression and reception a part of neural rendering.

The 2012 source does not need to contain the complete later PWD or NAPOT vocabulary to establish
this ancestor.

## The system itself is the observer

`a0625z.md`, `a0626z.md`, and `a0632z.md` reject the need for a little viewer inside the brain.
Different cells and arrays receive, transform, compare, and transmit different parts of the
process. Their recurrent interaction produces a larger coordinated pattern that can guide report
and action.

```text
many partial receivers
-> learned local transformations
-> recurrent projection and recruitment
-> larger coordinated state
-> selected interpretation and action
-> returned sensory consequence
```

The observer is this coupled operation. Cells can be compared with internal sensors, receivers,
screens, or transmitters only as partial functional analogies. No one cell watches the complete
render.

- [[perceptual-screen-neural-rendering]];

## The player-listener loop

The April 19, 2012 source describes the self through a musical analogy. Neurons and larger
populations both contribute to a pattern and respond to the pattern produced by the group.

```text
play a learned part
-> listen to the combined result
-> alter the next part
-> preserve or change the larger song
```

The analogy earns a recurrent self-model. It does not mean every neuron hears a literal global
sound or that one exact oscillation is a person. The durable point is that selfhood can be
distributed across a recurrent process whose current state affects its own next state.

Split-brain research raises difficult questions about the unity of that process, but does not
settle them by proving that surgery always creates two complete conscious persons. The current Wiki
therefore preserves the source's constructive self-model while marking the personhood inference as
contested.

## Memory is reconstructed in the present

The March 2014 discussion in `a0624z.md` and the later notes in `a0628z.md` and `a0632z.md` ask how a
past event can become present experience again.

The useful answer is not that a perfect film is stored and replayed:

```text
partial cue plus current state
-> recruitment of learned synaptic, dendritic, and circuit constraints
-> reconstruction of a distributed activity pattern
-> current image, feeling, prediction, or action
```

This makes memory state-dependent and compatible with structural plasticity and representational
drift. The reconstructed state can resemble the earlier pattern without being an identical copy.
See [[memory-state-drift-virtual-mind]] and
[[coincidence-to-structure-activity-directed-spinogenesis]].

## Shared experience is an interface problem

`a0624z.md` includes discussion of conjoined twins with a reported thalamic bridge and asks what a
brain-to-brain interface would need to transmit.

The strongest formulation is:

```text
one system forms a state
-> an interface measures a bounded signal
-> the channel transforms and transmits that signal
-> another system integrates it with its own learned state
-> the receiver constructs its own experience and response
```

This is more defensible and more useful than saying a complete subjective experience is copied
intact. Natural shared-sensory cases motivate the question. BrainNet shows that task-relevant
information can be passed among people through noninvasive interfaces. Neither establishes
unrestricted transfer of one person's complete experience.

This belongs to [[multimodal-neuroaffective-xr-closed-loop]] and
[[bci-instrument-atlas]].

## Emotion can be measured without reducing it to one signal

`a0625z.md` asks whether emotions correspond to detectable electrophysiological changes. That is a
legitimate measurement program:

```text
event, memory, bodily need, or appraisal
-> autonomic, endocrine, neuromodulatory, and circuit-state changes
-> changed excitability, timing, synchrony, spectral power, and behavior
-> multimodal measurement and within-person comparison
```

The SAN innovation is not that one EEG feature equals one universal emotion. It is the proposal to
track how distributed biological state changes alter receiving, rendering, valuation, and action
over time. The strongest test combines electrophysiology with behavior, report, autonomic signals,
and context rather than treating one frequency band as a complete emotional code.

## Dopamine changes the probability of action

`a0629z.md` points to a 2022 Quanta article. Its primary research source is Hamilos et al. 2021.
In mice performing a self-timed licking task, slowly evolving dopamine signals predicted when an
animal would move, and optogenetic manipulation shifted the timing probability.

That result supports this bounded chain:

```text
slowly changing neuromodulatory state
-> altered probability of movement initiation
-> changed action timing
```

It does not establish that dopamine is the only go signal, that every threshold crossing produces a
movement, or that the experiment validates multivesicular release. MVR remains a separate
presynaptic information-channel question under [[synaptic-unreliability-mvr-2021]].

## Plasticity supports learned specialization

`a0630z.md` combines personality discussion with examples such as Braille readers and London taxi
drivers. The strongest evidence-backed operation is experience-dependent specialization:

```text
repeated task and sensory demand
-> altered functional recruitment and sometimes structural change
-> improved task-specific performance
-> an individualized but not arbitrary functional profile
```

Braille studies support sensorimotor and cross-modal cortical plasticity. Navigation studies
support experience-related hippocampal differences. Those findings do not validate fixed MBTI
brainwave categories or percentages.

## The 2011-2014 Neo Mind Cycle device spine

`a0633z.md`, `a0634z.md`, and `a0635z.md` preserve early practical work with:

- consumer EEG;
- NeuroSky;
- Mind Workstation;
- Procyon and MindSpa light-and-sound systems;
- CES and Alpha-Stim;
- tDCS as a research and clinical technology;
- DBS as an invasive clinical technology;
- closed-loop audiovisual feedback.

The enduring design problem is:

```text
measure a changing brain-body state
-> transform a selected feature
-> return visual, auditory, electrical, or other stimulation
-> measure the next state and behavior
-> adapt the next cycle
```

This is a real engineering genealogy for [[neo-mind-cycle-neurofeedback-bci-genealogy]]. It does
not depend on calling consumer devices medical grade or presenting historical DIY stimulation as
current guidance. Each device must be separated by invasiveness, target, spatial and temporal
resolution, dose, indication, supervision, evidence, and risk.

## Medical and governance notes become structured questions

`a0620z.md`, `a0622z.md`, and `a0623z.md` are useful when reorganized into explicit ladders.

For treatment:

```text
association
-> candidate mechanism
-> controlled perturbation
-> measured cell or animal outcome
-> human safety and dose
-> controlled clinical outcome
-> replication and current guideline
```

For justice and governance:

```text
causal explanation
-> capacity and responsibility
-> consent and civil rights
-> treatment or rehabilitation evidence
-> proportional public-safety response
-> oversight and appeal
```

Brain injury, disease, metabolism, or developmental history can influence behavior. They do not
turn every offender into one medical category, authorize forced treatment, or erase legal and moral
distinctions.

## Quantum language remains a question until it has a mechanism

`a0637z.md` asks whether observation, absorbed signals, and emitted biological activity connect the
brain with quantum measurement.

A bounded research question is:

> What physical interaction occurs between a measured system, a biological detector, and the
> apparatus, and which part of the result depends on ordinary decoherence, amplification, or
> information transfer?

That question can be investigated without claiming that brainwaves program the universe or that
consciousness is required for wavefunction collapse. The historical speculation remains part of the
genealogy; it does not become evidence merely because later SAN and SIT use information language.

These comparisons confirm that several component mechanisms are legitimate research objects. They
do not turn the complete SAN synthesis into an established result.
