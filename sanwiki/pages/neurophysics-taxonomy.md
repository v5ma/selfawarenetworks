[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s working taxonomy of neuro disciplines that frame the SAN research program:

| Discipline | Concern |
|---|---|
| **Neuroscience** | Neuroanatomy — mapping connectomes, major brain regions. |
| **Computational (neuro)biology** | Functional connectivity — how information is distributed and flows in the brain. |
| **Neurophysics** | Theoretical physics of neuron and brain function at classical and quantum scales. |
| **Neurology** | Treatments and causes of neural dysfunction. |
| **Cognitive neurophysics** | All of the above integrated with cognitive neuroscience. |

The SAN approach is explicitly **cognitive neurophysics**: biology, theoretical physics, and computation as prerequisites, unified toward understanding consciousness as a physical phenomenon.
