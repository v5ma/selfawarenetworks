## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream neuroscientific treatments of [brain rhythms](brain-rhythms.md), SAN takes three positions inflected by Buzsáki's book. First, it accepts Buzsáki's rhythm taxonomy — delta / [theta](theta-oscillations.md) / alpha / beta / [gamma](gamma-oscillations.md) powerbands — as the empirical baseline (`a0007z--md`, line 534, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md)). Second, it specifically commits to the *[binding-by-synchrony](oscillatory-binding.md)* reading Buzsáki advocates and extends it via SAN's [phase-wave differential](phase-wave-differentials.md) machinery — meaning Buzsáki's [coupled-oscillator account](neural-oscillations.md) is preserved but reframed in SAN's relational/differential language ([08san](gh-08san.md) line 50 emphasis on "relational rather than absolute measures of neural" activity). Third, where mainstream rhythm research treats Buzsáki's framework as broadly descriptive, SAN combines it with [Peter Tse's criterial-causation](criterial-causation.md) (`00changelog--md`, line 611, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md)) to produce a *causal* account of how rhythms actually drive cognition — an extension that makes new testable predictions, and one that the recent [cortical-traveling-waves](cortical-traveling-waves-san-submission.md) literature is converging toward independently.
