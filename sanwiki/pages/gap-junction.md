# Gap Junction

**Gap Junction** in the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] reading is the *electrical synapse* — a direct cytoplasmic-bridge connection between cells whose post-synaptic signal is "generally smaller … due to attenuation, and because electrical synapses [do] not employ receptors and because the sign of [the signal is preserved]" ([[gh-a0571z|a0571z]]--md, line 21; [[gh-a0572z|a0572z]]--md, line 31). Inside the corpus it is treated as the *developmental ancestor* of the [[chemical-synapse|chemical synapse]]: "the electrical synapses which are basically every every chemical neuron starts out as a as a gap junction neuron, it starts out as an electrical sites for transitioning to being in some cases, becoming a [chemical synapse]" (original_archive_b0125y--md, line 65). It is also the corpus's link to the [[biological-computation|Michael-Levin-style biological computation]] reading: "Michael Levin says that when two cells have a gap junction and one of the cells is injured and that i[n]jury causes a calcium spike, well the whole network of connected cells is [going to do something]" ([[gh-veca|vecA]]--md, line 1210). At the metaphor level the corpus reads the synapse-as-gap-junction unit as a [[fractal-pattern|fractal]] of the neuron itself — "a synapse which is gap junction between transmitters & receptors can be thought of as a fractal of a neuron, with inhibitory & [excitatory contributions]" — extending the [[functional-fractal-isomorphism|functional-fractal-isomorphism]] / [[neural-functional-fractal|neural functional fractal]] doctrine all the way down to the synaptic interface.

## Position

The corpus uses gap junction in three connected ways. First, as the *electrical-synapse case* in opposition to the [[chemical-synapse|chemical synapse]]: "the amplitude of the electrical signal is generally smaller on the post-synaptic side of the gap junction due to attenuation, and because electrical synapses [do] not employ receptors and because the sign of [the signal is preserved]" ([[gh-a0571z|a0571z]]--md, line 21; [[gh-a0572z|a0572z]]--md, line 31). The reading is sober: gap junctions transmit faster but smaller, do not invoke [[3d-protein-receptor|3D protein receptor]] machinery, and do not invert the sign — three properties that distinguish them from the [[synaptic-computation-ltp|LTP-modulable]], [[synaptic-ion-channel-tone|ion-channel-toned]] [[chemical-synapse|chemical synapse]]. Second, as the *developmental ancestor*: "every chemical neuron starts out as a … gap junction neuron, it starts out as an electrical [synapse before transitioning] to being in some cases [a chemical synapse]" (original_archive_b0125y--md, line 65). This is a load-bearing claim — it makes electrical (gap-junction) coupling the *primitive* form of neural connectivity, with chemical synapses derived from it through development. Third, as the *Levin bridge*: "Michael Levin says that when two cells have a gap junction and one of the cells is injured and that i[n]jury causes a calcium spike, well the whole network of connected cells is [going to do something]" ([[gh-veca|vecA]]--md, line 1210), placing the SAN reading inside the broader [[biological-computation|biological-computation]] / [[every-cell-chemical-neural-network|cell-as-network]] / [[cell-as-chemical-neural-network|cell-as-chemical-neural-network]] / [[cellular-communication-graph-neural-network|cellular-communication-as-graph-neural-network]] frame the corpus develops elsewhere.
