## Transport And Receiver State

The dopamine transporter, DAT, normally supports uptake of extracellular [[dopamine]] into a nerve terminal. Under experimental amphetamine exposure, DAT can also support outward dopamine transport. Kahlig and colleagues observed both exchange-like and channel-like efflux in controlled preparations. The outward dopamine comes from an intracellular pool; it is not a vesicle passing through DAT. Vesicular storage and nonvesicular transport are distinct processes. [Kahlig et al., 2005](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0407737102)

Dopamine then acts through receptors on receiving cells. D1- and D2-family receptors are G-protein-coupled receptors, not ion channels through which dopamine flows into the cell. The receiver inset represents this receptor-to-intracellular-signaling relationship; it does not specify the full consequences in every cell type. Structural work resolves D1 signaling complexes with Gs and D2 complexes with Gi. [Zhuang et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33571431/)

Adderall's pharmacology is not limited to dopamine. The Adderall XR prescribing information describes effects on norepinephrine and dopamine uptake and release, while stating that the mode of therapeutic action in ADHD is not known. A transporter diagram therefore does not establish a complete explanation of either clinical benefit or an individual's adverse effects. [Adderall XR prescribing information, sections 12.1-12.2](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=aff45863-ffe1-4d4f-8acf-c7081512a6c0)

## Connection To SAN

SAN asks how the receiving system's existing state changes what an incoming signal does. In [[gh-a0272z|Micah's source discussion of neural signaling]], intracellular signaling, potassium-channel state, output duration and the consequences for subsequent receivers form a linked argument. Applying that question to dopamine means specifying the receptor, cell, compartment, signaling pathway and measured consequence. It does not reduce motivation to a single reservoir level.

This connects with [[gravity-of-attention]], [[forced-attention]] and [[neurotransmitter]] dynamics as topics for explanation and testing. Subjective focus, fatigue and cognitive flatness are observations to explain, not direct measurements of a depleted dopamine reserve. The SAN connection here is a receiver-state interpretation, not a claim that the cited transport and receptor studies tested SAN as a whole.
