# Early SAN Facebook Genealogy, 2010-2013

This page preserves an early public layer of the intellectual history that later developed into
Self Aware Networks. Thirty authenticated Facebook screenshots record twenty-six dated document
stages under Micah Blumberg's name. Twenty-four concern SAN, neuroscience, consciousness,
Artificial Neurology, prediction, memory, or embodied action. Two concern physics and are retained
as adjacent SIT history rather than counted as SAN mechanisms.

These are **historical source exhibits**, not medical illustrations and not proof that the proposed
mechanisms are correct. Their value is that they show what was being proposed, in what language,
and on what platform-displayed date.

![From Cycling Synchrony to an Active Canvas](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/early-facebook-genealogy/cycling-synchrony-active-canvas-20260811-v1.png)

*Modern companion figure for the July 24, 2013 stage, not a reproduction of its historical
screenshot. Ongoing state, phase-sensitive information, excitation, inhibition, and recurrent
population interactions are established components. Their proposed organization as a
receiver-relative Phase-Wave Differential mechanism that updates a distributed rendering is the
SAN hypothesis. The memory, perceptual-report, and motor icons denote population-level
consequences; they are not direct projections from one generic neuron.*

Micah Blumberg confirmed on August 11, 2026 that every supplied post was public when first
published and that the supplied posts are public again now. An earlier review packet classified
the audience icons visible at the time of capture; some then displayed a friends-only icon. That
capture-time setting must not be substituted for the original publication state or the current
access state.

The strongest evidence wording remains **Facebook screenshot with platform-displayed date**.
Direct Facebook object links have now been recovered for all twenty-six dated document stages.
Facebook export metadata, edit history, contemporaneous notifications, and independent web
archives should be added when available. A displayed date is meaningful provenance, but it is not
the same as an immutable repository commit or independent archive capture.

## Source-faithful interpretation rule

The early posts use metaphors and vocabulary that differ from later SAN. This page therefore keeps
four layers separate:

1. **Historical wording:** what the dated post actually proposed.
2. **Established science:** independently supported physiology or neuroscience.
3. **Later SAN connection:** the mature SAN concept that the early wording can legitimately inform.
4. **Open hypothesis:** the stronger causal or consciousness claim that still requires testing.

Terms such as NAPOT, Phase-Wave Differential, Tonic Canvas, Neural Tuning, and Field-Cell Self are
not projected backward into a source that did not yet use or fully specify them.

## Scientific calibration

Established neuroscience supports distributed neural computation, recurrent processing,
experience-dependent plasticity, inhibitory competition, population oscillations, predictive and
state-dependent processing, and multimodal sensorimotor loops. Those findings do not by themselves
prove SAN's stronger proposal that receiver-relative phase differences and tonic population state
jointly instantiate the contents of a distributed conscious rendering.

The historical record is therefore most useful when it shows the progression clearly:

```text
early public architecture
-> later named SAN mechanisms
-> medically bounded modern interpretation
-> discriminating experiment
```
