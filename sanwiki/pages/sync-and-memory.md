## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks, a memory is not a stored datum but a *replayable [[phase-pattern|phase pattern]]* — a configuration of [[neural-array|neural arrays]] that, when activated, falls back into the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] coalition under which it was first inscribed via [[hebbian-plasticity|Hebbian]]/[[synaptic-computation-ltp|LTP]] mechanisms. Synchronization, in this picture, is not a side-effect of remembering; it is the *physical form* a remembered pattern takes. The connection between the two is built on three substrate facts already documented elsewhere in the wiki: (1) [[memories-as-attractors|memories are attractors]] in the [[synaptic-connections]] / [[dendritic-morphology]] joint state, drawing the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|DPWDR]] back into a previously visited basin; (2) [[ltp-tempo-spatial-rhythmic-memory|LTP encodes tempo-spatial rhythmic memory]] — the exact rhythm under which the synaptic connections were strengthened becomes the rhythm under which they preferentially re-fire; and (3) [[oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration|oscillatory synchrony integrates across modalities]], which is why a multi-modal memory replays as a synchronous coalition rather than as separate sensory tracks. *Sync-and-memory* compresses these three into a single thesis: synchronization is the visible signature of a memory's instantaneous read.

## Relation to mainstream framings

Mainstream [[neuroscience]] treats synchronization and memory as deeply related but ontologically distinct: neurons synchronize *to encode* a memory (binding, communication-through-coherence), and memories *are stored* in synaptic weights/structures. SAN does not deny the empirical findings; it makes the stronger ontological move that the *act* of synchronizing *is* the act of having the memory present. This puts *sync-and-memory* in conversation with: [[predictive-coding]] (the [[prediction]] is the rhythm), the Hebbian / STDP literature (the connections that grew under one rhythm preferentially re-engage under that rhythm), [[free-energy-principle|free-energy]] frameworks (the rhythm is the latent variable being inferred), and the dynamic-systems / [[attractor|attractor]] tradition (the rhythm is the basin). Where SAN makes a *testable* [[prediction]] the published literature does not commit to: that memory recall fidelity should be measurable as instantaneous phase-coherence across the recall coalition, and that disrupting that coherence (e.g., via TMS at the wrong phase) should disrupt recall *without* disrupting the underlying [[synaptic-connections]] — connections persist, sync collapses, recall fails.
