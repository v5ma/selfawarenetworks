# Volume Recognition

**Volume Recognition** is the umbrella label, used in cross-references across [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]]'s [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks corpus, for the brain's capacity to identify, segment, and re-render *volumetric* — i.e. genuinely 3D, not picture-plane — structure from sensory input. It conjoins SAN's [[3d-semantic-segmentation|3D semantic segmentation]] thesis with the [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] tomographic-readout model, the [[neural-tomographic-lightfield-rendering|neural-tomographic lightfield rendering]] frame, and the [[sensory-stabilized-volumetric-rendering|sensory-stabilized volumetric rendering]] account. Substantive treatment lives across that family of pages rather than under "volume recognition" in isolation.

## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the brain is not a 2D pattern-matcher: it builds a [[learned-spatial-model|learned spatial model]] in genuine [[three-tier-spatial-consciousness-model|three-tier]] volumetric form, and "recognition" of an object is the act of [[neural-rendering|rendering]] it into that model and matching it against stored volumetric templates. The tomographic side of this story comes from the [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] family — [[napot-theory]], [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography]], [[napot-revision-2-exit-terminal-sdr]], [[napot-revision-3-anti-spike]], [[napot-tomography-not-holography]], [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]], [[neural-array-projection-oscillatory-tomography]], [[neural-oscillatory-tomography]] — which read distributed cortical phase patterns as projections through a volumetric [[phase-field|phase field]], in the same mathematical sense that a CT scan reconstructs volume from projections. The rendering side comes from [[brain-as-rendering-engine|the brain framed as a rendering engine]], [[continuous-neural-rendering|continuous neural rendering of reality]], [[consciousness-persistent-rendering|persistent rendering of consciousness]], [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering|its longer-form variant]], [[consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop|the multi-modal rendering loop]], [[neural-oscillation-rendering|neural oscillation rendering]], [[neural-rendering-of-qualia|neural rendering of qualia]], [[neuro-rendering|neuro-rendering]], and [[phase-differential-rendering|phase-differential rendering of qualia]].
