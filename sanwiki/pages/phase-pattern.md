## Post-SAN

The testable distinction the SAN framing makes is that the relevant cortical readout is the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] across a [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|cortical scale hierarchy]], not the phase-locking coefficient or the spike-time alignment in isolation — and that perceptual content can in principle be reconstructed from the phase-pattern geometry alone, given a sufficient [[oscillatory-tomography|oscillatory-tomography]] readout. Adjacent SAN concepts that mainstream work has not yet adopted — [[neural-phase-pattern-propagation|phase-pattern propagation]], [[oscillatory-qualia-imprinting|qualia-imprinting]] onto phase patterns, and the [[four-dimensional-phase-tracking|four-dimensional phase-tracking]] generalisation — define a contiguous research direction in which the Phase Pattern construct can be falsified or refined.
