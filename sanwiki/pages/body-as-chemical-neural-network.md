As a definitional consequence of the title, the concept asserts that endocrine, immune, and peripheral signalling channels behave as components of one extended chemical-electrical [[neural-arrays]] substrate, rather than as systems sealed off from cortical computation.

In its definitional reading, the **Body As Chemical Neural Network** generalizes the principles of the [[bridging-molecular-mechanisms]] program: every place in the body where receptor binding gates a state change is, in this view, a node in a network whose internal language is chemical concentration gradients, ion-channel kinetics, and graded electrical potentials rather than digital spike trains. This frames hormones, neuromodulators such as [[acetylcholine]] and [[gaba]]-related transmitters, and second-messenger cascades involving [[camkii]] as the long-range carriers of the same information that, inside the brain, expresses itself as the [[action-potential-waveform]] and [[neural-spikes]]. The framework is consistent with [[hebbian-learning]] and [[long-term-potentiation]] being one specialization of a body-wide plasticity rule rather than a uniquely cortical phenomenon.
