# Neural Lace Podcast S2E2: Jules Urbach, RTX and Rendering

The September 11, 2018 conversation with OTOY CEO Jules Urbach covers RTX, real-time path tracing,
volumetric capture, AI denoising, XR and the bridge from rendering technology to neural-interface
and self-aware-AI questions.

## What the episode contributes

Urbach explains RTX hardware, real-time ray and path tracing, VR frame-rate constraints, Octane,
Unity and Unreal integration, light-field and volumetric capture, and AI denoising. Micah connects
these external rendering pipelines to optical brain imaging, fNIRS, ultrasound, tomographic
reconstruction and deep learning on medical data. The conversation later turns toward self-aware
AI and systems that model both a world and themselves.

## SAN routes

- **Neural Rendering comparison:** capture, transform, render and update are useful operations for
  explaining the theory.
- **Read/output separation:** brain measurement is not the same problem as rendering an XR world.
- **Artificial neurology:** world modeling and self-modeling are raised without claiming that
  photorealism produces consciousness.
- **History:** the episode records a second Urbach conversation after the May 2017 GTC interview.

## Evidence boundary

OTOY and RTX are engineering comparisons, not proof that brains implement an identical renderer.
The episode also does not establish that the listed imaging systems can decode or write a complex
percept.
