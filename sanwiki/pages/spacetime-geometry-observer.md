An early (Dec 2010) philosophical seed note in [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s archive: the claim that the
geometry of spacetime is *sufficient* explanation for the existence of observers —
no additional mechanism is required, just as the geometry of gravity explains galaxies.

## The core claim

> "The geometry of spacetime is enough, and there is no need for anymore explanation."

The analogy: water geometry explains oceans; gravitational geometry explains stellar
structure. By extension, the geometry of spacetime — sufficiently complex and
multi-dimensional — may explain why there are observers asking why observers exist.

## Complexity vs entropy paradox

From a third-person perspective: complexity increases over time (matter-antimatter
asymmetry ratcheting toward richer structure). From another perspective: entropy
increases. From a hypothetical 4th-dimensional perspective: the system is static.

This three-perspective paradox — increase / increase / stillness — is a precursor to
the SAN idea that the [[tonic-canvas-phasic-paint]] model of the brain exhibits a
similar three-layer structure: stable tonic oscillations (background geometry),
phasic complexity (increasing information content), and the observer (the oscillating
group itself, which in one sense is always in the same state).

## Relation to SAN theory

The "observer is reality" intuition is undeveloped here but becomes concrete in
[[self-aware-networks]] via [[napot-overview]]: the observer *is* the oscillating
neural array — there is no homunculus watching. This note is a 2010 precursor to that
formal position. See also [[acausal-pattern]] for how cortical predictions constitute
the observer's experienced reality.
