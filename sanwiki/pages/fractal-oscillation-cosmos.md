SAN proposes that the cosmos is a **fractal of oscillating dissipative systems**: every scale of physical and biological organization — from quantum particles to neurons to cortical columns to social networks to galaxies — instantiates the same fundamental pattern of cyclical state change, vortex formation, and information encoding.

## Core Claim

"The universe is a fractal of oscillations." A particle, a planet, a neuron, and a brain are all *functionally isomorphic* — each is a vortex, a dissipative system, a dipole that cycles through states and encodes information in those cycles. This is not a metaphor: the claim is that the computational and informational structure is genuinely [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar across scales.

Corollary: a person is a fractal of a neuron; a cortical column is a fractal of a hippocampal column; the retina is a functional fractal of entorhinal cortex (following Jeff Hawkins' Thousand Brains reference frames).

## Oscillation as the Common Thread

Every vortex, every dissipative system, every particle oscillates. At short time scales oscillation appears random (beta decay); at longer time scales it becomes predictable — mirroring how [[neural-oscillation]] generates unpredictable moment-to-moment spikes but reliable large-scale [[brainwave]] rhythms.

[[tonic-phasic-signaling]] is the neural instance of this: [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]] is the stable [[attractor]]; phasic deviations are the information-bearing changes riding on top.

## Consciousness Implication

"Consciousness is the [[entification]] of a physical information vortex." The phenomenal field is not separate from physics — it *is* the vortex pattern viewed from inside. [[consciousness-energy-wave-differences]] — what we perceive — are the differential energy states of this oscillating system.
