A [[dendrite]] flash is most directly grounded in [[dendritic-compartmentalization|dendritic compartmentalization]]: each [[apical-dendrite|apical dendrite]] of a [[pyramidal-cell|Layer 5 pyramidal]] or [[Cortical-columns|cortical-column]] neuron can fire its own brief, near-action-potential-like event when local [[criteria]] are met, and these events sum at the soma to gate the [[action-potential|axonal action potential]].
