# Senses

**Senses** in [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) are the brain's multimodal input channels -- sight, hearing, touch, taste, smell, proprioception, interoception -- whose raw data is converted into [electrical patterns](action-potential.md) that the [cortex](cortex.md) integrates into a [continuously-rendered](brain-as-rendering-engine.md) internal 3D model. Within SAN, the senses are the entry side of the [rendering loop](continuous-neural-rendering.md) whose output is [qualia](qualia.md), and [Micah](gh-resnote04.md)'s earliest documented account of them -- from 2012 -- already had the SAN-shape: senses as signals traveling into oscillating neuron populations.

## Roots

The packet's earliest dated evidence is a cluster of 2012 [Neo Mind Cycle](neo-mind-cycle.md) / [Happiness Makeover](neo-mind-cycle-happiness-makeover-2012.md) transcripts (`core-set--1_Happiness_Makeover_w_Brainwave_Chart`, transcript, 2012; `core-set--Micah_founder_of_Neo_Mind_Cycle__Mark__Chickenman____Andrews__My_Happiness_Makeover`, transcript, 2012; `core-set--Chicken_Cloud`, transcript, 2012) and the 2012 Ben Thomas dialogue (`core-set--Micah_talks_to_Ben_Thomas`, paper by [Micah](gh-resnote04.md), 2012). In `core-set--Chicken_Cloud` [Micah](gh-resnote04.md) is already articulating the SAN-shape: "the way my neurons communicate inside, see what happens is all my senses, all my senses like literally like, even like, even if I even if I eat something and it goes..." -- the framing where senses are not ontologically distinct channels but converging streams into a single neural substrate. Even more striking, in the 2012 Mark/Chickenman/Andrews talk: "your senses are converted into electrical patterns once it goes it hits the eye and the light the photon" -- exactly the [electrical-pattern](action-potential.md) conversion SAN later formalizes. The [Happiness Makeover step-three](neo-mind-cycle-happiness-makeover-2012.md) protocol -- "check in with your senses. I'm touching blank, tasting blank, seeing blank, smelling blank" -- predates the formal SAN manuscripts by a decade and shows that the multimodal-sense framing was a working clinical practice for [Micah](gh-resnote04.md) by 2012. The 2012 Ben Thomas note "senses are converted into signals" closes the early lineage. By 2017 (`core-set--HackDays`, paper by [Micah](gh-resnote04.md), 2017) and 2017-2019 NLP transcripts the framing is fluent.

## Position

Within SAN, the senses are the input side of the [rendering pipeline](brain-as-rendering-engine.md). [a0003z](gh-a0003z.md) gives the bare minimum: "Perception through the senses is required for mental processes" (`a0003z--md`, line 7, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0003z.md)) -- the senses are upstream of cognition, not optional. [a0004z](gh-a0004z.md) situates them inside the [ground-of-being](ground-of-being.md) picture: "The [tonic oscillatory noise](tonic-oscillation.md) I argue provides for the [ground of being](ground-of-being.md), awareness, raw experiential conscious [expectation](expectation.md) that new information from our senses ... arises from" (`a0004z--md`, line 23, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0004z.md)) -- meaning sense data lands on a pre-existing tonic canvas. [a0010z](gh-a0010z.md) extends to the [predictive](predictive-processing.md) side: "the reflection of you, in the canvas of [tonic brainwave oscillations](tonic-oscillation.md), is your oscillating memories, your expectations" (`a0010z--md`, line 196, on github). [11san](gh-11san.md) supplies the integration claim: "This internal 3D model is constructed by integrating information from various senses and updating in real time" (`11san--md`, line 21, on github). [05san](gh-05san.md) gives the [traveling-wave](cortical-traveling-waves-san-submission.md) mechanism: "[cortical waves](cortical-traveling-waves.md) propagate across different sensory regions of the brain, ensuring that inputs from various senses are aligned in time and space. This [synchronization](synchrony.md) across regions mirrors the cross-array synchronization" (`05san--md`, line 358, on github). And [13san](gh-13san.md) supplies the recursive completion: "Chain many such loops: their phases lock into a global rhythm each loop both shapes and senses. That mutual embedding yields first-person presence" (`13san--md`, line 109, on github) -- the senses are not just inputs but participants in the feedback loop that constitutes selfhood. [a0025z](gh-a0025z.md) and [a0031z](gh-a0031z.md) describe the [BCI](brain-computer-interfaces.md) extension: "we are creating a feedback loop from senses to [brainwaves](brainwaves.md), to machine, to senses again, back into [brainwaves](brainwaves.md), back into machine" (`a0031z--md`, line 19, on github) -- sense organs as the natural interface boundary.

## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream sensory-systems [neuroscience](neuroscience.md), SAN takes three positions on the senses. First, it accepts the standard transduction picture (light -> photoreceptors -> action potentials, etc.) -- `core-set--Micah_founder_of_Neo_Mind_Cycle__Mark__Chickenman____Andrews__My_Happiness_Makeover` (transcript, 2012) already records this as a 2012 working assumption. Second, it specifically commits to a multimodal-binding-via-[synchrony](synchrony.md) account that locates the cross-modal merge in [cortical traveling waves](cortical-traveling-waves.md) across sensory regions (`05san--md`, line 358, paper by [Micah](gh-resnote04.md)) -- a stronger claim than the canonical perceptual-binding literature, and one that makes a testable [prediction](prediction.md): lesions or [TMS](transcranial-magnetic-stimulation.md) disruption of inter-regional traveling waves should preferentially impair [multimodal integration](multimodal-integration.md) even when individual sensory cortices remain intact. Third, where contemporary [predictive-processing](predictive-processing.md) frameworks treat sense data as Bayesian evidence updating priors, SAN re-locates the priors as the [tonic oscillatory canvas](tonic-oscillation.md) itself -- sense data are render-changes against an already-running pattern, not separate evidence streams (`a0004z--md`, line 23, paper by [Micah](gh-resnote04.md)) -- a substrate-level reframing that recovers the same input/output behavior through the [phase-wave differential](phase-wave-differentials.md) mechanism.
