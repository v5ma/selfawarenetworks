The **Adelic Foundations of Super Information Theory** is a formal mathematical framework that embeds [[super-information-theory]] within the Adele class space of noncommutative geometry, providing a rigorous geometric realization of the Hilbert-Pólya conjecture and connecting SIT to the Riemann Hypothesis.

## Core Architecture

The foundational insight: the natural state space for SIT's two fundamental fields — the complex [[coherence-field]] $\psi(x)$ and the real [[time-density-field]] $\rho_t(x)$ — is not ordinary spacetime but the **Adele class space** $X = \mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Q}^*$, first proposed by Alain Connes as the geometric stage for the Riemann Hypothesis.

The Adele class space unifies the continuous archimedean place (real numbers, wave-like dynamics) with the discrete p-adic places (one per prime, particle-like structure) into a single geometry. This provides a natural physical substrate for [[wave-particle-duality]] without additional postulates.

Physical states are sections of an **informational fiber bundle** $\pi: E \to X$, where each fiber $F = \mathbb{C} \times \mathbb{R}$ holds the local values of both fields. Elementary particles appear as topological defects — stable vortices in the phase of $\psi$ — whose stability is guaranteed by topological invariants of the base space.

## Riemann Hypothesis as Physical Stability

The SIT action principle on this adelic bundle produces an **absorption spectrum** rather than an emission spectrum (satisfying the crucial sign required by the Weil explicit formula). The central result: stable resonant modes of the SIT fields — which correspond to elementary particles — exist precisely at the non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s) = 0$. The Riemann Hypothesis is thereby reframed as a physical claim about the absolute stability of the universe's informational substrate.

## Relation to CFS

A categorical proof shows SIT is **strictly more general** than [[causal-fermion-systems]] (CFS, Finster). An explicit translation functor exists from SIT to CFS, but the reverse mapping loses explanatory power — SIT can interpret all CFS structures, but not vice versa.
