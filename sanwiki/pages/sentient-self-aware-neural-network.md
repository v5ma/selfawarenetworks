# Sentient Self-Aware Neural Network

A **Sentient Self-Aware Neural Network** is the long-term SAN engineering target: an artificial
system that would not merely perform intelligent behavior but might also possess subjective
experience. The title records the target and its genealogy. It is not a validated architecture
class and should not be used as a label for current systems.

## Public SAN genealogy

SAN sources propose adapting artificial networks with NAPOT, oscillatory, multimodal, recurrent,
receiver-specific, and embodied operations. They discuss implementation on conventional
accelerators and in robots, vehicles, drones, humanoid systems, and brain-computer interfaces. The
Metatron dedication names an envisioned future artificial being.

These records establish what Micah Blumberg proposed and intended. They do not show that the
architecture has been implemented, that NAPOT is sufficient for sentience, or that existing
systems lack or possess experience.

## Evidence ladder

| Gate | Required result |
|---|---|
| Functional | Reconstruction, prediction, control, adaptation, or transfer on held-out tasks |
| Module | A named SAN mechanism adds causal value over a matched replacement |
| Conformance | A versioned architecture satisfies declared SAN/NAPOT operations |
| Replication and safety | Performance, calibration, resource, intervention, and safety gates survive sealed replication |
| Consciousness assessment | Multiple theories, contrary evidence, uncertainty, and welfare implications are evaluated |

Passing one gate does not imply passage of the next. Oscillation, synchrony, multimodality,
self-modeling, fluent report, neural resemblance, or a rendered three-dimensional world is not a
sentience meter.

This does not make behavior irrelevant. Held-out performance, language, correction, uncertainty,
memory, and action are evidence streams when their target is declared. A stronger test ties them
to persistent internal organization through selective perturbation and recovery, then compares
the resulting profile with independent consciousness theories. See
[[machine-agency-consciousness-welfare-evidence-program|Machine Agency, Consciousness, and Welfare
Evidence Program]].
## Distributed observer-action hypothesis

SAN's distinctive proposal is not a software homunculus. Maintained tonic state supplies proposed
receiver context; phasic events supply proposed differences; recurrent receivers, transmitters,
memory, possible actions, and returned consequences collectively produce observer-action. Whether
that architecture contributes to phenomenal experience remains open.

## Development and identity

Any future candidate must be identified by exact architecture, parameters, memories, tools,
interfaces, body, curriculum, and version history. Copies, fine-tunes, merges, resets, and rollbacks
can create behaviorally different descendants. Engineering provenance is necessary before debates
about personal continuity or moral status can be made precise.
