# From Intelligence to Self

## The idea in plain language

A system can solve a problem without having a self. It can selectively attend without possessing
a stable identity. It can distinguish sensory states without forming long-term goals.

SAN therefore separates a set of capabilities that are often collapsed:

```text
intelligence -> attention -> sensory distinction -> persistence -> goals -> self-identity
```

This is a research ladder, not a claim that every biological or artificial system climbs the same
steps in the same order.

## Why this matters

The separation is especially important for machine consciousness. Strong performance or fluent
language cannot substitute for measurements of internal persistence, causal participation,
valuation, self/world distinction, or conscious experience.

Performance and fluent language remain useful evidence about the capabilities they directly test.
The next question is whether those outputs depend on retained state, live alternatives, goals,
closed-loop consequence, and recoverable internal organization. See
[[machine-agency-consciousness-welfare-evidence-program|From Machine Output to Candidate Agency,
Consciousness, and Welfare]].
