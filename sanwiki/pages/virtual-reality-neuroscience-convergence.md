Pending acquisition, the SAN-aligned reading is that "convergence" is not a metaphor but a concrete claim about shared substrate: the [[continuous-neural-rendering|continuous rendering]] the [[cortex]] performs, the [[neural-oscillation-rendering|neural-oscillation-driven rendering]] of the [[brain-as-rendering-engine|brain-as-rendering-engine]], and the GPU rendering pipelines that drive [[augmented-reality|XR headsets]] are all members of the same computational class — and that closing the loop between them via [[eeg-xr-neurofeedback-bci|EEG/XR neurofeedback]] turns the headset into an external cortical surface.
