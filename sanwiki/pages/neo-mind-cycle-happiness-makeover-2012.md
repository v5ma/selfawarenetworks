# Neo Mind Cycle and My Happiness Makeover (2012)

A 2012 public dialogue between [[gh-resnote04|Micah Blumberg]] and Mark "Chickenman" Andrews places Neo Mind Cycle inside a broader self-improvement program called *My Happiness Makeover*. The page is historical evidence for how the project was explained at the time. It is not a clinical efficacy record.

## The proposed two-way conversation

The dialogue contrasts prerecorded or fixed entrainment with a contingent EEG feedback loop. In the proposed loop, a selected EEG-derived value changes light or sound and the participant receives the changed sensory consequence. Calling this a "two-way conversation" describes recurrence between participant and apparatus; it does not mean the software understood the participant's belief, intention, or thought content.

The source argues that feedback related to the participant's current measurement could remain engaging and support learning. That is a historical rationale, not evidence that fixed stimulation is generally resisted, that mirrored feedback necessarily remains novel, or that the protocol produced a particular neuroplastic change.

## Wellness context

The source places neurofeedback near exercise, nutrition, gratitude, coaching, and proposed Reiki sessions. This establishes the program's wellness and values context. It does not establish that the combined program treated a disorder, increased intelligence, produced durable happiness, or isolated the effect of any component.

## Observer training and later SAN themes

The dialogue uses "shifting into neutral" as a strategy for responding to disorientation with patience and reduced judgment. That language can be retained as a historical bridge to later SAN questions about prediction, self-observation, value, and action selection. It should not be presented as a defined neural mechanism or medical intervention.

Later discussion of chickens, ants, flocks, colonies, co-regulation, and collective minds is source-specific speculation. It does not establish one conscious group organism or a validated SAN mechanism.

## Evidence boundary

The surviving public record supports the speakers, the 2012 publication context, the two-way-feedback explanation, and the broader wellness framing. Exact device efficacy, the identity of every named coach, the clinical status of the program, and the causal effect of the intervention remain unresolved. Modern neurofeedback claims require controls that distinguish target engagement from trainer, expectancy, repetition, technological setting, other practice, and natural change ([Ros et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaa009)).
