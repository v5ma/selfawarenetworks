The claim that humans are "metal robots in a valid sense" — not metaphorically, but because biological neurons are electrochemical signal processors built from charged metal ions (Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺) that generate voltage and electromagnetic waves, making the brain a physically grounded computational substrate.

## Core Thesis

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s 2018 [[gh-formulation|formulation]]: *"If you combine a digital signal processor with a deep learning neural network, a three dimensional point cloud and a fractal of feedback loops you get the human mind or a [[gh-a0206z|self]] aware network."*

This is the seed definition of [[self-aware-network]] — the brain is not *like* a computer but is one, grounded in discrete ion-based signal processing.

## Five Evidence Points (2018)

1. **Metal ions as hardware** — Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺ ions in cells enable action potentials and electromagnetic brainwaves. Biology is computing, not merely electrical.
2. **Predictive neural networks** — Lotter et al. (2018): a network trained to predict future video frames spontaneously replicates cortical neuron response properties, suggesting deep structural alignment between artificial and biological predictive coding. See [[predictive-coding]].
3. **Discrete information representation** — Tee & Taylor (2018): Shannon communications theory + behavioral experiments support discrete (quantized) over continuous neural coding. See [[discrete-neural-coding]].
4. **Grid cells in artificial agents** — DeepMind (2018): agents navigating 3D environments spontaneously develop grid-cell-like representations, echoing [[grid-cells]] in biological navigation circuits.
5. **Neocortical columns as grid-cell analogs** — Jeff Hawkins (2018): cortical columns may use grid-cell-like mechanisms to model every object's identity, properties, and orientation. See [[neocortical-columns-grid-cells]].

## Key SAN Sub-Concepts

- **[[coincidence-detection]]** — the neuron as a coincidence detector; neural "bits" are defined by co-activation patterns, not single spikes. Sub-threshold dendritic events count.
- **[[dendritic-computation]]** — dendrites as advanced data structures with temporal/spatial memory, capable of setting firing-threshold criteria and generating their own action potentials.
- **[[volumetric-predictive-rendering]]** — the brain's sensory model is not frame-by-frame video but a continuous 3D volumetric simulation that is both generated and learned from simultaneously.
