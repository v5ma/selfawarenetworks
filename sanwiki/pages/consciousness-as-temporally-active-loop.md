**Consciousness as a temporally active loop** is the SAN thesis that consciousness is the ongoing process by which physical systems compute narratives, render graphical representations, and synthesize audio sensory patterns — a loop that physically reorganises its own [[choice]]-making mechanisms over time.

The definition from `a0628z`:

> Consciousness is a temporally active loop of human computed stories & graphical renderings & audio sensory mechanical synthesis of patterns.

This is offered as the solution to the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness]]: consciousness is not a separate ontological category but the functional activity of a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-reorganising physical system. We are physical systems that "physically reorganize our own [[choice]] making mechanisms to make complex selections based on computed and predicted outcomes."

## Connection to Neural Array Projection

The phrase "graphical renderings" maps directly onto [[neural-array-projection]] — the SAN claim that neural arrays project volumetric representations that other arrays observe and integrate. The same note proposes that the brain has learned to render and imagine volumetric images from any angle, analogous to a 3D renderer or large model (the "GP3" reference is an early label for this rendering capacity).

## Dendritic Morphology Encoding Memory

A second idea in the same note: neuron shape encodes memory. The morphology of spine and [[dendrite]] — their topography — sets the neuron's voltage waveform and learned pattern amplitude. Long-term memory changes the neuron's shape via protein synthesis in stimulated areas. This is a precursor to the SAN claim that [[neural-oscillation-patterns]] carry persistent memory in their structural substrate.

## Relation to SAN and NAPOT

[[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) is the formalized version of this loop. The temporally active loop here is pre-formal: the loop is the oscillation; the computed stories are the projected representations; the graphical rendering is the tomographic recombination of projected patterns across arrays.

Classifying consciousness as a loop rather than a substance sidesteps both dualism and eliminativism — consistent with the broader SAN physicalist stance.
