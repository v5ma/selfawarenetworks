**Oscillatory qualia imprinting** is the mechanism by which a single neuron's firing event "steps out" of the collective oscillation and encodes a qualia representation onto the live temporal pattern of the neural assembly.

## Core Mechanism

In the [[thalamocortical-network]], neurons maintain a baseline oscillation (see [[napot]]). When one neuron fires, it deviates from the synchronized rhythm — creating a detectable difference in the temporal pattern. Other neurons in the oscillating bundle register this disruption as a materially distinguishable event. The firing neuron then gradually re-entrains back into the collective oscillation, but the information is already integrated: the oscillation carries the imprint forward.

This is why gamma waves are not merely synchrony-for-its-own-sake but serve a dual role: (1) readying cells to receive incoming signals and (2) rapidly integrating and erasing what they just processed, cycling the workspace for the next imprint.

## Binding into Mind Space

Sensory patterns collected across layers of networked neurons are **bound into a mind-space image** when the [[dendrite-flash]] occurs. The oscillating bundle notices the flash precisely because it is the *only* event not in oscillation — a figure-against-ground detection in temporal space. This is consistent with [[global-workspace-theory]], where broadcast signals become globally available by disrupting local synchrony.

## Relationship to NAPOT

This mechanism is a micro-scale instance of [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography). Each imprinting event can be understood as a projection: the neuron's firing encodes a local feature, the oscillation integrates it, and the thalamocortical loop broadcasts the composite representation.
