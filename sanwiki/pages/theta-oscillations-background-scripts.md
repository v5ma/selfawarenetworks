## Core Idea

In SAN theory, neurons encode daily behavioral strategies as "active background scripts" — persistent oscillatory states that run continuously beneath focal [[attention]]. Theta waves represent the cortical substrate of these scripts, reorganizing throughout the day as the organism observes and adapts.

The oscillation is not mere electrical noise: it is the compressed, energy-efficient encoding of behavioral repertoires. When a strategy is needed, the theta pattern unfolds into active computation; at rest it idles in background oscillatory form.
