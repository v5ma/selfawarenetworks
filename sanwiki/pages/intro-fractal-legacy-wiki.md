## Post-SAN

Mainstream fractal-[[cortex]] literature treats fractal organisation as a *descriptive* feature of cortical anatomy and dynamics — fractal dimension, scale-free statistics, neuronal avalanches at criticality. SAN's fractal-cluster pages take a stronger position: that the *function* of the fractal organisation is to allow the same [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave-differential]] readout machinery to operate across scales, so that [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]]-style tomography is *recursively applicable* from cortical-column-internal to whole-brain levels. This recursive-applicability claim is one of two SAN-side commitments worth flagging against the published literature: where the published work mostly *measures* fractal dimension as a property of the substrate, SAN treats fractal organisation as the *enabling condition* for cross-scale wave readouts.
