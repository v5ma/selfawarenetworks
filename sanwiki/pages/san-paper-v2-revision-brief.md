## The Bounded SAN Thesis

The paper does not claim novelty for oscillation, synchrony, phase coding, traveling waves, recurrent processing, or dendritic integration by themselves. Its proposed joined operation is:

**receiver state and learned timing -> receiver-relative differential event -> changed effective routing -> recurrent exchange of partial projections -> constrained update of a body/world/action state -> measurable report or action**

The main SAN terms name stages in that proposal:

- **Phase-Wave Differential (PWD):** a receiver-relative change in effective access, timing, routing, or downstream consequence. Phase difference alone is not enough.
- **Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography (NAPOT):** the stronger hypothesis that multiple recurrent neural projections identify and causally update a declared latent state.
- **Neural Rendering:** the continuing distributed construction and revision of a usable self/world/action state. No separate spectator views a finished screen.
- **Gamma Consideration Sandwich (GCS):** a proposed embodied timing relationship among sensory evidence, contextual or expectation-related activity, action-related state, and returned consequences. It is not a universal frequency-to-layer chart.

## Evidence Ladder

### Established Components

Receiver state affects nonlinear temporal integration. Relative phase can carry information. Traveling waves and recurrent dynamics occur in declared preparations. Dendritic branches can perform local nonlinear integration. These findings are not SAN-specific and do not establish NAPOT.

### Supported Interpretation

Timing, phase, spatial wave geometry, and recurrent state can regulate effective communication in some circuits. Their explanatory value depends on the species, preparation, brain region, task, receiver, measurement, and alternative models.

### SAN Predictions

SAN predicts that edge-conditioned PWD and spatial wave variables add reproducible information beyond matched rate, power, scalar-coherence, connectivity, and equal-input recurrent baselines. NAPOT further predicts that multiple partial projections identify a latent state and that selective perturbation produces the preregistered downstream reconstruction error.

### Stronger Open Claims

The current evidence does not establish that NAPOT is necessary or sufficient for phenomenal consciousness, that every coherent assembly is conscious, or that a phase-coded artificial network is sentient.

## The Tomography Gate

The word **tomography** is earned only when an experiment specifies:

1. a latent state distinct from the experimenter's observed target;
2. neural projection and observation operators;
3. linear rank and conditioning, or nonlinear Jacobian and gauge-identifiability conditions;
4. dynamic observability when recurrence is invoked;
5. held-out reconstruction beyond equal-information baselines; and
6. a biological projection perturbation with an operator-specific error predicted in advance.

If these conditions fail, the surviving description is **oscillatory recurrent integration**, not neural tomography.

## Receiver, Coincidence, and Direction Boundaries

"Coincidence is a bit" means a receiver event produced after continuous, state-dependent, multi-input integration. It is not assumed to contain exactly one Shannon bit. Its entropy and mutual information must be measured.

A phase difference also does not establish causal direction. Direction must come from declared anatomy, selective intervention, or a validated causal or effective-connectivity analysis. In one narrow frequency band, delay and preferred phase offset can be confounded; the experiment must fix one independently, use constrained multifrequency estimation, or report the equivalence class.

## Embodiment and the Distributed Observer

Vision, intended action, efference copy, proprioception, touch, vestibular and interoceptive state, and recurrent expectation may jointly update distributed variables for pose, agency, ownership, self-location, and action. Somatotopic maps are overlapping constraints within that distributed process, not a cortical person or inner observer.

Apical and basal dendrites are branching cable systems with local electrical compartments. A **dendritic input array** therefore means spatially arranged synaptic sites within a declared arbor domain. The source-faithful operation is **receive -> transform -> re-express** through changed spike or burst timing, axonal output, synaptic release, and downstream state. It is not a biological television or Gaussian rasterizer.

A proposed `20 -> 40/60/80 Hz` pattern remains untested. Because those frequencies are harmonics of 20 Hz, waveform shape can generate all three peaks without three distinct symbols. Any test requires cycle-resolved timing, waveform-preserving surrogates, aperiodic and broadband controls, source separation, artifact checks, and carrier-specific perturbation.

The primary embodied test uses active movement and a closed-loop virtual hand with delayed visual feedback, movement and touch localizers, trialwise perceived hand-position error, and held-out incremental prediction beyond an equal-information raw model. A delay must produce the preregistered circular phase shift and a phase-specific rescue. A positive result would support a body-state update, not phenomenal consciousness.

## Historical TMS Boundary

The August 2012 SAN discussion combines earlier TMS-EEG research with Micah Blumberg's speculative extensions. Wakeful propagation versus a more local non-rapid-eye-movement response belongs to the earlier Tononi-Massimini program. A linear `0-100` consciousness scale and separate regional scores are historical SAN extrapolations, not validated measurements. Later perturbational-complexity methods must not be backdated into that 2012 discussion.

## Scale Check

- **Micro:** receiver membrane state, synapses, dendritic branches, ion channels, spike or burst timing, and local release.
- **Meso:** recurrent populations, inhibition and disinhibition, waves, laminar interactions, thalamocortical loops, and body-related circuit variables.
- **Macro:** distributed self/world/action state, memory, report, agency judgment, and closed-loop behavior.

No local event is automatically a global render. No population rhythm is automatically a semantic symbol. No decoded stimulus or body part is automatically self-awareness.

## First Required Experiment

The first defensible empirical step is a preregistered reanalysis or prospectively collected dataset that compares SAN variables with rate, power, coherence, connectivity, recurrent, and equal-input raw sequence or graph baselines under leakage-safe validation. It must declare the receiver, latent target, projection operators, controls, uncertainty, perturbation, rescue, and rejection conditions. No favorable empirical result is assumed here.
