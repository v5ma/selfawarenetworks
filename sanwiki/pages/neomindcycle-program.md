The [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] is a 12-week, 24-session mental wellness protocol that integrates [[neurofeedback]] and [[brainwave-entrainment]] to facilitate cognitive optimization and [[gh-a0206z|self]]-directed neural recalibration.

## Program Structure

24 sessions of 35 minutes each over 12 weeks. The two core technologies are treated as complementary: neurofeedback provides active feedback-based conditioning ("healthy exercise for the brain"), while [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]] provides passive synchronization benefit ("a massage for the brain" — replicating measurable meditation-state effects).

## Neurofeedback Layer

Neurofeedback sessions give the practitioner real-time EEG signal feedback, enabling operant conditioning of desired oscillatory states. The mechanism aligns with [[napot]]'s prediction that consciously attending to oscillatory feedback can shift [[attractor]] states in [[self-aware-network]] dynamics.

## Brainwave Entrainment Layer

Entrainment uses audio (and optionally visual) stimuli at target frequencies to drive [[neural-timing-encoding]] toward coherent oscillatory states. The claimed benefit — "whole-brain thinking improvements" and enhanced creativity — reflects SAN's model of cross-frequency coupling enabling broader [[expert-data-structures]] access.

## Connection to SAN Theory

The program is an applied instantiation of [[neomindcycle]] theory: that deliberate oscillatory intervention can shift the brain's [[attractor]] landscape, increasing [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling capacity. See also [[neomindcycle-cognitive-audio-control]] for the EEG-interface extension.
