# Microtubule-to-Circuit Scale Translation

## The problem in ordinary language

Suppose a microtubule inside a neuron stores or changes a physical pattern. That finding would be
interesting, but it would not yet explain thought or consciousness. The rest of the neuron needs a
way to read the change. The effect then needs to alter a circuit, survive competing activity, and
change something the organism can report or use.

SAN turns that requirement into a chain:

```text
microtubule change
-> cellular reader
-> membrane or plasticity change
-> circuit consequence
-> behavior or conscious target
```

Every arrow needs evidence.

## Micah's 2021-2022 position

An October 2021 Recorder source describes learned patterns being magnified from small cellular
scales into larger neural assemblies and treats microtubules as one possible memory mechanism. In
June 2022 notes, Micah asks the harder question: how would a microtubule signal alter a receptor,
ion flow, membrane voltage, spike, burst, calcium signal, or vesicle release strongly enough to
matter?

The same notes say SAN works with or without quantum effects. That is important. SAN does not need
to deny microscopic physics, but it does require a measurable route from microscopic change to
distributed neural use.

An earlier multi-speaker source, [[gh-a0384z|a0384z]], is internally dated April 3, 2013. It asks
whether quantum-scale patterns could participate in thought and how consciousness could be
recognized in a machine. The discussion does not yet supply an operational criterion. Its lasting
question is exactly the scale-translation problem: what reads the microscopic state, and what
measurable cellular, circuit, behavioral, or conscious consequence follows?

`a0099z.md`, first Git-fixed on June 10, 2022, makes the optionality explicit: coincidence-like
patterns might extend into microtubules, but a microscopic signal may be too weak to perturb the
neuron and remains incidental unless it crosses the causal bridge. This is an early SAN boundary,
not a later dismissal of the microtubule question.

## The granularity requirement

`a0059z.md` asks whether microtubules are too small a scale to explain a whole conscious episode.
Its strongest modern form is a translation requirement: an intracellular contribution must show
how it changes dendritic integration, spiking, synaptic output, population organization, and
behavior beyond models that omit it. Ordinary-cell coordination does not settle that question,
and neither cellular coordination nor microtubule oscillation establishes consciousness.

## How this differs from Orch OR

`a0334z.md`, internally dated February 13, 2014, compares CaMKII/microtubule-memory proposals with
Peter Tse's criterial causation and asks whether microscopic indeterminacy explains choice. Its
strongest current use is the scale-translation question: what reads an intracellular pattern and
converts it into a membrane, circuit, behavioral, or conscious consequence? The first tracked Git
fixation is June 12, 2022.

[[orch-or|Orch OR]] proposes that orchestrated quantum processes in microtubules contribute to
conscious moments. SAN places the observer-action process across cells, circuits, body feedback,
and action. A microtubule could contribute to that process without becoming the place where the
observer sits.

## What is established

Microtubules support neuronal structure, transport, polarity, and remodeling. Dendrites contain
microtubules in mixed orientations
([Baas et al., 1988](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.85.21.8335)). Dynamic microtubule plus ends can
enter dendritic spines and influence actin-linked spine morphology and postsynaptic organization
([Jaworski et al., 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2008.11.013);
[Hu et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2445-11.2011)). These are concrete routes by
which microtubule state can affect the cellular conditions of learning and plasticity.

Prepared rat-brain microtubule bundles also generated electrical oscillations, commonly including
a 39 Hz fundamental, while membrane-permeabilized cultured neurites transmitted related
cytoskeletal signals
([Cantero et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30453-2)). This establishes a measurable
electrical behavior in the tested preparations and makes intracellular electrical coupling a
legitimate experimental question.

The comparison stops at the preparation and scale tested. These results do not yet identify a
content-bearing code in an intact neuron, establish microtubules as the source of scalp gamma EEG,
or demonstrate a mechanism of consciousness.

## The SAN extension

SAN asks whether those cellular roles and measured electrical behaviors participate in a larger
causal sequence:

```text
microtubule dynamics
-> identifiable cellular reader
-> membrane, transport, or plasticity change
-> changed dendritic or axonal output
-> changed population rendering, memory, choice, or action
```

That extension is additive. If the chain is demonstrated, it would deepen the intracellular layer
of SAN without replacing synaptic plasticity, recurrent circuit dynamics, body feedback, or the
distributed observer-action loop.

## The decisive test

A strong experiment would measure a proposed microtubule state in a living neuron, identify the
cellular structure that reads it, selectively perturb the state, measure the predicted membrane
and circuit effects, and test whether the declared behavior or conscious endpoint changes and can
be rescued.

## 2012 memory-seed formulation

The June 22, 2012 [[gh-a0553z|source]] proposes that a compact physical memory seed recruits a much
larger live reconstruction and explicitly invites criticism. The durable problem is seed-to-tree
translation. Microtubules participate in neuronal structure and plasticity; a decoded microtubule
memory-bit store or exclusive consciousness mechanism remains unestablished.
