## A 2017 causal ancestor of the later SAN mechanism

In the bounded Micah passage, a changing visual scene is used to explain why no two sensory samples
need be identical for object recognition. The explanation then follows the causal scale transition:
photons alter retinal receptor proteins; ionic charges are displaced; an action potential occurs;
neurotransmitter is released; and the resulting activity participates in a spatial-temporal
population pattern that a decoder may learn to distinguish.

```text
changing sensory scene
-> receptor-protein transformation
-> ionic and membrane change
-> action potential
-> transmitter release
-> spatial-temporal network pattern
-> learned neural decoding
```

This is an important 2017 ancestor because it does not stop at either the receptor or the spike. It
connects molecular reception, cellular output, synaptic handoff, and population patterning in one
continuous explanation.

## Relationship to the later waveform and receiver chain

The 2017 explanation compresses the chemical-synapse handoff into "neurotransmitters get released."
The 2021-2022 SAN sources increase its physiological resolution by separating recent membrane and
channel state, terminal waveform, calcium entry, probabilistic release outcome, bouton identity,
receiver state, and the later network consequence. See [[action-potential-waveform]],
[[synaptic-unreliability-mvr-2021]], and
[[contextualized-spike-receiver-relative-transformation]].

## What this source does not establish

- It does not contain the full later waveform-calcium-bouton-receiver chain.
- It does not show that every action potential releases transmitter at every terminal.
- It does not establish an essential calcium/potassium magnetic computational medium.
- It does not establish PWD, NAPOT, APME, qualia, or consciousness by itself, and those later names
  must not be backdated into this episode.
- It is Micah-source genealogy, not independent scientific validation.
