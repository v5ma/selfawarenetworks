Phenomenological consciousness is a higher-threshold, higher-bandwidth [[choice]] mechanism - the brain's same computational [[choice]] process operating at elevated resolution and coordination capacity.

## Core argument

Choices are computed uniformly whether they surface to awareness or not. Pre-conscious neural activity (the Libet-type anticipatory signal) reflects the brain pre-planning choices from generalized templates, then customizing them in real time to actual events - sometimes faster than awareness catches up.

Consciousness is not a separate decision-making system; it is the high-bandwidth channel of the same [[choice]]-computation system. It enables more complex, coordinated, and flexible decisions than the background pre-planning layer alone can support.

## Dominant phase wave differentials and behavior change

An unwanted behavioral outcome is reframed as the "unacknowledged aggregate [[gh-b0049y|summary]] of a grand [[choice]]" - a [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm]] that has become the dominant [[attractor]]. Changing behavior requires disrupting that dominant rhythm rather than suppressing individual choices.

## Critique of "consciousness as a memory system"

The paper *[[gh-a0312z|Consciousness as a Memory System]]* (LWW Cognitive and Behavioral Neurology) argues that episodic memory is the primary function of consciousness. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s assessment: philosophically redundant. No educated reader disputes that episodic memory underpins phenomenological consciousness; that framing adds nothing beyond Dennett (1991) and existing literature. The proposed reframe does not resolve timing puzzles in human cognition studies.

The argument may be useful to someone still puzzled about how the mind works, but it does not constitute a new discovery within the SAN framework.

## Connection to Physics of Representation and neural rendering

*The Physics of Representation* (Poldrack, Hipolito, Anderson - Synthese 2020) attempted to bridge artificial neural network inference to biological brain representation, linking predictive coding (Hawkins) to internal representations. [[neural-rendering]] in SAN extends this further: biological neural networks accomplish actual computational rendering to qualia - images, sounds, feelings, tastes, smells - not merely inference over abstract representations.
