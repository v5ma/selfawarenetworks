## Position

The SAN position has three components. First, temporal context is coded by oscillatory phase rather than by spatial recruitment: a single neural assembly can carry several context labels at once if those labels are tagged to distinct windows of the local [[theta-oscillations-background-scripts|theta]] cycle, with the [[spear-hippocampal-phase-offset|SPEAR]] mechanism providing the encoding-versus-retrieval separation and the broader phase-coding apparatus carrying additional per-context labels. This makes [[neural-timing-encoding]] and [[hippocampal-encoding-retrieval-separation]] structural neighbors of the temporal-context-coding slug, with [[grid-place-cells]] and [[grid-cells-place-cells-oscillators]] supplying the spatial counterparts to the temporal context.

Second, temporal context coding is the readout-side primitive that lets [[episodic-memory]] retrieval recover not just an item but the temporal frame the item was encoded inside. The SAN extension here is that the retrieval frame is itself an [[oscillation-memory-reinstatement|oscillatory reinstatement]] of the original encoding-window [[phase-pattern|phase pattern]], so what is recovered is a relative phase configuration rather than an absolute time-stamp. The [[ltp-tempo-spatial-rhythmic-memory]], [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves]], and [[beliefs-as-tempo-spatial-sequences]] articles handle the broader tempo-spatial reading; this page is the umbrella term for the phase-gated temporal-context label that those articles inherit.

Third, temporal context coding scales upward from the hippocampal theta cycle through the [[neomindcycle-program]] global cortical cycle and into the [[fractal-consciousness]] hierarchy, with the SAN claim being that the same phase-gating-of-context principle operates at every fractal level — synapse, neuron, [[cortical-column|cortical column]], region, whole-brain field — but with different oscillation bands carrying the labels. The [[functional-fractal-isomorphism]] article supplies the umbrella formal statement of that scaling claim; the temporal-context-coding slug is the specific information-content claim that what gets carried up the fractal is a phase-gated temporal-context label, not a time-stamped spatial pattern.

## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature most directly adjacent to this concept is the Temporal Context Model (TCM) tradition descended from Howard-and-Kahana and the medial-temporal-lobe-time-cell program associated with Eichenbaum and collaborators, plus the SPEAR-style phase-offset literature the [[spear-hippocampal-phase-offset]] page surveys. The SAN reading converges with the TCM tradition on the observation that temporal context is recovered alongside item content during episodic recall, and converges with the time-cell program on the observation that medial temporal lobe assemblies carry temporal-context-relevant signals; it diverges from both on the implementation, insisting that the carrier is phase-gated population activity rather than dedicated time-cells or a slowly drifting context vector. Where the SAN framing makes a falsifiable [[prediction]] the standard reading does not, it is that controlled phase-scrambling of the [[theta-oscillations-background-scripts|theta]] cycle should disrupt context-discrimination performance even when mean firing rates and population sparseness are preserved, and that [[neural-sequence-representation]] reactivation should be recoverable from [[phase-pattern|phase pattern]] alone. The [[episodic-memory]] and [[hippocampal-encoding-retrieval-separation]] articles handle the memory-retrieval and encoding-retrieval-separation specializations of this same claim; this page is the umbrella claim that temporal context is a phase-gated label.
