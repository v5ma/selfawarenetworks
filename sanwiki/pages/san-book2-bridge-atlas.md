# Book 2 and the Bridge Atlas

The October 11, 2026 author-review edition of **Self-Aware Networks** develops its argument through
41 Cycles. Its companion **Bridge Atlas** places original source arguments, illustrations,
measurements, and proposed tests beside that narrative. The edition's PDF has 1,826 pages; the
chapter catalog contains 52 sections, and the Atlas contains 150 identified section anchors and
137 figure containers. Nested sections and reused illustrations are not counts of unique
discoveries or independently confirming studies.

The Atlas belongs throughout the Encyclopedia. A source's question, mechanism, example, and
reasoning need an explanatory home as well as a citation. The reading routes below connect the
Book's topics to existing Wiki explanations. A topic route does not by itself establish that
every argument in the corresponding Atlas section has completed its source and science review.

## How the evidence connects

Four parts of a bridge should remain visible:

1. **The original argument:** what Micah proposed, its full context, its named influences, and its
   dated notes or recordings.
2. **The biological component:** what a particular study measured, in which species, preparation,
   circuit, and conditions.
3. **The SAN synthesis:** how the proposed receiving, transformation, projection, and learning
   operations connect those components into an account of perception, action, and experience.
4. **The deciding test:** what would distinguish that account from alternative explanations with
   comparable observations and learning resources.

An observation about a dendrite, a cellular current, or a phase-sensitive population can support
a specific component without establishing the entire consciousness proposal. An equation can
describe an operation without showing that living tissue implements that description. Retaining
these distinctions preserves both the source's positive conjecture and the actual experimental
result. See [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries|SAN Scale Ladder and Claim Boundaries]].

## A learned reference is part of the mechanism

Cycle 15's learning-pixel example asks what changes when elements learn which surrounding
patterns precede their own activity. The original public
[b0236y text](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/cd95efe985f2b21cca69eb3fd253cf473a268587/b0236y%20ctpr.txt)
connects written words, visual features, sound, and the network's own changing response. Its
red-shirt example locates recognition in the participating system's adjustment, rather than in
another observer watching a finished picture. These are thought experiments and proposed neural
operations, not measurements of a learning television cube.

That argument deepens [[tonic-context-rare-deviation-tomographic-language|Tonic Context and Rare Deviations]]:
the reference can be an active, learned receiving condition. [[information-rarity|Information Rarity]]
then asks a particular probability question about departures from it. A common event can still
matter to the receiver, and a rare event's surprisal does not by itself explain its meaning.

## A complete operation and a prospective prediction

SAN follows an event through its receiving route, consequence, and subsequent tuning. A test
predicting that consequence must distinguish the observations available beforehand from the
response measured later. Subsequent responses can become history for still later predictions;
the recurrent operation remains intact.

[[phase-wave-differential-calculus|Prediction, Information, and Causal Evidence]] explains why
information beyond a reduced summary, a useful representation of the same observations, and
causal evidence about a biological receiver are separate results. This formalization is one
candidate description of PWD-bearing relations. It does not define or outrank core PWD.

## Current edition and source access

The October 11 edition contains the Matrix Epilogue and a separate Afterword bridging toward the
planned third book's QGTCD and Super Information Theory discussion. The Epilogue's source-recognition
question belongs with [[simulation-consciousness|Simulation and Consciousness]]; it is distinct
from recognizing or segmenting an object within an experienced scene.

The [Book 2 reader](https://san-book-2-private-reader.micah3.workers.dev/san-book-2-latest-chapters-reader#front-matter)
uses the existing protected access. Its review-edition date identifies this edition, not the
first composition or public fixation of all its arguments. Original public notes and recordings
retain their own dates, authorship, and evidence boundaries. The Encyclopedia's explanations and
public source routes remain available independently of access to that reader.
