## Before Calling A Brain State Better, Define Better

The Neo Mind Cycle source begins with an honest problem. EEG can change during a
session, and a participant can report a change afterward, but neither fact alone
shows that the brain was "optimized."

The operation must be stated first:

```text
declare the person, task, target, and intended benefit
-> measure a baseline and relevant uncertainty
-> apply a controlled intervention
-> verify whether the neural target changed
-> verify whether the intended function changed
-> compare against sham, control, repeat, and follow-up conditions
-> record adverse and unintended effects
```

Systematic reviews find candidate neurofeedback effects while also identifying
weak controls and reporting problems. This supports the need for better
measurement; it does not validate every protocol or participant anecdote.

The same source opens a second question. Neural activity travels through
three-dimensional tissue with directional anatomy, convergence, divergence, and
recurrent feedback. Research on spike propagation distinguishes asynchronous
rate and synchronous-event modes whose stability depends on network structure.
The useful claim is therefore richer than "the brain is 3D," but narrower than
a universal six-axis cortical-column code.

SAN extends the measurement operation with a multimodal
read-intervene-readback hypothesis. A candidate electrophysiological change and
a candidate experiential or behavioral change should be measured separately,
then tested for reproducible covariance. Later APME, PWD, neural-rendering, or
consciousness terms are not backdated into the 2021 source.

## A Visual System Both Receives And Constructs

The visual-dots source resists two overly simple stories:

- the brain receives a pixel-perfect internal photograph;
- the brain receives almost no structured evidence and freely invents the rest.

The plain problem is to discover what information the system receives, pools,
loses, predicts, and samples again. Foveal detail, peripheral statistics,
crowding, eye movement, filling-in, memory, and task context all contribute.

A sparse-display VR experiment can make the question testable. Remove defined
classes of visual information, preserve others, and ask whether a target
percept, discrimination, or action remains possible. One failed sparse stimulus
does not refute all efficient coding; one successful stimulus does not show that
ordinary vision uses only those cues.

SAN extends the experiment into a receptor-to-render-to-action accounting
problem:

```text
structured retinal input
-> transformed retinal output
-> active gaze and repeated sampling
-> cortical and recurrent transformation
-> viewpoint-limited scene
-> action
-> changed viewpoint and next sample
```

The resulting rendering belongs to the distributed system. No little viewer is
placed behind it.

## Query A Dynamic Network, Then Test The Model

The brain-query source asks whether future reconstruction could learn a
network's functional organization through controlled interaction rather than
one perfect static scan.

The HTTP and FTP language is earned only after the operation is clear:

```text
supply a known perturbation at a declared access point
-> measure a constrained response
-> estimate a state or transformation rule
-> predict the response to a new perturbation
-> test the prediction
-> update or reject the model
```

Neural-code research shows why transmission matters as well as representation:
information must reach and affect another specialized population. TMS modeling
shows that local electric-field distributions can be used to estimate activation
thresholds for specified biophysical neuron models.

The comparison stops well before mind uploading. Brains expose no semantic HTTP
endpoint; current TMS does not query arbitrary memories; functional adequacy,
personal identity, and first-person continuity remain different questions.

SAN and NerveGear extend the operation into a bounded
read-transform-infer-write-return protocol with uncertainty, held-out tests,
consent, reversibility, and independent behavioral readback.

## Shared Control Preserves Intention And Override

The envelope-car source contains an engineering operation stronger than its
speculative vehicle shell. A person supplies goals through gaze, gesture, voice,
touch, or simple commands while an autonomous system handles continuous
low-level control. The system returns its interpretation through AR, haptics,
motion, or another visible state so the person can confirm, correct, stop, or
take over.

Research shows that gaze remains active during automated driving and that
human-machine intent can be blended continuously in a shared-control prosthesis.
These are component demonstrations, not proof of a Level 5 vehicle, a sentient
computer, or the source's extreme material-performance forecasts.

SAN's added proposal is a declared-intent loop:

```text
multichannel human signal
-> candidate intent plus uncertainty
-> confirmation when risk is high
-> bounded autonomous action
-> returned consequence
-> correction, interruption, or override
```

The same source connects dialogue, generative imagery, 2D-to-3D reconstruction,
and volumetric scenes. That is product genealogy for intuitive spatial
interfaces and NerveGear, not evidence that current generators reproduce
dreams.

## A Self-Model Is Updated By Its Own Consequences

Three sources converge on one operation.

- The dark-house dream distinguishes persistent structure from current
  activation and imagines one active coalition recruiting a wider quiet system.
- The action-perception source treats self-knowledge as a loop of directed
  attention, action, consequence, and revision.
- The Dolores source treats one process as predicting or evaluating another
  process in the same agent.

Plainly:

```text
current self-and-world model
-> intended action or internally generated output
-> returned sensory or internal consequence
-> match or mismatch
-> updated model and next action
```

Speech and reality-monitoring experiments connect self-agency with prediction
of self-generated consequences. They do not establish that two fictional
characters, two hemispheres, or the two poles of a current dipole are one
self-awareness mechanism. Neural current dipoles are measurable summaries of
current distribution, not hidden observers.

SAN extends the established pieces with a recursive, distributed rendering
hypothesis. The self-model is the network's ongoing prediction-and-update
organization; observation is performed by the interacting receivers,
transformers, projectors, and action loops.

## Artificial Neurology Requires Development, Not Just Assembly

The sentient-crops, relational-robot, and humans-as-robots sources approach
Artificial Neurology from different directions.

The combined plain-language problem is:

```text
initial body, constraints, and assigned role
-> repeated sensing, action, teaching, correction, and relationship
-> learned policy and autobiographical history
-> self-and-other model
-> possible competence, agency, awareness, or experience
```

These stages must not be collapsed. A learned role is not automatically a self;
a self-model is not automatically phenomenal consciousness; competence is not
automatically personhood. The biological comparison is nevertheless useful:
human development already joins gene regulation, cellular signaling,
homeostasis, embodiment, learning, and social feedback over years. A serious
artificial-mind architecture must specify which comparable operations it
implements.

SAN's extension is a developmental and ethical measurement program. Assigned
role, learned policy, relationship history, autobiographical continuity,
self-model, capability, possible experience, welfare, autonomy, and legal
status are evaluated separately.
