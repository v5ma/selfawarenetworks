# Synchronized Firing Patterns

**Synchronized firing patterns** are the temporally-aligned spike trains across populations of neurons that, in [[self-aware-networks]], are treated as the *physical realisation of beliefs, facts, and learned representations*. The SAN reading takes the same population-synchrony phenomenon mainstream [[neuroscience]] uses to describe [[oscillatory-binding-problem|binding]] and recasts it as the substrate on which [[engrams|engrams]], coincident percepts, and the [[ndca|NDCA]] reconstruction all run.

## Roots

The strongest definitional sentence in the corpus places synchronised firing at the substrate level for *belief itself*: "we assign to a belief, but neurologically speaking these beliefs, or facts, or theories may all be synchronized firing patterns, representing what has been learned overtime, through the empirical process of life" (`a0093z--md`, line 17, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0093z.md); restated at `a0095z--md`, line 20, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0095z.md)). The SAN manuscript chapter 2 frames the same concept in terms of [[neural-oscillations|neural-oscillation]] perturbations that occur "when neurons deviate from synchronized firing patterns" (`02san--md`, line 174, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/02san.md)) — the perturbation reading lets SAN connect synchronised firing directly to [[long-term-depression|LTD]] / [[long-term-potentiation|LTP]] outcomes.

## Position

In SAN proper, synchronised firing patterns serve three functions. First, they are the substrate on which [[belief-as-spatiotemporal-sequence|beliefs as spatiotemporal sequences]] are realised — the `a0093z` / `a0095z` claim that beliefs *are* synchronised firing patterns is load-bearing. Second, they are the substrate of plasticity: "neurons with synchronized firing patterns are more likely to form stable connections" (`GPT2023X--md`, line 1594, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2023X.md)) — the SAN reading of [[long-term-potentiation|LTP]] reduces to selective stabilisation of co-firing populations. Third, they implement [[oscillatory-binding-problem|binding]]: "[[oscillatory-synchrony|oscillatory synchrony]] — neurons often fire in synchrony, with rhythmic activation patterns at specific frequencies. These synchronized firing patterns are thought to play a role in neural communication and binding" (`GPT2024Jan1toJan22--md`, line 2165, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/GPT2024Jan1toJan22.md)). NotebookML_6 ties [[long-term-depression|LTD]] to *de-synchronisation*: "the reduction in synaptic strength when neurons exhibit less synchronized firing patterns. Furthermore, [[brainwave]] oscillations, as described by [[gyorgy-buzsaki|Buzsaki]] in 'Rhythms of the' Brain" supply the macroscopic frame (`NotebookML_6_Collection--md`, line 130).

## Post-SAN

Mainstream [[neuroscience]] treats synchronized firing patterns through three overlapping bodies of literature: [[oscillatory-binding-problem|binding-by-synchrony]] (Singer, von der Malsburg), [[gamma-oscillations|gamma]]-band feature integration, and [[gyorgy-buzsaki|Buzsáki]]'s "[[rhythms-of-the-brain|Rhythms of the Brain]]" framework that NotebookML_6 cites directly. The SAN extension that mainstream literature does not commit to is the strong identity claim: that beliefs *are* synchronised firing patterns (not merely *correlated with* them). This is a falsifiable position — it predicts that perturbing the synchronisation itself, with [[phase-reset|phase reset]] that preserves rate and amplitude, should perturb the corresponding belief structure. The SAN reading is broadly compatible with [[free-energy-principle|free-energy-principle]]-style accounts of [[cortex]] (synchronisation as the stable solution of variational inference) but commits more strongly to identity than the Friston framework does.
