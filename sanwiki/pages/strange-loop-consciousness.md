## Position

Within [[self-aware-networks]], the strange loop is a particular topology of [[ndca|NDCA]]: the system samples its own [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] reconstruction as one input among many, so the next reconstruction includes the prior reconstruction as content.
