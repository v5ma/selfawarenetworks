Mainstream glymphatic-system research (Iliff, Nedergaard, Mestre and collaborators) has steadily moved toward agreement with parts of SAN's framing — astrocyte aquaporin-4 dependence, sleep-state-dependent clearance flux, and a role in waste removal that turns out to influence neural excitability.
