# Predictions, Choice, and Behavior: b0160y

## Source boundary

This recording was made on **October 4, 2021**. Its rough transcript was first publicly Git-fixed
on **June 8, 2022** and explicitly marked "audio transcription needs fixing." A later
large-v3-turbo retranscription was used to recover obvious speech-to-text errors. The cleaned
reading below does not assign later terminology or later scientific support to the 2021 recording.

The immutable first public source remains available in the
[Self Aware Networks repository](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/09b8306fbb54536e20ba1071e91dff0cb8a4770f/b0160y%20ctpr.txt).

## Recovered source operation

The recording joins four ideas:

1. A self-model develops by associating sensory consequences with the organism's own motor
   activity.
2. Candidate actions can be evaluated through predicted consequences at more than one time
   horizon.
3. Present context and accumulated experience jointly shape which behavior becomes effective.
4. Choice can remain physically determined while the embodied computational system remains the
   causal system that sensed, predicted, acted, and received the result.

```text
sensory evidence + motor history + learned experience
-> candidate future consequences
-> comparison across short and long horizons
-> one selected behavior
-> embodied result
```

The recording's phrase "the brightest and most appealing path chooses you" is best read as an
image for value- and prediction-weighted selection. It does not establish a literal brightness
code, a single reward variable, or one anatomical chooser.

## What this source does and does not establish

**Historical SAN claim:** prediction and learned experience can constrain a physically lawful
choice without requiring a separate soul or inner executive outside the nervous system.

**Established scientific bridge:** neural systems can represent candidate actions, integrate
context and evidence, encode goals, and change behavior through distributed cortical,
subcortical, and sensorimotor circuits. Examples include simultaneous reach-option signals in
macaque premotor cortex ([Cisek and Kalaska, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2005.01.027)),
context-dependent recurrent population dynamics
([Mante et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12742)), and goal-dependent hippocampal theta
sequences ([Wikenheiser and Redish, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3909)).

**Later SAN extension:** the 2022 Choice material adds learned cellular criteria, temporary
effective neuropaths, inhibitory and disinhibitory route selection, receiver-relative phase, and
returned consequence.

**Not established by this recording:** one frequency band, one cortical layer, the prefrontal
cortex alone, or one universal reinforcement-learning algorithm implements all choices.

## Scale boundary

- **Cellular:** a neuron undergoes state-dependent integration and output; calling that transition
  a complete organism-level choice would anthropomorphize the cell.
- **Circuit:** competing population trajectories and effective routes can be biased, suppressed,
  recruited, and committed under a particular context.
- **Organism:** the full choice includes prediction, valuation, body state, action, consequence,
  report, and learning across the embodied system.
