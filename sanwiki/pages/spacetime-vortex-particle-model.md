The **Spacetime Vortex Particle Model** proposes that subatomic particles are not
objects embedded in spacetime but are instead localized contractions and expansions
of space itself — vortex-like phase patterns whose oscillatory behavior generates
the spacetime geometry they appear to inhabit.

## Core Claims

- **Atoms as vortices**: An atom is a vortex in spacetime, with atomic oscillations
  producing repeated phase patterns that collectively constitute local spacetime curvature.
- **Phase patterns as space**: The time required for a particle to traverse a path
  creates the phase pattern that *is* that path — spacetime emerges from particle
  activity rather than being a pre-existing container.
- **Electron as state transition**: An electron represents the activity of space
  folding between two conjugate states: having a definite position versus having a
  definite momentum. This maps to [[waveparticle-duality]] at the geometric level.
- **Scale-dependent time dilation**: Particle behavior remains statistically undefined
  until observation because observation implies scale coupling; the observed scale
  sets the effective time-dilation frame.
- **Compound vortices**: Combined atomic vortices produce larger structures with
  amplified spacetime warping — a fractal scaling of gravitational effect that
  anticipates [[gravity-of-attention]] and [[qgtcd]].

## SAN Relevance

This model is an early seed of [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s later [[qgtcd]] (Quantum Gradient Time
Crystal Dilation) framework, where temporal gradients at the quantum scale propagate
upward through scale hierarchies — the same fractal-scaling intuition as
[[fractal-consciousness]]. The vortex framing also resonates with the [[self-aware-network]]
principle that network nodes shape the medium in which they operate.

These ideas predate the formalized SAN framework. The draft is explicitly speculative
