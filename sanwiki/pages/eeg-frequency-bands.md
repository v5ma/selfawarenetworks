# EEG Frequency Bands

**EEG Frequency Bands** are the conventional frequency partitions of the human electroencephalogram — delta (≈0.5–4 Hz), theta (≈4–8 Hz), alpha (≈8–13 Hz), beta (≈13–30 Hz), and gamma (≈30–100+ Hz) — that the [[self-aware-network|Self-Aware Networks]] framework treats not as independent rhythms but as carriers in a [[oscillating-network-of-oscillators|nested oscillator network]] whose phase relationships do the cognitive work. In SAN, naming a "band" is a measurement convenience; the explanatory unit is the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differential]] across bands and the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|Dominant Phase Wave Differential Rhythm]] that arises when those differentials cohere.

## Position

SAN treats frequency bands as measurement ranges within interacting oscillator networks, not as independent organs. Its [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|Gamma Consideration Sandwich]] makes one specific functional proposal: alpha-associated primary-sensory-side input and beta-associated prefrontal thought/top-down input form the top bun; Layer-2/3 gamma performs consideration and proprioceptive coordination; and Layer-5 theta/gamma carries action-side output while interacting with returned body input in the bottom bun. These are defined SAN roles, not exclusive functions of those bands in every brain circuit.

Cross-frequency coupling can relate activity measured in different ranges, including theta-gamma nesting and alpha/beta-gamma relations. SAN asks whether receiver-relative phase relations among such events contribute to [[napot-theory|NAPOT]] reconstruction and [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]]. A coupling statistic or band-power peak alone does not establish tomography, a fixed cognitive role, or consciousness.

Third, the bands are the *signature of dominant rhythms*: when a [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differential]] crosses the dominance threshold and becomes a [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|DPWDR]], the band signature of that DPWDR — typically a sharp-band burst against the surrounding broadband activity — is what makes it visible in scalp EEG and in intracranial recordings, and is what couples to [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripple]] events in the hippocampal literature. The bands are therefore *how SAN rhythms become measurable* — the experimental anchor of the framework.

Fourth, the bands are the *target of intervention* in [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]], [[brainwave-driven-photic-stimulation|photic stimulation]], [[neuromodulation-brainwave-entrainment|neuromodulation-driven entrainment]], [[eeg-biofeedback-self-reflective-loop|EEG biofeedback]], and [[eeg-xr-neurofeedback-bci|XR neurofeedback BCI]]. The therapeutic pages in the corpus — [[oscillatory-ptsd-therapy|oscillatory PTSD therapy]], [[dreams-as-theta-wave-sequences|theta-wave dream sequences]] — all act on band-level structure as their handle on phenomenology, which is why an accurate concept page for *EEG Frequency Bands* sits upstream of the entire SAN clinical line.

## Post-SAN

The EEG-band literature is enormous and predates SAN, so the "Post-SAN" relation here is mostly *re-reading* rather than novelty. Where SAN agrees with the mainstream: bands are real, they index cognition, cross-frequency coupling is informative. Where SAN reframes: the bands are not generators of cognitive content but *carriers* of phase relationships, and the cognitive content lives in the [[phase-wave-differentials|differentials]] rather than in the band amplitudes; the consequence is that band-power analyses miss the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|DPWDR]]-class structure that phase-relational analyses would surface. The [[earl-k-miller-corpus-priority-review|Miller-corpus crosswalk]] makes this explicit: rotating theta/alpha/beta waves in prefrontal cortex during working memory match the SAN reading at the band-and-phase level. The closest published external converger is the cortical traveling-wave literature ([[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|cortical traveling waves]], [[cortical-traveling-waves-san-submission|the SAN submission]]). Specific predictions that follow from the SAN reading: (i) DPWDR-onset signatures should ride on [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripples]] rather than on broadband band-power increases; (ii) the [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|Gamma Consideration Sandwich]] should show alpha-associated primary-sensory input and beta-associated prefrontal thought/top-down input converging on Layer-2/3 gamma consideration and proprioceptive coordination, followed by Layer-5 theta/gamma action and returned body evidence; (iii) [[brainwave-entrainment|entrainment]] should affect [[neural-rendering-of-qualia|qualia rendering]] selectively at frequencies that match the DPWDR carrier in the modality being entrained.
