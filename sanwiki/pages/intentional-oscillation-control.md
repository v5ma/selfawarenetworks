# Intentional Oscillation Control

**Intentional Oscillation Control** is the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]]-adjacent claim that a person can deliberately *modulate their own brain oscillations* — adjust amplitude, frequency, or phase relationships across [[brain-rhythms|brain rhythms]] — through trained [[attention]], [[consciousness-as-neurofeedback|neurofeedback]], or [[neomindcycle-program|NeoMindCycle]]-style cognitive-audio control, and that this voluntary modulation is the substrate-level lever behind [[neo-mind-cycle|NeoMindCycle]] practice. It is the user-facing companion to [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]] (where the modulation is externally driven) and to [[consciousness-as-oscillatory-feedback-loop|the oscillatory-feedback-loop]] account of [[consciousness]].

## Post-SAN

Mainstream EEG-biofeedback and contemplative-neuroscience already accept that operators can learn to modulate specific rhythms (alpha, SMR, gamma) and that the modulation has measurable behavioural and clinical consequences. SAN sharpens this into a *substrate-level* commitment with two testable consequences. First, the SAN reading predicts that the *content* of conscious experience — not only its arousal level — should be measurably different across operator-selected oscillatory regimes; specifically, the *phase relationships* across [[cortical-traveling-waves|cortical traveling waves]] under deliberate modulation should produce *predictable* shifts in [[consciousness-canvas-wave-differential|wave-differential canvas]] content, not just shifts in vigilance. Second, by binding intentional oscillation control to the [[consciousness-as-high-bandwidth-choice-mechanism|high-bandwidth choice mechanism]], SAN predicts that the *bandwidth* of operator-selectable regimes should be measurable in bits-per-second and should be bounded above by the [[choice]]-mechanism bandwidth — a stronger and more falsifiable claim than the published neurofeedback literature commits to. Where SAN converges with mainstream work is in the [[consciousness-as-neurofeedback|neurofeedback]] account of [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modulation, in the contemplative-neuroscience literature on trained operators, and in the long EEG-biofeedback tradition that already accepts learned rhythm-control as a real phenomenon.
