# Entification and the Distributed Observer

## The problem before the term

SAN asks how a distributed physical system becomes a coordinated observer operation: it detects
changes in its own tonic field-cell state, uses those differences to select actions, and is changed
by the returning consequences. The observer is sparse and distributed across the participating
cells, pathways, body states, and timescales.

**Entification** is Micah's term for that proposed formation of a functionally unified entity from
many interacting oscillatory, cellular, and network processes.

## Minimal operational definition

An entified system would need more than simultaneous activity. At minimum it would require:

1. bounded but permeable organization;
2. recurrent access to internal and external state;
3. integration across relevant timescales;
4. selective propagation rather than universal synchrony;
5. memory-dependent state transitions;
6. prediction or consideration of alternatives;
7. action that changes the system's future input; and
8. a persistent self-related coordinate or control model.

Synchrony may contribute to coordination, but synchrony alone does not prove an observer,
consciousness, or personhood.

## Relation to Neural Rendering

The perceptual-screen problem concerns how a model is constructed. The observer problem concerns
how the constructing tissue is also the system that uses the model. SAN's answer is recursive and
embodied: local arrays sense, transform, project, and receive changes; larger loops integrate those
operations with body state, memory, prediction, and action; the aggregate process is the observer.

The stronger [[field-cell-self-bioelectric-information-entity|field-cell self]] route proposes
that the reciprocal process among cells, shared bioelectric organization, memory, action, and
feedback is the continuing information entity rather than a display presented to something else.

Two source layers make this route concrete. `a0404z.md`, internally dated November 1, 2013, says
that cells are the tissue sensing the changing activity and that a copied system becomes a
differently situated trajectory. `a0417z.md` preserves an April 2014 category-theory discussion
and then a clearly marked 2022 Micah retrospective in which many arrays register partial structure
while multiscale oscillatory activity binds those registrations with the body and its causal
action. The observer is the organized network operation, not a little witness behind it.

## Scientific posture

Distributed control, recurrent processing, self-modeling, interoception, and sensorimotor feedback
are empirical research areas. Entification is SAN's stronger constitutive proposal about when those
operations amount to an observer. It requires operational criteria and comparative tests against
global-workspace, predictive-processing, recurrent-processing, higher-order, active-inference, and
other accounts.

## Book route

The term should appear after the reader understands the distributed observer in ordinary language.
The book can then define entification as a proposed operational unification process and spend the
rest of the argument specifying what would distinguish it from mere correlation or synchrony.
