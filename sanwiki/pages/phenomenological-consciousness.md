**Phenomenological [consciousness](consciousness.md)** is the central explanandum of [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks.md) — the *first-person rendered field* of redness, A-tones, sound, smell, motion, and [self](gh-a0206z.md) that a brain produces — and within SAN it is identified, repeatedly and explicitly, as a *rendering*: a *temporally entified volumetric representation of reality* generated by an [oscillating-network-of-oscillators](oscillating-network-of-oscillators.md) under [NAPOT](napot-theory.md) / [neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography](neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography.md) dynamics, with the [brain-as-rendering-engine](brain-as-rendering-engine.md) as the substrate and [criterial-causation](criterial-causation.md) as the readout. It is the primary target of [Micah](gh-resnote04.md)'s solution to the [hard-problem-of-consciousness](hard-problem-of-consciousness.md) and the head concept under which [theory-of-redness](theory-of-redness.md), [neural-rendering-of-qualia](neural-rendering-of-qualia.md), [oscillatory-qualia-imprinting](oscillatory-qualia-imprinting.md), [consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop](consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop.md), and [consciousness-persistent-rendering](consciousness-persistent-rendering.md) are organised.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream [consciousness](consciousness.md) studies has used "phenomenological [consciousness](consciousness.md)" or its near-synonyms (Block's "phenomenal [consciousness](consciousness.md)," Chalmers's "experience," Nagel's "what-it-is-likeness") as the explanandum of the [hard-problem-of-consciousness](hard-problem-of-consciousness.md) for decades. The [Self](gh-a0206z.md)-Aware Networks reading takes the same explanandum but commits to a specific *mechanism*: phenomenological [consciousness](consciousness.md) is a rendering produced by a [brain-as-rendering-engine](brain-as-rendering-engine.md) running [NAPOT](napot-theory.md) / [neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography](neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography.md) dynamics over an [oscillating-network-of-oscillators](oscillating-network-of-oscillators.md), with [criterial-causation](criterial-causation.md) as the readout.
