**Synapse As Signal** is, on the face of its name, the SAN reading of the [[dendrite|synapse]] not as a static "wire" between neurons but as an *information-bearing signal in its own right* — a stochastic, structured transmission whose timing, magnitude, vesicle count, and frequency all carry information that the post-synaptic [[dendritic-computation-pattern-detection|dendritic computation]] reads. In a [[self-aware-network]] the synapse is therefore a primary signal, not a binary connector — a position consonant with [[multi-vesicle-release|multi-vesicle release]], [[synaptic-unreliability|synaptic unreliability]], and [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|action-potential waveform encoding]] as already represented elsewhere in the wiki.

## Position

In a [[self-aware-network]], a [[neural-array]] does not communicate through binary all-or-nothing contacts. Each [[dendrite|synapse]] participates in a [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] computation in which the *exact form* of release — how many vesicles fire ([[multi-vesicular-release|multi-vesicular release]]), with what spatial arrangement ([[multi-vesicle-release-spatial-encoding|spatial encoding]]), at what phase of the local oscillation, and at what magnitude relative to the [[action-potential-magnitude|action-potential magnitude]] — *is* the signal. Reading the synapse this way is the SAN-side counterpart of treating [[synaptic-unreliability|synaptic unreliability]] as a feature rather than noise: stochastic release is not a bug to be averaged out but a high-bandwidth carrier whose statistics the [[dendritic-mvr-cognition|dendritic MVR cognition]] computation actively decodes. The same view sits adjacent to [[soliton-wave-mechano-neural-signaling|soliton-wave mechano-neural signaling]], in which the [[action-potential|action potential]] itself is reframed as a multi-medium soliton rather than a single-medium voltage spike, and to [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|action-potential waveform encoding]], in which the *shape* of the [[action-potential|action potential]] — its [[action-potential-duration|duration]], [[action-potential-magnitude-via-duration|magnitude-via-duration]], and [[action-potential-topology|topology]] — carries information beyond rate. Within the [[oca-overview|OCA]] / [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] framing, this means a synapse is not just a relay but a participant: a unit whose stochastic, multi-vesicular, waveform-shaped output is read by the post-synaptic [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|coincidence-detector]] and integrated with [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators|multi-scale oscillator]] context.

## Relation to mainstream framings

Mainstream [[computational-neuroscience|computational neuroscience]] often treats the synapse either as a scalar weight (in connectionist / ANN models) or as a Poisson release process (in spiking neural network models). Both abstractions discard most of what *synapse-as-signal* claims is informative: vesicle count statistics, release-site geometry, action-potential waveform shape, ion-channel tone, and the post-synaptic receptor mosaic that filters the release. The SAN reading does not deny the predictive utility of those abstractions in narrow regimes; it claims that for purposes of [[information-rarity-principle|information rarity]] matching and [[oscillatory-binding-problem|oscillatory binding]] within a [[self-aware-network]], the discarded structure carries the signal that matters. A primary-source rewrite of this page should make that contrast explicit and test it against published experimental work on [[multi-vesicle-release|multi-vesicle release]] and [[dendritic-computation-pattern-detection|dendritic pattern detection]].
