## What Draft 3 was trying to say

Draft 3 proposed a recurring systems operation:

`distributed sensory and internal inputs -> synaptic and dendritic pattern sensitivity -> branch, somatic, and axon-initial-segment transformation -> axonal and terminal projection -> overlapping downstream population responses -> recurrent oscillatory coordination -> distributed working-model reconstruction -> action and sensory return`

This chain is more precise than the draft's camera, microphone, television, and speaker language. The draft explicitly said that no LED lights exist in the brain. Those objects were explanatory stand-ins for biological reception, transformation, and redistribution.

The central SAN proposition is not that a little viewer watches an internal screen. It is that the active distributed network performs the observing operation. Cellular receivers, axonal transmitters, recurrent populations, body-state pathways, and motor consequences collectively produce an observer-action process. See [[screen-metaphor-no-inner-viewer|Distributed Observer and the Screen Metaphor]].

## Translating the historical metaphors

| Historical language | Source-faithful biological translation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dendrite as an eye or microphone | Dendritic branches receive synaptic input and can perform compartment-specific, nonlinear transformations. | Dendrites are not optical or acoustic sensors unless they belong to a specialized sensory receptor pathway. |
| Exit terminal as an LED or speaker | Axons and presynaptic boutons redistribute a cell's output to anatomically connected receivers. | A bouton is not a pixel, and one cell does not display a complete percept. |
| Inceptive field | The set of downstream targets influenced through an axon's arbor and terminals, interpreted at population scale. | This is SAN terminology, not a standard anatomical field and not output through an apical dendrite. |
| Tomography | A hypothesis that multiple partial, time-varying neural projections can support reconstruction of an internal working model. | The brain is not claimed to run a literal CT scan, optical hologram, or hidden screen. |
| The oscillator is the observer | Observation is attributed to distributed recurrent activity and its embodied consequences. | Synchrony alone is not sufficient evidence of perception or consciousness. |
| Memory scaling | Local plasticity changes later receiver and ensemble readiness, helping partial cues recruit distributed activity. | One synapse, neuron, ripple, or waveform is not an identical copy of a whole memory. |
| Tonic canvas and phasic ink | A maintained receiver-relative context is changed by transient, differentiated events. | Tonic and phasic are functional roles, not permanent slow and fast frequency bands. |

## Scale ladder

### Micro: cellular receive-transform-project motif

Established physiology supports synaptic reception, nonlinear dendritic integration, somatic and axon-initial-segment spike initiation, axonal conduction, and probabilistic transmitter release from presynaptic terminals. SAN interprets this as a candidate receive-transform-project motif. One neuron does not perform the entire tomography operation and does not contain a complete picture.

### Meso: local population transformation

Local excitatory and inhibitory populations combine partial inputs, maintain state, compete, cooperate, and change their population trajectories through recurrent interactions. Draft 3's "array" is best read as a declared population in a particular preparation, not as one universal cortical-column algorithm.

### Macro: distributed reconstruction and action

Sensory, thalamic, association, memory, interoceptive, and motor systems interact through anatomically constrained recurrent routes. A working model is distributed across participating populations and changes with action and sensory return. No single convergence point, frequency, region, or bright central object is the observer.

## Evidence boundary

### Established or directly supported components

- Thin pyramidal-cell dendrites can exhibit branch-specific nonlinear integration.
- Presynaptic calcium influences neurotransmitter release nonlinearly and probabilistically.
- Learning can alter selected synapses and dendritic spines, and targeted manipulation of those traces can affect task performance in bounded preparations.
- Memory-related ensembles and synaptic connections can overlap while preserving distinguishable content, and engram-related activity can be distributed across multiple brain regions.
- Recurrent processing contributes to some visual recognition behavior.
- Feedforward and feedback influences can occupy different frequency channels in particular primate visual preparations.

These findings support components of the architecture. They do not establish NAPOT, PWD, a universal oscillatory code, or SAN's consciousness claim.

### SAN interpretation and open hypotheses

- A receiver-relative phase-wave differential may describe a typed change in timing, phase, frequency, amplitude, duration, release, and network context.
- Ongoing population context and differentiated transient events may jointly shape a distributed working model.
- Learned cellular and circuit changes may support pathway-specific, multi-scale reinstatement without copying one representation identically across scales.
- A tomography-like reconstruction may become scientifically meaningful if declared projections permit held-out reconstruction and selective causal perturbation.
- The distributed recurrent network, including embodied action and return, may constitute the observer operation rather than merely supply content to an inner observer.

### Claims not established by the source or current evidence

- Every neural array receives or transmits a complete picture.
- All relevant cells must share one phase or one gamma-frequency carrier.
- Tonic activity is always slow, high-magnitude, and low-information, while phasic activity is always fast, low-magnitude, and high-information.
- Action-potential duration provides a universal deterministic `0/1/2/3` vesicle alphabet.
- A sharp-wave ripple scales every local memory to the whole brain.
- Adding oscillatory loops to an artificial neural network is sufficient for sentience.
- A literal hologram, tomogram, screen, or localized observer exists in the brain.

## Modern medically accurate companion image needed

Prompt `IMG-SAN-WHITEPAPER-DRAFT3-001` is registered for a dedicated image session. It calls for a historical-to-modern translation plate with:

1. a small, clearly labeled strip of the historical metaphor images;
2. a bounded pyramidal-neuron receive-transform-project panel;
3. a multi-cell recurrent local-population panel;
4. a distributed sensory, thalamic, association, memory, body-state, and motor network panel;
5. an evidence ladder separating established physiology, supported interpretation, and SAN hypothesis; and
6. a causal reconstruction test with perturbation, matched controls, and rescue.

The modern image must not use literal eyes, screens, speakers, one generic neuron connected directly to many systems, a universal laminar circuit, a central observer, a universal gamma carrier, a deterministic vesicle alphabet, or science-fiction brain glow.

## Testable residuals

Draft 3 becomes experimentally stronger when its metaphors are converted into competing measurements:

1. Define a receiver, baseline window, anatomical route, and candidate PWD vector.
2. Test whether the full receiver-relative vector predicts a later population state, perception, or action better than firing rate, spectral power, phase alone, and raw activity history.
3. Record several declared projections and test whether they reconstruct a held-out state or relation.
4. Perturb one route or timing relation while matching mean activity, and predict a selective reconstruction or behavioral deficit.
5. Attempt a route- and timing-specific rescue.
6. Compare NAPOT against strong recurrent, attractor, predictive-processing, and distributed-engram alternatives.

Failure of the receiver-relative variables to add predictive or causal information would weigh against the stronger SAN operator. Successful decoding alone would not establish consciousness.
