# Emotion As Axon Frequency

**Emotion As Axon Frequency** is the [[self-aware-networks|SAN]] reading that the *felt charge* of an affective moment is carried, in part, by the *frequency dimension* of axonal firing — that emotional valence and intensity are encoded in how fast and how rhythmically axons are propagating action potentials, not only in which neurotransmitters are released. It is a refinement of [[electromagnetic-emotions|electromagnetic emotions]] and [[electromagnetic-theory-of-feeling|the electromagnetic theory of feeling]], specifying *which dimension of the substrate* a particular emotional moment occupies.

## Position

In [[self-aware-networks|SAN]], an emotion is the felt aspect of a [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|dominant phase wave differential]] (DPWDR) on the [[consciousness-canvas-wave-differential|wave-differential canvas]]. Emotion-as-axon-frequency narrows that to a substrate claim: the rate and rhythmicity of axonal firing along the participating projection neurons is one of the principal *carriers* of the emotional charge, alongside the chemical biasers (see [[dopamine-as-pattern-selector]]) and the [[neuron-shape-information-storage|neuronal-shape signatures]] that route the charge through the network. The frequency dimension matters because, in SAN's [[phasic-tonic-relationship|phasic-tonic relationship]], phasic bursts ride a tonic background and the *band* in which the burst lies determines the qualitative texture of what is felt — a high-band burst-train is felt differently from a low-band one, even when the chemical context is held constant. This is consistent with [[tonic-powerband-reference-frames]] (frequency bands as macro reference frames), with [[tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame]] (band as the predictive context), and with the [[tonic-oscillation-canvas|tonic-oscillation canvas]] reading of awareness.

The axon-side specifics matter for grounding. An [[action-potential|action potential]] is not a featureless spike: its [[action-potential-waveform-encoding|waveform shape]] and its [[synaptic-ion-channel-tone|ion-channel tone]] both encode information, and along the axon it generates an [[action-potential-magnetic-phase-field|magnetic phase-field signature]] that participates in the broader [[magnetic-phase-field|magnetic phase field]]. When such a train fires at an emotionally distinctive frequency, the *pattern* propagated downstream is what reaches the [[oscillatory-binding-problem|binding]] machinery and the [[brain-as-rendering-engine|rendering engine]] that turns it into a felt qualia. [[oscillator-level-awareness|Oscillator-level awareness]] then makes the frequency-dimension feature *available* to other ensembles, and [[oscillator-consciousness-threshold]] is the cut at which the dimension's contribution becomes felt rather than latent. [[neural-timing-encoding|Timing encoding]] is therefore not in tension with frequency encoding here — it specifies the precision of the dimension, while frequency specifies the band.

The reading also clarifies why [[criterial-causation|criterial causation]] and [[criteria-causation-single-neuron-pattern-detection|single-neuron pattern detection]] are compatible with the doctrine: a single neuron does not fire only above threshold; it selects which input *patterns* count, and the patterns it accepts include frequency signatures. Emotion-as-axon-frequency therefore *factors* — chemistry biases pattern selection, axon frequency carries the charge, the canvas renders the felt content, and the dominance structure decides which charge is *the* current emotion.

## Post-SAN

In published affective neuroscience the standard story splits roughly between chemical (dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin) and circuit (limbic, prefrontal) accounts; rate-coding and timing-coding debates run in parallel inside [[computational-neuroscience|computational neuroscience]] without typically reaching into affect. The SAN-specific addition is the *bridge*: emotional valence and intensity are made tractable as features of the same frequency-and-timing substrate that already carries percept, with chemistry as a modulator of which patterns become dominant rather than as the carrier of the felt thing. This re-expression keeps the chemistry intact, agrees with [[neural-timing-encoding|timing-encoding]] research, and connects naturally with [[oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration|synchrony-as-binding]] for cross-modal affect.
