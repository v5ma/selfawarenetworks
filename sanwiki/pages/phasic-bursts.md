# Phasic Bursts

![Phasic Bursts tonic differential canvas](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/phasic-bursts/phasic-bursts-tonic-differential-canvas-20260629-v1.png)

This diagram shows the page's core mechanism: tonic oscillation supplies the low-frequency reference canvas, a criterial sensory or mnemonic match gates a short high-frequency phasic burst, and the SAN-relevant content is the phase-pattern differential between the baseline and the burst event.

**Phasic Bursts** is the [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks.md) term for short, high-frequency salvos of neural firing that break above the [tonic-oscillation](tonic-oscillation.md) baseline, and within SAN they are not noise on top of "real" tonic activity but the *content* of conscious experience: the [phase-wave-differentials](phase-wave-differentials.md) between tonic baseline and phasic-burst events are what the [brain-as-rendering-engine](brain-as-rendering-engine.md) renders as redness, as A-tones, as percepts. The slug is the population-level companion of [phasic-burst-firing](phasic-burst-firing.md) and the close kin of [burst-event](burst-event.md), [burstlets](burstlets.md), [neuronal-sequences-population-bursts](neuronal-sequences-population-bursts.md), and [sharp-wave-ripples](sharp-wave-ripples.md).

## Post-SAN

Mainstream neurophysiology already maps phasic burst events at multiple scales: hippocampal [sharp-wave ripples](sharp-wave-ripples.md) in the Buzsaki line ([Buzsaki 2015](https://doi.org/10.1002/hipo.22488)), high-gamma dynamics in cortical recordings ([Gaona et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.4722-10.2011)), [dopamine](dopamine.md) reward-prediction burst regimes ([Schultz et al. 1997](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.275.5306.1593)), and burst-coding in thalamic relay neurons ([Sherman 2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-2236(00)01714-8)). The Self-Aware Networks reading converges on those bodies of work but does three things the published literature tends not to. First, it makes phasic bursts *the content of experience* by way of the gh-a0053z / gh-a0306z claim that the *differential* between tonic and phasic *is* what gets rendered as redness, as A-tones, as percepts -- not a correlate of perception but a constitutive part of it. Second, it asserts a hard [criterial-causation](criterial-causation.md) coupling: stimulation alone does not create phasic bursts (gh-a0331z, line 274) -- only top-down criterial-pattern matches do. This is a falsifiable wedge against pure bottom-up burst accounts: SAN predicts that direct electrical stimulation in the absence of ongoing criterial-template state should fail to elicit the same phasic-burst signatures that arise from sensory or mnemonic input. Third, the gh-a0007z NAPOT-side claim that *"oscillatory synchrony is noising (the opposite of denoising) and absorbing high phasic bursts through oscillatory dissipation of the high phasic (action potenti..."* (`a0007z--md`, line 432, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0007z.md)) inverts the standard signal/noise framing: synchrony is what *absorbs* burst content into the tonic substrate, not what cleans noise out of it. Where mainstream burst-coding work treats bursts as a separate channel layered on rate codes, SAN treats them as the *deviation* whose phase-pattern differential against tonic *is* the message. This page does not yet ground the criterial-stimulation prediction beyond the 96 packet quotes; widening the Lane B corpus is the next step.
