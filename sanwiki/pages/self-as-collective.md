# Self As Collective

**Self As Collective** is the [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks) claim that what feels like a unified "self" is the coherent activity of a population -- neurons, dendrites, [cortical-columns](cortical-columns), organs, and the broader nervous system -- synchronized through [phase-wave-differentials](phase-wave-differentials) rather than housed in any one location. It situates [consciousness-as-prediction-determinism](consciousness-as-prediction-determinism) inside a [fractal-cortical-column](fractal-cortical-column) hierarchy where each [oscillator](oscillator) behaves as an [agentic-viewpoint](agentic-viewpoint) and the [perceptual-screen](perceptual-screen) is the moment-to-moment [summary](gh-b0049y) the collective produces.

> **Scientific status:** Distributed neural, bodily, and environmental processes can jointly shape
> behavior and self-report without a central inner viewer. It is not established that every cell or
> cortical column performs the same computation, possesses an independent viewpoint, or is an
> agent in the psychological sense. Similar organization at micro, meso, and macro scales does not
> prove a fractally repeated consciousness mechanism. Oscillator coordination in fireflies or
> societies likewise does not establish a collective conscious subject. These are SAN hypotheses
> whose historical source claims are preserved below, not settled neuroscience.

## Position

Within [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks) the position decomposes into three claims. First, *every cell is an agent*: chapter `07san.md` states that "individual cells within a network can behave as intelligent agents, adjusting their [actions](actions) based on local information and contributing to collective computational goals" (`07san--md`, line 592, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)) -- a claim that runs through [criterial-causation](criterial-causation), [cortical-column-voting](cortical-column-voting), and [fractal-agent](fractal-agent) / [fractal-agency](fractal-agency) equally. Second, *coherence scales fractally*: the [fractal-cortical-column](fractal-cortical-column) account holds that "each cell or column senses and transmits signals in ways that mirror the larger system's organization, 'scaling up' until a coherent sense of self emerges" (`draft5--md`, line 749, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/draft5.md)), so the [fractal-modules](fractal-modules) of brain organization repeat from synapse to [cortical-column](cortical-column) to whole-brain [oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration](oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration). Third, *the binding signal is phase*: chapter `a0254z.md` argues that "[phase wave differential](phase-wave-differential) acts as the ink of distinction making for a conscious entity from a brain, to a collective of fireflies" (`a0254z--md`, line 54, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0254z.md); same passage at `rexnote13--md`, line 57) -- a single mechanism that spans neural and supra-neural collectives. The result is that "maintaining a coherent sense of self" becomes one of the load-bearing functions of the brain's gamma-banded "[consideration sandwich](consideration-sandwich)" (`vecA--md`, line 693, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecA.md)), which integrates [holographic-attention-schema](holographic-attention-schema) and [attention-schema-holographic](attention-schema-holographic) reads of [attention](attention) into one collective state. ## Post-SAN

Compared to mainstream collective-self literatures, SAN's position is more mechanistic in three respects. First, where [distributed-cognition](distributed-cognition) (Hutchins-style) and [distributed-consciousness](distributed-consciousness) traditions describe the self as "spread across" tools, agents, and environment without specifying the binding signal, SAN names the signal: [phase-wave-differentials](phase-wave-differentials) running through [neural-oscillation-synchrony-deviation](neural-oscillation-synchrony-deviation) and [oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration](oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration) mismatches. Second, where [Michael Levin](michael-levin)'s "collective intelligence of cell swarms" frames cells as agents pursuing morphogenetic goals, SAN (`a0254z--md` line 99, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0254z.md)) treats the same intuition as a special case of [cellular-oscillating-tomography](cellular-oscillating-tomography) -- the carrier is oscillatory phase rather than bioelectric pattern alone, and the collective extends across [fractal-modules](fractal-modules) from synapse to [inter-brain-eeg-synchronization](inter-brain-eeg-synchronization) in [brain-to-brain-eeg-synchronization](brain-to-brain-eeg-synchronization) and [neural-synchronization-bonding-analogy](neural-synchronization-bonding-analogy) / [holonic-rhythm-social-bonding](holonic-rhythm-social-bonding) settings. Third, where [attention](attention)-schema accounts ([attention-schema-theory](attention-schema-theory)) reduce the felt self to a single self-model running on top of brain activity, SAN ([holographic-attention-schema](holographic-attention-schema), [attention-schema-holographic](attention-schema-holographic)) treats the felt self as a *holographic [summary](gh-b0049y)* the collective renders for itself -- an interference pattern over [distributed-phase-representations](distributed-phase-representations) rather than a localized model. The testable [prediction](prediction) the collective view sharpens is that disrupting [cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration](cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration) or [cortical-column-oscillatory-synchronization](cortical-column-oscillatory-synchronization) should fragment the felt self even when the [connectome](connectome) is intact -- consistent with [neuromodulation-brainwave-entrainment](neuromodulation-brainwave-entrainment) and clinical reports of dissociation under desynchronizing protocols.
