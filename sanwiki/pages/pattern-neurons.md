**Pattern neurons** are neurons (or clusters of neurons) whose primary function is to create representations of *parts* of a larger pattern, with sequential aggregation knitting smaller pieces into larger patterns across nested neural scales.

The core idea: each neuron in a cluster holds parameters that represent a fragment of a pattern. Higher-order clusters in the sequence gather these fragments and integrate them into progressively larger, more abstract representations. This hierarchical assembly operates at multiple structural scales simultaneously.

## Scales of pattern aggregation

Within [[neural-circuits]], within [[micro-columns]], and across [[cortical-columns]], the same knitting dynamic recurs — smaller pieces are aggregated into larger representations. The [[suprachiasmatic-nucleus]] (≈20 million neurons oscillating in unison) illustrates the large-scale extreme: an entire nucleus maintaining a single temporal cadence, functioning as a biological pacemaker that synchronizes distributed groups.

> "The idea is that you have clusters of nodes, with all of their parameters, basically creating each a representation of part of a pattern. And then the other parts of the network … are taking smaller pieces of the pattern and knitting them together into larger patterns." — [[gh-b0247y|b0247y]] transcript

## Oscillatory identity of columns

Each [[micro-column]] may be identified by its characteristic oscillation — a tonic firing rhythm shared by the neurons in that column. Each [[cortical-column]] likewise has its own oscillatory signature. The rhythm IS the identity of the unit; it binds member neurons into a coherent functional volume.

## Volume recognition

Pattern neuron ensembles extend into 3D object representation: a higher-level cluster defines the *volume* that an object occupies, binding all the surface/interior points that belong to one named thing. The ability to say "this object here" rests on neurons that represent the bounding volume, not just surface features. See [[volume-recognition]] and [[point-cloud-neural-representation]].

## Brain state as electromagnetic configuration

The aggregate activity of pattern neurons constitutes a [[brain-state]] — measurable as an electromagnetic configuration of what the observer is currently attending to. Changing what you think about is, at the physical level, shifting this EM configuration. Deliberate practices (meditation, environmental immersion, sustained [[attention]]) can recondition which pattern-neuron ensembles fire tonically, reshaping habitual [[thought-patterns-as-em-patterns]].
