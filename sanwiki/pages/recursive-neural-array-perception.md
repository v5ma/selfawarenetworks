## The idea in plain language

Neural populations do not pass completed pictures to miniature viewers. They receive selected
activity from other cells and populations. Their membranes, dendrites, synapses, intrinsic state,
and local circuit context transform that input. Their output then changes other receivers, while
feedback and recurrent routes alter earlier stages.

SAN uses **perception** in this page as an operational proposal:

```text
partial source-population state
-> selective projection into a receiver
-> receiver-specific transformation
-> changed output and local population state
-> recurrent return, redistribution, memory, or action consequence
```

No step contains the complete scene, and no step watches a screen. The stronger SAN proposal is
that the distributed network's ongoing reception, transformation, recurrence, and embodied
consequences collectively constitute observer-action.

The recovered operation is therefore not "column A sees column B." It is:

```text
distributed partial state
-> many selective receiver relationships
-> local transformation at each receiver
-> recurrent inter-population influence
-> temporally extended reconstruction
-> body, memory, report, or action consequence
```

## What established neuroscience supports

Several parts of this operation have strong empirical counterparts.

### Population-to-population communication is selective

Simultaneous macaque V1 and V2 recordings showed that V2 fluctuations were related to a small
subset of V1 population activity dimensions rather than to every dimension active within V1. The
authors called this a **communication subspace**
([Semedo et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30770252/)). This supports selective
population-to-population influence. It does not show that V2 views a complete picture produced by
V1.

### Cortical processing is recurrent and pathway-specific

Tracer work in macaque visual cortex found structured feedforward and feedback counterstreams with
different laminar distributions and connection weights
([Markov et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23983048/)). Fast recurrent interactions have
also been implicated experimentally in human shape perception
([Drewes et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26740660/)).

In mouse V1, ensemble-specific optogenetic activation showed that recurrent effects depended on
distance and tuning: nearby similarly tuned cells could be facilitated while more distant cells
were suppressed
([The logic of recurrent circuits in the primary visual cortex, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-023-01510-5)).
This is a useful biological correction to the old picture. A receiver population does not merely
copy its input; its own organization determines the consequence.

### Rhythms can organize directed influence, but not as a universal code

In macaque visual cortex, feedforward influence was associated with theta and gamma synchronization
and feedback influence with beta synchronization in the studied pathways
([Bastos et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25556836/)). Human MEG work found a related
gamma feedforward and alpha-beta feedback pattern among visual areas
([Michalareas et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26777277/)).

These are bounded results from visual systems. They do not prove that every neural array uses the
same bands, that synchronization alone creates awareness, or that one frequency has one universal
meaning throughout the brain.

### There is no universal three-column anatomical stack

Cortical areas differ in lamination, cell composition, projection origin, projection termination,
and thalamic connectivity. Quantitative primate work finds area- and pathway-dependent structure,
including prefrontal regions ranging from simpler limbic architecture to more differentiated
six-layer cortex
([García-Cabezas et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40542691/)). A diagram may use a
declared sensory pathway as one example, but it must not present identical canonical columns as the
universal anatomy of SAN.

## The SAN interpretation

SAN adds a proposed relationship among recurrence, oscillatory context, neural rendering, and the
distributed observer.

1. A source population expresses only part of its current state through anatomically available
   projections.
2. A receiver samples that projection through its own dendritic, synaptic, membrane, and circuit
   configuration.
3. The receiver's ongoing tonic state supplies a local expectation or context.
4. An excitation-driven advance, inhibition-driven delay or omission, burst, waveform change, or
   structured phase departure can become a receiver-relative
   [[tonic-phasic-phase-wave-differential|phase-wave differential]].
5. The receiver transforms the difference and changes its own output, tuning, routing, plasticity,
   or participation in a population pattern.
6. Reciprocal and recurrent pathways redistribute the update rather than ending at one final
   anatomical screen.
7. Across time, these partial transformations can contribute to the distributed reconstruction
   proposed by [[napot-overview|NAPOT]].
8. Perception becomes consequential through memory, report, autonomic change, orientation, choice,
   and action. The network itself is the proposed observer-action process.

This formulation preserves the original SAN claim while translating "seeing" and "listening" into
measurable receiver operations.

## Scale discipline

| Scale | Established candidate operation | SAN interpretation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cellular | Synaptic input changes dendritic and somatic state; a neuron emits condition-dependent output. | A cell can implement a receive-transform-project motif. | One neuron does not contain or view the complete percept. |
| Local circuit | Recurrent excitation and inhibition reshape population activity according to connectivity and tuning. | A local ensemble can maintain context and express typed differences. | A local oscillator is not thereby proven conscious. |
| Inter-areal | Selected population dimensions influence other areas through reciprocal, pathway-specific connections. | Partial representations can be transformed and redistributed among arrays. | The cortex is not a simple serial stack of identical columns. |
| Whole system | Sensory, thalamic, association, mnemonic, affective, autonomic, and motor systems interact over time. | Their recurrent distributed activity may collectively implement rendering and observer-action. | No one hub or completed picture is assumed. |

## A decisive experimental program

Use simultaneous population recordings in two or more anatomically connected sensory areas during
a discrimination task with a delayed action report. Measure spikes, local field potentials,
population geometry, behavior, and the receiver's pre-event state. Perturb a verified feedback or
recurrent projection while preserving matched sensory input.

Compare at least three models:

1. **Feedforward activity model:** source spike counts and stimulus features only.
2. **Recurrent communication model:** adds receiver state, reciprocal influence, and communication
   subspaces.
3. **SAN model:** adds receiver-relative tonic expectations and typed phasic deviations, then asks
   whether those variables predict subsequent receiver transformation, reconstruction, memory, or
   action beyond firing rate, spectral power, and the recurrent model alone.

The SAN interpretation gains support only if its added variables improve held-out prediction and
if a targeted perturbation changes the predicted downstream transformation or behavior. It loses
support if the typed receiver-relative variables add no reproducible explanatory power, if the
purported recurrence is epiphenomenal, or if a simpler model explains the same effects.

## Why the earlier image failed medical review

The quarantined plate preserved the intended idea of transformed activity moving among populations,
but it could not serve as medical or scientific art. It:

1. repeated one canonical laminar circuit as three universal cortical arrays;
2. implied that Array B literally perceives a completed rendering made by Array A;
3. assigned a direct serial feedforward chain and symmetric feedback stabilization without a named
   pathway or preparation;
4. labeled phase synchronization as awareness rather than one candidate coordination variable;
5. treated identity as the product of a stable recursive pattern without marking that as a stronger
   SAN hypothesis;
6. omitted the receiver-specific transformations, inhibition, recurrence, body state, and action
   consequences central to the recovered sources.

The replacement prompt retains the source innovation but requires a declared pathway example,
selective communication subspaces, recurrent transformation, tonic/phasic state traces, explicit
micro-meso-macro boundaries, and a visible **no inner viewer** constraint.
