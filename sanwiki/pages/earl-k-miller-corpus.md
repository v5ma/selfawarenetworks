## Position

The Earl K Miller Corpus matters to SAN because its 2017–2025 oscillation results converge on territory that [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks had already mapped in 2017–2024. Six concepts carry the load of that convergence. First, [[coincidence-as-a-bit|Coincidence as a Bit]] (2017) — [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s claim that the neural information unit is a *coincident pattern* rather than a spike — corresponds to Miller-corpus claims that synchronous gamma bursts carry working-memory content; the SAN claim is dated earlier. Second, [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase Wave Differentials]] (2020) — phase offsets between oscillating neurons as the atomic encoding unit — corresponds to traveling-wave phase-gradient findings later observed in prefrontal cortex during working-memory tasks. Third, [[napot-theory|NAPOT]] (2022) — [[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography — corresponds to Miller-corpus reports of rotating theta/alpha/beta waves in prefrontal cortex, which the priority review presents as empirical confirmation of the NAPOT picture rather than as independent discovery. Fourth, [[oscillatory-binding-problem|oscillatory binding by synchrony]] (2018) corresponds to Miller-corpus feature-binding-by-phase-coherence claims. Fifth, the volumetric-3D-internal-screen reading of consciousness (2024) corresponds to "phase-structured field" / "brain-wide phase hologram" framings in the later Miller corpus. Sixth, [[entification|entification]] (2017) corresponds to Miller and Barrett's claim that categorisation is intrinsic to perception — what SAN calls *[[entification]]* the Miller corpus calls *baked-in categorisation*.

The SAN crosswalk does not say Miller is wrong; it says the cluster of seven invariants is unlikely to have arisen independently. The full GSEAP statistics — Bayes Factor 10⁷–10⁸ in favour of dependent development, K(later|Blumberg) = O(1), seven independent invariants converging — sit in the priority review. The position of *this* page is that the corpus exists, that it is being cross-walked against [[recursive-neural-array-perception|the SAN tomographic frame]], and that the comparison shapes how SAN should engage with the oscillation literature: not as a competitor but as empirical confirmation that arrived later under different vocabulary.
