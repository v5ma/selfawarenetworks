This SAN source note connects [neural arrays](neural-array.md), [actions](actions.md), and synaptic weighting as a compact prompt about how brain function may depend on more than one neural array passively seeing another.

The note points to "Mechanical actions of dendritic-spine enlargement on presynaptic exocytosis" (Nature, 2021; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04125-7) as source support for the idea that [dendritic spine](dendritic-spines.md) enlargement can mechanically affect [synaptic vesicle release](synaptic-vesicle-release.md).

The working claim should be read as a hypothesis: neural arrays may need changing [synaptic weights](synaptic-weight.md), layered three-dimensional organization, and neurophysical framing before they can explain brain function.
