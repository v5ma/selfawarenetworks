The 23 Jul 2012 source presents [Neo Mind Cycle](neo-mind-cycle.md) as a twelve-week program with twenty-four 35-minute sessions intended to "free your mind."

The source describes the program as combining neurofeedback with mental-healing and mind-optimization goals.

It also describes [Brainwave Entrainment](brainwave-entrainment.md) as a meditation-like support for mental performance in stressful situations.

The original page claims that each technology had forty years of documentation and research behind it, but this wiki page has not independently verified that research claim.

The source says the two technologies had been combined in a new way.

The source compares neurofeedback to healthy exercise for the brain and [Brainwave Entrainment](brainwave-entrainment.md) to a feel-good massage for the brain.

It invites readers to notice possible expansions of awareness almost from the first day.

The original promotional copy suggests that participants may feel more creative, write more, become more productive, or complete a delayed major project.

As the weeks progress, the source says that happiness-expanding shifts may occur. It also claims the program supports whole-brain thinking faster than competing technologies or programs.

The source further claims that the program makes neurons fire in a highly coordinated way, resulting in increased "cognitive juice" and a stronger experience of inner genius. This wiki page preserves those claims as source-lineage evidence rather than as independently verified scientific conclusions.
