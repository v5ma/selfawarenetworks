# SAN Dynamic Timeline, 2011-2026

The **SAN Dynamic Timeline** turns the source genealogy into a navigable public record. It follows
the changing language of Self Aware Networks across dialogues, recordings, articles, podcasts,
Git history, videos, books, papers, and the Encyclopedia itself.

The timeline is not a claim that every event introduced a scientifically validated mechanism. It
separates the date of the wording from later public fixation, distinguishes ancestors from mature
operators, and keeps source custody separate from conceptual fit.

## How to read the timeline

- **Zoom and pan** to move from the full 2011-2026 span into individual source clusters.
- **Group by idea or evidence property** to inspect concept families, source types, stages,
  evidence grades, biological scales, media, or publication states.
- **Filter without changing the record.** Empty years remain empty rather than being filled with
  inferred events.
- **Open an event** to see its bounded claim, source links, evidence grade, and what it does not
  establish.
- **Download SVG** for a large, scalable static picture of the current filtered view, or download
  CSV for independent inspection.

## Evidence grades and date boundaries

| Grade | Timeline meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | An immutable public source, platform record, archive, or Git commit fixes the relevant wording and date. |
| **A-** | Wording and date are strongly supported, but one custody layer remains incomplete. |
| **B** | A serious source exists, but the older date depends on owner-held metadata or later public fixation. |

An owner-dated 2012 note publicly Git-fixed in 2022 remains a 2012 genealogy event with a 2022
public-fixation boundary. It is not silently promoted into independent contemporaneous publication
proof. Likewise, a 2017 functional ancestor is not renamed PWD or NAPOT before those terms appear.

## Scope boundary

This is the SAN timeline. The Comparison Wiki's planned timeline may later reuse the interface and
add Comparison-owned events, rival-specific evidence, and verdict states. Those comparisons are
not generated or adjudicated here.

Private notes, private transcripts, internal review paths, and unpublished research are excluded
from the public data file. A private source may guide recovery, but an event enters this public
timeline only through an approved public route with its custody limitation stated.
