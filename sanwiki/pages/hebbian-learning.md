# Hebbian Learning

![Hebbian Learning](../assets/generated/san/hebbian-learning/hebbian-learning-20260702-v1.png)

This visual frames Hebbian learning as SAN's activity-dependent substrate rule: correlated pre/post activity opens NMDA/Ca2+ coincidence gates, CaMKII signaling and AMPA insertion strengthen the synapse, structural spine changes store the memory trace, and repeated co-activity reinforces cortical pathways that can later reactivate as oscillatory prediction modes.

**Hebbian Learning** is the activity-dependent strengthening of connections between co-active units, named for Donald O. Hebb's 1949 postulate that "cells that fire together, wire together," and treated within [Self-Aware Networks](./gh-a0206z.md) as one of the foundational learning rules through which a [chemical synapse](./chemical-synapse.md) reshapes itself in response to correlated firing. Within the SAN framework it is positioned alongside [backpropagation](./backpropagation.md), reinforcement-style learning, and unsupervised pattern formation as the substrate-level mechanism that lets [cortical columns](./cortical-columns.md) and [single-cell assemblies](./single-cell-hebbian-assembly.md) consolidate the experiential patterns that the larger [memory-prediction loop](./memory-prediction-as-intelligence.md) then exploits.

## Origin

Hebb's original rule entered the SAN reading list as the canonical first answer to the question "how do nervous systems learn from their own activity," and the source repository repeatedly returns to its 1949 framing as the historical anchor. In `a0038z.md`, [Micah](./gh-resnote04.md) situates it inside the standard chronology of behavioural and connectionist learning theory: "Like Classical conditioning (1897) and Operant Conditioning (1938), hebbian learning rules (1949), the perceptron (1957), back prop training of neural networks (1963), etc." (`[a0038z](./gh-a0038z.md)--md`, line 618, on github). That ordering is doctrinally important for SAN: it places Hebb's rule as the bridge between classical/operant behaviourist conditioning and the later [back-prop](./backpropagation.md)-trained artificial [neural networks](./deep-learning-neural-networks.md), framing all of them as partial instances of a single underlying [memory-prediction](./memory-prediction-as-intelligence.md) story rather than competing schools. Donald O. Hebb himself appears in the SAN cast of named contributors, listed alongside [Michael Levin](./michael-levin.md) (bioelectricity, cellular agency) and Jon Lieff (cellular communication) as one of the few historical figures whose mechanism is treated as still load-bearing.

## Position

Within [Self-Aware Networks](./gh-a0206z.md) proper, Hebbian Learning is treated as a substrate rule rather than the whole story of cognition: the SAN learning regime is "more akin to unsupervised or reinforcement-based Hebbian learning" while still admitting "the principle of error-driven learning---where discrepancies between actual and desired outputs" drive change (`[07san](./gh-07san.md)--md`, line 732, on github). A first non-obvious SAN claim is that [Hebbian plasticity](./hebbian-plasticity.md) does not require the dramatic event physiologists usually picture: "A soma bursting [action potential](./action-potential.md) event isn't necessary for Hebbian learning to take place" (`[a0011z](./gh-a0011z.md)--md`, line 78), which lets [sub-threshold graded firing](./graded-neural-firing.md), [dendritic spikes](./dendritic-spike.md), and [apical-dendritic](./apical-dendrites.md) events all count as Hebbian-eligible coincidences. A second is that the rule is the efficiency engine behind cortical routing - "Most frequent pathways for specific stimuli are reinforced through Hebbian learning. That's how the brain gets so efficient" (`[a0063z](./gh-a0063z.md)--md`, line 81) - which dovetails with the SAN account of [reference-frame](./cortical-columns-reference-frames.md) formation in [cortical columns](./cortical-columns.md) and with the [LTP-as-memory-trace](./ltp-bouton-synaptic-memory-traces.md) reading developed elsewhere in the corpus. A third, more distinctive claim is that the resulting structure is oscillatory: "I want to say it arises from unique oscillation patterns. The patterns are formulated by Hebbian learning, then when they are activated they glow" (`[a0122z](./gh-a0122z.md)--md`, line 5), folding Hebb's rule into the SAN [wave-oscillation](./traveling-waves-neural-oscillations.md) account where strengthened pathways are not merely heavier weights but persistent, re-ignitable [neural oscillation](./neural-oscillations.md) modes. [Long-term potentiation](./long-term-potentiation.md) in this framing is therefore both a [synaptic-plasticity](./bistable-synaptic-plasticity.md) phenomenon and a phase-coherence phenomenon - co-firing writes the synapse and tunes the [spectral signature](./spectral-graph-temporal-consciousness.md) simultaneously.

## Chronology

The 1949 date for Hebb's rule is fixed and uncontested; what the SAN corpus contributes is a reading of how that rule scales upward to cortical and behavioural phenomena and how it interacts with later mechanisms. [Micah](./gh-resnote04.md)'s writing repeatedly slots Hebbian learning into a longer arc that runs from Pavlov's 1897 classical conditioning through Skinner's 1938 operant conditioning, to Hebb in 1949, to Rosenblatt's perceptron in 1957, to early [back-prop](./backpropagation.md) in 1963 (`[a0038z](./gh-a0038z.md)--md`, line 618). That four-stage chronology is reused as a recurring pedagogical scaffold across the SAN drafts and the [GPT-assisted](./selfawarenetworks-gpt.md) notebooks, and it is explicitly the chronology against which the SAN claim - that none of these are complete without the [memory-prediction](./memory-prediction-as-intelligence.md) and [wave-oscillation](./traveling-waves-neural-oscillations.md) layers - is staged. The corpus does not assert any pre-1949 priority claim for Hebb's rule itself; the chronological work is in showing how SAN re-reads the same rule in light of [BrainGate](./braingate.md)-era recording technology, modern [LTP](./ltp-structural-memory-encoding.md) data, and the [reference-frame](./cortical-columns-reference-frames.md) proposals associated with [cortical columns](./cortical-columns.md).

## Post-SAN

Compared to mainstream neuroscience and machine-learning literature, the SAN reading of Hebbian Learning makes three points worth flagging. First, on the question of whether suprathreshold spikes are necessary, mainstream [spike-timing-dependent plasticity](./stdp.md) (STDP) accounts typically require pre- and post-synaptic spikes within a narrow window; SAN's claim that "a soma bursting [action potential](./action-potential.md) event isn't necessary" (`[a0011z](./gh-a0011z.md)--md`, line 78) is consistent with newer subthreshold and dendritic-plasticity findings but pushed harder, and makes the testable prediction that purely [graded](./graded-neural-firing.md) [dendritic computation](./dendrites-as-computational-units.md) should produce Hebbian-style weight changes even when no axonal spike occurs. Second, on the question of efficiency and routing, mainstream [connectome](./connectome.md) accounts treat path reinforcement as a network-level statistical phenomenon; SAN's framing through [cortical columns](./cortical-columns.md) and [reference-frame](./reference-frames-cortical-knowledge.md) formation aligns this with [Jeff Hawkins](./jeff-hawkins.md)-style [columnar](./cortical-columns-reference-frames.md) models and with Friston-style [predictive processing](./predictive-processing.md) accounts, while substituting [oscillatory binding](./oscillatory-binding.md) for [free-energy](./free-energy-principle.md) minimisation as the unit of explanation. Third, on the relationship to artificial [neural networks](./deep-learning-neural-networks.md), the SAN account treats Hebbian rules and [back-prop](./backpropagation.md) as both partial - the former is biologically plausible but locally myopic, the latter is globally optimising but biologically implausible - and predicts that any near-[AGI](./artificial-general-intelligence.md) architecture will need a [NAPOT](./napot-sentient-ai-architecture.md)-style hybrid in which [Hebbian local plasticity](./hebbian-plasticity.md) is gated by [NDCA](./ndca-non-linear-differential-continuous-approximation.md)-style differentiable global signals.
