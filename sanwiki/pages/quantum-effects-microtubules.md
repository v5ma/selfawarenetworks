# Quantum Effects in Microtubules

**Quantum effects in microtubules** are experimentally or theoretically described physical effects involving tubulin or assembled microtubules. This page separates those effects from the stronger claims that microtubules perform biologically useful quantum computation or produce consciousness.

## Evidence levels

| Level | Question | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Physical effect | Does a tubulin or microtubule preparation exhibit a defined optical, electronic, vibrational, or coherence-related effect? | Some preparation-specific effects have been reported |
| Living-cell persistence | Does the same state occur and persist in intact neurons under physiological conditions? | Requires direct in-vivo or living-cell evidence at the relevant timescale |
| Biological readout | Does cellular machinery selectively detect and use the state? | Not supplied by the physical measurement alone |
| Neural computation | Does perturbing the state change a declared representation or computation while preserving confounds? | Requires selective perturbation and rescue |
| Consciousness | Is the state necessary or sufficient for a declared conscious state or content? | Not established by current microtubule optical findings |

Reported in-vitro work includes electronic energy migration affected by anesthetics ([Kalra et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.2c01114)) and collective ultraviolet emission in organized tryptophan networks ([Babcock et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.3c07936)). These findings make the physical substrate testable. They do not complete the biological translation chain.

## SAN interpretation

SAN can ask whether a microtubule-scale effect changes cellular readiness, synaptic remodeling, or signal transformation. The relevant question is not whether a quantum effect exists, but whether an identified biological receiver uses that effect and whether it changes the larger observer-action network. Without that receiver and causal consequence, the effect remains Level 0 physical evidence on the [[consciousness-claim-and-evidence-ladder|Consciousness Claim and Evidence Ladder]].
