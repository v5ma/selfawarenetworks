# Nervegear Chronology

**Nervegear Chronology** is the dated source-lineage page for [[nervegear|Nervegear]], the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] label for a high-fidelity [[brain-computer-interfaces|brain-computer interface]] and the media/research thread around the [[nervegear-show-podcast|NerveGear Show]]. This chronology separates original source dates from later wiki import and maintenance dates.

This supports the etymology and design-archetype framing used by [[nervegear-augma-archetype|Nervegear Augma Archetype]] without turning the fiction into a real engineering claim.
- **2017 manuscript and article context:** The same packet lineage cites `a0223z--md` line 8, represented by [[gh-a0223z|gh-a0223z]], for the stack "Holographic Medical Imagining Devices + Volumetric Video Rendering + Brain Machine Interfaces (NerveGear) + Deep Learning Artificial Intelligence." - **2018 series expansion:** By 2018, the Nervegear packet describes the label as part of a recurring weekly discussion series. The existing [[nervegear|Nervegear]] article cites a recordings index and files including `b0209y--md`, `original_archive_b0311ygoogle--md`, and `original_archive_b0314y--md` for the "Neurophysics NerveGear Happy Hour" sequence; this is a series-level claim, not a verified complete episode list.
- **2023 retrospective framing:** The Nervegear packet records a 2023 "Director's Cut" ChatGPT export that describes Nervegear as an ultimate immersive BCI target. Because this is a retrospective note rather than the originating source, it should be read after the 2017 article/transcript lineage.
- **2025-2026 manuscript integration:** The existing [[nervegear|Nervegear]] article cites [[gh-saov9|SAOv9]] and related paper-review sources for later chapter headings such as "3 Life Goals: NerveGear, Artificial Cortex, Artificial Brains" and "NerveGear and the Future of Brain-Computer Interfaces." These later sources show wiki/manuscript integration of the concept, not earlier priority for the term.

## Chronology Notes

The current evidence supports a conservative sequence: first, a November 2, 2017 show/article/transcript artifact; second, 2017 contextual writings that connect Nervegear to [[brain-machine-interface|brain-machine interface]], [[virtual-reality|virtual reality]], [[augmented-reality|augmented reality]], and [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] themes; third, 2018 community-series expansion; fourth, 2023 retrospective framing; and fifth, 2025-2026 manuscript/wiki integration. The existing packet explicitly reports no pre-2013 evidence and no external paper evidence for the term, so this chronology does not claim independent scientific publication history for "Nervegear" outside the SAN corpus.
