# Temporal Tracking

**Temporal Tracking** is, on the face of its name, the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] subject of how a [[neural-array|neural-array]] coalition *follows a moving target through time* — both in the perceptual sense (tracking an object as it moves through the visual field) and in the broader cognitive sense (tracking a thought, a [[memory-prediction-cycle|prediction]], or an [[engram|engram]] as it evolves under the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|DPWDR]]). ## Position

In a [[self-aware-network]], tracking is a continuous predictive operation, not a fixed lookup. The cell-level mechanism is that a [[neural-array]] coalition that recognizes the current state of a target *also* predicts the next state, recruits the next set of arrays into its phase-locked coalition under the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|DPWDR]], and, if the [[prediction]] is correct, glides into a new bound coalition without losing the percept's identity. *Temporal tracking* names this glide. It depends on (i) [[oscillatory-temporal-memory-window]] (the rhythm-set holding interval), (ii) [[memory-prediction-cycle]] (the loop that generates and tests successive predictions), (iii) [[multi-timescale-reference-frames]] (concurrent frames at gamma, theta, and slower scales that supply position estimates at compatible resolutions), and (iv) [[predictive-motor-self-model]] (when tracking is yoked to motor output, the [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model that predicts where one's own body will be).

## Position — three flavors of tracking

(1) *Perceptual tracking*: following an external moving target. Implemented by a sliding [[neural-array]] coalition whose binding rolls forward as the target moves, with retinotopic / [[fractal-retinotopic-mirroring|retinotopic]] correspondences updated at gamma rate. (2) *Cognitive tracking*: following a thought, a chain of reasoning, or an [[engram|engram]] as it evolves. Implemented by a [[memory-prediction-cycle|prediction-loop]] running at theta-and-slower rates, with [[memory-wave-recontextualization|memory-wave-recontextualization]] supplying the gloss that lets the same [[engram]] be re-read in a new context. (3) *[[gh-a0206z|Self]]-tracking* / motor: following one's own body or [[attention]] through time. Implemented partly by [[predictive-motor-self-model|predictive motor self-models]] and partly by the [[attention-schema-theory|attention schema]] tracking where [[attention]] currently rests. The same SAN concept covers all three: a continuous, phase-locked, [[prediction]]-driven roll-forward of a coalition.

## Position — substrate

The substrate that implements temporal tracking is: (1) [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|traveling waves]] that carry the phase front forward across the [[cortex]] at well-defined velocities; (2) [[cross-scale-oscillation-communication|cross-scale coupling]] that lets fast updates ride on slow predictive envelopes; (3) [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] that gate which cells join the moving coalition; (4) [[tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame|tonic oscillations]] that supply the predictive reference; and (5) [[memory-prediction-as-intelligence|memory-prediction]] machinery that replaces the current state with the predicted state at each cycle.

## Relation to mainstream framings

Mainstream [[neuroscience]] and cognitive science study tracking under several headings: smooth pursuit (oculomotor), [[predictive-coding|predictive coding]] (cortical), [[attention]]-tracking (psychophysics), and dynamic-systems pursuit (control theory). SAN's *temporal tracking* is closest to the predictive-coding family but with two SAN-specific moves: (a) the [[prediction]] is *physically* the next phase configuration of the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|DPWDR]], not an abstract update; and (b) the tracking is *multi-scale* — perceptual, cognitive, and [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling tracking all share the same substrate primitives at their respective scales.
