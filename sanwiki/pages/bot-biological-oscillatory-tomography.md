# Bot Biological Oscillatory Tomography

![BOT - Biological Oscillating Tomography](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/bot/bot-biological-oscillating-tomography-medical-diagram-20260624.png)

This image separates BOT's three core states: phase misalignment with broken pattern parsing, signal-dissipation transition where phase error decays but synchrony is not complete, and signal sync where coherent manifolds allow stable biological pattern readout.

**Bot Biological Oscillatory Tomography** (BOT) is the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] umbrella term for the cell-scale generalisation of [[oscillatory-tomography|oscillatory tomography]] — the claim that biological cells, not only neurons, encode, transmit, and decode information through phase-coherent oscillations whose superpositions can be read out tomographically. In the SAN taxonomy it sits beneath [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] (the brain-specific instance) and alongside [[cellular-oscillating-tomography|COT]] (Cellular Oscillating Tomography), forming a three-tier reading-and-writing model that runs from whole-organism [[brainwaves|brainwaves]] down to single-cell membrane dynamics.

The defining sentence appears identically across three of the SAN draft chapters: "1. BOT Biological Oscillatory Tomography as a high level generalized explanation for how cells make, communicate, receive, and parse patterns" (draft1--md, line 11; draft4--md, line 11; draft5--md, line 17). That single line carries a lot of structural weight. By naming "cells" — not just neurons — the author extends the [[oscillatory-tomography|oscillatory-tomography]] reading beyond [[cortex|cortex]] and [[neural-circuits|neural circuits]] into the [[michael-levin|Michael Levin]] line of work on bioelectric signalling and morphogenetic communication, where non-neural cells also "make, communicate, receive, and parse patterns" via oscillating membrane potentials and gap-junction networks. The four verbs in the definition — *make, communicate, receive, parse* — also map cleanly onto the four operations the SAN [[concepts|concepts]] hierarchy expects of any pattern-bearing substrate: emit a signal, propagate it, detect it, and interpret it relative to a stored [[criterial-causation|criterion]].

In the wider SAN argument BOT plays the role of the bridge layer. Above it, [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] reconstructs cortical state from [[action-potential|action-potential]] / [[action-potential-duration|action-potential duration]] statistics, [[apical-dendrites|apical dendrites]], [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]], [[inhibitory-circuits|inhibitory circuits]], and [[hebbian-learning|Hebbian learning]] — all framed via [[predictive-coding|predictive coding]] and the [[jeff-hawkins|Jeff Hawkins]] tradition. Below it, [[cellular-oscillating-tomography|COT]] formalises the same reasoning at the level of an individual cell's oscillator. BOT is the generalised middle: by stripping the explanation to "how cells make, communicate, receive, and parse patterns," the author argues that the same tomographic inversion principle — recovering a hidden pattern from a population of phase-locked oscillators — applies whether the population is a sheet of [[cortex|cortex]] producing [[consciousness]], a developmental field producing morphology, or a microbial consortium producing collective behaviour. Treating BOT as a high-level explanation also explicitly frames the [[agentic-viewpoint|agentic viewpoint]] the SAN texts adopt: any cell capable of all four verbs is, in this account, a candidate substrate for [[attention]]-like and decision-like dynamics that lower-level chemistry alone does not capture.

Because the BOT entry recurs as item "1." in three independent draft outlines (draft1, draft4, draft5), it is intended as a foundational, top-of-list concept rather than a side note — the author starts the chain with BOT and then specialises down through [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] and [[cellular-oscillating-tomography|COT]] before connecting to [[artificial-intelligence|AI]], [[neural-rendering]], [[brainwaves|brainwaves]], and the rest of the [[self-aware-networks-theory|Self-Aware Networks theory]] surface.
