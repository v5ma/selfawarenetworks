What if the brain's ability to control devices that read brainwaves is influenced by sensory feedback? In other words, there may be sound frequencies or visual patterns that help signal when the EEG is receiving the intended brainwave pattern.

An EEG system can be connected to software that drives light and sound tracks for a mind machine, including LED flashing glasses and headphones. In that setup, the user may begin to notice how thoughts and feelings are reflected in the light and sound effects, because the measured brainwaves are driving those effects. The note says this was Micah's business in 2012, when he ran a neurofeedback salon where people could experience brainwaves driving light and sound effects.

Micah says he discusses the idea further in [the Neural Lace Podcast](neural-lace-podcast.md) #7: https://soundcloud.com/.../the-neural-lace-podcast-7...
