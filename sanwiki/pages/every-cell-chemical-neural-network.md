In [[self-aware-network]] theory, every cell in the body — not only neurons — functions as a chemical neural network unit equipped with threshold functions, [[3d-protein-receptor|3D protein receptors]], and [[coincident-detection]] analogous to [[hebbian-learning]].

## Core claim

Each cell "considers" its activated receptors. Receptors are 3D protein structures that encode learned information; they fire only when a pattern of molecular signals matches a threshold they have been shaped (by evolution and use) to detect. This is coincident detection — Hebbian-style plasticity — at the cellular level, extending learning below the neuron to every cell type.

The consequence: [[hebbian-plasticity]] is not a special property of synapses. It is a universal cellular mechanism. A 2022 Cell Reports paper on "Hebbian activity-dependent plasticity in white matter" independently confirmed aspects of this; [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] recorded this claim in a0597z on 17 Jun 2022, ten days before that paper published.

## Cell as clock

Every cell is also a biological clock. Timed gene → protein → RNA → chemical feedback loops create oscillations that can span hours or days. Chemical gradients (e.g., retinoic acid) serve as quantity-based timers that regulate when a cell starts or stops a given function. This cellular clock substrate is the micro-scale basis for [[cellular-oscillating-tomography]] (see `a0011z`).

Cells navigate using chemical gradients — neurons, stem cells, white blood cells, and cancer cells all use gradient fields to sense location and direction. Gradient quantities act as measuring tools rather than binary on/off signals.

## Body as distributed sensor

Because all cells are oscillating and communicating through chemical gradients and electrical signals, the entire body operates as a single connected sensor array. Touch anywhere on the body activates a cascade through this network; every cell that fires sends signals outward, and the system collectively "knows" about the event. This is the cellular substrate for the SAN claim that consciousness and information processing are distributed throughout all tissue, not confined to neural circuits.

## Entitativity

The note introduces [[entitativity]] — the extent to which a collection of elements is perceived or functions as a single entity rather than an aggregate. At the cellular level, [[entitativity]] emerges from chemical synchrony: cells that oscillate together and share gradient signals constitute functional entities. This mirrors [[hebbian-engrams|Hebb's cell assembly theory]] at a larger scale.
