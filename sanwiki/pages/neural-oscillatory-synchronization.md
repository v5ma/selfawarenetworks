> **Image status (2026-07-18):** The retained plate is conceptual and is not medically certified. It presents phase locking, amplitude coupling, nested rhythms, traveling waves, binding, and NAPOT projection as one measurable transition. These are distinct phenomena and do not form a universal consciousness or thermodynamic signature.

**Neural oscillatory synchronization** describes a statistical relation between neural signals over time. Depending on the analysis, it can mean phase consistency, coherence, spike-field locking, amplitude correlation, or coordinated bursting. The term is incomplete until the signals, reference, frequency, timescale, and null model are specified.

## Synchronization is not one biological state

Two populations can show high coherence because they interact, because one drives the other, because both receive common input, or because the same source is recorded twice. They can also communicate directionally with changing phase lags rather than zero-lag synchrony. Traveling waves contain spatial phase gradients and should not be reduced to global phase alignment.

Bosman et al. measured selective attention-related gamma synchronization between visual areas ([Neuron, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2012.06.037)). Besserve et al. found stimulus-modulated gamma phase shifts associated with directed information transfer within macaque V1 while explicitly discussing rate and spike-contamination alternatives ([PLOS Biology, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002257)). These are circuit-specific findings, not proof that synchrony binds all content.

## SAN interpretation

SAN proposes that some timing relations change which signals a receiver can use. This claim can support NAPOT or GCS only when the source, route, receiver, differential, and downstream consequence are declared. Synchronization alone does not establish a phase-wave code, a rendered field, a New Law of Thermodynamics, or consciousness.

The observer remains the distributed causal system. No synchronized projection is delivered to another viewer.

## Tests

Use source-sensitive and directional methods, control common input and volume conduction, separate narrowband oscillation from broadband activity, and verify the effect at the receiver. Perturb timing while monitoring rate and power, predict a selective functional loss, and test rescue.
