# Consciousness Claim and Evidence Ladder

The **Consciousness Claim and Evidence Ladder** prevents a measured neural effect from being promoted through several stronger claims without additional evidence. Use it whenever a SAN page moves from neural activity into representation, conscious access, observerhood, qualia, or phenomenal consciousness.

The ladder is not a theory of consciousness. It is a claim-control tool for stating exactly what an experiment, diagram, or argument establishes.

## First declare the target

Consciousness research contains several targets that can dissociate:

| Target | Operational question | Do not silently equate with |
|---|---|---|
| State consciousness | Is the organism awake, asleep, anesthetized, or clinically unresponsive? | A specific conscious content |
| Conscious content | What color, sound, object, body state, thought, or relation is experienced? | Flexible access or report |
| Conscious access | Can the content guide working memory, reasoning, report, and flexible control? | Phenomenal identity |
| Metacognition | Can the organism evaluate its own discrimination or access? | Awareness of every contributing process |
| Phenomenal consciousness | Why and how does organized physical activity have subjective character? | A decoded stimulus or behavioral response |

A page must name the target before assigning a mechanism or interpreting a result.

## The evidence ladder

| Level | Claim | Minimum evidence | What remains open |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Physical signal | A reproducible electrical, magnetic, chemical, mechanical, vascular, or behavioral change | Whether a biological receiver detects it |
| 1 | Biological sensitivity | A cell or circuit changes state in relation to the signal | Whether the change represents a declared variable |
| 2 | Representation | Neural state varies selectively with a content or relation under controls | Whether the organism or only an external analyst can decode it |
| 3 | External decoding | A held-out model recovers the variable from measurements | Whether a biological receiver uses that distinction |
| 4 | Causal use | Perturbing the state changes a declared discrimination, memory, body variable, report, or action | Whether the content is flexibly accessible or metacognized |
| 5 | Conscious access | The content becomes available across flexible memory, report, reasoning, and control tasks | Whether access exhausts phenomenal experience |
| 6 | Metacognition | The organism reliably evaluates its own access or performance beyond first-order accuracy | Whether the neural organization is identical with experience |
| 7 | Phenomenal identity | A theory proposes that the organized activity is the experienced quality or field | Why that identity holds and how rivals are excluded |

Evidence at one level constrains higher levels but does not automatically prove them. A classifier can decode information that no downstream biological receiver uses. A causal perturbation can affect report or action without settling phenomenal identity.

## Behavior is evidence before it is a boundary

A task result, sentence, correction, confidence report, action, or refusal can establish a bounded
capacity under declared conditions. Behavioral evidence becomes stronger when it generalizes to
held-out situations and changes in the predicted way after a selective internal perturbation and
component-matched rescue.

The stopping rule comes afterward. Visible behavior underdetermines the architecture that generated
it, and no one output is a transparent readout of experience. Machine-consciousness assessment
should therefore preserve behavior as one stream while joining it to versioned architecture,
persistent state, endogenous transitions, causal use, self-monitoring, perturbation, recovery, and
independent theory-derived indicators.

See [[machine-agency-consciousness-welfare-evidence-program|From Machine Output to Candidate Agency, Consciousness, and Welfare]].
## Apply the scale ladder

Every level must also declare scale:

- **Micro:** receptor, synapse, dendritic compartment, soma, axon initial segment, bouton, or one receiving cell.
- **Meso:** local circuit, layer, column-like population, nucleus, ensemble, or area-to-area pathway.
- **Macro:** distributed cortical, thalamic, subcortical, cerebellar, autonomic, body, and environment loops.

Properties do not transfer across scale by label. One neuron's sensitivity does not make it a conscious observer. A population decoder does not prove a single cell carries the decoded content. A scalp rhythm does not identify every cellular source or receiver.

## SAN interpretation

SAN's observer-action proposal begins below phenomenal identity. Receptors and cells register differences; learned structure selects consequential combinations; local and recurrent circuits transform them; and distributed memory, body, report, and action systems use the resulting state. The network itself performs this process; no hidden viewer is added.

[[tonic-phasic-canvas|Tonic and phasic]] language describes receiver-relative context and update. [[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-wave differentials]] propose a measured difference with a declared source, route, receiver, and consequence. [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] proposes reconstruction from partial projections. None of those labels moves a claim upward on this ladder without new evidence.

The strongest SAN claim - that a distributed causal organization is phenomenal experience - must be stated at Level 7 and defended separately from representation, decoding, access, or behavior.

## Required experiment contract

Before promoting a claim, state:

1. the target and biological scale;
2. the measured signal and reference;
3. the represented variable or controlled relation;
4. the biological receiver and physical route;
5. the behavioral, autonomic, report, or action consequence;
6. the matched alternative explanations;
7. the selective perturbation;
8. the predicted loss and rescue; and
9. the highest ladder level the result can support.

## Common category errors

- **Oscillation therefore consciousness:** a rhythm is a Level 0 signal until receiver and causal-use evidence is supplied.
- **Decodable therefore represented for the organism:** external decoding is Level 3, not biological use.
- **Integrated therefore conscious:** integration can occur without flexible access and does not prove phenomenal identity.
- **Necessary therefore sufficient:** a causal loss can establish necessity for one task without showing sufficiency for consciousness.
- **Distributed therefore observer:** distribution describes location; observer-action requires an organized causal loop.
- **Report therefore experience:** report can include decision, confidence, language, and motor preparation.
- **No-report marker therefore pure consciousness:** every proxy requires independent validation and may track another process.

## Primary anchors

- Fahrenfort et al. dissociated perceptual integration from conscious access ([PNAS, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1617268114)).
- Hatamimajoumerd et al. showed that report and no-report conditions change apparent neural signatures ([Current Biology, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.07.068)).
- The COGITATE Consortium preregistered divergent predictions and found results that challenged key claims of both IIT and GNWT ([Nature, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1)).
