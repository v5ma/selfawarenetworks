**Brain Complexity** is the [[self-aware-networks]] term for the irreducible, multi-scale richness of brain activity that emerges when [[oscillatory-tomography]], chemical signalling, and cellular agency interact, and it sets the explanatory bar that any model of [[consciousness]] or [[artificial-intelligence]] must clear. In SAN draft2 it is defined directly: "Brain complexity arises from [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organizing agency across scales. Molecular agents such as proteins and receptors adjust their functions" (draft2--md, line 391), positioning complexity as a *consequence* of agency operating across molecular, cellular, and systems levels rather than a measurement of network connectivity alone.

The SAN treatment locates the substrate of this complexity in the cooperative activity of neurons and [[glial-cells]] — most prominently [[astrocytes]] — whose calcium signalling adds an entire computational layer beyond the [[chemical-synapse]] and the [[action-potential]]. The a0270z page anchors this with an external reference to "Astrocyte Calcium Signaling Leads to More Brain Complexity" (a0270z--md, line 56; a0270z--md, line 58), and [[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]] repeats the framing as "Astrocyte Signaling and Brain Complexity" ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]]--md, line 14210). Within the [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]] (NAPOT) framework the same complexity is identified with the interplay of [[gamma-oscillations]] and synaptic plasticity: "**Complex Brain Dynamics (NAPOT 6)**: The exploration of brain complexity in NAPOT 6 includes understanding how gamma oscillations and synaptic plasticity contribute to the brain's overall functioning" ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]]--md, line 10873). [[Hebbian-Learning]], [[long-term-potentiation]], and [[long-term-depression]] are therefore not isolated rules but threads in a larger tapestry where [[engrams]], [[cortical-columns]], and [[neural-arrays]] coordinate through phase-locked timing — and where [[coincidence-detection]] at the [[nmda-receptor]] turns molecular state into network state.

A central SAN claim is that this complexity has both an electrical and a non-classical-electrical character. [[gh-veca|vecA]] poses the open question explicitly — "Can ephapticity contribute to brain complexity?" ([[gh-veca|vecA]]--md, line 2620) — extending complexity past axodendritic wiring into ephaptic field coupling, and the suggested-wikilink set further opens toward [[quantum-entanglement]], [[microtubules]], and [[functional-information]] as additional layers SAN considers. Because [[predictive-coding]] alone treats the cortex as a hierarchy of error signals, brain complexity in the SAN reading is exactly what predictive coding under-describes: the *qualia*-bearing, agent-bearing, [[criterial-causation]]-driven reality that sits behind the prediction machinery.

Operationally, SAN treats brain complexity as the metric AI must approach to be considered [[gh-a0206z|self]]-aware. [[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]] frames this directly as "Challenges in Mimicking Human Brain Complexity: Addressing the complexities and challenges in replicating the nuanced functionalities of the human brain in AI" ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]]--md, line 6596), and continues that "understanding how … synaptic connections and memory processes is crucial to grasping the larger picture of brain complexity and function as explored in NAPOT 6" ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]]--md, line 10875). Read together with [[biological-oscillating-tomography]], [[attention]], [[attractor]] dynamics, [[dopamine]] modulation, [[gaba]]-mediated [[inhibitory-circuits]], and the readout instruments [[neuralink]] and [[braingate]], brain complexity becomes the SAN-internal name for the explanatory target of the entire programme — the same target that [[fmri]], [[neurofeedback]], and the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness]] each approach from their respective sides.
