**Consciousness canvas and wave differential** is a historical SAN formulation joining the tonic
canvas metaphor to PWD. Its durable insight is that biological receivers respond to differences
relative to ongoing state, not to context-free absolute values.

## Bounded mechanism

- A receiver has a maintained cellular or population state.
- A new event changes timing, voltage, conductance, firing, release, or another declared variable.
- The receiver transforms that change and alters downstream state.
- Recurrent and body-world consequences update the next context.

This relation can contribute to perception without being a pixel raster, energy picture, or complete
conscious field.

## What the earlier page overclaimed

Uniform activity does not imply no percept in every system. Neighboring phase or amplitude contrast
does not by itself specify color, shape, location, or experience. A software simulation that
reuses one neuron model to instantiate many units demonstrates programmatic reuse, not compression
of a biological network into conscious wave differentials.

## Consciousness boundary

PWD may be tested as a receiver-relative information and control variable. The further claim that a
PWD constitutes phenomenal content requires discriminating evidence beyond rate, power, waveform,
movement, arousal, history, and ordinary recurrent processing. Representation, access, report,
action, and experience must remain separate outcomes.
