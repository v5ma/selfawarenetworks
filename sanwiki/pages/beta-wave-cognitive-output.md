# Beta Wave Cognitive Output: Thought and Top-Down Input

> **Image status:** The previous plate assigned beta to a generic cortical-output layer and failed
> source-faithful medical review. It is preserved in the failed-medical-review archive and is not an
> accepted figure.

**Beta Wave Cognitive Output** is a historical SAN label for beta-associated activity produced by
active thought, working memory, prediction, and top-down control. The word *output* refers here to the
output of prefrontal thought/context processing into a receiving consideration circuit. It does
**not** mean that beta is the final motor, vocal, or corticofugal action command.

## Role in the Gamma Consideration Sandwich

In the [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|Gamma Consideration Sandwich]], beta has a precise role:

```text
Layer-4/input-side alpha primary-sensory-side input
  + beta prefrontal thought/top-down input
  -> Layer-2/3 gamma consideration and proprioceptive coordination
  -> Layer-5 theta/gamma action-side output <-> returned body input
```

Beta therefore belongs to the **top bun**. It carries the thought, goal, expectation, or top-down
side of the input that gamma consideration coordinates with primary-sensory-side alpha activity.
The bottom bun remains the Layer-5 theta/gamma action-and-body side.

## Medical evidence and boundary

Beta activity is not exclusive to thought or prefrontal cortex. It also participates in sensorimotor,
basal-ganglia, attentional, and other circuit functions. The bounded empirical bridge is that
prefrontal beta has measured relationships to working memory, executive control of thought, and
top-down attentional selection:

- [Schmidt et al. (2019), beta oscillations in working memory and executive control of movement and thought](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1163-19.2019)
- [Dubey et al. (2023), prefrontal beta coherence mediating top-down attentional selection](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.06.025)
- [Lundqvist et al. (2016), transient beta and gamma bursts during working memory](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2016.02.028)

These findings support beta as a plausible carrier of prefrontal thought and top-down influence. They
do not establish one universal beta code, make every thought a beta event, or prove the complete GCS
operator. A GCS test must identify the prefrontal population, receiving circuit, direction of
influence, task epoch, beta event, gamma response, body-state variable, and action consequence.
