## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the question "what does a neuron's activity *encode*?" is answered at the level of the [[oscillator]] population rather than at the level of the individual spike train. A spike, in this framing, is a phase event inside a [[tonic-phasic-neural-oscillation]] regime; what a downstream reader-population takes from upstream is not "how many spikes per second" but "where in the [[gamma-oscillations]] and [[theta-oscillations-background-scripts]] phase landscape did the spike arrive." That commitment leaves Oscillatory Neural Coding adjacent to but stricter than the mainstream temporal-coding literature: under [[criterial-causation]] and [[criteria-causation-single-neuron-pattern-detection]] the relevant variable is a [[criterial-thresholds]] match against an incoming [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves]] pattern, and under [[neural-phase-pattern-propagation]] the readout is a [[distributed-phase-representations]] pattern rather than a count. The [[oscillatory-binding-problem]] page is the consequence: if [[oscillator]] phase is the coding variable, the binding question reduces to a question about [[cross-frequency-coupling]] and [[neural-ensemble-phase-reset-communication]] rather than to a separate "binding mechanism" pasted on top of rate codes. The [[brain-as-spectral-graph]] reading and the [[holographic-brain-distributed-memory]] reading both presuppose this kind of coding, and so do the [[oscillatory-qualia-imprinting]] and [[oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration]] pages.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream [[neuroscience]] has been moving toward phase- and oscillation-based coding accounts for decades — phase-precession in hippocampal place cells, theta–gamma cross-frequency coupling, communication-through-coherence, and the broader literature on [[oscillatory-binding|oscillatory binding]] all share the move that timing relative to a population rhythm carries information beyond firing rate. The SAN reading converges on those results but pushes further: the [[brain-as-spectral-graph]] / [[oscillating-network-of-oscillators]] ontology says [[oscillator]] phase is not just *one* coding channel among several, it is the *substrate* of the others, and the [[distributed-phase-representations]] and [[neural-phase-pattern-propagation]] machinery is offered as the testable mechanism. Where the published literature treats temporal coding as a refinement to a rate-coding default, the SAN reading treats rate codes as a coarse-grained projection of an underlying [[oscillator]]-phase pattern — with [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators]] and [[spectral-graph-theory-brain-oscillations]] supplying the multiscale structure mainstream models tend to flatten.
