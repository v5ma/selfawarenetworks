# SAN Authorship, Provenance, and AI Assistance

## The SAN theory was not authored by AI

Micah Blumberg is the originator and author of Self-Aware Networks and its central research program. The documentary record begins in 2011 and includes dated notes, neurofeedback and BCI work, recordings, podcasts, articles, videos, and repository material. Much of the foundational SAN architecture was developed and publicly documented before ChatGPT became publicly available on November 30, 2022.

This chronology does not claim that every later term, equation, figure, or refinement existed in its present form before that date. It establishes that the central research direction and many of its component mechanisms have a substantial pre-ChatGPT source record.

## What continued after 2022

The research continued from 2022 through 2026. Later work clarified terminology, added equations and software, recovered older sources, compared SAN with newer literature, developed tests, corrected ambiguous wording, and connected previously separate parts of the program.

Newer material must retain its real date. A later synthesis is not silently backdated merely because some of its components are older.

## How AI has been used

Under Micah Blumberg's direction, AI systems have assisted with:

- organizing and searching a large source corpus;
- grammar, spelling, structure, and reader-level explanation;
- mathematical and coding formulations;
- literature discovery and citation checking;
- medical, biological, physical, and mathematical consistency review;
- diagram planning, visualization, and accessibility;
- adversarial review and identification of ambiguous or overstated language.

These uses do not transfer authorship of the SAN theory to an AI system. AI dialogue is discovery and editorial material, not independent scientific evidence. Citations, derivations, interpretations, and generated language remain subject to source readback and human responsibility.

## What a timestamp establishes

A timestamp can establish that a particular expression or source atom existed by a date. It does not establish that the idea is correct, that it was independently validated, or that no earlier source exists elsewhere.

The SAN Wiki distinguishes:

- an internal creation or event date;
- a public article, audio, or video date;
- a first verified repository fixation date;
- a later transcription, recovery, or migration date;
- a later synthesis or current formulation date.

Where those dates differ, the page should show the difference rather than compressing them into one priority claim.

## Source hierarchy

Public claims should prefer:

1. immutable public artifacts and exact repository history;
2. provider metadata for public audio, video, article, and deposit records;
3. owner-held exports with hashes and explicit date provenance;
4. later copies or transcripts whose relationship to the original is documented;
5. AI-chat recovery only as a route to the underlying authored source.

Private AI-chat bodies, credentials, collaboration material, and unpublished personal records are not public SAN Wiki content merely because they helped locate a source.

## Scientific boundary

Authorship and chronology are different from scientific validation. SAN pages must separately identify:

- established scientific background;
- Micah Blumberg's interpretation or synthesis;
- a distinct SAN hypothesis;
- a proposed measurement or engineering program;
- a result that has actually been tested.
