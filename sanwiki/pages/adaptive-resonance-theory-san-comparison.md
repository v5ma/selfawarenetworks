[[adaptive-resonance-theory|Adaptive Resonance Theory]] (ART, Stephen Grossberg) and [[napot|SAN:NAPOT]] both describe
top-down/bottom-up comparison mechanisms in neural systems, but differ sharply in scope
and biological specificity.

This comparison plate maps the shared atom between ART and NAPOT: a top-down baseline pattern is compared with incoming signal, while preserving the boundary that ART names the threshold logic and NAPOT names the biological carrier.

## ART's Core Idea

ART holds that recognition emerges from interaction between top-down memory templates
(prototypes) and bottom-up sensory input. The *vigilance parameter* sets a mismatch
threshold: if the incoming signal diverges too far from the template, a new category is
formed rather than assimilation into an existing one. This solves the
*plasticity/stability problem* — how a system acquires new knowledge without overwriting
existing knowledge (incremental learning).

## Buzsáki Bridge

György Buzsáki's *Brain from Inside Out* maps closely onto ART at the conceptual level:
the brain starts from **pre-existing rhythms and instinctual connectivity bias**
(the template) and incoming sensory events modulate those rhythms (the mismatch).
Catastrophic forgetting is absent because the brain is never a blank slate.
See [[tonic-oscillation]].

## Where SAN:NAPOT Diverges

[[napot|SAN:NAPOT]] agrees that the brain performs a continuous comparison between
internal baseline patterns and incoming signals, but specifies the mechanism precisely:

- The comparison substrate is the **multi-dimensional [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]]** — e.g., theta
  rhythm in the hippocampus.
- Incoming sensory data arrives as **[[phase-wave-differential|phase wave differentials]]**
  riding on top of that tonic ground.
- The comparison happens not just across layers but **across brain regions** via phase-matched
  functional connectivity — oscillating feedback loops, not static templates.
- **[[apical-dendrite|Apical dendrites]]** serve as inceptive fields; basal dendrites as
  receptive fields — a directional architecture ART lacks.

## Key Contrast

ART models *when* a new category forms (threshold logic). NAPOT models *how* the brain
physically implements the comparison (phase differentials, functional connectivity,
dendritic polarity). ART is computationally intuitive; NAPOT is biologically grounded.
