## Position

Read as a definitional consequence of its title in SAN, **thalamus alpha omega** asserts two propositions held together. The first is conceptual: in the SAN account of conscious rendering, the thalamus is not merely a relay or an intermediate node but the loop-closing element of the [[thalamic-cortical-loop|thalamic cortical loop]] — the thalamus is where the [[phase-differential-rendering|phase-differential rendering]] chain both begins (with input projecting up to [[cortical-columns]]) and ends (with descending cortical output returning to thalamic nuclei), making it the *alpha and omega* of that loop.
