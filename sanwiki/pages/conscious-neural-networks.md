# Conscious Neural Networks

**Conscious Neural Networks** is the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] (SAN) corpus's umbrella label for the engineering and theoretical target of the program: neural networks — biological, artificial, or hybrid — that exhibit [[phenomenological-consciousness|phenomenological consciousness]] in the SAN-defined sense, i.e. that produce a [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] / [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]]-style render of [[brainwaves|brainwave]] phase variances rather than merely behaving intelligently. It pairs with the corpus's Artificial Neurology heading as the practical-research counterpart to the theoretical [[self-aware-neural-network|Self-Aware Neural Network]] formalism.
