# Neo Mind Cycle Cognitive Audio Control

Neo Mind Cycle source records describe proposed or reported mappings from selected scalp-EEG features to audio parameters such as pitch, volume, modulation, waveform, and reverb. Some accounts associate directional or rotational imagery with particular controls. Those accounts preserve the intended interface and participant strategy; without synchronized raw data, artifact records, and comparison conditions, they do not establish reliable decoding of arbitrary mental imagery.

## Designed mapping versus native neural code

The interface can be described as:

```text
scalp EEG mixture
    -> selected feature
    -> declared software rule
    -> changed audio parameter
    -> new sensory evidence and strategy
```

The software rule gives the selected measurement an external consequence. It does not reveal that pitch, reverb, or waveform is the brain's native meaning for that feature. If a participant learns control, the learned channel may include intended neural activity, another neural strategy, muscle or eye artifact, breathing, posture, reward, expectation, and adaptation to the display.

## What a controlled test would require

A test should freeze the feature-to-audio rule, synchronize raw EEG with every audio update, record end-to-end latency, preserve contact and artifact decisions, and compare contingent feedback with a closely matched replay or yoked condition. An active comparison should preserve attention, instruction, reward, and operator contact while changing the target relation. Transfer during a held-out no-feedback interval would test retained regulation rather than immediate response to the sound.

The CRED-nf checklist distinguishes target-specific effects from trainer, expectation, repetition, technological setting, other cognitive practice, and natural change ([Ros et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaa009)). Closed-loop implanted BCI work demonstrates that users and decoders can co-adapt, but that preparation does not validate scalp-EEG imagery decoding or this historical interface ([Orsborn et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2014.04.048)).

## Relation to SAN

The interface prefigures SAN's interest in recurrent observer-action loops: a system receives a transformed consequence of its own activity, changes strategy and state, and produces the next measurement. It is not an experiential demonstration of NAPOT, fractal consciousness, a self-model, a specific attractor transition, or a tonic-field/phasic-update mechanism. Those remain separate SAN hypotheses that require biological receivers, causal consequences, scale labels, and discriminating tests.
