# San Wikilink Density Repair

`San Wikilink Density Repair` is a maintenance page for the [[home|Self Aware Networks Institute Wiki]] that records a bounded link-density pass across the `san` topic. Its purpose is to connect underlinked SAN overview material to resolved concept pages such as [[self-aware-network]], [[saov9-self-aware-networks-overview]], [[napot-overview]], [[neural-array-projection]], [[phase-wave-differentials]], and [[neural-oscillations]] without introducing links that are absent from the page index.

The repair treats SAN as a theory cluster rather than a single article. Core SAN pages describe a [[self-aware-network]] as a recursive render-detect system, while [[recursive-neural-array-perception]], [[fractal-consciousness]], [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine]], and [[consciousness]] provide nearby pages for perception, scale, prediction, and phenomenology. This page therefore functions as a structural bridge that helps readers move from the broad [[saov9-self-aware-networks-overview]] into the mechanism pages that explain the vocabulary used in SAN prose.

The external comparison surface is kept explicit because SAN pages often discuss consciousness and prediction in relation to established cognitive-science frameworks. [[attention-schema-theory]] is relevant where SAN prose frames awareness as a model of attention; Graziano's 2015 Frontiers in Psychology review identifies AST as a mechanistic account of subjective awareness. [[predictive-processing]] and [[free-energy-principle]] are relevant where SAN prose discusses prediction and error minimization; Clark's 2013 Behavioral and Brain Sciences article and Friston's 2010 Nature Reviews Neuroscience review support those comparison points. [[global-workspace-theory]] and [[integrated-information-theory]] remain comparison pages, not proof that SAN reduces to either framework.
