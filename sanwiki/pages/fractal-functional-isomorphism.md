# Fractal Functional Isomorphism

**Fractal Functional Isomorphism** is the [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks claim that the same functional role recurs across nested neural scales — that is, the function performed by a [[dendritic-pattern-detection|dendritic arbor]], a [[cortical-column-oscillatory-transmission|cortical column]], and a [[neural-oscillation-hierarchy|whole-brain oscillator field]] is *isomorphic*, not merely analogous, when each is read as an instance of the same [[coincidence-pattern-neural-bit|coincidence-pattern]] cycle. It is the functional sibling of [[fractal-brain-structure]] and the load-bearing assumption behind [[functional-fractal-isomorphism]], [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators]], and [[fractal-retinotopic-mirroring]].

## Position

Fractal Functional Isomorphism is the *functional* leg of SAN's two-leg fractality commitment — the structural leg being [[fractal-brain-structure]]. Where structural fractality says the morphology repeats, functional fractality says the *role* repeats: a [[neural-functional-fractal|neural functional fractal]] performs the same kind of pattern-detect-then-predict operation at each level of the [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|nested-scale stack]]. The canonical evidence sentence (`05san--md`, line 78, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/05san.md)) ties the claim specifically to [[cortical-traveling-waves-san-submission|cortical traveling waves]]: their *function* — propagating phase-aligned predictive bits — is the same at every scale at which a wave can travel, which is what makes the cross-scale identity *isomorphism* rather than analogy. The category-theoretic framing from `a0551z--md` (line 63, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0551z.md)) — the set "abc inside object a, object b, and object…" — is [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s gesture at expressing fractal functional isomorphism as a containment functor: the same morphism repeats inside each level of the object hierarchy. Inside SAN this licenses [[multi-scale-causality]] (because functional roles are scale-invariant, no scale is privileged), [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators]] (because the oscillator's role is the same at each scale), and [[napot-theory|NAPOT's]] tomographic reading (because the same readout works at every level).

## Post-SAN

Adjacent published literature has approached parts of this territory under different names. Multi-scale predictive-coding accounts treat cortical computation as a hierarchy in which the same Bayesian update is performed at each level, but they typically stop short of declaring the levels *isomorphic* in the SAN sense. Renormalization-group framings of brain criticality (and the [[brain-self-organized-criticality|self-organized criticality]] literature more broadly) supply the mathematical machinery — scale-invariance under coarse-graining — that an isomorphism claim would need; SAN goes further by asserting that the *function*, not only the statistics, repeats. Where SAN makes a testable prediction the published literature does not is in [[fractal-retinotopic-mirroring]] and in the prediction that [[cortical-traveling-waves-san-submission|traveling waves]] of different sizes carry the same predictive role, which Lane G acquisition of mainstream wave-propagation papers can be used to test directly.
