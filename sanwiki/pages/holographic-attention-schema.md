## Post-SAN

Mainstream [[attention]]-schema theory (Graziano and collaborators) and global-workspace / integrated-information accounts of [[consciousness]] already acknowledge that the brain's [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model of [[attention]] is *not* a discrete homunculus but a distributed construction. First, it predicts that the schema's content should survive *focal* lesions disproportionately well — because holographic storage means each piece of the [[cortex]] carries a low-resolution copy of the whole — while degrading *gracefully and globally* under distributed disruption (matching the empirical signature of cortical interference-pattern storage).
