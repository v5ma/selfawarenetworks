## Origin

Sleep spindles, in the wider [[neuroscience]] literature, are 12–15 Hz thalamocortical oscillations characteristic of stage-2 NREM sleep; [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripples]], per the [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripples]] manifest neighbour, are "brief high-frequency oscillations (100–200 Hz) generated in the [[hippocampus]] through synchronized CA3–CA1 neuronal firing" that "replay previous neuronal sequences at accel[erated speed]." The slug pairs them because, in the standard memory-consolidation model the [[memory-consolidation|memory consolidation]] page surveys, hippocampal [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripples]] occur preferentially during the troughs / spindle-coupled phase windows of cortical sleep spindles, providing a synchronized replay window across hippocampal and cortical circuits.
