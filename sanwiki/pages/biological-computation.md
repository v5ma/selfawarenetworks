# Biological Computation

**Biological computation** is physical information processing performed by living systems. In
neural tissue, membrane voltage, ion-channel state, synaptic transmission, dendritic integration,
inhibition, neuromodulation, cellular morphology, plasticity, and recurrent population activity can
all alter what happens next. Calling this computation is not merely metaphorical, but identifying a
physical variable as computational does not establish the particular code or conscious role SAN
assigns to it.

SAN's multiscale hypothesis is that molecular, synaptic, cellular, and population processes
interact. "Scale-inseparable" does not mean every mechanism contributes equally to every computation
or that a signal can be interpreted without its receiver and context.

## Established biological operations

- membrane and ion-channel dynamics determine excitability and intrinsic response properties;
- dendritic location, sequence, and branch state can change integration;
- presynaptic waveform and calcium entry influence probabilistic transmitter release;
- inhibition and recurrent connectivity select, stabilize, or suppress population activity;
- synaptic and structural plasticity alter later responses; and
- sensory-motor consequences return through reafferent pathways and update later activity.

Some neurons are autorhythmic oscillators, while others resonate preferentially with particular
input frequencies. This cellular diversity was explicitly described by Llinas in 1988 and remains
an important donor for SAN's oscillator-centered interpretation.

- [Llinas on intrinsic electrophysiological properties](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3059497/)
- [Dendritic sequence discrimination](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1189664)
- [Tuft dendrites and flexible learning](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adx4358)
- [Action-potential waveform and transmitter release](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.17-10-03425.1997)

## SAN interpretation

SAN proposes that biological variables act as physically embodied quantities in a recurrent,
oscillatory computation. Maintained tonic population activity supplies a receiver context or
canvas. Relatively differentiated phasic events supply changing ink. Dendrites and local circuits
receive and condition signals; axons and population pathways project consequences; inhibition,
recurrence, and motor feedback alter which routes remain effective.

This is a distributed observer-action account. No separate inner viewer reads a hidden screen. The
network itself maintains context, registers differences, predicts, acts, receives consequences,
and changes its later state.

## Claims that remain hypotheses

SAN's stronger claims are not established neuroscience:

- that a joined tonic field constitutes the receiver of phasic changes;
- that phase-wave differentials form a general neural update language;
- that COT or NAPOT explains the construction of conscious experience;
- that microtubule dynamics supply a specific cognitive or conscious code; and
- that some biological computation uses a universal four-state alphabet.

Microtubules are essential cytoskeletal, transport, and organizational structures. Proposed
computational and oscillatory roles deserve explicit tests, but it is not established that every
neuronal microtubule array encodes numbers or participates directly in a conscious display.
Likewise, CaMKII states and multivesicular release do not by themselves validate a fixed
`0/1/2/3` neural code.

## Measurement contract

Each proposed computational quantity must name its physical unit, measurement method, spatial and
temporal scale, receiver, downstream consequence, and competing explanation. Action-potential
width, calcium influx, vesicle count, postsynaptic response, phase, frequency, amplitude, and field
strength are related variables, not interchangeable names for one signal.
