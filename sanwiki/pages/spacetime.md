# Spacetime

**Spacetime** in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] is not the inert Lorentzian manifold of textbook general relativity but a dynamical, [[oscillator]]-built medium whose local geometry, density, and phase structure are continuously produced and re-produced by the activity of physical [[oscillators]] — including neurons. The concept connects directly to the [[qgtcd|QGTCD]] / [[dark-time-theory|Dark Time Theory]] / [[super-information-theory|Super Information Theory]] stack and provides the substrate for [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]], [[particle-gravity-time-density|time-density gravity]], and the SAN claim that the [[brain-as-rendering-engine|brain renders experience]] in [[continuous-neural-rendering|continuous]] spacetime.

## Roots

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s documented engagement with spacetime as more than a coordinate scaffold goes back at least to a 2012 dialogue with Ben Thomas (`core-set--Micah_talks_to_Ben_Thomas`, line 89, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2012), where he was already using the phrase "path of SpaceTime" in connection with brain processes and observation — the earliest dated anchor in the packet. By the time of the [[a0001z|a-series]] writing he had crystallized a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-referential definition: "I am a convergence of patterns that exist & oscillate in spacetime. These patterns are rendered by a 3D neural network (the brain) and detected (observed) by the same 3D neural network (the brain)" (`a0001z--md`, line 16, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0001z.md)). The earliest abstract bump for the book frames the chapter scope explicitly: "I argue that vortices are [[oscillation|oscillations]], then I try to talk about the natural selection of spacetime" (`00abstractbump--md`, line 17, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/00abstractbump.md)). The 2017 SIT history note (`dl-current--history_of_SIT`, line 106, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2017) places the eventual mathematical formalization in dialogue with [[causal-fermion-systems|Causal Fermion Systems]] of Felix Finster, where "spacetime structure emerges from a concrete 'web of correlations' within a many-body quantum system" — an external lineage the SAN treatment converges with rather than imitates.

## Position

The SAN spacetime is generative, not given. In [[a0010z|a0010z]] [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] states the principle directly: "[[oscillators]] define spacetime, all [[oscillators]] create or manipulate the volume of space around them" (`a0010z--md`, line 5, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0010z.md)) — meaning the manifold is the running output of the [[oscillator]] population, not an empty container the population sits inside. This is the pivot that makes the neuron-level claim coherent: a neuron, when it fires, "is literally warping spacetime" ([[a0018z|a0018z]], line 8, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0018z.md)) — an [[action-potential|action-potential]]-scale [[particle-gravity-time-density|time-density]] perturbation continuous with the cosmological warping of mass-energy. From there the SAN frame extends naturally into the [[qgtcd|QGTCD]] picture: spiking neurons "balance a cellular [[dissipative-system|dissipative system]] in response to the changing density of spacetime so as to export entropy and maintain structural equilibrium" (`a0034z--md`, line 3, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0034z.md)). The geometry inherited from general relativity ("[[configuration-space|configuration]] spacetime for planets determines the path of planets, where the shortest path minimizes proper time," `a0041z--md`, line 64, on github) is preserved as the macroscopic limit, but reinterpreted: at all scales the curvature is a [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-field]] phenomenon — [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] even writes of "the spacetime phasefield" (`a0043z--md`, line 11, on github) and a "[[napot-tomography-not-holography|spacetime holographic tomography]]" (`a0026z--md`, line 26, on github). The unifying SAN claim is that the same [[oscillator]] algebra that produces the manifold also produces [[consciousness]]: "I argue that vortices are oscillations, then … the natural selection of spacetime" (`00abstractbump--md`, line 17). [[13san|13san]] is careful to deflate one mistake the framing invites — even if "deeper spacetime loops exist they add precision, not the spark of [[consciousness]]" (`13san--md`, line 269, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/13san.md)) — keeping the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness|hard problem]] from being smuggled into geometry.

## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream physics, SAN's spacetime makes three commitments that distinguish it from the textbook GR + QFT picture. First, it accepts emergent-spacetime programs ([[causal-fermion-systems|Causal Fermion Systems]], holographic correspondences, quantum-gravity time programs) as broadly compatible — the 2017 SIT history (`dl-current--history_of_SIT`, line 111, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2017) explicitly endorses CFS's reformulation in which "spacetime structure emerges from a concrete 'web of correlations' within a many-body quantum system." Second, it makes a specifically neural [[prediction]] not present in CFS or in standard [[quantum-field-theory|QFT]]: that neuron-scale events are not merely embedded in spacetime but actively warp local time-density (`a0018z--md`, line 8; `a0034z--md`, line 3) — a claim that is in principle testable through high-precision EEG-coupled gravimetry and through the [[qgtcd-dark-time-theory|QGTCD dark-time]] predictions [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] catalogs. Third, it deflates the temptation to identify [[consciousness]] with deep spacetime loops: per [[13san|13san]] line 269, even arbitrarily deep loops "add precision, not the spark of [[consciousness]]" — a constraint that separates SAN from panpsychist or Penrose–Hameroff style accounts that locate experience in geometry alone, without requiring the [[self-aware-networks|self-aware-network]] organizational principle.
