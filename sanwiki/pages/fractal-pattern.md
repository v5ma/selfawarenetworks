A **fractal pattern** in SAN theory is a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar structure that repeats functionally across scales — from quantum oscillations and single synapses up through neurons, cortical columns, whole brains, and the cosmos itself.

## Core principle

The key claim is *functional isomorphism at multiple scales*: the processing pattern of a single neuron mirrors that of a cortical column, which mirrors whole-brain activity (EEG/MEG/MRI). This is not merely visual resemblance — the same computational logic (oscillation, inhibition, phase-cycle) recurs at each level.

Related: [[napot]], [[neomindcycle]], [[self-aware-network]].

## Fractal dimensions in SAN

**Networks**: brain activity EEG follows a 1/F magnitude-frequency power law, linking single-cell dynamics to macro brain waves. The activity pattern of a small cortical patch reconstructs the whole-brain pattern — a focusing property [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] calls the *lens of the mind*.

**Morphology**: hand is a functional fractal of a [[dendrite]]; retina, cortical column, entorhinal-hippocampus, and gray matter are each scale-shifted instances of the same template. Jeff Hawkins' thousand-brains reference frames are cited as supporting evidence.

**Phases**: the tonic → phasic → inhibited state cycle is the *fractal phase* — the same three-phase rhythm observed at synapse, neuron, column, and global EEG timescales.

**Cosmos**: the universe as a fractal of oscillations — every vortex, particle, and dissipative system is cyclical. Galaxies, planets, and cells are functionally isomorphic because all are dipole-vortex-dissipative systems. See [[fractal-dissipating-cosmos]].

## In SAN theory

The fractal-pattern claim underpins [[napot]]: if each level of neural organization is [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar, then a perturbation at one scale (a single synapse firing) can functionally resonant at all scales — grounding SAN's account of consciousness as a *whole-brain* [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling event rather than a local one.

Cross-domain applications noted in source: society as fractal of neuronal connectivity; immune T-cells as fractal of public-servant roles; cancer/fascism as [[gh-map-intro-fractal|fractals]] of colonizing spread.

## History

Early formulations treated the fractal as primarily visual/morphological (retina resembles [[dendrite]]). Later SAN writing shifted emphasis to *functional* isomorphism — same computational pattern, not same physical shape. See [[neomindcycle]] for the scaling-fractal elaboration.
