# Metastable Synchronization

**Metastable synchronization** is the regime in which transient [[brain-rhythms|brain rhythms]] arise as partial, time-varying phase locking across distributed cortical areas — not stable global coherence, but a sequence of brief synchronized states whose collective frequency is *reduced* by inter-area conduction delays.
