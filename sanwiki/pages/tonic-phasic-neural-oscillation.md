# Tonic-Phasic Neural Oscillation

**Tonic-phasic neural oscillation** is the historical oscillatory form of SAN's canvas/ink model.
The medically bounded formulation is maintained at [[tonic-phasic-canvas|Tonic and Phasic Roles:
Canvas and Ink]].

## Current interpretation

Ongoing oscillatory state can constrain the effect of a transient event, and a transient event can
change later oscillatory and cellular state. The direction and function of that relation depend on
area, cell type, circuit, frequency, task, behavior, measurement, and timescale. It is not a fixed
two-layer anatomy.

The following historical claims are not retained as medical facts:

- tonic oscillations are always slow, high-amplitude, and globally synchronized;
- phasic oscillations span one universal 0.02-600 Hz content range;
- one phasic spike etches a conscious shape into a magnetic field;
- dendritic growth stores one represented shape as a literal branch drawing;
- a neural tonic state is a biological zero-point field;
- the same oscillator rule can be transferred to cosmology or quantum entanglement as evidence.

Oscillations and dendritic plasticity are established biological phenomena. Their unification as a
field-cell observer and rendering mechanism is the SAN hypothesis that requires separate tests.
