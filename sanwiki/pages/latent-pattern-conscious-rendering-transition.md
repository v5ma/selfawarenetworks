# From Latent Pattern to Conscious Rendering

## The missing question

What distinguishes information that is processed without current awareness from content that becomes part of a conscious experience? SAN does not answer by placing a consciousness switch in one region. It proposes a changing distributed relationship among latent memory constraints, reconstructed candidate patterns, the active observer-action state, and routes toward report or action.

The strongest current formulation is not **coherence equals consciousness**. It is a selective joining-and-leaving process:

```text
latent learned constraints + present cue and context
-> reconstructed candidate or virtual engram
-> selective recruitment and increasing reciprocal influence
-> temporary coupling to the active distributed rendering
-> persistence, flexible consequence, comparison, choice, report, or action
-> inhibition, detuning, competition, or loss of reciprocal influence
-> departure from the current rendering without erasing the latent substrate
```

The participating network performs the transition. There is no inner viewer that receives a finished picture.

## Five states that should not be collapsed

| State | Operational meaning | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Latent learned constraint | Synaptic, dendritic, cellular, glial, and circuit changes bias later recruitment. | A stored miniature experience or a currently conscious memory. |
| Active nonconscious processing | A pattern affects discrimination, priming, readiness, or behavior without demonstrated flexible conscious access. | Absence of processing or causal influence. |
| Reconstructed candidate | A cue and current state recruit a temporary distributed representation, called a **virtual engram** in SAN. | Consciousness merely because an engram ensemble reactivated. |
| Coupled conscious candidate | The reconstructed pattern gains recurrent, reciprocal influence with parts of the current distributed rendering and becomes available to broader consequence. | A universal frequency, one anatomical gate, or one scalar threshold. |
| Departing content | Coupling, persistence, or relative influence declines while other patterns gain control; the learned substrate can remain available for later reconstruction. | Erasure of the memory or complete loss of unconscious influence. |

The everyday term **subconscious** can refer to several of these conditions. Experimental work should instead state whether the content is latent, active but unreported, preconscious, inaccessible, suppressed, priming behavior, or available for flexible use.

## Selective joining and leaving

Coherence or phase compatibility can be one way a candidate becomes able to interact with an active assembly, but phase alignment alone is not the transition. The measurable operation must include a declared source and receiver, recurrent recruitment, effective influence in both directions, persistence over a stated interval, and a downstream capacity that was absent before recruitment.

In SAN's current interpretation:

- **joining** means that a reconstructed candidate and part of the active rendering increasingly constrain one another through recurrent biological routes;
- **relative dominance** means that the candidate gains measurable causal influence over relevant alternatives, not that it becomes the brain's largest waveform;
- **leaving** means that inhibition, detuning, competition, adaptation, or loss of reciprocal influence removes the content from the current distributed rendering;
- **persistence of memory** means that the underlying learned constraints can reconstruct a related pattern later, potentially with different participating cells.

This makes conscious content dynamic without making memory disposable.

## Choice as a topology-changing junction

Choice is where the changing functional topology can select which candidate becomes part of the current picture and which route proceeds toward action. A candidate that is not yet consciously accessible may prime or constrain the competition. Temporary reciprocal coupling can then unite parts of an active conscious state with a reconstructed candidate, while inhibition and loss of coupling separate other content.

The proposal is therefore not that a hidden chooser inspects one conscious route and one unconscious route. The distributed competition, joining, commitment, action, and returned consequence constitute the choice process.

```text
nonconscious candidates + current conscious context + learned criteria
-> recruitment, suppression, and changing effective connectivity
-> temporary functional union or separation
-> selected conscious content and action-ready route
-> action and returned sensory or bodily consequence
-> changed readiness for the next cycle
```

## Attention Schema Theory boundary

Early Attention Schema Theory contributes a different but relevant question: can a simplified internal model of selective attention help a system predict and control attention and attribute awareness? SAN accepts that control-model problem without treating it as the complete physiological transition from nonconscious processing to rendered experience.

This page assigns AST its attention-model and control role. It assigns SAN's virtual engram, receiver-relative tonic and phasic organization, PWD, recurrent joining, distributed observer-action, and choice topology to SAN's own source genealogy. Whether later AST formulations converge with parts of that architecture is a Comparison Wiki question and does not change the historical source boundary here.

## Established substrate, SAN proposal, and open claim

| Evidence level | Current status |
|---|---|
| Established neuroscience | Learned neural changes can persist; engram ensembles can be reactivated; processing can affect behavior without report; attention, visibility, access, memory, and motor preparation can dissociate; recurrent and effective connectivity vary with state and task. |
| Supported systems interpretation | Entry into flexible access involves distributed, recurrent, task-dependent changes rather than one universal anatomical gate. Content can cease to be reportable while learned constraints and nonconscious effects remain. |
| SAN hypothesis | A carrier-flexible virtual engram becomes part of the active rendering through selective reciprocal coupling, recurrent recruitment, receiver-relative timing, and rising causal influence; inhibition or detuning can reverse that relation. |
| Stronger open claim | The joined distributed state is not merely accessible but constitutes the corresponding phenomenal content. |

Primary evidence anchors include distributed engram reactivation ([Roy et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-29384-4)), representational drift with population-level stability ([Roth and Merriam, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-40144-w)), dissociation of perceptual integration from conscious access ([Fahrenfort et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1617268114)), and report/no-report differences in apparent frontal involvement ([Hatamimajoumerd et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.07.068)). These findings support components and discriminating experiments, not the complete SAN consciousness claim.

## A discriminating experiment

Track the same learned content across latent, primed, reconstructed, consciously accessed, and post-access conditions. Measure ensemble identity, firing rate, recurrent effective connectivity, bidirectional influence, relative timing, inhibition, persistence, flexible report, memory, and action. Then compare:

1. rate and ensemble-identity models;
2. ordinary recurrent-state and effective-connectivity models;
3. attention-schema or access-only models; and
4. the SAN virtual-engram, receiver-relative PWD, and joining-and-leaving model.

Perturb the predicted reciprocal route while preserving sensory input and approximately matched mean activity. The SAN-specific account gains support only if its added variables predict when content joins and leaves flexible conscious use, and if route-specific perturbation changes that transition in the predicted direction. Correlation, decoding, gamma power, phase locking, or report alone is insufficient.
