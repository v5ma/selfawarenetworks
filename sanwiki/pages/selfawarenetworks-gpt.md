# Self Aware Networks GPT: Historical Research Interface

The Self Aware Networks GPT was introduced as an AI-assisted interface for navigating Micah Blumberg's SAN repository, connecting questions and user-supplied files to relevant notes, and encouraging attributed research contributions through GitHub.

It is a historical research interface, not an authoritative statement of SAN, a medical reference, or evidence that any SAN hypothesis is correct.

> [!important] Assistant output is navigation, not SAN authority
> Speaker labels are part of the evidence. Micah-labeled text can be dated author-source evidence;
> Self Aware Networks GPT-labeled text remains assistant output, even when it says that an idea
> "aligns," "supports," "validates," or "fully validates" SAN. [[gh-02san|02san Source Record]]
> preserves a mixed dialogue under that exact boundary. Assistant wording also cannot demote
> [[phase-wave-differentials|PWD]] from core SAN architecture or turn the optional
> [[phase-wave-differential-calculus|calculus]] into PWD's definition.

## Historical Record

- `chat_with_selfawarenetworksgpt.md` first entered the public Git history on **January 5, 2024**.
- The alpha GPT used the SAOv9 table of contents as a map of SAN topics.
- It invited researchers to contribute useful chat findings through attributed GitHub pull requests.
- The alpha explicitly did **not** search the audio-transcription archive.
- A later same-day revision added the warning that AI answers could be wrong and should be checked
  against Micah's notes. That warning was not present in the first-fixation bytes.

That warning is the page's most important continuing rule:

**AI output routes attention; dated author sources and reviewed external evidence determine what may be stated publicly.**

## Current Status Boundary

The historical GPT link now resolves to [Self Aware Networks GPT on ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-FA3lrTWTq-self-aware-networks-gpt). Its present availability, configuration, files, retrieval coverage, and behavior may depend on login and may change independently of this Wiki.

Later source-recovery and retranscription archives are outside the 2024 GPT's documented alpha capability. This page must not imply that the historical GPT searches those files.

## Source Priority

When an AI answer describes SAN, verify it in this order:

1. the exact dated author recording, transcript, article, or repository note;
2. immutable Git history or another preserved public source;
3. the current SAN genealogy and source-map pages;
4. primary scientific literature for established component mechanisms;
5. AI-generated summaries only as secondary navigation.

An AI summary must not silently replace the source's wording, erase a source date, convert a metaphor into anatomy, or convert a SAN proposal into settled science.

## Frameworks the Interface Attempted to Route

### Self-Aware Networks and NAPOT

The historical page described oscillatory arrays, tonic context, phasic differences, recurrent projection, inhibition, perception, and memory in compressed language. These are now separated across [[self-aware-networks]], [[napot-overview]], [[phase-wave-differentials]], [[tonic-phasic-canvas]], and [[san-scale-ladder-and-claim-boundaries]]. PWD is core SAN architecture; NAPOT uses PWD throughout but does not contain, define, or outrank it.

The original wording must not be read as establishing that excitation is always perception, inhibition is always memory, or one frequency band has one universal function.

### Distributed Observer

The historical page referred to an observer "inside" an organism. The source-faithful SAN interpretation does not add a second inner entity; it places the proposed observer function in the receiving, transforming, transmitting, and recurrent network activity associated with observation and action. This is a SAN interpretation, not an independently established anatomical object. See [[screen-metaphor-no-inner-viewer]].

### NDCA

Non-linear Differential Continuous Approximation is a SAN proposal about stabilizing and updating useful continuous representations from changing neural activity. It is not established merely because neural manifolds, nonlinear dynamics, and predictive processing exist. See [[ndca-non-linear-differential-continuous-approximation]].

### QGTCD

Quantum Gradient Time Crystal Dilation is a separate physics proposal. Calling it a unified field theory states its intended scope, not scientific validation. Neuroscience evidence does not prove its claims. See [[qgtcd-overview]].

## Scale Check

- **Micro:** source statements about dendrites, membrane state, spikes, inhibition, and release require cellular evidence.
- **Meso:** oscillating assemblies, recurrent arrays, traveling waves, and cortical or thalamocortical interactions require circuit-level evidence.
- **Macro:** distributed rendering, observer-action, memory, report, and agency require network and behavioral evidence.

An AI assistant must not collapse these levels into one generic neuron, one brainwave, or one hidden observer.
