# Cortical Column Oscillatory Synchronization

> **Image status (2026-07-18):** The retained plate is conceptual and is not medically certified. It depicts cortical columns as local sensory frames, long-range phase alignment as the gate to consciousness, and apical pyramidal activity as an anesthesia bottleneck. Those universal anatomical and phenomenal claims are unsupported.

**Cortical column oscillatory synchronization** is a candidate description of timing relations among local cortical populations. A cortical column is not a universally bounded processor or display pixel, and columnar organization differs by area, species, scale, and definition.

## Correct the anatomical route

Long-range corticocortical projections are predominantly axons of excitatory pyramidal neurons. Inhibitory interneurons usually act locally, where they shape timing, gain, and recurrent dynamics. It is therefore inaccurate to describe global cortical coordination as being carried by long-distance interneuron networks.

Apical dendrites of pyramidal neurons integrate feedback and contextual input and are affected by many anesthetic mechanisms. But anesthesia does not prove that one apical pathway synchronizes columns into consciousness. Different anesthetics act on multiple receptors, channels, cell types, and brain systems, including arousal and thalamocortical circuits.

## No column-frame threshold

Local sensory activity can occur without reportable awareness, but this does not prove that consciousness appears only when columns phase-align. Attention, local recurrence, long-range interaction, report, confidence, and motor preparation can dissociate. The receiving circuit and experimental target must be specified.

## SAN interpretation

SAN can model local populations as partial contributors to a distributed working model. Their outputs may be selectively coordinated through corticocortical, thalamocortical, recurrent, and oscillatory relations. No column contains a complete frame, and no global synchrony gate creates an inner screen.

## Tests

Define the participating populations anatomically, measure direction and phase lag, control common input and movement, and perturb a specific projection rather than a band in isolation. Predict which content, access, or behavior should fail and which local responses should remain.
