**Entification** is the process by which many individual [[sensor-transmitter]] units — bound together through [[oscillatory-synchrony]] — merge into a single unified entity that is itself a [[fractal-agent]] containing [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar parts.

> **Scientific status:** Entification is a proposed eligibility framework, not an established
> process by which synchrony automatically creates an entity, agent, viewpoint, or consciousness.
> A candidate requires a measurable boundary, recurrent access to relevant state, selective
> propagation, memory-dependent transitions, alternative-sensitive control, action that changes
> later input, and a persistent self-related coordinate or control model. These provisional tests
> are neither necessary nor sufficient until validated. Similarity across molecular, cellular,
> circuit, organismal, social, and artificial scales does not prove the same computation or a
> conscious fractal hierarchy. Interpersonal coordination does not merge people into one subject.

## Definition

A group of smaller sensor-transmitter parts that oscillate in synchrony ceases to function as a collection of independent agents and begins to function as a single agent. The entified whole is a unified sensor-transmitter system whose parts remain recognizably [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar to the whole — a fractal organization.

A whole human is the canonical example: a single unified agent entified from billions of individual cellular sensor-transmitter systems, each itself entified from molecular components.

## Mechanism

Entification proceeds through [[nonlinear-differential-continuous-approximation]], which harmonizes the rendered patterns emerging from each lower-level agent into a coherent higher-level pattern.

Functional connectivity depends on **frequency matching**: when sub-agents produce congruent outputs they amplify one another, yielding a dominant [[phase-wave-differential]] that becomes a chain of conscious rendered thought and action. Divergent sub-agent outputs fail to entify.

## Scale-independence

The following list preserves the historical SAN scale hypothesis. Each transition requires its own
biological denominator, mechanism, intervention, and failure condition; it does not automatically
produce a fractal agent or transfer consciousness across scales.

The same process operates at every scale:
- Molecules → cells
- Cells → cortical columns
- Columns → brain hemispheres
- Brains → inter-human collectives (group agency, collective coordination)
- Collective intelligences → AGI minds composed of many smaller agent minds

Each level of entification produces a fractal agent with agency appropriate to its scale.

Within the brain, entification is enabled by [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography): groups of cells render patterns via oscillatory tomography, and these rendered patterns are then entified into larger unified viewpoints through [[oscillatory-binding]].

## Connection to Agentic Viewpoints

An agentic viewpoint is a proposed SAN correlate, not an established electromagnetic slice or a
validated mechanism of consciousness. Synchrony, field persistence, or molecular support alone is
insufficient to establish viewpoint, experience, or selfhood.

Each entified entity constitutes an [[agentic-viewpoint]] — a rendered slice of the electromagnetic field that is temporarily held coherent by its molecular substrate. Entification is thus both the mechanism of consciousness and the mechanism of [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organization at every physical scale.
