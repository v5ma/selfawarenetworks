# Retroactive Correction Audit: Grid and Reference Frames

## Why the old correction needed another review

The July 2026 rewrite made several pages more medically and scientifically precise. A later review
showed that three corrections also narrowed SAN more than the sources justified. This audit applies
the new rule: when wording looks wrong, first search Micah's work for the legitimate operation that
the rough wording was trying to express.

Retroactive review does not give new language an old date. It keeps four events separate: the
original source, the old Wiki page, the first correction, and the later recovery audit.

## The newly recovered point

The phrase "electromagnetic tunnels or vortices" was not simply invented by the old Wiki page. It
came from Micah's June 2022 `a0266z` note, beside a wave-of-magnification proposal and questions
about grid cells, place cells, and three-dimensional coding. Another June 2022 note described a
dissipating vortex as a transient or decaying oscillator or resonator. The constructive correction
is therefore:

```text
selected local pattern
-> recurrent or resonant persistence
-> re-expression through connected populations
-> measurable availability to downstream receivers
-> candidate contribution to a volumetric body-world model
```

That chain is a SAN hypothesis. It does not require a place field itself to be a literal vortex.
The missing field variable, pathway, spatial scale, receiver, and causal test remain open research
requirements.
