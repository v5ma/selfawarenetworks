## Position

In [[self-aware-networks]], the OFC plays two compatible roles. As an **input region**, it is treated as the olfactory analogue of a primary sensory [[cortex]] — receiving smell information via the olfactory [[cortex]] → dorsomedial-thalamus → OFC pathway named in `b0069y--md` line 235 (`b0069y--md`, line 235, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0069y.md)) — which lets the SAN account use the same [[cortical-columns-reference-frames]] machinery for olfactory content that [[3d-semantic-segmentation]] uses for visual scene structure and that [[auditory-cortex]] anchors for auditory content. As an **interface region**, the OFC is one of the cortical territories most directly accessible from outside the skull: the b0310y sources point to forehead-mediated access as the practical reason it shows up in [[brain-computer-interface]] / [[brain-machine-interface]] / [[neuromodulation-brainwave-entrainment]] discussions (`b0310ySci--md`, line 2269; `original_archived_b0310yGoogle--md`, line 1020). Tied together, this means the OFC sits in the SAN material at the intersection of "where olfactory content gets rendered into [[consciousness]]" and "where non-invasive [[eeg]]-class instruments can read or bias frontal-[[cortex]] activity" — which is exactly the kind of dual-use region the [[brain-as-rendering-engine]] / [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering]] framing requires when it claims both [[attention]] and [[attention-schema]] effects can be modulated by [[consciousness-as-neurofeedback]] practice.
