## Critique of quantum alternatives

The 2014 thread explicitly rejects quantum-based consciousness accounts:

- **[[observer-effect|Observer effect]]** means measurement disturbs quantum states â€” it does not grant consciousness primacy over physical existence.
- **Microtubule quantum coherence** ([[orch-or]], Penrose-Hameroff): produces only random noise at synaptic scale; random noise is not structured signal and cannot carry meaning.
- **Pineal gland mysticism**: lacks credible empirical support; claims do not trace to peer-reviewed research.

The brain-as-movie model requires no quantum mechanism. Classical synaptic signaling, shaped by natural selection, is sufficient.

## Neuroscience grounding

Evidence cited against soul-independence of consciousness: personality changes from drugs, disease, malnutrition, trauma; split-brain patients presenting as two distinct agents (left vs. right hemisphere). These facts constrain any theory of mind to one where [[consciousness]] supervenes on physical brain state â€” not on a substrate independent of spacetime.
