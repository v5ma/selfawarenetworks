# Super Time Position

**Super Time Position (STP)** is the [[micah-blumberg|Blumberg]]-introduced primitive that quantum particles carry an *ultrafast, deterministic phase position* — a "time-gear" angle that we cannot measure directly but whose under-sampling we observe as quantum randomness. STP sits inside the [[super-dark-time|Super Dark Time (SDT)]] / [[qgtcd|QGTCD]] / [[super-information-theory|Super Information Theory]] family of theories and is its specifically-quantum-foundational primitive: it is the candidate explanation, in this lineage, for measurement-collapse statistics.

## Post-SAN

STP's nearest mainstream counterpart is the family of *deterministic hidden-variable* interpretations of quantum mechanics — Bohmian pilot-wave theory and t'Hooft's cellular automaton interpretation are the two best-known. Where STP differs is its tying of the hidden phase to a *time-density* substrate that also carries gravity, [[entropic-gravity|entropic gravity]], and the [[hubble-tension|Hubble tension]] anomaly within one covariant action — a unification not attempted in pilot-wave or cellular-automaton accounts. The STP/SDT pairing also competes directly with the Relativistic Transactional Interpretation: the [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]]/RTI dialogue tabulates the comparison feature-by-feature (`QGTCD_EG_RTI_02--md`, line 681). The framework's falsifiable predictions — that [[bell-inequality|Bell-inequality]] violations should track local time-density gradients, and that [[gravitational-time-dilation|gravitational time-dilation]] should leak into quantum-statistics observables — distinguish STP from competitors. STP is also positioned as a [[dark-matter-alternative|dark-matter alternative]] mechanism in the same way [[mond|MOND]] is, but at the quantum-foundations rather than galactic-rotation scale.
