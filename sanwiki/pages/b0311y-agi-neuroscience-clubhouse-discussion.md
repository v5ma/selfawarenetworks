**[[embodiment-in-cognition|Embodiment in cognition]]**: The group debates whether AGI requires physical embodiment — whether spatial representation and environmental interaction are fundamental to intelligence. **[[neuroplasticity]] and circuit reorganization**: Adaptive vs. pathological plasticity (e.g., neuropathic pain), relating to SAN's view that [[gh-a0206z|self]]-aware networks continuously remodel their own representations.
