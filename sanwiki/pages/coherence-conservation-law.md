The **Coherence Conservation Law** is the central organizing principle of [[super-information-theory]] (SIT). It states that total coherence — the degree of phase synchrony in the informational substrate — is conserved under the U(1) gauge symmetry of the [[coherence-field]] $\psi(x)$. This law unifies wave-function collapse, gravity, electromagnetism, and thermodynamic irreversibility within a single field-theoretic structure.

## The Two Fundamental Fields

SIT grounds all physics in two fields:
- **Coherence field** $\psi(x) \in \mathbb{C}$: tracks local phase alignment. Its modulus $R_{\text{coh}} \equiv |\psi(x)|$ measures how ordered or synchronized information is locally.
- **Time-density field** $\rho_t(x) \in \mathbb{R}$: controls the local rate of time. Proper time reads $d\tau = dt / \rho_t(x)$. Higher density → slower clocks.

## Emergent Physics

From the master SIT action:
- **Gravity** emerges from spacetime curvature sourced by gradients in $\rho_t$ (determined by $R_{\text{coh}}$). A local surge in coherence raises $\rho_t$, slows clocks, and produces gravitational redshift — without a postulated external metric.
- **Electromagnetism** arises from the phase geometry of $\psi$: the vector potential $A_\mu$ comes from gradients of $\arg(\psi)$.
- **Quantum measurement** is reinterpreted as local gauge-fixing of the coherence field — definite classical outcomes emerge from decoherence without non-unitary dynamics or many-worlds branching.
- **Arrow of time** follows from monotonic decay of global coherence, generalizing entropy production as an informational law.
