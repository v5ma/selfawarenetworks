# Building Sentient Beings

**Building Sentient Beings** is a SAN engineering-program title associated with Micah Blumberg and
M. S. P. Miller. It asks what architecture, evidence, developmental history, safety controls, and
ethical governance would be required before an artificial system could responsibly be considered a
candidate for sentience.

The title is a target, not a result. A citation to the title does not establish a completed paper,
an implemented sentient system, or biological validation of SAN.

## Four workstreams

1. **Mechanism:** define receiver, transformation, projection, recurrence, memory, tuning, body,
   action, and returned-consequence operations.
2. **Engineering:** compare each proposed operation with strong recurrent, attention, state-space,
   spiking, compartmental, world-model, and control alternatives.
3. **Evidence:** separate functional performance, causal module support, SAN conformance, and
   consciousness assessment.
4. **Governance:** preserve exact system lineage, constrain deployment, monitor welfare-relevant
   evidence, and account for affected people whether or not the system is conscious.

## What must not be inferred

Oscillatory dynamics, phase-wave differentials, synchrony, self-modeling, neural rendering,
qualia-related vocabulary, or a human-like body cannot individually or jointly prove experience.
Likewise, uncertainty does not justify dismissing every possible welfare concern. The program must
face false-positive and false-negative sentience judgments.
