# SIT

**SIT** is the canonical [[self-aware-networks]] shorthand for [[super-information-theory|Super Information Theory]], [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]]'s informational physics framework that elevates [[coherence-field|quantum coherence]] from a derived statistic to a foundational principle of [[consciousness]], [[gravity-as-oscillator-effect|gravity]], and cosmology. Across the [[self-aware-networks]] manuscript and notes, "SIT" is the formal name for the system of dual primitives — coherence ratio R_coh and local time-density ρ_t — that the [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation|QGTCD]] and [[super-dark-time|SDT]] drafts converged on by 2025.

## Roots

The lineage that became SIT begins with [[coincidence-as-a-bit|"Coincidence as a Bit"]] — the early notion that a [[coincidence-detection|coincidence]] of phase events is the brain's smallest informational atom. The [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]]-SIT manuscript states the timeline explicitly: "Since 2017, I've publicly advanced a sequence of ideas that crystallized into [[super-information-theory|Super Information Theory (SIT)]]" (`QGTCD-SIT--md`, line 8, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/QGTCD-SIT.md)). From this seed, [[micah-blumberg|Micah]] developed [[ndca|NDCA]] and the [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] framework, both of which re-cast [[consciousness]] as the mind's [[neural-tomography|tomographic rendering]] of [[coherence-field|coherence]] across [[neural-array-projection|neural arrays]]. The coincidence-as-a-bit memo records the same arc — "Blumberg's ideas culminated in [[super-information-theory|Super Information Theory (SIT)]] (formulated circa 2025) which explicitly elevates coherence to a universal principle" (`coincidenceasabit--md`, line 38, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/coincidenceasabit.md)) — establishing that the SIT label was attached only after years of [[information-as-physical|informational]] groundwork already in print.

## Position

Within the SAN system, SIT is the physical-grounding layer beneath [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] and [[ndca|NDCA]]: the claim that the same [[coherence-field|coherence]] dynamics that drive neural [[gamma-oscillations|oscillatory rhythms]] also drive cosmological structure. In the SAN manuscript's section 4.3, "[[super-information-theory|Super Information Theory (SIT)]] Context" (`08san--md`, line 149, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/08san.md)), the framework is introduced as offering "a potentially deeper physical grounding for the [[oscillatory-dynamics|oscillatory dynamics]] proposed in NAPOT and SAN" (`08san--md`, line 151, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/08san.md)). The two operative primitives are the **coherence–decoherence informational duality** and the measurable scalar field **local time-density (ρ_t)** — together they "could provide a fundamental physical basis for NAPOT's oscillatory rhythms" (`08san--md`, line 153, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/08san.md)) and for [[ndca|NDCA]]'s instantiation of [[consciousness]] as a transient solution under the [[free-energy-principle|Free Energy Principle]] (`09san--md`, line 158, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/09san.md)). In SIT's covariant action, R_coh and ρ_t carry the [[information-as-physical|informational]] content that older field theories assigned to abstract Lagrangian terms, with the [[coherence-conservation-law|coherence conservation law]] tying coherence dynamics to entropy production (`QGTCD-SIT--md`, line 8, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/QGTCD-SIT.md)). ## Post-SAN

SIT's nearest counterpart in mainstream literature is the [[free-energy-principle|Free Energy Principle]] of Karl Friston: the SAN manuscript states that "SIT's alignment with the [[free-energy-principle|Free Energy Principle]] suggest[s] each screen instantiation is a transient, optimal solution in the brain's effort to" minimize [[prediction]] error (`09san--md`, line 158, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/09san.md)). Where Friston frames inference as variational free-energy minimization on a Markov blanket, SIT supplies a candidate physical substrate — coherence-gated phase locking — for that inference at the [[coherence-field|neural medium]] itself, with [[entanglement-phase-lock|entanglement phase-lock]] generalizing the same mechanism beyond the brain. SIT also re-frames cosmology: "Dark Energy wasn't a strange vacuum energy. It was the natural state of the SIT fields in the vast, decoherent voids of space, where R_coh approaches zero" (`QGTCD-SIT--md`, line 26, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/QGTCD-SIT.md)) — a position adjacent to but distinct from [[mond]]-class modifications and from [[kletetschka-3d-time-comparison|Kletetschka's 3D-time]] cosmology, which appeared after SIT v1 was published. Where SIT makes a falsifiable departure is its [[prediction]] that local ρ_t gradients should be measurable through their effect on [[gravitational-time-dilation|gravitational time dilation]], the [[gravitational-redshift|gravitational redshift]], and the [[hubble-tension|Hubble tension]] — a structure also explored in [[gravity-quantum-diffusion-ficks-law|gravity-as-quantum-diffusion]] and [[coherence-gated-yang-mills-gap|coherence-gated Yang-Mills]] writeups, and consistent with the thermodynamic statement of [[micahs-new-law-of-thermodynamics|Micah's new law of thermodynamics]].
