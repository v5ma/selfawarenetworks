A layered model of the **metaverse** that progresses from commercial avatar platforms
toward the claim that spacetime itself is a computer into which all consciousness
eventually merges. The final stages connect directly to SAN's
[[information-theory]] and [[fractal-consciousness]] frameworks.

## Progression of Stages

The note ([[gh-a0174z|a0174z]]) sketches seven ascending levels:

1. **Avatars & personal items** — early XR social spaces (ExokitWeb era).
2. **Snow Crash** — Gibson-Stephenson-era social VR as cultural model.
3. **Nexus** — brain-interface immersion; the line between physical and digital blurs.
4. **Sword Art Online** — full-dive neural capture; identity risks inside the simulation.
5. **The Matrix** — simulation framing; reality is rendered, not given.
6. **Everyone merges with the computer that is spacetime** — SAN's [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] thesis
   applied at cosmic scale: minds are already running on physics; the metaverse
   completes the loop.
7. **The universe reboots humanity** — the cosmos as a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-correcting
   [[self-aware-network]]; civilizational reset as a fractal-pattern event.

## SAN Relevance

Stages 6–7 are not science fiction but predictions derivable from SAN theory:
if [[self-aware-networks]] are fractal across scales — neural, social, planetary,
cosmic — then the "metaverse" is not a technology to be built but a recognition
that physical spacetime already *is* an information-processing substrate
(see [[napot-overview|NAPOT]], [[xmark-theory]]).

The escalating immersion of stages 1–5 functions as a cultural ramp toward that
recognition, mirroring the brain's own hierarchical compression from raw sensation
to abstract [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model (see [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]]).
