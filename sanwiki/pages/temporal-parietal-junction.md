# Temporal Parietal Junction

**Temporal Parietal Junction** (TPJ) is the [[self-aware-networks]] reading of the cortical region at the junction of the temporal and parietal lobes treated as a candidate [[consciousness]] module that links [[attention]] and conscious experience by representing the simultaneous activity of multiple lower-level sensory streams. Within SAN it is positioned as a meso-scale aggregator that lights up when [[attention]] selects a subset of the underlying micro-level activity, and is enumerated alongside the [[prefrontal-cortex|prefrontal cortex]], primary sensory cortices, [[thalamus-modular-agi-architecture|thalamus]], hypothalamus, [[hippocampus]]-and-[[entorhinal-cortex]] loop, [[default-mode-network|Default Mode Network]], and [[rich-club-network-consciousness|Rich Clubs]] inside SAN's "human brain" enumeration.

## Position

The SAN position has three components. First, the TPJ is a candidate [[consciousness]] module that mediates between [[attention]] and conscious experience. The `b0146yGPT` note states the position directly: "The temporal parietal junction (TPJ) potentially serves as a [[consciousness]] module, linking [[attention]] and [[consciousness]], and facilitating complex" cognitive integration ([`b0146yGPT--md`, line 13, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0146yGPT.md)). The framing puts the TPJ in the same family as [[attention-schema-theory]] and [[attention-schema-holographic]] reads of [[attention]] but with a specific anatomical commitment: the SAN reading insists the TPJ is doing the integration work that other theories distribute across the [[default-mode-network]] or the [[rich-club-network-consciousness|rich-club]] backbone.

Second, the TPJ activates as a representation of simultaneous micro-level activity rather than as a unique-source generator. The `b0131y` note makes the meso-versus-micro distinction explicit: "on a mesoscale, the idea that the Temporal Parietal Junction would activate as a representation of the fact that you have simultaneous activity at the micro level in the touch" and adjacent sensory streams ([`b0131y--md`, line 72, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0131y.md)). Read this way, [[multimodal-integration]] and [[oscillatory-synchrony-multimodal-integration]] are the operational mechanisms the TPJ exercises, with [[distributed-primary-sensory-architecture]] supplying the underlying micro-level streams the TPJ aggregates. The TPJ is not the source of conscious experience but the mesoscale population whose activity represents that the relevant micro-level coincidences have happened.

Third, the TPJ is anatomically positioned to be the [[attention]]-and-incoming-streams convergence locus, which is why it lights up under [[attention]]. The `b0112y` note articulates the why: "I suspect that is a second that could be a second reason for why the temporal parietal junction lights up when we're paying [[attention]] because I guess logically when we're paying" [[attention]] the streams converge there ([`b0112y--md`, line 123, [on GitHub](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0112y.md)). The `b0145ywhisper` note's "right at the center of your incoming" framing supports the same position. This makes the TPJ the SAN-side anatomical anchor for the [[multimodal-integration]] story, distinct from but complementary to the [[default-mode-network]] (which carries the resting-state [[gh-a0206z|self]]-related activity) and the [[rich-club-network-consciousness]] (which carries the integration-via-high-degree-hubs story).

## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature most directly adjacent to this concept is the TPJ-as-[[attention]]-and-[[gh-a0206z|self]]-other-distinction program associated with Decety, Saxe, Cabeza, and the Corbetta-and-Shulman ventral-[[attention]]-network mapping. The SAN reading converges with that literature on the empirical observation that the TPJ activates under [[attention]] and during [[gh-a0206z|self]]-versus-other discrimination, and converges with [[attention-schema-theory|attention-schema]] readings on the broader claim that [[attention]] has its own representation in the brain. It diverges in two specifics: it treats the TPJ as one node in a longer enumeration that includes the [[default-mode-network]] and [[rich-club-network-consciousness]] rather than as the unique seat of [[gh-a0206z|self]]-other discrimination; and it gives the TPJ a substrate-level role of representing simultaneous micro-level activity rather than computing a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-other contrast as such. Where the SAN framing makes a falsifiable [[prediction]] the standard reading does not, it is that controlled disruption of the TPJ should degrade meso-scale representation of cross-stream simultaneity even when the underlying micro-level activity in primary sensory cortices is preserved, with downstream effects on [[multimodal-integration]] performance that go beyond classical TPJ-lesion findings.
