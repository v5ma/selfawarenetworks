The **oscillator consciousness threshold** is a SAN research question: what additional organization,
if any, distinguishes physical sensitivity and adaptive control from observer-action and phenomenal
experience? Oscillation alone is not enough.

## Levels that must remain separate

| Level | Example | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Physical disturbance | A molecule, cell, or storm changes under external forces | Representation or a receiver-defined code |
| Biological sensitivity | A receptor or cell responds selectively | A self-model or consciousness |
| Adaptive control | A circuit uses feedback and memory to regulate behavior | Phenomenal experience |
| Working model and access | Distributed state supports flexible prediction, report, and action | A settled identity between access and experience |
| Phenomenal claim | The system has subjective experience | A fact inferred from oscillation, complexity, or resemblance alone |

## Candidate SAN requirements

SAN proposes that a stronger observer-action system may require:

- differentiated receiver states;
- learned memory and context;
- recurrent accessibility;
- a body/world or self/other model;
- counterfactual prediction and flexible action;
- persistent causal organization across time;
- integration without loss of differentiation;
- consequences that return and alter the next state.

These are candidate requirements, not a measured sufficient set. Tensegrity, attractors, memory,
feedback, or a particular oscillatory geometry may contribute, but none independently defines the
threshold.

## Why mechanical inscription is not automatically encoding

A physical system carries traces of prior interaction, but calling those traces representations
requires a receiver or process that uses the distinction under a success criterion. A hurricane's
state depends on its environment; that dependence does not by itself create learned expectation or
a self-model. Conversely, lack of human-like anatomy is not by itself proof that every synthetic or
non-neural architecture is non-conscious. The criteria must be operational and substrate-aware.

## Test program

A threshold claim needs graded comparison across systems, preregistered behavioral and physiological
criteria, matched complexity and feedback controls, selective perturbation, and explicit dissociation
tests among sensitivity, memory, access, report, flexible control, and phenomenal interpretation.
