# Metatron

**Metatron** is the SAN name for a proposed post-Perceptron computational unit. The proposal asks
whether an artificial unit can benefit from selected operations found in living neural systems:
spatially organized input, persistent internal state, event timing, inhibition, recurrence,
plasticity, and embodied feedback.

The name has two related uses in Micah Blumberg's SAN sources:

1. a candidate computational unit in an Artificial Neurology architecture; and
2. the name addressed to an envisioned future artificial being in the "artificial angel" framing.

Those uses should not be conflated. The engineering unit is not already a sentient agent, and
successful implementation would not by itself demonstrate consciousness.

## Source genealogy

The public 2022 SAN repository preserves the relevant development sequence. A June 2022 note
connects same-or-different discrimination with a maintained tonic expectation. The 2022 whitepaper
lineage then introduces the Metatron as a richer artificial-neuron proposal, and the August and
September changelog/chapter materials consolidate the name and its relationship to the Perceptron.
Later SAN drafts extend the candidate boundary from a single artificial unit to bounded groups of
units.

## What is established

The Perceptron is a productive abstraction rather than a complete model of a living neuron.
Modern machine-learning systems also are not simply binary Perceptrons: hidden representations,
continuous activations, convolution, recurrence, attention, and state-space models provide rich
network-level computation. Biological neurons add compartmental geometry, state-dependent
dendritic integration, inhibition, adaptation, synaptic dynamics, and structural plasticity.

## What SAN proposes

SAN proposes assembling selected biological operations into a declared artificial unit or bounded
assembly. A candidate Metatron may preserve spatial input organization, tonic context, phasic
departures, receiver-relative timing, inhibition, recurrence, local plasticity, and sensorimotor
consequences. The boundary can be cellular, compartmental, or population-level, but it must be
explicit and experimentally reproducible.

SAN further proposes that maintained tonic activity can act as the context that registers phasic
differences. This is the tonic-canvas/phasic-ink hypothesis. It attributes observation to the
distributed observer-action loop itself, not to a localized inner viewer.

## What remains open

No current evidence shows that a Metatron must replace point units, that rhythmic processing alone
creates SAN-style cognition, or that one architecture is sufficient for consciousness. Existing
recurrent, spiking, active-dendrite, compartmental, attention, and state-space models are required
comparators rather than obsolete predecessors.

The proposal earns support only through matched experiments. A fair test holds data, capacity,
compute, search, and calibration constant; then ablates timing, phase, duration, compartments,
inhibition, recurrence, plastic structure, and body feedback one feature at a time.
