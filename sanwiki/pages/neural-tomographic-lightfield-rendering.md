## The lightfield analogy

In a Light Field Labs display, every pixel contains a 360-degree spherical representation of the full image from that pixel's position. When all pixels fire together, the full volumetric image materializes where their projections overlap. The mind works analogously: each neural node processes a partial view of the sensory scene from its spatial and temporal position in the neural hierarchy. The combined overlapping of all these node-projections creates the volumetric rendering that constitutes conscious experience.

The consequence: **the volume of the head is filled up volumetrically with representations of the volume of space around you** â€” incoming senses, neural pathways, and binding oscillations conspire to produce an interior that closely matches actual exterior spatial reality. This is the *tomographic* in [[napot]] â€” [[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillatory Tomography.

## Telescoping mind

Information telescopes through the cortical hierarchy in two directions:

- **Microlensing** (sensory inputs): individual synapses and dendritic compartments encode high-resolution, novel features at fine scale.
- **Macrolensing** (hierarchical peak): higher layers abstract and scale up patterns into concepts, categories, and representations.
- **Feedback**: the hierarchical peak's macro-representations drive behavior by projecting back down to motor outputs.

Each cortical layer acts like a "photocopier enlarge function" â€” a single neuron fires, inhibits nearby neurons (the inhibitory halo described by Numenta), and the area of inhibition magnifies the pattern across the entire cortical column. The same sparse encoding that at layer 1 represents a single fine-grained feature is, by layer 6, a large-scale abstract concept.

## Phasic tonic contrastive rendering

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] calls tonic oscillations the "ground of being" â€” the background expectation set by past sensory experience. All conscious content is *contrastive*: phasic deviations from the tonic baseline represent what is novel or unexpected.

Within a single power band (e.g., alpha) there are multiple slightly different frequencies. These **contrastive differences within a power band** constitute the actual informational content â€” patterns are rendered by what is *different* from the baseline, not by what is identical to it. This is the mechanism by which the [[gh-a0206z|self]] "perceives reality and is simultaneously rendered by reality."

## Functional fractal at the synaptic level

The excitation/inhibition dynamic is a functional fractal: a sodium receptor inside a synapse is functionally excitatory (like an excitatory neuron), a potassium receptor is functionally inhibitory (like an inhibitory neuron). The same positive/negative duality repeats from receptor to synapse to neuron to cortical column to large-scale cluster.
