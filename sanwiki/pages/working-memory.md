## Post-SAN

Where SAN diverges from mainstream working-memory theory is in *what* it claims the gamma-in-theta [[multiplex]] is doing. The standard slot/Lisman-Jensen picture treats oscillatory coding as an organisational schema for a discrete capacity store; the activity-silent counter-camp (Stokes, Wolff and others) treats sustained activity as inessential and pushes the load into transient synaptic weight changes. The SAN account ([AUTOLNK_MASK_27, line 284](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0325zNAPOT6.md)) reads the two as complementary modes of the same NAPOT machinery — sustained [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]] when an item is actively rendered, residual synaptic-weight imprint when it is held silently — and predicts that switching between them is mediated by [[acetylcholine|cholinergic]] / [[gaba|GABAergic]] state shifts in the mediodorsal [[thalamus]]→mPFC loop. Earl Miller's PFC programme is taken as broadly correct on the executive side ("his work has significantly advanced our understanding of the neural mechanisms underlying executive functions, working memory, and [[attention]] by focusing on the [[prefrontal-cortex|prefrontal cortex]] (PFC) as a central hub", [AUTOLNK_MASK_28, line 360](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/draft6.md)) but extended by SAN's claim that "beta waves, often originating in the PFC, carry predictive and goal-oriented signals" ([AUTOLNK_MASK_29, line 362](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/draft6.md)) function as gating in the same [[criterial-causation]] grammar SAN uses elsewhere. The testable [[prediction]] the NAPOT framing makes that pure-[[attractor]] and pure-activity-silent accounts do not is that disrupting [[cross-frequency-coupling|theta-gamma coupling]] between MD thalamus and mPFC during a multi-item retention interval should selectively collapse held items in *order*-dependent fashion, with the items in later gamma slots failing first — a [[prediction]] directly entailed by the slot-[[multiplex]] + tonic-oscillation chain.
