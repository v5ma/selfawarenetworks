Four-dimensional phase-tracking is [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]]'s model of what consciousness *is*: a dissipative system that tracks and coordinates phase changes across a 4D (3D space + time) grid of interacting oscillators, from the Planck-scale quantum substrate up through neural assemblies to global cortical patterns.

The core claim: a mind does not merely *receive* patterns — it is constituted by the [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organizing vortex of phase change itself. Like a whirlpool in water or a tornado in air, consciousness is a stable, [[gh-a0206z|self]]-sustaining pattern in the phase structure of reality; remove the medium's dynamics and the vortex (the observer) ceases to exist.

The quantum substrate is relevant here: at the Planck scale, spatial positions function like pixels in a volumetric screen, discretely changing phase rather than smoothly displacing. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] proposes that macro-scale phase-change grids in the brain are not merely *analogous* to this quantum structure — they are a nested continuation of it, with each scale feeding upward and downward simultaneously (see [[multi-scale-causality]]).

## Why four dimensions

Consciousness, on this account, cannot exist in a single instant. The tracking of phase *change* requires duration. "In one second of time you would have no consciousness." The 4D character is not metaphorical — the math of what is "quantified and communicating" only closes over four dimensions: position and phase history together constitute the object being tracked.

## Relation to the passive-observer problem

Because the vortex-structure IS the observer, there is no causally inert spectator behind it. [[Passive-observer-fallacy]]: a "passive observer that has no control does not exist." What exists is, by definition, a node participating in causal exchange at every relevant scale. This grounds the rejection of purely bottom-up models (see [[multi-scale-causality]]).

## Neurofeedback as local instance

Voice-hearing-oneself and mirror-[[gh-a0206z|self]]-observation are small-scale instances of the same loop. Vocal output re-enters via auditory input; mirror movement feeds back into proprioceptive [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modeling. These external feedback loops extend phase-tracking beyond the skull boundary, reinforcing the 4D vortex with additional spatial channels.
