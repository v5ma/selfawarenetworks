# Early a0397-a0419 Obscure Argument Map

This map records sixteen fixed-queue SAN sources. It preserves an August 2018 world-body-brain
co-registration proposal, an October 2012 sensory-wave and semantic-token discussion, a 2013
no-inner-witness and situated-copy argument, the compact receptive/inceptive-field formulation,
and a 2022 retrospective description of information flow as distributed arrays. Physics,
spiritual philosophy, legal and medical speculation, other speakers, internal dates, later Git
fixation, and later retrospective text remain separate.

## Main result

1. `a0401z.md`, internally dated August 23, 2018, is the earliest currently governed compact source
   that explicitly joins multimodal medical imaging, deep learning, a sensor-built light-field
   model of the participant's surroundings, semantic segmentation, and cross-modal correlation.
2. `a0411z.md`, internally dated October 15, 2012, joins structured sensory waves, learned
   threshold relations, multiscale features, semantic tokens, cortical organization, plasticity,
   and meaning through prediction and movement. It is a multi-speaker source, not a Micah-only text.
3. `a0404z.md`, internally dated November 1, 2013, states that there is no added inner witness: the
   changing cells and activity are the sensing system. It also argues that a copied brain would
   immediately diverge under a different body, place, and causal history.
4. `a0417z.md` contains two date layers. The category-theory and binding discussion is internally
   dated April 27, 2014. Micah's detailed "series of arrays" observer synthesis appears in a clearly
   marked 2022 retrospective and must not be backdated to 2014.
5. `a0412z.md`, first Git-fixed in June 2022, states the compact NAPOT geometry: receptive fields
   converge many signals toward a cell; an inceptive field describes divergence from a cell toward
   many downstream cells.
6. `a0403z.md`, first Git-fixed in January 2024, is a later composite cortex-and-thalamus map. Its
   durable model is heterogeneous, cell-type- and area-specific recurrent routing, not a universal
   vertical column algorithm.

## A compact world-body-brain proposal in 2018

The `a0401z.md` proposal can be stated as a testable instrument design:

```text
multimodal neural measurements
+ synchronized body and behavioral state
+ sensor-built 3D or light-field model of the surrounding world
+ explicit semantic scene variables
-> registered world-body-brain event record
-> held-out correlation and perturbation tests
```

This closes an important chronology gap between the 2017 Neural Lace multimodal program and the
public 2021 3D segmentation articles. It does not show that the system was built in 2018 or that a
machine-scene label is identical to a neural representation.

## Sensory waves, semantic tokens, and organized plasticity

`a0411z.md` begins from patterned developmental visual activity and asks how a flowing sensory
state could leave learned geometric relations. Its strongest sequence is:

```text
structured sensory activity
-> thresholded and recurrent feature relations
-> relations repeated across scale and modality
-> learned concept or semantic-token state
-> prediction, communication, or movement
```

The same discussion makes a lasting Artificial Neurology point: a learning system need not choose
between rigid hand-coded modules and an undifferentiated blank learner. Developmental routing,
cell types, embodiment, and plasticity can jointly constrain learning. The named participants
disagree about models, embodiment, general cortical algorithms, and artificial intuition; the Wiki
does not turn that conversation into one authorial voice.

Ackman, Burbridge, and Crair directly observed spontaneous retinal activity propagating through the
developing mouse visual system before eye opening. That result supports patterned developmental
activity. It does not establish adult qualia, semantic tokens, or the complete Neural Rendering
mechanism.

## No hidden viewer and no stationary copy

`a0404z.md` treats the observer as the changing physical organization rather than a witness added
behind neural activity. Its copy argument is also relational: a duplicate placed in another body,
location, sensory stream, and causal history begins another trajectory immediately.

The strongest modern form separates three claims:

1. self-related processing is physically implemented and can be altered by brain state;
2. a continuing person depends on embodied memory, regulation, action, and causal history; and
3. SAN hypothesizes that the recurrent field-cell process performing those operations is the
   observer.

Split-brain research can show partly separable processing and self-recognition capacities across
disconnected hemispheres. It does not by itself prove that there is no self, that each hemisphere
is a complete person, or that mind uploading is impossible.

`a0417z.md` adds the positive mechanism in its 2022 retrospective:

```text
many arrays register partial structure
-> local and larger-scale activity changes other arrays
-> body representation, memory, and action join the changing state
-> the organized aggregate performs observation
```

## Receptive and inceptive fields

`a0412z.md` gives the shortest recovered statement of the receive-transform-project geometry:

```text
many upstream effects -> receptive field -> cellular transformation
cellular output -> inceptive field -> many downstream receptive fields
```

The value is operational. A receptive field names conditions that alter a receiver. An inceptive
field should name a target set, time window, intervention, and measured downstream consequence.
This bounded definition is independent of the source's later gravity, telepathy, and universal-
field speculation.

## Cortex and thalamus as heterogeneous loop chains

The later `a0403z.md` synthesis is useful after its broad anatomical prose is translated into a
cell-type-specific graph. Intratelencephalic, pyramidal-tract, and corticothalamic neurons are
glutamatergic projection classes. GABAergic interneurons are distinct local circuit classes.
Thalamocortical, corticothalamic, corticocortical, basal-ganglia, cerebellar, brainstem, and spinal
routes form interlinked loops whose connections and effects vary by cortical area, species, cell
type, state, firing rate, and synchrony.

This preserves SAN's important intuition that information flow is recurrent, layered, and tied to
action. It replaces a universal top-to-bottom column story with a falsifiable circuit map.

## Carrier and morphology adequacy

`a0409z.md` and `a0414z.md` ask a useful engineering question before the later APME terminology:

```text
Which cellular state variables and transformations must an artificial replacement preserve
for downstream receivers to behave equivalently?
```

The answer cannot be inferred from carrier material alone. A replacement must be tested for
input-output timing, adaptation, dendritic nonlinearity, waveform and burst structure, synaptic
release statistics, plasticity, local field interaction where relevant, downstream recruitment,
and behavior. The sources motivate the adequacy test; they do not establish photonic or protonic
equivalence.
