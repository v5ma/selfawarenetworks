[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]] uses the term **response-able** (not "responsible") to emphasize the reactive nature of conscious beings: humans are bodies of causation that respond — at macro, meso, micro, and quantum scales — to patterns arriving from all scales including astronomical ones.

This is a corollary of the SAN multi-scale view of the nervous system. [[criterial-causation]] operates at the neuronal level; but the body as a whole is embedded in and reactive to patterns at every scale of physical reality. The brain's role is not to generate behavior from nothing but to mediate and shape reactive responses to environmental patterns.

## SAN implication

If consciousness arises from multi-scale [[coincidence-detection]] — from quantum events through neural circuits through social-scale patterns — then "responsibility" in the moral sense is downstream of reactivity, not upstream of it. An agent is response-able first; the moral framing is secondary.
