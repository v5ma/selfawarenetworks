[[gh-resnote04|Micah Blumberg]]'s New Law of Thermodynamics proposes that the approach to thermodynamic equilibrium is not merely a statistical diffusion process but a sequential, wave-based computational process in which nonequilibrium energy differences resolve through ordered local signal exchanges.

## Core Claim

Entropy increase is reframed as the cumulative outcome of iterative, stepwise computations — each dissipation event is a local signal exchange that propagates and synchronizes neighboring oscillators. Equilibrium is the state at which those exchanges cease to produce net phase differences. This maps directly onto [[oscillating-spacetime-theory]], where field interactions propagate as phase-coherent waves rather than random collisions.

The local update rule for components i and j: `Qi(t+δt) = Qi(t) − α·ΔQij(t)` and `Qj(t+δt) = Qj(t) + α·ΔQij(t)` where `ΔQij = Qi − Qj` and `0 < α ≤ 0.5`. All components converge to `Q_final = mean(Q_initial)`; the variance is a Lyapunov function that decreases monotonically.

The framework grounds equilibrium dynamics in Kuramoto-like coupled-oscillator mathematics. When oscillators synchronize, the system "settles" — not because a statistical maximum is reached but because the computation converges. This yields testable monotonic synchronization trajectories in oscillator arrays, a prediction distinct from Boltzmann's statistical mechanics.

## Consciousness and Gravity Corollaries

The same wave-synchronization mechanism that drives physical equilibrium is proposed to underlie [[oscillator-consciousness-threshold]]: consciousness emerges when distributed cortical oscillators reach a sufficient phase-coherence threshold across cortical networks. See also [[cortical-column-oscillatory-transmission]] for how this signal propagation is implemented neurologically.

The law also extends to [[gravity-as-information]]: dissipation connects to phase dynamics within time-density fields, linking gravity to the same signal-exchange substrate. This is consistent with [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation]], where gravity emerges from temporal phase gradients rather than spatial curvature alone.

Empirical predictions include phase-sensitive calorimetry measurements and neurophysiology correlates that predict behavioral thresholds — cross-domain scaling laws that span physical and neural systems, making this a fractal-pattern claim across scales (see [[multi-scale-causality]]).
