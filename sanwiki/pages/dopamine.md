## Position

SAN's three load-bearing claims about dopamine are: it is a chemical [[attractor]]; its *temporal pattern* of release matters more than its identity; and its main observable effect is to bias [[oscillatory-neural-coupling|oscillatory coupling]] and [[ltd-inhibitory-pattern-sharpening|plasticity]] in ways that select among [[pattern-neurons|pattern neurons]]. The first claim — neurotransmitters as attractors — comes from `a0008z--md`, line 39 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0008z.md)) and is what makes [[dopamine-as-pattern-selector|dopamine pattern-selector]] the right SAN gloss rather than "reward signal." The second claim is anchored in `a0132z--md`, line 14 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0132z.md)): "The chemical properties of dopamine, serotonin, acetyl-choline, and gaba must have temporal effects determining the likely oscillation value for the group oscillation". The third — the plasticity / ripple gate — is stated cleanly in `06san--md`, line 38 and line 46 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/06san.md)): "Dopamine modulates plasticity indirectly influencing ripple generation and interacts with cholinergic waves in striatum"; "Dopamine influences plasticity and indirectly reduces ripple generation, particularly during active encoding states".

These three claims compose. If dopamine is an [[attractor]] whose temporal release pattern modulates [[tonic-phasic-neural-oscillation|tonic–phasic oscillation]] dynamics, then the [[hippocampal-encoding-retrieval-separation|encoding/retrieval]] schedule of the hippocampus — and its [[spear-hippocampal-phase-offset|sharp-wave ripple]] timing — is exactly the place dopamine's gating work would show up. The SAN reading subsumes the "reward prediction error" account without naming it as primary: the phasic burst that conventional accounts label as RPE is, in SAN, the temporal signature by which the dopamine [[attractor]] selects which [[multi-perspective-pattern-convergence|cross-array convergence]] gets written into [[holographic-brain-distributed-memory|distributed memory]]. The tonic / phasic split is acknowledged on its own terms in `a0053z--md`, line 40 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0053z.md)): "tonic dopamine levels provide a general motivating function while phasic DA permits the corticostriatal plasticity necessary for habit learning". The receptor-level mechanism (D1/D5 receptors and Src kinases for synaptic-strength scaling in stratum oriens) is captured in `a0007z--md`, line 257 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0007z.md)).

The position has consequences. It commits SAN to denying that dopamine is a privileged "reward currency" and to predicting that any neuromodulator with the same temporal-[[attractor]] profile would produce qualitatively similar pattern-selection effects — a falsifiable claim the published literature has tested only in piecemeal ways. It also predicts that the [[adderall-dopamine-dynamics|pharmacological perturbation of dopamine]] should not just blunt motivation but should reshape the *timing* of pattern selection, and therefore the [[attention-awareness-consciousness-triad|attention–awareness–consciousness triad]] in characteristic ways visible to [[neuralink-ethics-panel-b0303y|neural readout]] tools.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream neuroscience and machine-learning literature converge with parts of the SAN frame and diverge from others. The TD-error account of phasic dopamine, anchored by Schultz and colleagues, gives the published baseline that SAN explicitly quotes (`a0115z--md`, lines 20–22). The fly mushroom-body work cited in `a0011z--md`, line 155 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0011z.md)) — "odors alone induced value- and dopamine-dependent changes in the activity of mushroom body output neurons" — supports SAN's claim that dopamine's effect is to confer value on already-active patterns rather than to broadcast reward. Where SAN parts company is in framing: published literature treats dopamine as a *signal in a representation*, while SAN treats it as an *[[attractor]] whose temporal profile selects among representations*. The testable difference is that SAN predicts the *same* temporal-profile manipulation in a *different* neuromodulator should yield the same pattern-selection effect at the array level, which the reward-currency account does not predict. The white-paper-stage `b0324y--md`, line 2753 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/b0324y.md)) — "with your model, would you be able to predict what precise concentration of dopamine without obviously looking at the experiment" — is [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] putting that exact predictive-equivalence challenge to the SAN model.
