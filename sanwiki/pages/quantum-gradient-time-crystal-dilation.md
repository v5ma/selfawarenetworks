**Quantum Gradient Time Crystal Dilation ([[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]])** is [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s hypothesis that gravity and spacetime curvature arise from a statistical gradient in particle travel paths created by oscillating mass. Rather than curved geometry as a primitive, [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] proposes that dense oscillators warp the local phase field, creating more directional options for nearby particles toward the mass — producing gravitational attraction as a probabilistic outcome.

## Core claim

Every oscillating particle (electron, atom, planet, star) creates a **time crystal** at its center of mass: a region of compressed temporal interval where frequency is highest. Around this center, time intervals expand and spatial apparent volume grows. This gradient of temporal density defines spacetime curvature locally and statistically.

- **Center of mass**: highest oscillatory frequency → hidden surface area folded into time dimension → elevated odds of inward particle travel (gravity).
- **Field exterior**: lower frequency, larger apparent space → faster local clock rate (time dilation away from mass).
- **Particle traffic lanes**: the frequency gradient creates vertical collision lanes radiating from mass, balanced by horizontal lanes, whose geometry determines gravitational deflection.

All matter is fractal oscillations at nested scales. An important implication stated in the source: *"as an oscillator I am literally creating the spacetime environment around the mass that is myself."* This connects [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] to SAN's concept that the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is a [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves|distributed summary of criterial waves]] — in [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] terms, the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is the local phase-field generated by the brain's oscillatory mass.

Memory as spacetime: *"if my memories are [[attractor]] states in synaptic connections, then I am, through my memories, literally creating myself, growing my mind, and building via new protein synthesis my simulation of reality."*

## Particle-Collision Mechanism (raynote03)

A later [[gh-formulation|formulation]] ([[gh-raynote03|raynote03]]) refines the micro-mechanism: *particle collisions and near-field interactions* between neighboring atoms create localized time dilations. These microscopic dilations accumulate — more interactions → denser local time → stronger effective gravitational pull. The [[gh-a0206z|self]]-reinforcing loop (denser time → more particles drawn in → more interactions → denser time) explains planetary formation from dust clouds without invoking mass as primitive.

Cosmological implications: galaxy rotation curves and the Hubble tension may reflect regions of space with anomalous particle-interaction density, producing effective "dark" gravitational fields without visible mass.
