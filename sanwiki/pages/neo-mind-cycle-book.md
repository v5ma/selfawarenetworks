**[[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]]** is [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s book-length synthesis of neurofeedback theory,
oscillatory neuroscience, and brain-computer interfaces, organized as 14 chapters
across three thematic sections. It is a precursor text to the formal
[[self-aware-networks]] framework.

## Structure

- **Section 1 (3 cycles)**: [[brain-computer-interface|Brain-computer interface]] theory — recursive loops between
  user, computer, and neural activity.
- **Section 2 (8 cycles)**: Core neuroscience-to-physics scaling — [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations]],
  [[memory-consolidation]], [[sensory-processing]], and [[neural-drift]].
- **Section 3 (3 cycles)**: Higher-level neural systems and integration.

## Core Theoretical Contributions

### Oscillation Binding Principle
"Oscillations that oscillate at the same frequency merge; those at different frequencies
separate." This frequency-based binding bridges [[hebbian-learning]] to a universal
physics principle — a foundation for why [[neural-oscillation]]s can form coherent
perceptual objects from distributed activity.

### Traveling Wave Phase Arrays
The brain represents information through [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations]] that
intersect with [[phase-wave-differential]] arrays, yielding temporary and virtual (not
fixed) locations for memories and perceptions. This anticipates the [[napot]] formalism.

### Neural Drift
Memories and percepts are not localized — the same information is represented across
multiple brain areas with different emphases, analogous to holographic encoding where
texture, color, and motion aspects co-exist without a single storage address.

### Engrams as Oscillatory Units
[[Engrams]] are oscillatory patterns representing specific objects, not static synaptic
weight matrices. Memory = reactivation of a characteristic oscillatory signature.

## SAN Lineage

These four concepts — binding by frequency, phase-array representation, neural drift,
and oscillatory engrams — form the empirical scaffolding later formalized in
[[napot]] and [[fractal-consciousness]].
