Review scope and chronology

Source/editorial certification applies to the exact selected wording and bounded comparisons. It is not human scientific peer review, whole-author-program completion, universal priority, proof of influence, or a legal conclusion. Original source dates and qualifications remain on every card.

The Git commit dates this public backup, not the origin of the underlying ideas. Working evidence, private source archives, source PDFs, chat exports and operational review files are excluded. The larger local shared-concept map and timeline-tree work are not part of this selection.

Browser-layout acceptance and integration into selfawarenetworks.com remain separate tasks. Miller’s unchanged source edition retains its historical approval; a current global checker reports shared working-interface hash drift outside these copied files. Its release-local files and receipt bindings were independently rechecked without changing their bytes.
