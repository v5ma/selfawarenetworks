**Binary Cosmology** is the claim, introduced by [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]] in the 2017 [[neural-lace-podcast]], that the universe can be interpreted as a fundamentally binary system: discrete particles represent **ones** and continuous waves represent **zeros**. This digital-bits / continuum-emergence interpretation predates equivalent formulations in Causal Fermion Systems (CFS).

## The Core Claim

Reality at its deepest level has a binary character:
- **Particles (1 / ones)**: localized, discrete events — identified in [[super-information-theory]] as topological defects (stable vortices) in the coherence field $\psi$, and as [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence patterns]] in the neural-SAN framework.
- **Waves (0 / zeros)**: continuous, extended propagation — identified with coherent but non-localized states of the informational substrate.

This binary cosmology is not a mere metaphor: in SIT the informational substrate has both a continuous (archimedean, wave-like) component and a discrete (p-adic, particle-like) component simultaneously, as formalized in [[adelic-foundations-super-information-theory]].

## Precedence and Independent Validation

Blumberg's 2017 [[neural-lace|Neural Lace]] Podcast articulated this binary paradigm. CFS (Felix Finster) explicitly treated spacetime as emerging from discrete elementary atoms — bits — in publications post-2017, with Finster's 2015 work still describing spacetime as the support of a measure (not yet explicitly digital). This constitutes **independent validation** of the binary cosmology idea. See [[sit-cfs-conceptual-convergence]] for the full convergence timeline.

## Connection to Consciousness

In the [[self-aware-networks]] theory of mind, the same binary logic applies at the neural level: a **coincidence** is a 1 (a coincidence pattern = a bit), and background oscillatory noise is the 0-substrate. The [[tonic-phasic-canvas]] models the 0/1 contrast at the neural level. This extends the cosmological claim down to the cognitive scale — the same informational binary that structures spacetime also structures thought.

## Discrete-Continuous Synthesis

The binary cosmology is not a claim that reality is *only* digital. It is a **synthesis**: the continuous wave-field (coherence field $\psi$) is the zero-substrate from which discrete particles (topological defects, ones) emerge. This is why the framework is described as "digital bits and continuum emergence" — both are present and interdependent.
