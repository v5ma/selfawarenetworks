# Synaptic Connections

**Synaptic Connections**, in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], are not the connectionist scalar weights of an artificial neural network but the *physical, grown, prunable, and morphologically-shaped substrate* in which a neuron's [[engram|memory]] is stored — the substrate that, together with [[dendritic-morphology|dendritic morphology]], defines what pattern a [[neural-array|neural-array]] cell is tuned to receive, identify, and predict. The SAN reading splits long-term memory into two coupled registers, *synaptic connections* (unconscious, structural) and *active oscillation* (conscious, dynamic), and treats both as kinds of [[attractor]]: `a0122z--md` line 18 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0122z.md)) — "our long term (unconscious) memories as synaptic connections and our active (conscious) memories as the tonic [[brainwave]] oscillation, both types of memories are attractors that drive" cognition.

## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks, a [[neural-array]] cell's *function* is determined by what its synaptic connections allow it to recognize. `a0008z--md` line 54 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0008z.md)) is the cleanest statement: "synaptic connections define what a neuron responds to and what it doesn't, synaptic connections are a cell's learned memory response. The physical pattern it has grown to respond to." `a0008z--md` line 88 then folds this into the SAN signaling story — "Neurons are information programs that transmit a [[phase-change|phase change]] that represents the activation of a memory it learned to respond to through grown synaptic connections or not respond to via pruned synaptic connections." Reading the cell as an "information program" whose syntax is its connections lets [[napot-overview|NAPOT]] compose connections, [[dendritic-encoding|dendritic encoding]], and [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] into a single computation: the connections set what is recognized; the [[dendritic-morphology|dendritic morphology]] says *how strongly* and *with what timing*; the [[phase-change|phase change]] is what gets emitted. `a0126z--md` line 8 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0126z.md)) closes the loop: "[t]he neuron is projecting its [[phase-change|phase change]], that represents the pattern that its network position + synaptic connections represent" — connections are read at the [[synaptic-cleft|cleft]], integrated by [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|coincidence detection]], and re-emitted as a [[phase-change|phase change]].

## Position — connections vs. cell morphology

`a0011z--md` line 150 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0011z.md)) sharpens the picture by *limiting* what synaptic connections alone can do: "long term memories are not just from synaptic connections but essentially they are from the morphology of the cell itself, specifically the morphology of the [[dendrite]]." This is the SAN refinement of the classical synapse-centric view: connections without [[dendritic-morphology]] under-determine the memory, because the same set of connections distributed over a different dendritic tree responds to a different pattern. `a0007z--md` line 515 makes the unit explicit: "a [[dendrite]] & its spines (receptors) can represent a memory that is defined by its synaptic connections and its morphology which determine what patterns the neuron is receptive to and what it ignores."
