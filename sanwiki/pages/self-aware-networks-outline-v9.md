SAOv9 is the version-9 table of contents for [[gh-resnote04|Micah Blumberg]]'s *[[gh-a0206z|Self Aware Networks]]: Theory of Mind* - the primary written form of SAN theory. It organizes the book into three parts and more than twelve cycles, or chapters, each with five ten-page sections.

## Structure

**Part I - [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]]: [[brain-computer-interface|Brain-Computer Interface]] Evolution**

- **C1 Nerve Gear**: [[bci-history|BCI history]] from [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] and [[neural-lace|Neural Lace]] Podcast, through Neuralink-era industry; introduces the three life goals (NerveGear, [[artificial-cortex|Artificial Cortex]], Artificial Brains) and Brain Network Protocols leading up to [[napot-overview|NAPOT]].
- **C2 Holography & Neural Rendering**: the brain as holographic computer; covers [[attention-schema|attention schema]] theory, [[napot-overview|NAPOT]], neural rendering as [[3d-semantic-segmentation|3D semantic segmentation]], and the concept of 3D qualia.
- **C3 [[gh-map-intro-fractal|Fractals]], Gravity & Neurophysics**: fractal morphology in neural anatomy; 1/F amplitude-frequency relationship in EEG; whole-brain physics as dissipative systems (Prigogine); [[gh-map-intro-fractal|fractals]] in neural rendering.
