# Theta Oscillations

**Theta Oscillations** are the 4-8 Hz [brain rhythms](brain-rhythms.md) most prominently expressed in the [hippocampal](hippocampus.md)-entorhinal system, and within [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) they are one of the canonical [oscillatory bands](neural-oscillations.md) over which [phase coding](oscillatory-phase-coding.md), [phase precession](phase-precession.md), and [memory-prediction](memory-prediction-as-intelligence.md) are organized - the rhythm SAN treats as the [tonic oscillation](tonic-oscillation.md) of the [hippocampus](hippocampus.md) and which, in turn, supplies the [reference-frame](reference-frames-cortical-knowledge.md) machinery SAN ports to [cortical columns](cortical-columns.md).

## Post-SAN

Compared with mainstream theta-oscillation [neuroscience](neuroscience.md), SAN takes three positions. First, it accepts the canonical Buzsaki-lineage account - theta as hippocampal "tonic" rhythm supporting [phase precession](phase-precession.md) and [memory-prediction](memory-prediction-as-intelligence.md) - including the textbook 4-8 Hz / spatial-navigation framing (`07san--md`, line 54, paper-[micah](gh-resnote04.md); `GPT2023X--md`, line 41951).
