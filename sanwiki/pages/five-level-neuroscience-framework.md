The SAN five-level framework organizes neuroscience phenomena across nested scales from quantum/molecular to inter-brain social phenomena. Each level is both a distinct domain of study and a substrate for the levels above it, enabling the [[napot|NAPOT]] tomographic rendering to propagate from subcellular phase signals up to conscious experience.

## The five levels

**Level 1 — Subcellular**: proteins, ions, action potentials, DNA, quantum effects, electromagnetism, soliton waves. The physical substrate. Quantum effects remain speculative for consciousness but are not dismissed; soliton waves (propagating waveforms in lipid bilayers) may carry information beyond classical spike models.

**Level 2 — Microscale**: neurons, glia, neural circuits, cellular arrays. Individual cells and their direct connectivity. The level at which [[cellular-oscillating-tomography|COT]] and [[synaptic-computation-ltp|LTP]] operate. Glial cells are included as active computational participants, not mere support.

**Level 3 — Mesoscale**: individual oscillators and oscillator groups — cortical columns, hippocampus, brain nuclei. These structures link Level 2 to Level 4 by propagating rhythms into internal representations and coordinated [[actions]]. This is the level of the [[oscillation-photocopier|oscillatory photocopier]] effect.

**Level 4 — Macroscale**: whole-brain network coordination via thalamic-cortical and cortical-cortical loops. The [[thalamus-modular-agi-architecture|thalamus]] functions as a display for the neocortex, enabling system-wide coordination and spacetime-holistic awareness. Tonic/phasic dynamics operate at this level to bind representations.

**Level 5 — Ecosystem**: personal and interpersonal experience, inter-brain coupling (IBC). EEG studies simultaneously recording multiple brains during social interaction reveal synchronized oscillatory patterns across subjects — the brain is not merely an isolated processor but an interface node in a social neural network.

## Neural oscillation as photocopier

A key mesoscale mechanism: **[[action-potential|action potential]] decay rates establish inhibitory phases** for neuron groups, creating temporal sequences that magnify small upstream patterns for observation by higher layers. The oscillation does not merely time signals — it **amplifies and transmits pattern information upward**, analogous to a photocopier enlarging a small original. This is the mechanism by which Level 1 molecular traces become Level 4 conscious representations.

**Synchronization** is absorption: when phasic signals (rare, information-dense) are absorbed into the [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]] (common background), the weak phasic pattern is magnified into an observable mesoscale event.

## Consciousness as frequency rendering

The framework's claim: consciousness is a **frequency-based rendering** — molecular structures that produce oscillatory frequencies also experience them. The same physical structures are both the renderer and the observer. This sidesteps the homunculus problem by making the rendering process [[gh-a0206z|self]]-referential at each level.

## Sleep model

A three-phase oscillatory cycle maps onto sleep states:
1. **Inhibition phase** (deep sleep) — neural populations suppressed, consolidating LTP changes.
2. **Listening phase** — populations become sensitive, scanning for inputs.
3. **Speaking phase** — populations generate output, propagating patterns upward.

Whole-brain awareness emerges from this rhythm cycling across cortical populations.
