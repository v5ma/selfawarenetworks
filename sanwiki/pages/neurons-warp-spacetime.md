The claim that neurons literally warp spacetime when they fire: a neuron's soma expands
during the [[action-potential|action potential]], increasing local mass density and therefore gravitational force —
analogous to how any accelerating or expanding mass warps spacetime per general relativity.

This connects [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation]] (mass as time crystal) to neural
function: the expansion and contraction of the neuronal membrane during and after the action
potential is proposed to warp the local spacetime fabric in a momentary, directed way along
the axon's length.

The expansion also opens a pathway for [[soliton-mechanical-waves]] — pressure waves that
propagate along the neuron body, through the synaptic cleft, to downstream neurons —
suggesting a mechanical signaling channel parallel to electrochemical transmission.

## Oscillator-Flow Account of Gravity

Rather than positing a graviton as the mediator, [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] argues quantum gravity emerges from
aggregate oscillator flows: particles that oscillate together attract, particles that oscillate
apart repel. A neuron firing is one such oscillator event; at neural scale the gravitational
perturbation is tiny but non-zero and theoretically non-ignorable in a
[[cellular-oscillating-tomography]] model.

## Connection to NAPOT

In [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography), [[attention]] is the phasic
magnification of a synaptic pattern. The spacetime-warp framing adds a physical substrate:
each phase burst is simultaneously an electromagnetic wave, a soliton mechanical wave, and a
micro-gravitational event. The tomographic projection of a pattern through neural arrays is
therefore a spacetime event, not merely an information event.

phase field whose internal structure warps information paths into [[oscillatory-phase-field]]
