# NMDA Receptor

An **NMDA receptor (NMDAR)** is a heterogeneous ionotropic glutamate receptor whose current depends on agonist and coagonist occupancy, voltage-dependent magnesium block, subunit composition, localization, membrane voltage, ionic gradients, and modulatory state.

NMDAR signaling is graded and conditional. Receptor activation does not automatically produce a dendritic spike, LTP, LTD, memory, phase routing, or consciousness.
