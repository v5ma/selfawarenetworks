**Neural Circuits** are organized populations of neurons whose connectivity, layer structure, and discrete thresholds determine which inputs cohere into outputs — the canonical mid-level unit of cortical computation between single [[neurons]] and whole [[cortical-column]] populations. Within [[self-aware-networks]] neural circuits are the substrate the [[oscillatory-dynamics]] of [[brainwave]] activity ride on, the unit at which [[criterial-causation]] pattern-detection cashes out, and the connecting tissue between micro-scale [[dendritic-coincidence-detection|dendritic computation]] and macro-scale [[cortico-thalamic-loop|cortico-thalamic functional connectivity]].

## Post-SAN

Mainstream systems neuroscience treats circuits as the canonical level of analysis, with active areas in connectomics, microcircuit tracing, and circuit-perturbation pharmacology. SAN's distinctive move is the *oscillatory-dynamics-as-circuit-computation* identification: where mainstream work treats circuit firing rates as the primary observable and oscillations as a derived phenomenon, SAN treats circuit-level computation as expressed primarily through the [[oscillatory-dynamics]] those circuits produce, with rate-coding as a complementary read-out (`05san--md`, line 54, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/05san.md)). The [[gh-07san|07san]] agentic-cells claim — that [[astrocytes]] and immune cells co-modulate circuit function — is consistent with growing mainstream work on glia-mediated computation but goes further by integrating it into the SAN [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|memory-prediction]] story rather than treating glia as a separate modulatory channel (`07san--md`, line 82, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)).
