This page describes what I do and what I want.

It explains what I do, who I help, and what solution I provide.

It also explains what I am looking for now.

I work with entrepreneurs, business leaders, students, athletes such as pro golfers, adults with ADHD, autistic adults, and adults with PTSD.

They may want to perform better, struggle with stress, or need support for creativity, confidence, and focus.

My solution is to harness research on brain plasticity through a novel use of existing technologies, combining neurofeedback with [brainwave entrainment](brainwave-entrainment.md) and light-and-sound effects attached to brainwave patterns. The goal is to create engaging, stimulating, surreal, altered brain states that stimulate the whole mind and support happiness, creative ideas, and confidence.
