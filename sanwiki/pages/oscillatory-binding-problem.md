The **binding problem** asks: how does the brain unify separate feature representations (color, motion, sound, touch) into a single coherent percept? The early Livingstone–Hubel model proposed anatomically segregated visual streams, implying a "disassembly then reassembly" architecture. More recent work — and SAN's framework — rejects this model in favor of **oscillatory binding**.

## Oscillatory binding mechanism

Information binds through the physics of [[oscillation-frequency-matching|oscillators]]: neurons syncing like fireflies (Strogatz, *Sync*) at the spike level, and large groups of oscillating neural assemblies interacting across frequency bands (Buzsáki, *Rhythms of the Brain*) at the population level.

Binding is not a second reassembly step — it is the oscillation itself. Coincident firing at the receptor level unlocks learned cellular responses. At the dendritic level, spatiotemporal coincidence detection extends this. Pyramidal neurons add a soma-level threshold distinct from [[dendrite]] thresholds, making them the most sensitive sensors — with the greatest flexibility for pattern representation.

## The observer is the oscillation

Where is the "observer" inside the brain who watches its own representations? The observer is not a homunculus but the **flow of information through a series of arrays**, where oscillating activity at multiple scales (spikes → phase changes → brainwave dipoles) binds what each array perceives. The observer IS the binding oscillation — the set of synchronized arrays that constitute a moment of experience. This connects directly to [[napot]].

## Causal sets and hierarchical binding (Pissanetzky)

A complementary formal view: causal sets `(S, w)` with partial order `w` induce block systems on state variables, where the block system itself becomes a smaller causal set. This **recurrence property** — hierarchies of causal sets — explains how [[gh-map-[[gh-c0004x|intro]]-fractal|fractals]], power laws, semantics, and emergence arise from the physics of information without requiring special mechanisms. Oscillatory binding instantiates this pattern at the neural level.
