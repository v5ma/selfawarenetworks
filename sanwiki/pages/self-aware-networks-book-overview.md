**SAOv9** is the version-9 table of contents for [[gh-resnote04|Micah Blumberg]]'s book-length work *[[gh-a0206z|Self Aware Networks]]*, synthesizing brain-computer interfaces, neurophysics, and artificial consciousness across 14 chapters (C1-C14).

## Three-part structure

**Part I — [[neo-mind-cycle|Neo Mind Cycle]]**: traces the evolution from basic BCIs toward integrated neural systems. Covers historical initiatives ([[neural-lace|Neural Lace]] Podcast, Neurotech SF), key cited authors (Eagleman, Buzsáki, Sporns), and industry systems (Neuralink, OpenWater). Introduces novel neural communication protocols beyond traditional spiking models.

**Part II — Neurophysics**: the theoretical core. Proposes that [[conscious-awareness]] emerges from **phase wave variations** rather than spike-rate coding. Key constructs: [[fractal-oscillatory-tree]] patterns in neural architecture, [[oscillating-network-of-oscillators]] rendering reality through distributed representations, and the [[qgtcd-dark-time-theory]] framework linking neural oscillation to quantum-gravitational principles.

**Part III — Artificial Neurology**: applies the theory to machine consciousness. Covers deep learning through transformers, [[3d-semantic-segmentation|3D semantic segmentation]], [[gh-a0206z|self]]-aware robotics with oscillatory physics, and ethics of artificial emotion and morality.

## Central thesis

Consciousness is produced by **oscillating neural arrays** whose phase relationships - not their firing rates - encode and render perceptual reality. [[fractal-pattern|Fractals]] pervade neural organization at every scale, linking synaptic micro-dynamics to large-scale cortical rhythms and ultimately to the [[qgtcd-dark-time-theory]] cosmological framework.
