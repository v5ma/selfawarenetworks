TMS Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation


Another reference that I think supports the idea of a link between brain state criticality research, tonic theta oscillations, and NAPOT Theory comes from the article below. In the article, the researchers use transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) with the theta burst stimulation (TBS) protocol to drive more activity in the tonic theta brainwave frequency. They frame the memory goal as increasing cortical excitability, but my theory is that the theta [tonic oscillation](tonic-oscillation.md) absorbs and dissipates phasic spikes, such as spikes coming from sensory pathways. Synchrony is how the oscillating group acts as a collective knowledge sensor or learner. It is plausible that memory failure might come from factors such as flooded neurotransmitters, oxidative stress, inflammation, antigens, metabolic issues that may stem from diseased mitochondria, lack of oxygen, lack of [ATP](atp.md) from malnutrition, injury, viral attacks, bacterial attacks, cancer, neurodegenerative diseases, and so on.
