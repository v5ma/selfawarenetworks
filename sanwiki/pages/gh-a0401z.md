# A 2018 Proposal for World-Body-Brain Semantic Co-Registration

## Historical source

This compact note proposed synchronizing two incomplete views of the same event:

- multimodal measurements of a participant's brain and body; and
- a sensor-built, semantically labeled 3D model of the world around that participant.

Its phrase "lightfield hologram" is best read as historical shorthand for an external light-field
or volumetric scene representation. It is not a claim that a hologram exists inside the brain.

> What feels exciting to me about all of these new innovations in medical imaging is the
> possibility of multimodal brain imaging with deep learning, and simultaneous correlation with
> the real world around the subject via a light-field hologram of the world built from a variety
> of sensors, with the light field itself being capable of semantic segmentation and correlation
> to medical imaging.

## Recovered operation

The source is proposing an experimental registration pipeline:

```text
external cameras, depth sensors, and body tracking
                    |
                    v
3D scene geometry + object labels + gaze/body pose + task events
                    |
          shared clocks and coordinates
                    |
                    v
EEG, fNIRS, fMRI, MEG, or other modality-specific observations
                    |
                    v
test which world-body changes predict neural, perceptual, and action changes
```

Deep learning is an analysis and reconstruction tool in this proposal, not a biological mechanism.
The important idea is to specify what changed in the world, where the participant looked, how the
body moved, and what happened next before interpreting the measured neural response.

## What is established

- Machine-vision systems can estimate 3D occupancy and semantic labels from bounded sensor data.
- EEG and eye tracking can be recorded simultaneously and related to gaze or object-scene events.
- EEG and fNIRS can be acquired together, although they measure different physiological processes
  with different delays, spatial scales, artifacts, and uncertainties.
- Sensorimotor populations can express mixed and task-dependent reference frames rather than one
  universal coordinate system.

## What remains a SAN hypothesis

SAN proposes that a properly registered world-body-brain experiment could test whether
receiver-relative timing, phase relationships, recurrent transformations, and returned
sensorimotor consequences explain perception and action better than stimulus labels, average
power, firing rate, or anatomy alone.

That proposal does **not** imply:

- a direct pixel-to-neuron correspondence;
- that semantic labels generated by a computer already exist as labels in the brain;
- that a neuroimaging feature is a decoded thought, object, or conscious quality;
- that correlation establishes a causal neural-rendering mechanism; or
- that one medical-imaging modality can be substituted for another.

The participant and distributed nervous system remain the perceiving and acting system. No inner
viewer is introduced.

## Scale and measurement check

| Layer | What may be measured | What must not be inferred automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World | surfaces, depth, object hypotheses, sound, contact, change | ground-truth semantics from an uncertain model |
| Body | gaze, head and limb pose, movement, physiology, action | intention or experience from movement alone |
| Cellular/circuit | invasive spikes, local fields, or perturbations when ethically available | whole-brain content from one neuron or site |
| Network | EEG, MEG, fNIRS, fMRI, distributed propagation | a literal internal image or one conscious location |
| Task/consequence | instruction, report, choice, outcome, returned feedback | mechanism from classification accuracy alone |

## Why this source matters

The note is an early compact statement of a SAN instrument architecture:

```text
world state -> body-relative evidence -> neural measurement
           -> model comparison -> action -> returned consequence
```

The mature methods bridge is [[world-body-brain-semantic-coregistration|World-Body-Brain Co-Registration: From 3D Scene Models to Neural Read/Write]]. That page develops the coordinate,
timing, uncertainty, intervention, and falsification requirements without treating this historical
note as completed medical technology.
