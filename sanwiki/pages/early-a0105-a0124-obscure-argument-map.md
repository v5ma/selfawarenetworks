# Early a0105-a0124 Obscure Argument Map

## What this tranche recovered

Twenty consecutive SAN source notes were read as complete bodies. The highest-value recovery is a
June 2022 proposal for **multimodal neural-state reconstruction and perturbation**: co-register
complementary instruments, align their signals in time, infer a constrained state, perturb that
state, and test whether the model predicts propagation, perception, and action.

The tranche also preserves:

- the Telescoping Mind as successive perspective transformation and abstraction, not a hidden
  viewer or literal light-field display;
- the bat pre-vocal/action/returned-feedback sequence already used in the Agency page;
- a 2012 self-as-cooperative-rivalry of predictive loops formulation;
- a distinction between structural synaptic memory and active oscillatory memory attractors;
- an early "neurons pass their shape" ancestor of PWD/APME that must be separated from its
  incorrect electron and long-range-field claims;
- a 2013 Choice Refinement bridge between recurrent action, consequence, energy demand, and
  plasticity; and
- a historically valuable BCI note that records Micah correcting an exaggerated interpretation
  after inspecting a sensor system directly.

## The joined NAPOT measurement insight

`a0115z.md` is stronger than a list of instruments. Its structure is:

```text
known sensory input and behavior
+ synchronized complementary measurements
+ registered anatomy
-> multiple-source latent-state estimate
+ controlled perturbation
-> predicted network and behavioral consequence
```

The current Wiki now formalizes that proposal as an inverse problem with modality-specific forward
models and an intervention gate. That preserves the 2022 insight while removing the implication
that imaging modes can simply be stacked into a literal synaptic movie.
