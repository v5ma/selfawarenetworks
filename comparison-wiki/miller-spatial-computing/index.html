Buschman & Miller

1 December 2022 — Crossref publication record

Control comes from where in network space a stimulus representation is currently expressed.

Working Memory Is Complex and Dynamic, Like Your Thoughts

Read the target source · PDF page 5 (printed page 21), Top–Down Control by Spatial Computing

Partial operational match: SAN describes thought patterns using both active and inhibited regions. The later documents describe a changing spatial pattern that permits some memory-related activity and suppresses other activity.

What differs: SAN's passage concerns the proposed spatial shape of thoughts. The target describes working-memory control with specified frequency bands and separates coarse control from fine-grained item coding. Those additional claims are not contained in this SAN sentence.

Chronology boundary: the January 2021 target preprint already has the spatial-gating operation. This card establishes the direction of these two document dates, not firstness over that earlier version.

1 December 2022 — Crossref publication record

Alpha/beta inhibits gamma wherever they “collide” in cortex.

Working Memory Is Complex and Dynamic, Like Your Thoughts

Read the target source · PDF page 4 (printed page 20), control by oscillatory dynamics

Partial operational match: SAN links attention to increased activity in selected regions and suppression elsewhere. The later accounts use inhibitory rhythms to regulate access to memory content.

What differs: The shared operation is selective expression through excitation and inhibition. SAN's speculation about whole-brain electrical equilibrium and conscious/unconscious content is not established by the target experiments, and is not the target's stated explanation.
