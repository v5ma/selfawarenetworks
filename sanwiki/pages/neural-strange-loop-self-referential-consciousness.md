The brain is "a movie that is watching itself" — [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s 2014 [[gh-formulation|formulation]] of consciousness as a neural strange loop (Hofstadter). Ten-thousand frames per second, the brain adds incoming frames to its existing library of frames and uses them to plan patterns that serve nutrient/neurotransmitter reward.

## Core claim

Consciousness is not a separate substrate (soul, quantum field) — it is the strange loop that arises when the brain's pattern-prediction machinery turns its own pattern-prediction machinery as one of its inputs. The "soul" is the loop, not a ghost in the machine.

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s framing: "what we think is the soul is just this strange loop (Reference: Hofstadter) of cognitive songs with many layers of tempo-spatial expressions. The brain is more like a piano, the neurons more like piano keys."

This is an early direct ancestor of [[NAPOT]]: oscillatory patterns playing on neural "keys" produce the felt sense of continuous selfhood.

## Anti-quantum consciousness argument

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] explicitly rejects quantum-soul theories at every level:
- **[[observer-effect|Observer effect]]** = measurement interference with quantum events, not evidence for consciousness-primacy over matter.
- **Pineal gland** = an endocrine gland, not a "third eye." No credible peer-reviewed evidence for DMT secretion or consciousness-generating properties.
- **Quantum noise at microtubules** = random noise the synaptic system must overcome, not signal. If a "soul" communicates through quantum fluctuations it is just adding interference.

This position aligns SAN firmly with computational/synaptic theories rather than [[Penrose-Hameroff]] or similar.
