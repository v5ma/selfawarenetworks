## Post-SAN

Mainstream alignment discourse after roughly 2017 organized itself around capability-control, value-learning, and corrigibility frames; the 2011 transcript predates all of that vocabulary and uses a more emotive register ("empathetic and in kind", "mechanical incarnation of vishnu").
