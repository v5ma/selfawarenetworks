**Triune Harmonic Dynamics and the Universal Convergence Point** is a theoretical framework modeling transformation across three fundamental layers of reality. The full paper is archived at Zenodo (record 16813219).

## Overview

The framework proposes a triadic harmonic structure — a "triune" three-fold resonance — as the mechanism governing how transformation propagates across qualitatively distinct scales of reality. The term "Universal Convergence Point" designates the [[attractor]] toward which multi-scale harmonic dynamics converge under certain conditions.

This connects to the broader SAN / [[super-information-theory]] project in which oscillatory dynamics at multiple scales ([[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators]], [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy]]) are posited as the fundamental medium through which information — and therefore physical reality — is organized.

The three-layer structure echoes the [[functional-fractal-isomorphism]] principle: patterns that appear at the neural scale also govern cosmic dynamics, with the same mathematical form at each level.
