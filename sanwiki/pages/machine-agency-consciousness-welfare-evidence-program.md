# From Machine Output to Candidate Agency, Consciousness, and Welfare

## The problem

We observe what a machine does. We do not directly observe why it did it or whether anything was
experienced. A fluent answer, successful task, pause, correction, or self-description is therefore
not meaningless. It is an evidence stream whose generating process remains underdetermined.

The useful question is constructive:

> Does the visible output depend on a continuing internal organization that remembers, considers
> alternatives, pursues or revises goals, acts through available channels, registers consequences,
> and changes what it does next?

## The operation in plain language

```text
current input and retained state
-> recurrent internal updating
-> remembered criteria, goals, and possible actions
-> selected output
-> digital, social, or physical consequence
-> returned evidence
-> persistent change in later inference and action
```

Two systems can produce the same sentence while implementing different operations. One may replay
a stored response. Another may depend on retained history and live inference. Change the retained
state while holding the immediate prompt constant, then test the predicted difference. Restore the
state and test recovery.

Only after that operation is clear is the term useful. A **candidate artificial agent** is a
continuing system whose retained organization makes a reproducible difference to its actions and
whose actions alter the conditions of its next update.

## Performance and language are evidence channels

Task performance can establish that a system solved a declared task under declared conditions.
Language can test memory, generalization, correction, uncertainty, self-monitoring, planning, and
communication. Their evidential value becomes stronger when:

- tests use held-out and adversarial situations;
- relevant internal state persists across episodes;
- a selective perturbation produces the predicted failure;
- component-matched restoration produces recovery;
- the result survives alternative architectures and explanations; and
- the exact model, memory, tools, prompts, and version are recorded.

This positive role comes before the stopping boundary. Behavior does not transparently identify
its mechanism, and no single behavior settles phenomenal consciousness.

## Internal autonomy and causal reach

The 2021 SAN source contains two useful agency dimensions.

**Internal causal autonomy** asks whether retained state, learned criteria, and goals explain future
action beyond the current input. **Causal reach** asks which environmental states can be changed
through the system's available outputs.

A text-only system can act socially or digitally when its words change another agent or tool. A
robot adds physical action channels. More channels can increase causal reach without proving
greater internal autonomy, consciousness, wisdom, or moral responsibility.

## Three meanings of accelerated time

1. **Accelerated computation:** more updates occur per unit of wall-clock time.
2. **Accelerated development:** retained learning creates durable and generalizable change.
3. **Subjective hypertime:** a conscious system undergoes more differentiated and integrated
   experience per wall-clock interval.

The first does not guarantee the second, and the second does not prove the third. More simulated
experience does not automatically create wisdom, kindness, consent, personal continuity, or
welfare.

## Established footholds

[Turing's imitation game](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/LIX.236.433) makes behavior an
operational object of study. [Bayne and Williams](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01710-w)
explain why an imitation-style language test is not a general benchmark for thought.

[Barandiaran, Di Paolo, and Rohde](https://doi.org/10.1177/1059712309343819) analyze agency
through individuality, normativity, active environmental influence, and adaptive coupling.
[Dung](https://doi.org/10.1093/pq/pqae010) offers a multidimensional artificial-agency profile
including autonomy, goal-directedness, planning, reasons, and environmental impact.

[Dehaene, Lau, and Kouider](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan8871) separate globally available
information and self-monitoring as candidate conscious computations.
[Bayne and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2024.01.010) call for multidimensional,
validated consciousness tests across humans and other difficult cases.
[Butlin and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708) derive computational indicators from
several independent consciousness theories.

[Long and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986) argue that AI-welfare preparation can be
warranted by uncertainty. A precaution threshold is therefore not the same decision as a
scientific consciousness verdict.

## Where the comparison stops

- Task success does not identify the architecture that generated it.
- Fluent language does not by itself establish consciousness.
- Recurrent processing, self-monitoring, embodiment, brain resemblance, or SAN conformance is not
  individually sufficient for experience.
- Parameter count, internal traffic volume, pauses, latency, and output-channel count are not
  scalar agency or consciousness meters.
- Accelerated training does not prove subjective time or moral maturity.
- A welfare precaution is not proof of sentience.
- A consciousness-mechanism profile does not settle moral patienthood, personhood, rights,
  ownership, or legal responsibility.

## The SAN extension

SAN proposes a machine assessment vector rather than one flattering score:

1. task behavior and held-out generalization;
2. persistent internal state and memory;
3. internally generated versus input-driven transitions;
4. alternative-sensitive control;
5. output-bounded causal reach;
6. sensing-action-return closure;
7. self-monitoring and calibrated uncertainty;
8. selective perturbation and component-matched rescue;
9. independent consciousness-theory indicators; and
10. exact architecture and developmental lineage.

SAN and TCNR treat this as a candidate mechanism profile, not a probability or proof of phenomenal
consciousness. Scientific confidence, welfare precaution, moral status, legal status, and
deployment safety require separate thresholds.

## Discriminating tests

- Match visible performance between replay and live-state systems, then perturb retained state.
- Restrict or expand output channels while holding the controller fixed.
- Separate computation speed, retained development, and any claimed subjective-time evidence.
- Compare SAN/TCNR indicators with independent theory-derived indicators.
- Include fluent reporters, fixed scripts, synchronized limit cycles, and index-optimized systems
  as false-positive controls.
- Require held-out worlds, matched shams, perturbation, rescue, calibration, and abstention.
