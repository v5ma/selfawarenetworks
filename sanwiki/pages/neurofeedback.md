# Neurofeedback

**Neurofeedback** is, in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], the closed-loop measure-stimulate-measure operation that converts a person's [[brain-rhythms|brainwaves]] into driven light, sound, and isochronic-beat feedback — the "feedback loop, or cycle" that [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]] has been running on himself, his clients, and audiences continuously since at least **2011–2012**, and which gave the [[neomindcycle|Neo Mind Cycle]] its name. It is the practical [[brain-machine-interface|BMI]] technique that anchors the SAN program empirically: every later SAN claim about [[neural-oscillation|neural oscillation]], [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differentials]], and [[neural-array-projection|neural array projection]] traces back to this hands-on, measurement-and-driven-stimulation practice that [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] ran out of a [[fnirs-biofeedback|neurofeedback salon]] in San Francisco circa 2012.
