# BrainGate

**BrainGate** is the early academic-lineage [brain-computer interface](brain-computer-interface.md) — a population-of-neurons decoding system built around what the SAN sources also call the "Black Rock Array" — that the [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks.md) corpus uses as the historical baseline against which later commercial efforts such as [Neuralink](neuralink-ethics-panel-b0303y.md) are measured ([b0309ywhisper](gh-b0309ywhisper.md), line 143; [GPT2023X](gh-gpt2023x.md), line 31681). Across the SAN audio-transcript and conversation pages, BrainGate keeps reappearing as the first credible existence-proof for "populations of neurons being decoded in real time" (b0313ySci, line 398), which is the exact capability the SAN whitepapers want a future [read/write BCI](brain-computer-interface-neural-readwrite.md) to generalise.
