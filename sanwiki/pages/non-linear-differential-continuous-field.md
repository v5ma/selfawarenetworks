## Established background

Neural systems contain continuous variables, including membrane potentials, conductances,
concentrations, extracellular potentials, and population-level measurements. They also produce
discrete events such as action potentials and vesicle-release events. Mathematical neuroscience can
represent these dynamics with differential equations, dynamical systems, and field models.

None of that establishes one universal neural field, a consciousness field, or the specific object
implied by this page title.
