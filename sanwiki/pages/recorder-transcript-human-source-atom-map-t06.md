## Joined constructive result

The tranche supplies a multiscale causal spine:

```text
changing sensory, bodily, or intracellular conditions
-> receptor and dendritic detection
-> state-dependent ionic, biochemical, and branch-level transformation
-> phase-, timing-, waveform-, and receiver-sensitive output
-> recurrent convergence, inhibition, and transformed reexpression
-> currently useful body, world, object, and action relation
-> selected cognitive or motor route
-> action, feedback, learning, and changed later readiness
```

The system that receives, transforms, predicts, acts, and receives the consequences performs the
observer operation. A second inner viewer is not required. This strengthens
[[flow-of-information-in-the-brain]], [[entification-observer-problem]],
[[field-cell-self-bioelectric-information-entity]], and
[[feedback-strange-loops-self-model]].

## The BCI and XR problem came before the mature SAN vocabulary

Ranks 81, 82, 83, and 89 show that SAN's later Neural Rendering language grew from concrete
engineering problems:

1. volumetric media and point clouds required reconstruction from partial views;
2. semantic segmentation required deciding what samples belonged to one object;
3. XR current-view rendering showed that current detail can coexist with a longer-lived latent
   world model;
4. eye, head, and body movement changed the sampled evidence;
5. a useful BCI needed both recording and stimulation;
6. stimulation required individualized targets, bounded perturbation, readback, and
   co-adaptation;
7. a decoded pattern did not automatically preserve meaning across people.

This route explains why later SAN asks how biological populations maintain an action-ready
scene. The engineering history generated the question; it is not presented as proof that a brain
is literally a graphics engine.

See [[neo-mind-cycle-neurofeedback-bci-genealogy]], [[bci-instrument-atlas]],
[[world-body-brain-semantic-coregistration]], [[perceptual-screen-neural-rendering]], and
[[napot-revision-genealogy]].

## Reading, writing, and measuring are different inverse problems

Rank 82 supplies a useful design matrix:

```text
recording specificity
!= stimulation specificity
!= decoder accuracy
!= learned percept
!= phenomenal consciousness
```

Electrical arrays, peripheral interfaces, optical methods, ultrasound, fNIRS, holographic
reconstruction, and sensory substitution measure or perturb different variables at different
scales. A strong system therefore uses complementary measurements and verifies a perturbation by
its downstream neural, bodily, behavioral, and reported consequences.

## Dendrites supply structured reception, not a point-neuron bottleneck

Ranks 86, 88, and 94 recover a consistent operation:

```text
many spatially and temporally structured inputs
-> local nonlinear dendritic events
-> state-dependent somatic output
-> divergence to targets with different receptors and states
-> many receiver-specific consequences
-> a new recurrent population pattern
```

A dendritic branch can act as a computational subunit. A neuron's output compresses a rich
receptive state, but downstream populations can reconstruct richer relations by combining many
outputs. Basal and apical dendrites remain postsynaptic receptive structures; axon terminals
remain presynaptic outputs. Functional roles can alternate across the larger recurrent cycle
without reversing anatomy.

See [[receptive-inceptive-dendritic-projection]],
[[electrochemical-to-phase-graph-transformation]], [[neural-tuning]], and
[[synaptic-unreliability-mvr-2021]].

## What PWD adds after the problem is understood

Ranks 86, 92, 93, 94, and 95 first establish the problem in plain language. Frequency alone does
not identify a neural event's meaning. The result also depends on phase, timing, waveform,
duration, source, target, learned receptive structure, and current receiver state.

A maintained population relation supplies the current comparison context. A consequential event
changes that relation. **Phase Wave Differential (PWD)** names that typed, receiver-relevant
change; it is not a synonym for frequency, phase coding, coherence, or a traveling wave.

Tonic and phasic are relative roles:

- tonic is the maintained context for the selected system and interval;
- phasic is a consequential update relative to that context;
- neither role is permanently assigned to one frequency band.

See [[tonic-phasic-phase-wave-differential]],
[[tonic-context-rare-deviation-tomographic-language]],
[[continuous-dynamics-discrete-neural-information]], and
[[coherence-dispersion-sculpting-hypothesis]].

## Coherence, dispersion, selection, and action

Ranks 84, 87, 90, 92, and 95 connect representation to action:

- compatible timing can increase recruitment of a route;
- inhibition and phase dispersion can preserve alternatives or suppress competitors;
- recurrent maps predict possible movements;
- a selected route changes cognitive or motor readiness;
- feedback updates the map after action or failed prediction.

Coincident neural input can help motor neurons and motor pools cross threshold, but neural
voltage is not muscular fuel. Motor-neuron firing triggers neuromuscular transmission,
muscle-fiber depolarization triggers calcium release, and actin-myosin cycling uses cellular ATP
to produce contraction.

See [[neural-path-selection-interneurons-choice]],
[[agency-valuation-action-feedback]], and [[memory-prediction-rendering-sequence]].

## Representation is relational and cross-modal

Ranks 85, 89, 90, and 93 separate three spaces:

```text
physical tissue location
!= learned representational relation
!= graph or functional distance
```

Two populations can preserve a usable object relation without sharing identical microscopic
storage addresses. Visual, tactile, auditory, gustatory, linguistic, and motor systems can
transform and reexpress related properties while retaining modality-specific anatomy.

Color provides a compact example:

```text
spectral input
-> receptor and opponent transformation
-> learned relational category
-> context-sensitive rendered color
-> report or action
```

Culture and language can influence discrimination, memory, and report while retinal and cortical
physiology continue to constrain what can be detected.

See [[crossmodal-pattern-invariance-reexpression]], [[theory-of-redness]],
[[broccoli-shared-recognition-artificial-perception]], and
[[receptor-to-phase-sensory-quantification]].

## Artificial Neurology and developmental organization

Ranks 84, 87, 89, and 96 support an Artificial Neurology program in which a system develops
stable sensory, body, value, memory, and action relations through repeated closed-loop
experience. Development is more than a benchmark score. It includes changes in what the system
can detect, integrate, predict, select, recover, and learn.

Responsiveness, adaptation, learning, agency, consciousness, and self-awareness remain separate
claims. A machine-consciousness assessment therefore requires convergent perturbational,
recurrent, embodied, developmental, and behavioral evidence rather than one label or test.

See [[artificial-neurology-states-stages-development]],
[[dynamic-conscious-participation-resolution]], and
[[intelligence-attention-awareness-self-capability-ladder]].

## From cells to COT and SET

Rank 96 treats ordinary cells as context-sensitive receive-transform-project systems:

```text
external or internal signal
-> receptor-specific detection
-> intracellular transformation
-> RNA, protein, metabolic, or electrical state change
-> emitted signal and tissue consequence
-> adaptation, selection, and later response
```

This is a source ancestor of Cellular Oscillation Tomography and Super Evolution Theory.
Cellular regulation and adaptation are not automatically phenomenal consciousness or individual
learning. The constructive research program is to model and perturb the multiscale
receptor-to-tissue state transition.
