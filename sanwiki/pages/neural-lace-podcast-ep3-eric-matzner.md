A 2017 Silicon Valley Global News podcast interview between [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]] and Eric Matzner (founder of Nootroo), exploring the biological and energetic requirements of [[neural-lace]] implant technology and adjacent BCI research.

## Core Topics

**Energy and nutrition for [[neural-lace|neural lace]].** Matzner frames neural implant viability around the brain's metabolic demands — which brain regions to target, how mitochondrial function relates to sustained BCI performance, and what nutritional interventions (nootropics) might support implant hosts.

**Injectable and minimally invasive electronics.** The episode surveys syringe-injectable mesh electronics, stentrodes (endovascular neural electrodes), and blood-brain barrier manipulation via ultrasound as candidate [[bci-delivery-mechanisms]].

**Volumetric video as a [[neural-lace|neural lace]] tool.** HypeVR's VideoGrammetry (6DoF volumetric capture) and Richard Marks' PSVR work are cited as perceptual-layer complements to [[neural-lace|neural lace]], bridging physical implant and immersive-content delivery.

**Quantum mechanics and neural computation.** Matzner and Blumberg touch on quantum-level implications for neural signaling, connecting to SAN's broader interest in [[quantum-cognition]] and [[synapse-as-quantum-event]].

## Historical Context

Recorded April 2017, during the initial wave of public BCI discourse following Facebook's F8 announcements (alleged covert [[neural-lace|neural lace]] R&D). Predates the SAN Theory of Mind formalization; serves as an early node in [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s documented BCI-to-SAN intellectual genealogy.
