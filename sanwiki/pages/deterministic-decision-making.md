# Deterministic Decision Making

**Deterministic Decision Making** is, in the [[Self-Aware-Networks|Self-Aware Networks]] (SAN) corpus, the position that what looks from the inside like free [[choice]] is actually the output of a fully physical, fully causal cascade running across [[dendritic-spines|dendritic spines]], [[dendritic-branches|dendritic branches]], [[Cortical-columns|cortical columns]], and the broader [[cortex]] — a cascade whose criteria are themselves the residue of past activity, so that "deciding" is the act of evaluating those criteria against present input.
