# EEG Frequency Bands

**EEG Frequency Bands** are the conventional frequency partitions of the human electroencephalogram — delta (≈0.5–4 Hz), theta (≈4–8 Hz), alpha (≈8–13 Hz), beta (≈13–30 Hz), and gamma (≈30–100+ Hz) — that the [[self-aware-network|Self-Aware Networks]] framework treats not as independent rhythms but as carriers in a [[oscillating-network-of-oscillators|nested oscillator network]] whose phase relationships do the cognitive work. In SAN, naming a "band" is a measurement convenience; the explanatory unit is the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differential]] across bands and the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|Dominant Phase Wave Differential Rhythm]] that arises when those differentials cohere.

## Position

In SAN the EEG bands have four load-bearing roles. First, they partition the *carrier hierarchy*: slow bands (delta and theta) carry the [[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic canvas]] and the [[tonic-oscillation-as-predictive-reference-frame|predictive reference frame]] against which faster bands ride; gamma carries the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] that resolve into specific contents; beta carries the [[beta-wave-cognitive-output|cognitive-output]] step in working memory and motor planning; alpha mediates the [[oscillator-consciousness-threshold|attention/awareness threshold]] in the [[attention-awareness-consciousness-triad|attention–awareness–consciousness triad]]. The bands are *partitions of one phase-coupled system*, not independent generators — a stance enforced by [[oscillatory-neural-coupling|oscillatory neural coupling]] and made explicit in [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators|multiscale fractal neural oscillators]].

Second, the bands are the *substrate for cross-frequency coupling*: theta–gamma nesting (gamma riding on theta phase) and alpha-band gating are the carriers of working memory and selective [[attention]] in the [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|gamma consideration sandwich]] and in [[oscillatory-temporal-memory-window|oscillatory temporal memory windows]]. SAN reframes those couplings as *phase-relational tomography* in the [[napot-theory|NAPOT]] frame: the bands together do tomographic reconstruction of an internal volumetric scene, with each band contributing a different slice rate.

Third, the bands are the *signature of dominant rhythms*: when a [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differential]] crosses the dominance threshold and becomes a [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|DPWDR]], the band signature of that DPWDR — typically a sharp-band burst against the surrounding broadband activity — is what makes it visible in scalp EEG and in intracranial recordings, and is what couples to [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripple]] events in the hippocampal literature. The bands are therefore *how SAN rhythms become measurable* — the experimental anchor of the framework.

Fourth, the bands are the *target of intervention* in [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]], [[brainwave-driven-photic-stimulation|photic stimulation]], [[neuromodulation-brainwave-entrainment|neuromodulation-driven entrainment]], [[eeg-biofeedback-self-reflective-loop|EEG biofeedback]], and [[eeg-xr-neurofeedback-bci|XR neurofeedback BCI]]. The therapeutic pages in the corpus — [[oscillatory-ptsd-therapy|oscillatory PTSD therapy]], [[dreams-as-theta-wave-sequences|theta-wave dream sequences]] — all act on band-level structure as their handle on phenomenology, which is why an accurate concept page for *EEG Frequency Bands* sits upstream of the entire SAN clinical line.

## Post-SAN

The EEG-band literature is enormous and predates SAN, so the "Post-SAN" relation here is mostly *re-reading* rather than novelty. Where SAN agrees with the mainstream: bands are real, they index cognition, cross-frequency coupling is informative. Where SAN reframes: the bands are not generators of cognitive content but *carriers* of phase relationships, and the cognitive content lives in the [[phase-wave-differentials|differentials]] rather than in the band amplitudes; the consequence is that band-power analyses miss the [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|DPWDR]]-class structure that phase-relational analyses would surface. The [[earl-k-miller-corpus-priority-review|Miller-corpus crosswalk]] makes this explicit: rotating theta/alpha/beta waves in prefrontal cortex during working memory match the SAN reading at the band-and-phase level. The closest published external converger is the cortical traveling-wave literature ([[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|cortical traveling waves]], [[cortical-traveling-waves-san-submission|the SAN submission]]). Specific predictions that follow from the SAN reading: (i) DPWDR-onset signatures should ride on [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripples]] rather than on broadband band-power increases; (ii) the [[gamma-wave-consideration-sandwich|gamma sandwich]] should show theta–gamma nesting structure rather than independent band recruitment; (iii) [[brainwave-entrainment|entrainment]] should affect [[neural-rendering-of-qualia|qualia rendering]] selectively at frequencies that match the DPWDR carrier in the modality being entrained.
