# Suprachiasmatic Nucleus

The **suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN)** is the small hypothalamic structure mainstream [[neuroscience]] identifies as the master circadian [[oscillator]] in mammals — a population whose phase can be entrained by light cues delivered through the retinohypothalamic tract. Within [[self-aware-networks]] it is treated as the *largest-scale exemplar* of the same kind of [[oscillator]] that runs at smaller scales in [[cortical-column|cortical columns]], the [[hippocampus]], and other [[brain-rhythms|rhythm]]-generating circuits — i.e. it is in the same equivalence class as a [[micro-columns|microcolumn]], differing in scale and period rather than in kind.
