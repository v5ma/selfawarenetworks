# Entropic Gravity

**Entropic Gravity** in the [[self-aware-networks|SAN]] / [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] context is Erik Verlinde's macroscopic-emergent-gravity proposal, treated by [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] as one of two foils against which his own [[super-dark-time|Super Dark Time (SDT)]] and [[quantum-super-time-position|Super Time Position (STP)]] approaches are positioned. The [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] line of work positions itself between and against these by reformulating gravity as a *quantum wave-phase* phenomenon tied to [[qgtcd-dark-time-theory|dark-time]] structure rather than as a thermodynamic boundary effect.

## Position
