It depicts repeated NAPOT slices and EEG/MEG dipoles as if stable awareness were a sequence of complete cortical rerenders. Stable perception does not require one global frame, one refresh cycle, or a literal scene reconstructed on each oscillation.

**Persistent rendering** is SAN's proposal that a usable percept is actively maintained and revised rather than written once and passively stored. The medically careful claim is that perception over time depends on continuing, distributed neural activity and changing effective connectivity.

## Persistence is not one mechanism

Different kinds of persistence can rely on different operations:

- continuing sensory input and recurrent sensory processing;
- active population activity;
- short-term synaptic or intrinsic state;
- attention and working-memory maintenance;
- prediction and error correction;
- body and eye-movement feedback; and
- activity-silent states that can later be reactivated.

The brain therefore need not redraw a complete picture on every cycle. Some relations can remain stable while others are updated. This is closer to an asynchronously maintained working model than a film projector.

EEG and MEG measure aggregate electrical and magnetic consequences of population activity with different sensitivities. They do not expose separate layers of a render, identify every contributing source, or show that an electromagnetic pattern is itself the experienced scene.

## SAN interpretation

SAN can define persistence as repeated causal availability. Receiver-relative changes survive long enough to influence other populations, memory, sampling, and action, while recurrent evidence revises the state. [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] names a candidate many-to-one-to-many reconstruction cycle; [[nonlinear-differential-continuous-approximation|NDCA]] names a candidate account of continuity across updates.

The render is not watched. The populations that preserve, transform, and act on the distinctions constitute the proposed observer-action process.

## Testable predictions

A persistent-rendering model should declare what content is maintained, where it can be decoded, whether maintenance is active or activity-silent, which routes are necessary, and how a perturbation changes duration-specific perception or action. Phase-aware variables must predict maintenance beyond firing rate, stimulus duration, attention, eye movement, and recurrent connectivity.

The 2025 COGITATE study found sustained content-related responses in occipital and lateral temporal cortex while also challenging key maintenance predictions of both IIT and GNWT. This supports careful, content-specific testing rather than one universal persistence mechanism ([Nature, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1)).
