**Super Information Theory v76** is a December 2025 update to [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]]'s [[super-information-theory]] framework, published to document historical priority and conceptual lineage across 150+ downstream research programs.

## Core claim

SIT (originally published 2017–2022) anticipated functional mechanisms that established physics and neuroscience programs later adopted to bridge gaps between abstract mathematics and empirical reality, particularly during 2024–2025. The update provides timestamped forensic evidence via Wayback Machine, GitHub, and DOIs.

## Key SIT mechanisms foregrounded

- **[[coincidence-detection]]** — the foundational signal-processing primitive in SIT, describing how neural networks register temporal overlap of inputs.
- **[[time-density]]** — a measure of how tightly oscillatory events cluster in time; SIT links this to coherence and information encoding.
- **[[oscillatory-agency]]** — the claim that oscillations are not passive carriers but active computational agents that shape neural representation.

## Convergence areas cited

The v76 document maps SIT mechanisms onto: Causal Fermion Systems, Spatial Computing architectures, and the unification of time density with quantum coherence. Each convergence is timestamped to establish prior publication.

## History

Earlier SIT versions (v1–v75) developed the core oscillatory framework from 2017 onward. V76 introduces a new "Historical Priority & Conceptual Lineage" section and is the first version explicitly framed as a priority dispute rather than a theoretical exposition.
