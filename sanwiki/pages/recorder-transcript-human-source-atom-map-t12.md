## Joined Constructive Result

T12 recovers a 2021 mechanism chain that precedes the mature NAPOT name:

```text
external and body-state change
-> receptor-specific transduction
-> branch-local and cellular transformation
-> receiver-relative timing, waveform, and phase difference
-> recruitment of learned populations
-> recurrent, multimodal re-expression
-> body- and world-centered state model
-> candidate action and expected consequence
-> coordinated output
-> changed sensory return
```

This is not a little viewer watching a representation. The participating network receives,
changes, redistributes, and uses its own activity. What is later called Neural Rendering names
that distributed operation after the operation has been explained. NAPOT later adds a more

## An Object Becomes More Knowable Through Action

One view of an object leaves important questions unanswered. What is behind it? How heavy is it?
What changes when it is turned, squeezed, shaken, or combined with something else? Rank 177 starts
with this ordinary problem. Movement and manipulation reveal additional visual, tactile,
proprioceptive, and causal relations. Attention determines which relations receive more sampling,
so the learned object grows through a history of sensing and action rather than arriving as one
complete static picture.

This operation has an established scientific neighborhood. O'Regan and Noë's
[sensorimotor account](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12239892/) and Noë's
[*Action in Perception*](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262140881/action-in-perception/)
make action-dependent sensorimotor knowledge central to perceptual access. Controlled studies find
that active exploration can improve later recognition of novel three-dimensional objects,
including [multisensory manual
exploration](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38900046/).

The comparison stops before claiming that every percept requires an overt movement at that moment
or that the sensorimotor account already contains SAN's proposed internal neural rendering. SAN's
additional hypothesis is that exploration changes a distributed recurrent model whose learned
sensory, temporal, body, and action relations can later be re-expressed from a partial cue.

The same logic can be applied inward without saying that a person literally creates the organism.
A person can recognize an habitual response, imagine another response, and later discover whether
the alternative became easier to enact. Self-reflection and mental simulation have measurable
neural and behavioral correlates, but they do not guarantee change. The SAN extension treats the
self as a plastic set of organism-relative relations linking memory, body, predicted consequence,
action, and returned sensation.

The source family has a precise custody ladder. The recording event is dated 2021-09-27. A
lower-quality transcript was publicly Git-fixed as `b0183y ctpr.txt` on 2022-06-08 and later
received the stable `b0183y.md` route. The related `a0180z.md` note makes the
action-introspection-self-model relation explicit in 2022. The owner-dated 2012 layer in
`a0003z` preserves earlier predictive, embodied, movement-oriented self language, but its later

## A Shared Signal Still Needs A Prepared Receiver

Rank 177 next asks a useful engineering question: what would happen if a complicated mixture of
activity were made widely available? Availability alone would not make every receiving population
understand everything in it. Each receiver contributes its own learned structure, connectivity,
current excitability, and timing. It changes only when it can detect and transform a relation for
which it is prepared. In this operational sense, observing and responding are two phases of one
physical event.

Receptive fields, feature tuning, recurrent cortical transformation, normalization, and
state-dependent gain establish receiver-specific neural responses. The
[communication-through-coherence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26447583/) framework adds the
bounded proposition that rhythmic timing can make communication among neuronal groups more
effective and selective. Recurrent-circuit experiments further show that the same perturbation can
amplify or suppress activity depending on spatial arrangement, feature preference, and the
receiving circuit.

The comparison stops before one literal global waveform. The source's merged-signal passage is a
thought experiment, not a claim that EEG or LFP is a universal packet delivered identically to
every neuron. Anatomical routes, synaptic dynamics, inhibition, receiver tuning, and current state
remain indispensable.

Only after that operation is clear does the later SAN terminology earn its place. SAN proposes
that repeated receive-transform-project operations, occurring within nested oscillatory and
recurrent contexts, let local populations register and redistribute receiver-relevant
differences. The participating network is the observer because its own next state and action
change; no hidden viewer is introduced.

Connection count is also not personal identity. A bridge between two systems can transmit
information without erasing each system's persistent organization. Translation, reciprocal
access, timing, attention, memory, action, feedback, interruption, consent, and ownership all
matter. The Hogan twins remain a respectful boundary case: published analysis allows that some
contents may be shared while stressing that this does not automatically establish one wholly
unified consciousness.

The Extended Brain branch asks a separate testable question: if an external cortex increased the
number of reportable, discriminable, and later remembered cognitive events available within one
physical interval, would subjective duration change? Research connects experienced duration with
attention, memory, processing, event density, and rate of change. It does not yet show that faster
external computation necessarily lengthens subjective life.

## A Pre-NAPOT Receive-Transform-Project Architecture

Ranks 182, 185, 187, and 190 recover four complementary pieces:

1. a learned dendritic and synaptic configuration makes a cell selectively sensitive to a
   spatial-temporal input relation;
2. the cell transforms that relation under its current ionic, somatic, and network state;
3. its output changes the state of one or more receiving populations;
4. recurrent populations can re-express a larger learned relation that no individual cell
   contains by itself.

The 2021 material uses terms such as array, perspective, interlace, listen, speak, projection,
and differential phase. These are functional ancestors, not retrospective proof that every
detail of the 2022 NAPOT formulation was already explicit.

Spatial expansion does not create free energy. Feature dimensions are not extra physical
dimensions. A tomography claim additionally requires an identifiable transform, measurable
projection geometry, and reconstruction that outperforms matched alternatives.

## Stable Context And Transient Update

Ranks 180 and 184 support a role-relative distinction:

- **tonic context** is activity maintained long enough to constrain interpretation and action;
- **phasic update** is a receiver-relevant departure that changes that maintained state.

Neither role belongs permanently to one named frequency band. A phasic event can itself be
oscillatory, and broadband field-potential activity is not automatically slow or tonic. A slower
coordinating rhythm can spread relations over repeated cycles, while a brief high-frequency
event can rapidly recruit a structurally connected target. Those are different propagation and
coordination questions.

The useful SAN claim is therefore not "slow equals canvas and fast equals ink" as a universal
frequency table. It is that relatively maintained population context and relatively transient
informative differences can be experimentally separated, then tested for distinct contributions
to perception, memory, choice, and action.

See [[tonic-phasic-phase-wave-differential]],
[[coherence-dispersion-sculpting-hypothesis]], and
[[memory-prediction-rendering-sequence]].

## The Network As Its Own Observer

Ranks 177 and 187 make the no-inner-viewer point especially clear. A local change alters a
participating cell or population. Other cells receive its synaptic, axonal, recurrent, and
bounded field consequences. Their altered activity changes what the larger network can
discriminate, predict, and do. The network therefore registers a change within itself through
the same physical activity that constitutes its processing.

The camera, microphone, screen, speaker, and television comparisons describe capture,
transformation, and projection roles. They do not imply a hidden spectator. A cell can contribute
to a learned local feature while the organized population produces the usable scene, body model,
and action.

Connections alone do not merge two people into one permanent person. A reciprocal human-machine
loop may expand available memory, calculation, or control while the persistence, access,
consent, interruption, and ownership conditions determine whether it functions as a tool,
temporary coupled system, or proposed external cortex.

## Smell And Interoception Generalize The Model

Ranks 178 and 186 prevent SAN from becoming a vision-only theory. An odor experience changes
over time:

```text
chemical contact
-> receptor and transduction state
-> intensity and source-relative change
-> learned association and memory
-> affective and body-state consequence
-> approach, avoidance, investigation, or no action
```

The same general problem applies to interoception: the nervous system must transform distributed
signals about the body's changing condition into action-usable relations. This supports a
multisensory rendering program without asserting one universal neural code.

Olfaction does not follow the canonical first thalamic relay used to introduce several other
senses. Personal health observations, service-animal behavior, migraine, blood pressure, and
candidate odor biomarkers remain private or source-gated and are not promoted into medical
claims.

## Multimodal Measurement And Scene Registration

Ranks 179, 183, and 188 recover an engineering genealogy from Neo Mind Cycle into SAN:

```text
measured scene and body
-> external three-dimensional object and motion model
-> time-aligned EEG, MEG, EIT, hemodynamic, voice, and behavior measurements
-> candidate neural-state segmentation
-> predicted action and observed return
-> within-person co-adaptation and perturbation
```

The proposal is to compare external scene structure with changing neural and bodily structure,
not to assume that one modality already decodes conscious content. EEG, MEG, EIT, fNIRS,
ultrasound, imaging, behavior, and stimulation have different resolution, depth, inverse
problems, and safety constraints. They are complementary measurements rather than interchangeable
views of one hidden movie.

Graph-spectral and three-dimensional state maps can organize hypotheses about neural dynamics,
affordances, and movement. They do not remove the MEG or EEG inverse problem, establish an exact
neural object label, or produce a universal semantic decoder.

## From Representation To Action

Ranks 177, 180, and 190 join object learning with action:

- exploration changes which object relations are learned;
- recurrent activity keeps relevant scene and body relations available;
- candidate movements predict a changed view or body state;
- coincident and context-compatible inputs help target neurons and motor pools cross threshold;
- returning sensation updates the same distributed model.

Coincident neural inputs do not provide muscle energy directly. They organize effective neural
drive. Motor neurons then trigger neuromuscular transmission, muscle-fiber calcium release, and
ATP-powered contraction. Vocal behavior additionally requires coordinated respiratory and
laryngeal motor systems.

"Electrical valence" is useful only when defined as receiver-relative effective drive or a
measured population-state change. It is not identical to psychological valence, field-potential
power, spike amplitude, or metabolic energy.

## Dynamic Representation And Model Adequacy

Ranks 181 and 189 distinguish three questions that should not be collapsed:

1. Does a nervous system construct and use an internal model?
2. Can physical reality be described computationally?
3. Is our world an engineered ancestor simulation?

Evidence for the first does not prove the second or third. SAN's neural-rendering mechanism is a
claim about how a biological system transforms and uses information. Simulation arguments are a
separate philosophy and physics program with their own explanatory and falsifiability burdens.

The Beakbane discussion contributes a useful model-adequacy rule: representation should be
treated dynamically, through what relations a system preserves and uses, rather than as a static
symbol detached from its physical process. A scientific account remains revisable and should be
judged by explanatory compression, prediction, intervention, and comparison with alternatives.

Multi-speaker attribution remains unresolved for both discussions. Only explicitly identified
Micah intervals and generalized conceptual questions are routed here.
