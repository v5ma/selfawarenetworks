# a0016z

These notes mention [Neo Mind Cycle](neo-mind-cycle.md) and appear to come from the 2011-2015 era of that work.

The full [Neo Mind Cycle program](neomindcycle-program.md) included nutrition and supplements, not only brain stimulation and neurofeedback. The approach resembled a broad "try a little of everything" wellness program, including ideas associated with Jacob Teitelbaum's book _From Fatigued to Fantastic_.

In this note, "Neo Mind" refers to the neocortex: the newer cortical tissue that distinguishes many human cognitive capacities. The neurofeedback method described here treats feedback as a loop or cycle, so the phrase [Neo Mind Cycle](neo-mind-cycle.md) frames brain optimization as one part of a broader healthy-lifestyle practice.

## Related Research

Sanders et al. published "Self-modulation of motor cortex activity after stroke: a randomized controlled trial" in _Brain_ in 2022. The article reports a randomized sham-controlled study of real-time fMRI neurofeedback for chronic stroke survivors and is relevant background for the note's interest in motor-cortex self-modulation and neurofeedback. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awac239
