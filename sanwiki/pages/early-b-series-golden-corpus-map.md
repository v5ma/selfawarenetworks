## Source-layer rule

An original recording or transcript outranks a later AI summary when reconstructing what was said. A transcript establishes development and provenance, not biological correctness. Recorder URLs do not establish event dates without Recorder metadata, a local export, an associated timecode file, or another preserved source.

The missing `b0013y` and `b0019y` identities remain explicit recovery tasks. The graph does not manufacture files or silently compress the sequence.
