The SAN reading differs: Kuhn/Carr place mental space in a quantum-psi register, while [self-aware-networks](self-aware-networks.md) treats it as the wave-differential output of classical (though oscillatory) [brain-rhythms](brain-rhythms.md) interacting through [chemical-synapse](chemical-synapse.md) and [dendritic-compartmentalization](dendritic-compartmentalization.md) machinery — the [hard-problem](hard-problem-of-consciousness.md) frame is intentionally avoided. *Second*, the `ChatGPT2025June17` note records a Neukart-style entanglement reading the SAN account explicitly does not endorse: *"…just as entangled qubits create a spacetime geometry in his view, perhaps entangled neurons create a mental space.
