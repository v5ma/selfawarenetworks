# Cellular Oscillating Tomography

**Cellular Oscillating Tomography (COT)** asks which receive, integrate, retain, and transmit operations attributed to neurons in SAN also occur in other living cells. It is a bridge from Self-Aware Networks to the later Super Evolution Theory (SET) program. It is not a claim that every cell is a miniature brain, constructs a literal medical image, or is individually conscious.

The source-faithful question is:

> Can receptor configuration, cellular history, internal state, signaling dynamics, and intercellular feedback jointly preserve relations among partial inputs well enough to change a cell's later output, and can that proposal predict behavior beyond an ordinary pathway model?

## What established cell biology supports

Living cells do not respond to one input in isolation from their present condition. Receptors, ion concentrations, metabolites, organelles, gene expression, cytoskeletal state, and previous signaling can change how the same perturbation is interpreted.

| Established result | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| In human cells, signaling nodes captured partly nonredundant information about internal cellular state and supported context-dependent growth-factor decisions. | Multimodal, state-dependent cellular information processing. | Conscious perception, Hebbian memory in every receptor array, or tomographic reconstruction. |
| In human mammary epithelial cells, multiple inputs converged on an EGFR-ERK circuit with feedback, context dependence, integration, and amplification. | A measured example of a stateful signaling circuit combining inputs over time. | One universal cellular code or a complete COT mechanism. |
| Neutrophils relay leukotriene B4 and can form pulsatile, self-limiting swarming waves. | Intercellular amplification, collective coordination, and dynamically bounded relay. | Neural representation, selfhood, or learning by itself. |
| Developmental cell types can change functional roles across stages. | Cellular function depends on developmental history and current circuit context. | A universal learned program or a graph-neural-network implementation. |

Primary anchors:

- [Kramer et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35857483/)
- [Joslin et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20458382/)
- [Afonso et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22542839/)
- [Lammermann et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23708969/)
- [Strickland et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38971157/)
- [Cossart and Garel, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-022-00585-6)

These results make cellular integration and collective dynamics experimentally tractable. They do not prove the SAN interpretation.

## The bounded COT operator

The 2022 sources propose an ordered operator rather than the vague claim that cells are intelligent:

    spatially and temporally structured signals
    -> receptor-specific detection
    -> state-dependent thresholds and intracellular dynamics
    -> integration, amplification, suppression, or delayed response
    -> metabolic, transcriptomic, cytoskeletal, secretory, or behavioral output
    -> changed cellular and collective state
    -> altered response to the next input

Established biology supplies many components of this chain. COT's stronger hypothesis is that some cells preserve and reconstruct useful relations among partial inputs through their changing physical organization, and that an explicit COT model can predict held-out intervention responses better than matched signaling-pathway and dynamical-systems models.

## What "oscillating" and "tomography" mean

**Oscillating** refers to declared, measured dynamics such as calcium, cyclic-nucleotide, membrane-potential, ERK, metabolic, transcriptional, or collective signaling changes. It does not require every cell to share one frequency, behave like a neuron, or maintain a sinusoidal clock.

**Tomography** names a reconstruction analogy. Medical tomography infers structure from multiple partial projections. COT asks whether a cell or cell collective can integrate partial signals over time into a latent state that preserves useful relations and changes future output. The claim becomes scientific only when the inputs, state variables, reconstruction target, uncertainty, and competing model are declared.

## Scale ladder

| Scale | Established measurement surface | COT interpretation to test |
|---|---|---|
| Subcellular | Receptor occupancy, ion flux, calcium or cAMP dynamics, ERK activity, metabolites, chromatin accessibility. | Physical state variables may jointly encode a history-sensitive relation among inputs. |
| Single cell | Thresholded decisions, secretion, migration, proliferation, morphology, gene expression. | A cell may reconstruct a task-relevant latent state rather than merely relay one pathway. |
| Cell collective or tissue | Chemical relays, bioelectric coordination, signaling waves, collective migration, developmental patterning. | Distributed cells may combine partial local states into a collective update without a central observer. |
| Organism and evolution | Development, physiology, plasticity, inherited variation, differential survival and reproduction. | Cellular competence may generate constrained phenotypic variation on which selection acts; it does not replace natural selection or prove acquired inheritance. |

This ladder prevents intracellular oscillation, single-cell decision, tissue coordination, and evolutionary change from being treated as one already demonstrated mechanism.

## Intervention-response atlas

COT can be tested without assuming a universal cellular code. Establish a declared cell or tissue baseline; apply chemical, electrical, mechanical, optical, genetic, immune, or sensory perturbations; then measure trajectories across membrane, signaling, transcriptional, morphological, metabolic, and collective variables.

Two perturbations are equivalent only for declared variables, tolerance, context, and time horizon. Matching one endpoint does not make mechanisms identical. Duration, off-target effects, uncertainty, recovery, and inheritance remain separate. See [[multimodal-biosensor-fusion]].

A useful model comparison is nested:

1. pathway identity and dose only;
2. pathway plus present cellular state and dynamical history;
3. a COT reconstruction model that also predicts relational transfer across partial or novel input combinations.

COT gains support only if the third model produces reproducible, held-out predictive improvement and the inferred state survives causal perturbation. It is weakened if ordinary state-aware pathway models explain the same results, if the proposed latent relations do not generalize, or if the alleged oscillatory variable is only a correlated readout.

## COT and NAPOT

[[napot-overview|NAPOT]] concerns coordinated receive-transform-project operations across neural cells and arrays. COT asks which operators generalize to non-neural cells and cell collectives. The relation is a comparison of mechanisms and scales, not a hierarchy in which every cell runs a miniature cortical process.

Neither theory requires a hidden viewer. A cell changes because its receptors and internal machinery transform signals into consequential state and output; a tissue changes through coupled cellular consequences; a neural system changes through distributed neural reception, transformation, projection, and action.

## From COT to SET

[[super-evolution-theory|Super Evolution Theory]] asks how cellular competence participates in the production of biological form while natural selection remains the arbiter of heritable outcomes. The scientifically bounded transition is:

    inherited machinery + environmental and intercellular signals
    -> state-dependent cellular transformation
    -> physiological, developmental, or morphological consequence
    -> possible heritable variation
    -> selection

This is not permission to treat adaptation within one organism as inherited evolutionary change. Each arrow requires its own evidence.
