# Support Expansion Enablement Layer

## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks, perception is not a single feed-forward classifier but an oscillatory, multi-scale process in which recognized fragments of a [[fractal-pattern]] *recruit further fragments* through coherent timing. A [[neural-array]] running [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|neural-array phase tomography]] does not simply fire when a stimulus arrives; it joins a [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|dominant phase-wave differential]] rhythm that already binds related arrays across [[multiscale-fractal-neural-oscillators|multiscale fractal neural oscillators]]. A *support expansion enablement layer* is, by name, the architectural element that gates this recruitment — the seam at which a partially recognized configuration is allowed to *expand its support set* (the bound coalition of arrays, columns, and frequencies sustaining it) by enabling additional matches to count as members of the same coherent percept. In the same conceptual neighborhood as the [[oscillatory-binding-problem|oscillatory binding problem]] and [[cortical-column-oscillatory-collaboration|cortical-column oscillatory collaboration]], it is the component that decides *when* expansion is permitted versus *when* the percept must remain narrow, and it presupposes intact [[inhibitory-interneurons-plasticity-gating|inhibitory interneuron plasticity-gating]] to do so without hallucination. Without primary-source evidence the precise mechanism is not asserted here, but its place in the architecture — between recognition (the [[information-rarity-principle|information-rarity]] match) and consolidation (the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] that actually carries the meaning) — is the location its name picks out.
