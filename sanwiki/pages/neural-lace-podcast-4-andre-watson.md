A May 2017 episode of the [[neural-lace|Neural Lace]] Talks podcast hosted by [[micah-blumberg]], featuring Andre Watson (CEO of Ligandal), exploring [[ionic-magnetic-fields]], synaptic physiology, and the biological substrate of [[consciousness]].

## Core argument

[[micah-blumberg]] proposes that humans are effectively "metal robots": calcium and potassium ions in neurons generate and manipulate magnetic fields that are essential to cognition. This frames [[ionic-magnetic-fields]] not as a side-effect of neural firing but as a computational medium — a substrate for [[self-aware-networks]].

## Key topics

**Ionotropic vs. metabotropic signaling** — the distinction between fast ion-channel gating ([[ionotropic-signaling]]) and slower G-protein-coupled receptor modulation ([[metabotropic-signaling]]) maps onto two timescales of neural computation. Both matter for [[fractal-consciousness]].

**Dendrites as units** — [[dendrites-as-computational-units]] treats each dendritic branch as a miniature processor, an idea that parallels the SAN claim that sub-neuronal scale dynamics contribute to cognition.

**Glial and mitochondrial networks** — Watson highlights glial cells and mitochondria as parallel computational layers alongside the classical axon-synapse network, consistent with [[self-aware-networks]] multi-scale architecture.

**Deep learning as brain model** — the episode situates deep neural networks as partial models of cortical function; the Blue Brain Project's connectivity data is cited as a convergent empirical effort.

**Ligandal and gene therapy** — Watson's company works on personalized genetic nano-medicine targeting synaptic physiology, providing a potential near-term path to precision [[neural-lace]] interventions.

## Connections

Links to [[napot]] (ion channel states as micro-scale NAPOT oscillation substrates), [[quantum-cognition]] (quantum computing analogies in biological ion channels), and [[xmark-theory]] (diffusion tensor imaging as geometric framing of neural computation).

## History

Recorded May 4, 2017. One of the earliest [[neural-lace|Neural Lace]] Talks episodes explicitly connecting SAN ionic-field theory to practical neural-interface engineering.
