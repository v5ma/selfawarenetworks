**Dominant Phase-Wave Differential Rhythm** (DPWDR) is the temporally extended historical form of [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|Dominant Phase-Wave Differential]]. The term should mean a candidate pattern whose measurable influence persists or recurs across a declared interval, not a rhythm that **becomes the screen** of experience.

## What persistence adds

A rhythm claim should specify whether persistence means repeated bursts, phase consistency, sustained power, stable decoding, recurrent reactivation, or continued causal influence on a receiver. These measurements can dissociate.

DPWDR therefore does not supply a consciousness threshold by itself. A page must separately test representation, biological decoding, flexible access, metacognition, action, and phenomenal interpretation.

## Required contract

Every DPWDR claim must name:

1. the reference state;
2. the source population;
3. the physical route;
4. the receiving population;
5. the measured differential;
6. the definition and duration of dominance;
7. the downstream consequence; and
8. the matched alternatives and artifacts.
