# Phase Language Firewall

**Phase Language Firewall** is a SAN bridge page for keeping rhythm vocabulary precise. It prevents the wiki from treating gamma, phase, synchrony, traveling waves, cross-frequency coupling, communication-through-coherence, and SAN-specific phase-wave differentials as if they were one interchangeable claim.

This page should be used whenever a SAN page moves from measured neural rhythms into NAPOT, GCS, neural rendering, or consciousness claims.

## Core Rule

A rhythm term names a measurement, mechanism, or hypothesis only after the page states:

1. the biological scale being discussed;
2. the recorded or modeled signal;
3. the reference window or baseline;
4. the sender, receiver, or local circuit, if a communication claim is made;
5. the task or behavioral endpoint;
6. the alternative explanation being controlled;
7. whether the claim is established neuroscience, SAN interpretation, metaphor, or speculative extension.

Without those details, rhythm language is only descriptive. It should not be treated as proof of NAPOT, GCS, consciousness, or semantic content.

## Vocabulary Boundaries

| Term | Safer meaning | Do not silently equate with |
|---|---|---|
| Oscillation | Repeating or quasi-repeating neural activity at a measured scale | Consciousness, semantic content, or computation by itself |
| Frequency band | A measurement band such as theta, alpha, beta, gamma, or high-gamma | A fixed mental function across all tasks and species |
| Phase | Position within a cycle or event-relative timing variable | Causal direction, content, or synchrony by itself |
| Phase difference | Difference between two phase estimates | A SAN PWD unless the receiver, edge, and consequence are declared |
| Phase gradient | Spatial variation of phase across tissue or a sensor array | A traveling wave or PWD unless propagation and decoding are shown |
| Traveling wave | A propagating phase or activity pattern | Tomography, rendering, or consciousness by itself |
| Synchrony | Stable timing relation among events or populations | Useful communication unless information transfer is shown |
| Coherence | Statistical phase consistency between signals | Causal coupling unless volume conduction/common input are controlled |
| CTC | Communication-through-coherence: phase alignment gates effective communication | NAPOT tomography; CTC gates channels but does not claim reconstruction |
| CFC | Cross-frequency coupling between bands | SAN alpha-gamma channel unless direction and content specificity are tested |
| PAC | Phase-amplitude coupling, often slow phase modulating fast amplitude | A general proof that slower rhythms carry predictions or that gamma carries content |
| Gamma | Fast local/network activity, often 30-150 Hz depending on convention | Consciousness, binding, prediction, or PWD in every context |
| High-gamma | Often broadband activity related partly to local spiking and population activation | Narrowband gamma oscillation without spectral controls |
| PWD | SAN-specific receiver-relative, edge-conditioned phase deviation with downstream consequence | Any phase difference, traveling wave, synchrony, or gamma burst |
| DPWDR | A dominant PWD pattern with persistence, recruitment, decoding advantage, or causal leverage | Any large oscillation or visually salient wave |

## The PWD Test

A page should only call something a **phase-wave differential** when it specifies the differential, not just the wave.

Minimum PWD statement:

> Relative to reference state R, population or cellular system A changes along edge A to B with measured phase/event timing, amplitude or waveform, spatial extent, and receiver-specific consequence C.

That means a PWD needs more than a pretty waveform. It needs a declared comparison and a declared receiver. In SAN terms, the important question is not merely, "Was there a rhythm?" The question is, "What changed relative to what, where did it go, and what did the receiving system do with that change?"

## Established vs SAN Interpretation

**Established or conventional neuroscience can support:**

- neural oscillations across multiple bands;
- task-dependent gamma, beta, alpha, theta, and high-gamma changes;
- phase locking, coherence, CFC, PAC, and traveling waves;
- phase-dependent excitability and timing windows;
- communication-through-coherence as a serious channel-gating framework;
- anatomical delays, connectome topology, and local inhibition shaping timing relations.

**SAN interpretation adds:**

- PWD as a receiver-relative information unit;
- NAPOT as many-to-one-to-many tomographic update over neural arrays;
- DPWDR as a dominant update pattern that can bias the next perceptual or motor state;
- GCS as a provisional phase-structured timing rule for reconciling prediction, sensory evidence, and motor context;
- neural rendering as the working-state reconstruction that downstream systems can use.

**The firewall rule:** evidence for the first list is not automatically evidence for the second list. It may be a biological bridge, a partial match, or a test target, but the SAN-specific claim still needs its own operational test.

## Artifact and Confound Rules

Rhythm claims become fragile when they do not control common alternatives. SAN pages should name the relevant controls rather than treating them as cleanup details.

| Claimed signal | Required alternatives to address |
|---|---|
| Gamma content carrier | firing rate, broadband high-gamma/spiking, evoked power, attention, movement, arousal |
| Harmonic relation such as 20 to 40/60/80 Hz | nonsinusoidal waveform harmonics, line noise, stimulation artifact, filter leakage, aperiodic slope |
| Coherence or synchrony | volume conduction, common input, reference contamination, zero-lag artifact, spatial leakage |
| PAC or CFC | sharp transients, waveform shape, power-power coupling, comodulogram false positives, unequal trial count |
| Traveling wave | sequential activation, sensor mixing, vascular/hemodynamic delay, source localization error |
| Phase-specific causation | energy, sensation, attention, motor preparation, sham and random-phase controls |
| PWD decoding | rate, power, amplitude, raw-history, connectivity, and equal-input recurrent baselines |

## How to Rewrite Loose Claims

| Loose sentence | Better SAN Wiki sentence |
|---|---|
| Gamma carries consciousness. | Gamma may participate in local timing, binding, attention, or prediction depending on circuit and task; SAN separately proposes that certain phase-structured interactions can contribute to neural rendering. |
| Traveling waves are PWDs. | Traveling waves are one candidate substrate for PWDs when their receiver-relative phase geometry and downstream consequence are specified. |
| Coherence proves tomography. | Coherence can gate communication; tomography requires identifiable reconstruction from multiple partial projections. |
| Alpha controls gamma. | A specific slower rhythm may modulate a faster rhythm in a declared preparation; the direction, content, and causal role must be tested. |
| Phase difference encodes content. | A phase variable supports a content claim only if it decodes held-out content beyond rate, power, raw history, and confounds. |
| Synchrony binds the object. | Synchrony is a candidate binding mechanism when it predicts integrated content and disruption changes the reported or acted-upon object. |

## Relationship to NAPOT and GCS

NAPOT can use rhythm evidence, but NAPOT is not identical to rhythm evidence. The NAPOT claim is that partial projections are integrated into an identifiable working state and redistributed through a network. A traveling wave, coherence window, or PAC result can support one part of that story, but it does not establish the whole process.

GCS needs the same discipline. The current medically careful GCS frame is not "gamma equals consideration." It is a proposed timing rule for a declared target, task, and sensory-motor comparison, with the detailed layer/frequency assignment still provisional.

## Wiki Maintenance Use

Use this page as a correction target for older generated pages that overstate rhythm equivalences. The most common fixes are:

- replace permanent band-function assignments with task-specific language;
- replace "X is Y" with "X is a candidate substrate for Y when...";
- add receiver, scale, and endpoint labels;
- separate CTC/PAC/CFC from NAPOT/PWD/GCS;
- add artifact controls before treating a rhythm as content-bearing;
- state whether a page is preserving historical SAN language or presenting the current bounded version.

## Book 2 Use

Book 2 should introduce this firewall before the chapters that lean heavily on wave and rhythm language. The reader needs to know that SAN is not saying "everything is gamma" or "every wave is consciousness." The sharper story is:

> SAN treats neural rhythms as candidate timing and routing variables. PWD, NAPOT, and GCS are additional hypotheses about how those variables become receiver-relative content, reconstruction, and update control.

That distinction makes the theory harder to dismiss and easier to test.
