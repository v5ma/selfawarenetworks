contributions, speculative physics, health claims, and biological overstatement.

## Main recovery: multimodal closed-loop XR

`a0150z.md`, `a0160z.md`, and `a0161z.md` join three layers that were scattered across the Wiki:

1. a generative model of measured brain dynamics and uncertainty;
2. synchronized EEG, gaze, face, movement, cardiac, electrodermal, temperature, and task signals;
3. responsive light, sound, avatar, or XR feedback that changes the participant and later data.

The governed route is [[multimodal-neuroaffective-xr-closed-loop|Multimodal Neuroaffective State
Estimation and Closed-Loop XR]]. It does not claim direct emotion, intention, diagnosis, truth, or
consciousness readout.
