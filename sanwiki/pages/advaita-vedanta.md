# Advaita Vedanta

**[[advaita-vedanta|Advaita Vedanta]]** is a non-dualistic school of Hindu philosophy holding that the individual [[gh-a0206z|self]] (Atman) and ultimate reality (Brahman) are identical — a position the [[self-aware-networks-theory|Self-Aware Networks theory]] engages critically as both a philosophical touchstone and a foil for its own account of [[conscious-awareness|self-awareness]] ([[gh-gpt2023x|GPT2023X]]--md, line 13912). The traditional Advaita distinction between capital-S "[[gh-a0206z|Self]]" (Atma, God Consciousness) and lowercase "[[gh-a0206z|self]]" (the ego or personality) ([[gh-a0239z|a0239z]]--md, line 67) maps onto questions SAN takes seriously about whether the [[observer-as-aggregate-expectation|observer]] is a unified subject or a distributed statistical structure.
