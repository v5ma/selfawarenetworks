# Spatiotemporal Pattern

**Spatiotemporal Pattern** is the recurring [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] term for any structure that lives jointly in cortical space and brain time — [[functional-connectivity|functional connectivity]] read as one such pattern, [[cortical-traveling-waves|cortical traveling waves]] read as another, and the spike itself read as a "compressed token summarizing the spatiotemporal pattern of coincident inputs integrated by its [[dendrites-as-computational-units|dendrites]]". It is the descriptive primitive [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] uses to bind oscillation, [[engrams|engram]]/[[memories-as-attractors|attractor]] storage, and [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]] reasoning into a single representational frame, and it is the place where SAN's view contacts a long-running mainstream literature on [[oscillatory-binding-problem|binding]], [[cortical-traveling-waves|traveling waves]], and pattern formation.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream usage spans pattern-formation (Jirsa–Kelso, 2000), criterial-causation (Tse et al., 2008+), traveling-wave [[neuroscience]] (Muller, Sejnowski et al., reflected in `documents-root--Cortical_traveling_waves_in_time_and_space__1_`), and [[consciousness]] research ([[integrated-information-theory|IIT]]'s perturbational-complexity-index machinery in `pdf-review-aug-a--A_theoretically_based_index_of_consciousness_independent_of_sensory_processing_and_behavior` lines 162, 168, "describes the spatiotemporal pattern of [significant sources]"). SAN's distinctive moves relative to that landscape are: (1) reading [[functional-connectivity|functional connectivity]] *itself* as a spatiotemporal pattern rather than as a derived statistic of one, per `vecA`/`vecB` line 764/769; (2) using [[beta-wave-cognitive-output|β]]/[[gamma-oscillations|γ]] interplay as the binding modulation of that pattern, where mainstream binding accounts often stop at γ alone; and (3) tying the single-neuron spike to the spatiotemporal pattern of [[dendrites-as-computational-units|dendritic]] inputs as a token-[[gh-b0049y|summary]], a framing that anticipates the language used in the recent neural-firing-motif literature (`pdf-review-aug-a--2023.02.08.527772v1.full` line 676 — "motifs, each with a unique spatiotemporal pattern", and `dl-oct6-2025--s41467-025-58698-2` line 453, "Each motif reflected a unique, ~1 second-long spatiotemporal pattern"). The testable consequence — that SAN's β/γ-binding-pattern reading should predict not only *which* ensembles bind but the *order* in which they unfold across cortical space — diverges from a pure-γ binding model.
