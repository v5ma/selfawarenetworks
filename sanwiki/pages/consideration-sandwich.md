The *consideration sandwich* is a model of gamma waves functioning as mediators between two distinct cortical processes: sensory input (alpha waves from occipital cortex) and cognitive output (beta waves from prefrontal cortex). Gamma sits in the middle, translating between raw perception and deliberate thought.

## Structure

| Layer | Wave | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Bottom | Alpha | Sensory processing, relaxed receptivity |
| Middle | Gamma | [[attention]], integration, tuning mechanism |
| Top | Beta | Active cognition, decision-making |

The three layers interact via anticorrelated spiking — when alpha amplitude rises, gamma adjusts to prevent the prefrontal layer from being overwhelmed, and vice versa. This creates a continuous balance between external sensory reality and internal cognition.

## Proprioceptive integration

Gamma oscillations also integrate proprioceptive feedback — the brain's real-time sense of body position — with motor planning circuits (primary motor cortex, supplementary motor area, basal ganglia). This enables fluid movement adjustments without conscious bottlenecks.

## SAN framing

Within [[self-aware-networks]] theory, the consideration sandwich is an instance of [[oscillatory-coupling]]: the brain harmonizes discontinuous information streams from different cortical regions by synchronizing them through a shared gamma carrier. This is closely related to [[phase-wave-differential]] dynamics and the broader claim that conscious awareness emerges from coordinated oscillatory binding.
