# Temporal Phase Coding

**Temporal Phase Coding** is the [[self-aware-networks]] reading of how information is carried in the brain by the temporal phase of neural events relative to local oscillatory carriers — neurons that fire at consistently distinct phases of a [[theta-oscillations-background-scripts|theta]] or [[gamma-oscillation|gamma]] cycle carry distinct information labels — and is treated by SAN as the temporal substrate on top of which discrete spiking events sit. It is the temporal partner concept to [[discrete-neural-coding]] (the discrete spike-event side), pairs structurally with [[phase-precession]] (the canonical instance) and [[cross-frequency-coupling]] (the multi-band carrier mechanism), and is positioned by SAN against [[rate-coding-critique|rate coding]] as the primary information carrier.

## Position

The SAN position has three components. First, temporal phase relative to a local oscillatory carrier is the principal information label, not spike rate or spike-count averaging. The [[discrete-neural-coding]] page makes the relationship to [[discrete-neural-coding|discrete spike-event coding]] explicit: discrete events sit on top of the temporal-phase substrate. The [[phase-precession]] phenomenon — first articulated for hippocampal [[place-cells|place cells]], where firing phase advances within the theta cycle as the animal moves through the place field — is the canonical empirical demonstration that the same neuron can carry distinct information labels via phase alone. The [[neural-timing-encoding]] article is the broader timing-encoding umbrella the temporal-phase-coding slug specializes; the [[oscillator-environmental-encoding]] article handles the cell-level encoding-via-local-[[oscillator]]-position version of the same principle.

Second, multi-band carriers extend phase coding into a richer label space. The [[cross-frequency-coupling]] mechanism lets the phase of a slow rhythm gate the amplitude or phase of a fast rhythm, so a single moment in time can carry both a slow-phase label and a fast-phase label simultaneously. The [[theta-oscillations-background-scripts]], [[gamma-oscillation]], and [[brain-rhythms-in-cognition]] articles handle the band-specific carriers. The [[phase-wave-differentials]] and [[dominant-phase-wave-differential]] articles handle the SAN-specific phase-wave-differential primitive that the temporal-phase-coding slug supplies the information-content reading for. Read this way, [[neural-noise-to-coherence]] is not noise-becoming-signal but signal whose carrier was misread as rate.

Third, temporal phase coding is the route through which [[hippocampal-encoding-retrieval-separation|encoding-retrieval separation]] works. The [[spear-hippocampal-phase-offset|SPEAR]] phase-offset hypothesis, the [[temporal-context-coding]] umbrella, and the [[oscillation-memory-reinstatement]] retrieval mechanism all depend on the substrate-level claim that phase carries information. The [[ltp-tempo-spatial-rhythmic-memory]] memory specialization, the [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves]] criterial-causation specialization, and the [[beliefs-as-tempo-spatial-sequences]] belief-level analogue inherit the same substrate. This makes temporal phase coding the umbrella claim that ties the [[functional-fractal-isomorphism]] hierarchy together: at every fractal level the information is carried by phase relative to the local oscillation, with [[gamma-oscillation]] carriers at the [[cortical-column|cortical column]] scale, [[theta-oscillations-background-scripts|theta]] carriers at the hippocampal scale, and slower carriers at the [[neomindcycle-program]] global cortical scale.

## Post-SAN

The mainstream literature most directly adjacent to this concept is the phase-coding tradition descended from O'Keefe-and-Recce phase-precession, the Buzsáki-and-collaborators theta-gamma-coupling literature, the Singer-and-collaborators binding-by-synchrony program, and the Kayser-and-collaborators phase-coding-in-sensory-[[cortex]] work. The SAN reading converges with all four traditions on the empirical observation that phase carries information that rate alone does not, and adds the substrate-level commitment that temporal phase coding is the *primary* information carrier, with discrete spike-event coding sitting on top of it and rate coding being a downstream [[gh-b0049y|summary]] statistic that loses the underlying phase label. Where the SAN framing makes a falsifiable [[prediction]] the standard reading does not, it is that controlled phase-scrambling of cortical activity — even when total power and mean rate are preserved — should disrupt task performance in proportion to the loss of phase label, with [[olfactory-pathway-uniqueness|olfactory]] discrimination, hippocampal context discrimination, and cross-modal binding all sensitive to the same scrambling. The [[rate-coding-critique]] page handles the explicit anti-rate-coding argument; this page is the umbrella positive claim that phase is the carrier.
