Inside [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] it is read as the *deeper-region exemplar* of an oscillatory-rendering organ — the part of the brain where slow [[brainwaves|low-frequency]] memory waves cross-couple with [[phase-wave-differentials|fast phasic perception waves]], where [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave ripples]] consolidate experience, and where [[hippocampal-encoding-retrieval-separation|encoding/retrieval phase separation]] gives the [[spear-hippocampal-phase-offset|SPEAR]] mechanism — but *not* the privileged seat of mind, since SAN reads the whole brain as doing the same operation at every scale.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream hippocampal neuroscience has converged on much of what SAN reads it as: traveling waves across [[cortex]] and hippocampus (`06san--md`, line 9), CA3–CA1 SWRs as memory-consolidation signals (`06san--md`, line 11), theta-band phase coding of spatial-and-temporal information (`07san--md`, line 54), and cross-frequency coupling between theta and gamma as the binding mechanism for memory-into-perception (`05san--md`, line 348). Where SAN parts company is in the *generality* claim: mainstream treats hippocampal phase precession and SWR-replay as *hippocampal* specialties; SAN reads them as one instance of a *generic* oscillatory-reference-frame and replay-consolidation operation that should be measurable in any cortical region doing temporal prediction or memory consolidation. The empirical wedge is the `04san--md` line 293 prediction — that *sensory* neurons should also exhibit phase-precession-style firing-phase shifts to encode predicted timing — testable in concurrent extracellular recordings of sensory [[cortex]] during predictive paradigms.
