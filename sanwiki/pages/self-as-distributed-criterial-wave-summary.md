"I am the distributed [[gh-b0049y|summary]] of [[criterial-tempo-spatial-waves|criterial tempo-spatial waves]] in some vector." — [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]], Jan 2014.

This aphorism condenses the SAN theory of personal identity: the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is not a substance or a stored memory structure, but a running [[gh-b0049y|summary]] — an emergent aggregate — of the criteria-matching activity of distributed neural waves. No single neuron, column, or hemisphere is the [[gh-a0206z|self]]; the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is the *pattern of agreements* across many such criterial processes occurring simultaneously.

## What this means

The word **vector** is significant: the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is oriented, directional. It is not simply a cloud of activity but a structured convergence of wave-criteria toward a current moment of evaluation. Each wave carries a history of what it has matched; the [[gh-b0049y|summary]] that emerges is implicitly historical even though it is computed in real time.

**Tempo-spatial** emphasizes that the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is both temporal (the pattern recurs over cycles, is re-written, re-shared) and spatial (it has a distribution across the brain's physical array). Neither alone constitutes the [[gh-a0206z|self]]; the combination — a pattern in space that recurs across time — is what makes it recognizable as "I."

## Connection to other SAN concepts

This view complements [[criterial-causation]]: if neurons continuously reset each other's firing criteria, then the aggregate of those criteria at any moment is the functional description of the mind-state. The "distributed [[gh-b0049y|summary]]" is exactly what NAPOT's [[neural-array-projection|neural array projection]] builds up layer by layer.

In [[self-as-learning-loop]], the [[gh-a0206z|self]] is framed as a feedback process. The wave-[[gh-b0049y|summary]] model adds the spatial distribution and temporal oscillation: the feedback is not a single loop but a distributed resonance across many overlapping wave vectors.
