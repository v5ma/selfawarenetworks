**Periodic Physics** is a speculative cosmological framework by Oded Bar-On, notable in the SAN context because [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]] uses it as a comparison point for his own [[super-dark-time|Dark Time Theory]].

## Core claims of Periodic Physics

- **Variant-rate time**: alongside the familiar invariant-rate time, a *variant-rate time* exists whose flow changes over cosmic epochs, directly controlling the speed of light.
- **Decreasing light speed**: the observed cosmological redshift is caused by light traveling slower now than in the past — not by spatial expansion. The change is directionally periodic over vast timescales.
- **Six-dimensional universe**: an unobservable orthogonal universe runs alongside the observable 3D continuum; gravitational waves propagate there but carry no energy into our universe.
- **Gravitational crystal**: matter and antimatter galaxies distribute themselves in a "gravitational crystal" pattern due to mutual repulsion.
- **No dark energy, no expanding space**: redshift, galaxy clustering, and large-scale structure are explained without invoking dark energy or spatial expansion.

## Comparison to Dark Time Theory

Periodic Physics and [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s [[super-dark-time|Dark Time (SDT) / QGTCD]] both challenge dark energy, but diverge in mechanism:

| | Periodic Physics | Dark Time (SDT) |
|---|---|---|
| Time | globally slows down (time was faster in past) | locally denser near mass, no global trend required |
| Redshift cause | decreasing speed of light | local time-density variation + [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation\|QGTCD]] gradient |
| Hubble tension | not directly addressed | resolved via regional time-density variation |
| Spatial expansion | replaced by light-speed slowdown | not required at all |

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] finds Periodic Physics "very legitimate" as an alternative to ΛCDM but prefers SDT because it does not require time to uniformly slow down — the Hubble tension is better explained by *local* time-density variation than by a *global* temporal trend.

## SAN relevance

Bar-On's variant-rate time is conceptually adjacent to [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation|QGTCD]]: both locate the root of gravitational and cosmological phenomena in the behavior of time itself rather than in spatial geometry. The key SAN implication is that if time is dynamically structured at cosmic scales, then [[neural-oscillations|neural oscillations]] — which exist within that structure — participate in spacetime physics rather than being purely computational.
