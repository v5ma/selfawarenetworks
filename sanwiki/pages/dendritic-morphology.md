# Dendritic Morphology

**Dendritic Morphology** is the [Self-Aware Networks](gh-a0001z.md) (SAN) shorthand for "the structure of a neuron's [dendrites](dendrite.md), including the number and configuration of [spines](dendritic-spines.md)," which together with [synaptic connections](chemical-synapse.md) *constitutes* a memory: "a memory is both the synaptic connections, and the dendritic morphology (an assertion found in the [NAPOT](napot-overview.md) Whitepaper as well as other places)" (a0007z--md, line 113). The same claim appears verbatim across the SAN scrolls - "[NAPOT](napot-overview.md) 1: Addresses how dendritic morphology and synaptic growth define a memory" (NotebookML_6_Collection--md, line 1236) - and is the structural-memory leg of SAN's [dendritic-architecture](dendritic-architecture.md) / [dendrites-as-computational-units](dendrites-as-computational-units.md) / [dendritic-encoding](dendritic-encoding.md) cluster.

![Dendritic Morphology memory surface diagram](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/dendritic-morphology/dendritic-morphology-memory-surface-20260627.png)

This SAN image shows dendritic morphology as a spatial and temporal memory surface: branch geometry, spine/receptor placement, and synaptic timing jointly tune which criteria a neuron recognizes and how that selected structure becomes an active phase/firing readout.

Mechanistically, SAN reads dendritic morphology as a tuning variable for [criteria](criteria.md): "[d]endritic arborization & [branching](dendritic-branches.md) (or dendritic morphology, including between Basal & [Apical Dendrites](apical-dendrites.md)) is one of the ways neurons selectively tune into certain criteria for memo[ry]" (a0007z--md, line 127). The morphology fixes which inputs land on which compartment and which [NMDA](nmda-receptor.md) / [AMPA](chemical-synapse.md) receptors gate them, so the cell's [criterial-causation](criterial-causation.md) / [criterial-threshold](criterial-thresholds.md) decisions are baked into its tree shape. SAN extends this into a [vector-space](vector-embeddings.md) picture: "continuous adaptation effectively embeds each neuron within a high-dimensional vector space where dendritic morphology and [synaptic frequency](synaptic-frequency.md) variation together define the neuron's unique computational signature" (draft1--md, line 260), and "dendritic morphology could be captured in a [vector embedding](vector-embeddings.md) by encoding both spatial and temporal aspects of [synaptic connectivity](synaptic-connectivity-dynamics.md)" (vecB--md, line 4073). [vecC](gh-vecc.md) crystallises the temporal claim: "[d]endritic morphology and Temporal/Spatial Patterns: You've highlighted that dendritic morphology is crucial for tracking temporal and spatia[l]" patterns of inputs ([vecC](gh-vecc.md)--md, line 1585).

The [NAPOT](napot-overview.md) machinery makes morphology a per-synapse modulator of release: "the amount of [neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md) released is determined by the dendritic morphology of the postsynaptic neuron, which in turn is influenced by the presynaptic neuron's [action potential](action-potential.md)" ([NAPOT Concepts](gh-napotconcepts.md)--md, line 150). The biology behind that is published [long-term potentiation](long-term-potentiation.md) / [LTD](long-term-depression.md) / [plasticity](neuroplasticity.md) work - "On [LTP](long-term-potentiation.md) Memory & Dendritic Morphology" (a0011z--md, line 148) - and SAN draws on stress-induced morphology disruption studies ("Chronic social stress also perturbs CA3 dendritic morphology and concomitantly impairs spatial learning and memory, likely in a topographically patterned way") to argue that morphology is the durable substrate of [engrams](engrams.md) ([a0018z](a0018z.md)--md, line 57). This pulls in [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) CA3 [cortical-column](cortical-columns.md) / [cortical-column](cortical-column.md) biology, [memory consolidation](memory-consolidation.md), [Hebbian learning](hebbian-learning.md), and the [NMDA](nmda-receptor.md) / [synaptic vesicle release](synaptic-vesicle-release.md) machinery that physically rebuilds the tree. SAN also notes that "[n]eurons demonstrate very distinct nonlinear activation dynamics" tied to morphology, which is "[t]he importance of Dendritic morphology for memory & [neural firing](neural-spikes.md)" (a0196z--md, line 24) - i.e. morphology is also what sustains the [phasic-firing](phasic-firing.md) / [tonic-oscillation](tonic-oscillation.md) regime that NAPOT and [COT](cot-cellular-oscillating-tomography.md) read as [brainwave](brainwaves.md) tomography across the [cortex](cortex.md) / [PFC](prefrontal-cortex.md) / [motor cortex](motor-cortex.md) / [visual cortex](visual-cortex.md) / [auditory cortex](auditory-cortex.md) / [thalamic](thalamus.md) scaffolding.
