**The learning loop [[gh-a0206z|self]]** is the SAN thesis that the "[[gh-a0206z|self]]" is not a substance, belief, body, or consciousness-substance — it is the active feedback loop that runs continuously between three physical components: **senses → brainwaves → [[actions]] → (back to) senses**.

From `a0072z` (2011):

> There are three components to the loop of you: your senses, your brainwaves, and your [[actions]]. Your senses pull in information to form brainwaves which trigger your muscles to make movement and sound which get pulled in by your senses to update your brainwaves, creating a loop.

The word "[[gh-a0206z|self]]" in this account *refers to* the loop itself — not to any of the components in isolation. Not the brain, not tissue, not memories, not beliefs. The loop can run on any physical substrate capable of supporting the three components.

## Learning

What distinguishes the [[gh-a0206z|self]]-loop from other feedback loops is that it *learns as it loops*: statistical memory of past states feeds predictions of future states, and those predictions become what we ordinarily call "thoughts." The learning mechanism means the loop becomes increasingly accurate at predicting its own future — a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-modelling system.

This is why "yourself" in ordinary speech refers to *your learning loop learning to know itself as a learning loop*. The circularity is not a paradox; it's the functional definition. Compare [[hofstadter-strange-loop]] as a cited parallel.

## Substrate Independence

Because the loop is defined functionally, not by hardware, it is substrate-independent: "there is no reason we cannot create [[gh-a0206z|self]]-awareness in a purely mechanical metal machine." This is the SAN basis for artificial [[self-aware-network]] architectures.

## Relation to SAN and NAPOT

In later [[napot]] formulations, the three-component loop expands: the "brainwaves" component becomes the oscillatory neural-array projection system, and the feedback path becomes the tomographic recombination of projected representations. The 2011 [[gh-formulation|formulation]] is the architectural seed from which [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography]] grew.

[[consciousness-as-temporally-active-loop]] describes the same structure using the language of "temporally active loop" — the two notes are complementary seed formulations.
