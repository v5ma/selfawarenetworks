# Distributed Cognition

**Distributed Cognition** is the [[self-aware-networks]] reading of intelligence as an emergent property of many interacting agents — [[pattern-neurons|pattern neurons]], [[cortical-column-signal-projection|cortical columns]], and entire [[neural-array-projection|neural arrays]] — whose joint activity, rather than any single locus, constitutes cognition. The concept enters the SAN corpus as the explicit bridge between SAN's [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]] account of single-neuron decision and the more system-level theories of cognition that treat [[conscious-awareness|conscious awareness]] as something arising across coupled subsystems rather than within an isolated computational core.

## Position

In SAN's framing, distributed cognition is not a competitor to [[criterial-causation]] but its scaled-up consequence: if each [[pattern-neurons|pattern neuron]] decides locally — "this firing pattern, not that one" — then a network of such decisions, coupled through [[oscillatory-neural-coupling|oscillatory coupling]] and [[neural-ensemble-phase-reset-communication|ensemble phase reset]], performs cognition collectively rather than handing the work to a central executive. The `vecB--md` passage at line 1526 says exactly this: SAN "reframing aligns well with cutting-edge theories of distributed cognition and the notion that intelligence is an emergent property of many interacting agents" (`vecB--md`, line 1526, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecB.md)). A later `vecB--md` quote at line 1577 extends the claim into [[artificial-intelligence|AI]]: this concept "not only reshapes our understanding of distributed cognition in biological brains but also points the way toward developing conscious, adaptive artificial systems" (`vecB--md`, line 1577, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/vecB.md)). The SAN commitment, in other words, is that whatever solves the [[multi-perspective-pattern-convergence|pattern-convergence]] problem in biological [[conscious-neural-networks|conscious neural networks]] will also have to solve it in artificial ones — and that solution is necessarily distributed.

The position has structural consequences. It implies that [[consciousness-as-frames|consciousness-as-frames]] cannot be localized to a single brain region the way classic [[neuroimaging-localism-critique|neuroimaging-localist accounts]] supposed, and it pushes SAN toward its [[fractal-conscious-perception|fractal conscious perception]] commitment in which the same [[pattern-competition-consciousness|pattern-competition]] dynamic recurs at every scale of the [[fractal-neural-scale-hierarchy|fractal neural hierarchy]]. It also commits SAN to taking [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] seriously as the readout side of distributed cognition: if cognition lives in the joint activity of many arrays, then the relevant observable is the [[neural-tomography|tomographic]] composition of their phase-coupled outputs, not the firing of any single locus.
