Neural [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organization is the process by which a [[self-aware-network]] creates coherent patterns from an initially random distribution of sensory energy, without top-down control.

## Core Claim

A neural system that "organizes sensory noise creating coherency from an initially random distribution of energy" exemplifies the bottom-up emergence central to SAN theory. Thought activity begins as random or noisy activation, then [[gh-a0206z|self]]-organizes into structured, coherent states through local network interactions — not via a central controller.

## Oscillator Analogy

Independent oscillators (e.g., metronomes on a shared surface) spontaneously synchronize through physical coupling alone. This is proposed as a structural analogy for how [[napot|neural array phase oscillation tomography]] achieves phase-locked coherence across cortical regions without any master clock.

## Emotion as Frequency

In SAN framing, emotion and pain are axon-nerve frequencies rather than emergent properties layered on top of cognition. They are integral tuning signals that make [[self-aware-network|intelligent networks]] sensitive and fine-grained — features, not liabilities. See [[emotion-as-axon-frequency]].

## Relation to AGI

Because coherence emerges from noise via network dynamics, [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]] argues that [[artificial-general-intelligence|artificial general intelligence]] should mirror this architecture — including emotional/pain frequency signals — rather than stripping them out in pursuit of "pure" logic.
