# Cortex

![Cortex as a layered oscillatory workspace](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/cortex/cortex-layered-oscillatory-workspace-20260627.png)

This SAN mechanism image focuses on the page's core claim: cortex is both layered biological architecture and dynamic phase process, where L4 input, L2/3 traveling-wave coordination, PFC feedback to apical dendrites, and L5 coincidence gates cooperate to settle a rendered sensory/cognitive state.

**Cortex** denotes the cerebral cortex — the layered sheet of grey matter whose intricate workings and dynamic neural processes [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) (SAN) treats as the central staging ground for sensory rendering, [traveling-wave](cortical-traveling-waves.md) coordination, and higher-cognitive integration ([00changelog](gh-00changelog.md)--md, line 63). Its layered [cortical columns](cortical-columns.md), [apical dendrites](apical-dendrites.md), and [inhibitory circuits](inhibitory-circuits.md) recur across the manuscript as both static architecture and real-time substrate for [oscillatory tomography](oscillatory-tomography.md), encompassing "both the static architecture and the dynamic processes of neural activity" that the SAN scrolls return to repeatedly ([00changelog](gh-00changelog.md)--md, line 65). In SAN's reading the cortex is the platform on which [consciousness](consciousness.md), [predictive coding](predictive-coding.md), and [criterial causation](criterial-causation.md) all run.

In SAN's account, conscious sight is "a sequence of renderings, that is mostly in the first four layers of the cortex" ([01san](gh-01san.md)--md, line 16) — a layer-specific claim that ties [cortical rhythms](brain-rhythms.md) to [3D semantic segmentation](3d-semantic-segmentation.md) of the perceived world. [Gamma waves](brainwave.md) are described as carrying sensory information "all the way to the PFC, the prefrontal cortex" ([02san](gh-02san.md)--md, line 13), which then re-injects auditory renderings of "sounds, music, words, and thoughts" back toward the [sensory cortices](auditory-cortex.md) during inner speech and listening ([03san](gh-03san.md)--md, line 5). This bidirectional traffic between [PFC](prefrontal-cortex.md) and primary sensory cortex is the architectural backbone of the [attractor loop](attractor-loop.md) SAN names as the [agentic viewpoint](agentic-viewpoint.md), and it is the layer at which [attention](attention.md), [attention schema](attention-schema.md), and [conscious access](conscious-access.md) become tractable for [brain–machine interfaces](brain-machine-interface.md) like [BrainGate](braingate.md) and [Neuralink](neuralink.md).

The cortex is also where SAN locates the empirical signature of its [NAPOT](napot-overview.md) / [biological oscillating tomography](biological-oscillating-tomography.md) (BOT) hypothesis. "Neural activity often propagates as traveling waves across the cortex and hippocampus," with "rhythmic fluctuations [whose] phases move systematically across space" observed via [EEG](eeg.md), MEG, and ECoG ([06san](gh-06san.md)--md, line 9). "The visualization of these waves in neuroimaging studies, moving across the cortex, matches the idea that neural arrays project and perceive phase changes, contributing to the [entification](entification.md) of an observer within the [brain state](brain-state.md)" ([05san](gh-05san.md)--md, line 56). In [BOT](cellular-oscillatory-tomography.md) those waves function "like the many projections in a CT scan -- each frequency band and each region provides a 'slice' of information that, when combined, synthesize to form a unified 3D+time 'mind model'" ([07san](gh-07san.md)--md, line 39), so cortex-wide [brainwaves](brainwaves.md) become the tomographic basis SAN draws on for [3D+time](3d-semantic-segmentation.md) [fractal consciousness](fractal-consciousness.md). The framework leans on published neurophysiology for support: "visual evoked traveling waves percolate through the cerebral cortex and coordinate neuronal activity across broadly distributed networks mediating visual processing" ([a0001z](gh-a0001z.md)--md, line 23), and "Pathway-, layer- and cell-type-specific thalamic input to mouse barrel cortex" anchors the [thalamic](thalamus.md) gating story ([00linkreviewlist](gh-00linkreviewlist.md)--md, line 37).

Layer-specific microcircuitry pulls in a further constellation of wikilinks. Layer 5 pyramidal neurons in mouse primary visual cortex are profiled for "[coincidence detection](coincidence-detection.md) through bursting" ([a0007z](gh-a0007z.md)--md, line 38), while the upper neocortical layers are likened to "a fractal analogy of the retina of the brain, spreading along the surface" — an image SAN deploys to motivate [dendritic compartmentalization](dendritic-compartmentalization.md) and [fractal consciousness](fractal-consciousness.md) ([a0005z](gh-a0005z.md)--md, line 91). [Thalamic](thalamus.md) gating "play[s] a crucial role in gating the flow of information to the prefrontal cortex," with "[theta-driven synchrony](theta-oscillation.md) noted as important" for conscious perception ([08san](gh-08san.md)--md, line 62). The [13san](gh-13san.md) scroll closes the loop by reading the cortex as a Turing-like tape: "the timing gates that reset the whole cortex act like the machine's clock" so "the brain's nested waves can copy" arbitrary computations ([13san](gh-13san.md)--md, line 56). Together these passages frame the cortex as a [dissipative](dissipative-systems.md), [tensegral](tensegrity.md), wave-clocked substrate whose Neo Cortex — "the New Rind, aka the giant thing that makes human brains distinct" ([a0017z](gh-a0017z.md)--md, line 11) — is the platform on which [predictive-coding](predictive-coding.md), [engrams](engrams.md), [Hebbian learning](hebbian-learning.md), [long-term potentiation](long-term-potentiation.md), [NMDA receptor](nmda-receptor.md) dynamics, [GABA](gaba.md) / [acetylcholine](acetylcholine.md) / [dopamine](dopamine.md) modulation, and the [criterial-causation](criterial-causation.md) machinery of [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) are all enacted. Modulation of cortical state via [neurofeedback](neurofeedback.md), [fNIRS biofeedback](fnirs-biofeedback.md), and [brain–computer interfaces](brain-computer-interfaces.md) is treated by SAN as direct intervention on this [brain-complexity](brain-complexity.md) tomogram, with [Jeff Hawkins](jeff-hawkins.md), [Paul King](paul-king.md), [Michael Levin](michael-levin.md), and [Logan Thrasher Collins](logan-thrasher-collins.md) cited as co-travelers across [bioelectronics](bioelectronics.md), [connectome](connectome.md) mapping, [microtubules](microtubules.md), and [cortical plasticity](brain-plasticity.md) research.
