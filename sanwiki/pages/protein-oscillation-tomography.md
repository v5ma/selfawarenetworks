# Protein Oscillation Tomography

## The idea in plain language

A protein is not always in one fixed state. Chemical modifications, binding partners, enzymes, and
the surrounding cell can move it through different forms. Those forms can respond differently and
activate different cellular processes.

In 2022 SAN named a molecular extension of Cellular Oscillating Tomography: **Protein Oscillation
Tomography**, or POT.

## What is established

Protein modification networks can integrate several signals and select among different molecular
responses. Proteins also participate in feedback cycles, transport, condensation, and turnover.

## What POT adds

POT asks whether a time series of protein states preserves enough structure to reconstruct the
inputs and predict later cellular behavior. That is a stronger and testable claim.

It does not mean that a protein is a tiny conscious neuron. The comparison earns value only when it
improves prediction beyond ordinary biochemical models.
