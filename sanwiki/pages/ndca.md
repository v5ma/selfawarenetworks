# NDCA

![Conceptual SAN plate showing candidate NDCA relations across synaptic, dendritic, population, and cortical scales](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/ndca/ndca-continuous-phase-field-canvas-20260705-v1.png)

This is a conceptual SAN plate, not an anatomical map or a validated mathematical model. It should
be read as a set of proposed relations among scales, not as evidence that one universal phase field
or cortical-column mechanism has been demonstrated.

**NDCA (Non-linear Differential Continuous Approximation)** is SAN's name for the continuity problem:
how nonlinear, time-varying biological signals might preserve usable distinctions long enough to
support prediction and action. The page records the historical SAN formulations of that proposal.

## Scientific and scale boundary

Established neuroscience supports nonlinear synaptic and dendritic integration, recurrent circuit
dynamics, oscillatory and aperiodic population activity, and state-space analyses of neural
trajectories. NDCA is an additional SAN interpretation of how such components may participate in a
continuously updated working model.

- **Micro:** molecular, synaptic, dendritic, and somatic dynamics supply candidate components. They
  do not prove that each cell computes NDCA.
- **Meso:** local circuit trajectories and attractor-like behavior can be measured in defined tasks.
  Calling those trajectories NDCA requires a specified transform and causal readout.
- **Macro:** continuity of perception, body model, memory, and action is a distributed control
  problem. SAN proposes that NDCA helps describe it; no single column or global oscillator contains
  the complete model.

"Canvas of consciousness," "ink of qualia," "attractor cross-section," and "semantic rendering"
are SAN interpretations or metaphors. They are not established anatomical entities. A completed
NDCA model needs explicit variables, an update or differential rule, boundary and initial
conditions, a mapping to measured signals, a cross-scale composition rule, discriminating
alternatives, and falsification criteria. QGTCD remains a separate speculative physics extension.

The historical material below preserves how NDCA was described in the source corpus; quotation or
repetition of a source claim is not scientific endorsement.

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks NDCA does three things. First, it is the *mathematical scale-bridging* primitive: it characterises "how each scale—be it a single synapse, a dendritic branch, or an entire neuronal assembly—experiences distinct, non-linear inputs that manifest as variations" (`draft1--md`, line 167, [[gh-draft1|on github]]). Second, it is the *[[attractor]]-cross-section* model: `08san--md` line 88 reports the GPT-distilled [[gh-formulation|formulation]] that NDCA "involves 'computational renderings in neural arrays computed via the cross sections of unified [[attractor]] states'" (`08san--md`, line 88, [[gh-08san|on github]]) — placing NDCA at the intersection of the [[attractor|attractor]]-dynamics literature and SAN's own [[phase-attractor|phase-attractor]] reading. ## Post-SAN

Because `chronology_markers.external_paper_count` is 6 in this packet, the **Post-SAN** section is included. NDCA is *SAN-original* — it is not an established term in mainstream non-linear dynamical systems modelling — so the Post-SAN comparison is a positioning statement rather than a parallel-publication claim. The `REVIEW_COPY_BMMNOD` paper-external evidence at line 659 frames NDCA's mainstream context: "NDCA refers to the mathematical modeling of complex, continuous systems that exhibit non-linear dynamics: ● Modeling Neural Activity: Neuronal firing pat…" (`core-set--REVIEW_COPY_Bridging_Molecular_Mechanisms_and_Neural_Oscillatory_Dynamics__1_`, line 659, paper-external). The bridging paper at line 648 names the convergence triple: "[[biological-oscillating-tomography|Biological Oscillating Tomography]] (BOT), the Fourier Slice Projection Theorem, and Non-linear Differential Continuous Approximation (NDCA) converge to deepen our understanding of neural information processing" (`dl-oct6-2025--REVIEW_COPY_BMMNOD`, line 648, paper-external, 2026). The reading places NDCA inside the published literature on (a) the [[buzsaki-brain-inside-out|Buzsáki]]-style local-field-potential dynamics, (b) the Fourier-slice-projection geometry for tomographic reconstruction, and (c) the broader nonlinear-dynamical-systems modelling tradition — but with the SAN-specific commitment that the *continuous approximation* in NDCA is doing semantic interpretive work (extracting "meaningful distinctions from 3D sensory representations") rather than serving as a numerical-stability tool. Where the published literature treats nonlinear continuous approximations as an analytical instrument, SAN promotes NDCA into a *theoretical primitive* of [[consciousness]] — the canvas, not just the calculator.
