Life is a **dissipative system** whose fundamental signature is oscillation — to be alive is to oscillate. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] Blumberg]] describes the living body as a fractal of cooperative oscillations that contain [[expert-memory-structures]] operating inside a [[self-aware-network]].

## Core idea

The universe itself is fractal, and life inherits that structure as a nested hierarchy of oscillating sub-systems that cooperate without any single level knowing the global configuration. Each [[cortical-column]] functions as a sensor + transmitter + clock + memory that synchronizes with peer clocks — without knowing its spatial position relative to others.

## The fractal oscillation stack

From quantum substrate up to global awareness, the hierarchy described in the source:

- **Quantum**: [[orch-or]], electricity, ions, neurotransmitters
- **Cellular**: dendrites, neurons
- **Columnar**: cortical layers, [[microcolumns]], [[cortical-columns]]
- **Rhythmic**: [[power-bands]], dipoles
- **Subcortical**: [[suprachiasmatic-nucleus]], [[thalamic-nuclei]], [[thalamic-bridges]], hypothalamus, hippocampus
- **Sensory pathways**: auditory (ear → [[thalamic-nuclei]] → [[auditory-cortex|auditory cortex]]), visual, somatosensory
- **Cross-modal unity**: hand, eye, and ear share structural similarities; all sensory modalities are at least four-dimensional

## SAN implication

Expert memory is not centralized — it is distributed across cooperating oscillators at multiple scales. The globally aware [[self-aware-network]] emerges from synchronization of these local clocks, not from any privileged central node. This is a fractal principle: the same pattern (sensor/clock/memory/transmitter) recurs at every level of the hierarchy.
