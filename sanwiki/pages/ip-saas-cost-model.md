## Position

Inside the [[self-aware-networks]] program, the **IP SaaS Cost Model** is the layer that translates the research outputs — the published [[self-aware-networks-book]], the [[saov9-self-aware-networks-overview]] reference, the patent corpus catalogued at `wiki/san/sources/san-patent-corpus.md`, and the methods underlying [[brain-computer-interface]] and [[brain-machine-interface]] devices — into a recurring-revenue licensing surface. Each SAN-derived deliverable becomes a SaaS line item whose unit cost is set against the engineering load of the underlying technology: a [[clinical-bci-applications|clinical BCI]] tier carries the regulatory and [[bci-ethics]] overhead, a [[digital-native-economy-xr-bci|consumer XR/BCI]] tier carries the [[cognitive-privacy-xr-bci]] and [[cognitive-liberty]] obligations, and a [[self-aware-networks-autonomous-vehicles|robotics/autonomy]] tier carries the certification overhead implied by the [[brain-networking-protocol]]. The cost model also attaches to upstream artefacts: the [[brain-interface-bci-map]] is the catalogue against which licensable hardware lanes are enumerated, [[bci-delivery-mechanisms]] determines per-device cost-of-goods, and [[bci-history]] together with [[bci-industry-landscape]] sets the comparables against which SaaS pricing tiers are benchmarked. Companion concepts such as [[ai-creator-rights-fair-use-platforms]] and [[copyright-argument-jurisdiction-strategy]] are the legal boundary conditions the cost model has to respect when SAN-derived AI components enter creator-economy contexts.
