A Medium article by [[micah-blumberg|Micah Blumberg]] covering the convergence of NFT technology, digital fashion, and immersive platforms (VR/AR/WebXR) to create a native digital economy where creators and players generate sustainable value.

The article traces a three-layer infrastructure strategy: (1) content supply chains using on-chain ownership via ERC-721/1155 tokens; (2) file format interoperability via the DASH format, which solves 3D model portability across game engines; (3) player-creator economies modeled on Japan's billion-dollar mobile gaming markets (e.g., Fate/Grand Order's $4B+ revenue).

Brain-computer interfaces ([[bci-industry-landscape]]) appear as an emerging convergent technology. Combined with [[affective-computing|affective computing]] and biosensors, they are positioned to enable deeper neural correlation with digital environments. The article cites [[fnirs-functional-near-infrared-spectroscopy]] and semantic segmentation via deep neural networks as tools advancing neural research within immersive environments.

LiDAR-RGB fusion and light-field video are presented as enabling accessible volumetric 3D content creation — a technical convergence relevant to [[non-invasive-brain-stimulation]] and [[sensory-stabilized-volumetric-rendering]] research directions in the SAN corpus.

The article references "[[neural-lace|Neural Lace]]" as a conceptual horizon — the point where BCI and digital environments merge into direct neural interfaces with immersive platforms.

## Key platforms and formats named

- Platforms: Decentraland, Cryptovoxels, Webaverse, Hubs, WebXR
- Engines: Unity, Unreal, Godot, Three.js, A-Frame, React Three Fiber
- File formats: DASH, ORBX, FBX; protocols ERC-721, ERC-1155
- Economy model: PODE (Perpetual Ownership Digital Economy)
