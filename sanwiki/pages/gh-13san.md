# 13san: Computable Consciousness, Phase Variability, and Self-Modifying Timing

`13san.md` is a public June 2025 SAN working source about neural timing, phase-wave
differentials, coefficient-of-variation language, recursive state updates, neural rendering,
and whether the proposed mechanism could be implemented in software. It is not a 2022 paper,
not a settled neuroscience review, and not a clean single-speaker transcript.

The useful question preserved by this source is:

> Which measured neural differences, receiver transformations, recurrent updates, and
> cross-scale consequences would a simulation have to reproduce before SAN could claim that it
> implemented the relevant biological computation rather than merely imitating its output?

## The recovered operation

Across the mixed dialogue, the durable SAN proposal is:

```text
ongoing receiver and population state
-> timed input and receiver-relative departure
-> waveform, phase, rate, duration, or transmitted-quantity consequence
-> coincidence, interference, or selective routing at named receivers
-> changed local and population state
-> plastic or state-dependent change in later responses
-> recurrent contribution to perception, memory, choice, or action
-> computational implementation and ablation test
```

This chain is more informative than the earlier sentence that "each neuron is Turing-complete."
It identifies measurements, transformations, receivers, and consequences that can actually be
modeled and perturbed.

## What established neuroscience supports

Established results support several components without proving the complete SAN interpretation:

- In rat cortical pyramidal neurons under dynamic-clamp stimulation, action-potential waveform
  variation carried information about recent conductance history in that preparation. See
  [de Polavieja et al. (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0242-05.2005).
- At small cultured central nerve terminals, presynaptic potassium channels, calcium-channel
  abundance, waveform shape, and release probability were experimentally related. See
  [Hoppa et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2014.09.038).
- A local interspike-interval variability statistic distinguished firing-pattern classes across
  recorded cortical neurons in awake macaques. See
  [Shinomoto, Shima, and Tanji (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1162/089976603322518759).
- In anesthetized macaque V1, spike timing relative to low-frequency local-field-potential phase
  carried visual-stimulus information beyond spike count in the tested preparation. See
  [Montemurro et al. (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2008.02.023).

These results establish measurable waveform history, presynaptic transformation, interval
variability, and phase-of-firing information in particular preparations. They do not establish a
single whole-brain timing code, a universal cortical hierarchy, PWD semantics, NAPOT, or a
sufficient mechanism for consciousness.

## Coefficient of variation: what it can and cannot mean

For positive interspike intervals `Delta t`, the ordinary coefficient of variation is:

```text
CV_ISI = standard_deviation(Delta t) / mean(Delta t)              [1]
```

`CV_ISI` is a dimensionless summary of interval dispersion. It is not:

- a phase angle;
- a distribution of spike energies;
- a measure of semantic meaning;
- a complete phase-wave differential; or
- an established controller that directly computes the next spike's duration.

The recoverable 2025 SAN extension is narrower: recent timing variability may be one input to a
receiver's later state update. A valid study should compare ordinary CV with local `CV2`, local
variation, Fano factor, circular phase statistics, waveform features, rate, and the full typed PWD
vector. [[phase-wave-differential-calculus|PWD Calculus]] owns that formal comparison.

## A computability claim with three separate burdens

The source moves among three claims that must remain separate:

1. **Simulation burden.** If the proposed state variables and update rules are algorithmically
   specified to a declared precision, an ordinary computer can simulate that model step by step.
2. **Biological-equivalence burden.** The simulated variables must predict and survive selective
   perturbation of the corresponding biological route better than simpler rate, phase, waveform,
   connectivity, or recurrent-state models.
3. **Consciousness-identity burden.** Even a causally successful neural model does not by itself
   establish that its software implementation has first-person experience. That is a stronger SAN
   hypothesis requiring declared criteria, competing explanations, and an ethical test policy.

Self-modifying parameters do not automatically exceed Turing computability. If state and parameter
updates are generated by finite, algorithmic rules, they remain computable updates. Conversely,
calling a biological neuron "Turing-complete" requires an explicit encoding, control scheme,
memory model, and universality proof; it is not an anatomical or electrophysiological fact.

The strongest source-faithful version of the 2025 argument is therefore not that one neuron is
literally a Turing machine. It is that SAN proposes a computable, multiscale dynamical model and
rejects the claim that Godelian incompleteness or Penrose's argument has already proved biological
consciousness non-computable.

## Decisive experimental and simulation program

The 2025 source becomes scientifically useful when converted into competing models:

1. Fit rate-only, timing-only, phase-only, waveform-only, scalar-variability, conventional recurrent,
   and complete receiver-relative PWD models to the same held-out neural data.
2. Ask which model predicts a named receiver's later state, release, routing, population trajectory,
   plastic change, perception, or action.
3. Perturb the variable and route singled out by the winning model while matching rate, arousal,
   movement, common input, and recording reference.
4. Test loss and rescue of the predicted downstream consequence.
5. Implement the same bounded model in software and repeat the ablations. Report numerical error,
   identifiability, and alternative parameterizations rather than claiming equivalence from visual
   resemblance.

The source's stronger consciousness claim gains empirical support only if the joined model explains
and controls results that simpler alternatives do not. It is weakened if CV, phase, waveform, or
PWD variables add no reproducible predictive or causal value, or if multiple incompatible hidden
mechanisms produce the same observed behavior.
