**Phase Precession** is the hippocampal-discovered phenomenon in which a [[grid-cells-place-cells-oscillators|place cell]]'s firing systematically shifts to *earlier* phases of the ongoing theta rhythm as a rat traverses the cell's place field, producing a continuous theta-relative time code for spatial position. Within [[self-aware-networks]] phase precession is treated as the canonical empirical anchor for the broader SAN claim that timing-relative-to-oscillation, not rate, is the load-bearing code — a hippocampal precedent that the corpus generalizes into the [[phase-wave-differentials]] / [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography]] picture under the heading "Predictive Timing through Oscillatory Phase Precession."
