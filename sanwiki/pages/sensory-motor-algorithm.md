## Post-SAN

Compared to mainstream cortical-coding literatures, the sensory-motor algorithm is closest to active-inference / sensorimotor-contingency accounts but adds the column-level structural commitment. Against pure [[free-energy-principle]] active inference (Friston), the sensory-motor algorithm specifies *what* the column is doing: cycling through a [[reference-frame|reference frame]] and updating predictions from the result. Against pure rate-coding accounts, it commits to motion-driven encoding — the column must move (or simulate movement) to produce the next [[prediction]]. Against Graziano-style [[attention-schema-theory]], it grounds [[attention]] in the same loop rather than in a separate [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model. SAN extends Hawkins' algorithm in three places. First, the carrier becomes oscillatory: [[phase-wave-differentials]] running through [[gamma-oscillations]] and [[cortical-traveling-waves]] supply the timing the bare algorithm leaves abstract. Second, the threshold step becomes [[criterial-causation]]: Hawkins talks about [[prediction]] error and reference-frame selection in functional terms; SAN names the per-neuron mechanism. Third, the abstract-motion extension becomes [[predictive-motor-self-model]]: when the loop runs without physical motion, the same column-level circuitry produces the predictive [[gh-a0206z|self]]-model that [[awareness-as-predictive-myth]] reads as the felt agent. The testable [[prediction]] this sharpens — disrupting the gamma-band carrier should attenuate the sensory-motor loop without changing receptor activation — sits in the same testable-[[prediction]] space as the gamma-encoding literature [[sensory-cortex-encoding]] surveys.
