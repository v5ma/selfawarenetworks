# Mind Uploading

**Mind uploading** is the technology pattern in which the contents of a working brain are read out and re-instantiated in a non-biological substrate, treated in [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] not as bit-for-bit cellular *copying* but as a *query* against the brain's running data structure — closer to the [[brain-as-queryable-network|brain-as-queryable-network]] reading than to the static-snapshot model of classical mind-uploading discourse. It pairs in the SAN catalogue with [[building-sentient-beings|sentient-machine]] technology, [[braingate|BrainGate]]-class brain-machine interfaces, and the broader [[agentic-general-intelligence|AGI]] roadmap as a single cluster of read-write-brain technologies.
