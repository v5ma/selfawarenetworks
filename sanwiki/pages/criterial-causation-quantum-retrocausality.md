**[[criterial-causation|Criterial causation]]** (Peter Tse, *The Neural Basis of Free Will*, 2013) proposes that neurons set [[criterial-thresholds]] for future firing — giving neural systems genuine causal power over their own future states. This is the formal framework [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] engages when exploring quantum retrocausality in [[synaptic-connections]].

## Quantum effects at the synapse

Tse notes that neurotransmitters (glutamate, GABA, serotonin) are smaller than C₆₀ fullerene molecules that exhibit double-slit wave interference. NMDA receptor pores (~5.5 Å) are packed closely enough that simultaneously-open receptors could create multi-slit interference conditions. This leaves open the possibility that **wave-particle effects among neurotransmitters** shape neural processing in ways not captured by classical models.

## Backwards causation framing

Feynman's path-integral account implies that the realized path of a particle is a function of its future possibilities — the future interferes with the present. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] interprets this as support for the idea that **the predicted future drives present action**: when a brain predicts a pattern (e.g., danger behind the fridge door), that prediction — a representation of a possible future — causally influences present motor behavior.

This is not mystical retrocausality but a mechanistic claim: [[prediction-as-retrocausation]] — the brain's forward model of the future acts as a downward cause on current neural dynamics.

## Virtual energy thresholds

An adjacent note in the same source introduces the idea that particle repulsion arises from **virtual energy thresholds** — space warped by spin polarity creates an energy requirement that acts as a repulsive "virtual entity." These relational virtual entities (existing only between particles in certain polarity configurations) are proposed as a general physical mechanism relevant to how neural threshold-states form and propagate.
