The thesis that life is fundamentally an **oscillating network of oscillators** — each neuron, column, and region itself oscillating — and that [[conscious-awareness]] emerges from the coordinated phase relationships among these nested oscillators rather than from static connectivity alone.

This plate maps the SAN mechanism from ion-gated synaptic rhythms through tonic and phasic neuron firing, cortical-column coupling, inter-region synchrony, and EEG phase structure, including the critique that averaging can destroy relational timing.

## Core claim

Awareness arises when oscillating sub-systems collectively maintain equilibrium through dissipative processes. Faster or phasic activity perturbs slower background oscillations: "slower oscillations are disturbed by phasic burst firing patterns," temporarily de-synchronizing lower-frequency rhythms and opening windows for new information to enter the system.

## The EEG noise critique

A central methodological argument: neuroscience discarded meaningful temporal-spatial phase patterns by averaging EEG signals — "throwing out the baby with the bath water." What was classified as noise may encode significant relational information between oscillating circuits. This critique motivates studying [[tempo-spatial-phasic-coincidence]] directly rather than averaged evoked potentials.

## Multi-scale research agenda

The framework implies a layered program:
- **Macro** — whole-brain connectomics and large-scale synchrony
- **Meso** — brainwaves, cortical columns, inter-region coherence
- **Micro** — synaptic circuits, potassium dynamics, axonal action potentials
- **Temporal** — tonic vs [[phasic-burst-firing]] modes and their phase signatures

## Synaptic substrate

Potassium ion dynamics at synapses gate whether a neuron fires tonically (sustained low-rate) or in phasic bursts (high-rate packets). This switch shapes the phase signature propagated up to meso and macro levels, linking molecular biophysics to the large-scale oscillatory picture.
