# Spatial Information

**Spatial information** in [[self-aware-networks]] is the [[information-as-physical|informational]] content that the [[cortex]] extracts from the *spatial layout* of incoming activity — where on the dendritic tree a signal arrives, which subset of [[multi-vesicle-release|vesicles release]], which neighbouring [[apical-dendrites|dendrites]] co-fire — as opposed to the rate or frequency of firing alone. It is one of three SAN coding axes named in the corpus alongside frequency information and phasic information ([[ndca|NDCA]] couples them through [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|phase-wave differentials]] inside [[cortical-column|cortical columns]]).

## Roots

The earliest framing in the SAN corpus pins spatial information to phase-relative neural firing in the [[hippocampus]]: "in the [[hippocampus]], the relative phase of neuronal firing within theta oscillations can encode spatial information (neurons fire at progressively different phases to signal the animal's location)" (`07san--md`, line 54, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)). The same chapter cites the foundational external study — Pastalkova et al. (2006) "Storage of spatial information by the maintenance mechanism of [[long-term-potentiation|LTP]]" in *Science* — as evidence that long-term [[hippocampus|hippocampal]] memory of place is held by [[ltp|LTP]] machinery (`07san--md`, line 273, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md)). [[micah-blumberg|Micah]]'s `a0626z` note compresses the SAN reading of these results into a single line: "[[consciousness]] is from a specific frequency range of phase rate sensor/transmitters (nerve cells) that jointly detect & reconstruct tempo spatial information" (`a0626z--md`, line 2, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0626z.md)) — establishing spatial information as one of two channels (with temporal information) that [[coincidence-detection|coincidence-detecting]] neurons must reconstruct.

## Post-SAN

In mainstream [[neuroscience]], spatial-information coding is dominated by Hafting/Moser ([[grid-cells|grid cells]], 2005), O'Keefe ([[place-cells|place cells]]), and Jacobs et al. (human single-unit phase coding, c. 2013). Pastalkova et al. (2006) is the canonical external citation that the SAN manuscript leans on for [[long-term-potentiation|LTP]]-based persistence of spatial memory (`07san--md`, line 273). Where SAN diverges from the consensus is in *which signal carries the spatial channel*: rather than rate-coded place fields, the SAN line — pursued in `b0271y`, `b0277y`, `b0307y`, and `b0327ywhisper` — argues that the *spatial* component lives in [[multi-vesicle-release|multi-vesicle release]] patterns at the [[chemical-synapse|chemical synapse]], with [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|phase-wave differentials]] across [[micro-columns|micro-columns]] sampling them. This makes a falsifiable [[prediction]] the rate-coding view does not: spatial selectivity should disappear when synapses are forced into uni-vesicular release while leaving rate intact, and should persist across experimental scrambles of mean firing rate (`b0307y--md`, line 47).
