# Grid Cells

> **Evidence boundary:** Grid cells are established most strongly in the medial entorhinal cortex and neighboring hippocampal-formation regions during spatial behavior. Spatially periodic cells in rat primary somatosensory cortex have also been reported. A grid-like fMRI signature, an artificial-network unit, a cortical reference-frame model, and a recorded grid cell are not interchangeable findings.

A **grid cell** is identified from the relationship between a neuron's firing and an animal's position. In a two-dimensional environment, one grid cell can have multiple firing fields arranged approximately as a triangular lattice. Neighboring cells can share scale and orientation while differing in spatial phase. The pattern is measured across behavior; it is not a microscopic hexagonal structure inside the cell.

## What is established

Hafting and colleagues recorded spatially periodic firing in dorsocaudal medial entorhinal cortex in freely moving rats. Later work identified grid modules with different scales and showed that landmarks, self-motion, geometry, task, and network state can influence the pattern. Grid cells interact with, but are not identical to, place, head-direction, border, speed, and object-vector cell populations.

Grid firing is a population and circuit phenomenon. A single cell supplies a partial relation between firing and sampled position. No grid cell contains the complete map, percept, or observer.

## Findings outside the classical system

The phrase **grid-like** covers several evidence levels:

- Long and Zhang reported place-, grid-, border-, and head-direction-like firing in deep layers of rat primary somatosensory cortex during free foraging.
- Constantinescu and colleagues found sixfold, direction-dependent fMRI modulation while people navigated a learned two-dimensional conceptual space. This is a population-level imaging signature, not a direct recording of grid cells.
- Reports from other tasks and regions use electrophysiology, intracranial recording, fMRI, or computational models. Each method supports a different claim.
- The rat V2 result cited by the 2022 Frontiers article was preliminary preprint evidence at the time of that review and should not be presented as equally established peer-reviewed evidence.

These results make generalized coordinate coding plausible. They do not show that all perception and cognition uses one universal grid code.

## Relation to cognitive maps

A grid code can support metric or relational structure, but a cognitive map is not made from grid cells alone. Hippocampal-entorhinal navigation draws on multiple cell classes, sensory cues, recurrent dynamics, memory, action, and environmental boundaries. In abstract tasks, a grid-like population signature may reflect learned transition structure or relational geometry without reproducing the same cellular mechanism as entorhinal grid firing.

## SAN interpretation

SAN uses grid cells as an empirical example of distributed, receiver-dependent coordinate coding. The proposed extension is that local cortical populations may encode relations among features, objects, body state, and possible actions, then exchange updates through [[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]].

That extension should be expressed at three scales:

- **Micro:** one neuron contributes a selective response; it does not represent the complete reference frame.
- **Meso:** a local population can form a structured state space or reference-frame code.
- **Macro:** recurrent sensory, cortical, hippocampal, thalamic, and motor networks can combine many partial relations into an action-ready body-world model.

SAN further proposes that receiver-relative phase and amplitude differences help distinguish updates against a tonic action field. The shared [[phase-wave-differentials|PWD operational definition]] requires two compared states or signals, a receiver, an event window, declared measurements, and a causal readout. Grid-cell evidence does not by itself prove that mechanism, electromagnetic-field causation, or conscious rendering.

## Source-faithful SAN genealogy

Three SAN stages were previously compressed or omitted and must remain distinct:

1. **June 8, 2022 - functional-isomorphism proposal.** In `a0126z`, Micah proposed that cortical object and feature processing could be *functionally isomorphic* to aspects of grid/place interaction. The source contrasted a relatively persistent contextual or relational state with faster-changing sampled features across sensory systems. Its medically defensible residual is a hierarchy of differently persistent state variables, not the universal anatomical claim that slow grid cells occupy one cortical layer and fast place cells occupy another.
2. **August 22, 2022 - volumetric-coordinate proposal.** In `a0018z`, Micah proposed that hippocampal-entorhinal grid/place activity contributes spatial relations to a volumetric body/world representation while other cortical systems contribute object and sensory contents. This is a distributed-network hypothesis, not a complete three-dimensional picture located in the hippocampus.
3. **September 6, 2024 - field-organization proposal.** In `01san`, Micah proposed that population-generated electromagnetic patterns, including grid-like, spiral, and toroidal organization, help coordinate neural activity and self-rendering. This later field claim must not be backdated into the 2022 grid/reference-frame sources or confused with the measured behavioral firing lattice of a grid cell.

The recovered SAN proposition is therefore stronger and more precise than "grid coding might generalize": recurrent circuits may reuse a relational operation across domains; more persistent contextual states may constrain faster feature updates; and receiver-sensitive oscillatory or field variables may help organize the exchange. The first two components have partial empirical comparators. Local endogenous-field and ephaptic effects establish causal plausibility for the third at limited scales, but they do not establish a grid-shaped field code, a universal cortical algorithm, or consciousness.

## What would test the SAN extension

A useful experiment would record identified cell populations across layers and connected regions while an animal or person transforms between body-centered, object-centered, and world-centered coordinates. The analysis should compare firing rate, spike timing, local-field phase, population geometry, persistence of contextual state, and behavior. Causal perturbation should selectively disrupt the predicted coordinate transformation. A SAN-specific model must predict a receiver-relative phase effect that cannot be explained as ordinary correlated firing or inherited input. The functional-isomorphism claim additionally predicts a reproducible relation between relatively persistent context variables and faster sampled-feature updates across more than one sensory or task domain, without requiring the same literal cell type or laminar circuit. The field claim requires a separately manipulated field variable and an effect beyond synaptic connectivity and volume conduction.
