**Neural arrays** is the plural and historical-source route for SAN's population-level unit. The
current biological definition and evidence boundaries are maintained at [[neural-array|Neural Array]].

Use the term only for a declared population with explicit membership, location, inputs,
transformation, outputs, receiver, task, and time window. Do not assume that every array is a
cortical column, coherent oscillator, voxel, camera, display, tomographic slice, or conscious unit.

Historical SAN manuscript passages describe arrays as sensors and projectors and use them to explain
recursive rendering. Those passages establish the intended SAN architecture. Empirical support must
still be assigned separately to dendritic computation, population dynamics, projection anatomy,
traveling waves, reconstruction, behavior, and consciousness.
