What is an NFT?
What is an NFT? (Non-Fungible Tokens) — ERC-1155 VS ERC-721

The big idea is that you could receive an item from a game and you would get to keep that item outside the game. In the game Legend of Zelda for example you may get a sword, but you don’t get to keep it, when the game is over the sword is gone, trapped in the game. On the other hand if you were to get that item with NFT that would mean that you owned it and you could keep it even after you left the game.

NFT answers the question of how do you attach authenticity, verification, and digital ownership to an item. That could be a digital item, or a physical/digital hybrid item and how can you attach ownership to that?

Emma explained that in the past eight months the NFT market has exploded. A lot of artists have recently moved into the space to learn about NFTs and how they can use them to monetize their own work.

Dash, or the second layer of Emma’s strategy, is what interests me the most out of everything that Emma has been doing, although this article does not cover all of it. It enables the interoperability of the [Player Creator system](digital-native-economy-xr-bci.md) with the Artist-Fashion Community by solving a long-standing issue with [3D file formats](object-segmentation.md). Dash aims to solve a problem facing [3D artists](neural-rendering.md), game developers, and web developers alike with a novel approach that uses opinionated logic and [Fourier transforms](fourier-slice-theorem.md) to create an accurate and compressed representation of a [3D model](object-segmentation.md) in its original environment, such as a 3D program, and transfers its appearance accurately to another environment, such as a game engine.

Layer 2 is meant to answer the question: what is the technical innovation needed to bridge these 3D assets, how can you bring them in applications, where they are rendered correctly, and are usable.

Emma is creating Dash to answer the second part, it’s a new file format that solves the problem of 3D file interoperability in a smart way.

One research topic that this reminded me of is [3D semantic segmentation](3d-semantic-segmentation.md). I first learned about it via a talk by Or Litani in San Francisco years back: https://orlitany.github.io/

Like Material Instance Segmentation, which is accomplished with opinionated logic instead of neural networks, [3D semantic segmentation](3d-semantic-segmentation.md) is a niche inside semantic segmentation and inside [3D object segmentation](object-segmentation.md). Those topics are niches inside the idea of using neural networks for [capture](point-cloud-neural-representation.md) or [rendering](neural-rendering.md), and those topics are niches inside capture, rendering, and [deep learning](deep-learning-neural-networks.md), respectively. Deep learning is a niche inside [artificial intelligence](artificial-intelligence.md), rendering is a niche topic inside the computer graphics industry, and so on.
