**Neural Entrainment** is the phenomenon by which endogenous [[neural-oscillations|neural oscillations]] come to phase-lock and frequency-track an external rhythmic driver — a sound, a visual flicker, a transcranial alternating current — so that the brain's internal rhythms become coupled to the driver's. Within [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]], entrainment is one of the canonical mechanisms by which [[brainwaves|brainwaves]] across [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]] enter [[cortical-column-oscillatory-synchronization|oscillatory synchronization]], shaping [[attention|attention]], perception, and the [[continuous-neural-rendering|continuous rendering]] loop that produces conscious experience.

## Position

Within SAN the entrainment phenomenon is read as a special case of [[computational-phase-synchronization|computational phase synchronization]]: an exogenous oscillator (sound, light, or current) imposes a phase reference, and intrinsic populations re-time their firing to that reference, producing measurable changes in perception (`b0047y--md`, line 11, [[gh-b0047y|on github]]). This places entrainment alongside [[cross-frequency-coupling|cross-frequency coupling]] and [[cortical-traveling-waves|cortical traveling waves]] as one of the channels by which [[brainwave|brainwave]] populations coordinate across distances larger than any single column. Because the [[brain-as-rendering-engine|rendering-engine]] account already requires column-to-column phase agreement to bind features into a percept, entrainment is exactly the kind of perturbation SAN predicts should change perceptual content — which is what the cited tACS auditory-modulation finding shows.

The SAN reading also unifies entrainment with non-clinical entrainment phenomena: [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]] in audio/visual contexts, [[brainwave-driven-photic-stimulation|photic stimulation]], [[dreams-as-theta-wave-sequences|theta-driven dream sequences]], and the higher-level coordination implied by [[brain-to-brain-eeg-synchronization|brain-to-brain EEG synchronization]] and [[inter-brain-eeg-synchronization|inter-brain EEG synchronization]]. All are instances of the same underlying mechanism — a phase reference being shared across previously independent oscillators — applied at different scales.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream auditory-neuroscience work (e.g., the [[eeg|EEG]]/tACS literature represented by the cited passage) treats entrainment as a *measurable* coupling phenomenon useful for probing perception. SAN extends this in two directions. First, it generalises: the same mechanism is invoked for [[artificial-telepathy-via-oscillatory-synchrony|oscillatory-synchrony-mediated communication]] between brains and for [[agent-phase-sync-feedback-loop|agent-level phase-sync feedback loops]] in artificial systems. Second, it makes a specific testable bet: if conscious content depends on cross-column phase agreement, then entrainment that *reorganises* this phase agreement (not merely entrainment to a single sensory stream) should change [[conscious-access|conscious access]] in predictable ways — a stronger claim than the modality-specific perception-modulation results currently in the literature. Modulators such as [[acetylcholine|acetylcholine]] are predicted to gate the depth of entrainment by adjusting the gain of the underlying oscillators.
