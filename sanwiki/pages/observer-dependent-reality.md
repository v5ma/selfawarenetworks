# Observer Dependent Reality

**Observer Dependent Reality** is the [[self-aware-networks]] thesis that the observer is a feature of spacetime geometry rather than a separate metaphysical layer perched on top of it — and consequently that any account of reality must include the local aperture through which spacetime is being perceived. As a sibling commitment to [[neural-distinction-consciousness]] and to the [[spacetime-geometry-as-ground-of-being]] picture, it threads through [[observer-mechanism]], [[observer-as-aggregate-expectation]], [[observer-observed-entification]], and the [[subjective-objective-distinction]].

## Position

The SAN claim is that "observer-dependent" is a geometric statement, not an idealist or solipsist one. The observer is a local aperture of spacetime: a region of the same geometry that contains photons, atoms, and people, equipped to express that geometry through local perception. On this reading [[observer-dependent-reality]] is the relational-physics gloss on what [[spacetime-geometry-observer]] specifies geometrically and what [[neural-distinction-consciousness]] specifies cellularly — the three claims agree that the *minimal* unit of conscious reality is a registered difference between two distinct patterns by some local observer-system, and they disagree only on which level of description is load-bearing. Within the SAN canon the thesis pairs with [[observer-as-aggregate-expectation]] (the observer is an aggregate of priors, not a point particle), with [[observer-mechanism]] (the observer has a mechanism, not just a label), with [[contentless-observer]] (the observer can be picked out independently of any specific content), and with [[observer-observed-entification]] (the observer/observed split is a constructed [[entification]], not a primitive of nature). It also feeds into [[distributed-observer-vs-consciousness-module]] — the explicit SAN [[choice]] for a distributed-observer reading over a [[consciousness]]-as-module reading — and into the [[six-modality-sensory-architecture-seventh-observer]] picture where the observer is a seventh, integrative modality rather than an external witness. The thesis explicitly refuses the strong-anthropic move of "observer creates reality": instead, the observer is *of* reality, and reality remains observer-dependent only in the operational sense that what gets registered, rendered, and reported depends on which local aperture is doing the registering — a commitment that lines up with [[brain-as-rendering-engine]] and [[consciousness-multimodal-rendering-loop]] on the cognitive-architecture side.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream physics offers several adjacent positions that [[observer-dependent-reality]] resembles without coinciding with. Wheeler-style "participatory universe" framings and [[quantum-observer-effect-critique]]-style readings of measurement both treat the observer as load-bearing on what counts as reality, and both intersect the SAN thesis at the operational claim that registration is part of the physical story. Where the SAN framing departs is in refusing the strong-anthropic upgrade: on the SAN reading the observer does not *create* reality, it is *of* reality — a difference that aligns the thesis more closely with relational-quantum-mechanics-style accounts than with [[consciousness]]-collapse readings. Block-universe / eternalist readings of spacetime are consistent with the SAN "4D view" that [[spacetime-geometry-as-ground-of-being]] recites, but eternalism remains silent on observers; SAN's contribution is to insist that observers are part of the same 4D pattern rather than passengers added on top of it. [[integrated-information-theory]] and [[global-workspace-theory]] both treat [[consciousness]] as a substrate-level fact about systems, but neither makes the geometric move that the observer is a feature of spacetime; the SAN thesis is upstream of those accounts in the sense that it is a claim about what kind of entity the observer is before any specific theory of [[consciousness]] is built. Where Observer Dependent Reality makes a non-trivial bet that mainstream physics has not endorsed, it is the bet that observers can be specified geometrically — as local apertures of the same geometry that contains photons and atoms — and that the dependence of registered reality on the observer is therefore not metaphysical privilege but ordinary geometric fact.
