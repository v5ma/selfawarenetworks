Notation and Dimensional Consistency
------------------------------------

**Important:** In the original [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]]/SDT theory, the same symbols αρt\alpha\,\rho_tαρt​ and kρt\tfrac{k}{\rho_t}ρt​k​ were inserted across different types of equations (energy, time, force, etc.). This notation belongs to the broader [[qgtcd|QGTCD]], [[dark-time-theory|Dark Time Theory]], and [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation|Quantum Gradient Time Crystal Dilation]] cluster. To maintain dimensional consistency, we now introduce *distinct* coupling constants αi\alpha_iαi​ and kik_iki​ for each equation's domain.

For example, in **energy** equations we use αE,kE\alpha_E, k_EαE​,kE​, in **time** equations we use αT,kT\alpha_T, k_TαT​,kT​, and so on. The same distinction supports related [[field-coupling|field coupling]], [[particle-interaction-time-density|particle interaction time density]], and [[qgtcd-metric-tensor-extension|metric tensor extension]] pages. Thus, wherever you see

⋯-α(domain)ρtand⋯+k(domain)ρt,\dots - \alpha_{(\mathrm{domain})}\,\rho_t \quad\text{and}\quad \dots + \frac{k_{(\mathrm{domain})}}{\rho_t},⋯-α(domain)​ρt​and⋯+ρt​k(domain)​​,

they are meant to have the correct units for the equation at hand (energy, wavefunction amplitude, curvature, etc.).

In a fully rigorous version, you would:

-   Define the unit of ρt\rho_tρt​ (the "time density" or "dark time" variable),
-   Specify each αi\alpha_iαi​ and kik_iki​ with exact physical units,
-   Provide physical motivation for each added term.

±αρtor±kρt\pm\,\alpha\,\rho_t \quad\text{or}\quad \pm\,\frac{k}{\rho_t}±αρt​or±ρt​k​

±αiρtor±kiρt,\pm\,\alpha_{i}\,\rho_t \quad\text{or}\quad \pm\,\frac{k_{i}}{\rho_t},±αi​ρt​or±ρt​ki​​,
