# Fractal Conscious Perception

**FRACTAL** means **Functional Recursive Activity Cortical Telescoping Asymmetric Lensing**. The August 2022 SAN Whitepaper introduced it as a hypothesis about how locally learned neural state can be re-expressed through larger connected populations and become consequential for distributed perception, memory, attention, and action.

It does not mean that one synapse contains a complete memory, that sharp-wave ripples sweep every percept into a whole-brain broadcast, or that every cortical column runs one universal algorithm.

## The problem the source was trying to solve

Learning changes synapses, dendrites, cells, and ensembles locally. A recalled event can nevertheless influence widely distributed sensory, contextual, emotional, linguistic, and motor systems. The source asked how a small physical trace can acquire larger functional reach without requiring a central screen or inner observer.

The proposed answer was **cortical telescoping**:

```text
partial cue
-> reactivation of a learned cellular or local-population state
-> recurrent recruitment and suppression
-> transformed re-expression across connected populations
-> distributed consequence for perception, memory, report, or action
```

The medically defensible unit is a learned **ensemble and its synaptic organization**, not one generic synapse carrying an entire multimodal scene.

## What "telescoping" and "asymmetric lensing" mean

**Telescoping** means a change in scale of participation and causal reach. Activity that begins in a small population can recruit larger connected populations.

**Asymmetric lensing** means the larger pattern is not an identical enlargement. Each receiver transforms the input according to its anatomy, synaptic weights, inhibition, membrane state, neuromodulation, recent history, and current task. Some features are retained, some suppressed, and some combined with other state.

The words are functional analogies. They do not imply an optical lens, literal zoom, hologram, or lossless neural photocopier.

## Established evidence near the proposal

Several findings make parts of the sequence plausible without proving FRACTAL:

- A specific contextual fear memory in mice can involve a distributed complex of functionally connected engram ensembles. Simultaneous activation of several ensembles produced stronger recall than activation of one ensemble in that preparation ([Roy et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-29384-4)).
- Experience-related firing sequences can be replayed in hippocampus and visual cortex during slow-wave sleep, with coordinated replay in the two regions ([Ji and Wilson, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn1825)).
- Selective interruption of awake hippocampal sharp-wave ripples impaired learning and performance in a rat spatial-alternation task ([Jadhav et al., 2012](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1217230)).
- Intrinsic neural timescales differ across cortical areas, providing an empirical basis for nested temporal organization without implying identical computation at every scale ([Murray et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3862)).

Sharp-wave ripples are hippocampal population events with state- and task-dependent relations to replay and memory. They are not a generic name for all alternating excitation and inhibition, and they do not establish a universal synapse-to-consciousness pathway.

## Relationship to NAPOT

[[neural-array-projection-oscillation-tomography|NAPOT]] and FRACTAL address overlapping but different questions:

- **NAPOT:** how partial activity is received, transformed, projected, recurrently combined, and redistributed by neural populations.
- **FRACTAL:** how a learned pattern changes its scale of participation and is re-expressed asymmetrically through connected populations.

FRACTAL is therefore not merely "between NAPOT layers." Both mechanisms can operate within and across local circuits and distributed networks, provided the source, receiver, route, scale, and consequence are declared.

## The distributed observer

No region receives a finished percept for a separate viewer. The recruited neural and bodily populations are part of the perceiving and acting system. A representation has larger functional reach when it changes later discrimination, report, memory, valuation, or movement. See [[screen-metaphor-no-inner-viewer]].

## Claim ladder

1. **Established:** learning changes distributed neural ensembles; recall can reactivate connected populations; recurrent excitation and inhibition shape recruitment; hippocampal replay and sharp-wave ripples have causal memory roles in bounded tasks.
2. **Evidence-supported SAN interpretation:** local traces can acquire broader influence through anatomically constrained reactivation and transformed re-expression.
3. **Open FRACTAL hypothesis:** a recurring receive-transform-recruit-project motif links cellular, local-circuit, and distributed scales.
4. **Stronger conscious-perception hypothesis:** specific cross-scale transformations predict access, report, or action beyond firing rate, ordinary recurrence, distributed-engram membership, spectral power, and behavior alone.

## Discriminating experiment

Use a task with a learned multimodal object or event and record a declared local engram-related population together with connected hippocampal, thalamic, sensory, association, and action-related populations. Measure:

- cue-triggered local reactivation;
- the order and timing of recruitment;
- which representational relations are preserved or transformed;
- inhibition and route-specific effective connectivity;
- perceptual report, memory, and action.

Compare a distributed-engram model, an ordinary recurrent model, a firing-rate and power model, and a FRACTAL model. Perturb one declared route or timing relation and test for the predicted loss and a matched rescue. FRACTAL is weakened if its cross-scale variables add no held-out predictive or causal value.
