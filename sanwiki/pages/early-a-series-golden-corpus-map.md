# Early A-Series Golden Corpus Map

The early a-series map gives every source from `a0001z` through `a0020z` a positive editorial role, exact reader route, and downstream destination. These sources are not ranked as valuable versus disposable; the map records where each performs its strongest work.

## Placement vocabulary

- **Narrative spine:** a scene, question, or transition needed to understand the SAN argument.
- **Mechanism donor:** biological or computational reasoning that must be translated and independently sourced.
- **Historical bridge:** evidence of how the program changed and why later terms became necessary.
- **Evidence/reference reserve:** references, sidebars, endnotes, counterarguments, or later revision material.
- **Outward route:** a visible transition whose full derivation belongs in another paper or volume.
