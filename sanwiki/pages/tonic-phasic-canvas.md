# Tonic and Phasic Roles: Canvas and Ink

The **tonic canvas / phasic ink** model is SAN's functional description of how an ongoing neural
state can supply the conditions against which a consequential change is registered. The ongoing
state is the canvas; the change is the ink. The same participating network receives the change,
is physically altered by it, and carries its consequences into later perception, control, and
action. There is no separate listener or viewer.

## The medically bounded definition

**Tonic** and **phasic** are relative roles, not permanent frequency bands:

- **Tonic context** is a relatively maintained state over a declared time window. It may include
  membrane potential, synaptic conductance, firing probability, adaptation, neuromodulatory state,
  population activity, body state, and extracellular fields produced by participating cells.
- **Phasic difference** is a transient event or departure that changes a receiver's state and what
  that receiver can do next.

A signal can be tonic at one scale and phasic at another. A slow population change can be phasic
relative to a day-long state; a spike burst can be tonic within a short burst epoch. The labels do
not imply that tonic activity is always slow or high-magnitude, that phasic activity is always fast
or low-magnitude, or that all cells share one clock.

## What the historical source was trying to say

The canvas and ink language names an operational proposal, not merely a visual metaphor:

1. **A difference requires context.** A receiver can register a departure only relative to its own
   recent or maintained state. The tonic role supplies that context.
2. **A rarer departure can carry more information.** The June 2022 sources connect the relative
   rarity of a phasic event to Shannon information. Rarity increases surprisal; it does not by itself
   establish meaning.
3. **The participating system registers the change.** SAN proposes that cells, synapses, recurrent
   circuits, and their local field-cell state are altered together. The distributed system is the
   observer in the operational sense that it undergoes and uses the difference.
4. **The difference changes later processing.** A phasic event can alter membrane state, release,
   recruitment, routing, short-term dynamics, plasticity, recall, prediction, or action.
5. **The distinct event can fade without becoming consequence-free.** Later 2022 sources propose
   that recurrent coupling progressively reduces the event's separate decodability while its
   cellular, synaptic, and behavioral consequences can persist. Learned circuitry may later
   re-express a related pattern.

For receiver `R`, context `C_R`, and event `e`, the relevant Shannon quantity is:

```text
I_R(e | C_R) = -log2 P_R(e | C_R)                                [1]
```

This equation formalizes rarity relative to a declared receiver and context. A complete SAN code
claim additionally requires a recoverable distinction, a causal receiver use, and a consequence.

## The receive-transform-use chain

1. A receiving cell or population has an ongoing state.
2. Synaptic, sensory, recurrent, or bodily input changes that state.
3. Dendritic, somatic, circuit, field, and neuromodulatory dynamics transform the change.
4. Axonal output and synaptic release re-express some consequences to downstream receivers.
5. Recurrent and body-world feedback changes the next tonic context.

This chain is the biological translation of canvas and ink. The ink is not a substance painted on
a screen. It is a receiver-relative change in living tissue. In SAN, a candidate
[[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differential]] is a typed description of that departure, not
simply a synonym for phase coding or prediction error.

## What established neuroscience supports

Established work supports several components of this operation:

- Ongoing cortical activity can help predict variation in a subsequent evoked response
  ([Arieli et al., 1996](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.273.5283.1868)).
- Spike phase relative to a low-frequency population context can carry information beyond spike
  count in a measured visual-cortex preparation
  ([Montemurro et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18328702/)).
- The phase of thalamic firing relative to slow cortical activity varies with thalamic nucleus and
  the exact ongoing cortical pattern
  ([Slezia et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3375-10.2011)).
- Causal timing perturbation can selectively affect hippocampal memory encoding
  ([Quirk et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-021-00919-0)).
- Weak extracellular electric fields can change and entrain active cortical networks under bounded
  experimental conditions
  ([Frohlich and McCormick, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20624597/)).
- Subthreshold extracellular fields can change membrane potential and spike timing in cortical
  slice preparations
  ([Anastassiou et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21240273/)).

These studies establish state dependence, timing dependence, phase-sensitive information, and
bounded local field feedback. They do not by themselves establish the complete canvas-and-ink
operator, one global tonic carrier, a fixed phasic alphabet, or SAN's consciousness interpretation.

## The SAN field-cell interpretation

SAN proposes that the coordinated **field-cell** state is an active comparison context. Neural
membranes, ionic currents, synapses, extracellular media, glia, vasculature, and body state form one
interacting biological system. Phasic events change participating cells and the spatially structured
electrical activity they produce. Under demonstrated local conditions, extracellular fields can in
turn affect cells.

The historical sources also propose electrical, magnetic, mechanical, metabolic, thermal,
hemodynamic, and chemical state changes as candidate contributors to neural rendering. These
variables must not be collapsed into one generic field or discarded as decorative language. Each
requires its own measurement scale, receiver, causal route, and perturbation test. Local electric
field feedback has direct experimental footholds, while mechanical, metabolic, thermal,
hemodynamic, and chemical effects are established in different biological contexts. Their proposed
joined role in neural rendering and distributed observation, along with stronger endogenous
magnetic and cross-scale roles, remains a distinct SAN hypothesis to test at each claimed scale.

The network registers a difference by being changed by it and by using that change in later
recognition, prediction, autonomic regulation, sampling, memory, or action. The stronger proposition
that this distributed receive-transform-use operation is constitutive of observation or phenomenal
consciousness is the SAN hypothesis. No inner screen or localized homunculus is added.

## Dissipation, persistence, and re-expression

In the later SAN formulation, *dissipation* does not mean that a phasic event becomes irrelevant or
that a subjective image is passively erased. It names a proposed transition in which recurrent
mixing, coupling, inhibition, adaptation, and relaxation make an incoming difference progressively
less separately decodable from the evolving tonic context.

During that interval, the event can still alter short-term synaptic dynamics, spike timing,
recruitment, plasticity, recall, and action. A later cue may recruit learned circuitry that
re-expresses a related population pattern. The forward integration and later constructive
re-expression operations are developed in
[[tonic-absorption-generative-reexpression|Tonic Absorption and Generative Re-expression]]. They
are later 2022 stages and must not be projected backward into every earlier canvas-and-ink note.

## Scale ladder

| Scale | Candidate tonic context | Candidate phasic difference | Required receiver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | Membrane, synaptic, dendritic, and excitability state | Synaptic event, dendritic spike, burst, waveform change, or release event | Named dendritic compartment, soma/AIS, terminal, or postsynaptic cell |
| Meso | Local population state, inhibition, recurrent gain, or oscillatory relation | Ensemble transition, phase reset, traveling-wave passage, or routed population update | Named local or downstream circuit |
| Macro | Distributed brain-body working state | Sensory update, prediction mismatch, action consequence, or state transition | Named distributed systems that alter later behavior or report |

The same abstract relationship can recur at several scales, but that does not make one neuron a
complete tomographic renderer or make every local oscillator independently conscious.

## Boundaries that preserve the historical model

- A tonic role does not require every neuron to fire regularly. It requires a measurable reference
  state for a declared receiver and time window.
- Local coordination does not require all neighboring neurons or all brain regions to share one
  perfectly synchronized baseline.
- A phasic event is not automatically recognition or conscious content. The SAN claim requires a
  receiver-specific transformation and consequence.
- Return toward phase organization does not by itself establish integration or perception. SAN's
  stronger absorption account predicts a specific loss of separate decodability while measurable
  consequences persist.
- Inhibition is part of the registering and routing system, not a separate audience. In the SAN
  interpretation, the interacting distributed system collectively performs observation.
- The historical 0/1/2/3-vesicle proposal is a candidate synapse-specific transformation, not a
  universal fixed qualia alphabet.
- Anesthesia is a proposed perturbation test of coordinated context-and-difference processing, not
  an assumption that one universal canvas is selectively erased.
- **Piano and seismic language** are teaching analogies for transient departure, propagation,
  fading, and tissue sensitivity. They do not assert piano or earthquake anatomy.
- **Zero-point-field language** is preserved as an exploratory 2022 comparison. Its recoverable
  biological question concerns an ongoing oscillatory reference state; it does not require neural
  tissue to be a literal quantum vacuum field.
- **Electrical, magnetic, mechanical, metabolic, thermal, hemodynamic, and chemical language**
  identifies candidate physical variables, not one interchangeable metaphor. Their evidentiary
  status must be stated separately.
- **Cosmic or substrate-general extensions** belong to SAN's cross-scale hypothesis and genealogy,
  not to a literal anatomical description of one neural circuit.

## When a biological difference becomes a code

A firing pattern, phase relation, waveform, burst, or vesicle count becomes a code claim only when
the page identifies:

1. the sender and receiver;
2. the recoverable distinction;
3. the advantage beyond correlated rate, power, movement, arousal, adaptation, and history;
4. the receiver's causal use of the distinction;
5. the conditions under which the relationship fails.

Without those elements, the observation establishes biological richness, not semantic encoding.
