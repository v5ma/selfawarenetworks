# Neural Oscillations

**Neural oscillations** are recurring population dynamics measured at different biological scales. This page routes the plural topic to the canonical [[neural-oscillation|Neural Oscillation]] account and prevents band names from becoming anatomical or psychological departments.

## What must be separated

- intracellular membrane oscillation;
- rhythmic spiking or spike probability;
- local field-potential rhythm;
- intracranial or scalp EEG rhythm;
- MEG signal;
- transient burst;
- evoked response;
- narrowband spectral peak;
- broadband high-frequency activity;
- phase locking, coherence, and amplitude correlation; and
- traveling-wave propagation.

Two measurements in the same nominal frequency range may have different sources. Conversely, one circuit process may produce effects across multiple frequencies. Analysis must begin with the biological preparation and measurement rather than the band label.

## Relation to SAN

SAN proposes that some timing relations help neural populations register, transform, and redistribute differences. This proposal becomes testable only when a page declares the source, route, receiver, measured variable, behavioral consequence, and matched alternatives. Oscillations alone do not establish a neural code, perceptual render, observer, or consciousness mechanism.
