## The 14-cycle architecture

The two ordering PDFs preserve a much larger book conception than a short neuroscience paper:

1. Nerve Gear
2. Holography and Neural Rendering
3. Fractals, Gravity, and Neurophysics
4. Memory Prediction
5. Sensation and Perception
6. Memory
7. Object Recognition
8. Attention Scaling
9. Perception, Consideration, and Action Cycle
10. Emotion
11. Sensory Processing and Integration
12. Advanced Neuroimaging Techniques
13. Cognitive Enhancements
14. Future Directions in Neurophysics and AI

The order views contain 548 distinct recovered a-series identifiers from `a0001z` through
`a0651z`. This is strong evidence for the scale and intended breadth of the older book plan. It is
not proof that every listed note is publication-ready or that the automated summaries are exact.

Direct source review found concrete summary failures. The spreadsheets describe `a0032z` as a
neurofeedback-program note, `a0072z` as a brain-stimulation discussion, and `a0074z` as a
neurofeedback/entrainment note. Their unchanged raw files instead concern a mixed graph-brain and
sentient-biotech scope note, a pre-2011 senses-brainwaves-actions self-loop dialogue, and an
aging/socioemotional-selectivity/AGI ethics dialogue. The cycle assignments remain useful editorial
hypotheses, but the summaries cannot be used as semantic authority.

## Why the backlog remains visible

`00linkreviewlist.md` says explicitly that it was meant to become empty as items were reviewed,
moved into notes, or rejected. It also points to additional reservoirs in Toby, OneTab, browsers,
social accounts, cloud storage, and downloaded PDFs. The file therefore records both candidate
evidence and the existence of a larger unresolved intake problem.

The links cover thalamocortical movement, inhibitory interneurons, memory and spatial tuning,
synaptic structure, tactile imagery, neural recording, DIANA MRI, calcium waves, phase-of-firing,
reward learning, collective intelligence, and cautions about brain-like deep-learning models.
Each item needs a current primary-source check before it becomes a citation.

## Duplicate and derivative boundary

- A compilation PDF preserves a tranche; it does not supersede the individual Markdown source.
- A spreadsheet summary is an editorial map; it is not the authority for the source's exact words.
- The raw note or recording is the primary historical source when available.
- A derived text extraction should remain connected to its PDF rather than counted as a new work.
- Hash identity, Git chronology, intellectual lineage, and scientific validation are separate facts.
