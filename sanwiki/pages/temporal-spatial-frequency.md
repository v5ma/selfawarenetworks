## Position — frequency vs. pattern

The deep move in *temporal spatial frequency* is to read a [[tempo-spatial-patterns|tempo-spatial pattern]] not just as an unfolding sequence of array-states but as something with its *own* characteristic frequency — a "rate of pattern-interlacing." Two circuits that interlace the same patterns in the same order but at different *rates* implement different content. Two brains that hold the same pattern-set but oscillate it at different rates are different *kinds of mind*. This pushes SAN into territory adjacent to spectral-graph readings of [[consciousness]] (cf. ## Post-SAN — bridges to mainstream literature

Mainstream [[neuroscience]] uses "temporal frequency" (Hz) and "spatial frequency" (cycles per degree, in vision) as separate axes; the joint construction is more familiar from physics (e.g., dispersion relations) and from spectral methods.
