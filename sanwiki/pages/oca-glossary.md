# OCA Glossary

Normalized glossary page from the OCA draft appendix, with key SAN terms pulled out of pages 42 to 48 and prepared for cross-linking across the wiki.

## How to read this glossary

Some entries below name established neuroscience topics; others name SAN's interpretation or proposed extension of those topics. Follow each link for the page's evidence boundary, source history, and experimental status.

## Core SAN terms

- **[[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks (SAN)]]**: the larger framework connecting cellular reception, oscillatory coordination, distributed neural rendering, memory, and embodied action.
- **[[neural-array|Neural array]]**: a bounded group of cells or circuit elements considered as a coordinated receiver, transformer, or projector. The page distinguishes cellular, local-circuit, and distributed-network scales.
- **[[napot-overview|Neural Array Projection Oscillation Tomography (NAPOT)]]**: SAN's proposed many-to-one-to-many reconstruction architecture for gathering partial state, transforming it, and redistributing an update.
- **[[phase-wave-differentials|Phase-wave differential (PWD)]]**: SAN's proposed receiver-relative description of a neural event that differs from an expected tonic state across timing, phase, waveform, or transmission variables.
- **[[tonic-context-rare-deviation-tomographic-language|Tonic context]]**: the ongoing population state against which a receiver can register a changing event.
- **Phasic update**: a transient departure that carries a differentiated update relative to tonic context. See [[tonic-phasic-phase-wave-differential]].
- **[[coincidence-detection|Coincidence detection]]**: the established cellular and circuit principle that sufficiently convergent inputs can cross a receiver's response threshold; SAN uses it across several declared scales.
- **[[oscillatory-binding|Oscillatory binding]]**: coordination through timing relationships among neural populations. SAN treats both alignment and structured dispersion as potentially informative.
- **[[neural-rendering|Neural rendering]]**: SAN's term for the distributed construction and maintenance of a working perceptual or action-oriented state. It does not imply an inner screen or localized viewer.
- **[[observer-mechanism|Distributed observer]]**: the proposal that observation is performed by the participating network and its recurrent sensorimotor consequences rather than by a separate homunculus.
- **[[criterial-causation|Criterial causation]]**: Peter Tse's account of how prior neural changes can alter the criteria for future firing; SAN connects it to timing, routing, and choice.
- **[[artificial-neurology|Artificial Neurology]]**: the engineering program of reproducing relevant neural mechanisms and organizational principles in artificial systems.
