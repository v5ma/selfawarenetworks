## Position

In [[self-aware-networks]], TCNR is the proposal that conscious rendering can be quantified by a *coherence-rendering* index (TCI) computed from preprocessing band-limited signals, computing coherence + render + mutual information + counterfactual capacity, building null ensembles, and aggregating to a standardized score. The manuscript positions TCNR against [[free-energy-principle|free-energy-principle]] / predictive-coding accounts: where active-inference treats perception as scene inference under variational free energy, TCNR adds (i) an explicit oscillatory stitching operator, (ii) a coherence reward that preserves lawful phase ramps, and (iii) a composite null-standardized index with preregistered falsification tests (amplitude-vs-phase double dissociation, decoupling). TCNR sits inside the same [[super-information-theory|SIT]] / [[ndca|NDCA]] family as the [[volumetric-3d-internal-screen|volumetric 3D internal screen]] account but supplies the *empirical-index* layer the rest of the family was missing. The [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|traveling-wave]] literature provides the candidate physical substrate that TCNR's coherence operators read.
