# Thought Is Physical

**Thought Is Physical** is the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] commitment that thinking — the entire field of mental events, including imagery, judgement, anticipation, and the [[qualia|felt]] character of experience — is a literal physical process inside the brain rather than a separate non-physical substance. In SAN this is a *substrate-level physicalism*: thoughts are oscillatory, dendritic, and [[coincidence-pattern-neural-bit|coincidence-bit]]-encoded events with measurable [[brainwave-vortex-attractor|electromagnetic]] signatures, simply at a small enough scale that they are not ordinarily seen. The page sits next to [[consciousness-as-prediction-determinism]] and [[qgtcd]] as part of SAN's broader move to make mind a physics problem rather than a metaphysical one.

## Position

In [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the *thought-is-physical* commitment does three things at once. First, it dissolves the residual dualism in many otherwise-naturalistic accounts of mind: there is no separate mental substance, no extra ontological category, only physics at small scales — "thought is physical, it's just very small you do not usually see it" (`b0311ySci--md`, line 7606). Second, it places thoughts on the same ontological footing as the [[qgtcd|QGTCD]] physics-side picture: the [[brainwave-vortex-attractor|electromagnetic]] and dendritic events that constitute thinking are physically continuous with the field-and-particle physics that [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] describes, so thought, body, and world are governed by the same theory rather than by a special "mental" exception. Third, it grounds SAN's empirical posture: because thought is physical, it must in principle be measurable, recordable, and re-renderable — which is what licenses the SAN treatment of [[consciousness-as-temporally-active-loop|consciousness as a temporally active loop]] and the projection from there to BCI-class instrumentation that aims at decoding [[coincidence-pattern-neural-bit|coincidence-pattern bits]] directly. The "quantum realm is also physical and real" remark in the same passage extends the commitment downward: SAN does not allow physicalism to be partial — quantum-scale events are not a metaphysical reservoir but ordinary physics, just smaller.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream cognates run from Smart's identity theory and Place's "is [[consciousness]] a brain process" through the contemporary physicalism literature (Papineau, Stoljar) and the more biologically committed positions of Damasio and Edelman. Where those authors argue *for* physicalism at length, SAN treats it as a stipulated starting point and uses it to license substantive predictions: the [[brainwave-vortex-attractor|electromagnetic]] signatures of specific thoughts should be measurable, the [[coincidence-pattern-neural-bit|coincidence-bit]] code should be decodable, and the [[qgtcd|physics-side]] description should compose with the [[neuroscience]]-side description without an ontological seam. The "quantum realm is also physical and real" extension also gives SAN a particular relationship to interpretations of quantum mechanics — it rejects mind-as-quantum-special accounts (Penrose-Hameroff style) while accepting that quantum-scale physics participates in the brain's electromagnetics; in [[advaita-vedanta]]-adjacent framings it lets SAN treat non-dual experience as a physical fact about the rendering loop rather than as evidence for a separate [[consciousness]] substance.
