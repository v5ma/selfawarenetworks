# Microtubules

**Microtubules** are polarized polymers of alpha- and beta-tubulin that form part of the neuronal cytoskeleton. In neurons they help establish axons and dendrites, support intracellular transport, organize cell shape, and remodel during development and plasticity. These established functions matter to [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] because they can change what material reaches a synapse and how a neuron remains structurally prepared for later activity. They do not, by themselves, establish a microtubule memory code or a quantum substrate of consciousness.

## Established cellular physiology

Neuronal microtubules extend through axons and dendritic shafts. Molecular motors use them as tracks for cargo transport, while microtubule dynamics contribute to neurite growth, branching, polarity, and structural maintenance. Mature dendritic spines are primarily actin-rich. Growing microtubule plus ends can **transiently enter a subset of spines**; it is inaccurate to depict mature spines as generally filled with stable microtubule bundles.

In the vertebrate preparations studied, mature axonal microtubules were predominantly plus-end-out, whereas many dendrites contained both plus-end-out and minus-end-out microtubules ([Baas et al., 1988](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.85.21.8335); [Yau et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2430-15.2016)). This organization is relevant to directional motor transport and neuronal polarity. It should not be generalized to every species, age, cell type, or compartment: some invertebrate dendrites are predominantly minus-end-out, somatic organization is more complex than a simple antiparallel bundle, and second-harmonic imaging found strongly polarized arrays in mature mouse CA1 and layer-5 apical dendrites ([Kwan, Dombeck, and Webb, 2008](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0805199105)). "Dendrites have mixed polarity" is therefore a useful contrast with canonical axons, not an anatomical invariant.

Jaworski and colleagues showed that EB3-labelled growing microtubule ends can enter spines and influence spine morphology through actin-associated machinery ([Neuron, 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2008.11.013)). Hu and colleagues found in cultured mouse hippocampal neurons that BDNF-related increases in spine PSD-95 depended on dynamic microtubule invasion ([Journal of Neuroscience, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2445-11.2011)). These results support a route from transient cytoskeletal dynamics to postsynaptic organization. They do not show that tubulin lattices store complete memories, carry semantic content, or produce consciousness.

Cantero and colleagues recorded spontaneous electrical oscillations from voltage-clamped rat-brain microtubule bundles under intracellular-like solution conditions, including a prominent fundamental near 39 Hz. They also recorded oscillatory activity and longitudinal signal conduction in membrane-permeabilized cultured mouse hippocampal neurites ([Scientific Reports, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30453-2)). This establishes preparation-specific electrical behavior. It does not establish that microtubules generate cortical gamma synchrony, local field potentials, or scalp EEG in intact living brains.

## SAN interpretation

SAN can treat microtubules as a **cellular readiness and plasticity modulator**. By affecting transport, local structure, protein delivery, and spine remodeling, microtubules may alter which future inputs a neuron can receive and how effectively it can transform them. This is compatible with [[plasticity-cluster-local-memory-update-substrate|distributed memory updating]] in which synaptic, dendritic, molecular, glial, vascular, and network variables contribute at different timescales.

This role belongs to the micro scale. It does not create a miniature observer inside a neuron. Observer-action in SAN remains a distributed receive-transform-re-express-use process across cells, recurrent circuits, body feedback, and action. A molecular change matters to that process only when a downstream biological receiver preserves and causally uses the resulting difference.

genealogy. Those sources repeatedly ask how a proposed microtubule pattern would be retrieved,
amplified, translated into ionic or membrane dynamics, and expressed at circuit scale. See

## Evidence boundary

Keep four claims separate:

1. **Established:** microtubules support neuronal structure, transport, polarity, and dynamic remodeling.
2. **Supported but preparation-bounded:** transient spine invasion can participate in experimentally measured postsynaptic changes, and prepared microtubule assemblies can exhibit electrical oscillations.
3. **SAN hypothesis:** microtubule-dependent changes may help stabilize or reconfigure longer-timescale cellular readiness relevant to learning.
4. **Unestablished:** tubulin states are a general-purpose semantic memory code, perform the computation required for consciousness, or supply a hidden cellular viewer.

The stronger quantum-consciousness claim belongs on [[orch-or|Orch OR]], not in the anatomical definition of a microtubule. See [[microtubules-anesthesia-and-orch-or-evidence-boundary|Microtubules, Anesthesia, and Orch OR: Evidence Boundary]] for the complete claim ladder.
