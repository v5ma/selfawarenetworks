**Confidence In Neural Pattern** is, in [SAN](self-aware-networks.md)'s vocabulary, the *graded* property a [cortical column](cortical-columns.md) or [neural array](neural-array.md) assigns to its current best-fit pattern -- the cell-level analogue of a probability or evidence weight, expressed in oscillatory terms as the *amplitude* and [coherence](coherence-field.md) of the column's [phase pattern](phase-pattern.md) rather than as a scalar number written somewhere. The page exists because other [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) entries treat confidence not as an abstract Bayesian quantity but as a physical readout of how strongly the substrate is committing to a given [3D semantic segmentation](3d-semantic-segmentation.md) hypothesis at a given moment.

In Blumberg's framing, confidence in a neural pattern has three measurable expressions. First, it shows up as *coherence* in [computational phase synchronization](computational-phase-synchronization.md) -- when many [cells](cells-as-pattern-recognition-devices.md) lock to the same phase, the pattern's confidence is high; when they jitter, it is low. Second, it shows up as [burst rate](phasic-burst-firing.md): a column whose [coincidence detection](coincidence-detection.md) is firing many [burst event](burst-event.md) signals per unit time is broadcasting high-confidence support for its tuned pattern; sparse bursts mean weak support. Third, it shows up as *latency to commit*: confident patterns settle quickly into stable [brain state](brain-state.md) configurations, while uncertain patterns linger near [self-organized criticality](brain-self-organized-criticality.md) maintained by [tonic oscillation](tonic-oscillation.md). The trio gives SAN a physically grounded reading of "neural confidence" that does not require an extrinsic Bayesian [observer](observer-mechanism.md).

The concept is the bridge between SAN's substrate-level pages and its [predictive coding](predictive-coding.md)-flavored upper layers. Upstream, [NMDA receptor](nmda-receptor.md) kinetics and [CaMKII activity transfer](camkii-activity-transfer.md) determine how quickly evidence accumulates at [synapses](synapse.md); downstream, [memory as probability distribution](memory-as-probability-distribution.md) and [memory prediction as intelligence](memory-prediction-as-intelligence.md) read those accumulated states as probability distributions over remembered patterns. [NAPOT overview](napot-overview.md) tomography reconstructs the array-level confidence map; [BOT biological oscillatory tomography](bot-biological-oscillatory-tomography.md) does the same at cellular scale; [multi-frequency processing](multi-frequency-processing.md) explains how confidence in different cross-band patterns can be tracked simultaneously. Until further evidence is backfilled, this stub should be read as Blumberg's standing claim that "confidence" is a SAN-native quantity expressible in [brainwaves](brainwaves.md) amplitude/coherence/burst-rate terms, and that the [Self-Aware Networks](self-aware-networks.md) account replaces extrinsic-observer probabilities with intrinsic substrate measurements.
