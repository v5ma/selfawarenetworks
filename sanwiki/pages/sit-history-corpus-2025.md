## Theoretical Core

SIT posits information as a *coincidence pattern* — a registered temporal alignment of independent events — not a symbol. [[napot]] ([[neural-array-projection|Neural Array Projection]] Oscillation Tomography) models cognition as traveling waves scanning distributed networks, resolving the binding problem via sequential local signal exchange. [[quantum-gradient-time-crystal-dilation]] reframes gravity as emergent from gradients in time-density field ρt, not spacetime curvature.
