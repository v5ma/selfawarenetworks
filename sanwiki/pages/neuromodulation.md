## Post-SAN

External neuroscience converges on parts of this picture without committing to its strongest claim. Mainstream tACS literature treats stimulation as a tool to entrain endogenous rhythms and probe their causal role in cognition; SAN goes further by treating the entrainment as a direct intervention on the [[consciousness-as-oscillatory-feedback-loop]] that generates experience, rather than on a correlate of it. Pharmacological neuromodulation in the SAN reading is not just receptor agonism: [[dopamine]] and [[acetylcholine]] modulation, alongside psychedelic-class agents, are read as alterations in the [[cross-frequency-coupling]] regime that determines which predictions reach [[conscious-access]] — connecting the chemistry to the [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine]] story. Where mainstream computational psychiatry frames disorders as deficits in [[predictive-motor-self-model|predictive models]] or precision-weighting, SAN frames them as deficits in oscillatory phase relationships that the predictive machinery rides on; both views agree that neuromodulation is the lever, but disagree on which level of description is doing the explanatory work. The testable difference is concrete: SAN predicts that closed-loop tACS protocols phase-locked to a patient's instantaneous EEG should outperform open-loop tACS in schizophrenia, autism, and ADHD outcomes, because the relevant variable is phase coherence rather than amplitude alone.
