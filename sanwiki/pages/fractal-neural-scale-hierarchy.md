The brain organizes its computational activity across at least four nested fractal scales, each acting as an [[oscillator]] that feeds into the next, forming a [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similar hierarchy from the molecular to the thalamic level.

## Scale levels (bottom-up)

1. **Molecular / dendritic** — microtubule and [[dendrite]]-to-NMDAR level; the microscale where [[quantum-scale-hypothesis]] remains contested.
2. **Horizontal neural circuit** — lateral connections within and across cortical layers; intra-columnar oscillations.
3. **Cortical column** — the column itself as a mesoscale [[oscillator]]; groups of neurons acting as a coherent computational unit.
4. **Thalamic / macroscale** — the [[thalamus]] acts as a macro-frame of representation, synchronizing column-level activity across the cortex.

Each lower scale is nested inside the next, producing a [[fractal-pattern]] of pattern-activity: the same style of oscillatory action-prediction unfolds at every level.

## Fractal oscillatory action-prediction memory system

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s [[gh-formulation|formulation]] condenses the hierarchy into a single name: the brain is a **fractal oscillatory action-prediction memory system**. The key properties are:

- **Oscillatory**: rhythmic patterns drive encoding and retrieval at each scale.
- **Action-prediction**: the system continuously predicts sensory inputs from planned [[actions]] (and vice versa) — related to [[napot]] and [[predictive-coding]].
- **Memory**: each scale maintains a persistence layer — learned connection weights that are reinforced during sleep/dreams.

This framing is scale-independent. Whether or not [[orch-or]] quantum effects at the microtubule level are real, the classical-scale fractal hierarchy holds.

## Orch OR footnote

[[gh-resnote04|Micah]] argues that even if Orchestrated Objective Reduction ([[orch-or]]) is valid, microtubule signals are likely too small to meaningfully tip membrane voltage toward [[action-potential]] threshold. The fractal framework described above remains intact regardless.

## Application: enhanced prosthetics

If this fractal hierarchy is the brain's networking protocol, prosthetic sensory organs (artificial eyes, noses, tactile arrays) could be designed to plug into the same protocol — potentially with expanded spectral ranges beyond biological limits. See [[brain-networking-protocol]].

## Bird vision example

Bird visual cortex may exhibit a deeper fractal stack for multi-scale spatial resolution — driven by flight survival pressure (rapid scale-change perception). This is offered as an evidence-by-analogy supporting the scale-hierarchy thesis.
