# Electrical Synapses

An **electrical synapse** is a specialized junction through which current can pass directly between
coupled cells, usually through gap-junction channels. It is different from a
[[chemical-synapse|chemical synapse]], where a presynaptic terminal releases transmitter into a
synaptic cleft.

## Established physiology

Electrical coupling can transmit voltage changes with very little synaptic delay. The effect is not
one universal, sign-preserving copy operation. It depends on the voltage difference between the
cells, junctional conductance, coupling location, cell geometry, intrinsic membrane currents, and
whether the junction is bidirectional or rectifying.

Electrical synapses can help coupled neurons respond together and can support coincidence
detection. They can also interact with inhibition and intrinsic conductances in ways that change,
rather than simply maximize, population synchrony. A gap junction therefore supplies a physical
coupling route; it does not by itself prove a particular rhythm, percept, memory, or conscious
state.

## Recovering the SAN source idea

The historical SAN notes use electrical synapses to contrast two cellular communication modes:

```text
direct electrical coupling
versus
receptor-mediated chemical transmission
```

That distinction is physiologically legitimate. The sources also explore a broader developmental
claim in which gap-junction coupling is treated as a primitive predecessor of chemical synapses.
Electrical coupling is important during neural development and can coexist with changing chemical
connectivity, but the stronger statement that every chemical synapse literally begins as a gap
junction is not established. The recoverable SAN idea is a transition between communication modes,
not a universal one-junction lineage rule.

The longer historical source page is preserved at [[gap-junction|Gap Junction]]. Its quotations are
evidence of what SAN proposed, while this page supplies the present scientific boundary.

## Bounded SAN interpretation

SAN can treat an electrical synapse as one candidate **micro-scale coordination route**. Direct
coupling may help neighboring receivers share part of an ongoing state, align event timing, or
change the probability that inputs are registered together. In this interpretation it can
contribute to a local [[tonic-phasic-canvas|tonic context]] against which a later difference is
detected.

This does **not** mean:

- all neural synchrony is produced by gap junctions;
- every electrically coupled cell carries the same content;
- a gap junction transmits a complete percept or memory;
- electrical coupling is always excitatory or always synchronizing; or
- direct coupling removes the need for chemical synapses, inhibition, or recurrent circuitry.

## Testable bridge

A SAN-compatible experiment would compare the same local circuit with electrical coupling intact,
selectively reduced, and restored. It would ask whether coupling adds predictive power for
receiver-relative timing, coincidence, state persistence, or routing after controlling for firing
rate, chemical transmission, cell type, and network state. A result restricted to one preparation
should remain restricted to that preparation.
