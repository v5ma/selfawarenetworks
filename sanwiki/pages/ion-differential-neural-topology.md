Ion Differential Neural Topology is [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s proposal to express neuronal firing as a differential equation where the ratio of negative-ion change in dendrites to positive-ion change in synapses equals the resulting topological change in the neuron's structure.

## The Core Formula

```
ΔX / ΔY = Z
```

- **X** — change in negative ion concentration in dendrites (primarily Cl⁻ and intracellular organic anions)
- **Y** — change in positive ion concentration at synapses (Na⁺ influx, Ca²⁺ at active zones)
- **Z** — the resulting topological change: altered synaptic weights, dendritic spine morphology, axonal output patterns, and connection topology

The claim is that brainwave activity reduces to **electrical diffusion of potential differences** — essentially a continuous gradient-collapse computation described by differential calculus rather than discrete spike counts.

## Why Topology

Standard neural models treat Z as a scalar (firing rate). This model treats Z as a *topological change* — a vector pattern encoded across connected neurons. The neuron's firing event is not a signal but a transformation: it reconfigures the neighborhood structure of the local network.

This links to [[category-theory-petri-net-hybrid]] where morphisms represent state-change events within neural places, and to [[phase-wave-differential]] where information is encoded in phase modulations rather than amplitude.

## Scale Universality

The same dX/dY = Z structure is proposed to hold at multiple scales: ion channel → spine → [[dendrite]] → soma → cortical column. This [[gh-a0206z|self]]-similarity across scales connects to [[fractal-consciousness]] and supports [[napot]]'s claim that the same rendering operation recurs at every level of the neural hierarchy.
