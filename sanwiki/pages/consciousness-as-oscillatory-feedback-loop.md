It compresses signal selection, LTP/LTD, prediction playback, inhibition, oscillation, and consciousness into one loop. These are distinct processes with different substrates, timescales, and evidence burdens.

SAN proposes that consciousness depends on a recurrent, self-modifying observer-action architecture rather than oscillation, complexity, or information integration alone. This is a theory claim, not an established sufficiency criterion.

## Feedback must be specified

**Feedback** can mean local recurrent synapses, corticocortical return pathways, thalamocortical loops, cerebellar prediction, autonomic regulation, sensory reafference after action, or explicit neurofeedback training. These mechanisms should not be collapsed into one circular arrow.

Likewise, a system does not literally read and write neural data as a computer does. Presynaptic release, postsynaptic receptors, membrane state, dendritic integration, inhibition, plasticity, axonal output, and downstream response form conditional biological transformations. LTP and LTD are families of plasticity mechanisms, not one-step memory-writing commands.

## What oscillation contributes

Oscillatory activity can organize excitability, sampling, coordination, and effective communication. Whether a given phase relation is consequential depends on source, route, receiver, frequency range, task, and state. Oscillation alone is neither necessary nor sufficient for a consciousness claim, and synchronization can help or hinder different computations.

## SAN interpretation

The strongest SAN formulation is a receive-transform-re-express-use loop:

1. cells and receptors register structured differences;
2. learned state determines which combinations matter;
3. local and recurrent circuits transform the response;
4. outputs change downstream and returning activity;
5. memory, body state, and possible action are revised; and
6. new consequences become input to the next cycle.

This describes an adaptive control architecture. The additional claim that a particular organization is conscious must remain explicit and separately testable.

Molecular tensegrity, chemical waves, microtubules, and ephaptic effects may be investigated as contributing mechanisms, but they are not established macroscopic memory stores or consciousness thresholds.

## Tests

Specify one loop, one content or control variable, one receiver, and one timescale. Perturb the return path while preserving comparable feedforward activity, measure the predicted loss, and test rescue. Then compare phase-aware and SAN-specific predictions with simpler recurrent, predictive, and embodied-control models.
