External paper (Todd, 2025) characterizing intelligence not as symbolic computation, but as a system's capacity to maintain [[high-dimensional-coherence]] within thermodynamically admissible, sub-[[landauer-limit]] regimes.

## Core Mechanisms

1. **Unmeasurable Computation**: Intelligence solves intractable problems through continuous relaxation in a high-dimensional [[phase-space]]. Computation occurs below the [[landauer-limit]], where temporal structure is physically inaccessible.
2. **Dimensional Collapse**: Unmeasurable coherent states integrate and collapse into discrete symbols at a Landauer thermodynamic cost.
3. **Biological Implementation**: Neural systems scale this via [[cross-frequency-coupling]], forming a nested oscillatory hierarchy that maintains capacity without discretization until strategic collapse is required.

## SAN Parallels

This framework resonates with the [[napot]] view that [[neural-oscillations|neural oscillations]] are the substrate of intelligence, and with the [[teleonomic-principle]], where intelligence maximizes information per unit energy by preserving internal coherence against entropy. The view that symbols are shadows of underlying coherence supports the [[coherence-conservation-law]].
