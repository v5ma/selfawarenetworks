## The biological correction

The source labels the coactivity passage "Long Term Depression," but the described operation is
near-coincident firing followed by local excitation, spine emergence, and growth. The Wiki
therefore governs it as activity-dependent structural plasticity. It does not silently discard the
original heading or pretend that the source had already used current terminology.

Established research supports activity-dependent spine enlargement and formation, biochemical and
cytoskeletal control, and multistage contact with existing boutons in bounded preparations.
Applied electric fields can guide developing neurites in culture. None of those findings establishes
that quantum least action, heat-time identity, gravity, or an endogenous electromagnetic maximum
selects an adult dendritic spine's path.
