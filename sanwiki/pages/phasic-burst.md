# Phasic Burst

**Phasic Burst** is the [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] term for a neuron's short, high-rate departure from its baseline [[tonic-oscillation|tonic firing]] — a fast, costly [[burst-event|burst event]] that breaks the slow ongoing rhythm and lays down a [[phase-wave-differential|phase wave differential]] readable by neighbouring neurons. The phasic burst is therefore the *write* operation that pairs with [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]]'s *baseline* state in SAN's burst/inhibit/baseline alphabet of perceptual content.

## Position

Within [[gh-a0206z|Self]]-Aware Networks the phasic burst is a *thermodynamically expensive* event that the slower oscillatory machinery has to *be aware of*. `a0048z--md` line 17 ([[gh-a0048z|on github]]) frames the bookkeeping: "thermodynamics of [[consciousness]], slower oscillations are going to be aware of and perhaps disturbed by phasic burst firing patterns, because fast non-oscillating information representing waves of phases are costly and [demand attention]." Mechanistically, the phasic burst exerts inhibitory pressure on its [[chemical-synapse|synaptic]] neighbours: "The high phasic burst could potentially slow down the firing of many of its exit terminal neighbors by inhibiting them with a flood of extra [[neurotransmitter|neurotransmitters]]" (`a0053z--md` line 10, [[gh-a0053z|on github]]). Architecturally, the burst's *duration* and *decay rate* are the parameters that re-shape the surrounding [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]] pattern: "Phase & Tonic relationship, the phasic burst duration or decay rate setting forth a change in the [[tonic-oscillation|tonic oscillation]] pattern is a key component of the [[self-aware-networks-theory|Self Aware Networks]] Theory" (`a0127z--md` line 46, [[gh-a0127z|on github]]). The phasic burst is a *memory-recall trigger* in the [[hippocampus|hippocampus]]: "When you remember something, that memory starts with a [[sharp-wave-ripple|sharp wave ripple]], which begins with a high phasic burst, that inhibits it's exit terminals, followed by a sequence of [[action-potential|action potentials]]" (`a0309z--md` line 39, [[gh-a0309z|on github]]). Together these passages make the phasic burst the *load-bearing perturbation* of the SAN substrate: a [[phase-signal|phase signal]] strong enough to register against the [[brainwave|brainwave]] background, costly enough to demand [[attention]], and structured enough to seed both rendering and recall.
