# Impossible Spaces

**Impossible Spaces** is the XR-design term for *non-Euclidean* virtual layouts in which two volumes share the same physical room footprint — the user perceives, walks through, and uses spaces whose Euclidean union exceeds the room they actually occupy. Within [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]] this is the design lever that exposes the [[brain-as-rendering-engine|brain-as-rendering-engine]] commitment most directly: if the brain is *rendering* spatial experience from [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] rather than *measuring* a Euclidean ground truth, then *impossible* layouts that violate Euclidean closure are the experimental setting where the rendering substrate is least concealed.
