## Position

In SAN, the move from "protein" to "3D protein structure" is the move from a list of amino acids to a folded, conformational object whose *shape* is what other molecules read. Three points follow that the framework presses. First, in the [[3d-protein-receptor]] account, recognition is geometric: a ligand fits or it does not, and the receptor's 3D pose is what makes the fit a [[coincidence-detection|coincidence-detection]] event in the same arithmetic that elsewhere SAN gives as [[coincidence-as-a-bit|coincidence-as-a-bit]]. Second, in the [[microtubules]] / [[microtubule-memory]] line, the lattice geometry of tubulin is the substrate on which fine-grained information is held inside the neuron — the pages do not require a Penrose-style quantum reading to make the structural claim, only that the 3D arrangement be stable, addressable, and read out by other proteins. Third, the synaptic memory pages — [[kibra-pkm-zeta-memory-anchor]], [[dendritic-spine-temporal-memory]], [[engrams]], [[long-term-potentiation]] — all depend on protein conformation surviving long enough to anchor a state across [[brainwave|brainwave]] cycles; the 3D structure is the slow variable that the fast electrical activity rides on. Read together, the claim is that the cellular-and-subcellular layer of the [[fractal-brain-scale-hierarchy|fractal scale hierarchy]] is built out of 3D protein shapes whose persistence is what makes [[cellular-memory|cellular memory]] possible. The framework treats this as continuous with the larger [[oscillatory-convergence-memory|oscillatory convergence memory]] story rather than as an alternative to it.
