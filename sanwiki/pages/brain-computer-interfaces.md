# Brain-Computer Interfaces

**Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)** measure signals related to nervous-system activity, infer a bounded variable, and convert that estimate into an external action or calibrated intervention. Some systems record; some stimulate; some close both directions into an adaptive loop. No existing BCI reads an untouched thought or writes an arbitrary complete perception.

BCIs are central to the SAN and Artificial Neurology programs because they can turn broad claims about neural state, timing, routing, perception, and action into measurable experiments. They are instruments and intervention systems, not automatic validators of NAPOT, consciousness, or speculative physics.

## Five Different Objects Often Called “The Signal”

1. **Biological state:** membrane potentials, synaptic currents, spikes, transmitter release, metabolism, blood flow, muscle activity, movement, and network context.
2. **Measured physical quantity:** voltage, magnetic field, light, pressure, chemistry, or another quantity reaching a sensor.
3. **Instrument transformation:** tissue conduction, electrode geometry, reference, amplification, filtering, sampling, and noise.
4. **Model estimate:** the task variable predicted from the recorded data.
5. **Returned consequence:** cursor motion, speech, stimulation, sensory feedback, or another event available to the user.

These are related but not interchangeable. A decoded label is not directly observed semantic content, and a stimulation command is not the inverse of a recording.

## Capability Ladder

| Level | Demonstrated operation | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Detect | A signal changes with a condition | Meaning, source, or causal use |
| Decode | A model predicts a bounded task variable | Unrestricted thought reading |
| Control | The estimate drives an effector or communication channel | Natural movement or embodiment |
| Stimulate | An intervention changes tissue, report, or behavior | A specific reconstructed percept |
| Closed loop | Measurement changes intervention in real time | General adaptation or safety under drift |
| Artificial perception | A calibrated intervention evokes a specific usable sensory result | Full multimodal NerveGear experience |
| Cognitive extension | External computation repeatedly assists a user's ongoing loop | Merged identity or shared consciousness |

Progress at one level cannot be promoted to another without additional evidence.

## Established Examples

Intracortical recording has enabled people with paralysis to control cursors, robotic effectors, handwriting decoders, and speech neuroprostheses in defined tasks. Intracortical stimulation has produced localized somatosensory percepts in some participants. Adaptive stimulation has used measured biomarkers to update treatment in selected disorders.

Representative primary studies include:

- [Hochberg et al. (2012), neurally controlled robotic arm](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11076);
- [Willett et al. (2021), handwriting BCI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03506-2);
- [Willett et al. (2023), speech neuroprosthesis](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06377-x);
- [Flesher et al. (2021), intracortical tactile feedback](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abd0380); and
- [Little et al. (2013), adaptive deep-brain stimulation](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.23951).

Each result is preparation-, participant-, task-, sensor-, and model-specific. None establishes a universal code for objects, thoughts, selfhood, or conscious experience.

## Read and Write Are Different Models

A read model estimates a hidden state from measurements filtered through tissue and instruments. A write model chooses an intervention intended to produce a bounded consequence through uncertain anatomy and state-dependent dynamics.

A serious write experiment therefore needs:

- a declared target experience or behavior;
- target anatomy and route;
- spatial, temporal, waveform, and dose parameters;
- within-person calibration;
- sham and alternative-site controls;
- physiological, behavioral, and report endpoints;
- reversibility and adverse-effect monitoring; and
- a rescue or recalibration path.

Decoding broccoli-related activity does not imply that replaying the decoder's features will produce the experience of broccoli.

## What BCIs Can Test About SAN

A BCI can test a SAN mechanism only when the mechanism supplies an operational variable and a discriminating prediction.

Examples include:

- whether receiver-relative timing predicts downstream response beyond rate, power, waveform, delay, movement, and flexible recurrent baselines;
- whether recurrent projections reconstruct and causally update a latent state beyond equal-information alternatives;
- whether a declared neural-tuning change alters later effective routing; and
- whether an action-return intervention changes the next perceptual or control estimate in the predicted direction.

A positive result supports only the tested relation. It does not automatically establish NAPOT, PWD, the distributed observer, phenomenal consciousness, or all of SAN.

## Consciousness Boundary

BCIs can measure variables correlated with wakefulness, report, perception, confidence, attention, memory, motor preparation, or perturbational response. Those targets must remain separate. No single decoded pattern or complexity value is the resolution meter of consciousness.

Claims about conscious content or level require declared endpoints, no-report or report-matched controls where appropriate, strong alternative models, and selective causal evidence. Claims about machine or hybrid consciousness require an additional architecture and moral-status assessment.

[a0018z](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/7d1431213cd4ac9788848a5396ec2992ece11cfd/a0018z.md)

This genealogy establishes that BCI was not a late application added to SAN. It was intended as an

### Interface progression and multimodal extension

The source [a0073z](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/e2b44e57d1eb854928e4070d91cd3f3c8793012a/a0073z.md)
asserts an internal creation date of January 5, 2015 and was publicly Git-fixed in June 2022. It
proposed joining AI, neurofeedback, stimulation, virtual reality, and wearable computation into an
extension for organizing thought and memory. Those modalities are not all BCIs, and they do not
share one measurement or intervention model. The recoverable operation is a modular person-device
loop in which several separately validated channels contribute to one adaptive interface.

A September 28, 2021 recording, publicly Git-fixed as
[b0181y](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/29956416cb7c074bbfb8c32e13611a6568cc290e/b0181y.md)
in August 2022, developed the interface problem more explicitly:

```text
command line -> graphical interface -> virtual or spatial interface -> BCI
```

The proposal was not just to connect neural tissue to a computer. It asked how a person would work
with AI through language, images, sound, spatial organization, preferences, programs, and returned
sensory consequences. The detailed read/write and non-invasive-route recovery is preserved on
[[brain-computer-interface-neural-readwrite]].

### Consciousness measurement question

The June 2022 source
[a0049z](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/6ed486abf8c8bc2f8c1b4d9a63eb6128aa16eebd/a0049z.md)
asked whether present or future BCI measurements, including an integrated-information quantity,
might provide hints about the resolution, scale, location, or orientation of conscious patterns.
That is a historical SAN research question, not an established measurement result. No validated
single BCI variable measures the spatial resolution of consciousness, and an integrated-information
estimate must not be conflated with spatial resolution, content, level, report, or anatomical
location.

A scientifically bounded successor would test those targets separately. For example, a study could
ask whether a declared combination of neural measurements predicts perceptual spatial resolution,
content discriminability, confidence, or perturbational response beyond sensory input, task,
movement, arousal, report, and flexible recurrent baselines. Even a positive result would validate
the declared estimator and conditions, not a universal consciousness meter.

### Mind extension and subjective time

The same 2022 [a0087z source](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/009c908e6c4fba12166c6eb3b9df6623d2e5edbb/a0087z.md)
proposed that connection to an artificial cortex could increase the amount of cognition available
within an interval and thereby make experience feel slower or richer. Current neuroscience does not
establish that adding computation to a BCI physically slows time, increases a person's neural
processing rate, or extends experienced lifespan.

The recoverable experimental question is narrower and testable: can a calibrated assistive loop
change temporal discrimination, judged duration, event density, memory for an interval, workload,
or the number and quality of completed cognitive operations? Such a study would need controls for
attention, arousal, novelty, task difficulty, motor demand, expectation, and retrospective memory.
The outcomes could diverge; improved task performance would not by itself prove altered subjective
time.

### Source-faithful SAN test ladder

| Historical SAN proposition | Scientifically bounded successor | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| BCI can help test SAN or NAPOT | A declared SAN variable adds predictive or causal value in a bounded interface | Matched baselines, held-out data, perturbation, recalibration, and failure conditions |
| BCI may reveal conscious-pattern resolution | Separate estimators test content, level, spatial discrimination, confidence, and perturbational response | Strong confound control, declared endpoints, and no universal-meter inference |
| AI, VR, neurofeedback, and stimulation can form a mind-extension system | Separately validated modules form a recurrent person-device-environment loop | A measurement model, intervention model, adaptation model, safety case, and longitudinal outcome for each route |
| An artificial cortex may change experienced time | Assistive computation may change one or more psychophysical and cognitive time measures | Preregistered temporal tasks, workload and memory controls, within-person calibration, and replication |
| BCI may support Artificial Neurology | A hybrid loop can test selected perception-action, tuning, recurrence, or adaptation mechanisms | Incremental value over non-SAN models without inferring sentience from task success |

The durable SAN contribution is therefore not the claim that every BCI already reads, writes, or
extends a mind. It is the joined experimental question of how measurement, receiver-relative state,
recurrence, intervention, body feedback, external computation, and adaptation can form one
observer-action loop without inserting a hidden viewer.

## Safety

BCI development requires medical and research oversight appropriate to the intervention. Important concerns include surgery, stimulation effects, infection, seizure risk, model drift, unequal performance, privacy, agency, dependence, vendor control, cybersecurity, and withdrawal from an adaptive system. Wiki material is not clinical instruction.
