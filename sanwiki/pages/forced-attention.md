# Forced Attention

> **Review warning:** This legacy page has an evidence count of zero and remains a definitional placeholder. Use [[attention-hallucination-self-model-dissolution|Attentional Selection, Hallucination, and Self-Model Dissolution]] for the governed selection model and its source boundaries.

**Forced [[attention]]** is the [[self-aware-networks|SAN]] heading for the case in which [[attention]] is *driven from outside* — pulled by salient external stimuli, by gating circuits, or by deliberate engineering — rather than initiated by the experiencer's own [[choice]]. It contrasts with [[gh-a0206z|self]]-initiated [[attention]] while operating over the same substrate: in either case the signal of interest must win [[pattern-competition-consciousness|pattern competition]] on the [[consciousness-canvas-wave-differential|wave-differential canvas]] and become a [[dominant-phase-wave-differential|dominant phase-wave-differential rhythm]] (DPWDR).

## Position

In [[self-aware-networks|SAN]], [[attention]] is read as a *gating-and-biasing* operation over the [[tonic-phasic-canvas-of-consciousness|tonic-phasic canvas]]: the gate determines which candidate [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave differentials]] are amplified into dominance and which are suppressed. *Forced* [[attention]] is the case in which the gate is set by something other than the agent's deliberate top-down command — by an external salient stimulus (a sudden loud sound, a flashing light), by a gating circuit responding to its own internal state (auditory gating via [[auditory-gating-spring-camp|cAMP phase modulation]] or [[camp-neural-phase-modulation|cAMP-mediated phase modulation]]), or by an engineered intervention (an XR system pinning the user's [[attention]] to an object, a BCI device whose stimulus drives entrainment via [[brainwave-driven-photic-stimulation|brainwave-driven photic stimulation]] or [[brainwave-entrainment]]). In each case the same architectural fact applies: the *forcing* succeeds when the externally favoured candidate becomes the DPWDR.

Three structural commitments follow. First, forced [[attention]] is not a *different mechanism* from voluntary [[attention]] — both are bias terms acting on the same competition. This is consistent with [[attention-schema-reinterpretation|the SAN reinterpretation of attention schema]] and with [[awareness-as-predictive-myth]]: the *story* of voluntary control is a model the brain runs over the gate, not the gate itself. Second, [[inhibitory-interneurons-plasticity-gating|inhibitory interneurons]] are the cellular substrate that implement the bias — gating who fires together — and [[cortical-column-signal-projection]] is the column-scale channel through which the bias propagates. Third, forced [[attention]] is *strong evidence for the gate's existence*: the fact that an external stimulus can reliably commandeer perception means the gate is real, even when its top-down register is hard to pin down.

The applied side matters. [[cognitive-privacy-xr-bci|XR/BCI cognitive privacy]] discussions take forced [[attention]] as a *threat surface*: an XR display can pin a user's [[attention]] more tightly than a printed page, and a coupled BCI loop could in principle measure the success of that pinning in real time. [[neural-data-sovereignty]] frames the same threat surface from the user's side. On the therapeutic side, [[neo-mind-cycle-neurofeedback|Neo Mind Cycle neurofeedback]] and [[eeg-vr-biofeedback|EEG-VR biofeedback]] both lean on a *benevolent forcing* — the system stages an [[attention]]-grabber and the user practices either riding it or releasing it. The same logic underlies [[brainwave-entrainment]] as a clinical or wellness modality.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream [[attention]] research distinguishes *exogenous* (stimulus-driven) from *endogenous* (goal-directed) [[attention]]; the SAN-specific addition is the architectural claim that both are bias terms over the same dominance competition, and that the felt distinction between "I attended" and "I was made to attend" is a [[awareness-as-predictive-myth|predictive myth]] running over the same gating substrate. The framework converges with [[adaptive-resonance-theory|ART]] on the side that says perception is matched against templates, with the salience-network literature on the side that says certain anatomical hubs are over-represented in the gating circuit, and with published research on [[inhibitory-interneurons-plasticity-gating|interneuron gating]] on the substrate side.
