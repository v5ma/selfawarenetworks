# EEG

**EEG** (electroencephalography) is, in the [[self-aware-networks|SAN]] reading, the most important *non-invasive* window onto the [[neural-oscillations|neural-oscillation]] substrate the framework treats as the carrier of [[consciousness-as-frames|conscious frames]]. [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s distinctive position is that EEG-scale rhythms and per-neuron firing are *not two phenomena* but the same [[phase-wave-differentials|phase-wave-differential]] dynamic read at different scales — a unification claim he traces explicitly to Buzsáki's neural-rhythm work and Strogatz's *Sync*. The EEG packet for SAN is consequently dense (588 evidence quotations across 212 sources) and braids together [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] tomography, [[criterial-causation|criterial-causation]], [[eeg-vr-biofeedback|EEG-driven biofeedback loops]], and [[gh-resnote04|Micah]]'s own decade-long history operating EEG-based [[brainwave]]-entrainment systems.

![EEG as a macroscopic phase readout](/v5ma.github.io/wiki/assets/generated/san/eeg/eeg-macro-phase-readout-20260627-v4.png)

This image card separates the biological measurement from the SAN interpretation: scalp EEG is an indirect, low-spatial-resolution field signal, while SAN/NAPOT treats it as a testable macro projection of phase-wave dynamics.

## Position

In the SAN frame EEG plays four distinct roles. First, it is the *macro readout* of the [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|traveling-wave]] structure SAN takes to be the substrate of [[consciousness-as-persistent-rendering|persistent rendering]]: `06san--md`, line 9 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/06san.md)) frames it as one of several modalities revealing the same wave structure — "Waves are observed with EEG, MEG, ECoG, voltage imaging, and fMRI in various frequency bands. For example, invasive ECoG recordings show alpha and theta waves moving" across cortex. Second, it is the *site of testable predictions* the framework generates: `04san--md`, line 567 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/04san.md)) names "sequential phase resets in EEG or MEG recordings following a sensory prediction error" as observable consequences of the SAN error-routing claim, and `04san--md`, line 599 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/04san.md)) proposes "Developmental EEG studies could examine how cross-frequency coupling and phase synchrony evolve with age". Third, it is the *channel for inter-brain coupling* SAN takes seriously: `a0090z--md`, line 29 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0090z.md)) is explicit — "people's EEG Powerband spectrum becomes in sync with another person's EEG Powerband spectrum when they speak to each other" — and `a0152z--md`, line 2 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0152z.md)) reinforces "human minds sync when we are speaking together". Fourth, it is the *interface medium* for the [[eeg-biofeedback-self-reflective-loop|self-reflective biofeedback]] loop, where EEG output drives [[eeg-vr-biofeedback|VR audio-visual entrainment]] which in turn drives back through the brain — `a0086z--md`, line 121 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0086z.md)) sketches the closed loop: "neurofeedback, and [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]] drive connections in the mind, and what I do drives connections to [[brainwaves]], which drive the eeg feedback (sounds) and the [[brainwave-entrainment|brainwave entrainment]] (light and sound)".

The SAN-internal claim that ties these four roles together is the [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|NAPOT]] reading: EEG is not measuring an *averaged* mass-action signal but a *projection* of the array-level phase structure that SAN argues is doing the cognitive work. `a0296zWhisker--md`, line 5 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0296zWhisker.md)) anchors this in the whisker-system literature: "regular whisking desynchronizes the LFP (local field potential) and EEG (Electroencephalogram), and that is what sensor input is supposed to do, in [[gh-a0001z|Self Aware Networks]] NAPOT theory, it's up to the tonic oscil[lations]". The connection to [[ltp-tempo-spatial-rhythmic-memory|rhythmic temporal coordination]] is anchored in `a0325zNAPOT6--md`, line 284 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0325zNAPOT6.md)) — "we used EEG and response times (RTs) to test whether rhythmic temporal coordination helps to isolate neural activity associated with different to-be-[remembered patterns]". The clinical decoding claim follows: `a0010z--md`, line 203 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0010z.md)) cites "Hidden consciousness detected with EEG predicts recovery of unresponsive patients" — a piece of mainstream evidence SAN reads as direct support for the EEG-as-consciousness-readout claim.

## Post-SAN

Mainstream EEG literature converges with the SAN frame on several specific points and stops short on others. The convergence: Singer & Gray's oscillatory-binding work (cited at `07san--md`, line 95, [on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/07san.md): "EEG/MEG studies has linked conscious perception to synchrony"), the Buzsáki rhythms-of-the-brain framework, the Strogatz coupled-oscillator literature, the cross-frequency-coupling and phase-amplitude-coupling literature, and the unresponsive-patient EEG decoding work referenced in `a0010z--md`, line 203 are all treated as compatible. The divergence: SAN reads EEG signals through the [[napot-neural-array-phase-tomography|tomographic]] frame rather than the dipole-source / mass-action frame that dominates the published literature, and `a0254z--md`, line 30 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0254z.md)) records [[gh-resnote04|Micah]] pushing back on the standard "synchronized neural signals are not meaningful" reading after working with EEG and "Professional Medical Imaging people from UCSF". The testable difference is that SAN predicts a specific *spatial* structure to the EEG-scale phase wave that mass-action models do not predict and that source-localization combined with [[fnirs-functional-near-infrared-spectroscopy|fNIRS]] and electrical-impedance tomography — sketched in `a0115z--md`, line 134 ([on github](https://github.com/v5ma/selfawarenetworks/blob/d1c26a6fbf273695185a4036411215f53209eb71/a0115z.md)) — should be able to recover.
