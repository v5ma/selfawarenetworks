# DPWDR

**DPWDR** is the canonical SAN abbreviation for [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|Dominant Phase Wave Differential Rhythm]] — the [[phase-wave-differentials|phase wave differential]] that has out-competed neighbouring differentials and crossed a spatial-and-temporal persistence threshold, so that it can be rendered as the contents of a conscious moment inside the [[napot-theory|NAPOT]] tomographic frame. The full concept page is [[dominant-phase-wave-differential-rhythm|Dominant Phase Wave Differential Rhythm]]; this page documents the abbreviation as it appears in the [[self-aware-network|Self-Aware Networks]] book chapters and notes, and threads the immediate neighbours so that readers landing on the acronym find their way back to the full treatment.

## Post-SAN

There is no direct mainstream-literature use of the *DPWDR* acronym. The closest external terminology lives in the [[traveling-waves-neural-oscillations|cortical traveling-wave]] literature (Muller, Sejnowski, and adjacent), in oscillatory binding-by-synchrony work, and in the [[sharp-wave-ripples|sharp-wave-ripple]] and [[attention-schema-theory|attention-schema]] traditions. Where SAN's DPWDR adds to those is the *dominance criterion*: not every traveling wave or every binding event is a DPWDR — only those that out-persist their neighbours and beat the [[brain-as-memory-prediction-machine|predictive carrier]]. That criterion is what makes DPWDR the thing the [[continuous-neural-rendering|rendering loop]] reads, and it is what gives the acronym its load-bearing role in the [[saov9-self-aware-networks-overview|SAOv9 overview]].
