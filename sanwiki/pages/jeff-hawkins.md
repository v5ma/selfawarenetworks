# Jeff Hawkins

**Jeff Hawkins** is the computational neuroscientist, Numenta and Redwood Neuroscience Institute founder, and author of *On Intelligence* and *A Thousand Brains* whose work on [[cortical-columns|cortical columns]], [[hierarchical-temporal-memory|hierarchical temporal memory]], [[sparse-distributed-representation|sparse distributed representations]], and [[reference-frames|reference frames]] is a load-bearing influence on [[self-aware-networks|Self-Aware Networks]].
