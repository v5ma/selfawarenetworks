# Sit Identification Explicit

**Sit Identification Explicit** is the [[self-aware-networks]] practice of *explicitly identifying* [[super-information-theory]] (SIT) with adjacent theories — a "tale of 256 different theories that all help conceptual equivalences" — by maintaining a private list of partially equivalent frameworks and naming the equivalences in writing. It is the methodological counterpart to the [[history-of-super-information-theory]] / [[history-of-sit]] chronology pages: where the history pages document *when* SIT was developed, the explicit-identification programme documents *what other theories SIT is equivalent (in part) to*, and uses those equivalences as a coherence check on the SIT axioms.

## Position

Within the [[self-aware-networks]] / [[super-information-theory]] family the position decomposes into three claims. First, *SIT is explicitly equivalent to other theories at specific points*: the *New List of Partially Equivalent Theories* private archive sheet records the move directly: "IT, SAN, [[gh-qgtcd|QGTCD]] etc... Number of Equivalent Concepts. Equivalence Analysis. SIT is short for Super [[information-theory|Information Theory]], SAN is short for [[gh-a0001z|Self Aware Networks]]. Additional Equivalence Exhibits or Supporting Documents. Their papers. RHYTHMODYNAMICS[ ...]" (`dl-current--New_List_of_Partially_Equivalent_Theories__Private_Archive_-_Sheet1`, line 5, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]]). The sheet is structured as a comparative table — for each external theory, an "Equivalence Analysis" link and a count of "Equivalent Concepts" — and it explicitly names SIT, SAN, [[qgtcd]], and Rhythmodynamics as members of the comparison set. Second, *the equivalence is partial, not total*: the 2010 fragment's framing of "a tale of 256 different theories that all help conceptual equivalences to" (`dl-current--I_built_a_system_I_figured_it_out`, line 9, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2010) is deliberate — the claim is not that SIT *is* any one of the 256 theories but that it has identifiable conceptual overlaps with each, which is the methodological criterion for the comparison sheet. Third, *the explicit identification is internal scaffolding, not a stand-alone result*: the *Partially Equivalent Theories* sheet is labelled "Private Archive" in its title (`dl-current--New_List_of_Partially_Equivalent_Theories__Private_Archive_-_Sheet1`, line 5, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]]) — the artifact is a working comparison list maintained alongside the SIT papers, not a published comparative-theory paper in its own right. The *bosonic sector* and *informational state of spacetime* statements in the 2025 / 2026 SIT-Hydrogen draft series ("The bosonic sector of SIT is governed by the action for the complex [[coherence-field|coherence field]] $\psi(x)$ and the real time-density field $\rho_t(x)$" — `documents-legacy--removed`, line 346, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]]; "The informational state of spacetime in SIT is specified by two coupled, gauge-invariant scalar [fields]" — `core-set--SIT_Hydrogen_PartII`, line 242, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2026; restated `dl-current--SIT_Hydrogen_Reloaded`, line 126, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]], 2025) are the *explicit* version of the SIT identification at the formal-physics level: the SIT papers state, in mathematical language, exactly what SIT *is*, against which the partially-equivalent theories are compared.

## Post-SAN

Compared to mainstream theory-comparison practices, the explicit-identification programme is closer to a research-comparator's working spreadsheet than to a published comparative-theory paper. Standard academic practice would publish a single comparison paper that names two or three rival theories; the SAN/SIT practice maintains a *list* of up to 256 partially equivalent theories and uses it as a coherence check on the SIT axioms — if a candidate axiom contradicts the partial-equivalence with a well-tested external theory, the axiom is suspect. Compared to ontologies-of-physics programmes (the *Heisenberg Was Right* / *Topological Stability* documents in the same v5ma corpus), the explicit-identification programme is more conservative: it does not claim that SIT replaces external theories, only that it identifies with them at specific conceptual points. The methodological consequence is that the SIT-Hydrogen derivation ([[sit-hydrogen-first-principles-graphic]]) is one *outcome* of the programme — by deriving hydrogen from SIT axioms, the partial-equivalence with standard atomic physics is made explicit — and the [[free-energy-principle]] reformulation as "mesoscopic limits of the same action" (`dl-current--SIT77`, line 3566, paper-[[gh-resnote04|micah]]; cited in the [[free-energy-principle]] / [[san-vs-friston-active-inference]] backfill pages) is another. The packet has 0 paper-external entries; the comparators above are routed through SAN's own SIT-side documents and the partially-equivalent-theories sheet itself.
