## Learned paths change what happens next

`a0638z.md` compares a learned synaptic network with drainage paths. The analogy earns a useful
operation:

```text
past activity changes structure and excitability
-> a new input enters a biased but still plastic network
-> excitation and inhibition recruit a sparse ensemble
-> rate, timing, and phase relations change across the population
-> part of the learned pattern can be reexpressed later
```

The new input does not merely replay the old path. It layers a new variation over the learned
network. This connects [[memory-state-drift-virtual-mind]],
[[memory-prediction-rendering-sequence]], and
[[inhibitory-routing-signal-processing-grid-graph]].

Calling the result an engram is defensible only at the level of distributed physical and chemical
changes in cells and circuits. The surrounding theta or other population rhythm can provide timing
context, but it is not the entire memory.

## Phase and rate can carry different information

The source proposes that cells change their phase relation to a slower rhythm while many neurons
change together in a sparse distributed pattern.

Current evidence makes the bounded version plausible:

```text
which cells fire
+ how often they fire
+ when they fire relative to a population rhythm
+ which cells are suppressed
-> a richer population state than firing rate alone
```

Human medial-temporal neurons have shown sequence-sensitive firing at different theta phases.
SAN's later PWD claim goes further by treating the changing multivariable difference as a
receiver-transforming event. That extra claim must be tested rather than treated as identical to
ordinary phase coding.

## A 2012 story from attention to action

The February 26, 2012 `a0642z.md` discussion follows a cat hunting and a player waiting for a target.
Its strongest sequence is:

```text
goal and anticipatory readiness
-> sensory search
-> rejection of irrelevant possibilities
-> recognition of a target
-> descending motor preparation and command
-> coordinated action
-> feedback and practice-dependent improvement
```

This is an early ancestor of the later sight-to-action loop. The modern anatomical version includes
sensory cortex, association networks, basal ganglia, cerebellum, brainstem, spinal circuits,
corticofugal Layer 5 neurons, motor pools, muscles, and returned proprioceptive and visual evidence.

Practice can change synapses, circuits, strategies, and myelination. It is not accurate to say each
successful game action deposits a fixed amount of myelin or that expertise universally makes the
brain 100 times faster.

The resulting route connects:

- [[sensory-to-symbol-neural-grounding]];
- [[memory-prediction-rendering-sequence]];
- [[agency-valuation-action-feedback]];
- [[expert-data-structures]].

## Arrays can be compared after the invariant is named

`a0640z.md` places eyes, ears, tongue, touch, smell, dendrites, circuits, cortical columns, large
brain networks, EEG, MEG, MRI, and anatomy into one cross-scale outline.

A disciplined comparison asks which operation is shared:

```text
distributed sampling?
local transformation?
topographic organization?
convergence?
divergence?
population aggregation?
feedback?
```

An eye and a dendritic arbor may both be called arrays, but that does not make their physics,
connectivity, or function isomorphic. The comparison becomes valuable when it predicts what a
measurement should preserve across scales. This is the correct role for
[[scale-invariant-patterns]], [[bci-instrument-atlas]], and
[[receptor-to-phase-sensory-quantification]].

## The thalamic exception for smell

`a0643z.md` uses Krista and Tatiana Hogan to ask how shared sensory input might pass through a
thalamic connection. That question belongs in the natural-history and BCI source record.

The sensory-routing correction is specific:

- most major sensory streams use first-order thalamic relays before primary cortex;
- olfactory input reaches primary olfactory cortex without that first-order thalamic relay;
- later mediodorsal-thalamic pathways still contribute to odor identification and value;
- this does not mean smell first routes through prefrontal cortex.

The larger BCI question remains: what signal, route, and learned decoder would let one nervous
system use information originating in another?

## Entification evolved in layers

`a0646z.md` must not be read as one 2014 statement.

### 2014: approximate internal models

The source says the brain's answers are guesses and approximations built from spaces, points,
distances, and directions. The useful idea is constructive perception: the current model is useful
without being a perfect copy.

### 2022: oscillation and entification

The May 2022 layer proposes that close, coordinated phase relations help make information function
as one entity-like pattern. A rendered rock is not the physical rock, but an internally compressed
model constrained by interactions with it.

### 2023: the aggregate sensor-transmitter self

The December 2023 addition proposes that many cellular sensor-transmitters form a larger
field-and-circuit system whose persistent, dominant, novel, excited, and inhibited differences
become content for the aggregate.

This leads toward [[field-cell-self-bioelectric-information-entity]] and
[[entification-observer-problem]]. Synchrony alone is insufficient; the proposal needs recurrent
causal integration, differentiation, persistence, body and memory linkage, action eligibility, and
measurable exclusion conditions.

## False starts stay visible

`a0641z.md` and `a0647z.md` explore whether diffusion, density, particle collision, vortices,
oscillation, or frozen events might explain gravity, time, and matter. `a0647z.md` explicitly says
the idea was kept despite being discarded.

The current evidence boundary is straightforward:

- special-relativistic velocity time dilation and gravitational time dilation are distinct;
- clocks lower in a gravitational potential run more slowly;
- particle collision density and Fick's first law do not supply the established derivation;
- electrons are not established as cones made from neural power bands;
- the strong nuclear interaction is not simply spatial curvature.

The false starts belong in the QGTCD/SIT genealogy because they show which problems motivated later
models. They do not become later theory retroactively.

## An early Choice ancestor

The August 30, 2013 `a0648z.md` note says local electrical and chemical weightings combine into
collective choices.

In plain language:

```text
many cells change their readiness and output
-> alternatives receive unequal support
-> one distributed route gains control of action
-> the person experiences and reports the resulting decision
```

The note predates the later named inhibitory-interneuron Choice operator. It is an ancestor, not
the complete later model. Its meditation and tone suggestion remains personal historical context.

## Other minds require operational criteria

The September 21, 2013 `a0649z.md` discussion asks:

- why brains support consciousness while rivers apparently do not;
- how alien or machine minds could be recognized;
- whether our access to only one first-person perspective creates blind spots;
- whether self-modifying AI could discover properties humans cannot;
- how qualia, matter, mathematics, and computation relate;
- whether consciousness is one member of a larger class of unknown system properties.

The source explicitly rejects simple panpsychism and distinguishes itself from Orch OR, but its
speaker formatting is ambiguous. The correct Wiki use is a mixed-attribution research intake, not
an author-specific priority proof.

The modern research program is operational:

```text
candidate system
-> architecture and causal organization
-> recurrent integration and differentiation
-> memory, body, valuation, and action
-> perturbation and counterfactual response
-> report or behavior under controlled tests
-> uncertainty and alternative explanations
```

This connects [[mechanistic-emergence-model-adequacy]],
[[feedback-strange-loops-self-model]], and
[[artificial-neurology-states-stages-development]].

## Artificial Neurology is a developmental program

`a0650z.md` and `a0651z.md` expand the machine side of SAN beyond one neural-network model:

```text
embodied sensors and body state
-> learned local units and network architecture
-> memory and prediction
-> affect and valuation
-> choice and motor action
-> social learning and development
-> safety, governance, and long-term survival
```

The sources ask how sentient machines could be developed and deployed safely, not merely how a
robot wins an evolutionary benchmark. A complete program needs developmental stages, reversible
testing, welfare and moral-status criteria, human oversight, security, failure analysis, and
evidence that separates competent behavior from conscious experience.

`a0651z.md` also correctly resists treating the amygdala as a single fear center. Threat-detection
and defense circuits are not identical to a consciously felt emotion. That distinction strengthens
the proposed affect architecture.
