## Position

Within [[self-aware-networks|SAN]] *sensory pattern distinction* names the act by which one pattern becomes distinguishable from another at the input-side surface — the work that has to happen before [[dendritic-pattern-recognition|dendritic pattern recognition]] and [[dendritic-pattern-detection|dendritic pattern detection]] can produce a recognized percept. The SAN distinction-family treats *distinction* as more fundamental than *recognition*: distinction is what produces the contrast, recognition is the assignment of a label to the contrast. This page is the *sensory-input* application of that family: the distinction operation specifically as performed on the upstream pattern coming into a [[sensor-transmitter|sensor-transmitter]] array, rather than on internal state or on motor output.
